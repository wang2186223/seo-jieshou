Chapter 686

Lyra squinted at the horizon. Out there lay a legit, F1-grade racetrack. A couple of sports cars tore down the straightaway, tires screaming. The roar of those engines vibrated right through her boots. She let out a slow, wicked smirk.

By the time Penelope and Vivienne finally rolled up, their grand plan was already dead. They’d expected to find Lyra cornered, crying, and getting ripped to shreds by their crew. Nope. Not even close. Lyra stood dead-center of a massive crowd, soaking up the spotlight like a damn queen. Both girls froze, jaw-dropped.

"What the actual hell?" Penelope’s face twisted, pure green-eyed jealousy eating her alive. This was *her* playground. She’d set up this whole night just to humiliate Lyra and put her in her place. And honestly? Penelope was always the star of this show. Always.

She marched straight into the crowd, teeth clenched. "Why are you guys hanging all over her?" she shrieked. "She’s a broke-ass country cousin! She couldn't even get past the front gate without me, and she literally stole my car to get here!"

Vivienne grabbed Penelope’s wrist, putting on her best fake-nice voice. "Penelope, babe, chill. Lyra’s just... desperate to fit in, you know? She’s never had anyone look at her like this. Let her have her little moment."

"Fit in? With us? Please, she’s trash," Penelope snapped, dropping the sweet-girl act entirely. She scanned the parking lot. No sign of Silas. *Figures,* she thought. Silas probably got scared shitless by Lyra’s psycho driving and bailed. That gave her all the confidence she needed.

She stepped aside, revealing a crew of beefy street punks. Right at the front was Jasper, sporting neon-green hair. "Hey, Pen, if this chick doesn't know how we play, the boys and I can show her," Jasper said. He’d been Penelope’s loyal dog since high school. Seeing her pissed, the whole crew locked their eyes on Lyra, looking for a fight.

Jasper flashed a nasty, yellow-toothed grin. "Hey, hot stuff. New toys gotta follow the rules." He stepped closer. "Rule number one: newbies buy the boys a round. You keep us happy, you keep Penelope happy, and maybe we let you hang. Got it?" He jerked his chin at his crew. The thugs, used to taking whatever they wanted, lunged forward to grab Lyra, smirking like creeps.

Over in the pink supercar, Silas was slouching, enjoying the drama. Suddenly, he sat bolt upright. His dark eyes went dead cold, narrowing into slits. He reached for the door handle, ready to jump out—

*Crack!*

A sickening thud echoed, followed by the wet snap of breaking bones. Nobody even saw her move. The next second, the thugs were flying backward, crashing hard into a pile of scrap tires. They curled up on the asphalt, groaning and clutching their ribs, completely wrecked. The whole thing took maybe four seconds.

Lyra hadn't even shifted her weight. She stood there, one hand casually shoved in her pocket, looking totally bored. She didn't even glance at the guys crying on the ground.

"That all you got?" She let out a cold, mocking laugh. "You wanna teach me the rules? Please. You're not even worth my time."
