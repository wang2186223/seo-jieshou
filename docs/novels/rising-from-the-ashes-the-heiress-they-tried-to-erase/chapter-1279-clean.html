As a teenager thrust into the spotlight, Anti remained delightfully susceptible to flattery, unlike seasoned veterans who might brush it aside with cool indifference.

What had Maia Watson declared earlier?

That Anti reigned as the world’s most acclaimed jewelry designer.

And she was absolutely right. At least the woman possessed discerning taste amid her audacity.

Pivoting once more toward Maia, Anti appraised her rival’s poise: sharp-tongued yet strategically yielding, never reduced to a mere doormat.

“Since Ms. Cooper has requested a thorough explanation,” Anti announced, her tone laced with regained confidence, “I’ll oblige. But before diving in…” She allowed a deliberate pause, letting anticipation build like a gathering storm. “It would serve us better to unveil every piece at once. I detect a unifying theme binding them, if I’m not mistaken.”

Turning squarely to Kiley, she concluded, “Does that suit you?”

Kiley’s responding smile radiated approval, warm yet calculated. “Of course,” she affirmed, extending a graceful hand in gesture.

In that instant, the four remaining boxes parted with synchronized elegance, revealing jewels that danced with inner fire under the piercing stage lights. The flashlights from cameras formed a continuous stream.

A symphony of gasps cascaded through the hall, raw awe etching every face.

“An earring, a brooch, a necklace, and a ring. Who could have anticipated a flawless complete set?” one spectator breathed in wonder.

“Anti’s reputation is well-earned; she pinpointed the cohesion from the start,” another murmured admiringly.

“These designs are breathtaking. I’m already hooked,” a third confessed with fervent passion.
