Chapter 602

Evan got right in Rhys’s face, baring his teeth with a nasty click. "Damn straight," he growled, his cold eyes suddenly burning with pure hate. "You stole my kid." His fangs slid down, sharp and ready. "So when Roric told me the humans needed lab rats, I personally put Sera at the top of the list." His face went dead calm. "An eye for an eye. A kid for a kid."

"She was carrying!" Rhys shrieked, raw agony in his voice.

"So was my boy's girl," Evan shot back. He lunged. Fast as hell. A spray of red painted the air as Rhys choked on a scream. Evan spat a bloody piece of meat right out of his mouth.

Rhys cocked his arm back and drove his wolf claws straight through Evan's ribcage. "If I'm going down..." he wheezed, "you're coming with me." He yanked hard, trying to free his arm, and finally ripped it loose, stumbling backward right into me. He went limp against my chest, but his fist was still gripping Evan's dripping heart. Evan stared at the gaping, bloody crater in his chest and actually laughed.

He glanced at Caspian. "Don't think you're off the hook just because we're croaking. Caspian's in deep with the humans. He knows it all." Then Evan looked at me—really looked at me—for the first time since he'd walked into my office. "Man, we really blew it with you." He coughed up a wet laugh, blood spilling over his chin. "The proof is in my safe at home. All of it." He shifted his dying gaze to Orion. "I screwed up. I regret it." He gasped for one last lungful of air, looking back at me. "Hey... you think she'll ever forgive me?" Before I could even open my mouth, both of them crashed to the floor, dead.

Finnian let out a dry snort. "Seriously? Dude's dying and he's asking if the Celestia is gonna let him slide?"

"Nope," I said, shaking my head. I looked my uncle dead in the eye. "He wasn't talking about the Celestia. He meant his mate."

"Huh?" Finnian frowned, totally lost. "But we weren't even talking about her. Did I blink and miss a page?" He looked around, confused.

My dad gave a dark chuckle. "Think about it. You betray the King, and because of your little coup, your only kid gets slaughtered in the crossfire. Wouldn't you be sweating bullets about what your dead wife is gonna say when you meet her on the other side?"

Finnian chewed on that for a second, then winced hard. "Damn. Astrid would murder me." He paused, rubbing his neck. "Like, all over again."

Dad just nodded. Then he turned his eyes on the last two guys standing. "Caspian, buddy... you and I need to have a little chat." Dad's grin was pure nightmare fuel.

Caspian snarled. "You can't touch us. Our guards are right outside." He smirked, trying to send a panic call through his mind-link.

Elder scrambled backward, panicking. "Guards!" he shrieked. I winced and clamped my hands over my ears. "Guards, get in here!"

I looked at Orion, totally judging him. "Seriously? You actually listened to these clowns?" Orion just let out a self-deprecating snort.

"Hey, they sounded a lot smarter when I was a kid," he muttered, trying to defend himself. I couldn't help but laugh.

"Yeah, right." I turned back to Elder. "Hey, dumbass. The room is locked down. Soundproofed. Remember? Nothing gets in, nothing gets out." I shook my head at him. Caspian was still staring at the door like a dog waiting for its owner. "Keep staring, pal. Nobody's coming."

Caspian whipped his head around to glare at me. "What the hell are you talking about?"

I smirked. "Didn't you think it was weird that I was totally cool with locking us in here?" I tilted my head, mocking him. "Or why your buddies haven't buzzed your brain to tell you how the big slaughter is going?"

Caspian froze. "We... we told them to keep radio silence unless it was an emergency."

I nodded slowly. "Sure, but this long? Come on." I raised an eyebrow. "Or maybe, just maybe, your little plan went completely to shit?"

Elder lost it. "Guards!" he screamed again, backing up until his spine hit the wall. He slid down toward the door, frantically rattling the handle.

"Save your breath. That's not gonna work either," I said, casually scratching behind my ear.

"What did you do?" Elder kept twisting the knob like a madman. "Turn off the damn jammer! Now!"

Dad looked at me, amused. "Jammer? Soundproofing?"

I grinned, strolled over to the desk, and yanked open the top drawer. I pulled out a cheap, fake plastic remote. "You mean this piece of junk?" Elder and Caspian went stiff as boards.

"But... we saw you press the buttons!" Caspian stammered, shaking his head in denial.

"Yeah, you saw me play with a toy," I said, nodding. "But you completely missed the spell I whispered under my breath." I flashed them a blinding grin. "Did you seriously think Astrid and the twins were the only ones in this family packing magic?" I laughed in their faces. "Oh, please. You guys are way too old to be this damn stupid." I flicked my wrist, and a glowing ball of light popped into my hand.

"You're... you're a witch," Caspian whispered, his face turning white as a sheet.

"And so is he," I said, jerking my chin toward my dad. The two idiots choked on their own spit. Dad conjured his own ball of glowing light, letting it float over his palm. "Did you really think he was just lucky? That he didn't have his own shield?"

Caspian and Elder exchanged a terrified look. Elder swallowed hard. "What about... our men?" I waved my hand, dropping the magic barrier and opening up the mind-link. Instantly, a wave of agonizing screams blasted through the link, echoing in the room. Both of them dropped to their knees, faces twisted in horror. "No... oh god, no."

I nodded. "Yep. Your little plan to slaughter us and rip our wolves away? Dead in the water." I grinned like a shark. "Orion's heavy hitters and our pack just wiped the floor with your guards."

"And we got our toy back," Orion added, pulling the spear out from behind him with a smug grin. He looked at my dad. "Thanks for keeping it safe, old man."

Dad waved a hand dismissively. "Piece of cake." He stood up, stretching. "But before you start poking holes in these two, we need some damn answers first."
