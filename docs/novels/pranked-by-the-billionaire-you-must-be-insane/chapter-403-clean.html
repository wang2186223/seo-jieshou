Chapter 406

"A fake? You think I'm wearing knockoffs?" Beverly's face twisted. She stabbed a finger at her own chest and let out a harsh, mocking laugh. "Are you freaking kidding me, Wallace? You lost the company because you're a loser! I had to literally beg my parents for cash just so we could get into this Blackwood debutante party!" She stepped closer, glaring. "If it wasn't for me, you wouldn't even have a suit to wear tonight!"

She was practically shaking now. "I spent every single dime I scraped together to get Genevieve dressed. Where was I supposed to get cash for real diamonds? And now you're blaming me? If you're so damn successful, why don't you buy me the real thing?"

Her voice spiked, drawing stares from the nearby guests.

Benedict rubbed his temples. His head was pounding. "Mom, Dad, shut up! Seriously, this is humiliating. You guys are making a massive scene. Do you want everyone laughing at us?"

Beverly snapped her mouth shut. She shot Wallace a murderous look, her chest heaving.

With a sharp yank, she ripped the necklace off, throwing it into her clutch. "Where the hell is Genevieve? She's been in the bathroom forever. She's our only ticket out of this mess..."

She paused, looking around. "Wait, has anyone spotted the Blackwood heir yet? We need to get her in front of him—"

The words died in her throat. Her jaw dropped.

Genevieve was slipping through a side door. Her head was down, her steps shaky. She was clutching her chest, looking completely panicked.

The designer gown they’d ruined their bank account for was a total wreck. It was wrinkled, messy, and the hem was covered in some weird stains.

But that wasn't the worst part.

The strapless bodice was completely loose, slipping down. She was desperately holding the fabric up to keep her chest from spilling out in front of everyone.

"Genevieve!"

Beverly gasped, her heart dropping. "Oh my god, what happened? Look at you!"

That dress had cost her entire life savings. Seriously, Beverly wanted to cry over the ruined fabric. Back in the day, she wouldn't have cared about a price tag like that. But now? She was literally wearing fake jewelry. Every penny they had went into this dress and Genevieve's look. It was a total disaster.

She was so obsessed with the ruined dress that she didn't even notice Genevieve's face. Underneath the thick foundation, it was totally puffed up.

But Wallace saw it.

Under the bright chandelier lights, her face looked lumpy, bruised, and completely deformed.

Wallace’s stomach sank.

He needed Genevieve's pretty face to bail out the Wynn family. Looking like a beaten-up mess, she wasn't going to seduce anyone tonight.

He flashed back to Lyra walking in earlier. She looked like a literal queen—stunning, elegant, totally out of their league. Seeing Genevieve like this made his hopes instantly vanish.

If only Lyra were the one standing next to him right now.

With Lyra's face and her high-society connections, the Wynn family wouldn't be standing here looking like a bunch of absolute clowns.

Genevieve was already on the verge of a breakdown. Seeing the disappointment in her dad's eyes broke her. She knew exactly what he was thinking.

Tears ruined her makeup as she sobbed, "It... it was Lyra! She did this! She set me up, lured me away, and had someone trash me! That's why... that's why I look like this!"

Beverly lost it. Her face contorted with rage. "I knew that little bitch was plotting something! How dare she touch my daughter!"

Wallace frowned, his eyes narrowing at Genevieve. "Seriously? She already slapped you in front of everyone at the entrance. Why would she bother sneaking around to jump you in private?"

Genevieve stared at him, stunned. Her own father was taking Lyra's side. The tears flowed faster. "Dad, are you calling me a liar?"

"Wallace!" Beverly snapped, pulling Genevieve into a tight hug. "What is wrong with you? That trashy brat has always been a sneaky snake. She’s always hated Genevieve. Why else would she target her face earlier? She's just jealous because Genevieve is gorgeous and she's terrified Genevieve will snag a billionaire tonight and leave her in the dust!"

Wallace wanted to laugh. *Jealous of what?* If anyone was jealous, it was Genevieve.

But looking at his wife's hysterical face, he didn't have the energy to argue. He just scowled. "I told you guys. Play nice. Do not push her buttons, and do not start a fight!"

All he wanted was to keep a low profile and find a lifeline for Wynn Group. Every single time they messed with Lyra, they lost. He couldn't risk getting thrown out of the Blackwood gala. This room was packed with the elite. If they got kicked out, the Wynn name was dead. They'd never recover.

Genevieve couldn't believe her dad was blaming her instead of defending her. She sobbed harder, completely devastated.

Beverly's heart broke. She glared at Wallace, spitting venom. "Are you out of your mind, Wallace? Your daughter gets assaulted, and you're defending some outsider? Whose side are you even on?"

Wallace's head was spinning from the screeching. He was totally done.

Beverly gave him one last dirty look, then grabbed Genevieve's arm, pulling her toward the restrooms. "Come on. Let's get you cleaned up. Don't worry, baby, I've got your back. You're my daughter. You'll always be a million times better than that ghetto-raised little brat."
