Chapter 203

Juniper flops down. "Good."

I blink. "Good?"

She nods. "Thought you'd say yes. Had me sweating."

I snort. "Why's that?"

"Can't stand her. Never could. Thought you guys were tight when we rolled up. Then I saw your face—you didn't even know her. Freaked me out." She picks at her nails. "My wolf's got a bad vibe about her."

I chuckle. "Same. Mine hates her guts."

I sink into the new couch. A deep, happy purr rumbles in my chest. *Oh, hell no.* I shake my head, snapping out of it, and scramble up before I make a total fool of myself.

Juniper groans, pushing herself up. "Any more deliveries today?"

We scan the room. Just the new couch and loveseat. Super plush, light grey. The second I saw them with Flint, I was sold. Had to have them.

I shake my head. "Reading chair's backordered." Just thinking about that oversized, ultra-soft chair makes me smile. Umbra would go crazy purring in that thing. Picture this: me, curled up with a book, while Orion cooks dinner.

*Purrrrr.*

I freeze. *What the actual fuck?*

Orion isn't mine. Not today. Maybe never.

I gotta stop. This is just self-torture. Tears sting my eyes. I look up.

Juniper's right there. "Hey... you good?"

I shake my head. "I completely ruined everything." First time I've admitted the truth out loud since the whole Orion disaster.

"Spill," she says.

I drag her back to the couch. Time to dump it all. "I'm laying it all out. But you keep your mouth shut." I squeeze my fists tight. "I trust you. My wolves do too."

I look up. Her jaw hits the floor. "Wolves? Plural? Like, more than one?"

"I've got secrets. Big ones." She nods, listening. "Swear to me. Not a word to anyone. I haven't even told my bestie, but I need backup here, and you're the only one I trust."

Her face goes through a million emotions. Hard to tell on her dark skin, but she goes a bit pale. "Fine. I, Kira Stone of the Malawi pack, swear to keep your secrets locked down. No one hears a peep, not even if I'm tortured or ordered to speak, until you let me off the hook."

I let the oath settle, then nod. "Thanks." I wave off her hand with a smile.

"You're my person here, Lyra. Back home is back home, but here, we're locked in."

"Same here," I smile, then shake my head. "Look, I don't know what history your dad told you, but I gotta break this down." She leans back, totally locked in. "So, the very first werewolf? Born in the Agora. This witch's sacred land. Her only son got mauled by wild wolves. Their alpha had just been taken out by humans, so they went crazy on the first kid they saw. The witch found him dying. She begged her three-faced goddess to save him. But nope, the Moon Celestia stepped in. Brought him back, but different. Upgraded. He had the wolf spark, the power. Passed it to his kid, and so on, all the way down to me. I'm the direct bloodline of the Moon's first wolf and a witch."

Juniper's eyes go wide as saucers. "Holy shit. You're royalty? The first family?"

I nod, looking away. "Lycans are different, though. Straight-up descendants of the Moon Celestia. You knew that, right?"

Juniper nods. "Yeah, but it's weird because Lycans only bred with Lycans."

"Actually, there were two lines," Juniper cuts in.

I blink. "Huh?"

"Other gods saw the Moon goddess's cheat code and wanted in. They made their own Lycan and wolf lines to avoid inbreeding." Juniper spins a ring on her finger. Didn't notice that before. "Ma'at, the Egyptian sky goddess, asked first. Others followed." She looks at me. "That's my bloodline. The OG Egyptian wolves. But every line knows about the first family." She grabs my hands. "But why are we talking about Lycans?"

"Because I am one." I blow out a breath. "I'm Lycan, wolf, and witch. All packed into this tiny body."

She just stares. Total silence.

I nudge her arm. "Yo, say something."
