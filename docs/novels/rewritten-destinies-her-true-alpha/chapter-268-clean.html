Chapter 268

Those words hit me like a punch to the gut.

"Wait, what? Say that again," I gasped, staring at the anchor.

Her eyes went wide—she actually thought I already knew. Instantly, the whole pack of reporters smelled blood. They swarmed me like freaking sharks, everyone screaming at once.

"Your dad is officially a dead traitor! How's that feel?"

"How did you miss him teaming up with the rogues?"

"Are you in on the conspiracy too?"

"Did you even look at the news today?"

Suddenly, a ruckus broke out. A tiny female werewolf shoved her way to the front, a sharp, hungry look in her eyes.

"Alpha Lyra," she barked. She was the only one showing some damn respect by using my title. I locked eyes with her. "Look, today's headlines call your father a traitor. But my own digging turned up files proving he was set up as a scapegoat."

The whole crowd went dead silent.

"So, what's the real story behind your dad's legacy?"

Every single camera swung back to me. A tear threatened to spill, but I blinked it back, leaned in close to her mic, and forced a smile.

"Thanks for actually doing your job and finding the truth," I said. I took a massive breath as someone squeezed my hand. "My dad, Rhys Blackwood, was the most loyal man alive. Period. He stood by this kingdom and the king until the day he died. He poured billions into this place so our kids could have a future. Hell, right before he passed, he set up a trust fund for this exact school. He paid for your kids' tuition every single year during the lockdown, like clockwork. He kept us safe and fed so we could focus on our grades. He was a great man. A great dad. And a badass Alpha."

Juniper grabbed the mic, and the crowd shifted.

"Alpha Caspian was the most generous wolf out there," she yelled. "He sent his own fighters to protect small packs when the rogues hunted them. He saved my family. Traitors don't do that. Rhys Blackwood was loyal to the bone. Whoever is dragging a dead man's name through the mud is just trying to hide the real truth."

She glared at the cameras. "Do some real digging, like she did." She nodded at the reporter who just spoke, who smiled back. "Now, we're graduating today. Cut the bullshit. Get the hell out of our way. Now."

She flared her Alpha aura. The reporters scrambled, and we pushed past them inside.

But the whispers inside were everywhere. Everyone was muttering about my family being traitors. My blood started to boil.

"I'm going to murder them," Luna hissed. Sweet, quiet Luna.

I spun around. She had this crazy, feral look. "Whoa, what's wrong?"

"What's wrong? Are you kidding me?" she snapped. "They're talking shit about your dad like it's fact! It's driving me crazy!" She was shaking with rage. I knew what she was really thinking—*we know he's alive and working with the king.* But we couldn't say that out loud.

I pulled her in and smiled. Umbra pushed her way to the front of my mind, like she always does when things get too heated.

"Easy, little wolf. Let me take that anger," Umbra said. She and Terra used their magic to siphon the rage right out of Luna's wolf. It stopped her from shifting on the spot.

"You can't go wild today and rip your dress," I whispered.

Luna looked at me, tears welling up. "They can't get away with this," she whispered, sounding heartbroken now.

"It's fine. We'll handle it later. Today, we graduate," I said, forcing a smile. "You've got to control your new rank and the temper that comes with it. Otherwise, you'll end up like your first shift—totally out of control."

She nodded, taking a deep breath. "Okay. I'm good." She smiled, and we turned back to the crowd.

"Great. Let's go sit down."

We pushed through the packed room. Terra and Umbra did their best to keep me calm while the whispers buzzed around us.

Damon looked like he was loving every second of it. Every time someone whispered, he puffed his chest out and smirked at me. He thought this scandal would force me to say yes to his proposal.

I was trapped, and he knew it.
