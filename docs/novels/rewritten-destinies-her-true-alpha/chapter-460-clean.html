The line went dead quiet. Then, a heavy sigh. "Fine. But your ass better be on a plane here tonight."

"I'm on my way, swear to God," I groaned.

"Wait, what's wrong?" Her anger flipped instantly to pure worry.

"I gotta call the King." My stomach did a nasty flip.

"Oh, honey." She snorted, trying to hide a laugh. "Relax. You sound like you're heading to the electric chair."

I rubbed my eyes, pinching the bridge of my nose. "Uh, because I am? Did you forget what happened the other night?"

"I remember. And you'll survive." She sighed. "Love you. Dial the number. See Magnus. Finish your shit and get home."

"On it. Love you too." I hung up. I stared at my call log, winced, and spotted Gideon Stone's name. Perfect distraction. I tapped call.

"Hey... you good?" He panted, sounding stressed.

"Yeah. What about you? Why are you huffing?"

"Yeah." He exhaled hard. "System alert. Some asshole is hacking us. I'm putting my boots on to head back to the office."

"Daddy, please stay." A soft cough in the background—Lyra Bloom. My chest squeezed.

"I got this. Stay put."

He snorted. "Dude, you're literally in a hospital bed."

I wedged the phone between my ear and shoulder. "I'm fine. They're wrapping me up right now. I'll squash the hack, then head over. Plenty of time."

He hesitated. "Fine. Kill the hack, clear whatever tickets you can, and dump the rest in my inbox. You still hitting the road tonight?"

I smirked. "Right after dinner. Just gotta tie up some loose ends first. How long do I have before the system goes up in flames?"

A beat. "Maybe an hour or two. But seriously, the sooner, the better."

"Got it." I shoved my laptop into my bag, a plan clicking into place. "Gotta grab dinner supplies first. If I do work now, I'll run out of time."

"Alright, just text me when you're done."

"You got it, boss. Oh, hey—can you drag Elara Meadow and Rhys Thorne along? They're coming over to do laundry anyway."

He chuckled. "Sure." Then he sighed. "Gonna miss you, kid."

I laughed. "I know." I clicked off. The nurse walked in. She'd been buzzing around, wiping up blood, clearing trays, and mopping the floor while I was on the phone.

"Ready?" She eyed my bloody chest, squinting. "We had to shred your bra. Doc handed me this shirt for you. Figured you'd want a woman doing this part."

I nodded. Smart guy. "Works for me." I waved her over, then winced. "Wait, I thought he was wrapping my chest?"

She nodded. "I'll clean the blood, and I snagged a compression wrap. He'll come back once you're decent." She cleared her throat, grinning. "Actually, he's banned from looking at you bare-skinned. Some guy called the front desk a minute ago and laid down the law."

I groaned. "Oh, Jesus." She giggled. "Mind if I make a call while you wrap me?"

She smirked. "Go for it. After the earful Rhys got, I figured you would."

I groaned again. "Unbelievable." I grabbed my phone, tapped Orion's name, and hit speaker.

He picked up. Dead calm. Way too calm. "Hey, Lyra."

"My King, you're on speaker, and I've got company," I blurted before he could growl. "I guess you heard I got jumped?"

A dark, low chuckle rumbled through the speaker. "Oh, you guessed right."

"Right. Well, since you're up to speed, we can skip the recap." I tried to pivot, but he let out a low warning growl.

"No."

"No?" I blinked.

"No. We aren't brushing this off. Tell me what happened. Now."

My jaw hit the floor. The nurse snorted. I glared at her, but she just shrugged. "Hey, the man wants details."

"See? Who's that?" Orion demanded.

"Iris. I'm Lyra's nurse."

"See, Lyra? Jenn gets it. Spill."

I rolled my eyes and blew out a breath. "Fine. Damon was screwing Blair behind my back. She went and told the whole pack that our mating ceremony next week was actually *theirs*. I told him to pick a side. Last night, they both showed up at my place, and he chose her right in front of everyone. Then today, he walks into my office acting like everything's totally normal. I literally just asked him if he was brain-damaged. Gideon, my boss, cracked up. Next thing I know, Damon leaps over the desk and tackles me."
