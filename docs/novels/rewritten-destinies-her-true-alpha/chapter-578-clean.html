Chapter 578

I gave a quick nod, throwing my hand up. "Hold up."

I scanned the waiting wolves. The air reeked of pure terror and misery. They were already mourning my old man. I tapped into the pack link.

*Silvermoon. Listen up.*

Instantly, they froze. The link went dead silent. Orion gave me a nod.

"I'm gonna try and pull those guys out of the cave," I announced. "But I need muscle. If you're hurt or patching someone up, stay put. Everyone else? Get your asses to the cave. Now."

Orion nudged me. "Lyra? What the hell are you planning?"

I stared at my mate, letting him see the sheer panic in my eyes. "I don't know how much I can pull off. But if I can shift enough rock, we've got a shot. I can't do this solo, though. If I pass out... you guys have to dig them out. But we gotta move, like, yesterday."

Orion glanced at the cave. "But the ceiling's gonna collapse."

I nodded once. "I'll hold it. It'll be the last thing I let go." I didn't mention the real catch—this stunt would completely drain me, and probably him too. "Finn!" I yelled.

The Omega scrambled over. "Yes, Alpha." He was shivering.

I felt a pang of guilt. "Hey, I'm not gonna bite your head off. I need you right here." I pointed to the spot next to Orion.

"Whatever you need." Finn did a quick, shaky bow. I grabbed his collar and yanked him straight.

Orion scowled, eyeing the Omega. "What do you need *him* for?" He sounded hurt, like he thought he wasn't enough for me.

"He's our backup plan." I turned to the rockpile. "When I start, Finn, you watch us. If Orion starts shaking, turns pale, or drops..." I looked both of them dead in the eye. "You drag him away from me. Got it?"

Orion grabbed my arm, growling. "Lyra—"

I slapped my hand up, cutting him off. Finn swallowed hard. I grabbed his shoulder. "Can you do that for me?"

Finn nodded fast. "Yeah. Yeah, I got you."

Orion growled again, making Finn flinch.

"Orion, cut it out." I glared at him. "I know you'd give me every drop of your energy, but I'm not letting you die." I squeezed his hand. "I can't control the drain when I'm holding up a ceiling, moving boulders, and scanning for survivors. I need you alive. And I need someone to make sure you stay that way."

He stared at me for a beat, then finally nodded. "Fine."

"Thanks."

Finn leaned in, whispering, "Wait, what drain? Sucking what now?"

We both snapped our heads toward him. Finn raised his hands, backing up. "Sorry, sorry! None of my business."

I sighed, puffing my cheeks out. "No, actually, you're right. I've been hiding this for so long it feels weird to say it out loud." I took a deep breath. "I have magic."

Finn just nodded, waiting. I looked at Orion, totally confused. Finn looked back and forth between us.

Orion cleared his throat. "Like, actual magic, Finn."

Finn kept nodding. "And? What else?" He looked at us and shrugged with a tiny smile. "Your dad... yeah, he wasn't as sneaky as he thought. The inner circle... we've known for ages." He looked at me. "I figured you got his spark. That's how you saved us from Morgana, right?" He tilted his head. I just nodded. He gave a confident, warm smile. "So, what's the play? Just tell me what to do."

My jaw literally dropped. "Uh... I'm gonna use my magic to hold the ceiling up and blast those rocks out of the way."

He nodded. "Got it." He looked at the cave. "So when you run out of juice, you're gonna leech off the King. And you need me to pull the plug if it gets bad." I nodded. "Okay." He turned to Orion. "I won't let either of you die." He said it quiet, but he meant every word.

I patted his shoulder and turned to the crowd gathering around us. "Silvermoon, eyes on me!" I yelled, raising my hands to get their attention. "Our people are buried in there. I'm gonna do everything I damn well can to get them out. But I need your help."

The crowd roared. I waved them down. "When I move those rocks, be ready. The second you see someone, run in and grab them." Everyone nodded. "If I mess up, if I drop, don't just stand there. Run in and dig like hell. You'll have a tiny window to get them out."

Another wave of nods rippled through the pack. I turned back to the cave. "Alright. Let's do this."
