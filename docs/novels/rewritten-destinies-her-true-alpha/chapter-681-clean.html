Morgana cackled. "He didn't save shit."

She jerked back, trying to break away, but I didn't even look at her. I just squeezed her neck tighter. My eyes were locked on Orion. God, my stomach did a freefall. His chest barely moved. His breath was dragging. And my shirt? Soaked. I didn't want to think about what that wetness was, but I knew. It was his blood, warm and sticky all over me.

I whipped my head toward Morgana. That smug smirk on her face made a growl rip from my throat.

"That spell I just locked in?" I glared right into her eyes. "The one you went crazy trying to block? Yeah, it's done. It's shielding every single pack in the country, not just this one. Humans can't touch us. They can't even see us. You lost, bitch." I roared, wrapping my hand tighter around her windpipe, cutting off her air. "You fucking lost!"

She gasped for air, clawing at my fingers, but then she actually laughed. Pure, unhinged madness flared in her eyes.

"Sure, you saved the packs. But your mate? He's fucking dead." She slapped his back. Orion didn't even flinch. Nothing. "So yeah, I'd say I didn't completely fail," she sneered.

That was it. I snapped.

A spell flashed in my brain. Dark, nasty magic from the deepest, ugliest corner of my mind. Something I swore I'd never use. Inside me, both my wolves were screaming, howling at the moon for our dying mate.

*Do it,* Umbra growled, her voice cracked and raw. *She deserves this shit.*

Terra agreed. *Make her suffer. Every fucking second. And even then, it won't be enough.*

"Cort a dev kuva," I spat, grinding my teeth.

Morgana’s eyes went wide. She recognized the words.

"No," she whimpered, shaking her head. "You can't do this." She scratched at my hand, nails digging into my skin. I didn't care. I let my claws slide out and shoved them straight into her neck. She shrieked. I smiled.

"Kintashen!" I screamed the final word. I took every ounce of power Orion had poured into me and shoved it straight into the spell. "May the goddess Poppy show your wolf's soul more mercy than I'm about to show yours."

Morgana’s eyes popped. Her skin started to ripple. Then her body began to swell, bloating up as every single molecule inside her started to explode. The spell was pure, unadulterated torture. Every atom in her body grew, expanding with the raw power I was pumping into her. Her skin bubbled with energy and blood.

Then came the screams.

She ballooned like a sponge soaking up water, but it was her cells drinking in my deadly magic. It was brutal. Atoms swelled to the size of sand, then seeds. She was literally ripping apart at the molecular level, and there was no off switch. The spell kept her alive, forcing her to feel her body tear itself to shreds from the inside out. Finally, her skin popped. Blood sprayed across the sky, coating everything. To me, it took less than a minute. To her soul, trapped inside that agonizing loop, it felt like actual centuries.

Morgana was nothing but a red mist now, but her screams still echoed in the clearing.

Orion collapsed. I went down with him, my hands shaking as I hovered over his body. I was panicking. I didn't know what to do, how to fix this.

"Celestia, please, help me," I begged, staring at my mate. "You're gonna make it." I wiped the blood splatters off his face and laid him down gently. I cupped his cheeks, trying to hold him without hurting him. "You're gonna be fine. We're gonna heal you, and then we're gonna raise our boy." I kept whispering it. Footsteps pounded toward us. Someone let out a choked sob nearby.

But Orion just smiled. He weakly lifted his hand to my belly, and I pressed my hand over his, holding it against our baby.

"Take care of your mom, pup," he wheezed, coughing up a fresh mouthful of blood that ran down his cheek. "She's gonna need your strength." He looked up at me. "I'm sorry I won't get to see him grow up."

I sobbed, shaking my head. "No. Stop. You're gonna be fine. He needs his dad." I grabbed his face. His eyes started to drift, looking toward Shadow's. "Please," I begged him, begged the gods, begged anyone. "You can't die on me." My lips shook. I leaned down, pressing my face against his. "We're supposed to grow old together."

Someone dropped to the dirt next to us. I looked up through my tears and saw my mom. "Mom, please. Help us."

She just stared, her eyes full of pure heartbreak. "I can't." She reached out to touch me, but I flinched away and screamed.

"Dad!" I turned to my father. "Please! Help me save him!" Tears were pouring down my face.

"Kiss me." Orion's voice dragged me back. He tried to reach for my face, but his hand just fell limp. I shook my head, crying hysterically. "Please." A sob choked me as I leaned down and kissed his lips. I put every bit of love I had into that kiss.

"Tell... me... you... love... me," he whispered, smiling as my tears dripped onto his cheeks. "I want that to be... the last thing I hear."

The light in his eyes was fading fast.

"I love you," I choked out.

And then, the spark went out. His soul slipped away. The bond between us snapped.

"Orion!" I shook him. Nothing. His eyes were wide open, staring at me, but they were empty. "Orion, please!"

Hands grabbed me, trying to pull me back, but I thrashed and fought them off. I screamed. I let out all my agony, all my ripped-away love, in one final, shattering roar.

"ROWAN!"
