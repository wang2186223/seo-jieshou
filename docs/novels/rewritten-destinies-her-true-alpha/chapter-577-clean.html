Chapter 577

The smell of blood made my magic gag. But I kept moving. I stepped right over the red puddle, and then—bam. Her words finally hit me. I stopped dead. "Wait. My dad saved Finnian?" I stared. Her face was white as a sheet.

"Don't hate him, please!" She grabbed for my hand, desperate. "If you blame him, it'll kill him. He's already eating himself alive over this."

"Astrid, hey." I grabbed her hands. Hard. "Breathe, okay? Just breathe." I threw my gamma aura at her, forcing her system to chill. The last thing we needed was her dropping a baby right now. One birth tonight was already a shitshow. "Listen to me. I don't blame him. Not for a second. That's my dad. I know exactly what he did. The ceiling started coming down, and he shoved Finnian toward the door."

Astrid nodded, a tear slipping down her cheek. "Yeah. He grabbed Finnian and threw him clear. But the cave-in was too fast. It caught them anyway." She gasped, locking up as another contraction hit. She groaned, hands flying to her stomach.

Orion sprinted back, looking between us like a tennis match. "What's going on? You guys good?"

Astrid exhaled, forcing herself upright. "Just freaking fantastic." She took a deep breath. "There were more bombs. We missed them. The whole damn ceiling collapsed." She shut her eyes tight. "It took us an hour of digging just to pull Finnian out." She pointed to the rubble. "The rocks went all the way to there. We dragged twenty-five wolves out of the shallow end." She looked at me, eyes hollow. "They were right at the exit. Then the roof fell."

"How many are still under there?"

She shook her head. "No clue who died in the fight and who's trapped. But we're short fifteen wolves. The rest are out hunting."

Great. Fifteen bodies to find. "Orion, get her to her mate. Now." I turned my back and faced the rock pile.

"Lyra." Orion stepped up behind me, pressing a quick kiss to my shoulder. "Do not do anything crazy until I get back. Seriously." I could feel the tension radiating off him.

I looked back, giving him a smirk. "No promises, babe." I kissed him quick. "Hurry." He nodded, scooped Astrid up in his arms, and muttered, "Stay alive." Then he bolted.

I faced the rubble again. I let my magic loose, flooding it straight into the rocks. I focused hard, squeezing my awareness into the tiny gaps between the loose stones. Suddenly, something shifted down there. A few rocks rolled. Then, a psychic jolt hit me—a desperate cry for help as the weight crushed down harder on some poor wolf.

*Please... anyone...* The whisper buzzed in my head. My ears heard nothing, but my magic picked up the signal loud and clear. *I can't breathe.* I didn't know who it was, but the clock was ticking. If I wanted to save anyone—especially my dad—I had to move my ass.

I started pulling my magic back to lift the debris, but a sharp scream made me spin around. Crap. A massive slide of rocks came cascading down like a waterfall right at the entrance, pushing the pile a foot closer. An Omega lay there, panting on the ground, a huge boulder resting right next to him.

He looked up at me, terrified. "I'm sorry, Alpha Lyra! I was just trying to help, I swear!" He kept glancing between me and the rocks, practically sweating pure panic and grief.

I forced a calm smile. "Finn, hey. It's fine. Take a breath." I gestured to another wolf, who rushed over to pull him up. "Everyone, back up. Give me some space." They all nodded and scrambled back.

Inside my head, Umbra and Terra were pacing. *We need to move, now,* Umbra growled. *Or they're all dead.*

Terra sniffed the air. *The pile is totally unstable. And the cave ceiling is about to drop again.*

I swallowed hard. *So what's the plan?*

Umbra pointed toward the clearing. *Channel everything. Put it all into your magic.*

Terra agreed. *Hold the ceiling up so it doesn't collapse again, then yank the rocks out.*

*But we have to be lightning fast,* I thought, pacing back and forth. My wolves nodded. "I don't know if I have that kind of juice," I muttered.

"You can drain me, right?" Orion's voice made me jump.

I whipped around. "What?"

"My power," he said, stepping closer. "I don't have magic, but we have the bond. You can siphon it from me, can't you?"

I looked from him to the rubble. "I... I don't know."

"Only one way to find out." He smiled, stepping right up to my back. "Is there a spell for it?"

I flipped through the mental spellbook in my head. There it was—the siphon spell, plus the telekinesis for the rocks. I nodded. "Yeah. We can do this."

"Tell me what to do."

I swallowed the lump in my throat. "If I take too much, you have to break the bridge."

He wrapped his arms around me from behind. "The bridge? What does that mean?"

"Keep skin-to-skin contact. That's the bridge. If you start feeling sick or weak, let go immediately. Otherwise..."

"Otherwise what?" his voice whispered in my ear.

I turned my head to look at my mate. "I'll suck you dry and kill you."
