Chapter 801

"Uncle Silas, look, I want to trust Lyra’s crowd. I really do," Vivienne said, biting her lip like she was totally torn up inside. "But, I mean, she grew up in the middle of nowhere. The people she hung out with? Shady as hell. Sure, she’s back with us now, living the good life, but you can't just wash off that street dirt overnight."

She squeezed her eyes shut, looking stressed. "I hate to say it, but everyone else here is legit. Her friends? They’re the sore thumbs. Shouldn't they be the first ones we grill?"

She spun toward the royal guest. "Princess Elara, that Heart of the Sea necklace is worth a fortune. If it’s gone, our family is dead to the House of Vaelion."

That hit a nerve. The Vintons went stiff.

"Vivienne, shut your mouth," Adeline barked. Her eyes burned with pure disgust.

She saw the game. Vivienne was throwing meat to the wolves, trying to get the crowd to rip Lyra's friends apart.

Eleanor figured grounding the girl for a week would’ve knocked some sense into her. The only reason she let Vivienne show up tonight was to kill the gossip about Cora Blackwood getting busted. She was supposed to stand there, shut up, and look cute. Not this.

But here she was, starting a fire at the worst possible time. Was she stupid? If the Vinton ship sank, she went down with it. Did she honestly think she could throw Lyra's friends to the sharks, burn the family name, and somehow walk away clean?

That necklace wasn't just jewelry; it was a royal treasure. If it was gone for good... Eleanor’s stomach turned. The kid was brainless. The disappointment tasted like ash.

Vivienne didn't care. She was riding a high, loving every second of putting Lyra’s trashy friends under the spotlight. Around the room, the rich folks started muttering, throwing dirty looks at Lyra.

Grandma’s anger? Whatever. Vivienne just had to get through tonight. Once the dust settled, she’d play nice, and her grandparents would go back to spoiling her like always.

Time to play victim. Vivienne squeezed out a couple of fake tears, looking like a kicked puppy. "Grandma, Grandpa, I’m sorry, I didn't mean it that way... but a royal treasure is gone. We can't just sit here."

She stepped closer to the princess. "Your Highness, let's end this quick so we can get back to the party. Let's do a pat-down."

She threw her hands up. "Search me first! And, well... I hope everyone else plays along."

She glared right at Phoenix and her gang. Then she stood there, arms wide, acting like a saint waiting for the guards to frisk her.

Phoenix was busy stuffing her face with cake. But when she saw Vivienne’s smug face staring her down, she lost it.

*Clack.* She slammed the plate onto the table.

"Alright, you psycho, I’m done with your bullshit!"

Phoenix marched over, her stilettos clicking hard against the floor, and got right in Vivienne’s face. "You’ve been talking trash since the second I walked through that door. You’re just trying to drag me so you can make my girl Lyra look bad, right? I played nice because it's her night, but you seriously don't know when to shut the hell up."

Phoenix leaned in, looking ready to tear her apart. "Yeah, I’m from the streets. But I don't steal cheap shit. You got a problem with me? Say it to my face. Stop playing these passive-aggressive mind games, you fake bitch!"
