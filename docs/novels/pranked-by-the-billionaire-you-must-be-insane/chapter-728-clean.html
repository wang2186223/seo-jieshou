Chapter 735

"Arthur! Oh my god, Arthur!" Eleanor shrieked, totally losing it.

Nurses shoved the hospital bed out of the room, wheels screeching against the floor.

Penelope caught a glimpse of her granddad. The old man was thrashing on the mattress, his face a nasty shade of purple. He was shaking violently, white foam bubbling right out of his mouth.

Beside him, the heart monitor went nuts, beeping a frantic, high-pitched alarm.

Penelope knew this was coming, but seeing it? It scared the crap out of her.

It was way too fast.

Augus said the drug would take an hour to kick in.

It hadn't even been ten minutes.

Whatever. No turning back now. The wheels were turning.

Lyra was going down for this. Period.

The code team wheeled her granddad toward the ER. Penelope scanned the messy hallway.

Her eyes locked right onto Lyra, who had just run up. Lyra was already staring at her with those freezing, calculating eyes.

Penelope’s chest hammered. For a split second, her face twitched with pure panic.

But she snapped out of it fast. She threw her chin up, meeting Lyra’s gaze. "I told you, Lyra! I freaking warned you! Your little amateur act is nothing compared to real doctors. But no, you had to play god with your stupid herbal teas and needles! Now look. Look what you did to him!"

Silas bolted in right behind her. He grabbed Eleanor just as her knees buckled, stopping her from running after the stretcher. "What the hell is going on?!"

"It’s Lyra!" Penelope pointed a finger straight at her. "She messed with Grandpa’s meds! Fed him some weird-ass tonic and poked him with needles! She just wanted the family stock, she wanted to be the hero, and now she freaking killed him!"

Silas whipped his head around, his eyes drilling into Penelope. "Why the hell are you even here?"

He’d locked her up at the estate to teach her a lesson. How did she slip out?

The sheer rage in his eyes made Penelope flinch. She took a step back. "Grandma... Grandma missed me. She felt bad, okay? She let me out."

She swallowed hard, forced her spine straight, and pointed again. "But don't look at me! Look at her! You brought a damn snake into this family, and now Grandpa’s paying for it!"

"Penelope."

Lyra’s voice was dead. Completely empty. "Why are you so eager to pin this on me?"

That lifeless stare sent a cold shiver straight down Penelope’s spine. "Who... who else could it be?"

"I don't have time for your crap right now," Lyra said. She glanced toward the ER, then turned on her heel and walked straight into the private suite.

Penelope’s heart did another flip.

Why wasn't the bitch running to the ER? Why go into the room?

No way. She couldn't know. There was no way she was that smart.

Augus promised the stuff was totally odorless and colorless. You couldn't trace it.

Penelope tried to breathe, trying to calm her racing pulse.

Then Lyra’s voice cut through the air from inside the room. "Silas. Get in here."

Silas gave Penelope a look of pure disgust, then rushed inside.

Penelope’s stomach dropped again. Shaking, she scrambled after him.

She reached the doorway just in time to hear Lyra’s order: "Silas, grab the humidifier. Get it to the lab right now."

Penelope’s eyes went wide.

She knew? She knew about the humidifier?

How? How the hell did she figure it out?

The drug was supposed to be invisible. What did she see?

The blood drained right out of Penelope’s face.

If Lyra knew about the humidifier... would she trace it back to her?

No. No way.

She knew the room’s layout. The security camera completely missed the corner where the humidifier sat.

And she’d already flushed the bottle down the toilet.

There was zero evidence. Lyra couldn't prove a damn thing.

She had to keep her cool.

"I’m going to the ER," Lyra said, brushing past her. "Arthur’s going into surgery."
