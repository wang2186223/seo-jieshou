Chapter 588

Elara was shaking like a leaf, completely wiped out. "Is he... is he alright?" she gasped, her eyelids heavy as hell, but she still stretched her arms out.

My mom shoved the baby boy into Daisy’s arms. "Clean him up," she ordered, pointing to the bathroom. Daisy bolted inside. Mom turned back to Elara. "She’ll be right back with him, but hey, we’re not done here, Elara." Mom waited for a quick nod, then ducked right back under the sheet. "We gotta wait a beat for the next one to get in position. Just yell when you feel that urge to push again, got it?" Elara let out a ragged breath and nodded. She was too dead-tired to even squeeze out a word.

"Hey, can you get in here?" Daisy’s voice cracked from the bathroom.

Mom yanked her hands back, stood up, and wiped them clean on the fly. She locked eyes with me. I gave her a sharp nod—*I got her, go.* Elara tried to hoist herself up, weak as a kitten. "What? What’s wrong with my boy?"

Mom slapped a hand on her knee. "I’m on it. You just focus on the little girl still inside you." Then she vanished into the bathroom. Next thing we knew, hushed, frantic whispering drifted out.

"Lyra..." Elara’s scent spiked with pure, raw terror. "My baby’s fine, right? Tell me he’s fine." She panted, desperate, but then another brutal contraction hit and she screamed bloody murder.

"You good?" I leaned in close, checking her face.

She squeezed her gut, forcing a painful grin. "Guess I don't get a break. This girl wants out *now*. She’s chasing her brother." She clamped her jaw, groaning as the pain ripped through her again.

Mom and Daisy walked back out. Both of them looked like they’d seen a ghost. Mom got right back into position. "Alright, sounds like you're ready. Let me check, then we push. Okay?" Elara just nodded, exhausted.

Daisy was staring at us, eyes wide as saucers. Panic started creeping up my spine. Suddenly, Umbra’s voice echoed from the tree line. *Go to Daisy. Now. Look at what she’s holding.*

I glanced at Rhys Thorne. The guy was white as a sheet, but he was holding her up, doing his damn best. I leaned over. "I'm gonna go check the pup. Swap with me." I jerked my chin. Rhys gave a quick, tight nod and scrambled over. I patted Elara’s shoulder to keep her calm, then slipped over to Daisy.

I held my hands out. Daisy gave me a tight, forced smile and handed him over. "Here," she whispered. She slid the kid into my arms and, without making a scene, peeled back the blanket to show his arm.

"Oh, crap," the word popped out before I could stop it. Instantly, every head in the room snapped toward us.

"What? What is it?" Rhys’s voice cracked, totally panicked.

I forced a grin, looking down at the dark-haired kid. Man, those bright green eyes were stunning, staring blankly right at me. "Nothing's wrong! Look, ten fingers." I shifted the cloth, checking fast. "Ten toes." The little guy’s lip was quivering, cute as hell. I walked back to the bed. "He’s perfect. Seriously, he's blessed."

Elara groaned as another wave hit, but her eyes were glued to me as I climbed back onto the mattress. "Let me hold him. Please."

I smirked. "You will. The second your girl gets here. That way you aren't fighting contractions while holding him. You can just chill and bond with both." I slid in next to her, tilting my arms. "But look at your boy."

Rhys and Elara leaned in, completely forgetting the agony for a second just to stare at their pup. "He’s perfect," Temen muttered, his voice barely a whisper.

Elara leaned down, kissing his forehead. "Our Jasper," she whispered. Then she pulled back, and both of them froze, staring at his arm. "Wait. What the hell is that?"

"A blessing from the Celestia," I said. On Jasper’s soft pink shoulder, three pale, glowing stars were etched into his skin. I tapped the mark, and the damn thing actually brightened under my finger.

"What’s it mean?" Rhys’s finger traced the blanket, then touched the mark.

"No clue," I said. "Only the Goddess knows what she’s cooking up with her blessings."

Elara opened her mouth to speak, but instead, she arched her back and screamed. Mom’s head popped up from under the sheet, grinning. "Your girl is ready to roll!" She ducked back down. "Next contraction, we push. Got it?" It wasn't a question. Elara nodded anyway. "Okay." She gasped for air, and then the next wave slammed her. I passed the baby back to Daisy and grabbed Elara.

"Push!" I yelled, grabbing her leg. Rhys grabbed the other. Just like before, she screamed, pushing with everything she had left until she collapsed back against us, totally spent. "I can't," she wheezed.

"She’s right there!" Mom yelled from below. "Give me one huge push on the next one and she’s out!"

"You got this, come on!" I threw my arm behind her back, and Rhys pressed in close.

"Okay," Elara gasped as the pain hit again. She grunted, dug deep, and gave it one last, brutal push. A tiny, sharp wail cut through the room.
