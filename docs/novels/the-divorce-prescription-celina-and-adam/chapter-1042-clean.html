Jonah openly admitted that he knew Rebecca.

The entire lecture hall erupted in an uproar. For the students, his words were almost like an official announcement. It was clear to them that Rebecca was his girlfriend.

"Damn, Rebecca, you're so lucky! When you and Mr. Pilsner get married, you'd better invite us to the wedding!"

This was exactly the kind of attention Rebecca had always craved. But now, those envious glances felt like a thousand cuts.

Flustered, she quickly tried to correct them, "No, you're mistaken. Mr. Pilsner and I aren't what you think we are..."

Jonah smirked. "Rebecca's right. Our relationship isn't what you're imagining. We're just acquaintances. She's not my fiancee."

The students were left in shock.

Whispers quickly spread through the hall.

"Wait, what's going on here?"

"But given how excited Rebecca was around Mr. Pilsner, I really thought they were dating!"

Jonah's gaze fell on Rebecca. "Back then, I didn't know who Rebecca was. I only met her because she's a friend of my fiancee. My fiancee even invited her to stay at our place for a while. That's how we became acquainted."

Considering the scene Rebecca had caused earlier in the cafeteria, it didn't take long for the students to put the pieces together.

"Oh my god, Mr. Pilsner... is your fiancee... Hailey?"

In an instant, every eye in the room shifted to the back row where Hailey sat.

Hailey had remained silent throughout the entire exchange. With Jonah there, she didn't need to say a word.

She had known from the very beginning that Jonah had come to stand up for her.

The events in the cafeteria must have gotten back to him, which explained why he had come all the way to Aspenfield University.

Jonah's gaze shifted toward Hailey as he stepped down from the podium. With confident strides, he walked toward her.

"Honey, I'd like to make our relationship public today. Is that okay with you?" he asked.

Jonah's deep, captivating voice carried across the campus as he addressed Hailey. The moment his words sank in, the entire hall buzzed with excitement.

"Oh my God, Mr. Pilsner's fiancee is Hailey!"

"We should've guessed it earlier. Mr. Pilsner and Hailey are a perfect match!"

Amid the cheers, Hailey's lips curled into a bright smile. "Of course, I don't mind!"

She stood up and walked to Jonah's side.
