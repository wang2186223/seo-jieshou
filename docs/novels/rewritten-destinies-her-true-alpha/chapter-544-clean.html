Chapter 544

I shrieked. It wasn't even a choice—the sound just ripped out of me. Boom. Every single head in the room snapped my way. Roric snickered, yanking the blade straight out of Orion’s thigh. My guy just grunted, taking the hit like a champ.

"God, I love Omegas. Always so damn dramatic," Roric mocked, letting out a high-pitched "Ah!" before doubling over, cracking himself up.

The crowd started snickering too, except for a couple of girls in the back. Their faces stayed dead serious.

"Get over here," Garitli barked, waving his hand. Nobody budged. "Fine. *Itsi blugh dor not ihnas provon ti.*"

That did it. The crowd shuffled in, and Damon spun away from Roric, locking eyes on Freya.

"You're dead," Damon spat, lunging for Lyn.

She glanced at me. No panic, just... acceptance. She knew this was it. She gave me a quick nod. Then she smirked right in Damon's face.

"In your dreams, asshole," she cackled, throwing a wild punch.

I screamed again—pure, useless rage. I couldn't move. Suddenly, hands grabbed my shoulders.

"Don't," a voice whispered in my ear. "You can't."

"Freeze!" Roric barked at Damon. The command hit Damon like a truck; he stumbled. "I let these people out to watch a king die, not to watch you settle some petty grudge with a bitch. Sit tight and watch, or just stand there and shut up. You don't move a muscle until I say so. Got it?"

Damon glared daggers but nodded. He looked back at Freya, baring his fangs. "Enjoy your extra minutes. I'm still ripping your throat out."

Freya just laughed in his face. "Oh, I'm shaking. You look like a toddler playing dress-up, dude." A few people in the crowd snorted.

"Shut it," Roric snapped, turning back to Orion. Orion’s eyes were getting cloudy. Panic hit my gut, but I had to trust my guy. "Now, no more distractions while I play," Roric muttered. "*It lor kina sizet.*" The heavy weight of the spell slammed back down on everyone.

As the crowd mumbled, I breathed out the counter: "*Itsi blugh dor not indi ahsa.*"

Roric spun his knife. "Hey, King. Must suck being the last of your kind. Pretty lonely, huh?"

Orion slumped forward, playing along, acting like the silver in his veins was finally dragging him down.

"Look at you. Pathetic," Roric sneered. "Feeling that burn in your gut? Yeah, we spiked your drinks with silver. Should be pumping through your whole body by now."

Orion just smirked. "Big deal."

Roric laughed. "Oh yeah? Why's that?"

Orion tilted his head, sliding down another inch. "I can still shift. Lycans always turn when they're cornered."

Roric nodded. "Yeah, your mom taught me that." He yanked down his collar, showing a nasty scar. "Almost had me. Too bad her aim sucked. Missed my heart by an inch. Guess it's hard to aim when you're dying, right?" He laughed.

My arm started shaking. Thank God my long sleeves hid the fur sprouting on my skin.

Orion growled, deep and low. "You piece of shit."

"Guilty as charged," Roric grinned. "But hey, my own mother knows how I roll. And if I can butcher my own mom, what makes yours think she's special?" He grabbed another blade and looked at me. "I'd love to just end you, but where's the fun in that? Gotta drag this out."

"Ugh, why don't you just kill him already?" Blair groaned, sounding totally bored. "I'm starving."

Roric chuckled. "You'd actually be useful if you had a real rank, babe." Blair's face twitched, stuck between a pout and a smile. "But to answer your question, we need answers. I need to know who his mate is... and who the first family is."

Orion grunted as another blade sank into his leg. "You think I'm talking?"

Roric grinned. "You won't have a choice. See, my boys are already at the capital. And at that little cabin in the woods—your mom's old place. The one you spent a fortune rebuilding. Thought it was safe, huh? Nope." He pulled out his phone. "They're on site, breaking down that shield of yours. Pretty impressive work, honestly. So, who's your witch? Lyra?"

Rowas let out a roar so loud it shook the room. Roric froze. "Wait. Is your witch my granddaughter?" Orion kept his mouth shut. Roric leaned in closer, smelling blood. "Is she your mate?"
