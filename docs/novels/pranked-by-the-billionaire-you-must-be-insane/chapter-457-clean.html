Chapter 461

A text lit up the screen. "Told ya! That's Vivienne. Yeah, *the* Lancaster heiress. Vaelion's filthy rich royalty. She swept the international piano circuit, got a closet full of trophies."

Finn scoffed, looking kind of pathetic. "Big deal, she plays the piano. She’s still just a trailer-trash nobody, right? Genevieve said she got kicked out before she even got her high school diploma. No way she compares to Genevieve."

"And she thinks she's bagging a billionaire? Jasper's clueless about her trashy past, obviously."

"Zip it!" Caspian barked. His knuckles turned white, veins popping on the back of his hand. He glared at the screen like he wanted to kill it.

His buddy's words kept looping in his head.

Jasper wanted her.

Like, actually wanted to put a ring on it.

Lyra... what the hell?

How could she do this?

She dumps him, and suddenly she's the town flirt, bouncing from one rich guy to the next?

And the worst part?

Those guys were the heavy hitters. The VIPs he’d kill to grab a beer with, guys way out of his tax bracket.

But Lyra? She just waltzed right into their inner circle. She was crashing parties he couldn't even get a parking pass for.

She was absolutely glowing.

What else had she kept from him?

First, she’s some secret drift-racing prodigy—maybe even Skye.

Now, she’s playing circles around Vaelion’s richest trust-fund baby.

If...

If she’d just shown off a fraction of this stuff back then...

How the hell did he spend four years treating her like background noise?

"Whoa, dude, chill! That's my phone!" his buddy yelled, lunging to snatch the device before Caspian snapped it in half.

They wrestled, and a stray thumb swiped the video timeline.

Caspian froze. His jaw dropped.

He shoved his friend back and glued his eyes to the glass.

Right there. Standing off-stage, wearing a designer dress but looking like a hot, panicked mess—Genevieve.

Genevieve?!

Lyra only got into the Blackwood gala because she rode his coattails.

But how did Genevieve sneak in?

The Coles didn't even make the B-list for an invite.

The Wynns? They were bottom-tier. No way in hell.

So what was she doing at the Blackwood heiress's birthday bash?

And if she had a ticket, why the radio silence?

Why didn't she bring him as her plus-one?

An invite like that...

If he’d just gotten his foot in the door...

He could’ve recouped every single cent he lost on that track disaster.

The Cole family crown would be his again.

Why did she keep this a secret?

He squeezed the phone, then slammed it down.

With a dark scowl, he whipped out his own cell and punched in Genevieve's number.

Classic toxic ex behavior.

Cocky, arrogant, completely blind to his own BS.

It didn't even cross his mind that *he* was the one who ghosted her after the racing crash, dodging her calls to prep for a breakup.

But now? Seeing her at that party?

Suddenly, Genevieve was useful again.

Meanwhile...

The Wynn family and those two wannabe influencers were currently face-planting on the pavement, tossed out like trash by the Blackwood security team.
