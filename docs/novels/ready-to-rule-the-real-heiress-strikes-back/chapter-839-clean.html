Lucinda folded her arms, still looking annoyed. "Well, at least you know now."

Lucinda shot him a look.

A few outfits? Seriously?

He really thought a couple of outfits would win her over?

Caspian caught her expression and realized he'd put his foot in his mouth. Lucinda was the daughter of the wealthiest man in the country. Did she really need him to buy her clothes?

He quickly added, "And bags, and jewelry too. We can look at whatever you want. I heard the brands here have some really amazing stuff."

Lucinda's face finally relaxed a little. That was more like it.

"Let's go."

They walked into a well-known designer bag store. The saleswoman greeted them with a big smile and welcomed them inside.
