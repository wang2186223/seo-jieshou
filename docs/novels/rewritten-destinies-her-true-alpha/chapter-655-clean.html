Chapter 620

Green smoke choked my throat. I couldn't see shit—just a wall of green. I gagged, tears streaming down my face. My body went into full panic mode, trying to lock the smoke out.

"Chill out," a sweet, young voice echoed in my head. The Maiden. "Your body's fighting me. Let me in."

I squeezed my eyes shut. *Holy crap, it's the goddess.*

"Duh. Who else did you expect, babe?" She giggled. I forced my muscles to go limp.

*I don't know,* I shot back. *Usually, the Moon Celestia just hijacks my body whenever she feels like it.*

The Three-faced goddess laughed, her voice suddenly sounding way older. "That's because your wolves are hooked up to her. She uses their connection like a backdoor. Easy access."

*Then why can't you use that door?* I hacked, my throat squeezing shut again.

"Just swallow it," Orion's voice popped into my head. "Trust me, it helps." I wanted to roll my eyes, but I gulped anyway. Boom. The smoke slid right down.

"Well, at least your guy has some brains," the Crone rasped. She sounded ancient, like she'd been around since the dawn of time. She cackled at my thoughts. "Damn right I have. I've got a million secrets." I felt her ghostly hands touch me. "I can't use that door because our magic is totally different. I don't do the whole 'sliding down moonbeams' thing." She cackled again.

My lungs were screaming for oxygen. Everything went black. *Yep, I'm dead.*

Cold fingers brushed my cheek. "Hey, believe in yourself. Your magic, your strength," the Maiden whispered. "And trust me. I won't hurt you."

My eyelids felt like lead. I gave up trying to open them and just shut them tight. I focused on the smoke, sucking it in like fresh air. It rushed down my throat.

Suddenly, my mom shrieked. It broke my heart. I pictured my dad wrapping his arms around her, holding her tight.

"We gotta trust her," Dad's voice was quiet but rock-solid. He believed in me. He believed in the goddesses. "They won't hurt her. Lyra knows what she's doing. That's why she said yes."

Then came Leo's nasty laugh. "Ten minutes, guys. She's fucking dead." *Smack.* Someone got hit, followed by a scream. "She's gone! And you idiots just watched. The goddess killed her own little whore. God, this is beautiful." He laughed like a maniac. "Congrats, losers."

Baan growled, and I heard the heavy thud of fists hitting meat. Over and over. "Shut the fuck up. She's not dead." His voice went dead low. "Keep lying to yourself, asshole. The goddesses wouldn't dump Lyra like that." A choking sound, then a gasp. "But hey, let's say she is dead. You think that saves your skin?"

"What... the hell... do you mean?" Caspian wheezed.

Orion let out a cold, amused chuckle. "You brought the spear, Caspian. You brought the one weapon that can rip your wolf's soul right out of your body. You brought it to threaten us, and now you're trapped here, waiting to die. If Lyra doesn't make it, I'm taking that spear and ending you. Either way, you're toast, and your wolf is gone."

"You wouldn't dare," Caspian growled. "You always said you'd never use that spear. You swore when you were a kid it was too messed up, too cruel."

"People grow up, Caspian," Orion snapped. "If my mate is ready to throw her soul on the line to save this world... then so am I."

Caspian panted, then burst into a crazy, unhinged laugh. "You'd throw away your entire moral code... for a girl?"

"For her? Absolutely. In a heartbeat, no regrets," Orion shot back.

Suddenly, the goddess stepped in. The air shifted. "That's enough," the Moon Celestia's voice boomed through the room.

The Mother's voice whispered in my head. "Your man is loyal. I like that. I was worried about your second shot at life after that first disaster, but with him? You're in good hands."

*You actually care?* Something tight in my chest finally let go.

"Of course I do, sweetie. Phoebe might have had you first, but you're my girl too." She sighed. "I know you've seen where your line starts. But you haven't seen the whole picture."

*What else is there?*

"Do you trust me?" Her voice shifted back to the sweet Maiden, full of hope.

I didn't even blink. *Hell yeah, I do.* I took a massive gulp of air. My eyes snapped open. The room rushed back.

"Lyra!" Mom screamed, running toward me. I managed a quick smile. "Mom," I gasped, reaching out.

My hand grabbed empty air. The room vanished. Pitch black again. But this time, a wolf howled in the distance. And from the other side, a woman was singing.

"Orion, you little rascal, get back here."
