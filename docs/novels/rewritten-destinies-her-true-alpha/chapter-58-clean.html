Chapter 58

Another wolf circled me. Looking for a gap. I didn't budge. No way in hell I'm dropping dead and letting him get these kids. I forced my voice out. "Hey. You two. Listen up." I waited. Heard them gasp. "Can you... do something for me?"

"Alpha Lyra?" the boy whimpered. "Is that really you?"

"Yeah, buddy. It's me. Just in my wolf form. Do me a solid, okay?" My legs shook. I locked my knees. Had to stay upright. Had to get them out.

"Okay, Alpha."

"Good kid. Be brave for me, alright? Grab your sister. Run." I wanted to look back. Give him a reassuring nod. But I couldn't blink. One look away, and this bastard wolf would spring.

"Are you coming too?"

"Duh. I'm right behind you. Just gotta make sure you're safe first, then I'll grab your folks and catch up."

"Promise?" His squeaky voice ripped my chest open.

"Cross my heart. Just get somewhere safe." I poured every ounce of truth I had into those words. "Go. Now. I've got your back." I threw my head back. Let out a massive, desperate howl. *Move!* "Go! Run!" I screamed, lunging straight at the enemy. Behind me, I heard their tiny paws scramble away.

"Stupid bitch. You're dead meat," the rogue growled, dropping low, ready to spring.

"Maybe. But those kids live. That's all that counts." Terra channeled her energy straight to Umbra. I jumped in, pushing my own power into the mix.

*We're with you, Umbra. If we go down today, we go down saving those kids.* I yelled to my wolf. Her muscles bunched. She sucked in every drop of power left. We were bleeding out fast, we knew it, but she didn't care. She was buying those kids time.

"You didn't change a damn thing," the rogue sneered, crouching lower, tracking us. "I'll rip your throat out first. Then I'll hunt them down. They'll end up just like their parents. And I'm gonna eat their tiny hearts raw."

"Not gonna happen." But then Umbra tripped. Hit the dirt hard on her side.

*Get up!* I shrieked inside my head.

The rogue cackled. He leaped, jaws aiming right for our neck. But Umbra snapped up. Lightning fast. She launched herself. Her teeth slammed shut around his throat. She bit down. Hard.

She hit the ground on all fours. The rogue's head popped off, flying through the air. But her legs gave out again. She tumbled, face-planting into the dirt. Umbra just lay there. Terra and I huddled close in our minds, whispering to her.

*You did awesome, girl,* I told her. I felt her heart shattering. It was our collective agony. I tried to soothe it. *It's fine. We did the job. The kids are safe.*

*But we're gonna die...* Umbra's voice faded.

*I loved this life with you. Just knowing you saw me... that was plenty.* Terra nuzzled me. A tear slipped down my cheek.

*We kicked ass. We saved those kids. This life was way better, and honestly, I'm just glad I had you two.* I sniffled, letting out a weak laugh. *You were badass, Umbra.*

*At least I got to feel the wind in my fur,* Umbra whispered. The bond was slipping. Fading. The world was going gray. *Sorry you didn't get a turn, Megani.*

*Next time, Umbra. There's always a next time.* Terra and I felt Umbra slip away, retreating deep inside. My body started changing back. Fur vanished. Bones cracked and popped, forcing themselves back into human shape. God, it hurt. Way worse than the first time. I shrieked as the shift finished, collapsing into the dirt, gasping for air.

The gush of blood from my neck slowed down. But it wasn't a good thing. Usually, shifting heals you. Not this time. I was just running dry. I was hitting the finish line.

Barely any blood left in my veins. Barely any seconds left on the clock.

I squeezed out the last bit of my energy. Sent one final mind-link to my dad. *Love you, Dad.* I shut my eyes. Slid back into my mindscape. I threw my arms around Terra and Umbra, burying my hands in their fur. *I'm sorry, Lyra. I couldn't save us,* Umbra choked out, fighting for every word.

"We had each other's backs. I was right there. Look at me." Umbra lifted her head. Looked me dead in the eye. "I don't regret a single second with you. Not one. You did incredible with the hand we were dealt. I never doubted you. Not once."

"But we're croaking..."

"Yeah, but we went out saving two terrified kids. I'd say that's a damn good reason to die."

"Yeah. Yeah, it is." Umbra slumped back down. I curled up right beside her.

"Catch you guys on the flip side," I whispered. Then, the lights went out.
