Chapter 507

We locked eyes. Dead silence. Then, a light tap on the wood.

"Get in here," Orion barked, staring right through me.

Elara rolled in a fresh cart. "Four bottles. Figure you'll need 'em," she said, wheeling it over with a grin. "Plus, chef sent up two slices of that strawberry cake you love, Lyra. I'll just leave the cart. Just kick it into the hallway when you guys are done." A quick bow, and she slipped out.

I took a slow gulp of wine. Orion chugged the rest of his first bottle. Sixty seconds ticked by. I leaned back. "Look, if you're gonna play dumb, let's cut the crap. I'm leaving."

He nodded. "Yeah, I've been chewing on that. But man, I couldn't live with myself if I didn't even try. Shadow would lose his absolute mind if I just let you walk." He pointed at my empty chair. "Sit. Please."

I bit my lip, hesitating. *Can you really blame him?* Umbra whispered in my head. *What if he actually had a reason?* That hurt. A reason? For all the bullshit he pulled? *I don't know, okay?* Umbra snapped back. *But get the facts before you blow this up.* Terra gave a silent nod of agreement.

"Fine." I dropped back into the chair and stabbed my fork into the food. I slammed the rest of my wine, snatched the next bottle right out of Orion's reach, and poured us both a heavy double. "Spill it. From day one."

He sliced off a piece of steak, smirking. "Alright. Here goes." He leaned in. "Honestly? You had me at 'help me torture this guy'." I let out a dry laugh, but his eyes went dark and hungry. "Then I actually saw you. Game over. But who you are deep down? That's what totally wrecked me. I fell hard."

He rambled about our past, then hit the heavy stuff. My heat. "If we crossed that line, you would've hated my guts. I had to drag myself away. Even Shadow was fighting me on it, and he was totally obsessed with his gorgeous wild wolf." Another smirk.

My plate was clean. I swirled the dark liquid in my glass. "So you bailed." I took a hit of wine, fighting to stay in the present instead of drowning in those old, raw feelings.

He nodded. "Went back to my place and completely lost my mind. So, I rang your dad."

"My dad?" I stabbed at the strawberry cake, my stomach doing flips. "Why the hell would you do that?"

"I needed advice." He took a swig. "Shocking, I know, but I'm not exactly Mr. Popular. No friends."

Wine almost shot out my nose. I choked, coughing. "No shit, Sherlock."

He chuckled, but the humor died fast. "I wanted him to tell me it was fine. That I could choose you and forget about waiting for some fated mate." He grabbed the bottle, drinking straight from the neck. "You can guess how that conversation went." He snorted.

"What'd he say?" I whispered.

"He said we had to play the long game. If we hooked up then, you'd have a massive bullseye on your back. Magnus and Damon wouldn't have let you get anywhere near them, and your dad needed you on the inside. He told me to back off. Said he'd explain everything later, but if we wanted the plan to work, I had to ghost you." He took a massive gulp from the bottle.

Something broke inside my chest. "The letters... they said you guys planned stuff. But I had no idea he ordered you to do that."

"Wait, you read them?" He looked totally thrown off.

I nodded. "Found 'em a few days ago. Before that? Total blackout. You ditched me, and then you just went radio silent." I grabbed my own bottle and ripped the cork out. Screw it.

"Radio silent?" He shook his head wildly. "I never ignored you! I called, I emailed—nothing but dead air. So when your dad asked me to watch your back, I literally built Cipher for you." He chugged the rest of the bottle. "Not that it mattered, apparently."

I let out a low growl. "Not that it mattered?" I slammed a hand against my chest. "It mattered to me, you asshole!"

"You're the one who iced me out!" he roared back. "I was fucking drowning, and you wouldn't even give me the time of day!"

I kicked my chair back and stood up, screaming. "I never fucking ignored you! You ghosted me!" The dam broke, all the old pain flooding back. "You walked out right when my world was falling apart! No calls, no texts, fucking nothing! I LOVED YOU, YOU BASTARD, AND YOU JUST LEFT ME!"
