Magnus shook his head. "No freaking way."

Orion smirked. "Oh, she's the real deal, man. Honestly, I gotta thank you for dropping the ball twice." Orion rolled his eyes, pointing a thumb at Damon. "If you hadn't been dumb enough to pick Blair over there, I'd never have found my queen. My literal, goddess-blessed mate." He ripped his shirt off, flashing the mate mark. "Everything is set up. She's taking my spot."

"You do know I'm just gonna murder her, right?" Roric grinned.

Orion threw his head back, laughing so hard he choked on a cough. Roric's face went pitch black. "Go ahead and try," Orion said, putting his hands up. "But she's way tougher than you think, old man."

Roric sneered. "Whatever. I'll just get my son—"

"The kid hiding in the caves with Morgana?" Orion cocked an eyebrow. "Yeah... don't hold your breath on him. She's already a corpse, and your boy? Zero survival instincts. You get what I'm saying, right?"

Roric went white as a sheet. "No."

"Hey, Magnus did say she was coming for your servers," Orion smiled.

Roric whipped out his phone, punching in a number. "Pick up, damn it." He spun around, barking at the crowd. "Get 'em!"

The mob surged forward. A couple of girls slipped through the guys as Roric tried dialing again. Freya smirked. "No dial tone, huh?"

Roric let out a low growl. "If he's got a scratch on him, you're dead."

Freya bared her fangs. "Newsflash, buddy: I'm already dead." She cackled as he redialed.

Finally, the line picked up. Screams echoed through the speaker. "Dad?" Corbin gasped.

Roric gripped the phone like he wanted to crush it. "Corbin! What the hell is going on over there?"

"The caves... we're getting hit!" A howl cut through, and Corbin's voice turned cold. "Morgana's gone. Some tracker gutted her the other day. I got a few people out, but other packs are blocking the exits."

Roric glared at Orion, who was just grinning. "Oh, did I forget to mention that part?" Orion teased.

"Corbin, listen to me," Roric snapped. "Screw the rogues. Just save your own skin. Get out of there."

"Dad, I can't do that. I'm their alpha," Corbin said, his voice deadpan.

"Yes, you damn well can! Do what I tell you, boy!"

Silence stretched over the line. Then Corbin sighed. "Fine. But I'm dragging out as many as I can." Click.

Roric stared at the blank screen, then chucked the phone straight into a stone pillar. It exploded into a million plastic shards. He spun back to Orion, eyes burning. "I am going to rip you apart."

"Be my guest." Orion pushed himself up from the wooden pew, bouncing on his heels.

Roric laughed, shaking his head. "You think you're actually a threat?" He snorted. "You're just as brainless as your old man." He waved at the crowd. "Grab him."

A couple of guards and a chick lunged, pinning Orion's arms behind his back. Roric stepped up close. "I'm gonna have them hold you down while I carve that heart right out of your chest."

Damon frowned, totally lost. "Wait, why don't you just use your alpha voice? Or magic?"

Astrid rolled her eyes hard. "Because it doesn't work like that, dummy. Magic and commands only go so far. If a wolf knows it's about to die, survival instinct kicks in and breaks the spell."

Damon pointed a finger at Gareth's body. "Bullshit. Gareth was glued to the floor while that bitch sliced him up."

Astrid groaned. "No, your beta was just a complete idiot." She kicked Gareth's limp hand. Damon growled, but she just rolled her eyes again. "His wolf was just as delusional as he was. They convinced themselves they were safe because he didn't think a girl could hurt him. He could've stood up whenever he wanted. But he just lay there, waiting to heal. Total dumbass."

Damon stared down at his best friend. "He... he could've just stood up?"

Roric let out a dry chuckle. "Wouldn't have mattered anyway, kid. He was a goner the second that second blade went in. Once silver gets that close to the heart, game over. He was already dead; his body just hadn't realized it yet." He turned to me, snapping his fingers. "Get over here." I stepped up, holding out the toolkit. "Now, back to business." He grabbed another blade. "Good girl."

I gave him a sweet smile. "Thanks." I quietly slid a knife out of the kit as he turned around. I watched him walk toward my mate. "I do my best, Alpha."

"Man, if only my own blood was this damn useful," Roric muttered, dragging the cold steel across Orion's cheek. "Alright... let's get this over with."

"Well..." I smiled, my voice soft. "You're in luck."

Roric froze. "What?" He turned to look at me just as the mask I untied slipped off my face.

"I said, you're in luck... Grandpa."

In one swift motion, I spun and ripped a guard's throat wide open.
