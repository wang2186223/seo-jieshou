Chapter 40

Lyra just stood there, watching the two idiots put on their little circus act. She rolled her eyes, a slow, nasty smirk creeping onto her face. "Stalking you?" she drawled. "Please. You're not even in my league, babe."

Spencer’s jaw clenched. Her attitude set him off like a match to gasoline. He took a step forward, ready to blow a fuse.

Genevieve grabbed his arm, pulling him back. "Wait, Spencer, don't. I mean, yeah, she’s totally trying to steal my guy, but look at her. It’s sad. She got kicked out of the family and can't handle being poor. She’s just desperate for cash."

She shoved a plastic card right at Lyra’s face. "Seriously, Lyra, drop the pride. It’s pathetic. You used to be part of the family. Don't drag our name through the mud for some pocket change. Just take the money, get a real job, and maybe you can—"

She didn't get to finish. Lyra snatched the card and whipped it right across Genevieve’s cheek. *Smack.* The plastic bit hard into her skin.

Genevieve shrieked, clutching her face. But before Spencer could even blink, Lyra lunged. She grabbed a fistful of Genevieve’s hair and ripped her straight out of his grip.

*Crack!* Lyra’s palm connected with Genevieve’s face, hard. Then she slammed her hand onto the back of Genevieve's head, forcing her down. Genevieve hit the floor, her knees cracking against the tile.

"You bitch—" Spencer roared, but the words died in his throat. Lyra raised her boot and stomped it right onto Genevieve’s head, grinding her cheek flat against the floor.

Lyra stood over her like a queen, looking down at the mess. "Cut the holy-act, sweetie. Your fake, plastic face literally makes me want to throw up. Oh, and another thing..."

She let out a cold, nasty laugh. "I don't know you. Stop calling me your sister. I don't have a walking curse for family."

"Lyra!" Spencer’s face went white, then purple with pure rage. He finally snapped and charged her. "Get your fucking foot off her!"

He screamed obscenities as he lunged. "A curse? You’re the goddamn curse! You got thrown out like trash! What did you ever do for us? Nothing! But the second Genevieve got back, the Lancasters practically begged us to partner up!"

"We're talking about the Lancasters, loser! The richest people in Vaelion! You couldn't even breathe the same air as them if you lived a thousand lives!"

"That deal is putting us on top of the world. You're just a broke, bad-luck loser. You're the real trash here!"

He threw a fist, aiming right for her face. But Lyra was faster. Her long leg whipped up, planting her boot right into his solar plexus.

Spencer doubled over, stumbling backward. He clutched his gut, his face twisting in pure agony.

"The Lancasters?" Lyra smirked, her eyes dripping with pure contempt. "Hate to break it to you, but your little lucky streak ends today. They aren't signing shit with you. And neither is anyone else in this city."

Spencer’s lackeys grabbed him before he hit the floor. He wheezed, trying to catch his breath. "Who... who the hell do you think you are? You don't run this town! As long as Harley’s with us, we’re golden. You aren't even fit to clean her shoes, bitch!"

He turned and screamed at a guard standing nearby. "What are you standing there for? Throw this trash out! Look at her cheap-ass clothes. She doesn't belong in a place like this!"

The guard blinked. He knew Spencer—guy was a major VIP. But the girl... damn, she was gorgeous, even if she was wearing basic, cheap clothes. He hesitated, then took a slow step forward.
