Chapter 605

I whipped around. Juniper was hauling ass straight toward us. "Wait, what the hell did you just say?" she yelled. I squinted, trying to see who she was aiming that at, but it was a total clusterfuck. Just a massive wall of guys packed shoulder-to-shoulder.

She charged the crowd, then froze. Her eyes locked onto the silver cuffs and heavy chains binding them all together. She tripped over her own feet, stopping dead next to Orion, looking completely wiped out. "My... my mate..."

"Which guy is it?" I scanned the area for my grandma. Classic. She’d already pulled her usual vanishing act.

Juniper shook her head, frantic. "I caught his scent, but now? Nothing. It's like someone blocked it." She stared at me, eyes wide. "Is my mate a damn traitor?"

I blew out a breath, popping my lips. "Well, guess we gotta find out." I turned to the Elite guards. "Hey! Unlock anyone with a star on their forehead. Now."

Orion’s boys jumped to it, pushing into the mob. Metal clinked and chains hit the dirt, but nobody budged. Every single one of them just stared at me like I had two heads.

I glanced at Orion. He let out a soft laugh and ran a finger right over my forehead. "Well, looks like the Celestia wanted a cheat sheet for who's good and who's bad."

I popped an eyebrow, ready to say something, but then Orion’s forehead lit up like a neon sign. A hollow crescent moon sliced right into his skin, with sharp lines branching out toward his temples. "Huh. Guess so," I mumbled, poking the glowing mark on his head. Then I spotted my dad holding his phone up. "Dad? Really?"

"Your mom's gonna love this," he grinned, snapping a quick pic of Orion and me. "Don't mind me. Go ahead, talk to your boys." He gestured toward the waiting guards.

I rolled my eyes so hard they almost got stuck. I turned back to the crowd. "Alright, listen up! If you just got unchained, step forward. Give us your name and tell us who you swore your oath to." I reached out to Juniper. "Hey. Get over here." She shuffled over and grabbed my hand, squeezing tight.

*I'm freaking out,* her voice buzzed inside my head.

I flashed her a reassuring look. *Chill. You deserve a decent guy, and the Moon Celestia knows it.* I pushed the thought into her mind, and I felt her shoulders drop as she relaxed.

The first guy stepped up, practically shaking. "Name?" I asked.

"Jasper, ma'am." He hit the dirt, dropping to one knee. "I was sworn to Hawthorn. Haven't made a new deal with anyone else."

I looked back at Orion. "A new oath? How's that work?"

"When the original boss dies, the first oath stays locked in, but they can stack a new one on top," Orion explained. "It flips their priorities. The new oath takes the wheel."

I gave a quick nod and looked back down at the guy. "So, Jasper. You want to swear a new oath?"

His head snapped up, totally shocked. "Wait... what?"

I squatted down, getting right in his face. "I asked if you want to swear a new oath. Yes or no?"

"I... I don't know." He frowned, looking completely lost. "Nobody ever asks us what we want."

"Well, I'm not forcing shit. You actually have a choice here."

"Actually, they don't," Finnian muttered, staring at the dirt. "Once you kick them out of the pack system, they're locked out forever." He looked up, his eyes swimming with regret. "They end up as rogue powerhouses. Alphas with zero leash. Nobody can control them because they don't take orders from anyone." I stood up, dusting off my hands. *Oh yeah? Let's test that theory.*

I smirked and let my aura rip. It blasted out of me like a shockwave, slamming into every single wolf in the yard. "Bow," I commanded. Instantly, every body bent double at the waist. Only Orion and my dad stayed upright—though my dad's face was turning red from the sheer effort of fighting it.

"Holy hell," Finnian choked out. "I knew you were a badass, kiddo, but I haven't felt you flex like this since you were a brat." He tried to look up at me, but the pressure kept his head glued down. "Uh, can you turn the dial down a bit?"

Finnian grunted, "Yeah, okay. I was dead wrong."

I snorted and sucked my power back in. Everyone stood up, shaking, a few of them dripping sweat from the strain. Juniper smirked, looping her arm through mine. "Yeah, I don't think 'uncontrollable wolves' are gonna be an issue with the King and Queen running the show."

I grinned at her, then turned back to the guy kneeling in the dirt. "Alright, Jasper, here's the deal. You've got choices. But if you join the royal pack, you're starting at the bottom. You'll take orders like any regular grunt. Or, you stay a guard and swear to someone else." I squatted back down, but he wouldn't look at me. I hooked my index finger under his chin and forced his head up. "Your call, man."

"I—" He choked, his throat tight. He swallowed hard and tried again. "I want to swear to you. Only you."

I gave him a firm nod. "Alright. Let's sort out the rest of these guys first. I'll figure out how this oath magic works, and we'll do it later." I stood up and threw my hand out to help him up. "Deal?"
