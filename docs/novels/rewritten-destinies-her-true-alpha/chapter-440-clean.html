Chapter 440

I swiped a hand across my face. Damon whipped around, locking eyes with Blair. Man, she looked wrecked—I could literally smell the pure misery rolling off her, her eyes blazing. "How could you do this?" she choked out. The words slapped him right in the face. She snapped her head toward me, but I threw my hands up instantly. "Whoa, back off. Don't glare at me," I snapped. She was boiling. "I literally told him he had to clear the air with you before we did any mating ceremony."

Damon pivoted back to me, his face a messy mix of fury and some weird emotion I couldn't name. But then Blair started gagging, and he whipped right back to her. "Wait, your mating ceremony?" She darted her eyes between us, totally lost. "You guys are actually still going through with it?" She took a shaky step back, her shock turning into pure, unadulterated rage. "Is that a fucking fact?"

Damon's head was on a swivel, looking from her to me, before he finally locked onto me with these pathetic, begging eyes. "Lyra, please..." I let out a dry laugh. "Of course. I fucking knew it." I glared hard at him. "Look, if she's the one you want, go mate her. Leave me the hell out of your drama." I spat the words out, turning on my heel toward the house.

"Hold on!" Damon yelled as I yanked the front door open, slamming it against the wall. I spun around. "What? Seriously, what the fuck do you even have to say right now?"

Blair let out a low growl. "Hey, don't you dare speak to him like that." I looked right at her. "Blair, I love you. I'm so sorry I got dragged into this mess." I glanced at Damon, who was just standing there like a deer in headlights, looking back and forth. "Listen, if either of you had just been straight with me from day one, I would've never agreed to date him, let alone mate him." I squeezed out a few fake tears, letting them well up. "I was literally trying to avoid this exact fucking trainwreck."

Blair cackled. "You love me? Bullshit!" she shrieked. Her screaming was finally bringing people out of their houses. Perfect. Let everyone watch. Damon started looking around, sweating as he realized a crowd was forming. "You don't give a shit about me!" she kept yelling, tossing her head so hard her hair flew wild. "If you actually cared, you wouldn't have touched my boyfriend!"

"Guys, let's just chill out," Damon muttered. I burst out laughing. "Oh, so he's your boyfriend now?" I marched right up to him, jabbing my finger hard into his chest. "I knew it. You literally told me she was just a placeholder. You said you only hung out with her to get to me, and then things just got messy. Isn't that exactly what came out of your mouth?"

He shot a panicked look at Blair. She looked completely crushed. "Did you really say that about us, Damon?" Damon took a step back, raising his hands. "What? No! No way, that's a lie."

I scoffed. "Oh, great. So now I'm the liar?" I growled, then pivoted to Blair. "Look, I know we couldn't stand each other as kids, but use your brain for one second." She glared, but listened. "Did I ever, even once, show any interest in Damon back in high school?" She shook her head. "Right. Exactly." I whipped back to Damon. "I never even wanted you. You pursued me. You swore up and down that you and Blair were completely over. You told me you'd already broken it to her. And now look at this shitshow." I threw my arms wide. "We are literally less than two weeks away from OUR mating ceremony, and I'm stuck dealing with this garbage."

Blair rolled her eyes, scoffing. "Your ceremony? Please. You didn't even lift a finger to plan it."

I dragged my fingers through my hair, letting out a bitter laugh. "Yeah, because you two talked me into letting you handle it! I was busy working my ass off to protect this pack and bring in cash, while you two were supposed to be planning OUR big day." I laughed again, shaking my head. "God, I'm such a fool. I actually trusted you guys. But you were playing me the whole damn time. You weren't planning my ceremony. You were busy setting up your own!" I wiped the tears from my cheeks, raising my voice so the whole neighborhood could hear. "I was totally blind to the absolute garbage you fed me. 'I love you, Lyra. I only want you, Lyra.' Lies! Every single word was a fucking lie!"

"Lyra, please, just stop," Damon begged, reaching out to grab my arm. I shoved his hands off me, letting the tears stream down my face. "No!" I screamed, my voice cracking. The sudden shout made everyone jump. Damon blinked, totally stunned by my explosion. "You promised me you'd never hurt me! You swore on your life you'd never cheat! But look at you. Look at what you're doing right now!"

"Lyra..." he tried again, but I let out a loud, pathetic sob. "I actually trusted you!" I shrieked, and even more pack members poured out of their houses to watch the show. "You ran after me. You told me you loved me. And now? Now I'm somehow fighting my own fucking sister for a spot next to you?" He reached for me again, but I slapped his hands away. "I'm out." I took a step back, then looked straight at Blair. "He's all yours. Take him. Take the mate, take the ceremony, take my whole damn title." I caught the flash of smug victory on her face and had to bite my tongue to keep from laughing out loud. I turned back to Damon, whose face was completely white. "We're done."
