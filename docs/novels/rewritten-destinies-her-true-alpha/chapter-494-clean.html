Chapter 494

Freya threw her arms around my mom. "Thanks," she whispered. "Like, for everything."

Mom squeezed her tight. "Cut it out. No thanks needed. I'm gonna be your Luna soon anyway."

Freya shook her head, pulling back. "You already are." She turned to me, eyes locked. "Let's do this. I'm ready."

"Good." I grabbed my mom for one last hug. "Keep your eyes peeled for those trackers."

"Don't get killed," she said, hands framing my face. "Tell your dad I love him."

I gave a quick nod, then spun toward Juniper and Freya. "Move out." I bolted for the cliffside. Juniper was right on my heels, but Freya froze for a split second.

"Wait, aren't we heading back to Magnus's place for the flight?" No panic in her voice, just pure confusion.

"Nope," I said. "Found a shortcut." I hit the edge, found the drop-off, and scrambled down the steep path toward the cave. The steps were slick from the crashing waves, but the girls stayed glued to my back. I slid inside the dark cave opening and waited for them to squeeze through.

"How the hell did you even find this place?" Juniper gasped, shaking her head.

"Came in from the flip side last time." I shoved my way through the narrow rock crack and started down the stone stairs, keeping a slow pace until both of them cleared the tight squeeze.

"Oh my god..." Freya stared around the dark space. "This is right under the cabin?"

I nodded. "Crazy, right?" I let out a quick laugh as they nodded back. "Come on, run!"

"Run where?!" Juniper yelled after me.

I just laughed. "You'll see!"

We sprinted through the dark. Suddenly, a weird buzz washed over us—the magic barrier.

"Whoa, what was that?" both of them yelled.

I grinned. "Magic, baby!"

We kept hauling ass until the path sloped upward. I hit the wooden hatch and shoved it open.

Freya scrambled up behind me, with Juniper right on her tail, ducking low. "Wait, did this bypass Magnus's territory to get us closer to the runway?" Freya looked around, totally lost. "Where the hell are we?"

I slammed the hatch shut. A hum of magic sealed it tight. Nobody was getting through that way. I darted to the side of the gazebo, popped the hidden wall panel, and slipped out. I took a massive gulp of fresh air. God, those familiar pine trees felt good.

Freya stepped out first and instantly went stiff. "No way."

Juniper shoved past her to get outside, blinking. "Where are we?"

"My dad's territory." I grinned. Just then, the back door of the packhouse creaked open. A familiar face peeked out, and I just bolted. I heard the girls running behind me, but I only had eyes for that face as it slipped back into the dark house. I kicked the door open and threw myself forward.

"Baby girl!" My dad caught me mid-air.

"Daddy!" I sobbed, burying my face in his shoulder.

Behind me, someone gasped. Juniper pushed past us and threw herself into a pair of arms. "Caspian!" she choked out. "Mom!"

"Oh, our sweet girl," a woman's shaky voice whispered.

"Freya," Uncle Finnian called out. I heard him give her a solid pat on the back. "Man, you look like a ghost. You doing okay?"

I pulled back from my dad, smiling. "Uncle Finnian!" Freya shot me a look of pure relief as I wrapped him in a hug.

"Hey there, ladybug." He squeezed me tight.

"I missed you so much."

"We missed you more, kiddo." Dad reached over, tugging a strand of my hair. "You're a hard girl to track down."

I snorted. "For a dead guy, you've been pretty damn demanding." That got a laugh out of everyone.

"Lyra?" Juniper called out. I spun around. "I want you to meet my parents. Properly. Mom, Dad, this is Lyra."

"The girl who saved my baby!" Warm arms wrapped around me, pulling me into a tight hug.

"Mom," Juniper laughed, rolling her eyes. "Let her breathe."

My dad called out across the room. "Conall?"

Juniper's dad looked up. "Yeah?"

"Lyra's gonna fill us in on everything that went down these past few years up in my office. You're welcome to join. Bring the other Alphas too."

Conall nodded. "I'll round up the guys. See you up there in five."

Dad nodded. "Let's head up."

I looked around the room. "Where's Elara?"

"Guarding the caves," Finnian said, patting my shoulder as we turned toward the stairs. "Once you're done catching us up on your last few years, I'll fill you in on ours." I nodded.

"Did a lot change?" I followed them up the steps, passing one dusty, empty room after another. I kept looking around until Dad pushed open his office door. Sheets covered every single piece of furniture. I stopped dead in my tracks. "Yeah. Guess it did." Dad walked over and yanked the first sheet off.

He nodded. "Had the omegas cover the place before we cleared out."

"But why does the whole world think you're dead?"

Dad let out a dry snort. "Magic."
