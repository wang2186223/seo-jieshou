Chapter 581

My eyes locked onto the little kid right in front of me. His tiny sister was practically glued to my hip. All around us, people were screaming. "Is that all of 'em?" "Hell if I know!" someone yelled back. "How many are still unaccounted for?" "How many did we actually drag out?"

"Babe." Orion's voice snapped me out of it. "Lyra? Hey."

"Huh?" I jerked my head, trying to shake off the thick fog clogging my brain.

"You listening to me?" Orion leaned right over my shoulder, staring straight into my eyes.

"What?"

"Did you hear a word I said?"

My brain finally clicked into gear. "What? No—wait, yeah. Yes. I hear you."

Orion smirked. "Yeah, that's a hard no. You didn't hear jack." I scowled. His grin widened. "Stop hauling rocks, Lyra. Scan the rubble. See if anyone's still buried down there." I probably looked like I'd seen a ghost, because his hands immediately cupped my cheeks. "We gotta make sure, babe. No one gets left behind. Not like this." His smile vanished. "Everyone gets to go home."

I gave a stiff nod. My tongue felt like sandpaper as I licked my dry lips. "Right." I forced down the lump in my throat. "Gotta bring 'em all home." I let go of the boulders my magic was holding, dropping them with a dull thud. Then, I shoved my power deep into the collapsed cave. It snaked through the cracks, squeezing into every tiny gap left in the rubble. Nothing. Not a single heartbeat. "They're all dead," I whispered, meeting Orion's gaze.

He nodded. "That's fine, babe. Find the bodies. We're bringing everyone home."

Dead bodies. Right. Got it. I pushed my magic back out, sliding through pools of blood and floating dust, hunting for anything out of place.

"Stick to pack mates," Orion added. "Forget the rogues."

My magic sputtered. "They're dead, Orion. I can't tell who's who when they're corpses."

"Our guys are all accounted for!" someone shouted from the crowd.

Finnian's voice shook right next to me. "We got everyone out. Get clear. Lyra, when I say the word, drop the magic."

I shook my head, panicking. "But my dad—"

"He's already out," Finnian cut in, his voice cracking like he was hiding something terrible. I finally nodded. "Okay. If you're sure." My vision started to blur. Orion gave me a firm nod. "Just tell me when everyone's safe."

A moment later, his voice cut through the haze. "Let it go."

I took a massive breath and yanked my power back. *Boom. Boom. Boom.* The cave collapsed, section by section, as my magic retreated. My vision went black for a split second. I gasped, stumbling backward straight into Orion's chest as the magical backlash hit me like a freight train. He scooped me up, arms locking under my knees and around my back. My head slumped against his shoulder. "I got you, babe," he whispered. And then, the sheer exhaustion dragged me straight into the dark.

*Umbra! Terra!* I screamed into the void of my own mind as everything went black. *Are you guys there? Shadow? Obsidian?* Panic clawed at my chest. Dad was dead, but maybe, just maybe, I could keep his wolf alive inside my head. Just a tiny piece of him. But there was only silence. *NIX! MEGAN!* Oh god. Did I lose everyone?

*Still screaming your head off, huh?* Umbra's exhausted voice echoed from a dark corner of my mind.

I let out a mental sob. *You're alive!*

*Please, you can't shake us that easily,* Terra chimed in, though she sounded completely drained.

The mental clearing flickered to life as the blackness rolled back. Just as the light hit, I saw two dark silhouettes slipping into the tree line. *Obsidian! Wait! Don't leave me!* I bolted toward the trees, chasing his disappearing back.

*He can't stay, Lyra. You know how this works,* Umbra's voice called out. I spun around. My two wolves were curled tightly around a flowerpot.

I collapsed onto my knees right next to them. *I can't lose him.* I reached out, grabbing my wolves, dragging myself onto my side.

*You won't lose him, Lyra. He's our dad,* Umbra said, nuzzling my tear-stained cheek. *Even if he goes to the Goddess, he's a part of us forever.*

Terra crawled into my lap, curling up. *You've still got us.*

That wrecked me. I sobbed harder. My wolves—I'd almost lost them too. I squeezed them both tight. *Don't you dare leave me.*

*Never,* Umbra murmured, pressing against my ribs.

*We're not going anywhere, Lyra,* Terra sighed, burying herself deeper into my lap.

*You're stuck with us for good,* Umbra mumbled, collapsing against me. *Love you, Lyra.*

Terra gave a weak nod. *Love you too, Lyra. Seriously glad we didn't kick the bucket.*

I buried my face in their thick fur. *I love you guys so fucking much.* Slowly, the blackness started eating away at my mind again.

"Sleep, kiddo." A gentle voice drifted from the woods. I forced one eyelid open. My grandmother was walking toward us. "I've got you three. Just sleep. Get your strength back. This fight isn't over." Her final words sounded like a cold, whispered threat hanging in the air. I was too damn tired to ask what she meant. The darkness dragged me all the way down.
