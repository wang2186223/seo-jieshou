Chapter 1108

Lincoln's head was spinning. He glanced at Lydia and asked, “Not even my parents can know?"

Lydia nodded. “Not right now. Every extra person who knows means a bigger risk."

Lincoln groaned, dragging his hands through his hair. He looked at Lydia again. "What about Marian? Is there a chance she's been replaced too?”

Lydia frowned, annoyed with herself for not thinking to check on Marian earlier. “It's possible. You have to keep an eye on everyone, but don't let them notice. If they catch on, things will get a lot worse."

A heavy sense of dread settled over Lincoln. He nodded. "Alright."

When they left the Lowe estate, Juna felt like she was walking through a dream. She glanced at Lydia. "Should I go home and check up on my grandpa?"

Lydia raised an eyebrow. "He's acting weird?"

"Not that I've noticed." Juna paused, then threw the question back at Lydia. "What about your grandpa?”

Lydia hesitated, then shrugged it off. "I'm exhausted. Let's go home and get some sleep."

Juna sighed, checking the time. It was almost two in the morning. "Fine, fine. It's late. Time to call it a night."

They were just about to get in their cars when Lydia caught a flash of movement out of the corner of her eye. She snapped her head toward the bushes nearby. Sure enough, two figures were crouched in the shadows.

Those two were the tails Grace had sent after Lydia. They'd missed her going into the estate, but spotted her car and waited for hours, hoping to snap a few pictures for Grace. They'd been hiding there, thinking they were clever, but now they'd been caught trying to get just a couple more photos.

If they'd known this would happen, they never would have pushed their luck.

"Go, go!" one of them hissed.

The two scrambled from the bushes and sprinted toward a motorcycle parked by the curb.

A cold glint flashed in Lydia's eyes. She didn't even hesitate. She shot after them, moving so fast she was like a streak of black in the quiet night.

Juna, halfway into her car, froze for a second. Then she caught on and chased after them too.

The two men reached the motorcycle. One fumbled with the key, trying to get it in the ignition, while the other kept glancing back at Lydia closing in.

Before they could get away, Lydia was already there. She kicked the back of the bike hard. The force knocked the motorcycle over taking both men down with it.

They hit the ground hard, yelping in pain.

Lydia didn't give them a chance to recover. She grabbed one by the collar and hauled him up off the ground.

"Who sent you? Why are you taking pictures of us?" Her voice was icy. There was something dangerous in her eyes.

Juna caught up and kicked the other guy, who was trying to crawl away. He rolled over with a groan, and Juna planted her foot on his chest. "How dare you spy on us?"

The two men clearly had some skills, but they never expected Lydia to be that strong. She'd knocked them both over, motorcycle and alb before they could react

When they stayed silent, Lydia tossed her guy to the ground like he weighed nothing and gave him two sharp kicks.

He screamed in pain.

"Talk," Lydia demanded.

“It... it was Grace. She told us to follow you and send her pictures of where you

went..." one of them stammered, finally breaking.
