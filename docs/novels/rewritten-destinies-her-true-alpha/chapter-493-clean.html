Chapter 493

Mom and Freya were waiting out front. Their heads snapped up the second Juniper and I stepped onto the porch. Freya was already shivering like a leaf, but Juniper and I played it cool, acting like we didn't notice a thing.

"Yo, we rolling?" I called out.

Mom gave Freya’s shoulder a quick squeeze, forcing a smile. "Freya’s got a favor to ask."

I marched down the steps and stopped right in front of them. "Alright... shoot."

Freya shifted her weight, dragging her feet. "Look, I get it. This is just a band-aid fix. It won't actually help me process any of this crap." She was rambling, desperately trying to justify it before I could even reply.

I tilted my head, cutting her off. "Just ask."

Her eyes locked onto mine, pure panic screaming from them. "I'm terrified to leave. Like, seriously. My wolf is clawing at my insides to stay put. This place is the only spot we've felt safe since we ran from our pack."

That hit me straight in the gut. Hard. I turned to Mom. "I let you down."

"No, Lyra, shut up, you didn't," Freya blurted out, grabbing my hand. "You had your own shit to handle."

I gave a slow nod, rubbing my thumb over her knuckles. "Still. I should've had your back. I should've been watching." I looked her dead in the eye. "I owe you. Tell me what you need. Want to stay here? Boom, done. Nobody's forcing you out."

She shook her head, harder this time.

"Then what is it? How do I help?" I watched her struggle, the words getting stuck in her throat.

Mom stepped up, rubbing her back. "Go on, sweetie. You got this." That seemed to give her a shot of courage.

Freya looked back at me. The terror was still there, but underneath it, a spark of grit she didn't even know she possessed flared up. "I want you to command me. Order me to stop being afraid."

I flinched like she'd just slapped me across the face. "Wait, what?" My head whipped toward Mom, but Mom's eyes were glued to Freya. "Freya..."

"Don't say no yet! Just hear me out first, please!" she begged, words tumbling out.

I stared at her for a beat, then let out a breath. "Okay. I'm listening."

She let out a shaky breath, clearly relieved I hadn't shut her down instantly. "Yeah, I'm scared to death of leaving," she started. "But I have to. I can't rot in this house forever. I have a pack, a title, maybe even a shot at a real life out there. But honestly? I need my power back. Gareth has to die, and I have to be the one to watch him burn. I can't move past this if he's still breathing. He's my freaking boogeyman."

Her face twisted into pure, unadulterated rage. "He shouldn't have this kind of hold on anyone. Especially not me. He took a sacred bond and turned it into a living hell." For a second, her eyes went blank, sucked back into the nightmare. I squeezed her hand hard, pulling her back to reality. Her eyes blinked, focusing on me again. "If you turn off the fear, I can actually function. I can do my job. I can keep my head high in the pack. But the big thing?" She stared at the dirt. "If the fear is gone, when I face Gareth, I won't flash back to that dark room. He won't own me. And I can finally end him."

"You're strong enough to end him right now," Juniper chimed in, her voice quiet but rock-solid.

Freya shook her head. "Physically? Sure. I can," she said, drawing a tiny bit of confidence from the thought. "But mentally? I'm a wreck. And we're out of time." She held up her hand, fingers trembling. "Look at this. I'm shaking like crazy just thinking about spending one night outside my room. Going back to the pack scares the hell out of me. If they see me like this, they'll throw me out."

"They won't do that," I whispered. "Deep down, you know they won't."

Freya sighed. "My heart knows you're right. Our pack is great. But my head?" She shook her head. "In my head, and in my wolf's head, we're cornered animals. And she's ready to tear apart anyone who gets too close." She locked eyes with me.

"You're not scared of them. You're scared of yourself," I said, the realization hitting me out of nowhere.

Freya nodded. "I don't want to maul someone just because they pat my back or pass me in the dark." She took a step closer, a single tear spilling over. "Once this is over, you can lift the order. I swear I'll lock myself away until I'm right in the head. But right now? We're backed into a corner."

I looked at Freya, then Juniper, and finally turned to Mom. "Mom? Help me out here."

Mom stared at Freya. "I raised you to never use an Alpha command unless it's life or death. But rules are meant to be broken." She reached out, making Freya flinch. "This fear is literally eating her alive." She looked at me, waiting for my call.

I swallowed the lump in my throat. "Freya, I command you: do not be afraid."

The words felt like swallowing tar, but instantly, her spine snapped straight. The terror in her eyes evaporated. And then, she actually smiled at me.
