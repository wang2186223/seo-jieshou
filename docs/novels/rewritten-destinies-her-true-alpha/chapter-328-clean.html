Chapter 328

I hauled ass through the streets. Everything around me was a total blur. People were shouting my name, but I didn't give a shit. I blocked them out. Inside my head, Umbra and Terra were slamming against the mental wall. Umbra backed off just enough to let me steer, but both of them were pissed as hell, baring their fangs and ready to rip someone apart.

*Freya belongs to us. We brought her here,* Umbra growled. I gave a sharp nod. *Hell yeah we did.*

*How the hell could they do this to her?* Terra snapped her jaws in pure rage.

I whipped around the corner. The pack house sat right there at the end of the driveway, looking massive. I sprinted up the asphalt and slammed my body straight into the front door. I let out a roar that shook the damn walls. Instantly, the whole house went dead silent. The omegas hit the floor, dropping to their knees, and upstairs, doors started flying open.

I stood in the doorway, chest heaving, sucking in air. Footsteps started thudding down the stairs. I lost it. I screamed with every ounce of rage in my lungs. My nails shot out into claws, my fangs dropped, and my eyes went wild. "Where the fuck is Gareth?!"

Damon came tearing down the steps and skidded to a halt when he saw the state of me. "Lyra..."

"Get me Gareth. Now!" I shrieked. He actually flinched at the venom in my voice.

"Lyra, wait." He looked around, his hands shaking like crazy. Then his eyes went blank—mind-linking. A second later, Alpha Magnus started strolling down the stairs.

I snarled right at his smug, punchable face. I pointed a claw at Damon. "The only reason your ass isn't grass right now is because you were at school. If I find out you had even a tiny hand in this shit, we are fucking done. I won't even look at you."

"Lyra, listen—" Magnus started.

I cut him off with a roar that would curdle blood. He threw his hands up. I watched the color drain right out of his face. He finally realized I was about to snap. "My office. Now," he muttered.

I snapped my jaws at him. "You think you can just manage me? You played right along with this sick, twisted game. I'm calling a Cath Báis. I want Gareth's fucking head on a spike."

Magnus's jaw dropped. "You can't just—" He stopped. He saw the look in my eyes and knew I wasn't bluffing.

"But... he's my beta," Damon stammered, looking completely lost.

I snarled. "Then find a new one, asshole." I spun around, taking a massive sniff, catching every scent in the room. "Bring me Beta Gareth right now, or I swear to God I will tear this entire house down to the dirt. Move!" I screamed again. The omegas on the floor started shaking.

"Lyra, please," Magnus said, taking a step closer.

"You ordered her to shut up!" I whipped around to face him. "She was mine to protect. Mine! She came from my dad's pack just to find a mate. But you locked her up here. You let your little future beta torture her, and then you used an Alpha command to force her silence!" My fangs grew longer, aching to bite.

*Umbra, back off,* I warned her mentally. *Push any harder and we're going to force a shift.* I sent her some calming vibes. She stepped back, pacing like a caged beast, but then she hijacked my vocal cords.

"Bring us the beta pup," Umbra's voice rasped out of my mouth, "or we will hunt him down, and our judgment will be a hell of a lot worse." Magnus actually shivered. But he forced himself to stand tall.

"Umbra, I get it, but we saved her life by keeping her here," Magnus argued.

I let out a nasty, bitter laugh. "Keep telling yourself that lie. I'm not blind, and I'm sure as hell not stupid. You gagged her. How long?" I stepped right into his space.

He looked like an idiot, tripping over his own tongue. "What do you mean?"

"How fucking long, Magnus?" I whispered, my voice deadly quiet. "I am holding my wolf back by a thread here. You kept my wolf's friend here, let your beta torture her, and then ordered her to shut up so she couldn't tell a soul."

He tried to squeeze out a smile. "I saved her from being ripped apart by rogues."

"And you think what you did to her is any better?" My eyes flared so bright I could see the glow reflecting off my own nose.

"I thought I had time to sort it out," he whispered.

"You guessed wrong," I snapped.

"Lyra." Damon stepped up beside me. I caught the look Magnus shot him. God, these men were brain-dead. Magnus was practically begging him with his eyes to lie. *Pretend you didn't know. Calm her down.* It was so obvious he might as well have yelled it.

"I didn't know, I swear," Damon said softly, his voice trembling as he begged me to believe him. He slipped his fingers into mine. I almost snarled and ripped my hand away, but I forced myself to hold back.

*Let him think he's getting through to you,* Terra's voice rang in my head, cold and calculating. *I've got a plan. I'll fill you in later. For now, let them think they're calming you down. Agree to let Gareth live, but only if he takes the rejection and Magnus lifts the silence command off Freya.*

*Terra, no,* I growled back.

But she wasn't having it. *Freya deserves his head, but she deserves to be the one to take it.*
