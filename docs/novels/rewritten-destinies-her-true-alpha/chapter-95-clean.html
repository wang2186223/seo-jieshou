Chapter 95

I spun back to the mess. I grabbed the busted plastic bottle, chucked it in the bin, and swiped the mop over the wet floor.

"Since when do you work here?" Damon blurted, sounding totally blown away.

"I don't," I lied straight to his face, keeping my smile locked on. "Ronnie’s tight with my mom. We just came to grab some scent-blocker. But then Ronnie started asking about some old contact from my grandpa's pack, and boom—they took off. Left me holding the fort."

"Funny. I didn't see them walk out." Magnus spun around, shutting Damon up with a single, icy glare.

"Oh, my bad. Didn't know you were keeping tabs." I let out a dry chuckle, watching Damon flinch. "They slipped out the back door. That's where the truck is parked anyway. Mom rode with me, but Ronnie's dropping her back here later so we can head home."

"So where'd they head?" Magnus froze mid-shop. I slid behind the counter, praying to God I could figure out how to run this damn register.

"Said they needed supplies down the road," I shrugged, hopping onto the stool. "Might even have to drive all the way to my grandpa's pack for some rare ingredient. Apparently, he's whipping up a fresh batch of spray."

"You don't actually know, do you?"

I snorted. "Hey, she’s the mom, not me. Pretty sure you do plenty of stuff Damon has zero clue about."

Magnus locked eyes with me. "Yeah, but I'm the Alpha."

I cocked my head. "And you keep forgetting my mom is one too." I shrugged again. "Learned a long time ago to just let her do her thing."

Magnus gave a stiff nod and grabbed a few items. Damon, though? He saw his opening. He sauntered over, leaned his weight on the counter, and flashed his textbook player smile. "Hey, Lyra."

I knitted my brows. What the hell was his game? "Uh, hey, Damon."

"How've you been?" He locked his eyes onto mine, flexing his bicep on the counter. I did a slow, heavy blink to hide the massive eye-roll.

"Living the dream. You?" I forced a polite grin and glanced at Magnus. The guy was staring at us like a total creep. The second our eyes met, he looked away and pretended to shop again.

"Way better now that you're here."

Gross. I almost threw up in my mouth. I just nodded. "Oh, yeah? You need me to pass a message to Blair? Or did you lose your phone again and need me to play messenger?" I played dumb.

"What? No, babe. I just wanted to see you." Damon stood up straight, shooting a weird, tense look at his dad. Who, surprise surprise, was staring at us again.

"Why?" I leaned forward, propping my chin in my hands.

"Why what?" Damon stammered, trying to play it cool.

"Why did you want to see me?" I sat back up as he tried to lean in close. "We aren't friends. We're barely even acquaintances. We've spoken, what, three words to each other? And they were all about Blair. You know, my stepsister? The girl you're currently dating?" I smirked, watching him scramble for an excuse.

"Well, that's only because..." He choked on his words, glancing back at his dad. I watched Magnus send him a quick mind-link. Instantly, Damon turned back, his fake smile returning. "It's because you're so gorgeous, Lyra. You intimidated the hell out of me. I talked to Blair about you, and she swore you'd only notice me if we pretended to date. The whole thing was just a stunt to get your attention." He leaned back over the counter, getting in my space.

I leaned forward too, watching his grin grow wider and more cocky. I brought my face inches from his, stopping just a hair's breadth from his lips. I stared straight into his eyes. "Well, it's kind of hard to miss you when you and Blair are screwing for hours in the room right next to mine."

He flinched back, his smile instantly vanishing. Mine, however, stretched wide. "I mean, great stamina, dude. But seriously? Did you actually think those noises she made were real? It sounded like a cheap porn movie." I pushed myself off the counter, looking from Magnus to Damon.

"If you wanted my attention, banging my stepsister was a pretty brain-dead way to go about it. I want a mate who actually waited for me, just like I did for them." This time, I was sticking to my guns. In my past life, I let this crap slide. Look how that turned out. "Now. Can I actually help you guys buy something, or what?"
