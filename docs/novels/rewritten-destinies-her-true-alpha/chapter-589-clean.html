Chapter 589

Mom grabbed the newborn and bolted to the bathroom for a quick wash. I passed the baby boy over to Elara. She immediately started baby-talking him, and Rhys hovered right over her shoulder, practically glued to her. Man, they looked perfect. Just two parents totally obsessed with their kid.

Boom. Just like that, my own baby popped into my head. I could still smell him. Right before they tore him away from me. My eyes started stinging, so I looked away from the happy family. Daisy was right there, holding her hand out. I grabbed it, forcing a weak smile. "That obvious, huh?"

"To the rest of them? Nah," she said, shaking her head. "To me? Hell yeah." She yanked me in for a tight hug. "Look, it's okay to miss your kid."

I let out a heavy sigh. "I get it. But I've got way too much other crap on my plate right now."

"Bull," she countered. "Nothing comes before our babies. Period." She squeezed me tighter just as Mom walked back in. We both spun around to check out the new little girl. Damn, she was flawless, just like her brother. Only difference? Her blessing mark was on her right side. Jasper's was on the left.

"Meet your daughter," Mom said, handing baby number two over to the parents. She backed off to give them some room and slid in next to us. "Still gotta wait for the placenta, but she's totally out of the woods." Mom took a massive breath. I could tell she was gearing up to grill us, but then—*flash*.

The Celestia materialized right by the mattress. Elara started shaking like a leaf. "Please," she whimpered. "It's not time yet."

The Celestia just smiled. "I know. But I'm here for you this time."

Elara shot me a terrified look. "Why?" she gasped.

The Celestia ignored the question. She leaned over the little family, smiling softly. "They're absolutely perfect." She reached down, tracing the tiny star marks on their shoulders. "I'm not telling you what their blessings are yet." She tilted her head, locking eyes with Elara. "They'll figure that out when they grow up. But you? Damn, girl. You did amazing." She leaned in and kissed Elara's forehead. Instantly, three stars lit up on Elara's skin. "Tonight was a total shitshow, but you still did the right thing. Even when it absolutely crushed you." She stepped back.

Rhys nodded along, soaking in every word. "She's not lying." He planted a kiss on his mate's cheek. "You're the toughest woman on the planet."

Elara shook her head. "I'm just an Omega." She stared down at her twins, her voice dripping with pure misery. "They're gonna be just like us. Born to serve." Then, she froze. Her brain caught up to her mouth, and her eyes whipped over to me. "Oh god, Lyra, I didn't mean it like that."

"Hey, don't be scared to speak your mind," the Celestia sighed. "Your old pack put you through hell. Of course you're bitter." She tapped Elara's forehead. "But these kids aren't serving anybody. And neither are you." Her voice went dead serious, almost holy.

Elara didn't even hear her. She was still staring at me, terrified. I gave her a quick nod. "It's cool. My dad's pack is nothing like Magnus's crew."

Elara let out a breath and nodded, finally relaxing. But then, the Celestia's words registered. The whole room went dead silent. "Wait, what do you mean by that?" Elara squinted at the Celestia, then looked back at her babies. "We're Omegas. Omegas serve. That's just how it works."

"Who said they're Omegas? They literally just got here," the Celestia laughed.

"But we're both Omegas," Rhys argued, staring at the twins. "Two Omegas having ranked kids? That's basically impossible."

"Rare, yeah. But not impossible." The Celestia ran a finger over the star marks on the boy's shoulder. "So, what are we calling them?"

Elara smiled. "Jasper for the boy. Willow for the girl."

Rhys squeezed her shoulders tight. "Elara, you sure about that?"

Elara nodded hard. "Hell yeah, I'm sure. You threw away everything to save us. We're only breathing because of you. Of course I'm naming them after you." She leaned up and kissed him. "It's the least I can do to show some respect. I want them to know exactly who their dad is."

Rhys kissed her back, hard. "Thank you. Seriously, thank you. I love you so much." His voice cracked on the last word. A tear slipped down my cheek. I swiped it away real quick, but damn, I knew I'd never forget this.

"Great names," the Celestia said, taking a step back. "Just make sure you train them right when they grow up, Gamma Elara Meadow." And boom—she vanished.

"They're so pretty... wait, what the actual fuck did she just call me?" Elara's head whipped up. Honestly, the rest of us were just as clueless.

I took a sniff of the air and stepped closer to the mattress. "She said Gamma." I leaned down, putting my nose right against her sweaty forehead. "Gamma Elara Meadow."

"But I'm not a Gamma!" Elara gasped, jerking back, her eyes wide as saucers.

A massive grin spread across my face. "Well, you are now."
