Chapter 354

Celine smiled sarcastically at Carly's suspicion of her trailing them. "Carly, letting your imagination run wild is a problem too. This is a hospital; you should get that checked. It’s just a coincidence that we keep running into each other. You must still have feelings for Adam. I can’t help but think it’s more than I wherever we go!" Tracy promptly chimed in, "You and Mr. Alvarez are divorced. He doesn't want you anymore, so stop being thick-skinned and following him!"

Lucy was surprised to learn that Celine was a brilliant woman, but she thought it didn’t matter much. She chuckled, "Celine, I didn't expect you to be a genius. But you didn’t pursue a career after graduation. Instead, you married Mr. Alvarez and took on the role of a housewife. Now that he has divorced you, it seems like you feel lost and directionless. Instead of fixating on Mr. Alvarez and Carly, it’s time to focus on yourself and find a job. Financial independence is important for a woman."

Celine was at a loss for words. Just then, her new car pulled up. "Everyone, I'm simply waiting for my car here. I won’t be able to chat any longer. I'm leaving," she announced. Carly laughed, "Waiting for a car? A taxi? It’s hard to hail a taxi outside the hospital." Celine had a history of relying on taxis, which naturally led Carly to think of her in this way. Tracy cast a disdainful glance at Celine. "Celine, I have to say I truly look down on you. Just look at other geniuses; they all own homes and cars and are thriving. Yet here you are, still relying on taxis for transportation. You're bringing down the reputation of a genius!"

Lucy quickly intervened, "Forget it. That’s enough. Celine’s actually quite pitiful." Celine noticed her new car had come to a halt, and the driver exited and approached her. She moved past Tracy and Lucy, positioning herself directly in front of Carly. With a mischievous wink, she exclaimed, "Carly, you were absolutely right. It’s incredibly hard to hail a taxi around here."

Upon hearing that, Carly appeared pleased. Celine turned to Adam, who was seated in the Rolls Royce, and inquired, "Mr. Alvarez, would you give me a ride?" Carly's face went pale. "No way!" Celine stared at Carly. "If that’s the case, what are you going on about with all this nonsense?" Carly felt completely defeated. Adam glanced at Celine. It had been two days since he returned, and they hadn’t spoken. Today, her long, silky hair flowed gracefully around her, framing her stunning face that captivated anyone who laid eyes on her.

He remarked, "It’s indeed difficult to get a taxi around here." As he was wrapping up his words, the driver approached Celine and said, "Hello, Ms. Tate. I’ve picked up your new car for you. I took it for a test drive, and it performs wonderfully. It’s ready for you to drive now." Celine nodded, "Right. Thank you." After that, the driver left.

Carly was stunned. "New car? Celine, did you buy a car?" Tracy and Lucy were somewhat skeptical. They couldn’t help but question when Celine had purchased a new car. Celine retrieved the car key from her bag, making a ding sound, and was taken aback to see the luxury vehicle parked by the roadside. It was a Rolls Royce, resembling the Wraith Luminary Collection, specifically crafted for women.

"Celine, did you really buy a Rolls Royce? How did you manage to afford it?" Tracy exclaimed in disbelief. They were taken aback by Celine's unexpected reveal. Celine smirked and replied, "Take a guess." With that, she hopped into her new car, pressed the accelerator, and elegantly sped away.

Adam watched as Celine vanished from view, her hair dancing in the wind as she drove off. She looked stunning and outstanding, radiating an aura of confidence. He was struck by an incredible sense of familiarity with her, as if he recognized her from a past encounter. It dawned on him that she resembled a remarkable woman—the founder of Alpine Pharma, whom he had met briefly in Lapland.
