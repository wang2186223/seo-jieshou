Chapter 368

Total silence hit the table. Gideon snapped out of it first. "Whoa, hold up. How the hell do you pull that off?"

"No spoilers," I said, locking eyes with Gideon. "But I swear, I'll poof you all out of here in one go. Poof. Gone. Nobody will have a clue. And look, once the shit hits the fan and war breaks out, you'll get a roof over your heads, real jobs, and a spot in a pack that actually gives a damn about you. Rank won't mean squat."

"We're talking your old man's pack?" Gideon asked.

I gave him a firm nod. "Yep. Exactly."

Rhys shook his head, looking miserable. "No way. Magnus has us on a leash. He'll never let us walk."

I flashed a smirk. "Who cares? By the time he realizes his cage is empty, we'll be miles away."

That got Elara's attention. Her head snapped up. "Are you screwing with me, Lyra?" she whispered, her eyes suddenly shining with desperate hope. "Because if this is a joke, you need to kill it right now."

I threw an arm around her shoulders. "Hey, I'm dead serious. Why would I—" I stopped. My hand was resting on her waist, and then... *thump*. A tiny, faint flutter buzzed against my palm. My jaw dropped. I stared at her. "Oh my god. You're knocked up."

Elara's eyes went wide as saucers. "How... how did you feel that?"

Beside us, Rhys practically jumped out of his skin. "Wait, hold on. What did you just say?"

I winced, looking at Elara. "Shoot. My bad. I didn't mean to blab your secret."

"Not a secret," she whimpered, shaking like a leaf. "Just... pure, unadulterated terror."

Rhys pulled her close, wrapping her up. "Babe, why didn't you say anything?"

She just stared at him, speechless. Gideon cut in instead. "Come on, man, you know why. She's literally banned from having kids."

I leaned back, glaring. "What the hell does that mean?"

Gideon whipped out his phone, tapped the screen, and shoved a screenshot in my face. I sucked in a sharp breath. "You have got to be shitting me." I looked at the screen, then at him. "Is this a joke? Tell me this isn't real."

Elara peeked at the screen and broke down, tears spilling over. "It's real," she sobbed.

Rhys looked totally lost. He snatched the phone from Gideon and scanned the text. *Clack.* The phone slipped from his fingers and hit the table. I could see the brutal fight inside him—his inner omega screaming to submit, while his mate instincts screamed to fight. "Fine," he choked out. "We just won't have babies. Ever."

"Too late for that," Elara said, shivering. "I've already got a bun in the oven, Tern. And I can't even go to the clinic. The second Magnus gets wind of this, he's going to make us pay."

"Pay? How?" I demanded.

Gideon looked like he was about to puke. He grabbed his phone, swiped to another picture, and shoved it back into my hands. A deep, nasty growl ripped out of my chest. Inside my head, Umbra and Terra went from lazy mode to straight-up bloodlust. They wanted Magnus dead, like, yesterday. "Tell me this is a sick joke," I hissed.

"Hey there, folks!" The waitress popped out of nowhere. "Can I grab you guys anything else?"

I forced a massive breath down, plastered on a fake smile, and turned to her. "Actually, yeah! It's our girl's birthday, and we want to celebrate. Let's do cake for the table. What's the best slice you got?"

"Our coconut white cake is to die for," she beamed.

I glanced at Elara, but she was basically melting into her seat. I shot a quick look at Rhys.

"Coconut's a no-go, she's allergic," Rhys cut in. "But if you have carrot cake, she's obsessed with that."

The waitress's face lit up. "Oh, our carrot cake is killer! I barely pitch it because people get weird about veggies in dessert, you know?" She chuckled. I squeezed out a fake laugh to match. "Alright, four slices of carrot cake it is. Any coffee with that?"

"Nope, we're good. Just the cake and the check, please. Thanks a ton." I kept the smile glued on until she walked off, then let my face drop instantly. I turned back to Gideon, waving his phone in his face. I gripped the edge of the table so hard the wood literally groaned. "Tell me this is fake. Right now."

Gideon avoided my eyes. Beside me, Elara let out a wet sniffle. Rhys reached for the phone again, but Elara snatched his wrist. "Please, babe. Don't look at it."

He stared at her, then whipped his panicked eyes to me. "I have to see it," he said. His voice was barely a whisper, but it was dead serious. He was going to lose his mind if he didn't know.

"Please, Tern. Just don't," Elara begged, her voice cracking.

But he just turned, framing her face with his hands. "I have to see, babe. I have to know."

"Why?" she sobbed.

"Because if you vanish out of nowhere and I'm left in the dark, it'll literally kill me. If they take you and I don't know why, how am I supposed to go get help?" He swallowed hard. "Look, I get it. I'm an omega. I'm never going to be the big, tough mate who can bust down doors and fight off an army for you. But damn it, I will find a way to keep you safe." He pressed his forehead against hers. "Even if it kills me, I'm getting you out."
