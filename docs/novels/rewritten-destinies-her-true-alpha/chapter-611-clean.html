Just like that, the glowing star on Orion's forehead went dark. Finnian pulled back, his knees hitting the floor. "But why trust me? I fell asleep at the wheel. I let those bastards plot right under my nose."

Orion grabbed his shoulder, stopping him from bowing any lower. "Hey, nobody's perfect at reading people." I shot my dad a smug look and grinned. Dad pointed a finger at me, letting out a low, warning growl. I threw my hands up like, *my bad*, but I got up anyway and walked over to Finnian.

I offered him a hand. "Yeah, we screw up, but the Celestia makes damn sure we know who's got our backs." I tapped the exact spot where Orion's star had just burned. *Whoosh.* My own forehead caught fire as I hauled Finnian up. "See? The big boss upstairs always rewards loyalty." I gave him a quick wink. Finnian stared, his eyes popping out of his head.

"Holy shit... you're Celestia-Blessed." Finnian’s hand shook as he reached up, barely touching the air over my mark. "I never thought I'd see another chosen one before I kicked the bucket."

"You lying pieces of shit!" Elder shrieked. He pointed a trembling finger right at my face. "No way in hell would the Celestia pick you!"

I turned around, real slow, letting my mark blast them right in the eyes. "Ain't that funny."

"What's so damn funny?" Caspian spat out.

"Just hilarious how you guys ditch the Celestia, then turn around and try to tell her how to do her job." That shut them up real quick. Their jaws dropped. I grinned right at their death stares, then looked over at Dad. "We good to go?"

Dad strolled over to the desk and dropped into the leather chair. He leaned back, giving the council a glare cold enough to freeze hell over. Any normal wolf would've pissed themselves. But I caught his hands shaking. He was faking it—still hurting, just putting on a tough show. He smirked, yanked open a drawer, and said, "Done and done. Gideon Stone came through with the goods." He tapped the desk. The black glass surface lit up. A projector whirred, and a massive screen slid down the back wall. Dad swiped the screen, and the display flared to life.

"What the hell is that?" Evan asked, finally looking like he was sweating.

Rhys rolled his eyes hard. "Who cares?" He glared at my dad. "For a dead guy, you look pretty damn lively."

Elder kicked a chair out of the way. "You played us! You lied to the whole damn kingdom!"

Dad’s grin turned nasty, like a wolf cornering a rabbit. "You've got some balls getting mad at me for lying." He tapped the screen, pulling up a bunch of documents and photos. "Is this you, Elder?"

Elder tripped backward, hitting the wall as his dirty laundry flashed on screen. "This... this is—"

I tilted my head back, showing off my matching mark.

More gasps echoed, but then Rhys started laughing. His cackle filled the room, making my guys growl. "What's so damn funny?" Orion growled, taking a threatening step forward.

Rhys wiped his mouth, trying to choke back his giggles. "I mean, this is just fucking beautiful."

And since you're all trapped in this room... we can wipe you all out in one go. Boom. No more King, no more whore Queen, and no more of you nosey bastards." He laughed again, a nasty sound. "Our spears are gonna paint these walls with your blood. Not even your precious Celestia can save you now."
