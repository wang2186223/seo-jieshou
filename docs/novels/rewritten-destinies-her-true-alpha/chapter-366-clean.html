Chapter 366

The table went dead silent. Gideon leaned over, eyes locked on me. "Cut the crap. You don't have a kid."

"Yeah, I know," I muttered, letting out a heavy breath. "But my bloodline is loaded. Blessed by two different goddesses, actually. I'm straight-up royalty. My wolf—"

*Hold it,* Umbra snapped in my head, shaking her head. *Don't tell them about us. They're cool, but keep your cards close.*

*They swore an oath,* I shot back.

But Terra chimed in, backing Umbra up. *She's right. Don't blow their minds. Just play the magic card for now.*

"My wolf comes from a goddess," I pivoted, taking a swig of my drink. "And my witch side? Hand-delivered by the Triple Goddess herself. And yeah, that comes with a pretty sweet perk."

Elara swallowed her last bite of steak. "What kind of perk are we talking about?"

I spun my butter knife on the table. "A do-over. Courtesy of my dad's side."

Gideon shoved his empty plate aside, staring. I just nodded. "Yep. A literal second chance."

"See, I've already done this whole life thing once," I said, my hand starting to shake as I grabbed my glass. "I was twenty-four. My mate strapped me down to a cold metal table. Had his doctor rip my baby right out of my stomach. Why? Because Blair told him I cheated. Said the kid wasn't his." I took a hard gulp. "Total lie, obviously. I was loyal. But he broke our bond, claimed my stepsister, and murdered my baby." I scanned their shocked faces. "I died right there, cursing his name. Next thing I know, I wake up on my eighteenth birthday. The exact day I was supposed to meet him. So I bolted. Ran straight to my dad's pack to get strong enough to fight back."

"Who was the guy?" Elara whispered.

A cold smile crept onto my face. "Damon. He was my mate. He screwed my sister and killed my kid. So this time? I'm taking him down. Him, and everything he loves." My blood was boiling.

Gideon looked totally lost. "Then why the hell are you sleeping with the enemy?"

I almost laughed. "Because the king and my dad needed a mole to crack Magnus's firewalls. Damon was my golden ticket. Plus, Magnus and his kid are greedy bastards. They want cash and power, and I've got truckloads of both."

Gideon's eyes flared with sudden interest. He leaned closer. "Why spill all this to us? My entire job is keeping hackers out of that exact system."

"Exactly," I nodded, locking eyes with him. "Like I said, we can help each other. And honestly? You're drowning."

Gideon sucked in a sharp breath. "What do you know?"

"Nothing. But I can read your face. You look more desperate every single day. Terrified. You need a way out, and you've got nobody to turn to." I reached out, grabbing his hand across the table and giving it a firm squeeze. "Let me help you. I swear I've got your back, even if you can't do a damn thing for me. No strings attached."

He shivered, his voice cracking. "My little girl is sick. I told you that. Magnus is holding her meds over my head."

Elara gasped. "Are you kidding me? How can he do that?"

I let out a dark, humorless laugh. "Magnus will leverage anything to keep people under his thumb." I turned back to Gideon. "What's she got?"

"The doctors call it the withering."

My stomach dropped. "How old is she?"

"Ten," he choked out, shaking all over. "She's only ten."

I leaned back, shutting my eyes. I flipped through my mental grimoire, searching. No way. The withering doesn't hit kids that old. "She's way past the age for that."

"I know," Gideon said. "She was totally fine, super healthy when she was born. It only started when we moved here."

My eyes snapped open. "Wait. What?"

Gideon frowned, confused. "She got sick right after we switched packs. The doc said losing our family in the attack probably triggered it. Said she was just fading away from grief."

"How old was she when you guys moved?" I finally found the page in my head.

"Around five," he said, staring at me. "Why? What's going on?"

"The withering only targets infants. Not toddlers, not five-year-olds. But..." I trailed off, scanning the mental text. It was a spell. Looked just like the withering. "There's a hex. Mimics the symptoms perfectly. But the caster has to do it in person. Takes a full hour. And here's the kicker—it has to be topped off every single month with a specific elixir."
