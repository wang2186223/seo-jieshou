NovelVibe

Chapter 452

Elara smirked, her eyes crinkling with pure, evil joy.

*Karma's a bitch, ladies.*

They deserved every bit of this for ganging up on Lyra. Like she didn't know Vivienne and Beatrice pulled the strings on Harland and Winslow. Hell, they probably got the Wynns in on it too. All just to screw with Lyra. Fine. They wanted a fight? Let’s see who plays dirtier.

"Elara..." Vivienne choked out, her face red and eyes watering with pure rage.

This was a joke. Judge what? She’d lost, plain and simple. Elara was just rubbing salt in the wound, treating her like a toy to make Lyra look good. And the bet? Crawling out of here on her hands and knees? No freaking way. Not happening.

Beatrice looked like she’d just swallowed a toad. Lyra could play? And not just play, but absolutely crush it? Since when did a hillbilly girl have those kinds of chops?

"Gustav, what's the verdict?" Alexandra chimed in, seizing the moment.

Up front, a fifty-something guy in a slick designer suit bolted out of his chair. He actually knocked the seat over, but didn't even blink. His eyes were bloodshot, staring at Lyra, his mouth twitching. The guy looked like he’d just seen Jesus. He was looking at her like she was some kind of goddess.

"Ma—" he started, the word slipping out.

But Lyra shot him a freezing glare, locking him down instantly. He shut his mouth.

Alexandra frowned at Gustav’s red, sweaty face. He rubbed his chin, trying to play it cool and act like a professional again. She looked back and forth between him and Lyra. "So, Gustav? Give us the official call. Who’s better? Lyra or Vivienne?"

Gustav snuck a look at Lyra, his voice shaking. "I... honestly, I can't judge this."

Beatrice’s face lit up. She grabbed Vivienne’s arm and hissed, "Oh, my god. Vivienne, think about it. Gustav loves you. He’s been dying to mentor you. He’s totally covering for you!"

Vivienne’s heart did a flip. Oh, wow. Yes. Gustav was obsessed with her talent. Sure, she’d gotten wrecked up there, but if he refused to judge, he was throwing her a lifeline. One word from him and she wouldn't have to crawl. She stared at him, practically begging with her eyes.

"So, Gustav," Beatrice piped up, winking hard at him. "By 'can't judge,' you mean that girl just copied Vivienne's style, right? Like, a cheap knockoff can't beat the real deal. So Vivienne still wins on actual skill, yeah?" She kept blinking, basically screaming: *help us out, and she'll sign on as your student tonight.*

Gustav stared at her ridiculous, twitching face and burst out laughing. He looked at the two of them like they were absolute clowns.
