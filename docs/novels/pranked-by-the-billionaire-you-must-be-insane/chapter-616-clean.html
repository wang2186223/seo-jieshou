Chapter 623

Hell no.

She was already skating on thin ice with the Lancasters. If they thought she had a hand in this mess? Game over.

Panic hit her like a truck. She threw off the covers, scrambled out of bed, and tripped her way over to the vanity.

The girl in the mirror looked like trash. Pale, stiff, hair like a rat's nest, eyes totally bloodshot. Yeah, she looked sick, alright. But nope, still not pathetic enough.

She snatched a makeup wipe and aggressively rubbed her eyes. She had to get rid of that angry, pissed-off redness. Then she slapped her own cheeks, hard. She kept at it until every last drop of color drained from her skin, leaving her face and lips completely white.

Perfect. She looked like a ghost. She grabbed a loose cardigan, threw it over her shoulders to look extra fragile, and forced herself to limp out the door.

She hit the top of the stairs, and boom—the living room was buzzing. The Lancasters were down there, losing their minds.

"I mean, seriously, I still can't process this. Lyra is actually Aurelia? The Aurelia?" That was Genevieve, sounding totally blown away.

"Haha! Told you guys! My grandbaby is a straight-up genius!" Reginald bragged, practically bursting with pride. "She's Queen! She started a massive global luxury brand! And she wasn't even fifteen when she built the Queen Group! Honestly, she makes this old man look like a total slacker!"

Edward let out a loud laugh. "It's insane, man. The kid is a beast. Like, is there anything she actually sucks at?"

"She's a miracle..." Eleanor's voice cracked, tears welling up. "If we hadn't lost her back then, she would've... oh, she would've been..."

A literal genius, treated like garbage by those awful Wynns, stuck suffering in some backwater town since she was a kid. And she still pulled off something this huge.

If she’d just grown up with them, with all their money and support... she wouldn't have had to go through all that hell. The thought of it literally broke Eleanor's heart.

"Hey, come on, we should be celebrating her. Let's not ruin the mood with those trashy Wynns," Frederick said softly, rubbing his wife's shoulder. "Wipe those tears. The fact that she made it big even in the middle of nowhere just shows she never quit on herself."

The whole family was riding this massive high, just hyping Lyra up non-stop.

Up on the landing, Vivienne stood frozen, pure, toxic jealousy burning right up her throat.

She had literally busted her ass, crying and bleeding to build Aurevion Jewelers from scratch, fighting like hell to make it a luxury name.

Every rich snob in high society used to kiss her feet. They called her a twenty-two-year-old prodigy, the genius Marington College student who somehow ran a top-tier brand on the side.

But her own family? They barely gave her a pat on the back. They had never, not once, lost their minds over her achievements like this.

Why? Why did they only treat Lyra like some kind of god?

Vivienne squeezed her fists so hard her nails dug into her palms.

That country bumpkin! If she grew up in the sticks, why couldn't she just stay in her lane and act like a grateful peasant?

Why did she have to ruin Vivienne's life and stomp on her at every single turn?

First, she got absolutely wrecked by Lyra on the piano.

And now, the jewelry brand she literally built her entire identity on was getting crushed under Lyra's boots.

Aurevion Jewelers wasn't even in the same league as the Queen Group. It was a joke compared to them.

The sheer humiliation made her dizzy. Her knees gave out, and she slammed hard against the wooden banister.

Downstairs, the happy chatter cut out instantly.

Every single head snapped up to look at her.

"Vivienne?" Eleanor jumped up. "Oh my god, why are you out of bed? We told you to rest, sweetie. If you needed something, you should've just called a maid."

Wobbling on her feet, Vivienne put on her best, most pathetic smile and looked down at her mom.
