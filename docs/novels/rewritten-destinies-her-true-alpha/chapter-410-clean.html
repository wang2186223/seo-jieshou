Chapter 410

Magnus was screaming behind us. I didn't care. I shoved Terra, forcing her to move faster. Then, boom—the first trap hit. It started sucking our energy like a vacuum. Shit. I forgot to carve the damn rune on Terra. My wolf brain was screaming at me to run, to just get the hell out of there, but we wouldn't make it ten feet without that rune. I had to do it now.

"Lyra, run!" Umbra shrieked in my head. I ignored her and forced Terra’s legs to lock up. I yanked our leg forward, popped a claw, and sliced right into the flesh. Heavy footsteps were thudding right behind us. "Lyra, seriously! It’s not just Magnus! We gotta move!" Umbra was losing her mind, trying to force us forward, but I held her down.

"Shut up and trust me," I snapped. "We don't do this, we're dead." I glanced back. Magnus burst through the brush, cutting off our escape. A nasty grin spread across his face, but then he froze, staring at what I was doing.

"What the hell are you doing?" Magnus took a step closer, sniffing the air as the smell of fresh blood hit him. His eyes locked onto my bleeding leg. "If you wanted to die so bad, you should've just let us drain you. Why kill my guard just to come out here and mutilate yourself?"

I let out a sharp, nasty laugh. Almost done. Just had to buy a few more seconds. "You really think I’d give you the satisfaction?" He flinched like I’d slapped him across the face. I dragged the claw down again, stepping closer.

"Stop that!" he growled, watching my blood drip into the dirt.

"Why? If I'm spilling blood, I'd rather feed the dirt than give you bastards a single drop." I snarled, and Magnus’s eyes went dead cold.

"Who the hell are you?" he muttered. Suddenly, Gideon Stone and Damon stepped out from the shadows of the trees.

I chuckled. "Oh, look. The three musketeers. You really think you three are enough to stop me?"

They actually hesitated. Damon recovered first, letting out a mocking laugh. "You seriously think you can take us?"

I laughed right back, slicing another line into my leg. One more cut and I was golden. "I could put you in the dirt in thirty seconds, kid. You're not even a speed bump to me."

Damon lost it and lunged, but Magnus grabbed his collar, holding him back. Magnus was staring at me, his eyes scanning, calculating. Damon screamed and tried to break free again, but Magnus held tight. "Cool it," Magnus barked.

Damon stopped thrashing and glared at his dad. "You seriously think she can beat me?" I couldn't help but laugh out loud. "Shut the fuck up!" Damon screamed, spinning on me.

"Aw, is the little puppy crying because daddy knows he's weak?" I watched his face twist from pissed off to pure, unadulterated rage. Magnus kept a tight grip on him while I just laughed. "Too bad. I would've loved watching the light leave your eyes, Damon."

The name hit them like a physical blow. They all froze. Then Damon shook his head, trying to play it cool. "Nice try. You just heard them say my name back in the clearing."

I just grinned, dragging my claw through the final line of the rune. Instantly, the draining feeling stopped. I let out a breath, stood up on all fours, and flashed them a wicked smile. "Well, this has been great, boys. But I gotta bounce."

Gideon Stone stepped forward, a flicker of panic crossing his face. "I don't think so, sweetheart." He tried to hide it, but he was sweating.

I let my tongue hang out, grinning like a maniac. "Oh, I think so." I looked at Magnus and Damon. "Catch ya later." I winked, spun around, and bolted. I headed straight for the mating ceremony field. I knew the pack streets like the back of my hand. The woods were a deathtrap anyway.

I let Umbra take the wheel. I stepped back, channeling our magic straight into Terra's paws. Umbra wanted to howl with pure joy—feeling the wind in our fur again was heaven—but she kept her mouth shut.

We were flying. I heard Gideon eating our dust behind us, but then came a heavy thud. "What the actual fuck?" Gideon yelled back to Magnus. "Something's wrong."

Magnus rushed to his side as Gideon collapsed. "What is it?"

Gideon was shaking, sweating bullets. "I'm empty. I feel totally drained." He stared up at Magnus, terrified. "I don't know what's happening."

I skidded to a stop a block away. Far enough to have a solid head start, but close enough for them to hear me loud and clear. "Your witch isn't the only one who knows tricks, boys!" I yelled. Then I took off into the night.
