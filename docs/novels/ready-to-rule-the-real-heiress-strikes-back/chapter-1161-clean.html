Chapter 1161

"I can't take it in here anymore!" The words barely left Rosie's mouth before Benjamin just about lost it. His face twisted, his hands trembled. This girl... She hadn't learned a thing. She'd messed up so bad and dragged everyone down with her. Now she wanted him to take the blame? Did she think he had no pride left at all? If he, the Chief of Protocol, landed in jail, everything would be finished.

"You... You're unbelievable! Your mother and I spoiled you too much, and look where it's gotten us. You made a huge mistake. Now sit in here and think about what you've done." His voice shook with anger as he turned away, grabbing Mandy's arm and leading her out, ignoring Rosie's frantic cries.

For once, Mandy didn't look back. Rosie had truly let her down. Everything Rosie did was for herself. She never cared about what her parents might lose.

Behind them, Rosie's voice grew shrill. "Mom, don't leave! You love me most! Mom, please... Can't you help me? Dad doesn't care about me at all! Don't go! Don't leave me here, please!"

Outside the police station, Mandy lifted her head, staring up at the sky. She wondered if she'd been wrong all along. What had all those years of spoiling Rosie really gotten her?

She thought of Lydia. Mandy hated her right now, but there was no denying it— Lydia was the same age as Rosie, yet so much more mature, so much more impressive.
