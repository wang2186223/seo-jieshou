Chapter 628

I shoved through the barrier. Boom—my mind slammed back into Umbra right as she crushed a wolf's skull like a ripe melon. Chunky brain-matter splashed straight down my throat. I jerked back, hacking and spitting. I kept my eyes up, refusing to look at the mess while I dry-heaved. Umbra let out a snort in my head. I gagged again. "You did that on purpose, you jerk," I wheezed.

*Nope,* Umbra shrugged. *But hey, didn't think you'd pop back so fast.* I took a breath, shaking it off, and spun toward the crew. Juniper and Rhys were hovering over Zahra, checking her for scratches. "Yo, how long was I out?" I asked.

Umbra ignored them and looked away. *Under five minutes,* she linked. *Rhys and I easily trashed those four mutts that tried to sneak up. But the shitshow by the pack house is getting way worse. Oh, and Dad called for us while you were gone.*

"We good to move?" I yelled to the three of them. They snapped their heads around. Zahra pushed herself up, took a couple of shaky steps, and nodded. "Let's go get our kid." Rhys gave a firm nod. I turned my eyes to Juniper.

"Stick with your folks," I said, pointing toward the main building. "I've gotta carve a path to the pack house." Juniper bit her lip, looking completely split. "No way, we stick together."

I bumped her shoulder with mine. "Can't do it. Your job is to find Daisy and your brother. Mine is to get Orion and my folks." She stepped right into my space. "I'm coming with."

I shoved her back toward her mom and dad. "They need you, period. If I spot Daisy or the others, I'll ping you. But if you tag along with me, you'll just be freaking out about your parents the whole damn time."

"Juniper!" a choked voice barked out. We spun around. A scruffy tan wolf was hauling ass toward us. Honestly, the only reason I didn't rip his throat out was the glowing moon mark on his forehead. I jumped back, thinking it was her brother. But then he started licking her face, and I realized—oh, wait, that's Kael.

I cocked my head. "Wait, how'd you even know it was her? I can't smell a damn thing." The two of them looked at me. "We shifted right next to each other before the chaos started," Juniper said, letting out a breathless laugh. "Thank God, too." She rubbed her nose against his. "Otherwise, how the hell would I find him in this mess?"

I let out a massive breath I didn't even know was stuck in my chest. "Alright, you four stick like glue. I'm out." I turned to bolt, but Juniper shrieked, "Hold on!" and threw her body right in front of me, cutting me off.

I tripped over my own paws trying not to plow right into her. "Juniper, seriously? I have to go save my parents." She groaned. "I get it. But if you won't let me tag along, you're taking Kael."

I snorted. "Oh, come on. If you're gonna stress about your parents, you'll be sweating bullets over Kael." She shook her head hard. "Nope. No, I won't." She gestured to the three behind us. "He's a Royal Guard. The guy is built for war, and he'll have you." She took another step closer. "And I know damn well you'll do whatever it takes to keep him breathing."

"Juniper... I'm going straight into the meat grinder." I turned and pointed toward the pack house. Thick black smoke was billowing up from some fire. But the real problem was the brutal snarls, the agonizing screams, and the massive, swirling knot of wolves tearing each other apart. It was going to be a bloodbath just to get through. "You're seriously telling me you're cool with your mate diving into that hellhole, with nobody but me watching his back, while you go look for your brother?"

She looked me dead in the eye. "Yeah. I am. You're my Queen, and I'd trust you with my own life. Why wouldn't I trust you with his?" I stared at her, a sudden wave of warmth hitting my chest. "You really trust me that much?" I asked, my voice catching a bit.

She nodded. "If I can't be there, Kael's got your back." She whipped around to him. "Keep her alive." He let out a low chuckle. "With my life, babe." He gave her face one last lick. "Meet up with us when you can."

Juniper nodded, spun around, and jogged back to her folks. "Let's move!" The three of them bolted toward the edge of the field. Their plan was to flank around and check on the pack.

I looked at Kael. "You good with this?" He laughed. "Hell yeah, my Queen. You're the one who trained her to fight. Plus, she's got her folks, and they're steering clear of the hot zone." He nudged my shoulder. "Come on, let's go get your parents."

We took off, but a sudden thought hit me, and I veered left. "Wait, we need to hit one spot first." "Why's that?" he asked. I liked that he questioned me. A guard with blind faith is a dead guard.

"Because I saw Orion over this way when I got a bird's-eye view. I want him with us before we push to the main house." I threw a quick prayer to the goddesses. *Keep him breathing, please.* Suddenly, Terra burst out of the tree line. *He's still there,* she linked. *Shadow is waiting for you.*

Umbra flooded our legs with pure power. Kael was panting hard next to me, struggling to keep up. "Holy shit, you're fast," he grunted as we cleared pile after pile of dead bodies. I let out a breathless laugh, but it died instantly. Up ahead, my blood turned to ice. There was a pack of them, and right in the middle was Orion, barely hanging on.
