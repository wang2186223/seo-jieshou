Chapter 625

Terra was holding out on me. "What the hell are you hiding?" I ducked a swipe, but my wolf didn't say a damn thing. I gave her a minute. Nothing. She wouldn't even look at me. "Screw it. Keep your secrets. I'm doing this my way." Her head whipped around. Umbra let out a low growl.

I tuned them both out and lunged at the wolf blocking my path. Just needed to cripple him to get through. Terra growled in my head. *You're wasting time.*

"And you're a liar. We swore years ago we were done with the BS." I grabbed the bastard's leg and yanked. *Snap.* I dropped him like trash and bolted toward Juniper. "It's all or nothing. You don't get to demand blind trust when you're playing secret agent. Either you trust me to handle my business, or you don't." I rammed into the pack swarming Juniper and her mom, snapping jaws at their ankles. "Make up your mind before I make it for us."

Terra shot a look at Umbra. My Lycan just shook her head. *Trust her. Or don't. Your funeral.*

She hesitated for a split second. Then, boom. The mind-link slammed into my brain. Pure chaos. Everyone was screaming. My dad was frantically searching. *AMY, TALK TO ME.*

I froze. Just for a second. Then I grabbed a wolf by the spine and snapped it like a twig. "You hid this from me?!" I clamped my jaws onto another wolf's tail, tasting hot copper as I ripped half of it clean off.

*I—* Terra tried to squeeze an excuse in, but I howled over her.

"YOU HAD NO DAMN RIGHT!"

*I needed you to trigger the spell,* Terra cried.

"Then you should've trusted me!" Tears burned my eyes, but I shook them off. "You stabbed me in the back when everyone's lives were on the line."

Terra flinched. *I just wanted you to believe in yourself. The way I do.*

"Well, you went about it like an idiot," I growled, pissed off. I took a split second to scan my brain for a spell to tweak. Found one instantly. "Desig nocta il orta nu." I dumped every ounce of my new juice into it. The shockwave didn't just ripple; I literally saw it. A blinding white ring blasted out from my chest, sending wolves flying like ragdolls. Trees bent backward as the wave tore through the woods. The screaming died down for a second. I seized the moment. *Silvermoon and allies, listen up! Look for the moon marks. If they don't have one, shred them!*

*AMY!* My dad's voice roared in my head. *Where the hell are you?*

*Pushing through to Juniper. I'm fine. Stay alive, I'll find you.*

My dad snorted. *That's my line, kid.*

Someone else chimed in. *Are we sure about this? I can't smell a damn thing.*

I tapped into my Gamma juice, sending a wave of pure calm through the link. *Someone's messing with our noses with magic. Trust the moons. I put them there. Trust me, and you won't hit our own.*

A howl echoed through the clearing. Further off, someone let out a frustrated, angry shriek.

*Someone's trying to break our spell,* Terra said. She sounded proud, but her voice was hurting. *But they can't. It's too damn strong.*

"You wanted me to see how strong we've gotten, didn't you?" I already knew the answer.

*Yes,* she whispered.

"An active ambush is not the time to lock me out just to prove a point," I growled. But then a thought hit me. "Umbra... use some magic. Cleanse whatever spell is messing with Terra right now."

Umbra stalked toward Terra. Terra backed away. *I'm fine. No spells on me.*

Umbra kept coming. *Exactly what a brainwashed wolf would say.* Umbra nudged her and muttered a cleansing chant. Terra shrieked. Right on cue, another scream echoed from near the pack house.

Terra dropped like a stone, but I didn't have time for her. Juniper's mom screamed again. I had to get there now. I threw a quick spell to blast the crowd out of my way and dove into the fray. Juniper was bleeding, limping on three legs but still biting. Her mom was flat on her stomach, panting, trying to drag herself up. A jagged bone was sticking out of her back leg—totally shattered—but she was still trying to shield her pup.

"Stay down!" I stood right over her, pinning her to the dirt. "Heal up. I've got Juniper's back." She let out a ragged sob of relief and collapsed.

"Thank you," she wheezed, her strong voice reduced to a gravelly groan.

Juniper flipped a wolf over, spun to check on her mom, and locked eyes with me. Relief washed over her face. "Lyra!"

"Watch your mom. Yell if anyone gets close." She nodded. "We move when you're patched up." She limped over to her mother, holding her bad paw up. I gave her neck a quick nudge. "Glad you're still breathing." Then I spun around and clamped my jaws onto the throat of a wolf trying to sneak up behind us. The pack closed in again.

"Be careful!" Juniper yelled.

I grinned, showing off a mouth full of bloody teeth. "This is where the fun starts."
