"Drink up and get some rest," he said. "We'll grab something to eat, then I'll take you home."

Lydia was wiped out, but no matter how tired she got, she could never just fall asleep. Her eyes felt heavy, though.

"I have to go back to campus tonight," she said. "Need to pack up my things first."

Lydia let out a soft sigh and nodded. "That feels really good."

Connor grinned. "You know you're exhausted, but you still insist on handling everything yourself?"
