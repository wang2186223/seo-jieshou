Chapter 88

"So, you two tight?" Ronnie pivoted, locking his eyes on me.

Freya didn't even let him finish. She shook her head fast. "Hell no. I asked for a transfer here without a clue that her mom ended up in Oakenfang." She didn't look at me. She didn't look at my mom. Her eyes were glued straight to Ronnie.

Ronnie cocked his head, suspicious. "Yeah? Then why'd you two look like besties walking in?"

Freya let out a soft laugh. "Beta, come on. If you looked out the window, you’d see I kept my distance from Alpha Lyra and Beta Finnian. I only followed her inside because, well, it’s her house. And you're giving me a bunk while I hunt for my mate." She shot me a look packed with pure, quiet venom. "Alpha Lyra and I play nice, but friends? No way." She looked back at Ronnie and bowed her head. "I’m just following my Alpha's orders."

Boom. Just like that, she dumped the whole thing on my dad. Zero ties to me. Ronnie’s suspicion? Gone. Honestly, that worked for us.

Ronnie relaxed his shoulders, then snapped his gaze back to me. "Lyra?"

"Yeah, Ronnie?"

I watched his temper flare. He hated that. He’d told me a thousand times to call him *Beta* Ronnie. But he shut his mouth because I’d already made it clear: he calls me Alpha Lyra, or we have a problem.

He ground his teeth, turning back to Freya. "Fine. Blair’s gonna show you around. We got some new blood while Lyra was gone. You get the lay of the land, Lyra gets to unpack."

I glanced at Freya. She didn't look back, just gave a tiny, almost invisible nod.

"Works for me," I said. "I'm beat anyway. Need to scrub my room and wash Blair’s nasty stench off my clothes. Don't want Damon getting any more dumbass ideas about me being his mate."

Ronnie’s jaw went rigid. "Right."

"Besides, Blair’s probably throwing a party right now," I added.

Right on cue, a low growl rumbled from the hallway.

"What's that supposed to mean?" Ronnie barked, eyes darting toward the door where Blair was hiding.

"I mean, Damon’s her guy," I said, walking over to the fridge. "But he tried to claim me—her stepsister. Now that the air is clear, she can have her little prize back." I smirked, pulling the fridge door open.

"Shut your damn mouth!" Blair burst into the kitchen, fuming.

I ignored her, rummaging for food. Inside, I was laughing my ass off. Blair was so damn easy to read. She hadn't learned to hide her tells yet. That came later.

"You think you're hot shit, don't you?" Blair sneered. "But you can't even shift. God, you're pathetic. An Alpha who can't even pull out her wolf."

Deep inside, Umbra pushed forward. *Who says?*

"What?" Blair blinked.

I stood up, keeping my back to them, and slammed the fridge shut. "I said, who says I can't shift?"

Blair snorted. Ronnie let out a dry chuckle.

"You've been gone a few months, Lyra," Blair mocked. "You were a joke of a wolf then, and you're a joke now."

I heard Ronnie grunt in agreement.

I turned around.

The room went dead silent. Gasps cut through the air.

My face was half-shifted—snout elongated, jaw sharp. My teeth had lengthened into lethal fangs. My eyes glowed with Umbra’s pure gold. I held up my hands. Razor-sharp claws tipped my fingers, and thick black fur coated my arms all the way to my elbows.

"What was that?" Umbra's voice came out like grinding rocks. "A joke? Pathetic? You're the kid of an unranked Beta. You dare call me pathetic?"

Both of them started shaking.

Umbra unleashed her raw aura. The pressure slammed into the room.

Blair and Ronnie hit the floor, knees cracking against the wood.

"Lyra!" My mom gasped, her voice thick with sudden hope. But she forced her face back into a mask. "Let them go."

I whipped around and growled at her.

"Umbra, please," my mom whispered.

"They disrespected us," Umbra roared, the sound vibrating the walls. She dialed up the pressure, aiming it straight at the two on the floor. Damon whimpered from the hallway.

I crouched down right next to their heads. "Still think I'm pathetic, huh?"

"No," Blair squeaked, sounding tiny.

"What about you, Beta Ronnie?" I growled, right in his ear.

"No," he choked out, his voice dripping with pure rage.

"No, *what*?" I got lower, letting my hot breath hit his neck.

"No, Alpha Lyra," he spat, forcing every single syllable out.

I snapped the pressure off. They gasped for air.

"Watch your step from now on."
