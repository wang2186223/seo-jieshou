Chapter 502

Sure, these high-society players wouldn't go bankrupt over one dead contract. But this was Hank Magnus. The guy basically owned the global market. Teaming up with him was like printing free cash, and nobody turns down a payday. If Hank blackballed you, though? You're done. The rumor mill would go wild, other firms would back off, and your family name would be trash. It was a straight-up trainwreck.

The targeted family bosses turned white as sheets. Beatrice lost it, screaming, "Magnus, are you crazy? I'm a Blackwood! You can't do this to me!"

Hank didn't even look at her. He just shrugged, turning his eyes to Julian Vance and Arthur. His hard face relaxed, and he gave them a quick, respectful nod. "Arthur, Nicholas, look—no disrespect to the Blackwoods. I'm just settling a score for the Ghost Doctor. If I stepped on any toes, I'll make it up to you guys once this party wraps up."

Julian flicked his wrist, his dark eyes dead cold. "Cut the polite crap, Magnus. Beatrice made a total fool of the family tonight. If you didn't handle her, I would've." He shot a freezing glare at Beatrice's ghost-white face. "Pack your bags. You're on a flight out of the country tomorrow. Don't even think about coming back until you learn how to act."

Julian scanned the crowd, locking eyes with a guy hiding in the back. "You got a problem with that, Uncle?"

Beatrice's dad, who'd been playing dead the whole time, jumped like he'd been shot. He swallowed his anger, forcing a pathetic grin. "No... no way, Julian. She totally crossed the line. She, uh, she deserves whatever she gets."

Beatrice froze, her eyes popping. She knew her old man was a spineless coward, but this? This was pathetic. Julian wasn't sending her on some fancy European vacation. He was dumping her. No cash, no credit cards, totally cut off in some random country. They'd strip her title, her trust fund, everything. It was a slow-motion execution.

And her dad? Too scared of getting dragged down with her to even open his mouth. She was totally on her own. She had to save herself. But as she opened her mouth to beg, she caught Julian's eyes. They were pitch black. Dead. The words choked in her throat, and nothing came out.

She got the message. One more peep, and she'd be banned from Elyndor forever. Her face drained of color, and her knees buckled. She hit the floor like a sack of bricks. Game over. She was done.

Watching Beatrice crash and burn, Vivienne felt the room spin. She wobbled, barely staying on her feet. She'd clawed her way up just to get on Hank's radar, trying to prove she was a player. Now? That same move had just dug her own grave.
