Chapter 422

Three hours. That’s how long I’d been digging through this damn box of papers. My brain was fried, buzzing with lines of code and Gideon Stone’s messy, half-baked notes on his security system. *Beep!* My alarm blared. I groaned. I hadn’t even scratched the surface, and honestly? I had no freaking clue what I was even searching for.

I dropped back onto my heels, letting out a heavy sigh. I scrambled off the bed and bolted downstairs to grab a drink. Right then, a knock rattled the front door. I checked my watch—three minutes until my video call. *Bam, bam, bam!* The knock came again, louder this time, totally impatient and annoying as hell. Rolling my eyes, I stomped to the door and yanked it open. There stood Damon, holding a picnic basket and a bunch of flowers.

"Uh, hi..." I blocked the entrance as he tried to push his way inside. "Damon? What the hell are you doing here?"

He flashed a grin and shoved the bouquet right at my chest. "Hey, babe. Dinner's on me. I got everything we need for a romantic picnic under the stars." My gut twisted. Something was definitely off.

I glanced down at my wrist. Two minutes until my meeting. "Are you serious? I literally told you today I had a nine o'clock call, and you show up *now*?" I arched an eyebrow. "For dinner?"

His grin stiffened. "Oh, right. Forgot about that. Can't you just push it back?"

I let out a breath, crossed my arms, and leaned hard against the doorframe. "No, Damon, I can't. This has been on the books for weeks. I'm not canceling sixty seconds before the call just because my boyfriend wants to stargaze." I stared him down. "And besides, we're on pause. I told you this. You need to handle the Blair situation first. The whole pack is still gossiping about your mating ceremony in two weeks. Yours, Damon. Not ours." I took a step back, but he lunged forward, grabbing the frame. "Damon," I warned. A growl clawed at my throat. I wanted to lose my absolute mind, but I forced myself to keep it together.

*Relax. We can just rip his throat out in two weeks. Keep your cool,* Terra chimed in. She sounded so bored I almost snorted.

"Come on, Lyra, please. I'm trying here. It just takes time," he pleaded, gripping the door to force his way in. Right then, a whiff of something familiar hit my nose, but I couldn't quite put my finger on it.

"I get it, but I won't let you ruin your reputation any further. I already laid this all out for your dad." I gripped the edge of the door, blocking his path with my body. Upstairs, my laptop started ringing. "Look, thanks for the food, but I seriously have to go."

"Wait, hold on!" Damon threw his weight against the door, crowding my space so I couldn't shut it. "Just let me in. I want to stay over tonight." He leaned in close, taking a deep sniff of my neck. "Damn, you smell amazing today. Strawberry?" He leaned down for a kiss. I gave him a quick, dry peck on the lips just as my computer chimed again upstairs.

I shoved him back onto the porch. "I'm leaving. And like I said, I'm saving myself for our actual mating ceremony. So, nope, no sleepovers." I held the door, glancing across the street. Sure enough, a pack member was staring right at us. "Look over there. Mrs. Finch is watching, and she is totally judging us." Classic her, honestly—she judged everyone. "Everyone thinks you're mating Blair now. If you want to save face, you should probably go through with it." I forced a sad look. "She loves you, Damon." He opened his mouth to speak, but I cut him off. "I love you too. Enough to let you make your own choice. You keep running back to me, begging to be together. But have you actually thought this through? If she's the one who makes you happy, go to her. I won't stand in your way. But if you want me, you need to break it off with Blair. For real, but don't be a jerk about it."

"I get it," he mumbled, looking away.

"Do you really? Because here you are again, desperate to prove you want me, but you clearly haven't said a word to Blair. The whole pack still thinks you guys are a thing." I pointed at Mrs. Finch, who hadn't blinked. "I'm sick of repeating myself, Damon. I deserve some damn respect." My laptop chimed again. Now I was pissed. "You think throwing flowers, food, and wine at me fixes things? It doesn't. You want to fix this? Go sort out your feelings. Shut down Blair's rumors. Decide who you actually want. Because I'm not sharing my mate, and I sure as hell won't be your side piece." I slammed the door right in his face and threw the deadbolt.
