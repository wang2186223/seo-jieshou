Chapter 571

Heads snapped around. There she was—the Moon Celestia, chilling in the corner, eyes locked right on us. The noise dragged my mom and Daisy straight out of the bathroom. They took one step out, tripped over their own feet, and hit the floor hard on their knees.

"Oh god, Moon Celestia," Mom gasped, slamming her forehead right onto the floor. "Please, don't listen to Elara Meadow. Forgive her." Fear practically rolled off her in waves. "She’s a total wreck, okay? Just lost her mate. Her hormones are driving her crazy. She didn't mean a word of it."

Daisy dropped right next to her. "Yeah, what she said. Please forgive her." She faceplanted onto the floor too, and both of them froze there like statues.

The goddess blinked. For a second, she actually looked thrown off—a weird look on that perfect, otherworldly face of hers. "Get up, Luna," she said, gliding forward and sticking a hand out to my totally lost mom. "I'm not here to punish the new mother. I'm here to settle a score. A debt."

Mom rocked back on her calves, her eyes popping at the hand in front of her. "Wait, what?" She grabbed the goddess's hand, letting herself get pulled up. "A debt? What do you mean?"

The deity gave a quick nod. Then she looked down at Daisy. "You too, kiddo. Up." Daisy grabbed her hand, scrambled to her feet, and glued herself to Mom's side.

The three of us by the mattress shut our mouths and watched. The goddess finally turned our way. "Hey, Elara Meadow. Your guy has been hyping you up big time. Seriously, he hasn't shut up about you for years."

"Years?" Elara whipped her head toward Rhys Thorne. "But... I literally just lost him..."

Rhys Thorne let out a weak, sad laugh. "Time's a whole different beast on this side, babe." He sighed, leaning his back against the headboard right next to her.

The goddess strolled over to the foot of the bed, nodding. "I hit the brakes on time for dead souls. Gives them a minute to process kicking the bucket. That way, when they come back to watch over you guys, it's not a raw wound for them anymore. Even if you're still bleeding, they're ready to help you pull through the mess."

"But why?" Elara shook her head, totally lost. "Why snatch him away just to make him watch me suffer?"

The goddess stepped closer. She sat right by where I was squatting and grabbed our hands. "Oh, honey," she smiled, all warm and gentle. "Nobody's forcing anyone. Trust me, almost every soul I drag upstairs shows up begging for something. Before they even realize they're dead, they're already stressing about the people they left behind. Take Rhys Thorne here. He literally begged me to let him come back. All he cared about was keeping an eye on you and the pups. Rules? He didn't give a damn. He threw away his ticket to a brand-new life just to stick around and watch over you."

Elara gasped, staring at her man. "Teren, no." She reached for his cheek, then yanked her hand back, freezing. "I'm not worth that kind of sacrifice." She opened her mouth to keep arguing, but a massive contraction ripped through her, and she screamed.

He just gave her this heartbreaking smile. "Babe, you're worth the damn world."

The goddess looked over at Rhys. "Grab your girl's hand."

He looked at her like she was crazy. "We both know that's impossible. I'm not really here."

The goddess rolled her eyes and waved a hand. "Excuse me, who's the deity here? Me or you?"

His jaw hit the floor. Still, he reached out and touched Elara's hand. Boom. The second their skin met, a warm glow flooded the bedroom. Elara shrieked, wrapping herself around him like a vine. "How the hell...?" Rhys gasped, looking at the goddess while Elara absolutely crushed his fingers.

"Because when you showed up at my door, I promised you wouldn't miss this," the goddess said. "You earned the right to be here with them. Consider this my final parting gift. Besides, Lyra has to hit the road, and she'd be stressing way too much about your girl if I left you like this."

The contraction finally let go. Elara sucked in a massive breath. "Rhys..." She stared down at their locked fingers. "You're solid. I can actually feel you."

He grinned. "Yeah, Anwyl. The boss lady..." He jerked his chin toward the goddess sitting on the mattress. "...she hooked us up one last time."

Elara looked at the goddess, her bottom lip shaking. "Thank you. Seriously."

The goddess just reached out, cupping Elara's cheek. "Today was absolute hell, sweetie, but it's going uphill from here. I promise." She looked over at Rhys. "You get the whole night. You can stay for exactly one hour after the twins pop out to hold them and your girl. But after that..." She let the words hang. "I'm sorry, but that's the hard limit. Your time is up." The goddess pressed a quick kiss to Elara's forehead, and poof—she was gone.

Rhys gave a firm nod, stood up, and scooped his girl into his arms. "Where do you want us, Luna?" he asked my mom.

"Right there, middle of the mattress," Mom said, pointing. He nodded.

He climbed onto the bed, pulling her back against his chest, wrapping his legs around her. "We're doing this, Anwyl." Elara nodded just as another contraction slammed into her. Her hands clawed at the air, desperate for a grip, and Rhys immediately locked his fingers through hers. "Breathe, baby. Just breathe."

She let out a shaky gasp, and then a blood-curdling shriek tore out of her throat. Mom scrambled down between her legs. She went a little white, but she looked up at me and snapped, "Get out of here, sweetie. I've got this covered."

I whipped out my phone, tapped the screen to double-check a setting, and grinned when I saw it would work. "Hey, Daisy!" I hissed, waving her over while shoving my phone back into my pocket. She jogged right over.

"What's up?"

I leaned in close, whispering in her ear. "Get a camera rolling on this, okay? Rhys is actually showing up on screen. Elara's gonna want proof of this night." Daisy didn't even hesitate—she spun around and bolted.

I turned back, planting a quick kiss on Elara's sweaty temple. "Love you guys. Take care of those babies. Can't wait to squeeze them."

Mom looked up, locking eyes with me. "Go make sure your dad is still breathing, sweetie. But keep your head down." I gave her a quick nod and got the hell out of there.
