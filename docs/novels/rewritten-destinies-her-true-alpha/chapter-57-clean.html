Chapter 57

I lunged. Slammed right into the wolf aiming for the kid. Snapped its neck in a second. Done. I spun around to the family. They were sobbing. I marched over, gave them a quick sniff. No blood on them. Good. "Hey! You're fine," I barked. "Get to the nearest house. Lock the damn door!"

The mom and kid kept bawling. The dad just nodded, shaking. "Thanks... Alpha..." he muttered. Oh, great. They were too terrified to even catch my scent.

"Relax, I'm Umbra. Lyra's wolf." I licked my jaws, wiping off the fresh blood. Didn't want to freak the kid out. Too late—the little girl threw herself right at me. "Lyra?" she whimpered.

"Yeah, kid. It's me. You're safe, Luna. Now move it. Go hide." The parents snatched her off my leg and bolted into the nearest house. *Bam!* Front door slammed. *Click.* Lock turned. Good. I bolted toward the next howl.

I tore through the territory. Pure chaos. Screams everywhere. Sometimes I got there first, sometimes another packmate beat me to the punch. I was heading for another howl when it suddenly cut off. Then, a scream. Not a normal one. This was pure, raw terror. I pivoted on a dime and ran like hell. I rounded the corner, and man, my stomach just bottomed out.

Two parents—newbies, didn't know them—lay in a pool of blood. In front of them, their kids. A seven-year-old boy was swinging a stupid stick, trying to hold off three massive rogues. Beside him, his four-year-old sister was screaming bloody murder, shaking her unconscious mom. *Please, Moon Goddess, let them just be knocked out,* I prayed. But looking at that massive, expanding puddle of red? Yeah, no way.

I blindsided the first rogue. Didn't even see me coming. My jaws clamped onto his throat before he could blink. *Crack.* I dropped his dead weight. The other two spun around, eyes wide.

One lunged for my throat. I ducked, dodging by an inch. I snapped at the guy in front, but *bam*—the second one slammed onto my back. Teeth sank deep into my shoulder. I yelped, thrashed hard, and threw him off. I spun, snapping my jaws. They danced back, keeping their distance.

These bastards were a team. Weird as hell. Rogues are solo players. They don't do group projects. Sure, they might use each other as distractions, but actual teamwork? Never. But these two? Total sync. One lunges, the other waits for the opening, then strikes. They had each other's backs. If I got close to finishing one, the other threw himself in the way.

I was sick of this tag-team crap. The parents were dying on the ground, and the two kids were sobbing, waiting to be eaten next.

*Dad! Help!* I mind-linked.

*Where are you, baby girl? What's happening?*

I shoved a messy pile of mental images through the link. Hope he gets the picture. Then, I prepared to cut him off. *Don't do anything stupid. Love you, Dad.*

*Lyra! Umbra! Don't you dare!*

I severed the link. Blocked him out. I locked eyes with the two rogues. Decision made. In the distance, my dad's panicked howl ripped through the trees. He was coming. But he'd be too late.

I charged the first wolf. We clashed. The second one hovered, waiting for his moment. Not this time, pal. I sank my claws deep into the first wolf's flesh. It shrieked. I bit down hard on its shoulder just as the second one slammed into my ribs. We went down, a rolling mess of fur and teeth. The first wolf pinned me. The second circled. The kids screamed. I tucked my back legs up and jammed my claws straight into the top wolf's gut.

He tried to back off, but I clamped my jaws tighter on his shoulder. No way. The second wolf lunged for my throat. But before his teeth could sink in, I ripped the first wolf's belly open. Dead. I kicked his heavy carcass off me and thrashed, tearing my neck free from the second wolf's grip. I stumbled up, legs shaking, and threw myself right between the last rogue and the kids. I growled, raw and mean, even as my vision blurred and blood gushed from my neck.
