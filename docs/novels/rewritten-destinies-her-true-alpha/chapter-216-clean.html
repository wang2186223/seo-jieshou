NovelVibe

Chapter 216

She swiped her arm. Boom. The whole scene vanished. Those brain-splitting screams? Dead silent. Just like that, we were back in my head, standing alone under the trees.

"Look, your blood made the first Lycan," I said. "And your power? That's where the wolves came from."

"Spot on," she said, taking a couple of steps. "But your family tree? That's a whole different beast. Your dad's side goes back to the OG wolf and some heavy-hitter witches. But your mom? Her Lycan blood is straight from me." She squeezed my arm hard. "Yeah, I fell hard for a guy." She was crying now as we walked, but she still flashed a massive grin. "The dude I saved had a massive family. Kids, grandkids, generations of 'em. Right down to Obsidian."

"Wait, who?" I asked. "How come nobody's ever heard of this Obsidian guy?"

"Well..." She shook her head. A tear hit my hand, stinging like a tiny spark. "Because I loved him way too much. Then he stabbed me in the back, so I kicked his ass out of here." She let out a heavy sigh. "Man, he was my whole world."

"What did he do?"

"I finally got a grip on my powers," she said. "I left my sky-palace to hang out with the wolves down here. It was peaceful, you know? Just me, my pets, and the earth." She smiled. "No more loneliness. But I still kept an eye on my Lycans from above, making sure my first creations were doing okay. Then, I ran into Obsidian. One look, and I knew he was mine. I could smell it. We had this instant spark. Back then, there was no mate bond. No magic ties. Just pure, raw love, like humans do. He thought I was just some regular chick, and honestly? I loved the attention. He made me feel gorgeous." She shut her eyes, shivering. "Every single night, I went back. And there he was, waiting. We got close, real fast. But he kept his secrets, and I kept mine." She looked at me, tears cutting two glowing silver tracks down her face. It literally took my breath away. "Look, I was desperate for connection, and he became my entire life. We built a home. I basically ignored my goddess duties, trying to forget what I actually was. We did a handfasting under my moon, swore our lives to each other. Soon enough, I popped out his baby girl. But hey, good times don't last." She dragged me forward, her heavy grief choking the air. "Our girl was tough, beautiful, just like her dad. But I was running on empty. I had to head back to the sky for one night to juice up my powers. And that's when I caught them."

She went dead quiet. I already knew the answer, but I had to ask anyway. "Caught who?"

"Obsidian had a side piece," she spat. "While I was busy raising our kid, he was out getting cozy with some other woman. I literally watched them do a handfasting under my own damn moon. It broke me. Our daughter was old enough to start looking for a husband, so I decided to stay up in the sky for good. But right then, I made the mate bond. One soul, locked to another. No exceptions. I did it so her father could never cheat again. So my baby girl would never get her heart ripped out the way mine was."

"Holy crap," I muttered. "The guy cheated on the Moon Goddess?" She nodded.

"So yeah, I get your pain. Trust me," she said, grabbing my face in her hands. "You're not going through this hell alone. We're gonna sort your wolves out together, okay?" I nodded. We turned back to the trail. The woods around us got pitch black, super thick, and dripping wet.

"How much further is this?" I tried to feel for my wolves. Nothing. Total radio silence.

She shrugged. "Hey, it's your brain, your soul. Your wolves just ran to their ultimate safe space."

We'd already left that candle clearing behind. Now we walked past that creepy, dark corner of my mind where my magic was thumping. That green glowing orb was just sitting there, humming steadily, keeping me sane.

The trees vanished. Now we were just stumbling through pure blackness. "Uh, I can't see a damn thing," I said, waving my hand in front of my face. She just giggled.

"We're inside your head, dummy," she said. "Shut your eyes. Focus. Listen to that gut feeling and find them." She stood there while I did what she said. So we waited. And waited.

Then, boom. A tiny spark popped up way out there. I spun toward it. We kept walking until we finally stumbled right onto my two wolves, curled up together, paws tangled.
