Chapter 481

Boom. My hips slammed the table. I reeled. That damn power surge—now that the leech was toast—hit me like a freight train. My head spun. I almost hit the floor. But bam, Juniper and Mom grabbed me. "I've got you, sweetie," Mom said. Her voice was a rock. It dragged me right out of the fog.

I gave my head a hard shake. "Gideon, ditch the towel." He nodded, ripping it off her armpit. Man, it was a shredded, bloody mess. "Mara, heal up. Now." She gasped. We stared, holding our breath, as the skin started crawling and stitching back together.

"Is it... is it gone?" Termen muttered. He sounded desperate but scared to believe it.

I reached for the towel, but damn, it was dripping red. I looked at Juniper. "Yeah, we're gonna need a ton more rags."

She snorted, giving my ribs a quick squeeze. She bolted to the kitchen and came back with a tub of old rags and water. "Told ya," she grinned. "The guy didn't miss a beat." She dunked a rag, wrung it out, and scrubbed Mara's skin. Boom. The rune was history.

Mara craned her neck, trying to see. "Did we do it?"

I grinned. "Hell yeah." The relief washed over me, and I slumped right into Mom. Gideon scooped Mara off the table, planting a massive kiss on her.

"You did awesome, babe," he whispered, hugging her tight. "So damn good."

I let out a long breath and locked eyes with Elara. "Your turn."

She instantly clutched her belly. "Wait. Will it hurt the baby?"

I circled the table. "Not half as much as that nasty rune is." I held my hand out. "But once this thing is dead, your pups can actually breathe. They'll grow up healthy and strong, I promise."

The whole room went dead silent. Rhys lunged forward, right next to Elara. "Whoa, hold on. What did you just say?"

I smirked, keeping my hand out, waiting for the green light. Elara gave a quick nod. I pressed my palm to her skin. "Honestly, I wasn't sure until a second ago. Your perfume blocked it, and my brain is fried from the drain. But yeah. You've got two pups in there. A boy and a girl." I grinned as two tiny kicks hit my palm. "Super feisty. But definitely twins."

"Are you... are you for real?" Elara gasped, completely breathless.

I nodded. "Dead serious. Thank god we got you out tonight, because you're about to pop." Her eyes went wide as saucers. I laughed. "Hey, not literally! I just mean you're like two and a half months along."

Mom stepped up, hand out. Elara nodded, and Mom pressed her palm to the belly.

Mom beamed. "Lyra's spot on. The little guys are lying sideways—almost like they were trying to hide from us. But they're already turning. Honestly, you might wake up twice this size tomorrow. This is the big growth spurt. Pups double or triple in size during months two and three." Mom puffed her own stomach out with her hands, making a huge circle. "In a month, you're gonna be a house." She smiled. "Let's kill this thing now so they get all the juice they need to grow."

Elara looked at Rhys, then gave a firm nod. She walked over, and Rhys hoisted her onto the table. I went straight to work. Now that I had the hang of it, Elara and the pups were a breeze. I fried her leech in half the time. Still, that backdraft of power made my brain rattle.

I stumbled back into Mom. Juniper wiped the bottom of Elara's foot and smiled. "All clean. You and the babies are safe."

Elara burst into tears. "Oh my god, thank you." She slid off the table and threw herself into Rhys's arms, sobbing. You could literally feel the relief in the air.

But I shook off the dizziness and looked right at Rhys. "Next."

Rhys nodded, kissed Elara quick, and hopped onto the table.

In seconds, I was slicing the rune off his neck. I went slow this time—way too close to the jugular. Then, I plunged straight into his head. His wolf was a total sweetheart, just doing his best, but he was shivering in a dark corner, terrified. Still, he was ready to die to protect his girl and the pups.

We ran together, deep into the pitch black where he hadn't gone in forever. Ash—that's Rhys's wolf, a soft grey guy—shook like a leaf. He tensed up right before the leech lunged. I just laughed and pumped the thing full of way too much juice. Umbra and Terra pinned it down, and I burned that sucker from the inside out.
