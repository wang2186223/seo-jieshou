Chapter 207

My knees turned to jelly. I stumbled, nearly eating the floor, but Juniper grabbed my arm and yanked me upright. I knew this trainwreck was coming. Just didn’t expect it to hit us this damn fast.

"Are you freaking kidding me right now?!" Daisy screamed.

Luna and Fawn let out these low, nasty growls. Flint actually flinched. Not because he was scared, but damn, those two sounded straight-up murderous. "Tell me you're bullshitting us, Flint," Fawn hissed. Her voice was pure ice. I didn't even have to look to know her wolf, Muse, was clawing to get out.

Flint dodged my eyes. "Look, I don't know. Finnian just dropped the bomb on me. But the King's making some massive announcement tomorrow. I figured you guys needed a heads-up." I gave him a tight nod.

"Keep 'em downstairs. Give me a minute." Flint nodded, snatched his gear, and bolted. I spun around to the girls. "I gotta check my phone. Now."

They all nodded. Juniper trailed me right back into my bedroom. "If that asshole hasn't texted or called to explain this shit, I'm going to personally rip his head off."

A dry laugh squeezed out of my throat. It was all I had left. "Let's find out." I flipped open my laptop, yanked my lockbox open, and grabbed my phone.

Boom. Three missed calls, a text, and a voicemail. I tapped the text first.

*Orion: Check your email. Right now.*

I shoved the screen in Juniper's face. She scanned it, snorting. "Well, huh. At least the guy's got half a brain." She swiped the screen to play the voicemail. I punched in my passcode, and his voice filled the room.

"Hey, Lyra. Look... fuck. I don't even know how to say this to you. I have to make this announcement tomorrow..." Suddenly, a sharp knock cut him off. Then came a loud clatter—he'd dropped his phone on the desk. Juniper’s eyes went wide. She shot me a look.

"What the hell is going on?" she whispered. But then the door creaked open, and we both glued our ears to the speaker.

"My King," a sugary voice purred. My stomach instantly did a flip.

Orion’s voice went dead cold. "Did I give you permission to walk in here, Astrid?"

The sharp click-clack of heels strutted across his hardwood floor. Then, a smug little giggle. "Oh, come on. Since when do I need an invite to see my own mate?"

Orion let out a savage, throat-ripping growl. "You are not my mate. You never fucking were."

"Oh, but I will be," she oozed, her voice getting closer and closer to the mic.

"The council screens the candidates, and then I make the call," Orion snapped. "You're not even guaranteed a spot on the list."

Juniper gripped my shoulder so hard it bruised. I slapped a hand over my mouth, choking out, "Celestia..." My whole world was spinning out of control again.

Astrid scoffed, sounding totally full of herself. "Please. I'm the best option you've got."

"According to who?" Orion barked back. "Yeah, you're a badass warrior, I'll give you that. Doesn't mean I want you in my bed." A heavy fist slammed onto the wooden desk, making the phone rattle. "If there were any Lycans left alive, you wouldn't even be in the conversation."

Juniper gasped, pointing wildly at the phone. I hissed at her to shut up.

"Well, lucky for us, the Lycans are extinct," Astrid purred. Something heavy thudded onto the floor. Fabric rustled. Then, a sharp gasp.

"Did I say you could touch me?!" Orion roared. "Get the fuck off your knees and get out of my office. Nobody touches me but my mate, and that sure as hell isn't you."

"Not yet," she spat, her voice dripping with pure venom.

"Get. Out." Orion growled. The voicemail timer ran out, and the line went dead.

"What in the actual fuck was that?" Juniper stared at me, jaw dropped.

I just shook my head, completely numb. "I have no damn clue." I tossed the phone onto the mattress, ripped my laptop open, and clicked on an email flagged *URGENT* from Orion.

*Hey,*

*The council's been riding my ass for months to find a mate. I dragged my feet as long as possible, but I'm out of moves.*

*Every time I say no, another pack gets hit. When I play ball, it buys us time to figure out our next move.*

*My plan is to kick off the search and let them get buried under a mountain of paperwork. That should buy us at least six months before I actually have to go on a single date.*

*Apply. And tell every single girl you know to apply too.*

*Orion.*
