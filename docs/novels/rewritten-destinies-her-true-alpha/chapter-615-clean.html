Finnian cracked his neck, letting out a heavy sigh. "Look, watching two grown-ass men wet their pants is hilarious and all, but I’m dead tired." He marched over to Elder, slapping heavy silver cuffs onto his wrists. *Click.* The silver hissed against skin. Elder sucked in a sharp, painful breath.

Finnian didn't even blink—he just booted the back of Elder's knees, sending him crashing to the floor. Then he pulled out another pair of cuffs and eyed Caspian. Caspian flinched, trying to back away, but Orion shoved the sharp tip of his spear right against his throat. "Don't even think about it, pal," Orion growled. The threat was ice-cold.

"This is illegal! We're the damn Council!" Caspian sputtered, trying to play his useless card. The whole room burst out laughing.

"You think anyone gives a crap about your title now?" My dad stepped out from behind the desk. *Clack.* Finnian locked the cuffs on Caspian's wrists. Dad sneered, "You threw away your power the second you plotted against the royals." He reached down and hauled Elder up by his collar.

"Please, man!" Elder squirmed, trying to wiggle out of my dad's grip. "I swear, I don't know a thing!"

Dad leaned in, getting right in his face. "Oh, really? Then I guess you're gonna scream for absolutely nothing. Because after the crap you pulled, you think we're that stupid? You guys have zero honor. You betrayed the King, you betrayed the Celestia, you betrayed your own damn people, and you even stabbed each other in the back. You're all liars. And until I'm absolutely sure you're useless..." Dad grinned, giving Elder a violent yank. "...you're just gonna have to take the beating."

Finnian grabbed Caspian by the arm, and Orion backed off. "I'll catch up with you guys," Dad said. "But keep 'em alive. I need to give a speech to the crowd first." Dad nodded, turning to grab the doorknob.

"Uh, Lyra?" Dad turned around, grinning. "Mind unlocking this thing?"

"Oh, right! My bad." I dashed behind the desk and slammed my finger onto the release button.

"Hold up," Finnian paused, looking at me. "You used high-level magic to soundproof the walls and jam our mind links, right?" I nodded, keeping a straight face. "But to lock the actual door... you just pushed a regular button?" Finnian scratched his head, totally baffled. "Man, I don't get you." Dad and Finnian headed out, dragging the two prisoners behind them like sacks of potatoes.

I snorted. "Why waste mana when werewolf-proof steel does the job just fine?" I shrugged as the door swung open. "Look, half of using magic is knowing when *not* to use it. It's not some magic wand for every little thing."

Finnian nodded. Then, suddenly, he hit the deck right in front of Orion. "My King," he choked out, pressing his forehead flat against the floor. "I am so incredibly sorry. I can't ever make this right."

Orion put his spear away, walked over, and squatted down next to the old man. "Get up. You didn't do anything wrong." He grabbed Finnian's shoulders and hauled him back to his feet.

"But I sat on that damn Council," Finnian groaned. "I let you down."

Orion shook his head. "Look, we didn't always see eye-to-eye, but I always respected your gut. And when the shit hit the fan, you had my back."

Finnian shook his head, staring at the floor. "I'm the one who pushed you toward Astrid and Raven. It felt wrong, but they had all the answers. I was just terrified of the attacks getting worse, and we didn't have an heir. I panicked." A tear rolled down his wrinkled cheek. "I basically handed them the knife to slaughter the royals, the Lycans... our own people. I did that."

Orion squeezed his shoulder hard. "You didn't know, Finnian."

"That doesn't make it okay!" Finnian cried out. "We wiped out your whole bloodline."

I opened my mouth to speak, but shut it. Today had been a total shitshow, and I needed to tread lightly. Just then, the star on Orion's forehead lit up again. Umbra gave a low, comforting grunt, pressing her weight against the mental barrier. Her raspy voice echoed in my head: *The Celestia wants you to trust this guy. He's got a role to play.* I chewed on that for a second, making up my mind.

I stepped closer. "Actually, you didn't." God, I hoped I wasn't screwing up here. "A few Lycans made it out alive. They've been lying low for over twenty years. We'll have to track them down eventually." Orion whipped his head toward me, his eyes practically glowing with sudden hope. "But that's a problem for another day."

Finnian's eyes lit up with the exact same look. "They're alive?" I gave him a firm nod. "Tell me what to do. I'll dig up whatever I can."

A lightbulb went off in my head. "Once the dust settles, you're the last Councilman standing." I gestured toward Orion. "He's gonna need a right-hand man. Plus, we need you to whip the royal guards into shape. But honestly? I've got an even better gig for you. You and Gideon Stone are teaming up."

Orion turned to face me. "Finnian and Gideon? Doing what, exactly?"

"Finnian knows the old Council inside out. And I know for a fact those bastards were hunting for the Lycan camp. You can dig up all their secret files, and Gideon can crack the codes."

"That's a massive job. Could take years," Finnian warned.

Luna's face popped into my head. I smirked. "Yeah. That's exactly the plan."
