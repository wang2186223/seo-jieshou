Chapter 30

[Take a hike and get out of Chief Lydia's comments.]

[Is this little troll just here trying to get attention? We're not falling for it. Go bother someone else.]

[Troll, leave. Don't come here and ruin Chief Lydia's comment section.]

Connor narrowed his dark eyes and immediately clapped back at 'C. Lambert'. [Kid, I think there's something wrong with your brain. Maybe go see a doctor.]

People in the comments went wild, cheering Connor on. In just three minutes, his reply racked up over a thousand likes.

C. Lambert, probably thinking Connor was just some clueless newbie with a blank profile and no avatar, fired back. [Son, show some respect!]

Connor's reply was even sharper. [You dropped your manners somewhere. Get on your knees, apologize to your me, then pick them back up.]

C. Lambert lost it. “You're all just a bunch of brain-dead superfans!]

Connor didn't let up. [You're just some gross thing that crawled out of a toilet. You're not even good enough to be posting here. Go hang out in the sewer where you belong.]

The crowd loved it. Likes poured in as more and more people joined in, backing Connor up and taking shots at the troll. His latest comment blew past ten thousand likes in seconds.

Connor was just getting warmed up when a new message popped up on his screen.

MikeyDrunk: [Bro, Grandpa caught me and Chris. We're already on our way to the Norwood family's place. We're going to the matchmaking meeting for you! You owe us, big time!]

Connor: [What's it got to do with me if Grandpa wants to find you two a girl? If you want compensation, go talk to him.]

MikeyDrunk: [*eyeroll* Don't throw your brothers under the bus like this. Lydia is supposed to be your fiancée, not ours. Don't think you can get out of it just because you're stuck in a hospital bed. We're not letting you off that easy!]

Connor couldn't be bothered to argue with Chris. He had zero intention of marrying that girl anyway. Since the two of them were already on their way to the Norwoods, Lydia could just pick one of them. Problem solved.

He closed the chat and went right back to roasting trolls.

Meanwhile, Lydia was hunched over her desk working on a deadline when her phone rang again. It was Sue.

She picked up, and Sue's voice came through, half frustrated, half excited. "Lydia, you need to check your Chief Lydia account right now. It's chaos over there!"

Lydia blinked in confusion. “Didn't I already let everyone know I'm okay?"

Sue was quick to agree. "Yeah, but your fans don't believe it unless they see you. You have to show up. And the trolls are still causing trouble in the comments, but one of your biggest fans is fighting back and it's amazing. You have to see it!" Lydia let out a sigh.

By the time she logged in, Connor had already led the fan army and chased 'C. Lambert' out of the comment section for good.

Connor's latest post read: [Looks like your IP is in the Capital too. I'll give you one day to disappear. You are ruining the air quality here. Don't make me come after you.]

The fans were obsessed, hyping him up like crazy. His follower count shot up to thirty thousand in no time.

He was definitely making the most of the moment.

Lydia glanced at the mysterious blank profile named 'CNR', thinking for a second, then typed a new comment. “Thank you all for standing up for Chief Lydia. I'll see you in the livestream tonight at ten."

Immediately, the replies blew up. See you at ten! We'll be there!

When Connor saw Lydia finally show up, he sent her a private message right away. For once, his tone was actually polite and a little hopeful. “Lydia, can we meet?”
