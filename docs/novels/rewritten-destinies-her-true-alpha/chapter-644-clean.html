**Chapter 610**

"Yo, Freya." I cornered her right outside her room. "Got a second?"

She spun around, giving me a tense nod. "Yeah, sure. Get inside." She unlocked the door and stepped into her suite—a carbon copy of mine. Her eyes darted to the mattress like she wanted to faceplant right onto it. I let out a dry laugh.

"Relax, I won't keep you long. Just wanted to make sure you're good, away from nosy ears." She turned, throwing me a skeptical look. I winced, throwing up a soundproof barrier around the room. "My bad," I muttered, dropping hard into a chair. "I'm dead on my feet."

Freya sighed. "Tell me about it. We're all running on fumes after this week." She took the seat opposite me. "So, what's on your mind?"

I dragged my palms down my face. "How are you holding up? For real." I let my hands drop. "We haven't had a single quiet second since that absolute shitshow at the mating ceremony."

Her shoulders tensed, creeping up to her ears. "I'm good." I just stared at her, deadpan. "Seriously, I am." She dodged my gaze, her hands starting to shake. "I put him in the dirt. So yeah, everything's great now." She was lying to herself, and we both knew it.

"Want me to use an Alpha command on you?" She flinched, letting out a tiny whimper. Damn it. She thought I was threatening her. But look, Freya was too proud to just spill her guts, and a command would let her bypass the emotional block. "Hey, I can phrase it so it doesn't hurt. Like you're just reading off a grocery list." I leaned back in the chair. "Shit, sorry. That sounded like a threat. It wasn't. It's an option." I stared at the ceiling. "I get it. Talking about it sucks because you have to live through it again." I shut my eyes, and boom—I was back on that damn metal table. The blades, the fire, the agony. Tears started stinging my eyes. "But trust me, getting it out is the only way to breathe. The more I talked, the less power it had over me." I looked back at her. She was staring right at me. "I'm not forcing you to do jack shit. Not without your say-so. But if you let me command you... I can shoulder the weight. You can just spit the facts. No judgment. Promise."

"You're gonna hate me." Her voice cracked. She snapped her head away, letting out a low, angry growl. She wasn't mad at me; she was pissed at herself.

"Not a chance." I reached across the coffee table and grabbed her hand. "Listen to me, and listen good. I'm only saying this once." She glared at me, eyes blazing with fury. I couldn't tell if she wanted to kill me or herself. "You did what you had to do to stay alive. Period. Whatever kept you breathing was 100% the right call. End of discussion. What happened to you... that's all on Gareth. His sick games—"

"It wasn't just him." The whisper was so quiet I barely caught it. I froze, leaning closer.

"Say again?"

She took a shaky breath. "Gareth was the ringleader... but Magnus and Damon got their hands dirty too. Hell, even Ronnie took a few swings." She yanked her hand back. She stared at the floor for a long, heavy moment before finally looking up. "Don't command me." A single tear rolled down her cheek. "Just... swear you won't look at me differently."

"I swear on the Celestia, nothing you went through will ever change how I see you." I stuck out my pinky, waiting for her to crack a smile. "Pinky swear."

She locked her pinky with mine and nodded. "I don't even know where the hell to begin." She rubbed her palms together, trying to find the guts to start. Suddenly, a knock rattled the door. I raised a finger to stop her. "Who's that?" she muttered.

"Enter," I called out. The door clicked open, and an Omega pushed a cart inside.

"Alpha Lyra." Elara ducked her head. "Gamma Freya." Another quick bow. "Brought the hot cocoa you wanted." She smiled, wheeling the cart right up to us. "The chef left your dinner in your room, but I grabbed the Gamma's portion too." She parked the cart by our table.

"Just leave the food on the cart for now, Elara." I smiled, grabbing the mug she handed me. Damn, the chef went all out. Whipped cream, caramel drizzle—the works. Hell yeah.

"Thanks," Freya said, taking her mug. Elara slipped out, shutting the door. Freya frowned at her drink. "What's with the hot cocoa?"

I snorted. "Hey, we need a drink, and while a shot of whiskey sounds amazing, we need our heads straight for this shit. No booze." I took a gulp. The warm chocolate hit my tongue, and I definitely let out a little moan. Freya snorted. "Besides," I set the mug down, looking her dead in the eye, "back at the cabin, whenever you were losing your mind, my mom would whip up some cocoa and you'd instantly chill out."

She nodded, taking a sip. "My mom used to make this for me when I was a kid." She set the mug down, wrapping both hands around it to soak in the heat. Her eyes locked onto mine. "I guess... it all started when I caught Blair and Gareth screwing, and I rejected him..."
