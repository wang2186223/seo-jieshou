Chapter 234

My eyes snapped open. Daisy and Juniper were hovering right over me, staring like they were waiting for a bomb to go off.

"What?" I muttered.

Daisy shot a look at Juniper, then turned back to me. "Well? Did it work?"

I blinked, totally blank.

She forced a quick smile. "Your wolves. Are they alive?"

I tried to smile, but a massive, jaw-cracking yawn cut me off. "Yeah," I wheezed, offering a weak grin. "They're exhausted, but they made it."

"Thank the goddess." Juniper sank back and went right back to working on my hair. "Now, let's talk about your wounds."

"The Mara. I had no clue she could actually hurt me. But when I bolted from the caves, she grabbed my hair. Dragged me backward. I knew if she pulled me too deep, I was a goner. Lost forever. So I popped my claws and sliced my own hair off. But she'd already done a number on me."

"A number on you?" Daisy snorted. "Girl, you're missing a massive chunk of hair. I know you heal fast, but your body is totally out of whack right now, so who knows if it'll grow back."

Juniper stopped, her fingers tapping a super sore spot. She drew a huge circle on my scalp. "This whole area? Gone."

My eyes went wide. I whipped my head toward Daisy, who just nodded grimly.

"When I was combing through it with my fingers, I felt some shallow cuts. But that main spot? She didn't just rip out the hair, she took the skin right with it."

I swallowed and nodded. "Well, hey. I got away."

"Yeah, and ruined your hair in the process," Juniper muttered, snip-snipping away. "Hopefully I can salvage this mess and you won't hate the new look."

I let out a dry chuckle. "I'm sure it'll be fine." I closed my eyes, resting my forehead on my arms.

"What about your back and arm?" Daisy pressed.

"She caught up a few times. Clawed my back, grabbed my arm, trying to keep me from reaching my body." I shivered, the memory of that first slash down my spine making my skin crawl. "She wanted to slow me down so she could steal my body first." I turned my head to look at Wendy. "She almost did. She leaped right past me at the last second. I thought I was done for, but then... something happened."

"What?" Juniper's fingers kept moving, trying to style what was left.

"The goddess showed up." I shut my eyes, reliving those final seconds before I snapped back into my physical shell. The Mara had leaped past me. I could feel her hair brush my arm as she screamed with joy, dropping right next to my body. She tried to slide in. I screamed 'No!' But then a hand yanked her back. "She beat me to my body. She was actually slipping inside. But the goddess grabbed her by the shoulder. The Mara tried to fight her off, but then she realized who it was." I winced, remembering her scream. "The goddess yanked her back, and the Mara just froze." She stopped screaming mid-shriek and collapsed. I opened my eyes and looked at Daisy. "She started sobbing. Begging, actually. She begged the goddess to let her have my body. Her words are still echoing in my head."

"What do you mean?" Juniper's hands stopped.

"The goddess pulled her back and shook her head. Told her my body wasn't hers to take." I swallowed hard, trying to clear the lump in my throat. "She wailed and collapsed at her feet. Her voice was totally shredded as she screamed. The Mara—this woman—she begged the goddess to let her have me. Said she'd been wandering for over a thousand years, totally lost and alone, and this was her only shot to live again." I shivered.

"What did the goddess say?"

"She told her she'd been looking for her for a really long time. Said it was time to come home." I could still hear her agonizing wail in my head. "The Mara kept begging. She was terrified of dying."

I felt Juniper wrap her arms around me. "It's okay."

I shook my head and whispered, "She was scared out of her mind. Honestly, I don't know if she was more terrified of wandering for another thousand years or actually dying." Juniper squeezed me tighter. "But the goddess knelt down and hugged her tight. She told her that even if she was scared to die, wasn't the wandering worse? The pitch-black, the freezing cold, the absolute loneliness." I looked over my shoulder, locking eyes with Juniper. "In that exact moment, if I didn't have you guys, my mom, my dad... I would've given it to her. My body. I would've just let her have it."

"Lyra!" they both snapped, but a tear slipped down my cheek anyway.

"In that moment, I felt her loneliness. It was like this massive, pitch-black abyss with no end in sight. And she lived in that for a thousand years."
