Julian shoved a hand into his pocket and dropped back into the chair. He ran a finger along his jaw, tilting his chin just so.

"Grandpa, you're clueless. She totally eats this up," he said, grinning like a stray cat that just scored a fat mouse. "A little eye candy goes a long way."

"Oh, really?" Arthur scoffed, his face twisting in disgust. "If it's so great, why'd she bolt?"

"She's just flustered." Julian rolled his eyes. "Seriously, Grandpa, you didn't see that? How did you even land Grandma anyway?"

"You're bragging about making her blush? Go actually win her over, you idiot!" Arthur stomped over and jabbed Julian’s boot with his cane. "Think I was like you? Back in my day, I closed the deal. Fast."

Julian chuckled. He rubbed his chin, staring out toward the exit. "That was the dark ages, old man. You don't get how we do things now."

Besides, if the girl was blushing, she was hooked.

At least his face and his food were doing their job.

That was a start.

He just had to play the long game.

Arthur rolled his eyes at the smug look on his grandson's face. "Yeah, yeah, I'm clueless. You're the genius. So why are you still single? Girls like Lyra don't sit around. Some guy's gonna snatch her up, and you'll be crying like a baby."

Julian kept his lazy smirk.

But his eyes went dead cold. The playful vibe vanished. "Nobody touches what's mine. Lyra is mine."

Lyra stepped outside, clutching the takeout box.

She marched straight to the back garden, letting the night air hit her face.

It didn't help. Her cheeks were still burning.

Her chest was thumping like crazy.

She slapped her own cheeks.

That absolute jerk.

Always flexing his looks the second he got a chance.

He was definitely doing it on purpose.

Using his body as a weapon.

She needed a minute before she faced her parents at the party.

The Blackwoods were throwing this bash at their estate.

The room she just left was their private family lounge.

The actual party was on the other side, half-outdoors.

Behind the lounge sat this massive, fancy garden.

In the dusk, the shadows of the trimmed hedges danced around.

The air smelled like fresh dirt and flowers.

Perfect for clearing her head.

Lyra walked over to a white, vine-covered gazebo.

She leaned against a post, staring at the bright flowers, breathing in the grass.

Her pulse finally slowed down.

She looked down at the box in her hands. Suddenly, a mental image popped up—him unbuttoning his shirt, looking way too hot and teasing.

She rolled her lips. "What a freak..."

Seriously.

What kind of trashy romance movies had he been binging?

Lyra shook her head to clear the brain rot.

She set the box on a stone table.

Then, she froze. Her eyes narrowed, locking onto a spot in the shadows.

A girl in a white strapless dress was stomping down the path. She had tons of makeup on, showing off a lot of skin, and was frantically looking around.

When she spotted Lyra standing there, looking gorgeous even next to the flowers, her eyes flared with pure jealousy.

Target locked. She marched over, her face twisted in ugly rage. "Lyra Sterling! What the hell are you doing hiding out here?"

She kept looking past Lyra, searching the area.

Lyra’s face went cold. She saw right through her. "You're not even supposed to be in this section."

The guests for Elara’s party were supposed to stay in the main area.

This garden was strictly off-limits.

"If you can be here, so can I!" Genevieve snapped, pissed that she didn't see anyone else.

She sneered. "What are you sneaking around for? Doing something dirty you don't want people to see?"

Lyra just stared at her like she was a pathetic clown.

She didn't even want to waste her breath.

She grabbed the food box, ready to walk away.

Genevieve lost it. She jumped in front of Lyra, blocking her path. "Don't act innocent with me, Lyra! I saw you sneak off with some old guy! What, did he finish too fast? Is that why you're back already?"

"You've got some nerve. You used Nicholas to get into this party, and now you're sleeping with some old geezer? You're disgusting!"
