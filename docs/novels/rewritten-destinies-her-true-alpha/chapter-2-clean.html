Chapter 2

My eyes snapped open. Holy crap, I was breathing.

"Lyra! Get a move on, you're gonna be late!"

I bolted upright, head spinning. What the hell? Where was I?

"Lyra!"

The voice yelled again. I shook off the daze, slid out of bed, and scanned the room. Wait. No way. This was my old bedroom from back in the day.

I hadn't set foot in this place since the day I got matched with Damon, my so-called mate.

*Knock, knock.*

The door swung open. It was Mom. My brain short-circuited. I didn't even think—I just lunged forward and threw my arms around her.

"Mom! Oh my god, you're actually here!" I burst into tears, sobbing into her shoulder. "I missed you so damn much."

"Whoa, Lyra. Easy, girl." She chuckled, giving my back a gentle pat. "I literally saw you yesterday." She pulled back, her warm amber eyes—the exact same shade as mine—staring into my face. "Happy birthday, sweetie."

I leaned my face into her hands. Then, it hit me. Today was my last day in this house. I was turning eighteen. The day I met my mate.

"Thanks, Mom," I mumbled.

"Fingers crossed you find your mate today and start your real life," she said, beaming.

My stomach did a violent flip. Cold sweat broke out. Hell no. I didn't want to meet Damon. Not after the horror show I'd already lived through.

"Honestly? I'd rather just stay right here with you," I said, forcing a smile and planting a quick kiss on her cheek. "I don't wanna leave you and Ronnie behind."

But inside, my mind was racing. Damon. That bastard thought I cheated. He murdered my baby, then he murdered me. And Mom? He slaughtered her too, claiming she helped me cheat. Ronnie—Blair’s dad and Mom's husband—just stood there and watched her bleed out. Sure, the guy loved my mom, but he was a spineless coward. His snake of a daughter, Blair, was the puppet master behind the whole damn thing, and he didn't do a single thing to stop her.

I got why he backed his own kid, but I hated him for it. Both of them could rot in hell.

"Oh, baby. You won't miss us," Mom said, kissing my cheek. "We’re gonna be right there with you, every single step."

I squeezed her hand tight.

"Mom, seriously. Can we just pack up and go? Just you and me. Let's dump Ronnie and Blair and head back to Dad's pack."

My dad, the Silvermoon Alpha, was a crappy husband, but he wasn't a bad guy. Yeah, he got wasted and cheated on Mom. He swore up and down it was a setup by some rival Alpha to tear them apart, but Mom said it didn't change a thing. The damage was done.

That betrayal had caused her to miscarry my baby brother. She just couldn't get past the pain.

"No, sweetie. I can't go back there." Mom’s smile vanished, and she looked away, her shoulders slumping.

"I don't want to get stabbed in the back, Mom. Not like you did. I don't want a mate. Period." I let the words slip out, cold and honest.

"Mates don't hurt each other, baby," she tried to reassure me, but her smile was shaky.

I shuddered. Oh, Mom. You have no idea what mates are capable of.

"Would you hate me if I rejected him?" I asked.

"God, no, sweetie. The Moon Goddess lays out the path, sure, but we're the ones who walk it. If he's a jerk, reject his ass."

"What if he's the Alpha's son?"

Mom froze, her eyes locking onto mine. She knew the drill. If it was the Alpha's kid, I was trapped. They'd never let me walk. An Alpha needs to claim his true mate—even if it's just for twenty-four hours—to unlock his full power.

"Lyra... what do you know?" she whispered, searching my face.

I grabbed her arm and dragged her deeper into the room, away from the door. "I don't know anything for sure. But I'm terrified. If I end up with Blair's boyfriend, I'm a dead woman walking. Mom, think about it. The odds of him matching with some high-ranking Alpha girl are way higher than him getting stuck with a Beta's daughter." I shook my head. "I want out."

She went quiet, processing the math. I could see the realization hit her. "Okay, baby. But running away is out. It'll look way too suspicious. You'll just have to mask your scent whenever he's around."

I threw my hands up. "And how the hell am I supposed to do that?"

"There are things from my past I wanted to leave buried... but if you're dead set on this..." She paused, waiting. I nodded hard. "Fine. Let me make some calls. You're staying home from school today."

I threw my arms around her neck again. "Thank you, Mom. Seriously. I'd be lost without you."

"I love you, baby. I'm always in your corner. Promise." She kissed my cheek and slipped out, closing the door.

The second she was gone, I grabbed my phone. I stared at the screen, checking the date. 2018. Holy shit. I was six years in the past. Six years before the nightmare. Today was the exact day I met Damon. This was my do-over. I could fix everything. I could survive. And this time, my baby would live.

I pressed my hand against my flat stomach. "Hang tight, little one. This time, nobody touches you."

I stripped, ran into the bathroom, and hopped in the shower. No way was I facing this new life looking like a hot mess. Afterward, wrapped in a towel, I stood in my closet trying to pick an outfit.

Then, I heard the bedroom door creak open.

I was about to call out to Mom, but the footsteps were too light, too sneaky. Not Mom. I slipped behind my heavy winter coats and held my breath.

"I'm telling you, if she's Damon's mate, I'll slit her throat."

"Are you crazy, Shan?" Terra's whiny voice came through the phone speaker.

"I'm dead serious. Damon is mine."

"Yeah, only because you threw yourself at him. He was totally hoping Blair would notice him."

"Shut your damn mouth, Terra!" Blair hissed, her eyes probably flashing. "He belongs to me."

"Whatever, I gotta go. Get your ass moving before I leave you behind," Terra snapped, and the line went dead.

A car horn honked outside. Blair reached out to grab something from my shelf, but I lunged and snatched her wrist.

"What the hell do you think you're doing in my closet?"

"Lyra?!" Blair gasped, her jaw dropping.

I squeezed. Hard. *Crack.*

She started to scream, but I slapped my hand over her mouth, muffling the noise.

"You're lucky I only snapped one finger, bitch. You just threatened to murder me. Did you forget who you're talking to? A Beta's kid threatening an Alpha's daughter?" I scoffed.

"I didn't say shit!" she snarled, her bones already knitting back together.

"Lie to me again and I'll break every single bone in your hand and hold them in place while they heal crooked." I grinned, watching her start to shake. "Now. Did you threaten me?"

"Yes!" she shrieked. "Because you're gonna steal Damon!"

"I don't want your pathetic little boyfriend. Keep him. He's trash." I shoved her back. "Now get the hell out of my room before I decide to actually break something else."

I turned my back on her, grabbing a pair of low-rise jeans and a tank top.

"What the fuck is your problem?!" she spat.

"You. Get out." I let my wolf push to the surface, my eyes flashing. I slammed her with an Alpha command, forcing her body to move. She stumbled backward, out of my room.

Stupid bitch. Sneaking into my space to steal my stuff? My wolf growled deep in my chest. *Our territory.*

I took a breath, calmed down, and locked the door. I hopped back onto the bed and grabbed my phone. I needed to map out every single detail of the next six years. Damon and Blair had no idea what was coming. They were going to pay. Every. Single. Cent.
