Chapter 393

Magnus locked eyes with me, then shot Ronnie a skeptical look, raising one eyebrow. "Well, damn," he said, leaning back in his chair. "Looks like we're out of options here, huh?"

Ronnie whipped around to face me. "Lyra, cut the crap. You can't do this. I raised you, damn it! I was your freaking father!"

My eyes flared. In a split second, Umbra shoved me aside and took the wheel.

"Say that garbage one more time," Umbra snarled, right in his face. "I will drag you in front of the Cath Báis for that sheer, delusional audacity. When we were just a pup, you didn't give us a single glance. You did everything you could to hog our mother's attention and keep her away from us. And the second I finally shifted? You hijacked our bonding night. So watch your mouth, beta. Spit on my father's legacy or my mother's sweat and tears again, and I won't hold back. I'll rip your throat out and send you straight to hell to join your dead mate." Umbra let out a low, vicious growl.

Ronnie's face went totally white. "Umbra... wait, I didn't mean—"

"Didn't mean to steal credit for a life you had zero part in?" Umbra snapped. "You didn't raise me. You didn't pay a single dime for me. You didn't even put a roof over my head. Lyra bought that house. Lyra's own cash paid for every single thing she and her mother needed. Hell, you were constantly begging her for loans just to take care of Blair! Lyra shut up and took it to protect you. But I'm done playing nice. You parade my status around this pack like you earned it. You used it to boost yourself and your precious daughter. You used my mom's misery to keep us on a tight leash. Well, guess what? The leash is broken. If my mom is single, she can pack her bags and escape your garbage, ancient law about betrayed females becoming slaves. And don't even think about claiming me as some 'old age security' slave. I don't fit your stupid 'betrayed female' criteria anyway, even if Damon was busy banging your little whore, because we were never officially mated."

"Umbra, stop!" my mother called out, though she was shaking her head at the same time.

"No, Mom. Lyra might let these clowns play her, but not me." Umbra turned her glare onto Damon. "She might be head over heels for you, kid, but I don't trust you for a second." I saw Magnus's eyes narrow, his jaw tightening.

*Umbra, stop! You're blowing our cover!* I screamed internally. She growled back at me, but before she could snap, Terra shocked us both. She lunged forward, seizing control and doing her best to mimic Umbra's deep, rough voice.

"You've got a few weeks to prove you're not a liar," Terra barked at Damon, trying to sound tough. "Otherwise, this whole mating thing is dead." Then her voice softened just a fraction. "Don't screw this up."

With that, she shoved me back to the front. I blinked, the wolf-glow fading as my human eyes returned. Mom rushed over, cupping my face in her hands. She leaned in close, giving me a quick, hidden wink. "You back with me, baby?"

"Yeah," I whispered, shivering. She wrapped her arms around me, holding me tight.

Magnus watched us like a hawk. "She still can't control her wolf?"

Mom shook her head, putting on a show. "She shifted way too late and went straight to school. She hasn't had time to bond. Umbra is just too damn strong. When she takes over, Lyra is totally helpless." Mom spoke in a quiet, worried voice. Inside my head, both my wolves snorted in amusement. "She barely remembers anything when Umbra pushes her out."

"Is that so?" Magnus stared at me. Suddenly, I felt a heavy, invisible pressure creep into the room. Magic. "Is that the truth, Lyra? You can tell me." It sounded like a casual question, but the Alpha command crawled over my skin like hot needles.

Umbra laughed in my head. *Pfft. His magic is a joke.*

Terra sniffed. *That's because it's not his. He's borrowing someone else's power.*

Umbra sniffed too. *She's right. This magic feels...*

*Familiar,* Terra finished. Then she nudged me. *Play along. Answer him, but make it look like a struggle. Act like Umbra is fighting you, but his 'mighty' magic is forcing the truth out of you.*

I started shaking violently. I forced a choked grunt out of my throat, letting my eyes flash wolf-gold for a split second. Finally, I let out a ragged scream, gasping for air as I forced the words out. "Yes... when she... disagrees with me... she just takes over... and knocks me out cold." I let my eyes go wide with fake terror, looking at Magnus with a heavy sigh. "How did you do that?"

He just smirked, looking smug. "You're stronger than you think, kid." He turned back to Ronnie, shrugging. "Well, Ronnie, looks like my hands are tied here."

Ronnie stared at me. I bared my fangs and snapped my jaws at him. He flinched hard, then tried to play it cool. "Elara, please. There's got to be another way." He looked at me, desperate. "If your mom agrees to work things out with me, will you drop this whole challenge?"

I shrugged. "If she wants to. It's her call, not mine."

Ronnie turned his pathetic, puppy-dog eyes to my mom. She just leaned in close, her voice dripping with pure venom. "I would literally rather see you rotting in a grave than spend one more second with you." Ronnie's eyes welled up with tears. He turned around to slink away.

"Hold on," Magnus barked. Ronnie froze. Magnus opened up the pack-wide mind link, broadcasting his voice to every single member. "As Alpha of the Oakenfang pack, I hereby declare the bond between Beta Ronnie and Elara Maclean officially dissolved. The marriage is void. Both are single and free to mate again."
