Chapter 382

Heads snapped. Everyone stared, totally freaked. Luna stood frozen in the grass, her eyes rolled back to pure white. When she spoke, the voice was way too deep, definitely not a kid who just turned seven. Gideon whipped around to face me. The goddess had bailed from my body, leaving just the two of us. "Tell me that ain't who I think it is," he muttered.

Luna let out a raspy, grown-up laugh. "If you're asking if I'm the Shaman, bingo." She locked her blank stare onto me. "Look up. Where the stars touch, that secret you're hunting? It's already taken root."

A knot untied in my chest. My soul already got the memo, but my brain was still lagging behind. "Spell it out for me," I said.

"You already know." She smirked, and then her knees buckled. Her eyelids fluttered shut as she went limp. I lunged and scooped her up right before she ate dirt.

"Holy hell, the Shaman is right here?" Gideon started pacing like a caged animal.

"Wait, Lyra Bloom is hanging out with the Shaman?" Mara's face twisted in total confusion.

Good old Rhys Thorne just blinked at us. "Uh, what the hell is a shaman anyway?"

Total silence for a beat. Then we all cracked up. Even Luna, blinking herself awake, started giggling. That stupid question totally popped the bubble of tension and brought us back to reality.

Daisy, Juniper, and Lyra Bloom walked out of the cabin, heading straight for our laughing huddle. "What's the joke?" Juniper asked, looking nervous.

I nodded. "We're good. Why?"

Juniper pointed a finger at Luna. "Well, the girls were playing dolls, and then this kid suddenly bolted out the door like a bat out of hell."

Luna squirmed, wanting down. I set her on her feet. "I felt a vision hitting me. I had to get to Lyra fast," she said, looking up at the adults. "Sorry for the scare."

Daisy dropped to her knees, smiling. "No biggie, sweetie." She gave Luna's ribs a quick tickle, making her squeal. "We get that your magic makes you do crazy stuff. We just get worried, okay?"

Luna threw her arms around Daisy, then looked up at me. "You got what I said?"

I nodded. "Yeah."

"Good." She spun toward Lyra Bloom. "Wanna go finish playing dolls?"

"Hell yeah!" Lyra cheered, bouncing in Juniper's arms.

"Whoa there, hold your horses, girls." Mom's voice drifted over from the porch.

We all spun around. "Mom?"

She chuckled. "Ronnie and Blair cleared out. Locked the place up. I brought dinner over." She gestured for us to get moving. "Get inside and eat. We'll figure out the game plan after." With that, she slipped back indoors.

"Wait, Ronnie actually left?" Juniper's eyebrows shot up.

I nodded. "Oh, you missed a massive shitshow today." Juniper and Daisy's eyes lit up. "Fill you in later."

"Porch wine?" Daisy raised an eyebrow.

I grinned. "Oh, absolutely. Porch wine." I gave her a wink as we started walking toward the house.

"What's porch wine?" Mara caught up, walking beside me.

I laughed. "Yeah, once Luna's knocked out, the three of us grab some wine and gossip on the porch. Juniper coined the term on night one." I looped my arm through hers. "Since you're staying, you're officially invited."

"Oh, no, I can't," she said, shaking her head with a soft laugh.

"Why the hell not?" I gave her arm a playful tug.

"Because I've got Lyra Bloom to watch."

I snorted. "Gideon's right here, and she'll be cured by then anyway. Pretty sure your mate can handle his own kid for a single night."

She peeked at me through her eyelashes. "You promise?"

I nodded, my mind already drifting. "Promise what? That he won't break his own kid? God, I seriously hope so." I laughed.

Mara laughed along as we hit the porch steps, but she shook her head. "No, silly. Promise that Lyra Bloom will actually be cured?"

I yanked her to a halt right before the door. "Look, the only reason I haven't fixed her yet is because Magnus would feel it instantly. Or whoever he got to cast the curse. It's tethered to the caster. If the curse snaps or the caster dies, they get a massive red alert."

She started shaking. "I can't lose my baby."

"You won't," I said, giving her a tight squeeze.

"Mommy, hurry up! Grandma Elara made lasagna and garlic bread!" Lyra Bloom was practically bouncing off the walls.

I looked down at her. "Lasagna your favorite?"

She nodded eagerly. "My mommy makes the absolute best lasagna." Lyra looked up at Mara.

I grinned. "Well, I think my mom's got her beat."

"Guess we'll have to do a taste test. Tonight we eat my mom's, and hopefully soon, we'll try yours."

She beamed, and we finally walked inside. But the second I stepped through the door, my gut twisted. Something was dead wrong. Leaving the crew downstairs, I headed up. Inside my head, Nels and Terra were growling, their hackles raised. I broke into a run. Something was seriously messed up.

I sprinted to my room. Mom had left the connecting doors wide open in her rush to bring dinner over. I reached out to slam them shut, but then a sound stopped me cold. Metal scraping metal.

I slipped through the doorway, quietly shutting the doors behind me. I reached my bedroom door, and there it was again. A faint, scratching sound right at my lock.

I grabbed the handle, twisted the lock, and yanked the door open.

"What the fuck do you think you're doing?"
