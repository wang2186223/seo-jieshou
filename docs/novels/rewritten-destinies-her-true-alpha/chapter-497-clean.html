Chapter 497

Everyone stared. My dad just stood there, trying to wrap his brain around the fact that his old man was still breathing. "What about my mom?" he choked out.

Grandma tight-lipped it and shook her head. "He was done with her. Had no use for her." She stared at the floorboards. "She wasn't his first victim. Hell, she wasn't his last, either." My dad just gave a stiff nod.

"Then why the hell did he stay?" Ronte piped up. "If he hated this place so much, why stick around?"

Grandma let out a heavy sigh. "Being Alpha kept him comfortable while he hunted for what he wanted." She locked eyes with my dad again. "And keeping your mother alive was just a business decision. He knew he'd lose his crown if he dumped her. He was the outsider here." She scanned the room. "Nobody liked him anyway." She started pacing. "Your mother... god, she was just like Army. Tough, bright, full of love. She was hilarious. The whole pack adored her."

"So what went wrong?" my dad asked.

She gave him a look that said, *you already know the answer*. "He happened. He took that bright, warm, beautiful woman and broke her. Turned her into the cold, lifeless shell you grew up with."

"How does someone even do that?" Finnian muttered.

Grandma opened her mouth to speak, but I cut her off. My voice stopped the whole room cold. "Easy. You love someone who doesn't give a shit about your words, your feelings, or your life. So you shrink. You make yourself tiny just to beg for a crumb of their love. Bastards like Roric, or Damon—they throw you a bone of affection once in a while, and you swallow all their abuse just to get it." I looked Finnian dead in the eye. "They slow-boil you. You don't even realize you're changing. Not until it's too late. Not until you're quiet, brainwashed, and totally obedient. Not until getting beaten is just another Tuesday, and the love they used to give you for free is something you have to beg for on your knees." My dad let out a strangled gasp. I just smiled. Yep, just spilling my guts to a room full of guys I barely knew.

"Ladybug." Finnian stepped over and wrapped his arms around me, pulling me tight. "You don't ever have to shrink yourself. Not anymore."

I pulled back and flashed a grin. "Trust me, I got the memo this time." I stepped out of his embrace and cleared my throat. "Anyway." I spun around to face Grandma, but—poof. She was gone. But then my eyes locked onto Cipher.

His fists were white-knuckled. Dude looked pissed. He was facing me, and even though I couldn't see his eyes, I felt his gaze burning right through me. No clue why. I stared back for a second before my dad cleared his throat, breaking the tension.

"Well, guess she told us everything she could," Dad said. He sounded steady, but I knew him. I could hear the faint, messy confusion rattling around in his voice.

I nodded. "So, what's the play?"

Dad exhaled hard. "We park the Roric shit for now."

Somebody snorted, but Cipher stood up, shutting them down. "Caspian's right. Roric being alive doesn't change a damn thing. We're here because Lyra flagged that rogue camp in the caves."

"Are we sure they're still there?" I asked, looking around before locking onto Finnian.

He nodded. "Yeah. Trackers from every pack scouted the caves. They all confirmed rogue activity. Elara's on-site right now, keeping eyes on them."

I turned to the other Alphas. "So we're all on the same page? Rogues are public enemy number one right now?" The Alphas nodded. "Look, I get it. You guys might be twitchy because of the whole family drama with me, my dad, and Roric. But let me make one thing clear: my dad and I are 100% ride-or-die for the Goddess and her will."

"And how do we know that for sure?" Rhys chimed in. "No offense, Leo. Seriously. You saved our asses, kept us alive, and fed us all these years. But... that guy is still your dad."

Silence stretched. My dad just nodded. "I get it. Fair point."

I was staring at them, trying my absolute best not to lose my temper, and then—bam. I was sucked right back into my mindscape with my wolves. I looked at Umbra and Terra. *What the hell is happening?*

*“You want proof Lyra is my chosen? You want proof Caspian is my chosen?”* My voice boomed, but it wasn't mine. I looked out, and every single guy in the room froze solid.

"Moon-blessed..." Juniper's dad whispered, staring at my glowing body in sheer shock.

My body moved on autopilot, turning to face my dad. "You've been through hell," the Goddess said through my lips. She walked over, cupping his face. "But we're at the finish line." My dad smiled. "You held the line. You did so damn well. Lyra blew all my expectations out of the water. Your loyalty to my path has been absolute."

My dad just shook his head, eyes shining. "You're the one who saved me."
