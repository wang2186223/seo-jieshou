Chapter 451

I kicked the pack house door open and bolted inside. Some omega was scrubbing the floor. She shrieked when I blew past her like a hurricane. I didn't stop. I sprinted straight for the office.

Elara Meadow sat behind her desk, white as a sheet and shaking. Her eyes locked onto mine, pure terror written all over her. Gideon Stone's door was shut tight. Rhys Thorne was right there, rubbing her back to calm her down. Damn. Something was seriously messed up.

I snapped back into my human skin. I threw my office door open, jerked my head, and signaled them to get inside. Elara scrambled out of her seat in a flash. I held the door for Rhys, waited till he slipped in, and slammed it shut.

I pulled Elara into a quick hug. I inhaled, but Rhys's nasty, sour scent hit my nose instead. Gross. I shoved both of them into the chairs opposite my desk, dropped into my own seat, and whipped open my laptop. "Morning, guys," I said.

"Hey, Lyra," Rhys muttered, forcing a weak, fake smile.

"You guys look like hell. What's the damage?" I asked. Meanwhile, my fingers flew across the keyboard, typing: *Lyra—What the hell is happening? You look like you're about to throw up.* I spun the laptop screen around so they could see it.

"Oh, nothing," Elara sighed, playing along. "Gideon's just having a crappy morning. Lyra Bloom is super sick, and Magnus showed up way too early today. Said he needed to talk to him." She leaned closer. "You still sorting through his paperwork?" Her eyes darted to mine. I gave her a quick nod.

"Yeah, actually. I need you to check out this filing setup. Take a look and type up your notes in the document." She nodded, leaning over the keys. Rhys glanced at the screen, then looked back at me.

"Man, I don't get any of that stuff," Rhys said, trying his absolute best to sound casual. "Gideon's filing system gives us omegas straight-up nightmares."

"Tell me about it. The guy's a lunatic," I laughed, keeping up the act. Elara finally sat back and slid the laptop back to my side.

"Looks decent, but I made a few quick edits," she said. She laced her fingers through Rhys's and offered a small smile.

I nodded, grabbing the computer. "How'd yesterday go? Any security breaches?"

Elara nodded. "Yeah, a couple of alarms tripped while you were out." She turned to Rhys and started babbling about the security protocols to keep him distracted. I looked down and read her typed message.

*Elara: Magnus showed up at the crack of dawn. Before Rhys got here, he stopped by my desk, took a massive sniff, smiled, and tapped on the wood. He was about to say something, but then Gideon walked out. But Gideon is acting crazy. Lyra Bloom is deathly ill, and he was hunting for you earlier, totally freaking out. I think he's scared she's gonna kick the bucket. I'm terrified Magnus caught the baby's scent. Plus, Rhys just got a text about some sudden border reassignment. And Gideon and Magnus were screaming their lungs out at each other literally ten minutes before you burst in. Everything is going to hell, and I'm losing my mind. Help us.*

I looked up and locked eyes with her. I forced a reassuring smile. "Hey, these notes are awesome," I said, pulling the laptop onto my lap. "I'm about halfway through scanning his box of crazy." Elara let out a tight laugh, and I grinned. "I'll dump these into the system tonight." I snapped the laptop shut and looked up. "Hey, why don't you guys swing by my place for a BBQ tonight?"

Elara looked over at Rhys. "We got plans tonight?"

Rhys thought about it for a second. "Just laundry." He looked at me and made a face. "Our building doesn't have a washer, so we gotta hit the laundromat on Main Street." Elara turned back to me, pouting. "Sorry, Lyra."

I smiled. "No biggie. Just bring your dirty clothes to my place. We can run a load while we eat."

Rhys's eyes went wide. "Seriously? It's a ton of stuff."

I waved my hand, brushing it off. "Don't worry about it. Honestly, the house has been way too quiet lately. Having you guys over to do laundry is perfect." I booted up my desktop. "Once Gideon is free, I'll ask if he wants to bring Mara Croft and Lyra Bloom over. We'll make a whole night of it."

Elara nodded. "That'd be awesome. They've been stressing so hard over Lyra Bloom. Getting out of the house might do them some good."

I let out a sigh. "Yeah, maybe." I checked the clock. "But I don't want to drop this on Mara Croft at the last second, either." I looked at Elara. "I know Gideon is tied up with Magnus, but can you call her and see if she's down? Tell her she doesn't have to lock it in until she talks to Gideon, but at least she can get Lyra Bloom ready in case they do come."

Allanah jumped right up. "On it!"
