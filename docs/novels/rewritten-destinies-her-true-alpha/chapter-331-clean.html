The whole room dropped to their knees. Boom. Just like that. And I wasn't even trying—barely half-ass flexing. Pathetic. I let my eyes burn wolf-gold, stepped right up to Gareth, and hooked my claw under his chin. "Well, well. Look who's the little bitch now, Gareth."

He was shaking like a leaf. "You..." he whimpered.

"Yeah, dude. Me." I shoved his head back further, digging my claw right into his neck. Warm blood started dripping down his throat. Man, that felt good. "Not Magnus. Not Damon. Just me, you piece of trash." I slammed my palm against his windpipe and squeezed hard. Hearing him gasp for air? Pure heaven. I yanked him off the floor. "You got any idea why I'm here, pup?"

Gareth's face went totally purple, so I dropped him. He hit the floor like a sack of potatoes, hacking and wheezing. Damon stepped up, reaching for my hand. I shot him a look that said *don't even think about it*. Umbra took over, using my voice to spit: "My human might have a soft spot for you, kid, but back the hell off."

Damon swallowed hard and held his hands up. "Alright, alright. I'm not stopping you. Just wanted to show Lyra some support, okay?" He offered his hand again. Inside my head, Umbra lost it laughing. *Man, this kid is really laying it on thick, huh?*

Terra curled up next to her, chuckling. *Let him play the nice guy. It makes Magnus feel like he's still got some control.*

I gave him a tiny nod and let him take my hand. Then I squatted down, dragging him down with me. I leaned right over Gareth, who was still coughing his lungs out. "Let's try this again. Why am I here?"

Gareth finally looked terrified. He clutched his neck, shaking his head. "I... I don't know." His voice was totally shot, and I could literally smell the fear sweating off him.

"I'm here for your head," I said, flashing a grin. "I'm gonna rip it off your neck and parade it down the street. And yeah, I can absolutely do it. You're probably thinking, 'Hey, I'm a Beta, I'm stronger than this chick.' But trust me, I'll have you eating dirt in two minutes flat." His face flushed red, pure rage bubbling up. "Oh, you think I'm bluffing? Try me. Maybe I'll play with my food first. Give you a little hope, then tear you apart piece by piece."

Gareth wheezed, "No way. You couldn't."

"Oh, I can, and I damn well will." I smirked. "Maybe I'll start with a hand. Or an ankle. I'll whittle you down until you're just a crying, broken pile of bones, and then I'll take your head. Want to know why?"

Gareth looked over at Magnus, begging for help. But Magnus was just staring at Damon holding my hand, watching his own anger slowly fizzle out. "Alpha Magnus!" Gareth choked. Magnus blinked, snapping out of it.

"What?" Magnus barked.

Gareth gasped, "Are you seriously just gonna let her do this to me?"

Magnus scowled. "I don't 'let' Lyra do anything. She's an Alpha, she knows the old laws. She has every right to call for a Cath Báis." He looked at me. "Once you're done with him, I'll apologize and fix my own mess." I nodded, then turned back to Gareth. The guy looked like he'd seen a ghost.

"You're an Alpha," I smiled, letting my fangs slide down.

"Damn right I am." Damon stepped closer, and Gareth scrambled toward him.

"Damon, come on, I'm your Beta!" Gareth begged, grabbing his pants. Damon just looked at me.

"And if I play my cards right," Damon said, kicking Gareth's hands off him, "Lyra's gonna be my Luna."

Gareth fell back on his ass. "What? No way."

"I am going to ask you one more fucking time. Why. Am. I. Here?" I leaned in, snapping my fangs right in his face. "Stop looking at them. Look at me. They aren't saving you. I'm the one who's gonna kill you." I lunged, grabbing his throat again and squeezing. "Think, boy! We just met, and somehow you managed to piss me off enough to want you dead. What did you do?"

He stared at me, terrified, before choking out, "I don't know!"

"Do you even know who I am?" He shook his head. "My name is Seraphina Thorne. Daughter of Alpha Maclean." I watched the realization hit him, turning his face into pure horror. "Yeah. That's right. Freya is my wolf." I climbed right onto his lap, getting nose-to-nose with him. "SHE IS MINE, AND YOU FUCKING TORTURED HER AND TRIED TO SHUT HER UP!" I screamed right in his face, digging my claws deep into his throat.
