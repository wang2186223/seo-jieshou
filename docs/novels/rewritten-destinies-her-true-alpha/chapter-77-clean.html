Chapter 77

I froze outside his door. Part of me wanted to kick it open and beg for five more minutes. No use. Once that guy locks in, he’s a brick wall. And right now? His head was already back home, dealing with whatever shit was hitting the fan. I let my hand drop. God, I felt like absolute trash. But fine, I’d give him his space. Going back to Mom’s sucked, and we both knew it. I let out a heavy sigh and dragged my feet down the stairs.

The house was dead quiet. Creepy quiet. Usually, kids are tearing through the halls and omegas are scrubbing the floors. This silence? It literally hurt my ears. *Stop being a drama queen,* I told myself. I just hated leaving. But when I hit the lobby, there they were. The two families I’d dragged out of hell, standing by the door next to Finnian. "Whoa," I blinked. "What’s the occasion, guys?"

Boom. Four tiny bodies slammed into me, locking their arms around my waist. "Don't go!" Finn’s voice cracked, louder than the rest. I dropped to my knees, wrapping my arms around the whole pile of them, then gently cupped his little face.

"Man, I wish I could stay, bud. But Mom’s expecting me. I gotta head back." I wasn't just trying to convince him. I was trying to convince myself.

"But you live here too!" He stomped his little foot. I snorted. Seriously, the kid was too cute for his own good.

"Yeah, you're right. It is my home. But I still have to go." I squeezed his tiny hand, the cold hard truth hitting me. This wasn't just about Mom or some stupid promise. Dad was right. I had unfinished business. I had to face my past. I had to look Damon and Blair in the eye and show them they couldn't break me again. Not this time.

"But why?" The other kids shuffled back to their parents, but Finn clung to my arm like glue.

"Because I gave my word," I said, pulling him in for another squeeze. "And you know the rule, Finn. When you make a promise..."

"...you keep it," he mumbled, looking up at me. "Dad says a wolf's word is law." I nodded, locking eyes with him. "Are you coming back?"

"Duh. Absolutely." I messed up his hair, gave him a big grin, and winked. He winked right back, then bolted to his parents. They still looked pretty beat up, but way better than yesterday. "Good to see you guys standing," I said.

"Tell me about it," one of them sighed.

"We wanted to catch you before you left," Finn’s dad said, stepping forward. "You saved our lives. All of us." Behind him, the others nodded. "Look, nothing we have can ever repay that. Not even close. But... we want you to have this." He held out a small bag.

"Oh, no way. Keep it. You don't owe me a thing." I waved it off, but Finn’s mom snatched my hand.

"Please," she whispered. Her eyes were watery, her bottom lip shaking. "Like he said, it’s nothing fancy. But we’ve been putting this together for a while. It’s the only way we knew how to say thank you. For everything."

I looked at the two couples. The other parents gave me a firm nod. Finn’s mom squeezed my hand and turned to the other woman, throwing an arm around her shoulders. "You really don't get it, do you? This is my sister. You didn't just save my kids, my husband, and me. You saved her, her husband, and my niece and nephew." She smiled through the tears. "We made this together. We both wanted you to have it."

"Alright, fine," I said, finally taking the bag. I gave them all a quick, tight hug, then turned to Finnian. "Let's roll."

Finnian jogged out first. I stepped out right behind him—and froze dead in my tracks. Holy shit. The entire pack was standing in the front yard. The heavy hitters were right at the front. Astrid, Kaelen, and Elara stood at the bottom of the steps, while the rest of the pack lined the walkway all the way to the street.

I shot Finnian a look, but he just smirked and walked down the path toward the car where Freya was waiting. "What is this?" I muttered, taking one step forward. Instantly, the entire pack dropped to their knees. Every single one of them.

"For everything you did for this pack!" Astrid bellowed.

"For the lives you saved!" Kaelen roared.

"And for nearly dying to save our babies!" Elara’s voice cracked, but she screamed it anyway.

"Silvermoon! Salute!" My dad’s voice boomed from the balcony above.

In unison, every wolf slapped a hand over their heart and let out a massive, sky-shaking howl. That did it. The waterworks started, and I couldn't stop them. I walked down the path, tears streaming down my face as whispers of "thank you" followed me from every side. By the time I threw open the back door of the car, I was a sobbing mess. Finnian and Freya hopped into the front. "I hate you guys," I blubbered. They just laughed.

*I love you, kiddo.*

*Love you too, Dad.*
