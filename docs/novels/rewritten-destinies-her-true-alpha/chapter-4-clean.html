Chapter 4

I shoved my bags in the trunk, dove into the backseat, and clicked the belt. Before my lungs could even take in air, I scrambled over the console and threw myself right into the driver’s arms.

He let out a low chuckle, making my eyes sting. "Hey there, kiddo."

"Uncle Finnian! God, I missed you." I hauled myself over the seat and dropped into the shotgun spot. "What the hell are you doing here anyway?" Finnian was my dad’s beta. Not blood, but he basically raised me.

"You seriously think I’d let my favorite smartass niece head to pack territory solo? Hell no."

"Dad sent you, didn't he?" I snorted.

"Yep, your old man sent me. Dude is a total worrywart." He grinned, chuckling along with me. "The second your mom called, he prepped the private jet."

"He didn't need to do all that." I rolled my eyes, clicking my seatbelt into place.

"Of course he did. He barely gets to see you, and now you're dumping your whole summer on us. What gives?" My uncle shot me a look. I just shook my head.

"I don't even want to be here."

"Why's that?"

I stared down at my hands, fingers twisting into knots. "I'm terrified my fated mate is lurking around here. And I really don't want to be right. I figured if I ran, I could buy myself some time."

"Why the hell wouldn't you want your mate?" my uncle asked, gunning the engine down the highway toward the runway.

"I'll lay it all out for you and Dad. Just... not right now." My uncle studied my face, searching for clues, before finally nodding.

"Fine. But I want the whole damn story."

"You'll get it, promise." I pointed at the windshield. "Now watch the road before you kill us both!" I lunged, grabbing the steering wheel to yank us back into our lane.

"Holy shit!" Uncle Finnian ripped the wheel back, eyes locked on the asphalt. "I'm just stressed about you, kid." I couldn't help but laugh. Werewolves stop aging at twenty-five, so he looked about my age.

"I know, I know." I slumped back into my seat with a sigh. "Trust me, what happened isn't going to make you feel any better." I wiped my face. "I don't even get why Mom shipped me off to Dad. She just said he could fix things."

"Well, let's get back fast so we can sort this mess out, Sugar." My uncle slammed the gas, and we tore into the airport in seconds.

We tossed my bags inside and boarded the jet, getting comfortable. "Close your eyes. You look like absolute hell, and we've got a few hours." My uncle gave my head a quick pat, sat across from me, and flipped open his laptop.

"Work?"

"Always." He nodded.

"Hey, can you teach me some business stuff this summer?" A plan was clicking into place. A way to stay free from my pathetic excuse of a mate, and make a boatload of cash while doing it.

"Sure thing, Sugar." My uncle flashed a grin. "You know, your dad would teach you everything he knows if you just let him."

"I actually asked to transfer to your pack permanently." I snuggled into the blanket the flight attendant handed me. Finnian froze solid.

"What the hell is going on?" His eyes flared.

"I'll explain later. Just promise you'll believe me and won't judge."

"Always, Sugar." He smiled, turning back to his screen. "Now get some sleep."

"On it." I rolled over and passed out instantly.

A shake on my shoulder woke me up. Finnian was hovering. "We're here."

I let out a massive yawn, popped my jaw, and rubbed my eyes. "Got it." I handed back the blanket and wiped my face again. "Thanks."

"Anytime, Princess." The flight attendant smiled. I realized a second too late that she was one of our pack members, too.

"You don't have to call me Princess."

"You're the Alpha's kid." She gave a quick bow and left us to it.

"Move it, your dad has already mind-linked me three times since we touched down." Finnian laughed, ushering me toward the waiting SUV. Thirty minutes later, we crossed into pack territory. "Your dad would let you move here in a heartbeat if you wanted, Sugar."

"I know. But Alpha Dan won't let me leave..." I sighed, looking out the window. "I think he suspects I'm running from something, and he won't let me go until he gets the truth."

"You gonna spill yet, Sugar?"

I hesitated as we pulled up to the massive pack house. Before I could even speak, the car door flew open. Strong arms wrapped around me, and a scent washed over me that instantly quieted my racing heart.

"Dad."

"Hey, my girl." His deep voice instantly grounded me, and my eyes started to burn.

"I missed you so much." I sniffled as he unbuckled my seatbelt and pulled me out.

"Missed you too, kiddo." He kissed my temple. "Now, we need to talk." He bolted inside with me in his arms before I could say a word, Finnian sprinting to keep up.

"Slow down, you idiot! Some of us aren't that fast!" Finnian panted, struggling behind us. My laughter echoed through the pack house hallways. We finally crashed in my dad's office. Finnian was out of breath and scowling. God, I missed these guys.

"Tell me what's wrong, kid." I looked around, my tongue tying itself in knots. Dad cupped my face, shaking his head. "Nothing you say changes a thing. I believe you, no matter how wild it sounds."

"Same here, Sugar."

My chest loosened seeing how dead-serious they were. They'd believe my crazy story. No matter how insane it sounded, my dad and Finnian had my back.

I didn't have to worry about looking crazy here.

So, I finally took a breath and let it all out.
