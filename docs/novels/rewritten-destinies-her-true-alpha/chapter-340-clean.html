Chapter 340

I yanked my power up and slammed it right into Freya. Her back arched off the bed. She shrieked. Daisy and Juniper came running. Far off, Luna let out a tiny whine. I'd explain later. Right now, my eyes were locked on the girl dying on my mattress.

"What the hell, Lyra?!" Daisy lunged to my side.

"Stop! Lyra, seriously, stop!" Juniper rushed in from the cabin.

I shook my head hard. "Can't." I clamped my hand over her wrist. The blood kept gushing. "Get me a towel. Bathroom. Now!" Juniper bolted.

"What happened?" Daisy dropped to her knees, pressing two fingers against Freya's neck. Juniper sprinted back, shoving a towel at me. I yanked my hand away from the cut. Both of them gasped.

"I needed her blood. For the barrier. If she's not locked in, they'll track her here. I only wanted a tiny drop." I wrapped the towel around her wrist and squeezed. I shoved her arm at Daisy. "Hold it. Tight."

"Why isn't she healing?" Juniper stared at the red puddle on the floor.

I bared my teeth. "Magnus. Gareth. Had to be."

Juniper stared at me, watching me wrap both hands around Freya's arm. "What did those bastards do?"

"Wolfsbane," I choked out. "Muffle her. Luna can't hear this, and I need every ounce of my juice to fix her."

Juniper nodded. She scrambled onto the bed, pulled Freya's head onto her lap, and clamped her hands over Freya's mouth.

I looked at Freya. "This is gonna hurt like hell. Sorry, babe."

I dove deep into my mind, straight to the plant. Usually, tapping my magic is a breeze. It's always blooming. But right now, I needed the hard stuff. I shoved my hands right into the mental dirt. Power flooded my veins. I dragged it up, faster, faster, but damn it, it still felt like molasses.

I had to wait. If I dumped it too fast, Freya was toast.

"Lyra?" Juniper stared, eyes wide. "What are you doing? Lyra!" She looked down at Freya, losing her mind. "She's slipping, Lyra!"

I nodded. I knew. But I had to hold.

Daisy gripped the towel harder. Blood soaked right through, dripping onto the floor. "Lyra, she's losing way too much." She wasn't screaming yet, but her voice was shaking. "What do I do?" She stared at me, begging for a plan, a command, anything.

But I was at my limit. My brain was locked up. I couldn't move. Everything I had was dialed into one thing: building that charge, watching the tiny spark in Freya's chest flicker. It was dying. I shut my eyes. *Goddess, please. Don't let me kill her.* I snapped my eyes open. A massive wave of energy hit me. Her spark stabilized.

"One more second," I muttered, staring at my girl, my packmate. She was basically gone, but no way in hell was I letting her die.

"Lyra!" Juniper shrieked. Freya stopped breathing.

Right then, my magic topped off. Max capacity. Daisy stared at me, her jaw dropping. "Holy shit. How the hell did that happen?"

Next second, I unleashed it. I took every single drop of that power and slammed it straight into Freya. It felt like raw, crackling electricity. I screamed my lungs out, forcing my will into her. "Cleanse! Heal!" Daisy and Juniper squeezed their eyes shut, praying. I didn't pray. I just kept shoving the magic in.

I pictured the wolfsbane in her veins and blasted it toward the cut. Blood sprayed out. Daisy screamed. But she needed this. "Lyra, stop!" Daisy was totally panicking now.

Juniper let go of Freya's mouth. Freya wasn't screaming. She wasn't even breathing. "Lyra, seriously, stop!"

"No!" I roared. "You don't get to die. Not today." I slammed another massive wave of healing magic into her. I pictured the wrist wound. *Close.* The skin knit together. Then I forced more power in to rebuild her blood, pumping life back into her.

With the absolute last drop of my juice, I punched it straight into her heart. One command.

*Breathe.*
