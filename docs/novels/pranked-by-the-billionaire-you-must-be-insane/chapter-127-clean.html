Chapter 127

Vivienne froze, the blood draining straight out of her face. Her jaw hit the floor. "No way," she choked out, her voice shaking like crazy. "Hell no. Julian is mine. Period."

"Damn right, Viv. Don't you forget it," Agnes snapped, her voice hard as nails. "Listen to me. Nicholas is your golden ticket. He's the only way you keep your spot in the Lancaster family and flip the script on your life. You lock him down and you marry him. Got it?"

Exactly. She had to grip Julian Vance with both hands and never let go. The guy belonged to her. That trailer-trash hick Lyra loved stealing her shit, but no freaking way was she letting anyone touch Julian.

Vivienne killed the call and bolted back down the hall toward the VIP room.

She stopped short as Lyra’s icy, detached voice drifted through the door. "Up until now, Vivienne was the Lancaster heiress. So, going by the arrangement, you and she..."

Wait, the arranged marriage? Vivienne’s heart slammed into her throat. Were they seriously talking about the Lancaster-Blackwood contract right now?

Hearing her name made Vivienne freeze, her nails digging into her palms. No way Lyra was being nice. Why bring her up to Julian? Was she actually trying to hand him over on a silver platter?

Before she could even process it, Julian shut Lyra down. "Forget 'before.'"

His voice was quiet, but it hit like a sledgehammer. The usual smirking, bad-boy vibe in his eyes vanished. "Until you showed up, I didn't give a single crap about this whole arrangement."

The romantic vibe shattered instantly. Old Man Lancaster had a sudden lightbulb moment and slapped the table hard. "Damn straight! You Blackwood punk, back at your eighteenth birthday bash, you looked your granddad and me dead in the eye and said the deal was off! Your old man was absolutely livid!"

"Yeah! You called it off yourself, so there is no freaking engagement! Period! Back off my granddaughter!" He glared daggers at Julian, his face practically screaming, *You think you can flip-flop now? No way in hell!*

The Lancaster parents traded quick glances and jumped right in. "Exactly, Julian. You made it crystal clear back then. It was dead then, and it's dead now!"

"Hey, your words, not ours. Off means off," Ethan added, pushing up his gold-rimmed glasses with a polite, smug little smile.

Staring down three generations of Lancasters ganging up on him, Julian didn't even blink. "Look, Mr. Lancaster, everyone, Ethan... the only reason I said no back then was because I, Caspian, only do the one-woman thing."

His smirk widened as his eyes locked onto Lyra, burning right through her. "I'm only signing up for this marriage... if it's with Lyra."

His dark, bedroom eyes flashed with something heavy and hypnotic, making him look ridiculously hot. "But hey, that's just me. I'm not forcing anyone's hand. The whole childhood promise thing is just paper. It's about the vibe, the heart. If Lyra's down, she's the only fiancée I'll ever want."

He put the ball right back in her court. "So, Lyra... what do you say?"

Lyra stared back into his intense gaze. His eyes were practically melting with pure, unfiltered devotion. Her dark lashes blinked, and her red lips parted, ready to give him an answer—

"Julian!" a squeaky, thrilled voice cut through the air from the hallway.

Vivienne strutted in, rocking a classy, soft-knit outfit, clutching her designer bag. She locked eyes with Julian, playing totally dumb like she hadn't heard a single word. She put on her best cute, shy act, her voice turning pure sugar. "Oh my god, Julian! You're actually here! Did you come all this way just to see me?"
