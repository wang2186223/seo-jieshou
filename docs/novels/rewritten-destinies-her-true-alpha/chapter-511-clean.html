"Orion, no. You haven't." I backed up, shaking my head. "You always said my scent was off. Guess what? You were right. I had to mask it so Damon wouldn't sniff me out. But here's the thing—you can't just pick and choose who smells you."

He stared blankly. "I got nothing."

I smirked, poking his forehead this time. "I had to fake a whole new scent so he wouldn't realize I was his mate."

He shook his head. "Right, I know..."

I laughed out loud. "Which means... I had to wear that fake scent around *everyone*." I dragged the words out.

He nodded slowly. "Okay..." Then, boom. The lightbulb finally went off in his eyes. "Wait. We spent a ton of time alone."

I nodded. "Exactly. But I kept the fake one on so my real scent wouldn't rub off on anything."

He took a shaky step back. "So... you never smelled me. And I never smelled you."

"Bingo."

"So you could be my mate?" He sounded desperate, totally raw.

I winced. "I already had a mate."

"But you dumped him." He closed the gap between us. "Which means... you could actually be mine."

I looked up, seeing the sudden hope burning in his eyes. "I mean... maybe. But what are the odds?"
