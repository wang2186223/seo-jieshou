Chapter 337

The door clicked. Blair gasped. "No freaking way!"

My mom whipped around. "What the hell is this?"

"Come on, Mom," I smirked. "You think I didn't see you drooling over Finnian’s SUV last week?"

Mom’s face went red. She pouted. "Hey, it was a nice ride."

Blair sprinted over. "Who’s that for?"

I ignored her. The flatbed was empty, and the delivery guy was walking our way.

"Looking for a Mrs. Maclean?"

I grinned at my mom. "An Eleanor Vance."

Mom’s head snapped toward the guy. "Uh, yeah. That’s me."

The driver chuckled, tossed the keys, and she caught them. "Enjoy the ride." He turned on his heel and left.

Mom stared at me, her jaw dropped.

"Yeah, yeah, I might've got you a new toy," I laughed. "But look, I gotta go help Freya and Juniper pack. Go check it out!" I shoved her toward the shiny new ride and walked away.

Blair was right behind me, whining. "You seriously bought your mom a brand-new car?"

I nodded, trying to brush past her.

"That is so not fair!" She literally stomped her foot like a toddler.

I stopped. "Oh? Why’s that?" I glanced back at the red SUV.

"I don’t even have a car!" She crossed her arms, glaring as my mom climbed into the driver's seat.

"Well, guess what? You can have her old junker." I laughed in her face and walked inside, leaving her out there screaming and throwing a fit.

I ran upstairs, slipped into my room, and locked the door. Freya was buried under a mountain of blankets on my bed. Juniper was sitting beside her, rubbing her back.

"How’s she holding up?" I asked.

Juniper sighed. "Cried herself to sleep. Honestly? I’m worried."

I nodded, walking over. "She’s tough. She’ll bounce back." I knelt by the mattress and brushed a stray hair from her forehead.

Freya’s eyes shot open. Pure anger. Accusation.

Her nostrils flared. She was sniffing me. I stayed still, waiting.

"You didn't sleep with him," she whispered, sounding totally lost.

I gave her a soft smile. "Hell no. Even if he wasn't your mate—which he is—the guy is a total scumbag."

She closed her eyes. A single tear rolled down her cheek. "Then... why?"

I stroked her hair. "Why what, babe?"

Her eyes snapped open again. This time, it was just raw pain and fury. "Why didn't you kill him?"

I smiled, tucking her hair back. She was pissed at me, but I could take it. "Because. We’re going to get you back on your feet, and then *you* are going to kill him."

She blinked, stunned.

"After everything he did to you... don't you want that payback?"

She choked up, nodding fast. "Yes. God, yes, I want that." She looked down at her hands, then her body. "But look at me. I'm too weak."

Juniper rubbed her back again. "We'll fix that," she smiled. "Nothing a hot meal, some solid sleep, and some training can't handle."

"I can't stay here," Freya muttered. "Not with him around."

"And you won't." I leaned down and kissed her temple. "I'm fixing that right now."

"How?"

I stood up and eyed the closet. "Magic."

I marched over to my dresser and grabbed a scented candle. I barely ever lit the thing, but it’d do. I looked at Juniper. "Where’s the book?"

She pointed to the closet. "Stashed it in there so I could get Freya settled. She was shivering like crazy, I got scared."

I nodded.

I walked into the closet, dropped to my knees by the door, and dumped my bag of spices and the candle on the floor. I flipped the book open to the spell.

"Umbra, I need you to get this to Daisy’s wolf. Tell her she needs to scratch these exact symbols into her door frame."

Umbra pushed her way to the surface of my mind. She stared at the page, then vanished. No mind-links for people to eavesdrop on. No texts to track. Just pure, silent wolf-to-wolf connection. It was our ultimate secret weapon.

I sat there, waiting for her to get back. To pass the time, I started rearranging the closet. I needed clothes blocking the door, but not the whole heavy rack. I dragged the front rack closer to the door and pushed the back one up.

I was almost done when Umbra stepped out of the mindscape trees.

*What are you doing?* She sounded amused.

I shoved the last few boxes around, then opened and closed the door a few times to make sure it cleared.

*Just making sure we have an easy exit,* I thought back, turning to the book. *Is it done?*
