Chapter 304

"Hell no," Luna muttered, shaking her head before staring right back into the bowl. "Did not see that coming, though." She swiped at the air like she was swatting a fly, and we all cracked up.

Fawn’s eyes were glued to my bowl. "Man, that was..." She couldn't even finish.

Daisy let out a dry laugh. "Heavy. Seriously heavy."

I shrugged. "Yeah, well. Magic doesn't do half-measures."

Juniper snatched up her herb bundle. "Move over, I'm up," she said, giving me a wicked eyebrow waggle.

I leaned in, grinning. "What's got you so hyped up, huh?"

She practically bounced right out of her seat. "Look, I know my bloodline. But I've never actually *seen* her. Not like you did." She let out this long, shaky breath. "Watching the Moon Goddess mark you? Insane. Absolutely nuts." She glanced around at the rest of the crew. "I need a piece of that action."

"Alright, let's see what happens," I said, sliding my chair right up against hers. I slapped the blade into her hand. "Light the wick. Spill some blood." I raised my voice so the whole table could hear. "Once those herbs catch fire, I'll grab her hand. Then we chant. Together."

Daisy’s face went totally white. "Uh, guys? I blanked on the words."

I waved a hand, dismissing it. "Relax, it's a breeze. *Ano septa clu dine*."

"Hold on," Daisy said, scrambling up to find a pen.

I snorted. "Sit your butt down, Daisy." She dropped back into her seat with a thud. "Just listen and copy me." I scanned the table. They all nodded. "First part: *Ano*."

"Anu?" Luna tried.

I shook my head. "Nah. *Ah-no*." They nodded, mouthing it. "Good. Now: *Septa*. *Clu*. *Di-neh*." I paused after each syllable, making sure they nailed it. "*Ano septa clu dine*." Once they stopped stumbling over their tongues, they sat back, ready. "Alright," I said, locking eyes with Juniper. "You good to go?"

"Born ready," she said. She grabbed the knife and poked her finger.

"Whoops, the candle!" I burst out laughing.

"Oh, shit!" she cursed, fumbling around to get the tea light lit and dropped into the bowl. By the time she was done, her inner wolf had already healed the cut. She had to stab her finger all over again. "Okay, for real this time," she muttered. I grinned, nodding at her bleeding hand.

"Three drops. Go." She nodded, letting the blood drip-drip-drip into the bowl, then snatched up her herbs. I grabbed her hand, squeezing tight. "Light 'em up. Say the words." I channeled a surge of raw power straight into her. She swirled the leafy bundle right over the flame. She locked eyes with me, and we roared it out together: "*Ano septa clu dine*."

Boom. Thick, pale grey smoke exploded upward. Out of nowhere, phantom sand whipped through the air, and *she* materialized. Her hair was a crown of thick black braids. Her skin? A deep, midnight-blue void. Stars literally dusted her skin, but what blew my freaking mind were the swirling galaxies, shooting comets, and glowing nebulas painted across her body. Gorgeous didn't even cover it. She caught my eye, giving me a tiny, respectful nod. She didn't have to do that, but she did.

"To honor Phoebe, we recognize Selene's ultimate masterpiece," she spoke. Her voice was barely a whisper, but it made the whole room shake. The goddess turned her gaze to Juniper. "I, Ma'at, claim you." Her eyes flared with a soft light as the link snapped into place. Then, poof. She was gone.

"Holy crap," Fawn gasped, turning to Daisy. "Did you see that cosmic skin? Insane!"

Juniper just stared at the fading smoke, completely dazed. When she finally looked at me, her eyes were swimming with tears. "Thank you," she choked out.

I smiled, pulling her into a tight hug. "Anytime, babe." I let go and faced the rest of the girls. "Listen up. Things change starting today. Once I get a breather, we're digging deep into all of your goddesses."

"Why's that?" Daisy asked, always the skeptic, leaning back with her arms crossed.

"Because we've all been slacking," I said. Her face fell a bit. "Hey, I'm guilty too. But after what we just saw? No more excuses."

"Slacking how, exactly?"

"We aren't showing up for our ancestors. We aren't honoring our goddesses." I stood up, pacing around the table until I stopped right behind Luna. "Your turn, Luna. Let's go."

Luna let out a heavy sigh, then nodded. "I mean, yeah, I want to see Amaterasu. I just doubt it'll actually change anything." Juniper scrambled out of her seat, swapping places so I could slide in right next to Luna.

"Maybe it won't," I said. "But it'll get you on her radar. And maybe, just maybe, we can pull some blessings down for your family." Luna chewed on that for a second, then grabbed the lighter. She prepped the bowl, sliced her finger, and grabbed my hand. I pumped a wave of raw energy into her. She sparked the herbs, and we muttered the chant in unison.

A thick cloud of glowing white smoke erupted. Out stepped a woman draped in heavy, flowing ceremonial robes. Luna's eyebrows knitted together in confusion. The goddess smiled. Her long, jet-black hair fell straight down her back, completely loose. Her skin was so pale it practically radiated light. With a sharp *snap*, she whipped open a folding fan and turned to me, giving me a deep, respectful bow. She stood back up, locking her eyes onto Luna. "Surprised to see me, little one?" Her voice was soft, carrying a thick accent.

Luna went stiff as a board, then gave a slow nod. "You're... you're not Amaterasu," she whispered.

The goddess's smile widened as she leaned in close. "And you... are definitely not from Japan." Luna flinched back, her jaw dropping. "I, Chang'e, claim you."
