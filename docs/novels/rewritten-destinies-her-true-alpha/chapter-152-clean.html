Chapter 152

Umbra locked onto the deer. Screw the guy. Shadow kept buzzing around us, nipping heels, begging for a game, totally desperate for attention. We ignored him. We had a target. Umbra slammed the brakes. Shadow slid to a halt right beside us, jaw open to bark, but she instantly dropped low, slipping under a thick bush.

Out in the clearing, the buck stood frozen. Ears twitching. Eyes scanning. Shadow dropped down right next to us, finally locking eyes on the prey. Boom. We split up. We flanked the target, moving like clockwork. Then, we struck. One synchronized leap. Umbra aimed straight for the throat while Shadow slammed the buck's body, pinning it to the dirt. I recoiled mentally the second our fangs ripped into the neck and hot blood flooded our mouth. Terra laughed in my head, mocking my gag reflex while Umbra kept tearing in.

"Hey, you don't have to watch this part," Terra chimed in, sounding way too cheerful. "Just tune out. Let Umbra handle the messy stuff."

"I know," I replied, burying my hands deep into her thick fur. "But I want in on your big moments. You guys had my back, so I'm having yours. Even if I have to close my eyes when it gets too gross, I still want to be here."

Terra gave me a warm nudge. We just chilled in my head while Umbra finished up. The buck took its last breath. Shadow sat back, watching. The moment he caught Umbra looking, he slid the carcass over to her. "Go ahead. Ladies first," he mumbled, his voice deep and quiet.

Umbra didn't hesitate. She dug right in. But inside, Terra's eyes went wide. "Uh, what's the matter?" I asked. I felt her shudder, but she quickly pulled herself together.

"So, how much do you actually know about wolf rules?" Terra asked.

"Basically zero," I said. "Mom promised to give me the talk when I turned of age, but I ended up crashing at Dad's all summer. Why do you ask?"

Terra slipped away from my grip and started pacing. We watched Umbra tear into the meat. "Listen," Terra said. "An alpha male only gives up his food for two reasons: his actual mate, or a girl he wants to claim. He's totally hitting on Umbra."

My jaw dropped. "Whoa, hold on. We don't even know this guy's name!"

"Exactly," Terra sighed. "But Umbra just said yes to his move by eating that meat."

Umbra's voice cut in. "Please. This is my dinner. No way in hell is Shadow taking credit for this kill. So what if he's crushing? Doesn't mean I'm interested."

Terra snorted. "Oh, cut the crap. You totally are."

"Key word: *was*," Umbra snapped back. "Before he ghosted us for months. Seriously, school started like three months ago. Flaky guys get zero play." She took one last bite, swallowed, and sat back on her haunches.

"Eat," she barked, cold as ice.

"Wait, you're mad at me?" Shadow sounded genuinely shocked.

Umbra rolled her eyes. "I don't care enough about you to be mad. Eat, starve, whatever." She stood up to leave.

"Hold up," he said, stepping in to catch her scent. But just like she did with Damon, Umbra dodged him. He plopped down right in front of us, tilting his head like a confused puppy. "Seriously, why the attitude?"

"I'm not mad," Umbra huffed, rolling her eyes. She let out a sharp snort. "You guys always think you're entitled to my body, my scent, and my time without doing a damn thing to earn it. I don't owe you jack just because you're an alpha. Newsflash: I'm an alpha too."

"Hey, don't compare me to that little punk," he growled, his voice turning sharp.

"Why shouldn't I? What makes you any better?" Umbra shot back. That shut him right up.

"I... well, I just..." He stammered, completely lost for words.

"You figured since you're the big bad alpha, and every other girl drools over you, I'd do the same." Umbra scoffed, strutting right past his nose and slapping him in the face with her tail. "Dream on."

He sat there stunned for a second before scrambling up to catch up. "Wait, do you even know who I am? Or who my human is?" he asked, totally baffled.

"Nope. Do you know who I am? Or my human?"

"Uh, no," he admitted. We both burst out laughing.

"So I don't deserve the actual effort a guy is supposed to put in? You think you're so damn special you can just show up and I'll fall at your feet? Please. I'm no pushover, and flaky guys don't get a second of my time." With that, Umbra bolted back toward the school. *Yeah, let him chew on that.*
