Chapter 425

Alexandra rolled her eyes. Clearly, she wasn't buying the BS.

"God, you're fake," she sneered. "It's exhausting."

"Look, if you want a guy, go get him. You want something? Grab it. That’s how the world works. Since you’re so 'thrilled' for Nicholas, sweetie, why don't you step aside? Go stand in the back and let the girls with actual guts do the chasing."

Alexandra jerked her chin toward the main stage.

Over there, Elara had just finished killing it on the violin. The crowd went wild. Now, a bunch of trust-fund girls were swarming her, kissing Arthur's ass, and trying to flex their own skills.

They were reciting poems, showing off paintings, playing chess—doing whatever they could to grab the spotlight and keep the vibe going.

A few girls finished their little acts.

Alexandra threw Vivienne a nasty smirk. "So, 'Princess Perfect'—or whatever your fake title is—just sit tight and watch."

She spun on her heel and headed for the stage, oozing confidence.

"Happy birthday, Elara," Alexandra said, handing over a sleek, wrapped box.

Elara took it with a polite smile. The host held it up for the room to see.

The crowd collectively gasped.

"Holy crap, is that the Whispering Dawn?" someone whispered. "The last piece Holly ever made? Didn't some anonymous bidder drop forty million on that?"

It was a pink diamond butterfly brooch, looking like it was about to fly off. Insanely detailed, sparkly as hell, and totally Elara's style.

"So the mystery buyer was Hale's kid."

"Well, her dad owns Marivelle Port. Of course she's loaded. Guess the Blackwoods and the Hales are tighter than we thought."

Elara’s eyes went wide. She loved it. "Oh my god, Alexandra! How did you know? I’ve been dying to get this!"

Alexandra smiled. "It's your eighteenth. You deserve something that actually blows you away."

"I love it so much," Elara beamed.

Arthur’s smile grew wider. His company had just closed a massive deal with the Hales, and he already liked the girl's hustle. He nodded at Alexandra. "You really went all out, kid."

It wasn't about the price tag.

It was the effort.

"Anything for Elara," Alexandra said, keeping it classy.

The crowd started whispering.

Arthur’s reaction said it all. These two families were definitely close.

And Alexandra had clearly done her homework.

Was there a wedding in the cards?

An alliance like that would shake things up.

A dozen judgy eyes slid over to Vivienne, waiting for her to break.

The richest girl in Vaelion versus the richest girl in Marivelle Port.

Talk about a catfight.

Vivienne’s face turned white.

She usually loved being the center of attention.

Now, those stares felt like hot needles.

She was about to lose it.

Beatrice grabbed her wrist, squeezing hard.

"Viv, get a grip!" she hissed. "There's no official deal between their families. Besides, why would Julian Vance ever look at her? Don't let her get to you."

"She's performing, right? You go next. Didn't you just win that big piano competition? Go up there, play, and crush her. Show everyone that a Vaelion heiress runs circles around some Marivelle Port chick."

Vivienne’s hands shook. She took a slow, deep breath.

Right.

She had to nail this.

Once Alexandra was done, she’d take the piano and blow everyone away. She'd show them Alexandra wasn't even in her league.

Then, a white grand piano rolled onto the stage.

Alexandra sat down, smooth as silk.

Vivienne’s stomach dropped.

The piano?

Did Alexandra know she was going to play and intentionally steal her spotlight?

The second Alexandra hit the first chord, Vivienne froze.

"Wow, she studied under a legend, right? You can tell."

"The emotion, the technique... it's insane."

"So good! Guess the Hale girl actually has talent."

The praise kept coming.

Vivienne clenched her fists until her knuckles turned white.

Over in the corner.

Lyra watched the stage, a smirk playing on her lips.

The girl could play. No doubt about it.

It actually sounded good.

Turns out these rich, spoiled princesses actually had some real skills when they wanted to.
