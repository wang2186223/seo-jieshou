Chapter 361

My fingers were flying, coding a new firewall, when a weak-ass knock hit my door. I looked up. Elara Meadow stood there, looking totally spaced out. "Hey, Lyra. You got a second?"

"For you? Always." I slammed Ctrl+S, leaned back, and crossed my arms. "What's the damage?"

She crept inside. "Look, someone wants to talk to you. But honestly? I really don't want him in here."

My eyebrows shot up. "No? Why's that?"

She stared at her shoes. "It's Rhys Thorne."

A smirk tugged at my lips. "Bring him on in." I propped my elbow on the desk. "Actually, I wanted to grill him about yesterday anyway."

Her head whipped up, eyes wide. "Wait, why?"

"Damon said he was putting him on some new house duty. Just wanted to check the vibe." Right then, she went stiff as a board. Her face completely dropped. "Whoa. What's wrong?"

She shook her head fast. "Nothing. It's fine."

"Bullshit." My stomach did a nasty flip. I bolted up, marched around the desk, grabbed her wrist, and yanked her toward a chair. "Sit down."

"I can't!" She glanced back at the hallway. "Rhys is right out there." I shoved her into the seat anyway, strode over, and ripped the door open.

Rhys Thorne was standing there, back to me. "Rhys. Get in here." He spun around. Holy hell. His face was a nasty mess of bruised green, yellow, and ugly purple. "What the actual..." I cut myself off, waving my hand. "Whatever. Just get inside." I waved him through the door.

Next, I went to grab Gideon Stone. "Yo. You ready?" He looked a bit lost, but nodded anyway. He pushed his chair back. "Yeah, let's roll." We walked back into my office. His eyes locked onto Rhys and Elara. I felt him freeze up next to me, but he kept his cool. "Hey, guys. What's the word?"

Rhys and Elara forced these fake-ass smiles. "We're good," Rhys said. His voice was barely a whisper. Raspy. Like he'd spent hours screaming his lungs out. My blood boiled instantly, but I forced myself to chill.

"Alright, since the whole gang's here, let's grab some food." I grabbed my purse, flashing a huge grin. Elara and Rhys looked totally lost, but Gideon caught on quick. "Perfect. We got a table in thirty." God, the guy's a genius. He patted his pockets, playing right along. "Ready when you are." He headed for the door, then paused. "Hey, Lyra, you mind driving? My mate took our ride."

"No problem!" I beamed, snatching Elara's hand. "Birthday lunch, baby!" I literally bounced on my heels, faking this massive burst of energy while Rhys tried to block his face. "Move it!" We reached the main office door, but it flew open before Gideon could even touch the handle.

Damon stood on the threshold, practically out of breath. "Hey." He plastered on a massive smile, but his eyes were dead cold.

"Hey, babe." I dropped Elara's hand, stepped up, and threw my arms around him. Inside my head, my wolf Terra growls. *My bad,* I flashed back to her. "What brings you by?"

"Came to take you out for lunch." He grinned down at me, but I backed off.

"Oh, shoot, I'm so sorry! But remember, I told you yesterday Gideon and I were taking Elara out." I threw him a massive pout. And yeah, I *had* told him. I hadn't actually planned on going anywhere, I just needed some damn breathing room. The guy showed up every single day for lunch. I was sick of it. So I made up this whole birthday lie just to get him off my back.

Damon looked down, frowning. "Guess I forgot." Then his head snapped up, a grin returning. "No biggie, I'll just tag along."

I let go of his hand, crossed my arms, and pouted hard. "If you come, everyone's gonna be super tense." I batted my eyelashes at him. "We eat together literally every day. You promised I could hang with my friends today." God, I sounded like a whiny brat. But honestly? That was the only way to play Damon. He needed to see me as a kid to give me my way. Which, when you think about it, is fucking repulsive.

The guy's a giant toddler. He doesn't know how to handle real, adult conversations. Inside my head, Terra snorted, took one last mental jab at him, and vanished back into the woods. He was just so damn pathetic. I couldn't even stand the sight of him.

"You just don't want me there," he whined, trying to pout back.

"That is so not fair!" I spun around, turning my back on him. "I told you yesterday, the two omegas are terrified of you." I whipped back around and pointed at them. "It literally took Elara four months to stop calling me 'Alpha'!" I forced some tears to well up in my eyes. "You promised!"

Damon opened his mouth to speak, but the door burst open. Blair barged in. "There you are!" she squealed, then froze dead in her tracks when she saw me.

Damon turned to face her. I could see the pure rage bubbling right under his skin. "What are you doing here?" His voice was totally calm, but the guy was about to absolutely lose his shit.
