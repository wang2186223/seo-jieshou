All of her call logs, chat history, and transfer records had been leaked online. Even if Mandy wanted to help her, there was just nothing she could do now.

If she didn't want her daughter to end up in prison, her only hope was to beg Lydia for mercy. As long as Lydia was willing to give a little, maybe they could still fix things.

Benjamin watched Rosie with obvious disappointment. His frustration was written all over his face. The Gibson family's reputation was completely ruined. But there was nothing left to say.

Mandy gently helped Rosie up from the floor and kept her voice soft. "Just stay here for now, okay? I'll get you out. I promise."

How could she let her own daughter suffer in a place like this?

Even with Mandy's reassurance, Rosie couldn't stop imagining being trapped in that dark, hopeless cell alone. She'd heard all the rumors—stories about the twisted people in jail, especially the ones who liked to torment young girls. Rosie was still so young and pretty. She couldn't stand the idea of staying here.

“No, Mom, Dad, please don't leave me. I don't want to be here alone...”

Right now, Rosie had totally forgotten what Mandy had gone through when she took her place in prison. She only cared about herself.

Benjamin stomped his foot in anger. "You don't want to stay here alone? What, you want your mother and me to stay here with you?”

Rosie just broke down crying. If she could, she really would want her parents to stay in this cell with her. Of course, she knew how selfish and ridiculous that sounded, so she kept it to herself.

Mandy gently smoothed Rosie's messy hair. "Rosie, I said I'd find a way to get you out and I will. Trust us, alright?"

Eventually, the guards came and pulled Rosie away. Her screams echoed through the whole police station, desperate and raw.

Once they were outside, Mandy wiped her tears away, her hand trembling. "We're going to the Norwoods."

Benjamin hesitated, clearly not happy with the idea. "The Norwoods?"

After how things ended last time, going to the Norwoods now felt like serving up their pride on a silver platter. They'd be humiliating themselves.

Mandy caught the uncertainty on his face and gave a cold little laugh. "If we don't go to the Norwoods, then tell me, how else are we supposed to save Rosie?"

After everything that had happened, Mandy was finally seeing Benjamin's true nature. He was a coward, always hiding behind her whenever things got tough.

Her icy stare made Benjamin shrink back. He had no choice but to nod. "Alright, fine. We'll go to the Norwoods."

If Lydia agreed to settle things privately, maybe they'd have a shot. But if she pressed charges, Rosie could be found guilty of slander and face up to three years in prison.
