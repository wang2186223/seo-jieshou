NovelVibe

Chapter 273

Spencer grabbed Genevieve’s wrist before she could unclasp the chain. "Keep it on. It’s paid for. Why the hell would we return it? You want people to think I’m broke? If I take back a gift, my name is trash."

He couldn't do it. Returning a hundred-grand necklace would make him the joke of the town. The youngest Wynn heir, returning jewelry? Sure, the family was bleeding cash, but the public didn't know that yet. He had to keep up appearances.

"Appearances?" Wallace let out a dry, nasty laugh. He snatched a throw pillow and whipped it straight at Spencer’s face. "The company is tanking! We’re about to eat dirt, and you’re whining about your damn image? What image do you even have left, huh?"

The scream drained him. Wallace’s vision went dark. He slumped back onto the cushions, chest heaving. The rage triggered a killer migraine. He dug his thumbs into his temples, grimacing.

God, it was happening again. He used to get these brutal headaches back when work stress kept him up all night. But then Lyra came back from that backwater town. Every single time his skull felt like it was splitting open, that quiet, scared little girl would slip into his office. She’d bring him some warm tea and rub his head. Her hands always knew exactly where to press. Come to think of it, ever since she moved in, his headaches had basically vanished. Now? She was gone. The pain was back. And nobody gave a shit about him.

The thought made him boil. "Go get your mother," Wallace barked, hacking up a dry cough. "Now."

Spencer whined, lip trembling. "Dad, come on. You wrecked her. She’s upstairs trying to sleep, and you want her down here?"

"I said get her!" Wallace’s eyes shot open. He slammed his hand on the sofa so hard the frame groaned.

Spencer jumped, pulling his head into his shoulders.

Genevieve scrambled to her feet. "Dad, please, just breathe. I’ll go get her. Right now." She bolted toward the guest room, her fingers still locked around the necklace she hadn't managed to take off. She just wanted out of that suffocating room.

A minute later, Genevieve was back, helping Beverly hobble into the room. Beverly looked like hell. Her face was a mess of purple bruises, her lip was split, and a thick white bandage wrapped her wrist. She dragged one leg, leaning heavily on her daughter. She stared at Wallace, her eyes burning with pure resentment. Her mouth twitched, but she kept her teeth clenched. She was waiting. Waiting for him to say he was sorry.

Wallace didn't apologize. He didn't even look at her injuries. "Where’s Lyra’s room?" he demanded.

Beverly’s hurt expression vanished, replaced by instant panic. She looked at the floor, sweating. "Why... why do you care?"

"I asked you a question! Which room was hers when she lived here?" Wallace’s stomach dropped. He raised his voice, his patience gone.

Beverly flinched. The memory of the beating he’d given her outside the Obsidian Palace was still fresh. She shrank back, stammering, "She’s... she’s gone anyway. We cleaned the room out. Why does it matter now?"

"Tell me!" Wallace roared. He slammed his hand down again and forced himself up, ignoring the sudden wave of vertigo that threatened to knock him flat.

Beverly ducked her head, whispering, "She... she slept in the storage closet. The one for old junk. At the end of the first-floor hall."

"The closet?!"

Wallace’s voice cracked. He stared at her, his mind going completely blank as the room started to spin.
