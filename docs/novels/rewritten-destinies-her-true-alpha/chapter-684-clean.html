Chapter 649

My parents looked like they’d just taken a bullet to the gut. They hit the dirt, arms dangling like wet noodles. "Lyra..." they whimpered, reaching out. I ignored them and locked eyes with the Goddesses.

"Did you guys know about this?" My voice was pure ice.

They both shook their heads. Phoebe dropped to her knees first. "I don't see the future, okay? I only see the turning points. I did everything I could to back you up. To dodge your last bad ending." She stroked Orion's hair. "God, I didn't want this."

"Bring him back." The sheer force in my own voice made me flinch. "Sorry," I muttered, looking up. "Please. Name your price. Bring him back, or just let me die too." I clutched my chest. What was left of my heart was screaming in agony.

Phoebe grabbed my face. "I can't, honey." She kissed my forehead while a sob choked out of me. She backed off, and Morrigan stepped right into her spot.

Morrigan tilted my chin up and kissed my brow too. "Let's see if your shield spell can hold without you first. Then we'll talk options." She shifted into her Maiden form, her eyes reflecting my own pure misery. "I'm so sorry you have to go through this."

I nodded. "Yeah. Me too." I forced a smile, but it was totally busted. Just like me.

Morrigan glanced at Phoebe. Phoebe gave a sharp nod. "Rhea! Neptune! Aether! Get down here right now!" Phoebe's voice boomed. A massive shockwave rippled out, dragging the gods to us.

They popped in, one after another. The dirt bubbled, and Rhea crawled out. She scanned the area, her eyes landing on me and my dead guy. "Oh, honey, I'm so sorry." She walked over to Phoebe. "What's the emergency?"

A geyser of water blasted out of the dirt, and Neptune stepped out. He locked eyes with me and cocked his head. "Man, I flirt with you once and your mate drops dead..." He tried to smile, but his usual smugness was totally gone. He looked around and shook his head. "I hate seeing you on your knees, little wolf. It's not a good look." He stood by Rhea and looked at Phoebe. "You called?"

Thunder boomed. A lightning bolt ripped through the clear sky. Then came a low thud, followed by a loud whistle that made everyone look up. Boom! A missile-like streak slammed right into the dirt. Aether pushed himself up from one knee, staring hard at Orion. He took a long beat, then turned to me. "Kid, what do you need?"

That did it. The waterworks started again. I grabbed Orion's freezing hand and pressed it to my chest. "Help me save him." I stared at him, picturing his stupid, beautiful smile, and just lost it. It took me a solid minute to pull myself together before looking at the five gods. "I did everything you asked! I saved your precious creations. They're safe. I'll help Luna bring the lycans back. I'll spend my whole life worshipping you guys. Just give him back to me."

I let go of his hand and crawled over to them. I slammed my forehead right against the dirt. "I'll do whatever dirty work you want. As long as I can do it, and even if it's evil. Just bring my mate back." I looked up, staring at the whole pantheon. "Please." My lips were shaking so bad I had to bite down hard.

I knew I sounded pathetic. Begging gods to raise the dead. I glared at Phoebe. "You did it for Orion, damn it!"

"I had to give Orion a different soul to pull that off," Phoebe's soft voice cracked. "Orion already has two souls inside him." She shook her head.

"I have three souls! Can't you make him like me?" I begged.

She shook her head. "No. You're different." Phoebe looked away, a tear sparkling on her lashes.

I felt everything slipping away. I gave a slow nod. The soul bridge between me and Orion was cracking. Umbra and Terra bolted toward it. That was it. I made my choice.

I looked at the other gods. "If I die, will the shield protecting the packs hold?"

Rhea gasped. "Lyra, don't you dare!" She shook her head.

Neptune and Aether traded looks, then dropped down. Neptune grabbed one of my hands, Aether took the other. Neptune spoke first. "Your power and the lives sacrificed on this dirt are the fuel. You die, the shield dies."

"We aren't allowed to interfere," Aether added. "Once your juice runs out, the shields drop, and the humans will slaughter your people." He tried to say it gently, but the thunder in the sky told me he was feeling my pain.

I let out a scream, and a bolt of lightning ripped the sky open. Rhea sighed. "Aether, keep a leash on your power." He shot her a look, but I was too exhausted to care what it meant.

"Is there any way to redirect the spell? Can my dad take over powering the shield?" I looked at them, clinging to my last shred of hope. They shook their heads. "So that's it? I have to live without my mate forever just to keep everyone else safe?" The brutal truth snuffed out my last spark of hope.

Someone cleared their throat. "Well... there might be one way."
