Chapter 20

"Look at this crap, Professor. The CTs, the charts—it’s a total disaster. She was literally dying," one of the residents muttered, shoving a thick manila folder into the older man's hands. "I mean, seriously? Who lets some random teenage girl cut open Miss Blackwood?"

Silas stood there, draped in a heavy black overcoat. He had that classic, warm grandpa look—sharp jaw, silver-streaked temples, and a thick white beard. Not a single trace of panic in his eyes. He grabbed the folder, taking his sweet time flipping through the pages.

He let out a heavy sigh. "Damn. This is way worse than I thought. Honestly? I couldn't have promised to keep her alive, let alone save her spleen. No way."

His finger tapped a line on the chart. "Look here. Blackwood’s got a bad heart. Toss in a brutal car wreck and a massive allergic reaction from a meds screw-up... Jesus. If that girl hadn't jumped in right then, we'd be looking at a corpse."

Olivia and Derek went white as sheets. Around them, the other chief surgeons and heads of departments just stared, jaws dropping. This was *the* Golden Hand. Professor Whitman. The absolute god of medicine in Elyndor. And he was basically saying he would've choked? But some nobody girl actually pulled it off?

"I need to see this patient. Right now," Silas said, his eyes lighting up as he bolted down the hallway toward Elara’s room.

Nathan shot a quick, questioning look at Caspian. "Julian Vance?"

Caspian didn't blink. His ridiculously hot, stone-cold face didn't show a damn thing. He started walking, his long legs eating up the floor. "Reginald isn't stupid," Caspian muttered, his voice icy. "He wouldn't risk his career—or his neck—on a gamble. I don't give a shit who did the cutting. I just care that she's breathing."

The whole crowd marched down the corridor like a small army. Olivia stood there, totally blindsided by their reactions. She ground her teeth, swallowed her anger, and stomped after them.

Inside the room, Silas checked Elara’s pulse and ran through a quick, old-school physical exam. His face lit up with pure shock and respect. "Holy hell. This is a straight-up miracle. There's no other word for it." He stood up, grinning from ear to ear. "Nicholas, relax. She’s out of the woods. Totally safe."

He spun toward Reginald, still beaming. "Reg, you lucky bastard. Or maybe Elara’s the lucky one. She had one foot in the grave, and some freaking wizard pulled her back." He was so hyped he actually used the word *wizard*.

"A wizard?!" Olivia lost her mind, her face twisting in pure shock. "Professor Whitman, you’re the freaking Golden Hand! That little bitch doesn't know jack about medicine! She’s a total fake!" The embarrassment and shock had completely fried her brain, and the words just spilled out.

Her screechy voice made Silas wince. He looked up. His face was still calm, but his eyes went dead cold. "There’s always a bigger fish, kid. The girl is alive. That’s all the proof you need." He took a step closer. "Medicine is a massive world. There are plenty of people out there who can run circles around me." He stopped, his voice dropping an octave. "But speaking of you... Astrid, right? Word on the street is you're the one who triggered that nasty allergic reaction in the first place."
