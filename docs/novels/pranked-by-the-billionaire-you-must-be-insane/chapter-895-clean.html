Chapter 906

"Hurry the hell up, you old geezer!"

"Miss Lancaster! Oh god, I'm so sorry! Traffic was a nightmare!"

The shouting hit the room first.

Then, Chancellor Reginald burst through the door, dripping sweat and gasping for air. Right on his heels were Mr. Harrison and a pack of top-tier academic deans. They were huffing like they'd run a marathon, terrified out of their minds that their VIP had already walked out.

Director Reginald stood there, jaw dropped. Seeing Mr. Harrison and these big-shot scholars sprinting down the hallway was surreal. Sure, they were on the Marington payroll, but they usually lived in their labs like ghosts. You never saw them unless they were lecturing.

Snapping out of it, the Director pasted on his best kiss-ass smile and jogged over. "Chancellor! Mr. Harrison! Deans! What's the occasion? Look, I'm incredibly sorry about this. Some brat snuck in and took your seat. I was literally just calling security to drag her out—"

*Whack.*

The Chancellor’s hand whipped across his face before he could finish the sentence. The slap cracked like a gunshot.

The Director stumbled back, head spinning, eyes watering. "C-Chancellor?" he stammered, clutching his burning cheek.

He looked up, expecting sympathy, but froze. It wasn't just the boss who wanted him dead. Harrison and the deans were staring at him like they wanted to bury him in the campus yard.

"Who said you could come in here?" the Chancellor growled, eyes drilling into him. "Did I give you the keys to my office, Reginald?"

The humiliation stung worse than his cheek. The Director couldn't believe he was getting trashed in front of the whole board. "Sir, you've got it wrong!" he whined, trying to save face. "That garbage over there broke in and stole your chair! I was just trying to—"

*Thud.*

A heavy wooden pen cup slammed right into his chest. Dean Sterling, the world-renowned biologist, had thrown it like a baseball.

"Who the hell are you calling garbage?!" Sterling roared. "Do you even know who is sitting there?!"

The Director winced, rubbing his bruised ribs. "Dean Sterling, what did I do? She’s just some country bumpkin—"

"Country, my ass!" Mr. Harrison snapped. The usually polite, classy gentleman was spitting mad. He shoved the Director out of the way and lunged forward. Spotting the blacked-out admissions file on the floor, he scooped it up, blew the dust off, and set it back on the desk with trembling, respectful hands. "I am incredibly sorry, ma'am. We got held up, and this absolute moron insulted you."

The Chancellor practically doubled over in a bow. "Miss Lancaster, this is on me. I haven't trained my people right. Please, don't waste your breath on this idiot. I'm firing him right now. He won't set foot on this campus ever again."

Suddenly, the snobbish academic elites transformed into a bunch of eager puppies, crowding around Lyra with massive, desperate smiles.

"Are you okay, Miss Lancaster? If you're mad, we can ruin his life. Just please, don't walk out on us!"

"Is that seat okay? We can go to my lab right now! I got a custom ergonomic chair just for you."

"How long has she been waiting? Where are your manners, people? Get this lady some coffee, now!"
