Chapter 470

I slammed the garage door shut the second my car cleared the frame. *Ding-dong.* Seriously? I groaned, threw the front door open, and there they were. Gideon Stone and the whole damn crew. "Yo," I said, waving them inside. "Get in here."

Rhys Thorne’s jaw hit the floor. "Holy crap, what the hell happened to your face?"

Elara Meadow let out a heavy sigh. "Yeah, I kept my mouth shut. Didn't want you guys freaking out." I just smirked, swinging the door wide. They filed past, but I grabbed Gideon Stone's shoulder before he could step inside.

"Hold up," I muttered. "I'm opening the garage. Go park inside. We can't have your ride sitting out on the curb for everyone to see." He gave a quick nod and jogged back to his car.

*Umbra, patch me up. Now.* I snapped internally.

*About damn time,* she grumbled. She and Terra got to work instantly. By the time my finger hit the garage button, every scratch on my body was gone. Riek rolled his car inside, and I slammed the door shut behind him. He hopped out, staring at me like he’d seen a ghost.

"Wait, what?" He gestured wildly at my face. "How the hell...?"

I just chuckled. "I blocked my wolves from healing me," I whispered. "Gotta play the victim. Let the pack see me all busted up so they think, *oh, poor, helpless Lyra.*"

He burst out laughing. "Damn. That's cold."

I winked, turning around. "But wait," he asked, "what about the guests tonight?"

"I'll just fake it with magic," I said, cracking my neck.

Gideon Stone suddenly went stiff. "Lyra..." his voice dropped, dead serious.

"Zip it," I said, turning to him. "What's wrong?"

He pointed around the room, then slowly mouthed: *The place is bugged.*

I snorted. "Relax. Remember a few weeks back when Blair started lying about her and Damon? I did a massive, loud 'spring cleaning' to throw out her and Ronnie's junk. I 'accidentally' found every single wiretap and tossed them into their moving boxes, acting like they were just ugly knickknacks. We're totally clean."

He let out a breath, nodding. "Okay, but why not just use magic to heal up in the first place?"

"Because Magnus needed to smell actual blood," I explained. "If I used magic, he’d downplay the injuries and force me to stay. No way in hell was I letting him do that."

Gideon Stone shook his head, baffled. "But why go through the whole pack transfer hassle? We're already on the run anyway." We walked into the living room, and Lyra Bloom immediately sprinted over, throwing her arms around me.

"Are we going to see Luna now?" she asked, looking up.

I booped her nose. "Not yet, kiddo. But tonight, definitely." I backed into the kitchen, keeping my eyes on Gideon. "Alphas have different rules. I have to officially transfer to rebuild my dad's old pack. If I don't leave this pack first, I can't claim any land or start fresh. If I tried, Magnus would swoop in and claim ownership over everything."

I grabbed the steaks I'd prepped last night from the fridge. I headed for the back door, but right before stepping outside, I cast a quick illusion to make myself look half-dead again. I walked out and fired up the grill. "Gideon Stone!" I called out.

He stepped onto the patio. "Yeah?"

"You and Teren mind taking over the grill?" Right then, the doorbell rang. "Sounds like the party's starting."

He smirked. "You got it." He walked over to the heating grill. "Where's the seasoning and tongs?"

I nodded. "I'll have Rhys bring them out." I headed back inside. "Rhys!"

"Yeah, Lyra?" he called, jogging over.

I motioned him into the kitchen. I grabbed my mom's homemade steak rub from the cabinet, grabbed a plate and some tongs, and snatched two cold beers from the fridge. I shoved it all into his hands. "Go help Gideon with the meat, will ya?"

His face lit up. "On it!" He bolted outside just as the doorbell chimed again.

I rushed to the front door, paused to wink at Lyra Bloom, and slouched my shoulders to look weak. I opened the door. It was Gideon and his mate, holding their little boy. "Hey," I mumbled.

"Hey," he replied. I stepped back. "Come on in."

He stepped inside, looking hesitant. "Look, I know you're hitting the road tonight, but I was really hoping..." He trailed off, unable to finish.

His mate nudged forward. "Leo's fading again," she whispered, eyes on the floor. "Can you... do that thing you did last time? Please?"

"Say no more," I said, reaching out my arms. "Mind if I take him upstairs?"

She nodded quickly. "Please, go ahead."

Gideon locked eyes with me, his face tight. "He's my only kid. My whole world."

I nodded solemnly. "I know. I'd die before I let anything happen to him. I swear it on the Goddess." He searched my face for a long second, then finally passed the boy into my arms.

I faked a heavy limp as I headed for the stairs. I glanced back at Elara and Mara. "If more people show up, just let 'em in. I'll make this quick."

They both nodded. "We got it," Mara said, giving me a knowing wink. "Don't rush." I headed up the steps, ready to start the first round of healing.
