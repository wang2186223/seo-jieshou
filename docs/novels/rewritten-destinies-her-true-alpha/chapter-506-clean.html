Chapter 506

I rolled my eyes, stomped over, and kicked a chair out. "The hell is this?" I plopped down and locked my arms over my chest. Orion didn't say a word. He just popped the cork on the wine, glugged some into a glass, and slid it right to my side of the table. Then he filled his own.

"Just having dinner," he said, sounding way too damn sincere. "And trying to fix things with a very special girl." I let out a loud, ugly scoff.

"I'm not eating," I barked. Right on cue, my stomach let out a massive, embarrassing roar. Traitor. Orion just cocked an eyebrow. "Ugh, shut up!" I snapped, grabbing the wine and taking a huge gulp. He chuckled and lifted the silver dome off my plate.

Boom. The scent of juicy steak and loaded baked potato smacked me right in the face. My stomach roared again, even louder this time. Orion smirked, uncovering his own food.

I let out a low growl, glaring at him over the rim of my glass. God, I needed to be drunk for this. I chugged the rest of the wine in one go. Orion already had the bottle ready, so I just shoved my empty glass at him.

He shook his head, a little smirk playing on his lips. "Look, I get it. You hate my guts, and you have every right to," he said, setting the bottle down. "But just hear me out while we eat. After that, if you still want me dead to you..." He paused, looking dead-serious. "God, I hope not. But I'll back off. I promise." For once, he actually sounded like he meant it.

"Fine," I muttered. I slammed my glass down and grabbed my fork. No way was I going to starve myself just to make a point. Besides, he wasn't leaving until he talked, and I wasn't letting Elara's cooking go to waste. "But," I pointed my steak knife right at his face, making him look up. "Once we're done here, you're dead to me. No talking, no visits. Nothing. Unless it's about Magnus and his circus of idiots."

Orion leaned back, looking like I'd kicked his puppy. "No talking? At all?" He bit his lower lip. Inside my head, I groaned. Hard.

I was staring at his mouth. *Focus, girl, focus.* My inner wolves, Umbra and Terra, were absolutely useless—they were already purring like happy kittens. I shook my head to clear it. "I'll do my job. I'll help you keep your crown and protect our pack. But for my own sanity, I need a clean break." I tilted my head, locking eyes with him. "And that means Shadow stays the hell away from Umbra and Terra, too."

*Whoa, hold on!* Umbra barked in my head.

*Shut up,* Terra snapped back. *She's right. If we keep hanging out with him, we'll never move on. I'm not hurting Lyra anymore.*

Umbra looked at us both, let out a heavy sigh, and slunk off into the shadows of my mind. *Fine.*

Orion stared at me for a long, quiet moment. Finally, he slumped back and nodded. "Fine." The knot in my gut loosened. "Good." I brushed off the weird ache in my chest and stabbed a piece of meat. After a few quiet seconds, I looked up. "Well? You gonna talk, or just creepily watch me eat?"

He cracked a tiny smile, grabbed his wine, and chugged the whole damn glass. He set it down with a soft click, then picked up his utensils. "It's kind of funny, actually," he chuckled, cutting into his meat.

"What's funny?" I asked, stuffing a bite into my mouth.

He chewed slowly, swallowed, and looked at me. "I had this whole speech ready. After you dusted Shadow earlier, I ran straight here to wait for you. I spent hours scripting exactly what to say to fix us..." He stopped, his knife hovering over the steak.

"And now?" I popped another piece of meat in my mouth, tilting my head.

He let out a heavy sigh. "Now? I'm totally blanking. Honestly, I don't think any words can change your mind anyway." He grabbed the bottle, topped off his glass, and whipped out his phone. He tapped the screen and put it to his ear. "Hey, Elara? Can you send like, three more bottles of wine up to Lyra's room? Yeah." He paused. "Whatever you have is fine. I just need some serious liquid courage right now." A tiny laugh came through the speaker. "Thanks, appreciate it." He hung up and looked at me. I raised an eyebrow. He just grinned, downed another glass, and said, "Hell, I don't even know if I should bother explaining."

A sudden tightness squeezed my throat like a damn noose. I forced myself to ignore it and shook my head. "Oh yeah? And why's that?"

"Because," he said, taking a long, deep breath. "You deserve a million times better than a guy like me."
