Chapter 422

Lyra let out a soft laugh. She gave the girl’s hand a quick squeeze, her lips curving up. "Yeah, I'll be right here."

Julian stared at their locked fingers. They were huddled way too close for his liking.

Honestly?

His own sister was starting to get on his nerves.

"Lyra!" Elara gripped her hand back, her eyes wide and watery. "When we do the cake... you're cutting the first slice with me, right? Please say yes!"

"I'll be right there in the crowd, watching you," Lyra said, flashing a quick smile. "Tonight is your show, kiddo."

And what a show. The Blackwood family's golden girl was turning eighteen, and every high-society shark in the city was in the room. The heavy hitters had all showed up.

Sharing that cake knife meant stepping into the spotlight. It was a massive statement. Standing next to Elara under those blinding lights would turn her into instant gossip bait.

She couldn't afford that. Her real identity was still under wraps. One sharp look from some old-money billionaire, and her cover would blow sky-high.

Elara slumped, her face falling, but she didn't push it. She just nodded. "Fine... but you swear you'll watch me? From the crowd?"

"Promise."

A couple of Blackwood bodyguards showed up, ushering Elara toward the main hall.

Julian turned. His eyes locked onto Lyra's sharp, gorgeous face. Slowly, he offered his hand.

"Shall we?"

Lyra took a step back, putting some solid distance between them.

"If I walk in on your arm, we'll be front-page news by morning. 'Blackwood Heir Snags Mystery Girl.' No thanks. I don't do drama, and I don't want people talking."

If she wanted attention, she wouldn't have just shot down his sister.

She gave a lazy wave. "I'm heading back. Have fun basking in the glory, Nicholas."

She spun on her heel and strolled out of the dressing room.

Julian watched the flash of her red dress vanish. She hadn't hesitated for a second. He let out a dry chuckle, shoved a hand into his pocket, and followed her out.

Just outside the glass window.

Jasper and his buddies had been watching the whole silent movie. They couldn't hear a word, but they definitely saw the terrifying Julian Vance get shot down. Cold. Hard. Rejected. She hadn't even looked back.

"Holy crap, man," Finn gasped, eyes popping. "Your dream girl just dumped Julian. Julian! You seriously think you've got a shot?"

Jasper puffed his chest out, his eyes practically glowing. "Shut up, you single loser. What do you know? She’s got backbone! She doesn't care about money or power." He tossed his red bangs back, looking smug. "That's exactly my type."

Finn and the other guy just sighed, shaking their heads. The dude was completely whipped.

"Move, move! We gotta catch up. I need her name!" Jasper grabbed his friends and dragged them down the corridor.

They burst into the hallway, but the space was empty. This was the only path to the ballroom. Where the hell did she go?

Jasper scratched his head, nudging the guy next to him. "Hey, did you see where she went?"

No answer. His buddies had gone stiff as boards, faces pale.

Jasper lost his patience and shoved them. "Are you guys deaf? I asked if you saw my god—"

"Jasper."

A cold, quiet voice cut through the air.

It wasn't loud, but it made Jasper freeze instantly. Like someone hit his pause button.

Julian stood there, looking like death.

"J-Julian..." Jasper squeaked, shrinking back.

Julian's eyes swept over him, totally blank. "The party's starting. Why are you loitering in the halls?"

"Just, uh..." Jasper swallowed hard. Julian had just been talking to her. He probably knew her name. He tried to ask. He really did. But one look at Julian's icy stare, and the words died in his throat.

His mouth flapped open and shut like a fish. Finally, he hung his head. "We're... we're going to the hall now."

Julian didn't say another word. He just walked past them, his tall figure disappearing down the corridor.

The second he was gone, the three of them let out a massive breath. They looked like they'd just escaped a firing squad. Jasper's back was soaked in cold sweat.

"Dude, your cousin is a freaking monster," Finn muttered, rubbing his arms.

"My legs are literally shaking," the other guy groaned.

Jasper slapped his own chest, trying to look tough again. "Whatever! Let's go. The party's starting. We'll just ask Elara who she is!"
