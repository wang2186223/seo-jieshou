NovelVibe

Chapter 922

"Say what?" Willow stopped dead. "Why shouldn't I care? She’s literally killing the vibe in Vanguard. Everyone's checked out because of her!"

Eldridge let out a heavy sigh. He darted a quick look at the office door.

Hallway? Totally empty. Good.

He leaned in, dropping his voice to a whisper. "Look, Willow, some stuff is off the record. Lyra? The dean brought her in himself. He’s covering for her. Big time."

"The boss made it clear. Lyra is a special case. She does what she wants, and we don't say a damn word."

"We don't just back off. We give her a free pass. For everything."

"Unless she literally burns the building down, she could throw a wild party right in the middle of my lecture, and we have to play blind."

He let that sink in, then tried to talk some sense into her. "The dean isn't stupid. He’s not about to trash Marington's name for kicks."

"So, Willow, do your job. Study. Get a killer score on the Global Elite Challenge. Stop wasting your energy on some girl who doesn't even matter to your life, okay?"

Eldridge thought he laid it out perfectly.

But Willow wasn't buying it.

Like, seriously? What kind of cheat code was that?

It was too damn obvious.

Who the hell would just take that lying down?

She stomped straight back to the classroom and spun Eldridge's words into a massive, toxic rumor for the rest of the Vanguard crew.

These high-IQ kids already had massive egos and hated Lyra's guts.

Hearing that some lazy slacker got a golden ticket while they busted their asses? It set them off.

"That is so gross."

"Is this even Marington? I worked my ass off to get here. This is a joke."

"Who even is Lyra? What did she do to the dean to get VIP status? What makes her so special?"

Willow smirked. "Special? Please. She probably used some trashy moves. Honestly, I bet she rode her way to the top on her back—"

She didn't get to finish the trash talk.

Lyra slowly lifted an eyebrow, throwing a freezing glare right at her.

Just one look.

It choked the words right out of Willow's throat. She couldn't squeeze out another sound.

Her face flushed bright red from the sudden silence.

She wanted to scream, to curse her out.

But her jaw was locked.

Lyra’s heavy, suffocating aura completely shut her down.

The scarier it got, the more humiliated she felt.

She was a Doyle, for God's sake. Nobody pushed her around. Especially not in front of the whole class.

Willow forced her shoulders back, trying to stare her down.

But Lyra had already checked out. She was too bored to deal with these nobodies. She yanked her hood over her face.

Back to sleep.

To Willow, that was the ultimate slap in the face.

It was like Lyra was calling her a bug. Just some annoying pest buzzing around, not even worth a swat.

Willow shook, burning with useless rage. She spat out a weak threat. "You... you just wait! The dean won't let you drag us down once he hears about this. I'm going to his office right now!"

Lyra didn't even flinch.

Still sleeping.

But before Willow could even take a step.

Boom. A massive campus-wide alert hit everyone's phones.

The Global Elite Challenge was officially live.
