Chapter 94

That froze me. I whipped my head toward Mom. She flashed a quick grin. "Ronnie's a rogue, honey. A nomad. He started in my dad's pack, tagged along to your dad's, and wound up here. But he wouldn't bow to Magnus. No way. So he stays off pack territory. Set up this little shop. Magnus lets him slide because he's one of the few guys who can whip up descenting spray." Mom walked over and planted a kiss on Ronnie’s cheek.

"Always a treat, my Luna," Ronnie said, smiling.

"Oh. So he's your guy." I pointed a finger back and forth between them. Mom glanced up at Ronnie. He gave a tiny nod. "Well, yes and no," Mom said. "Ronnie's also a shaman."

"A what now?"

"A shaman." Mom reached out. I stepped closer, taking her hand. "Your dad had his grandma to help run his pack," she said, locking eyes with me. "My dad had Ronnie." Her stare begged me to get it.

"So... shamans are like Grandma?" I tilted my head.

Ronnie let out a chuckle. "Yes and no. Again." He shrugged. "The Goddess blessed us, sure, but our mojo is different. We're plugged into the land and the Goddess. Mostly, though? We just get these vibes."

"Vibes?"

"Yeah. Like, say a sick wolf walks in. Boom. I get this sudden wave of pain or just feel super uneasy. No clear pictures, just... I know something's messed up." He rubbed his neck, laughing. "Sounds crazy, I know. But if you had it, you'd get it."

"Daddy? Who's that?" A little voice called out. A tiny girl popped out from the back room.

"Come here, baby girl." Ronnie waved and scooped her up. "I want you to meet someone really special." She was freaking adorable. Bright blonde pigtails, big chocolate-brown eyes, and a face smeared with peanut butter and jelly.

Mom gasped, hands flying to her mouth. Tears welled up instantly. "Ronnie... you never told me."

"Things have been crazy, and I couldn't risk it," Ronnie said, his eyes darting between us. "Magnus would've snatched her up in a heartbeat."

Mom choked back a sob. My gut screamed that trouble was coming. I bolted to the front door, threw the lock, and flipped the sign to CLOSED. "Look, I don't know what's going down, but we need to get out of sight. Now." I scanned the empty parking lot through the glass.

"Sweetie, what is it?" Mom started toward me, but Ronnie grabbed her arm.

"He's coming," Ronnie muttered.

I spun around. "You knew?"

"Not exactly. But I brought my kid to work today. I never do that." He squeezed the little girl tight.

"Why?"

"Because you needed to see you still have people on your side." His voice dropped to a whisper, his eyes going totally glassy.

I looked back outside. Damn it. Magnus and Damon were marching straight toward the shop.

"Mom."

"Yeah, baby?"

"Take Luna and Ronnie. Hide. Now."

Mom grabbed Ronnie and the kid, dragging them into the back. I spun around to face the shop.

I scanned the shelves. Fast. There. Descenting spray. I yanked a bottle down, slammed it onto the floor, and stomped hard. The plastic cracked, and the liquid splattered everywhere.

Instantly, every scent in the room vanished. I dashed to the back, grabbed a mop and a "Wet Floor" sign. Mom was staring at me, eyes wide with panic. I pressed a finger to my lips. *Shh.*

*Bam! Bam! Bam!*

A heavy knock rattled the door. Little Luna looked terrified. Mom hugged her tight, while Ronnie just stared into space, completely zoned out. Like his mind was miles away.

I marched back out. "Hold on! Just a second!" I yelled, tossing the yellow sign down. I stomped right in the puddle, splashing the liquid around to spread the smell.

Another loud knock.

"I said, hold on!" I huffed, making sure I sounded totally pissed off. I grabbed the mop and yanked the door open. "Alpha Magnus. Damon." I feigned a gasp, jumping back. "Whoa. What are you guys doing here?"

"We were follow—" Damon started.

"We came to see Ronnie. Need to grab some supplies," Magnus cut him off, sharp.

I nodded, dragging my mop bucket over to the mess. "Oh. Well, Ronnie had to run out to restock. Won't be back for a few hours." I lied straight to their faces. I turned to them, leaning on the mop. "Can I help you find something?"
