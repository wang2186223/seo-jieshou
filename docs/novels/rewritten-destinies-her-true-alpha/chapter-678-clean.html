Chapter 643

I burst through the dirt, hacking and coughing. My eyes snapped open. I hit the ground hard. Dad stared, his forehead creasing. He opened his mouth to ask what the hell was happening, but I slapped my hand up. *Not now, old man.* I sucked in one massive, clean breath. Time for round two.

I had to hook up with Neptune. Now.

I dialed into the deep water running right under my boots. I shut my eyes. I pictured the waves slamming against the cabin walls, the cold spray hitting my face at the dock, and those deep, untouched veins of the earth.

"Nei tok lo sin kipa!"

I threw my arms wide, flicking my power out like water off wet hands. It sank deep. Instantly, the ground bubbled. Cold water swirled around my ankles, then my knees, climbing fast. I visualized a massive dome over the whole pack territory. A hard-ass shield. We could walk right through it, but humans? No way in hell.

"Tosh hi fic nedit kan ya!"

Boom. Water slammed over my head. My chest squeezed tight. I kicked hard, trying to swim up for air, but the undertow grabbed me. I was trapped, sucked into a fast current heading straight for the ocean.

It was freezing, a wild, pitch-black whirlpool. Honestly? It felt like a crazy water slide. I actually laughed out loud as it dragged me under. I was totally riding the high of the dark water until—whoa. A massive shadow loomed ahead. I dived, kicking like mad to dodge the god tailing me. He sliced through the water, but I did a quick roll right down his massive side.

He whipped around, coming right back at me. Guess he didn't expect me to play tag. Neptune’s deep, booming laugh rattled my ribs. His snake-like body looped around me in a blur. Then, he flashed a fin. *Grab on.* His voice echoed in my head, dripping with fun. *I gotta show you something.* Next thing I knew, we rocketed off.

We nosedived. The sweet water turned salty and thick. The pressure started crushing me. I clamped my mouth shut, lungs burning, freaking out that I was gonna black out. *Breathe, kiddo,* his voice boomed, totally stoked. *My ocean won't hurt you.* He was like a kid wanting to show off his playground. *It’s been ages since I had a playmate. Come check out my core.*

I hesitated, then took a leap of faith and inhaled. Water flooded my throat. I panicked for a split second, but then—nothing. No choking. No burning. I took another huge gulp. The water was literally pumping oxygen straight into my blood. "Holy crap, I'm not dead," I gasped.

He chuckled and gunned it. The deeper we went, the blacker it got. Total pitch-black. Then he said, *Look.*

Bam! Light exploded everywhere. I gasped. We were in the deepest, darkest trench on earth. Weird, freaky fish I’d never seen swam past. Right at the bottom sat a pulsing, pure white coral heart. Gorgeous. I reached out and grabbed a branch. The god shivered. *Focus, kiddo. Keep touching me like that and I might have to fight your mate for you.*

My jaw hit the floor. I shook it off, closed my eyes, and got to work. "Neptune, king of the sea, hear me! Let my power flow through you. Shield my packs. Keep them safe from the bastards coming for them."

Boom. My magic rippled out, tearing through the deep. My grip slipped off the coral. Neptune spun me around and threw me into a fast current heading straight back home.

I shot through the water and exploded back onto the dirt. I collapsed, catching myself on my hands, panting like a dog. My clothes were dripping wet. I blinked, and there was my dad, dropping to his knees right in front of me. Our eyes locked. I couldn't speak, so I just gave him a quick nod and a thumbs-up. Just give me a second.

I hauled myself up. Power was still buzzing in my veins. I yanked the absolute last drop of magic out of the earth. Every single blade of grass that had been stained with blood was clean now. I’d sucked it all dry for the spell.

I spun around. Right then, guards crashed through the tree line. They sprinted straight for my dad, probably to tell him the human convoy was practically on our doorstep. Elara was right there with them, hauling ass toward us.

*Focus on the magic. Call my brother,* Neptune’s voice teased in my head. *No time to catch your breath, kid. The humans are right outside.*
