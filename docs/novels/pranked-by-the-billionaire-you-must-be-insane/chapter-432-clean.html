Chapter 435

"Gen, once we wrap up here, you're coming with me to see Ms. Rainsford," Wallace said. His voice was suddenly all warm and fuzzy.

Just minutes ago, seeing her stand there with the Blackwood girl and the Lancaster heiress had his chest puffing out. Then, boom—Ms. Rainsford actually walked over to ask about Genevieve.

That one little move changed everything. Suddenly, all those high-and-mighty players who used to blow him off were crowding around, trying to chat. Some even dropped hints about setting up their sons with her.

Wallace was sold: Genevieve was his golden ticket.

Genevieve’s heart hammered against her ribs. She forced her face to stay blank and gave a stiff nod. "Sure, Dad."

Wallace grabbed Beverly and bolted, eager to work the room and lock down some cash.

"Miss Lancaster..." Genevieve flashed Vivienne a sweet smile. "Thanks for letting me show off my stuff. Don't worry, I'm signed with Aurevion, so you guys are always my number one."

She was basically kissing Vivienne's ring, making sure the girl knew that even if she became Rainsford's pet, she wouldn't forget who put her on the map. After all, Vivienne was a Lancaster—the richest family in Vaelion. You don't cross someone like that.

But Vivienne just scowled. To her, Genevieve’s little speech felt like a passive-aggressive slap for doubting her earlier. Still, she swallowed her anger for Rainsford's sake and tilted her chin up. "Good. Don't you forget it."

"Oh, please. She's trash from a nobody family," Beatrice sneered, throwing Genevieve a dirty look. "Even if Rainsford takes her in, she's still just your lapdog."

She didn't give a crap that Genevieve’s face turned sour. Beatrice totally despised the girl. One look at Genevieve's cheap, tacky dress made Beatrice feel dirty just standing near her. But hey, a tool is a tool. She still needed her.

Beatrice looked away, focusing back on Vivienne. "Look, your main job right now... is to wake Julian Vance up!"

Vivienne’s eyes darted to the corner of the room. Her fist clenched tight. "But Julian..."

"Screw that country bumpkin! As long as Julian hasn't claimed her in public, you're still in the game!"

Beatrice’s eyes flashed. "Vivienne, Alexandra just finished her little show. She thinks she's hot shit, right? Fine. Use her. Make her performance look like a joke compared to yours. Crush her. Trust me, I'll make Julian see the massive gap between you and that trailer-trash girl. I'll make sure Julian and the entire Lancaster clan lock her out forever. Then Julian will finally see who actually belongs on his arm. And the Lancasters will know who brings the family glory... and who's just a total embarrassment."

Beatrice let out a slow, nasty smirk.

Vivienne blinked. Beatrice was right. If she absolutely killed it on stage and blew Alexandra out of the water, she'd lock down her spot as the city's top IT girl. Then everyone would start shipping her with Julian, saying they were the ultimate power couple.

Vivienne smiled, sashaying up to the main stage. "Happy birthday, Elara."

First up: the gift. Standard protocol.

Compared to Genevieve's cheap box, Vivienne's gift wrap was pure luxury. When the assistant popped it open, the crowd gasped just as loud as they did for Alexandra's brooch. Inside sat a blinding set of diamond jewelry. Right in the middle was a sparkling tiara.

Vivienne beamed. "This is a custom set from Aurevion's head designer, made just for your eighteenth."

Elara stared at it for a second, then pouted. It wasn't even half as cool as the stuff Lyra gave her. And this was from a head designer? Seriously? This was their best?

But Vivienne represented the Lancasters. Elara couldn't just trash them in public, so she forced a fake, tight smile. "Thanks."

It was way colder than her reaction to Alexandra.

Vivienne's smile froze, but she pushed through. "I know you're obsessed with jewelry, Elara, so I planned this forever ago. The main rock on the tiara? It's from an old Caldorian empress's crown. And those hundred and eight diamonds around it? Straight from South Africa."

She dragged out every word, flexing the insane price tag. She practically radiated smugness. Her full set was worth way more than Alexandra's cheap little brooch.
