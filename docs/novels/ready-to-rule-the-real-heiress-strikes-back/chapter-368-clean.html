"Go get some real sleep. You can deal with everything else when you wake up.”

Lydia was running on fumes after two sleepless nights. All she wanted now was to crash and let her brain recover.

"Did you get the room ready?" she asked.

Damien nodded. "It's all set. I'll take you and Miss Lydia there now."

"Alright."

Damien led them to a suite with the best view on the whole mountain. The place was clearly meant for Connor.

Inside, it was as lavish and cozy as a presidential suite in a fancy hotel.

"You both get some good rest," Damien said, giving Connor a look that Lydia couldn't quite read.

She raised an eyebrow. "Only one room?"

Damien hesitated. "This is the only one that's really suitable for you both."

Connor chimed in. "You can have this room. I'll find somewhere else."

Lydia glanced around, taking in the luxury. "Let's just share."

Connor gave her a surprised look. Lydia shrugged. "You haven't slept well these past couple of days either. Let's both get a proper night's sleep. We've got to go herb picking tomorrow."

"Alright."

Damien took the hint and left without another word.

The room was quiet, the soft lights making everything feel warm and dreamy.

Lydia looked over at Connor. "Do you want to shower first, or should I?"

Her question caught him off guard.

Connor stared at her perfect face, not sure what to say for a second.

Lydia smirked. "What's with that look? I mean shower and then sleep. Nothing weird."

Connor felt his face get hot. He had to admit, for a split second, his mind went elsewhere.

"You go ahead. I'll check if there's another bathroom next door."

Lydia nodded. "Sure."

Connor grabbed a robe and headed to the room next door. He took, quick shower and came back to find Lydia already washed up and asleep on the huge bed.

He walked over quietly, his eyes drawn to her peaceful face. Her skin glowed softly under the light, impossibly beautiful.

She reminded him of a sleepy kitten, curled up under the blanket, lost in dreams.

He couldn't resist. He leaned down and pressed a soft kiss to her forehead. "Goodnight, Lydia."

He slept deeply, without a single dream.

When Lydia woke up, she realized she was tucked into a strong, warm embrace.

Connor felt her shift and looked down, his voice rough with sleep. “You're awake?”

Lydia's ears burned. She cleared her throat and nodded. "Yeah."
