Chapter 592

Doc grabbed my old man’s bag first, ripping it open to show us the trick. Then we scrambled. We sprinted across the room, hitting every single wolf and cranking their IVs wide open.

I ended up at this tiny, fragile-looking lady. Man, I felt like dirt. "Sorry, ma'am," I muttered, twisting her valve. Her face went paper-white, and she started shivering hard.

She sucked in a sharp breath, squeezing her eyes shut. "It's fine," she whispered. "We get it." I gave her hand a quick pat and went to stand up, but bam—her hand shot out and locked onto my arm. "We’re gonna pull through, right?" Her eyes were just pure panic and hurt.

"Honestly? I don't know," I said. No point lying to her. "But we’ll bust our asses to keep you guys safe. Promise." She gave a tight nod and let go.

I was halfway across the room when a phone buzzed. Finnian picked up. "Jasper? Good. They asking questions?" He shut up, listening. "Alright, pull the car to the far side of the tarmac. Just wait 'em out till they bolt." Another pause. "Yeah, exactly." Finnian let out a heavy sigh. "Thanks, man. Hit me up when they roll out." He clicked off, sliding down next to my dad's cot. "He'll keep us posted."

Orion and I reached my dad at the same time. "Did your contact say if they brought muscle?" Orion asked.

Finnian blew out a breath. "Nope, the goons are on a separate flight. Guess the big-shot council thinks they're too damn fancy to fly with the help." He let out a dry, humorless chuckle. "That's the holdup. The guards' bird took off half an hour after the bosses. So they're just chilling, waiting for backup. Oh, and they booked a bus and a limo."

"A bus?" I whipped my head toward Orion. "What the hell do they need a bus for?"

Orion’s face drained of color. "They probably called in the whole damn army." His black burner phone buzzed against his palm. He snatched it up. "Talk to me." A beat. "We got guys at the airport. Pull 'em back. Get everyone here now. Good." He hung up.

"Pull who back?" I frowned, totally lost.

"My personal guard," Orion said, talking slow like I was the dumb one.

"Why the hell are they here?" I shot him a look. "Aren't they supposed to be guarding the capital?"

Orion stepped up and slapped a hand on my forehead like I had a fever. "You feeling okay?" He leaned in close. "Babe, I sent my guys here years ago to keep an eye on your dad's pack and Luna. Don't you remember?"

I blinked, brain stalling, until a memory clicked. We were on the phone, way back when I first mentioned Luna. "Wait. That was my freshman year of college. They've been here this whole damn time?" He nodded. "Then who the hell was watching your back at the capital?"

Orion scowled. "Nobody. That's why I had to play nice with Astrid. I couldn't fight back with my best fighters gone." My jaw hit the floor. "I figured you knew," he muttered.

I shook my head, dumbfounded, then spun toward my dad. "Did you know about this?"

My dad shook his head. "No... I mean, yeah, I knew he sent guys for Luna. But once we stashed Ronnie and the kid at the cabin, they vanished. We figured they went back to you."

"Nope," Orion said. "I kept them here, working the shadows to keep Ronnie and Clary safe and fed, especially after you guys went into hiding in the Agora." God, I felt like a total asshole. I'd spent years hating his guts, and the whole time, he was just trying to keep us alive. Orion grabbed my face, forcing me to look at him. "Hey. Stop beating yourself up. Don't do that."

"But I hated your guts for years," I choked out. "And the whole time, you were keeping my kid, my family, and my pack safe." He gave me a soft smile. "That's what a king does. Plus, I was crazy about you, so that made it easy." He kissed me quick. "Still hate me?"

I shook my head. "Hell no. I love you." I gave his shoulder a hard smack. "I've always loved you, you idiot. Even when I wanted to kill you."

He kissed me again, deeper this time. "You're my mate. Even when I was pissed at you, I loved you. Hell, even if we weren't mates, I would've protected Luna. She's the Shaman. We can't let anyone get their hands on her. So drop the guilt." He tapped my nose, then looked at my dad. "My guys will be here in ten minutes. They're hauling ass." My dad's jaw dropped, but he just nodded.

"Alright," my dad said. "Let's figure out how the hell we're gonna survive this absolute shitshow."

I threw my hand up like a kid in class. "Hey, does the council actually know who's in this pack? Like, every single face?"

My dad frowned. "Not sure. They definitely know Finnian, Astrid, and me. Why do you ask?"

"Because I got a plan. But I need to know exactly who they can identify. Then, the rest of you need to vanish." I looked straight at Orion. "That means you too, big guy."

Orion stared at me, his eyes narrowing. "No way in hell am I leaving you alone with those bastards."

"I get it. But they'll get cocky if they think it's just me." I rubbed my hands together, grinning. "And that's exactly how we spring the trap."
