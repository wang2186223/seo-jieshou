Chapter 102

Edward clenched his fists.

If it were Lyra... She’d never pull a stunt like Genevieve. No way. For four years, whenever his dad lost his temper and went to swing, Lyra was always the first to jump in front of him.

The front door slammed open. Wallace’s secretary stood there, white as a sheet. "Edward! Damn it, why aren't you picking up? I called you a million times! The company is blowing up!"

She looked ready to cry, hyperventilating. "They’re pulling out! One after another, everyone is canceling their contracts with Wynn Group! Our cash flow is dead. Zero. And the banks? They’re calling in our loans right now!"

Wallace froze, struggling to breathe. "What? How? Those freeloaders who only signed with us to suck up to Lancaster... didn't they already bail?"

"It’s not the pending deals!" the secretary gasped. "It’s Titancrest! And NovaForge! They just sent official notices. They’re cutting us off completely. No more business. Period."

"Are you kidding me?!" Wallace’s voice cracked. "Titancrest and NovaForge? We’re tight! I literally played golf with them last week. We had a great chat! How the hell does this happen overnight?"

Was this Lancaster's doing too?

No. That was impossible.

Sure, the Lancasters were the richest family in Vaelion City. But Titancrest and NovaForge? They were national heavyweights. Elyndor-wide giants. They could easily go toe-to-toe with the Lancasters.

For four years, Wallace had been on great terms with both CEOs.

Wynn Group bled cash, but those guys never complained. They just kept writing checks, dumping millions into his lap.

They always patted him on the back, telling him he was a business genius and his losses were just bad luck.

And now they just dump him? Just like that?

"It's true, Edward," the secretary whimpered. "We lived off Titancrest and NovaForge. They kept us afloat. Without their cash... we’re dead in the water."

For years, Wynn Group had treated those two giants like a personal ATM. Their entire business network was built on that easy money. Now? The plug was pulled. The heart was ripped out.

Wallace grabbed his temples. The room spun. He stumbled backward, collapsing onto the sofa.

"We're ruined... Oh my god, how did this happen?" Beverly’s eyes went glassy. She clung to Genevieve like a ragdoll, her knees turning to jelly.

The secretary swallowed hard. "Edward, what’s the play? If we don't find a massive cash injection right now... we’re filing for bankruptcy."

Wallace’s brain was short-circuiting. Yesterday, the Lancaster deal had them on top of the world. Today, it was the first domino in a total collapse.

"What if... what if it’s Lyra?"

A hollow, raspy voice drifted down from the landing.

Julian stood at the top of the stairs. The second son looked like absolute trash. He wore a sloppy, unbelted robe, his face covered in dark stubble, eyes sunken from days of hiding away.

He gripped the banister, his hands shaking. "Spencer saw her. She was at the Obsidian Palace. He said... he said she bragged about it. Said she could kill the Lancaster deal in five seconds flat."
