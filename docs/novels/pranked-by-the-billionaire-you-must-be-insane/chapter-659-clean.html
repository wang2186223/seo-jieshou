Chapter 666

Lyra flopped onto the mattress. "I've been off the grid for four years, Phoenix. Honestly, I didn't think the 'Demon' tag carried any weight anymore."

"Are you kidding me? You're literally a legend!" Phoenix squealed, going full fangirl. "Your name is straight-up gold in the streets, babe. Always has been, always will be."

Lyra let out a soft laugh. The girl was too much.

"Oh, wait, hold on," Phoenix clicked her tongue, switching gears. "So, Caspian. I don't know if the guy got a whiff of something, but he went absolutely ballistic right after the heist. He literally locked down the whole city looking for us."

"Seriously, he's a psycho. He even started poking around the Gray Zone."

"I was prepping some dummy leads to throw him off, and then—bam. His guys just vanished. Stopped the hunt cold. It’s like he suddenly doesn't give a crap about a billion-dollar chip."

Phoenix sounded totally baffled. "I mean, seriously? Who just walks away from a billion bucks? Like, hello?"

"I ran a background check. This is totally out of character for him."

The files showed Caspian was a cold-blooded bastard who destroyed anyone who crossed him.

No way in hell he'd just eat a loss like that.

Lyra froze, the towel still draped over her wet hair.

That weird, knowing look her uncle gave her outside the hotel flashed in her mind.

She stared into space for a second. "Hey... what if..."

"What?" Phoenix asked.

"What if Caspian knows who I am, and that's why he dropped it?"

Silence stretched over the line, then Phoenix lost it, laughing hysterically. "Oh my god, babe, stop! If you actually knew the guy, you wouldn't have robbed him blind! A billion dollars, Lyra! You don't even screw over your worst enemies that hard!"

Well, put like that, she actually felt a sharp pang of guilt.

She rubbed the bridge of her nose, clearing her throat. "Yeah, well... Caspian is my uncle."

The cackling stopped instantly.

Total, dead silence.

After a long, agonizing pause...

Phoenix let out a dry, choked cough. "Wait, hold on. Uncle? Like, the scary-as-hell new kingpin of the URS underground... is your godmother's actual brother? Your real-life uncle?"

"Yep."

"Your actual uncle? Which makes him my god-uncle? And we just ripped him off?" Phoenix wasn't exactly a saint, but this hit a nerve. Her godmother had basically raised her, treating her like gold. That made Caspian family. And they just stole a billion dollars from family. How the hell did they play their own people like that?

"I literally just figured it out," Lyra sighed.

If she'd known, she never would've touched him. She respected her grandparents too much to rob their son.

"So, what now? Do we... give the billion back?"

Phoenix sounded like she was passing a kidney stone just saying the words.

Giving up that kind of cash was pure torture.

But she loved her godmother way more than she loved money.

You don't rob family. Period. That was her one hard rule.

Lyra pictured her uncle's face again. "Don't bother."

He'd never take it back if they just handed it over.

She tapped her chin. "I'm staying in the URS for a bit. We'll find a quiet way to launder the billion back into his accounts."

"Ugh, why is this world so damn small?" Phoenix groaned.

A cool billion, right there in their pockets, gone just like that.
