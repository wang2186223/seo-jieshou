NovelVibe

Chapter 81

Finnian’s jaw hung open. "Hold up. Let me get this straight. Your mom hitched herself to Ronnie just to keep Blair? Then how the hell did the deal change? And why?"

I rolled my eyes so hard it hurt. "A few years in, Ronnie caught feelings. He fell for her. His own mate bond was long dead, sure. But my mom? Her real bond was still kicking."

"It never went away..." Freya whispered, the horror finally clicking.

I nodded. "Bingo. Mom never let go of her true mate. She liked Ronnie as a friend, nothing more. But then he wanted a real marriage. He pushed. She shut him down. Hard. But then—"

"He threatened to dump her," Finnian cut in, leaning over the seat, staring right at me. "Used divorce to force her hand."

"Exactly."

Finnian gasped, running out of air. "That’s why... that’s why your dad didn't feel the mate-break pain. Not until you finally ran away."

"Yep. And that's why she's a drunk." I slumped back, letting out a heavy sigh. "She gets absolutely wasted so she can tolerate Ronnie's bed. It keeps him happy. It doesn't happen often, and Ronnie hates the drinking. He tried to force her to stay sober once. She almost tore his throat out."

"Wait, what?" Finnian shrank back into his seat.

"He tried to make her do it sober." I swallowed hard, fighting off a shudder. "Her wolf snapped. Almost shredded him to pieces. He never pushed her again."

"Why didn't she just pack up and leave?"

I let out a dry, bitter laugh. "Are you seriously this dense?"

"I don't get it."

Freya growled, losing her temper. "Listen to me, idiot. If a female is owned, her kids are owned too. Even when they grow up. The law locks them down. No mating. Ever. They're trapped."

Finnian’s head snapped up. He stared at me, begging for a lie. I just nodded. "She would've died before betraying my dad. Even after he threw her away. But she had to save me. It was the only way."

Finnian looked at Freya, then at me. Searching. Hoping. Then, he just snapped. Total meltdown. His eyes went wild, claws ripping out of his fingers. He lunged forward, shredding the leather seats in front of him. "No!" he roared. He ripped a whole seat right out of the floorboards, lifting it to throw. The flight attendant shrieked, bolted down the aisle, and pounded frantically on the cockpit door. Fat lot of good that would do.

I jumped up, grabbed the seat, and slammed it down. "Finnian! Stop!" I tackled him, forcing him to look at me. "Uncle Finnian! Cut it out!"

"No!" His voice cracked, raw with agony. "You're lying." He spun on me, his wolf eyes burning with pure torture. "Tell me you're making this up, kid. Tell me!"

I just shook my head, slow and heavy.

He collapsed. His knees hit the floor. The claws shrank back into normal fingers as he stared up at me, begging. "Please. Tell me it's a lie."

"I can't," I whispered.

And then, the tears came.

"We left her," he choked out.

"What?" I dropped to my knees, wrapping my arms around his shaking shoulders.

"We walked away. We thought we were doing the right thing." He pulled back, gripping my face, forcing me to look at him. "Your dad tossed her aside. I turned my back on her. She was his mate, but damn it, she was my best friend. And we erased her. We left her with zero options."

I could only nod. "Yeah. You did."

"Fifteen years," he whispered, his voice cracking. "Fifteen years I spent hating her. Disgusted by how fast she moved on, how she betrayed your dad. But she had no choice." He slumped back onto his heels, totally defeated. "We turned our backs because we thought we knew best. And we forced her to sell her body to save you. We're monsters."

I nodded again, a single tear slipping down my cheek.

Watching my uncle break down, the truth finally hitting him, made my chest ache. All three of them made their beds. Now they had to lie in them. No one could turn back the clock. Not even my father had that kind of power.

"The Luna lived in hell all these years," Freya spat, her voice dripping with venom. "All because some men thought they knew better." She stood up, shaking her head. "Take this back to your precious Alpha. Make him choke on the consequences of his own damn choices."

"Freya?" I flinched. I'd never heard her sound so vicious.

"No, Lyra." She glared down at Finnian. "People need to learn. When you lie to 'protect' someone, you just end up ruining their life instead."

She turned on her heel and walked away, leaving us alone in the wreckage.
