Chapter 372

I killed the engine in the driveway. Right behind me, Rhys Thorne and Elara Meadow pulled in and parked. Gideon Stone yelled that he was gonna go grab his wife and kid and be back in five flat, then he tore out of there. I hopped out. Mom was already walking out the front door. We just stood there on the asphalt, waiting for the other two to get out of their ride.

Boom. I threw up a soundproof barrier around us. I waited until they walked up to my hood, then leaned over to Mom. "Okay, spill. How'd you know I needed a solid excuse to drag them over here?"

Mom let out a dry chuckle. "Please. Umbra’s got way more brains than you think. She tipped me off while you guys were grabbing lunch. I just sat on it to make sure the timing hit right." I turned to look at her, ready to say something, but she already bypassed me. She looked right at Elara Meadow and Rhys Thorne. "Hey there. I'm Elara, Lyra’s mom. You guys must be Elara Meadow and Rhys Thorne."

They gave a quick nod. "Yeah, that's us." Elara Meadow started to drop into a formal bow, but Mom cut her off fast. "Whoa, stop right there, sweetie. I haven't been a Luna in forever. And honestly? Even when I was, nobody bowed to me. Ever."

Elara Meadow snapped back up, looking back and forth between Mom and me. "But... our Luna makes us do it."

Mom snorted. "Ugh, please. That's because she’s a total fraud. A cheap, insecure backup. The only reason she even got the title is because she got knocked up by Damon and ran off. Then she crawled back the second Damon was a pup and Magnus couldn't keep lying to his old man."

Holy crap. Our jaws literally hit the floor. "Wait, seriously?" I stared at Mom.

She nodded, totally casual. "Yep. Magnus was hunting for some high-class Alpha female to marry. Then boom—our 'beloved' Luna, who was actually just some bottom-tier Gamma, shows up holding a baby. The old Alpha forced them to mate." Mom glanced down, checking her nails. "He only keeps her around because she doesn't give a crap about his cheating or his shady plans."

Rhys Thorne blinked, looking around the empty yard. "Uh, is there a reason we're standing out here in the cold?"

Mom smiled. "I'm waiting on Gideon Stone and his crew. Plus, Lyra made sure nobody can bug us out here. Inside? The walls have ears, unless we lock ourselves in her bedroom." Mom shot me a sharp, knowing look. "You taking them to the cabin?"

"Yeah," I said, nodding. "I know it's gonna be tight. But hey, at least we have enough bathrooms to handle the crowd."

Mom chimed in, "Besides, a little camping will do wonders for a kid who's been locked up her whole life."

"Yeah, hopefully. I just hope she and my girl hit it off." I smiled as another engine rumbled up the driveway. "We gotta make a massive shopping list."

Mom nodded. "We're gonna need a ton of extra gear, too..."

I bit my lip. Damn. No way I could sneak all that stuff through my closet. But then—oh, wait. The tunnel to Dad's pack house. "Actually, I have a workaround." I spun around as Gideon Stone, his mate, and his little girl walked up the driveway. "Hey, Gideon!" I waved and walked over. "You must be Mara Croft." I threw my hand out. She smiled, but man, her eyes looked heavy. Tired.

"That's me," she said, shaking my hand. She nudged the kid. "And this is Lyra Bloom." I looked down. The little girl had this dull, faded red hair, dark circles under her eyes, and a missing front tooth.

I dropped down on my knees to eye level and held out my hand. "Hey, Lyra Bloom. I'm Lyra, too." She smiled, giving my hand a little squeeze.

"Hi." She stared at me, her eyes tired but super curious. "Wow. You're really pretty."

"So are you," I grinned. But her face fell. She looked down, sad. "Hey, what's up, sweetie?"

"I'm not pretty. I'm sick." She looked back up at me. "Sick people can't be pretty."

"Who told you that lie?" I tilted my head. "Honestly? I think sick people are the absolute prettiest. Want to know why?"

She looked over at her mom. Mara gave her a little nod, so the kid turned back to me. "Why?" But before I could even answer, she dropped her head again, staring at her shoes.

"Because I think the prettiest people in the world are warriors." I gently reached out and lifted her chin up. "Being sick? Like you are? That's a straight-up war. Just getting out of bed is a massive fight, right?" She nodded. "But you do it anyway. Every single day. You get up, you get dressed, and you push through, no matter how crappy you feel. That is pure strength. Real strength. And to me, that makes you gorgeous. Don't ever let anyone make you feel bad for being sick. It's not your fault, and they have no clue how much work it takes just to breathe sometimes."

"Really? You mean it?" Her big green eyes went wide and bright.

"Cross my heart." I smiled, and she beamed back. I stood up and noticed Gideon and Mara were both tearing up.

"Well, shoot," Mom sniffled, wiping her nose. "Who's ready to eat?"
