Chapter 320

My apartment? Cleared. Bags? Dumped in the hallway. Next stop: Juniper’s place. We bagged up her remaining junk, then I spotted it—a bag from Daisy’s mom, complete with a sweet little note. I shoved it straight into my backpack. We hauled the rest of her crap and piled it right by her door.

"Well, that’s the last of it," Juniper groaned, letting out a heavy sigh. "Man, I’m actually gonna miss this dump." I gave her a quick nod. "Upward and onward, babe." I nudged her shoulder. "Come on, let’s grab the luggage cart for this mountain of bullshit. We still gotta swing by my house before we even figure out how the hell we’re hauling all this crap to the cabin." I scanned the pile. Honestly? I had zero freaking clue how we’d pull this off.

Juniper slapped my back. "Hey, look on the bright side. You’ve got a truck." I just grunted, locked the door behind us, and headed for the elevator.

The doors slid open downstairs, and boom—there they were. Damon and Magnus, just waiting. I shot Juniper a look, but she just gave me a blank shrug. She was just as clueless as me. "Alpha Magnus," I said, putting on a fake smile as I walked toward the front desk. "Damon." I gave him a stiff nod, then turned to the guy behind the counter. "Flint?"

Flint jumped up from his chair. He’d been staring daggers at the two guys. "Lyra. Hey. What do you need?"

"Can I borrow the luggage cart? We’ve got a ton of gear to haul down." Flint didn't waste a second. He bolted from behind the desk. I started to follow, but he waved me back, cutting a sharp look at Magnus. "I got your bags. You guys clearly have stuff to talk about."

"Flint, my bags are right inside my door. Lyra’s are out in the hall," Juniper called out, trailing after him but looking back at me. "Actually, you know what? I’ll help you." She threw me a quick wink and hopped into the elevator with him.

*Fuck you,* I mouthed as the doors shut. I turned around, putting on my best face for Magnus and Damon. "Didn't know you guys were waiting around for us to pack. I would've rushed if I knew."

Damon took a step closer, flashing a warm smile. "I wanted to help you carry your stuff." I stared at him—my ex-mate—and wondered if Terra, my wolf, ever regretted dumping his ass.

*Hell no.* Terra was watching him through my eyes, totally disgusted. *Sure, he’s got a pretty face, but he’s spineless. A total puppet. He let our pup die because he was too damn stupid to see he was being played.* She snorted, flicking her tail, and checked out. *We’re way past regretting this loser. Or any guy, for that matter.*

I laughed internally. *What about Orion?*

*Well, obviously he’s the exception to our self-control,* Umbra chimed in, rolling her eyes before curling up to ignore me again.

A sudden touch snapped me back. Damon had grabbed my hand. I froze, forcing myself not to yank away, probably looking totally bewildered. "Sorry, what?" I asked.

Damon smiled. "I asked if you wanted to grab some dinner tonight?"

"Can't do tonight." His face instantly fell. I quickly squeezed his hand, playing the part. "I'm really sorry, but I promised my mom I'd eat with her. Plus, after yesterday's drama, she’s probably dying to chew me out." I looked down, letting a shy smile slip, giving his fingers another squeeze. I needed him to think I was flirting. Inside my head, Umbra and Terra were both gagging, but I had to reel this idiot in.

Damon beamed. But Magnus? He just frowned. "Why would she chew you out?"

I let out a heavy sigh. "Because I lost my temper when people started talking trash about my dad." I shook my head, letting my grip loosen just enough so Damon had to squeeze tighter to keep holding on. "I know he’s been dead for two years, and yeah, he screwed her over when I was a kid. But he was still my dad. I loved him. And he set me up for my future." I looked up, locking eyes with Damon. "And the future of my kids."

Magnus nodded, but I caught that quick, greedy glint in his eyes. "Right. The king came down. Said you guys had a long talk about rebuilding the Silvermoon pack."

I nodded, twisting my face into a bitter scowl. Umbra flashed my eyes wolf-bright for a split second—just for some extra drama. Her mental cackling almost made me lose my straight face. *Give 'em the old razzle-dazzle, girl!* she cheered. I barely kept it together.

"He shot me down," I spat. "Said it’s too dangerous for a single female to rebuild a pack. Said I have to figure out something else."

"Did he give you any conditions?" Magnus asked, his mind clearly spinning. I needed to bait the hook a little more.

"Not really. Just said the only way I could rebuild is if the rogues are wiped out... or if I find a mate." I rolled my eyes hard. "Like *that's* ever gonna happen."

"A mate?" Damon perked up instantly, his hand twitching in mine. "Like, a fated, goddess-blessed mate? Or just a chosen one?"

I knitted my brows, acting clueless. "He didn't say. Why?"
