Chapter 543

Freya smirked like she’d just won the lottery. Damon lost his mind. "Gareth!" he shrieked. Nothing. Gareth just sat there on his knees, stiff as a board, staring at nothing with dead, glassy eyes. Blood leaked out of his cuts, slow and lazy, just dripping down. No heartbeat pushing it. His chest was dead quiet. "No, damn it, no!" Damon roared. Freya just cackled. "You crazy bitch, I'm gonna tear you apart myself! He didn't have to die like this!"

"Oh, he deserved every single cut, you asshole!" she screamed right back. "Every touch, every burn, every time he carved into me. Yeah, I kept score. I counted every single breath he wasted on my neck. The bastard got lucky I got locked up before I could finish the job!"

Damon’s eyes went red. He opened his mouth to spit more fire, but Roric cut him off. "Shut the hell up!" Roric snapped. He jerked his chin at me. I walked over. "Attagirl." He popped open the toolkit I was holding and grinned. "Be a doll and hold this for me, will ya, babe?" He played it off like a polite favor, but I felt the greasy pull of his alpha command trying to force my hands.

Orion let out a dry laugh. "Seriously? You pulled all this crap to take my crown, and *that's* the best command you've got? Man, you are pathetic."

Roric bared his teeth. "I don't need the crown. My kid's gonna be way stronger anyway." He smirked, snatched my arm, and dragged me behind the church bench right against his leg. He zipped open the kit, showing off a row of shiny blades. "Alright... time to slice and dice."

"Caspian wouldn't do your dirty work in a million years," Orion taunted, pushing his buttons.

"Caspian's a total bust," Roric spat. "Good thing I had a plan B. Sure, Corbin's a weakling with zero magic, but hey, beggars can't be choosers, right?"

Corbin... Wait. That name rang a bell. The wolf hanging out with Morgana back in those dark caves. Inside my head, Umbra let out a low, nasty growl. She remembered.

Roric sighed, shaking his head. "Man, I really wanted Caspian's chick. Now *she* had some real juice. But..." He glared over at Damon. "Some idiots had to ruin it." He shifted his eyes to Blair. "And some people got way too greedy for their own good. Whatever. It is what it is." He looked back at Orion. "Doesn't even matter. I'm right on the edge of it."

"Close to what, exactly?" Orion scoffed. "You kill me, you get zero power. My Luna's already lined up to take the throne." Hearing that made my gut do a hard flip.

Roric waved it off like it was nothing. "I'll deal with your little Luna later. But the First Family? I'm right on their heels. I can feel it in my bones. I've been tracking their bloodlines, and I'm right there." Umbra snorted in my head. *Oh, you're closer than you think, dumbass.*

Orion actually laughed out loud. "The First Family? No shit, Sherlock. I'm a Lycan. I *am* the First Family."

"Not you Lycan freaks, you moron. The original wolf bloodline." Roric's face went weirdly soft, almost like he was praying. "If I can get my hands on that blood... any kid I breed with them is gonna be a god. Then," he looked back at Orion, his eyes turning nasty, "I'll just slaughter the rest of the family so nobody can get in my way."

Orion chuckled. "And what if they think you're a joke and tell you to screw off?"

"Easy. I'll put 'em down. Just like I did when the King refused to hand over his little girl."

The whole room went dead cold. Orion's eyes flared into that bright, icy blue I loved. "Say that again. I dare you."

Roric burst out laughing. "Oh, you didn't know?" He threw his head back, spinning a knife between his fingers. "Your mom was knocked up with a baby girl. Your old man was hyped. I mean, Lycans only get one kid, right? So the second he smelled that pregnancy, he threw a massive bash. Finding out he had a daughter on the way made him soft. He wanted to hand over the reins to some Alphas. Said the world was changing and we had to keep up." Roric rolled his eyes. "The guy went weak. So I offered him a way out."

"A deal?" Damon blurted out. "To give up his spot? Why the hell would he do that?"

Roric glared at my ex. "Because I was playing the long game, you idiot. You guys think like ants. I told the King if he promised me his unborn daughter's hand in marriage, I'd walk away with a smile."

"She wasn't even out of the womb yet, and you were trying to claim her?" Orion growled, his voice deep and dangerous.

Roric shrugged. "He shot me down. Your dad was another small-minded fool. Said the Moon Goddess would choose her mate, end of story." He let out a dark chuckle. "Yeah, right."

"Wait, what did you do?" Blair asked, totally lost.

"Well..." Roric shrugged. "If he'd just given me the girl, none of this blood would be on my hands. But instead..." He looked at Blair, his fake smile vanishing. His eyes went totally blank, cold as ice. All the life drained out of him, leaving him looking like a walking corpse. Honestly, he probably was one. "Instead, I walked away from my pack, yeah. But then I slaughtered his mate, his unborn kid, and his entire freaking species. I wiped out every single Lycan. Well, almost all of them... until right now." He turned back to Orion, a dead look in his eyes. "But hey, better late than never, huh?" And with a hard thrust, he buried the knife straight into Orion's chest.
