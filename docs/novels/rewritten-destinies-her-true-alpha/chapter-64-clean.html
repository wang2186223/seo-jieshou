Chapter 64

Ten minutes of straight shivering. Still no food cart. I locked eyes with my dad, riding out the agony and forcing myself to breathe. "Hang in there, kiddo," he muttered. I just gnashed my teeth and gave him a tight nod.

"Ah, fuck!" A fresh wave of pain ripped through me, making me shriek. My skeleton started warping. I grabbed my dad's claw and shoved it way deeper into my flesh.

"Whoa, Lyra, stop. That claw is doing more harm than good," the doc barked, rushing over to pull us apart. "You're bleeding out here!" I just bared my fangs and snarled at him.

"You want me to go full wolf? No? Then leave the damn claw in!" I snapped. "I'm trying to lock this shit down!" My jaw bones began to crack and reshape. Screaming, I jammed the claw even deeper.

Bam! The door slammed open. In rolled a food cart, pushed by Hazel, who looked like she'd been dragged through a dirt pile. "Jesus, Hazel, what went down?" I gasped.

"Ran into some trouble outside... but I made it," she wheezed, shoving the cart toward me. I lunged. I grabbed a raw steak, tore a massive chunk out of it, and let out a heavy groan as the bloody juices hit my tongue. I went feral on the meat, completely zoning out until my dad stood up.

Five steaks later, I finally blinked and looked up. The whole room was just gaping at me. "What the hell are you guys looking at?" I mumbled.

"Uh, nothing. Just... wow," the doc muttered, turning back to pack the wound with fresh gauze. My dad covered his mouth, coughing out a dry, "Damn. Impressive." Then he whipped around to face Hazel. "Spill it. What happened?"

"Okay, so there's a crowd outside crying over the Beta," Hazel said, shifting nervously from foot to foot. "The Gamma tried to block me. I told him the Alpha ordered me to bring this food straight to Lyra. He tried to snatch the cart, but people heard us arguing and stepped in. The Gamma literally threw me to the dirt, but then a bunch of pack members—even Freya and Astrid—backed me up and cornered him. I grabbed the cart and bolted. Alpha, seriously, what is going on?"

"Cyrus is out there?" Dad's eyes locked onto mine. I stared at Hazel, who was shaking like a leaf, as a bad feeling started eating at my stomach. I pushed myself up, huffing, but Dad blocked my path. "Hazel, head back to the kitchen. Have a different omega bring the next batch, got it?" She nodded and scurried out. Dad turned on me. "Chill out, kiddo. Take a breath. We don't have the facts yet, and you're way too worked up."

A growl ripped from my throat as my bones started to warp again. Dad just pointed a finger at the bed. I looked over at Uncle Finnian, and inside me, Umbra let out a frustrated snarl but backed off. I nodded, slumped back down, and grabbed another steak. "Yeah. You're right." I chewed, eyes shut, rocking back and forth to fight the urge to shift and tear someone's throat out.

*Terra wants blood. I do too,* Umbra's voice echoed in my head, sounding totally exhausted and desperate. *Rune is dumping all his excess rage on us while he patches up Finnian. We've never dealt with this kind of heat before. Rune's usually better at holding back the wolf, but this bloodlink is frying our brains. He wants blood, but he has to focus on saving his human. We're sorry.* I didn't say anything, just shoved more steak into my mouth.

"Don't sweat it. I got this," I mumbled through a mouthful of meat.

"What was that, sweetie?" Dad asked, turning back to me.

"It's Umbra..." I choked out, swallowing hard. "She's drowning in Rune's urge to shift and kill. She apologized for losing her grip, but Rune is absolutely out for blood right now."

"Rune? Out for blood?" Dad looked totally baffled. "That wolf is literally my voice of reason." I let out a dry laugh.

"Well, not today. He's absolutely livid. Right now, he's burning all his energy to keep Finnian alive, but once that's done? You're gonna have to hold him back, Dad. He won't stop until he gets payback." I ripped into the last piece of meat just as the door clicked open. In came another cart of raw meat.

"Alpha..." the new omega squeaked, swallowing hard. "The Gamma said this is the last cart. He's blocking anyone else from coming in. I told him it was your direct order, but he said unless you show your face and tell him yourself, he's calling bullshit."

"Are you fucking kidding me?" Dad growled, his eyes flaring with wolf-light.

"He knows something's fishy," I said, gesturing for the omega to swap the carts. "Either he's onto me, or he wants to make damn sure Finnian is dead. Go out there and lie to him. Tell him I'm awake but barely holding on. Do whatever it takes to make him believe Finnian kicked the bucket. Sell it." I dug into the third cart, feeling the urge to shift finally back off a little. "Go. I'll keep an eye on Uncle Finnian." Dad nodded. "Alright." And then he bolted.
