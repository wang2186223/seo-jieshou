Chapter 500

Dad and Chris walked off. They huddled in the corner, whispering. The rest of us just stood there, waiting. I caught Finnian staring.

"Hey," he said, sliding into my dad's empty chair. "You good? You look totally wiped. What's wrong?"

Cipher cut in, turning his head. "What went down last night with Gideon Stone? Did the kid make it?"

I leaned back, eyeing both of them. I nodded. "It was a shitshow. But yeah, everyone's alive."

Finnian's eyebrows shot up. "Everyone? I thought it was just the kid."

I shook my head. "Way more messy than that. But I handled it."

Juniper's dad leaned over. "Juniper said you almost bit the dust."

My dad whipped his head around. "Wait, what?" He glared at Chris. "Look, we'll finish this later. I'm in." They shook on it, but Dad's eyes were already glued back on me.

Finnian scrambled out of the chair. Dad claimed his seat back, and Finnian hovered right behind me.

Dad leaned in close. "What did Conall just say?"

I groaned.

"Just spit it out," Cipher rumbled. The voice modulator buzzed, grating on my ears.

I sighed, shaking my head. "Look, by the time I got everyone to the cabin last night, I was already running on empty."

Finnian leaned over my shoulder. "Why? You sick or something?"

"No. Just exhausted."

"Bullshit," Cipher scoffed. "Damon jumped her. Cracked ribs, busted cheekbone, fractured eye socket. She had internal bleeding and still dragged her ass out of the hospital to get back to the pack house."

I stared at him. "How the hell—"

"How do I know?" I couldn't see his face, but I knew he was glaring daggers. "I've had eyes on you since they locked you up." He shook his head. "You let him beat you."

"Yeah, I did," I shot back. "And I'd do it again."

Cipher growled. Deep and angry.

I smirked. "Growl all you want. My body, my call."

I ignored him and looked at Dad. "I needed an excuse to ditch the pack. Damon's little tantrum was the perfect cover."

Cipher growled again, playing the big bad wolf.

"Ladybug," Finnian murmured. I looked up. He looked wrecked. "You could've died."

"It was the only play," I said.

I turned back to Dad. He was dead silent. "I used the injuries to get the king to sign off on my transfer. But it was also a smoke screen."

Dad leaned in. "For what?"

"I went back acting hurt, saying I was just packing up. But really? I was ripping Magnus's mainframe. I've got Gideon Stone running the data right now. That's how I grabbed the emails between Roric and Magnus."

Dad's jaw dropped. "You actually pulled it off?"

I pulled out a flash drive and nodded. "Yep. Copied the whole thing while arguing with Magnus to let me go. Here's your copy."

Dad leaped up, grabbed me, and spun me around. "You're a genius!" He kissed my cheeks. "I knew you had this!" He set me down.

Suddenly, a hand snatched the drive.

"I'll take that," Cipher said, pocketing the USB.

I shrugged. "Knock yourself out. Hope it's got what we need." He nodded and sat back down.

Dad sighed, touching my face. "But you let him hurt you."

I smiled. "Who cares? It worked. And I wasn't in real danger anyway."

Cipher snapped his head toward me. Dad frowned.

"I let him hit me," I explained. "Then I ordered my wolves not to heal me."

"You said that was for the transfer," Dad said, brow furrowed. "But you could've gotten out without getting thrashed."

I snorted. "Yeah, right. Besides, Damon's a joke." A few guys chuckled. "And honestly, hurt or not, I'm no big loss. Look, I know you love me, but I'm replaceable. You and Mom can just make another kid. No mate, no strings. If I kick the bucket, I kick the bucket."

Cipher bolted upright. A roar ripped from his throat, shaking the damn walls. Suddenly, his mask let out a high-pitched, deafening screech. Everyone screamed, clapping their hands over their ears.

With a wild yell, Cipher ripped the mask off. Blood dripped from his ears. He stood there, chest heaving, staring at me.

"You fucking bastards."
