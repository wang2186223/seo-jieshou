Chapter 168

I squeezed out some fake tears, shaking like a leaf. "Please, Alpha! I know you were just taking orders. Just tell the truth, okay? I don't want you to die!"

Magnus let out a huge sigh of relief. Orion, though? He almost lost his shit. I could see the smirk he was trying so hard to hide in his eyes. "Well, Alpha Magnus?" Orion stepped up, completely towering over the guy.

"Alpha Orion—" Magnus started.

"King Rowan," a voice yelled from the crowd. Sounded like Raven. The King flashed a quick grin.

Magnus choked on his words, then corrected himself. "King Rowan... look, I'm stuck between a rock and a hard place here."

"Spill it." Orion grabbed a chair from the side of the stage, dragged it right in front of Magnus, and sat down with his arms crossed.

Magnus froze. "Huh?"

"I said, spill it. You're trapped? Fine. Explain it to me. Right now."

Magnus's jaw dropped. Behind him, three council guys started sweating and fidgeting. Orion just flicked his wrist, telling him to get talking. "What? I mean... come on, how could I ever go against you, my King?"

"Oh, so now you remember I'm your King?" Orion's voice boomed, making everyone in the room flinch. "You remember I run this show? Not because some suits picked me, but because the Goddess chose me. The last damn Lycan."

Magnus hit the floor, bowing low. "I'm sorry! Please, forgive me!"

The guy was a good actor, but his eyes kept darting back to the council. I watched them. One of the councilors was shaking his head, giving him the side-eye.

I leaned over and whispered in Luna's ear. She pulled back, nodded, and spoke up. "My King, looks like this Alpha's loyalty is split. Half for you, half for the council."

Orion glared at the council members. He let out a dark growl that literally forced them to their knees. "Let me remind you, Vincent, one last time. The council is just a beta and a gamma to an alpha. You only have the power I give you. And starting right now? That's zero."

The crowd gasped. The council had been running things since before the old king died. Now Orion was basically firing them all on the spot.

Magnus bowed even lower. "I'm an idiot! The council has been throwing their weight around, bullying all the packs!"

The room went wild. Orion just raised his hands to quiet them down. "Keep talking."

"They used the rogue attacks as leverage. They told the Alphas they'd only help if we did what they wanted."

I nudged Daisy, who was sitting right in front of me, and whispered, "Wait, your pack never got hit, right?"

Daisy blurted it out. I slapped my hand over my face as Magnus whipped his head toward her. "Well, yeah, but we're a huge pack!" Magnus stammered.

"Bullshit!" a deep voice scoffed from the crowd. I didn't know who it was. "Oakenfang is average at best. Silvermoon is the biggest pack, and they've been hit a million times."

Magnus went white. His whole story was falling apart. "Well... the threat of an attack... without the council's help to rebuild... that was enough to—"

"Enough?" Orion leaned in close.

"He's lying!" one council guy barked.

Orion shut him down with a raised hand. "I'll deal with you in a minute." The councilman shut his mouth and nodded. Orion turned back to Magnus. "Enough of what?"

"A threat! They've been riding my ass all week! When the Alphas made the call, the council said too many spots were taken by unranked wolves. They said if we're keeping everyone here, we need to kick the useless ones out!"

Orion stood up. "The useless ones?" His voice dropped to a deadly whisper. His eyes shifted. Dark grey, almost black fur started sprouting on his arms. "The useless ones?!" he roared. "Unranked wolves are the damn backbone of our packs! They are the exact wolves we Alphas are born to protect! They're the whole reason ranks even exist, and you call them useless?!"

"I... I... I..." Magnus stammered, shaking. Orion was absolutely livid.

"You are nothing without your pack! If your unranked wolves walked out, you'd lose your rank and end up a rogue! If you can't get that through your thick skull, I'll kick your whole pack out and let you wander the wild!"

"Please, my King, mercy!" Magnus begged.

Suddenly, a guy in the crowd stood up. "Wait, what did he mean by keeping us here?"

Orion walked up to the podium, growling until Magnus scrambled out of the way. "Look, we didn't want to drop this news on you guys like this. But the attacks are getting worse. Your families agreed. We're scrapping the two-semester system. No summer off, no Christmas or Easter breaks."

"So what's the plan?" the same guy asked.

"Year-round classes. You stay here until you graduate."

The room went dead silent.
