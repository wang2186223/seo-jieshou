Chapter 9

The second we stepped through the door, my dad strolled in. "Look who it is. My two absolute favorites."

"Hey, Dad." I marched over, threw my arms around him, and gave a quick, respectful nod. Skylar stood there, stiff as a board. "Good to see you, Alpha Devlin."

"Drop the formal crap, Skylar. We're family here. Just call me Dev." Skylar's face turned bright red, and she gave a quick nod. Dad turned to her. "Mind if I grab some one-on-one time with my girl?"

"Sure thing, Alpha." Skylar backed out, her eyes screaming pure panic. I shot her a look to calm her down, but honestly? It was useless. She knew damn well what was coming. I trailed Dad straight to his office. Finnian was already waiting. We sat, and Dad didn't waste time. "Spill it."

"What the hell is happening to this pack, Dad?"

"What are you talking about?"

"Since when do low-tier she-wolves get to push the weak ones around?" I snapped my head toward Finnian. He immediately dodged my gaze. "Oh, don't play dumb. You know exactly what's up. What the hell are the beta and gamma females actually doing here?"

"Whoa, hold on!" Finnian glared at me. "That's a low blow."

"I don't care about fair." I locked eyes with my dad. "Your pack is rotting from the inside out, and I want answers. Now."

I stared him down. Hard. Obsidian rumbled in his chest, a warning growl, but Umbra snapped right back, baring her teeth. No way in hell was I backing down while my home went to shit. His growl vibrated through the room, getting louder, but I didn't flinch. Finally, Dad's face softened into a grin. Umbra backed off. "Look at you. All grown up."

I gave a tight nod, then cocked an eyebrow. Dad let out a heavy sigh. "It wasn't supposed to go downhill this fast."

"What is that supposed to mean?"

"Blame Finnian. It was his genius idea," Dad said. I whipped my head toward my uncle. The guy turned white as a sheet.

"Hey, come on, I really thought it would work!" Finnian stammered.

"What would work, exactly?"

"I figured... if your mom saw everyone she cared about hitting rock bottom, she'd run back to us." Dad's voice trailed off, barely a whisper by the end.

I threw my hands up, totally losing it. "Are you kidding me? She's completely cut ties! She literally begged her Alpha to ban anyone from mentioning this pack so she could get some peace and quiet!" I shook my head in disbelief. "What the hell were you thinking?"

"I wasn't." Dad buried his face in his hands, looking completely defeated. "It's been years, Lyra. Years without my mate. I'm losing my mind."

"I get it." I stood up, wrapping an arm around his shoulders. "But Mom still thinks you stabbed her in the back. She's still a total wreck over it."

"I know, I know. But I honestly thought her duty as Luna would override the pain." Dad looked up, and his clueless expression made me burst out laughing.

"Dad, you are an idiot." I shook my head, dropping back into my chair. Then I whipped around to face Finnian. "And you're just as dumb, Uncle."

"Hey! Watch it!" he protested, but I glared at him until he shut up and nodded in agreement.

"We're cleaning up this mess this summer. Period. I am not heading back to Mom's place with this absolute shitshow running wild."

"Alright, sweetheart. We'll handle it."

"No. You guys aren't touching this." Umbra was already on the same page, humming in agreement inside my head. "This is women's business. Get the beta and gamma females in a room with me before tonight's party." Dad flinched, jumping back in his chair. I laughed. "Seriously, Dad? You trusted my best friend with a secret? Dumb move number one. The girl has zero filter. Dumb move number two: even if she kept her mouth shut, I would've sniffed something out from Morgana." I shot a sharp look at my dad. Instantly, his eyes went totally blank.

"Great. Now you've done it," Finnian groaned, slumping back in his seat.

"What?"

"Seriously, every single time someone drops her name or he catches a glimpse of her, he goes completely brain-dead. Then he won't shut the hell up."

"She's just... gorgeous. The most stunning woman on the planet," Dad droned.

"Prettier than Mom?" I threw the bait out, fully expecting him to snap out of it and say no. But he didn't even blink.

"Oh, absolutely. Your mother can't even compete with Morgana. Not even close."

I bolted upright. Something was seriously off. I marched over and grabbed Dad's face in my hands. His eyes were totally vacant, staring at nothing. "Does he always zone out like this when you say her name?"

"Every single time," Finnian said, rolling his eyes. "Give him five minutes, and he'll snap out of it like nothing happened."

"And you think this is normal?" I glanced back at Finnian. Holy shit. His eyes were glazed over too.

"Yeah. Why wouldn't it be?" Finnian muttered, staring right through me. My stomach dropped. This was bad.

"Dad?" I yanked his face close, rubbing my cheek against his while pushing a hard pulse of my aura into him. Dad jerked back, blinking. "Hey, sweetie. When did you get over here?" He smiled, totally clueless. My knees turned to jelly. Something was seriously, terrifyingly wrong. And I was running out of time to fix it.
