Chapter 559

Phoenix still didn't get it. Seriously, what the hell? She’d literally done exactly what Demon wanted. She even snatched up that guy Demon had her eye on and gift-wrapped him right to her. So why was she the one getting absolutely wrecked right now?

Demon had gone full psycho. Way worse than their days back in the Gray Zone four years ago. She’d pitched this whole "let me show you around Elyndor" sisterly bonding crap. But Lyra’s idea of a city tour? Dragging her straight to food street central.

It was pure evil. Phoenix had to stand there, watching mountains of sizzling, mouth-watering food practically mock her. And the catch? Zero bites allowed. Who does that? It was straight-up psychological warfare.

By the time they got back, Phoenix’s willpower was completely shot from holding back her drool all day. And the grand finale? No freaking dinner. Like, actually zero. Just a bowl of hot water masquerading as broth. Seriously, a stray dog wouldn't touch this garbage. *Just shoot me already,* she thought, blinking back actual tears.

So there she was, slouched in the corner of the table, clutching her bowl of sad, tasteless water while everyone else pigged out on a massive feast. Her mouth started watering all over again. Demon was a monster. No competition, hands down the worst human alive. *Just wait,* Phoenix vowed. *The second I get back to the Gray Zone, I’m telling the guys exactly what kind of sick, twisted stuff this devil is pulling.*

Phoenix shot death glares across the table, but Lyra just kept chewing, totally unfazed. Cold as ice. The rest of the Lancaster family traded awkward looks, silently agreeing to mind their own business. Phoenix took another depressing sip of her hot water. The smell of roasted meat drifted right past her nose, making her stomach growl like a wild animal. Watching them eat was pure torture.

She slumped lower, slurping her sad soup as loudly and aggressively as possible just to annoy Demon. Then, a tiny tug pulled at her shirt hem. Already pissed off, Phoenix whipped her head around, ready to bite someone's face off. But her eyes locked onto a juicy, perfectly glazed piece of meat dangling right in front of her.

Her scowl vanished. Her eyes went wide, practically sparkling. She looked up and saw Ethan’s clean-cut, handsome face. He gave her this soft, warm grin. Honestly? In that moment, the guy looked like a literal angel descending from heaven to save her soul. She swallowed hard, pointing a shaky finger at her own chest. *For me?*

Ethan checked to make sure Lyra was looking the other way, then gave a quick, subtle nod. Phoenix didn't hesitate. She shoved her spoon out like a lifeline. *Plop.* The gorgeous piece of meat landed right in it. She shoved the whole thing in her mouth in one shot. Oh, thank god. Heaven.

She chewed, swallowing the savory goodness, her eyes welling up with actual tears of joy. Finally. Real food. This was what living felt like. She blinked up at Ethan, her eyes wide and begging. She looked exactly like a stray kitten waiting for another treat.

Ethan’s smile reached his eyes, softening even more. This girl was something else. She had this edgy, badass look—like she belonged in some wild action movie—but then she had this totally innocent, college-girl vibe going on. It was a crazy contrast, but damn, it was cute. He let out a quiet chuckle, waited for Lyra to look away again, and slipped her another piece.
