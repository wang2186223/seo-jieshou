Chapter 349

Ronnie yanked her inside, threw her into the room, and slammed the door shut. I made a show of stomping to my own room, banging my door loud enough to wake the dead. The second it clicked, I muttered a silencing spell under my breath. My boots went dead silent. I crept back, pressing my ear against Blair’s door.

"Dad, seriously? Why'd you ask her? Why do you keep doing this to me?" Blair was sobbing. Inside, Ronnie’s heavy footsteps paced the floor.

"Shut it, Blair!" Ronnie barked. A sharp whimper cut through the wood. Then his voice dropped, footsteps shuffling closer to her. "I told you already," he hissed, barely a whisper. "We play nice. We kiss Lyra’s ass."

"But why?!" she whined. *Thud.* She was pounding on his chest.

"Blair!" he snapped. "Stop. Just fucking stop. You are not mating with Damon." The rage in his voice was back.

"I’m sick of this!" Blair wailed.

Ronnie dropped his voice to a low hiss again. "She owns this place, Shan. And Magnus needs her on a short leash."

"Why?" she cried. Sounded fake. Like she was putting on a show.

"Because he’s the alpha, damn it!" Ronnie roared, then instantly choked it back to a whisper. "Her old man left her a mountain of cash. Magnus wants to see the numbers first. See if she's worth a mating bond. Relax. Even if Damon marks her, you’re still his number one. Just... behind closed doors."

Blair sniffled. "I'm sick of being his dirty little secret. When we were official, people actually respected me. Now we're hiding, and nobody gives a shit what I say." The bedsprings groaned as she flopped down.

"I get it, but we need her money." Ronnie sat down beside her. Out in the hallway, I leaned my back against the wall, listening hard. "Caspian left her filthy rich. Magnus needs that vault open."

"So why doesn't he just take it?"

Ronnie let out a heavy sigh. "If he could, he would've done it already. But Lyra hates his guts, and she's an alpha. He can't force her mind, and he can't freeze her accounts. Trust me, he tried."

Inside my head, Umbra bared her teeth. *That motherfucker.*

I sent her a wave of calm. *We knew they'd try this. Our money is locked down tight.*

"Why can't we just put a bullet in her head?" Blair whined, her voice muffled like she was burying her face in her hands.

Ronnie snorted. "You think we didn't think of that?"

Blair shifted on the mattress. "If she kicks the bucket, doesn't everything go to Elara?"

"Yeah. And if this was last year when Elara was still head over heels for me, I would've pulled the trigger already. But she's been freezing me out ever since the night of Lyra's run." He growled under his breath. "She still hasn't forgiven me."

"Are you serious?" Blair sounded shocked. "But you guys are sleeping together again!"

Ronnie let out a nasty chuckle. "Only because I threatened her. Well, technically, I had Magnus threaten her, then I played the hero and saved her."

My fists clenched so hard my knuckles popped.

Blair laughed. "And she actually bought that crap?"

"Magnus plays the bad guy perfectly. Why wouldn't she?" Ronnie sighed. "But just because she's back in my bed doesn't mean she's letting me touch her. She's got this stupid pillow wall built between us."

"What about the kudzu?" Blair stood up. Footsteps headed toward the door. I braced to bolt, but I couldn't walk away yet.

"Failed. Freya ruined the first batch. So I had Magnus and Gareth take care of her."

My blood ran cold. My fists squeezed tighter. He was in on it.

Inside me, Terra snarled, snapping her jaws. *When we burn this place to the ground, he burns first.*

Umbra slithered out of the shadows. *Oh, he's dead.*

I gave a sharp nod, locking my focus back on the room.

"The second time, she just dumped it," Ronnie grumbled. "Like she doesn't trust a single thing I hand her."

*No shit,* I thought. *Because she doesn't.*

"Do you have any left?" Blair asked.

I froze.

"Yeah. One last dose. I need to slip it into something Lyra gives her. That way, she won't think twice before taking it. But..." He trailed off.

"But what?" Blair walked back into the room. I crept closer to the wood again.

"I need you ready," Ronnie said.

"Ready for what?"

"Damon took the rest. He's going to use it on Lyra."

"What?!" Blair shrieked.

A scuffle broke out. Ronnie must've grabbed her. "Shut up and listen!" he hissed, wrestling her down. "Magnus made some trash investments. They need Lyra's money. Now."

"He's mine, Dad! You guys promised!" Blair started bawling.

"I know, I know. And Damon still wants you. I told you he had to push you away so Lyra thinks they're done. We needed her to let her guard down and get close to him." He sighed. "I swear, he misses you. Once she's knocked up, he's coming right back to you."

"Why does she have to get pregnant?" Blair sniffled. "Why can't they just mate, and then he kills her?"
