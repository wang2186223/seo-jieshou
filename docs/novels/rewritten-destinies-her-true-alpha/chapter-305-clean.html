Chapter 305

Total silence. The room went dead. Every single eye was glued to Luna. She sat there, stiff as a board, completely paralyzed. A whole minute ticked by. I couldn't take it anymore, so I grabbed a fistful of her hair and gave it a gentle tug. "Yo, you still breathing?" Luna blinked, snapped out of it, and slumped back. She looked at me, jaw hanging open, but not a damn sound came out. I grabbed her hand and shook it. She let out a massive sigh, like a popped balloon. "Come on, spill."

"But... my family is Japanese," Luna finally stammered, getting her brain back online.

"Sure, maybe for a few generations," I shot back. "But who's to say they didn't migrate there from somewhere else way back when?"

Luna frowned, chewing on that. "I mean... I guess that's possible." She locked eyes with me. "But I have zero clue where they came from before that."

"Easy fix," I said, shutting my eyes to work my magic.

"Hold up!" Daisy blurted out.

My eyes snapped open. I whipped my head toward her. "What's the issue?"

Her face went bright red. She immediately started chewing on her fingernails. "Look, before you go digging into where Chang'e came from, can you... like, do Fawn and me first?"

"Why's that?" Juniper reached over and slapped Daisy's hand away from her nails.

Daisy looked up, totally stressed. "Because I don't know jack about gods." I choked back a laugh and just nodded. She let out a heavy sigh. "Look, if Fawn and I don't know our goddesses either, we might as well find out all three together. I don't know what kind of crazy ritual you're about to pull, but maybe we should just wait. Just in case." She flashed a messy, apologetic grin.

This time, I actually snorted. "Fair point. I was literally just gonna call my grandma. Or, you know, Google it." Fawn threw her head back and cracked up. I shrugged. "Hey, don't laugh! Daisy's right. I know as much about these gods as I do about where they came from. Which is basically zero."

Luna looked back and forth at us, then nodded. "Yeah, she's right. Let's wait. We can get all our answers in one go." She scraped her chair back, stood up, and pointed at Fawn. "Your turn. Get up there."

Fawn looked like she was about to puke. But she shook her head, trying to snap out of it. "Actually, why am I even sweating this? No goddess is gonna claim a nobody like me anyway." She laughed it off, grabbed the lighter, and prepped her bowl. I slid into the hot seat. Luna took my old spot. By the time I turned around, Fawn was already squeezing blood into the bowl. I grabbed her hand, locking my fingers with hers. Boom. Thick, dark blue smoke exploded into the air right in front of her face. "No freaking way," Fawn whispered. But the entity forming in the smoke just chuckled.

"Is that any way to greet your creator, kiddo?" The goddess had this smooth, melodic accent, but her voice was warm. Her eyes glowed with a soft silver light as she smirked down at us. "Took you long enough to remember where you came from. Welcome home, girl. I am Branwen, and you are mine." And just like that, poof. She vanished.

Fawn whipped her head toward me, eyes wide. I gave her a reassuring grin. "Told you so," she whispered.

"Maybe," I said. "But we won't know for sure until Daisy goes." All eyes snapped to Daisy. The girl was practically vibrating with nervous energy as she snatched up the lighter. I laughed, swapping seats with Fawn. The second my butt hit the chair, Daisy was already dripping blood into her bowl. I grabbed her hands, and we chanted the words together.

Pitch-black smoke billowed up. We all froze. The figure taking shape in front of Daisy made our jaws drop. It was a guy. Dark hair, piercing silver eyes, staring right down at her. "Well, look at that. Thought you forgot about me, pup." The god smirked, totally amused, and threw her a wink. "I, Odin, claim you." Boom. Gone.

I turned to Daisy, my mouth hanging wide open. "Holy crap. I did not see that coming. Why the hell did I think we'd only get goddesses? But that guy..."

"...was a straight-up god," Daisy finished for me.

I nodded fast. "Uh, yeah. Absolutely."

"I'm... I'm descended from a god?" Her eyes were huge, her bottom lip quivering.

"Hell yeah, you are," I said, letting out a breath and leaning back. Damn, that felt good. Total validation. "Alright. One last thing, then we crash." I shut my eyes. "Grandma, I need backup. I don't know these gods and goddesses for shit, and I need to know who we're dealing with."

A ghostly touch brushed my shoulder. I looked up. There she was, standing right next to me. She leaned down and pressed a kiss to my forehead. "Sweetheart, you are running on fumes."

I blinked my eyes open and nodded. "I know, but we had to settle this tonight."

Grandma nodded back. "Damn right you did." She turned to the rest of the crew. "Hey, girls." She smiled, walking over to Juniper. "I know, I don't need to explain much to you. You know where you come from. Your history is alive and well. Ma'at is watching over her own. Her sands will always be your home."

Juniper beamed and nodded. "Still, seeing her was absolutely insane." Grandma leaned down, kissed Juniper's forehead, and then turned her gaze to Luna.
