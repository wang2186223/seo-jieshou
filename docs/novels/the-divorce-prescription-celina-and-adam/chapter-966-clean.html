Hailey was left speechless by Jonah's remark.

Kaia was usually sharp-tongued, quick with a cutting remark. She had plenty to say, but Jonah's gentle "my lady" left her completely speechless.

That simple phrase was the most heartfelt thing she had ever heard. It was more meaningful than any "babe" or "darling" could ever sound.

Grumbling, Kaia stayed quiet.

Hailey gave Jonah a sharp look that said, "Alright, you win."

Jonah smiled.

Soon, they arrived at the bar.

Justin and Joanne had both shown up. Jonah led Hailey and Kaia to a private luxury booth, where a few trust fund kids were already waiting.

Everyone took their seats. Justin looked at Hailey and asked, "Ms. Hailey, what are you doing here?"

Hailey replied, "Your best friend, Jonah, invited me. Honestly, I wasn't planning on coming."

Furious, Joanne clenched her fists.

Hailey said nothing, sitting quietly and watching the show unfold.
