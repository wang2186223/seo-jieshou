NovelVibe

Chapter 213

The banner went live. I plopped back into my seat. Orion was finally cutting to the chase. I kept a sneaky eye on Damon. Thank god, he’d cooled his jets.

"Get to the point, Your Majesty!" a reporter snapped. She looked ready to pop a blood vessel. The girls next to me snickered.

"What's so funny?" I nudged them.

Fawn leaned in close. "She already knows the big news, but she can't leak it until Orion spills the beans. It’s killing her."

I stared at my screen. "Who even is she?"

"Can't recall her name," Fawn said, tapping her phone. "But she’s a regular at these gigs. Look at her. She’s practically drooling. She’s been thirsty for the King since he took the throne and she was just a rookie reporter."

"Ugh, gross." He was basically a kid back then.

"Yuuup." Fawn popped the 'p'. "My mom said this chick has been trying to get a rise out of him for years. He always shuts her out."

Orion shot the reporter a death stare, then flashed a grin. "Alright, here's the deal. The council says it’s time. I’m starting the search for a mate."

The room went absolutely nuts. Orion threw his hands up, yelling, "Whoa, hold your horses! Let me talk!" But the crowd was a pack of starving wolves. They weren't listening.

"Quiet."

Even through a tiny phone screen, his alpha aura hit like a truck. Holy crap. "Now, let me explain how this works."

Instantly, the room froze. People who had jumped up scrambled back into their seats.

"We’ve got an application form. Anyone can apply. Look, I’m not saying guys can’t submit one, but I’m straight, so... do the math." He smirked. My heart did a weird little flip. "Fill it out, send it to the council’s official inbox. And hey, if you’ve got my personal email, shoot it there. I’ll print those out myself."

"What’s the game plan?" someone yelled.

"Is there an age limit?" another barked.

"No age limit. If you’re a single wolf, you’re in. You’ve got exactly two days, so don't slack off. The form went live last night, so get on it. But seriously, only one submission per person. Once the clock runs out, the council and I will screen them. We’ll pick the best candidates, and then I’ll personally reach out to set up a meeting."

Some councilman strutted onto the stage, wearing this smug, punchable grin. God, I wanted to slap it off his face. "What the King means," the guy chimed in, "is he’s going on dates with the top picks."

Orion glared at him until the guy took a step back.

"No. I said a meeting. I need to vibe-check them before I agree to a real date. But yeah, after that, we’ll do the dating thing and weed out the weak links until we have our finalists."

"And then what?" The first reporter looked ready to jump his bones right there.

"Well, it depends," Orion smiled. "I haven't planned that far. There’s still a shot I find my actual, goddess-given mate in this pile. If that happens, game over. The contest is done."

The council looked ready to explode. But seriously, what did they expect?

"Wait, so if you find your true mate, the other girls just get dumped?"

Orion chuckled. "Duh. Obviously. I want my true mate. The one the goddess picked just for me. My soulmate. Why would I settle for a runner-up when I can have her?" He shook his head like it was the dumbest question ever. Honestly, it was. Who chooses a backup over their actual mate?

That same annoying councilman stepped back up. I could see the vein throbbing in Orion’s forehead. "The odds aren't great, unfortunately," the guy said. Orion glared daggers. "Lycans usually mate with other Lycans. Since he’s the last of his kind, he’ll likely have to pick a mate from this contest in the coming months."

Orion let out a low growl. The councilman practically tripped over his own feet backing away.

"Sure, the odds are slim," Orion snapped. "But this genius keeps forgetting that Lycans mate with whoever the goddess chooses, wolf or Lycan. Still, I’m a man of my word. The search starts in the coming months." Orion shot the councilman a look of pure, unadulterated hatred. "We're done here."

Then he stormed off the stage.
