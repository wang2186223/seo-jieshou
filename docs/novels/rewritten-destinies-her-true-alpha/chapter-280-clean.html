NovelVibe

Chapter 280

I ripped the envelope open. God, my chest ached like I'd been kicked. There it was—Ronnie’s neat, perfect handwriting staring back at me.

*Lyra,*

*By the time you read this, I’m probably rotting in some cold dirt. I did what I had to do to save my baby girl. I know you just spent the last hour babysitting her, and seriously, thank you. But listen to me. Do not let her stay awake. She’ll end up hurting herself—I’ve seen it happen. I’m begging you, put her under.*

*Knock her out with your magic every single night. Do it for the next few months. Yeah, she’s just a kid, but she’s my kid. I know how her brain works. She’s got this ancient soul trapped in a tiny body, and she notices way more than you think. But she doesn't get the big picture yet. If she tries to piece it together, she’s going to get herself killed. And she’ll drag the whole damn world down with her.*

I snapped my head up. Juniper was hovering right there. "Hey, what's up?" she asked, taking a step closer.

"He wants me to drug her," I muttered. "With magic. For months."

Juniper snatched the paper and scanned the lines. "Damn. He saw the future," she said, shoving it back into my hands. "Look, I get it. The kid is grieving. Hell, when I thought my folks were dead, I was ready to do some real stupid, reckless shit. But you pulled me back from the edge."

My jaw dropped. "You never said a word about that."

She shrugged a shoulder. "Well, I hadn't really figured out the details. I just knew I didn't want to exist anymore if everyone I loved was gone. Just like her." She started twisting her family ring around her finger. "Except my parents weren't actually dead. Hers is. Even if she can't fully process it, she knows she lost her dad. Her whole world." Juniper gestured toward the paper. "Do what he says. Put her to sleep."

I gave a slow nod and looked back down at the page.

*I hate dumping this on you. You're already doing a million things, basically raising my kid now. But please, bury my body near the cabin. I want Luna to have a place to visit me, to know I'm still around. I want to hear her when she talks to me, to walk beside her when things get rough. I know deep down she'll feel it, and you'll keep my memory alive, but I need this. I need it so my soul can finally rest.*

*There's a sweet spot right behind the cabin. Right where the woods start, under that massive oak tree. We used to hang out there all the time, eating picnics and watching the sun go down over the roof. She always told me it was her favorite spot in the world. I want to stay right there with her.*

*I added some extra rooms for your crew. They’ll back you up while you're busy running things with your pack. Seriously, take that job Magnus offered you. I'm not going to nag you about it anymore, but you have to do it. Trust me, the commute won't be a pain at all.*

I scowled. How the hell was that supposed to work?

*Take a look at that stone of yours,* the letter read. *You'll get it.*

Jesus. The guy was just as bad as my grandma with the riddles.

*Just a couple more things. Luna’s got a box of letters in her room, and I tucked another one in here for her. I wrote them for all her big milestones so she knows her dad’s still got her back. Talk to her about me. Tell her about her mom. I left some videos and some more letters for you about our story. Show them to her when she’s grown up. She needs to know her roots, and how crazy in love we were when we had her. I want her to know how much her mom blew my mind. Our time together was short—just a blip in my long-ass life—but it was intense. Meeting her mom was the second best thing that ever happened to me. But Luna? Luna was always number one.*

*Holding that baby in my arms, even while my heart was breaking over losing her mother, was the only light in a life that was pitch black. I’ve been around a long, long time—longer than anyone realizes—and I never knew what warmth felt like until those two walked into my life. Luna needs to know that.*

*Her wolf is going to be weird. Like, nothing you’ve ever seen. But hey, you and your pack know a thing or two about being freaks, right? Don't let her feel insecure about it. She’s going to feel like an outsider, and I don't want that. She’s got a crazy road ahead of her, but she needs you to hold her hand until she can stand on her own two feet.*

*But here’s the big one. Promise me this. Love her. Love her like you gave birth to her yourself. Love her the way I do. Even when you find your mate, even when you start popping out your own pups—keep loving her for me.*

*Meeting you was the third best thing that ever happened to me, Lyra. Because the second I saw you, I knew my baby would be safe.*

*Thanks for everything.*
