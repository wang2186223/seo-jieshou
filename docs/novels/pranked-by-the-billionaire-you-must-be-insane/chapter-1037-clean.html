Chapter 1054

A massive racket shook the dorms. Since The Spire sat right in the middle of the residential zone, everyone dumped their half-unpacked bags and sprinted toward the noise. A mob of students packed the hallway outside the busted-in door, stretching their necks to peek inside. Holy crap. Heads shook, and mouths hung wide open.

Lyra had officially lost her damn mind. Seriously. She’d literally broken the Chief Arbiter an hour ago, and now she was straight-up looting his penthouse? Didn't she care about getting kicked out of the tournament? Actually, wait. No. Why would she? The girl was a freak of nature—ten straight team wins, shattering every record in the book. Her score was so high the runner-up wasn't even in the same zip code. Besides, the solo matches were up next, and the feds needed her to absolutely destroy the foreign tech nerds. No way the suits upstairs would disqualify their golden goose over a little brawl. She knew she was untouchable.

"She’s begging to get killed," Willow hissed.

She and Vivienne shoved through the crowd, stopping dead at the sight of Brutus’s designer suits, bags, and cologne smashed all over the dirt. Their faces went totally white. Brutus was their only ticket to survival here. They’d already messed up their chance to humble Lyra on day one. Were they seriously just going to let her run this whole island?

Pushing to the front, they saw six massive, jacked security guards charging straight at Lyra. A nasty grin spread across Willow’s face. Oh, hell yes. Finally. Crush her. Slam that cocky bitch into the floorboards. Let’s see how tough she acts with a face full of dirt. Lyra could fight, sure, but six highly trained, ex-military pros? She was dead meat.

"Whoa, this is messed up," someone whispered, backing away. "Six huge guys against one girl? Seriously, no shame?"

"She’s done for," another muttered.

The crowd shivered, terrified. Those guards were built like brick walls. Lyra was about to end up in the ICU.

Except, she didn't. The beatdown everyone expected never happened. Before anyone could even blink—*BOOM!*

The first two guards didn't even get close enough to grab her jacket. A nasty, bone-snapping crack echoed through the room. Both guys flew backward, feet off the ground, defying gravity for a split second. They hit the back wall so hard the drywall crumbled. They couldn't even squeeze out a scream before sliding down into a silent, unconscious pile.

The other four guards froze, trying to slam on the brakes. Too late. Lyra blurred. She was fast as hell, but every move looked effortlessly smooth, almost classy. A low kick snapped a knee. A brutal elbow shattered a jaw. Every hit was precise, mean, and targeted a sweet spot.

In under thirty seconds, the whole squad of elite vets was completely wrecked. They were rolled out across the fancy rugs, groaning, holding broken arms and busted ribs. The last guy took a brutal boot straight to the chest, stumbling backward until he crashed hard into the doorframe—right on top of Willow and Vivienne.
