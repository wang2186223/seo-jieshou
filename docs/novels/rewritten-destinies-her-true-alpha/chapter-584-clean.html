Chapter 584

We packed the bags tight. Magnus’s stash of lupine juice was bone dry, so we hauled ass out of the clinic. Rhys clicked the lock shut. I gave him a look, and he immediately started rubbing his neck, looking guilty as hell.

"Uh, force of habit, I guess," he mumbled, his ears turning bright red when I looked away. "Everyone's already over at the main house waiting for the meeting anyway."

*Smack.*

Orion shoved him hard, making Rhys let out a sharp yelp. "Back off my girl, man," Orion growled. "You're my buddy, but I will absolutely end you."

I couldn't help but smirk as I started walking.

"Hey, I wasn't doing anything!" Rhys protested, but Orion just snorted and kept pace behind me. Rhys cleared his throat, trying to change the subject. "So, look, if you need this juice so damn fast, why are we wasting time on a town hall?"

I sighed, thinking about my dad's pack. "Yeah, we need it. But I'm the Alpha here now. I can't just ignore these guys."

We rounded the corner onto the main street, and my jaw dropped. The place was a total shitshow.

"What the actual fuck?" Rhys gasped.

It was pure chaos. Wolves were covered in blood, throwing punches. In the back, two guys were half-shifted, ripping into each other with claws out.

Orion groaned. "See? This is exactly why we gotta handle this right now."

I nodded, took a breath, and slammed them with my aura.

Boom. The bystanders hit the dirt instantly, dropping to their knees. But the guys actually fighting were too hyped up on adrenaline to feel it. I looked at my guys. "Go split them up. Now."

Orion and Rhys bolted into the fray while I grabbed the heavy bags and marched toward the porch steps. Every eye locked onto me. I dialed up the pressure. My Alpha energy pinned them to the ground, but I laced it with my Gamma power to stop the panic.

"Oakenfang! Listen up!" I barked.

Heads bowed. Once the boys got dragged apart and the rest of the crowd finally hit their knees, I sneered at them. "Seriously? One day without a boss and you guys turn into animals?" I raised an eyebrow, and the elders actually had the grace to look embarrassed.

Then the shouting started.

"Where's Magnus?"

"Where the hell is Ronnie?"

"What about Damon and Blair?"

The crowd was losing its mind. I slammed the bags down on the wood, crossed my arms, and glared. "Oh, so now you're begging for the guy who literally treated you like livestock?"

"What the hell does that mean?" an old woman yelled.

I gestured for her to stand up.

"What's the play here, Lyra? You trying to hijack this pack? The King and the Council won't let you get away with this."

I just smirked and pointed behind her. Orion was already walking up. I waited until he stood right next to me before saying a word.

The old lady's jaw dropped. She bowed fast. "Your Majesty? What... what is this?"

The crowd started buzzing, but Orion put his hands up to shut them down. "Look, we're on a clock here, so I'll make this quick. If you want details, ask the parents with young pups—they'll fill you in on the horror stories. All you need to know right now is that Magnus, Ronnie, and Damon are locked up behind bars for the sick shit they did here."

"Wait, what about the Gamma?" someone yelled.

I shook my head. "Dead."

The crowd gasped, totally shocked.

"Then who's running things?"

"What happens to us?"

"Are they kicking us out? What about our houses? The kids' schools?"

Panic was spreading like wildfire. Orion pinched his nose, looking stressed as hell. He shot me a look. "We gotta go. We don't have time for a Q&A."

I threw my hands up. "Chill out, Oakenfang!" I blasted them with another wave of calming energy. The noise died down instantly. "Look, this all just went down, so we're still figuring it out. But get this straight: nobody is getting evicted. Nobody is moving unless they want to. Your jobs and your homes are safe."

The crowd relaxed a bit. I scanned the faces. "Rhys, get up here."

Rhys squeezed through the crowd, looking totally lost. I waved him over and dragged him to the front.

"Wait, what do you want me to do?" he muttered.

Orion got the hint immediately. He grinned, slapping Rhys on the back so hard he nearly stumbled. "Folks, meet Dr. Rhys," Orion announced. "He's an Alpha wolf who went to med school because he was way down the line of succession. He trained under the Royal Doc in the capital and has been helping out poor families all over the territory." People started nodding, liking what they heard. "He's taking over as your temp Alpha until we get a permanent boss."

Rhys froze, slowly turning his head to stare at Orion. "Wait... what?"

"Congrats on the promotion, buddy," I laughed, giving him a quick pat on the back. "We gotta bolt, but you've got Orion's number. Hit him up if anything goes sideways."

Orion and I snatched up the bags and leaped off the porch.

"Hey! Hold on a second!" Rhys yelled after us, but we were already sprinting back to my place.
