Chapter 518

*Bam, bam, bam.*

Someone was hammering on the door. I let out a miserable groan. "Ugh, go away..."

"Lyra?" It was Finnian. I groaned louder. "Ladybug, open up. We need to talk."

Orion squinted one eye open. "Who the hell is banging on the door this early?"

I dragged myself up, aching all over. Damn, my body was sore. I checked the clock. "Seriously? It's nine AM." I rubbed my eyes, and as I turned, the sheet slid right off. The cool air hit my bare skin, giving me instant goosebumps, and I gasped.

Orion's hand shot out. "Give me five minutes to shake off the sleep, babe, and I'm all yours." His finger traced my breast. I whimpered. "God," he muttered, "I'm one lucky bastard."

"Ladybug?" *Knock, knock.* "You good in there?"

I blew out a breath and dodged Orion's hand.

"Tease," he muttered, sitting up with the blanket bunched around his waist.

I snorted, snatching my robe. "Pretty sure you're running on empty, big guy."

He laughed. "Hey, a guy can try."

I stood there, waiting. Usually, the fever would flare up right about now and drag me straight back to bed. I spun around. Orion raised an eyebrow, watching me. We waited.

"Nothing?" he asked.

I tilted my head, gave it another second, and nodded. "Yeah. The heat's totally gone." I grinned.

"Any regrets?" He tried to sound casual, but his knuckles were white, gripping the sheets.

"Lyra?" Another knock rattled the door.

I ignored it. My eyes were locked on my mate. I walked back, climbed onto his lap, and looked at him. "Not a single one. What about you?"

He cupped the back of my neck and pulled me into a hard kiss. A shiver shot straight down my spine, and my skin tingled all over again. He pulled back just enough to look at me. "Hell no." He gave me one last quick kiss. "Now go deal with him."

*Bang, bang, bang.*

"Lyra!" Finnian sounded panicked now. "What the hell is happening in there?"

I sighed, stood up, and turned around. Orion leaned over and slapped my ass, grinning like an idiot. I laughed, rolled my eyes, and walked over to open the door.

"Hey," I said, leaning against the frame. "What's the emergency?"

Finnian scanned me from head to toe, frowning. "What the hell happened to you?"

I looked down at myself, accidentally letting the door swing wide open. Finnian gasped. His eyes went sharp.

"Uh, nothing..." My voice trailed off. I followed his stare straight to the bed. Orion was sitting there, smiling like a kid who just stole the whole cookie jar.

"Morning, Finnian," Orion said, giving a little wave.

Finnian whipped his head back to me. "Lyra." He looked totally lost. "I thought you were waiting for your actual mate..." He looked hurt, and then his face went red with anger.

I pulled my robe down at the shoulder, and Orion tilted his head to show his neck. The matching marks were right there. "I did wait," I said.

Finnian leaned closer, staring. His eyes traced the moon and stars etched into my skin. "You... you're mated to the king?" He stumbled back a step, then dropped straight to one knee. "My queen."

I lunged forward and grabbed his hands. "Uncle Finnian, stop! Get up, please." I glanced back at Orion, who was practically choking trying not to laugh. "You are never bowing to me." I hauled him to his feet. "Seriously, what's going on? Why are you here?"

He swallowed hard. "Your dad sent me. The rogues are on the move." He looked back and forth between us. "He wants both of you in his office right now. I was actually coming to find you." He glared at Orion. "How the hell did this even happen?"

I let out a laugh. "We actually talked, okay? We decided to stop lying to each other. And, well, my heat kicked in last night."

Finnian's eyes locked onto Orion like a hawk. "You took advantage of her heat to claim her?" he snarled, sounding completely feral. He took a menacing step toward the bed.

I threw my hand out. "No! God, no!"

"Are you threatening me, Beta Ronald?" Orion growled, his eyes flashing dangerous and dark, shifting to Shadow's.

I whipped around and pointed a finger right at Orion's face. "You better shut the fuck up right now."

Orion glared at Finnian for a beat, but then his anger just melted away. "Whatever you say, babe," Orion said, leaning back against the headboard with a smug grin.

I shook my head, exasperated. "He didn't just choose me, Finnian. Look closer at the mark." I leaned in so he could see. "This is a goddess-given mark."

Finnian stared at my shoulder again, and I watched the tension leave his shoulders. "He's... your true mate?"

I nodded. "I let my scent out, and then Orion did the same." I looked back at my guy. "He's my fated mate. The goddess chose him."
