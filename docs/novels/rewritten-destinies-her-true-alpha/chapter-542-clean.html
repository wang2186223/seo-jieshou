Chapter 542

Roric spun back to Orion. "Alright... what the hell am I gonna do with you now?"

Astrid smirked, sliding right onto Orion’s lap. "See? Much better, huh? You could’ve had all of this, babe. If you just played nice, we would’ve let you live." She tapped his cheek, totally ignoring his low growl. "Look at you, big, tough guy, right?" She laughed, stood up, and whipped her hand across his face.

The slap cracked through the makeshift room. "Astrid, cut it out."

"Hell no, I won't. You have no idea what this bastard did to me. The sheer humiliation." She smacked Orion again. Deep inside me, both my wolves started snarling.

*Guys, chill. You’re making it impossible to hold it together. I can handle the magic for a sec. Go check on your mate and cool off.* I shoved them both toward Orion. They needed to see Shadow was okay, and I needed a damn second to steady my shaking hands. If this father-daughter freak show kept pushing, I was gonna blow our cover.

Freya burst out laughing. Astrid spun on her heel. "What’s so damn funny?" she shrieked right in the gamma’s face.

"You." Freya cackled harder. "Look at yourself. You’re just like Blair."

Every eye in the room snapped to Freya. Astrid marched over and grabbed Freya’s jaw. "Say that again."

Freya smirked. "I said you’re a carbon copy of Blair." I watched Freya’s eyes flick down to the girl shivering at Damon’s feet.

Astrid squeezed her face hard. "Spit it out. What do you mean?"

Freya looked back and forth between the two. "Look at her. Face down at Damon’s boots, begging for a scrap of attention. Chasing a mate who doesn't want her."

"And what’s that got to do with me?" Astrid bared her fangs, leaning in close.

Freya snorted. "Play dumb all you want, honey, but you’re the exact same. Sure, you’re not begging on the floor, and yeah, you’ve got more muscle than Blair. But you’re still chasing a guy who isn't yours."

Astrid rolled her eyes. "It was a tactical move. Part of the plan."

Freya let out a dry laugh. "Nice try. I might’ve believed that, but I saw your face when you spotted the king’s mate mark. You looked absolutely wrecked. You realized he’s totally out of your league now."

"Shut your mouth."

"You actually thought if you forced him to mate, you could change him. Never mind that you murdered his dad, hunted his pack to near extinction, and played him like a fiddle. You’re delusional." Freya chuckled darkly.

"Shut up!" Astrid squeezed harder, pinching Freya’s lips together. "You don’t know shit."

Freya stared at her for a beat. "I actually feel sorry for you." Astrid ripped her hand back like she’d touched hot iron. "You what?"

"I pity you. I might be crazy, but even I can see you never had a snowball's chance in hell of getting Orion to mate you, let alone getting him to flip sides."

"SHUT UP!" Astrid shrieked, whipping around to face Orion. "We can still do this. We’ve got him cornered!"

Orion let out a rough, gravelly laugh. "The council? They’re already done for. Executed. The crooked royal guard? Taken care of. I already pulled the trigger on all of it. Dead or alive, you’ve got zero leverage on my Luna."

Astrid gasped, choking on her own spit. "Your Luna?"

Orion glanced over at Roric. "Man, you really pick the dumb ones, don't you? At least Caspian has a brain."

Roric just grinned. He scanned the room, then pointed a finger. "You. Go grab my gear."

"Your gear?" A guard pointed at his own chest, but Roric shook his head. "The omega... the bag on the back bench. Get it." I felt the heavy pull of his alpha command, but I forced myself to stay put. Roric rolled his eyes. "Hey. You."

"Like father, like son, huh?" Orion coughed up a laugh. "Your own damn spell has her locked up like the rest of us, genius. She can't move a muscle, no matter what you order her to do."

Roric growled. "Itsi blugh dor not ihnas." He pointed at me, and the heavy pressure of the spell snapped off. "Now get moving. Bring me the tools on the bench."

I tripped, barely catching myself on a pew, then scrambled toward the bag. The sharp, toxic stench of silver hit my nose before I even touched it. Shaking, I carried the kit back to the front. Every eye in the room tracked me—until Freya started laughing again. Everyone looked back at her as I stopped right next to Gairth.

"He’s dead." Freya’s laughter cracked, turning into raw, ugly sobbing. "He’s dead... and he can’t hurt another wolf ever again."
