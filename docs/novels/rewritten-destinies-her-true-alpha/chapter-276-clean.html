Chapter 276

I spun back to Ronnie. He just shook his head. "Too late, kid," he muttered. I stared at him, begging with my eyes. No dice. His mind was made up. Mine wasn't.

"Bullshit," I snapped, shoving him. "I crawled back from the edge of death. You can too." I tried to yank my hand back, but he shook his head again. His skin was turning a nasty, ash-gray. The spark in his eyes was dying out. Still, he sucked in a ragged breath and forced himself to focus.

"Daddy, please. Just let Lyra help," Luna whimpered. It broke my damn heart. "Please!" She squirmed, trying to pull away from his chest. Ronnie hacked up a wet cough. His eyes went wide, and a thick stream of blood spilled past his lips. "Daddy!" she shrieked, thrashing to get away.

Blood smeared all over her back. Ronnie’s grip slipped as he choked on his own lungs. He gasped, finally catching a breath, and locked eyes with Luna. All that defeat in his eyes melted into pure, raw love for his kid. "Hey. Baby girl. Look at me. What’s our number one rule?" His breathing was a wet, gurgling mess now. Fluid was filling his chest. He was slipping fast. We all knew it. But the man refused to die until he knew his kid was gonna be okay. He leaned down, pressing a dry kiss to her forehead.

She shivered, shaking her head. "No, Daddy, stop," she sobbed. "Don't leave me. Please." She leaned up, kissing his cheeks over and over. "Let me fix you. Let Lyra do it. Come on, Daddy, please."

His eyelids fluttered shut. You could see his willpower cracking. He wanted to stay so bad. "What's the rule?" He pulled back, forcing her to look at him. "Come on, baby. Tell me. What's the first rule?"

Her bottom lip quivered. Tears kept pouring down her face. She sniffled, wiping her nose on her sleeve like kids do, and swallowed hard. "Only one shaman at a time," she whispered. "One shaman at a time. To keep the world in balance." Luna stared at him. Ronnie nodded. Another tear rolled down her cheek, and she leaned in closer. "But I can't do this yet. I don't know how. You promised you'd teach me!"

Ronnie went limp. I grabbed Luna, pulling her back, and eased him down to the dirt. He tried to sit back up, but I held him down, resting his head right on my lap. Luna scrambled over, curling against his chest. He threw his arms around her and looked up at me. "I wish we had more time, kiddo. I really do. But you're special. Just like Lyra. That's why the goddess picked her to guide you through that first vision. And why I'm leaving her in charge of you."

"But you said you'd watch over me!" she cried, burying her face in his chest. Ronnie's face just crumpled.

"I will, baby. I swear I will. I'll be watching both of you every single day." He kissed her head, over and over. "I won't have a body, but as long as the goddess lets me, I'm right here with you." He squeezed her tight. I ran my fingers through his hair, trying to soothe him.

I glanced up at Shadow. Honestly? I didn't give a shit about him anymore. "There's a way to visit," I said.

Ronnie's eyes snapped open. "What?"

"Look, it can't be a regular thing. But the goddess will let it slide. I know she will." I gave them the only thing left—hope. "Luna, when you miss your dad so much it hurts, when you can't take it anymore, I promise you he'll be there. He'll help you get through it."

She shook her head, looking up at me. "Liar. Once you die, you're gone."

I just gave her a soft smile. I closed my eyes, tapped into my power, and reached out. *Grandma, I need you. Right now. This kid needs hope, and this man needs some goddamn peace.* I felt a warm hand drop onto my shoulder. Everyone gasped. I opened my eyes. There she was, smiling down at us.

"Hey there, Ronnie." Ronnie stared up at my grandma. He closed his eyes, a wave of pure peace washing over his face. "Your little girl is absolutely precious, sweetheart. You did a hell of a job." She knelt down. Luna stared at her, wide-eyed. "Hey there, little one."

Luna sniffled. "Who are you?"

"I'm Lyra's grandma. And this..." She waved her hand. The new cottage shimmered, morphing back into her old, rustic cabin. "This used to be my place, once upon a time."

"Daddy said the lady who lived here died ages ago."

"Oh, I did." She waved her hand again. The old cabin burst into bright flames. "There was an attack. Just like this one. And just like your daddy, I laid down my life for the people I loved. The goddess gives me a little extra wiggle room for that."
