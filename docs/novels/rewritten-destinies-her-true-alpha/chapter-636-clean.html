Chapter 607

The two of them finally broke apart. Took 'em long enough. Kael hit the dirt, dropping to one knee right in front of Juniper’s old man. "Alpha," he muttered, keeping his head down. Rhys shot a look at me, then whipped his eyes over to Finnian.

"He's your guy?"

Finnian gave a slow nod. "Yeah. But the Queen's right. If his heart's not in the job, I don't want him guarding my back." Kael flinched. He scrambled up, turned to Finnian, and bent low.

"Alpha Finnian... man, I've looked up to you forever," Kael said, his eyes glued to the dirt. "I just... I don't know..."

I nudged Orion. "Hey, can guards with different bosses actually mate?" Then I looked at Finnian. "Honestly, what's the big deal?"

Finnian let out a heavy sigh. "Sure, on paper it's fine." I bit my lip. "But what if we get jumped? His focus gets split. Kael's been my head guy for years, you know?"

I faced Juniper. "Look, I'm not gonna stand between you and your mate." I planted a quick kiss on her cheek. "Go swear your oath to Finnian. That keeps things simple. No split focus between me and Kael." I glared at Finnian. "You better treat her right." It absolutely gutted me to let her go, but I couldn't be the jerk blocking their happiness. Finnian let out this massive, whiny groan—like a literal toddler. Orion snorted.

I blinked. "What? What's so funny?"

"You're too good to be true," Orion chuckled. Finnian groaned again, then shoved his hand out toward Kael. "Fine. You're free. Kael Durant, before the Celestia and everyone standing here, I, Finnian Reese, officially release you from your oath." A glowing magic band wrapping their arms shattered into dust. Kael just stood there, his jaw hitting the floor.

"Why the hell did you do that?" Kael stammered, taking a step toward him.

Finnian grinned at the kid. "Because I was right all along." He glanced my way. "And so was Her Majesty." He rubbed his shoulder with a sigh. "If you two were guarding different people, you'd both be useless in a fight. You'd be worrying about her, she'd be worrying about you, and someone would end up dead." He locked eyes with me. "Look, I wanted to be selfish. I wanted to take Juniper's oath just to keep you around. If the Queen had played dirty, I would've fought dirty to keep you. But she didn't. She just showed how much she actually cares about her friend." He looked down, shaking his head. "She put me to shame. I was only thinking about what I wanted, not what was best for you." He looked up, his eyes softening. "You're like a son to me, Kael." He yanked him into a tight hug. "Go be with your girl."

Kael bowed again, his voice thick. "Thank you, Alpha." He spun around to Juniper, then stared at me. "You... you really would've just let her walk away? Even after she promised to guard you?"

I shrugged. "Duh." I grabbed Juniper's hand. "She's my bestie." I gestured toward her family. "Go say hi to your folks. Besides, we've got a whole line of guards to interview." I gave her a playful shove toward Kael. "Get lost. We've got this."

"Are you absolutely sure?" She reached back out for me, but I just laughed and shoved her again.

"Yes, I'm sure! Now beat it." I turned back to the guys. "Alright, let's get this show on the road."

"What's the hurry, kiddo?" my dad teased with a chuckle.

My smile vanished. I just nodded, pointing a finger at the guards still groveling on the dirt. "We've got a lot of interrogating to do."

Orion squeezed my hand. "My boys can handle the grunt work."

I looked up and flashed him a cold smile. "Sure, they can grill the guards. But Elder and Caspian?" I glanced back at the house, my eyes narrowing. "No fucking way. Those two are mine."

Dad stepped in closer. "Sweetheart, you don't need to get your hands dirty with those bastards either."

I grabbed his hand, squeezing tight. "Yes, I do, Dad."

"Why?" He cupped my face in his hands. "If I could shield you from this crap, I would."

"I know, Dad. But these scumbags... these wolves used their power to torture their own people. They trafficked our kind, slaughtered our pack, and thought they could just walk away." Umbra's dark energy flared in my eyes. "They owe me answers." Dark magic sparked at my fingertips. Orion quickly slid his hands over mine, smothering the black flames before they could erupt.

"Alright," Orion murmured, a wicked grin spreading across his face. "This is gonna be fun."

Dad's eyes went wide. "I've never used magic to torture information out of someone. Tell me you're not going down that road."

I stared at him, keeping my mouth shut for a second. "And why shouldn't I?"

Dad looked like he was about to throw up. "Because that's not what magic is for, kiddo."

I let my eyes burn with pure rage. "But—"

"Your dad thinks just like me, sweetheart." Grandmother's voice drifted through the wind. Dad jumped out of his skin—he heard it too. "I always taught him that magic is for healing, love, and keeping people safe. He doesn't get that you're doing exactly that." She let out a soft sigh. "Do what you gotta do. Only you can decide how far into the dark you have to go to save this world."
