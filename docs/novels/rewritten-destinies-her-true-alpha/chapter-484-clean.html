Chapter 484

That damn hive was massive, easily the size of a telephone pole. Thousands of bees swarmed it. Maybe millions. *Zap.* One nailed me. Pain flared hot, but the magic kickback actually pumped me up. I felt the juice rushing back into my veins.

I scanned the clearing. Nothing. Zip. "Ugh, Lyra... you've really outdone yourself with this stupidity," I muttered. I kicked through the dirt. Looking for what? A weapon to fight bugs? Right. My wolves couldn't keep running in circles forever. And there was no dodging these bastards. They had a one-way ticket—sting once, then drop dead.

The buzzing went up a pitch. They were getting pissed. Why? I stepped closer to find out. The swarm was a black cloud now. *Pop, pop, pop.* Three stings, back-to-back. My knees buckled. I almost hit the dirt. But then, a voice cut through the noise. That's what was riling them up.

"Astrid, they're gone!" Magnus. He sounded totally freaked. I spun around, but duh—we were inside Gideon Stone's head. Magnus wasn't physically here. "They were supposed to be right here! Someone broke them out!" he shrieked. Pure rage. I only had half the call, but I knew he was throwing a tantrum in real-time. I crept closer to the hive. Three more stings bit into my skin. God, it hurt. I was right on top of the hive now. I clamped my teeth down on my inner cheek, drawing blood to keep from screaming. I had to hear this shit.

"What's your game?" I breathed. Still nothing in the clearing but this giant bug house. I grabbed a handful of magic and whipped up a raven. "Go. Find out if there's another hive in these woods." The bird croaked, flapped its wings, and bolted. I locked back onto the hive—and Magnus.

"Astrid, I'm not joking! Gideon, Mara, that dead kid of theirs—all gone! They even snatched the damn omegas!" Magnus growled. A beat. "So what?" he snapped. "Yeah, they're trash, but I needed Gideon!" He went quiet. "Say that again?" His voice dropped to a whisper. Dude was barely holding it together. "Not you, Astrid. Damon, speak up." A low growl. "The omega is knocked up? You sure about that?" Damon must've answered, because Magnus let out a nasty, cold laugh. "Oh, I'm gonna love breaking her when we find them. She only got lucky because she snagged a mate before I could ruin her." Astrid cut in, and he snapped. "No! I can't track them! I tried, okay? Why do you think I'm calling you, genius?"

I let out a quiet laugh. He was losing his mind over people he called "useless." "The power's dead! No, I can't just drain them! Are you brain-dead? You said it yourself—the kids weren't ready. Again!" God, I wished I could hear the other side of this shitshow. "Yeah, I'm still linked to the pack. Thank the Goddess those bonds are holding." I growled at that. *Zap.* Another sting. Hearing this asshole thank the Goddess like she approved of his bullshit made my blood boil. "Gideon's link? What about it?" Magnus asked.

"Shit." The hive was vibrating now, totally pissed that I was standing right there. Magnus's voice kept cutting out, drowned by the deafening buzz. I took another step forward. *Ouch.* Another welt. Inside my head, Umbra and Terra howled in agony. It hurt like hell, but I had no choice.

"I haven't checked Gideon's power rune. Why bother? It's just a waste of juice." A pause. "Fine, whatever. I wasn't thinking about him, but let's do it. Can't have him running his mouth. If Caspian were alive, I'd sweat it. But what's he gonna do, cry?" Magnus started to laugh, then choked on it. "Wait, what? The king is still tracking Lyra? I thought you had him on a leash!" The hive went into overdrive, shaking violently. "So if Gideon gets to the king through that bitch, we're done for?" Magnus roared. "Fine. Kill him. Make it hurt." Suddenly, the entire swarm shifted.

The nest shuddered. They were about to dump out. If they swarmed now without a distraction, Tick was dead meat. I kicked around the dirt again. My boot hit something solid—a thick, heavy branch right under the hive. I dropped low, grabbed the makeshift club, and stood back up. The buzzing was deafening now.

I took a step back. The first wave of bees burst out. I swung.
