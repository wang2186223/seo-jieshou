Chapter 362

Agnes popped the plastic latches on the first-aid kit. She dabbed ointment onto Vivienne’s cheek, her hands shaking with pure rage. "That little psycho," Agnes spat, rubbing the cream in. "Look at your face! Why didn't you scream, Vivienne? You should've let your parents walk in and see exactly what kind of monster they brought home."

That did it. The dam broke. Seeing the raw worry in Agnes’s eyes, Vivienne lost her grip. She lost her cool, completely. She lunged forward, locking her arms around the older woman’s neck, and just bawled. "Agnes, oh my god... you’re literally the only one left," she choked out, tears soaking Agnes's shoulder. "Nobody else gives a damn about me. I’m supposed to be a Lancaster. Some heiress I am! I couldn't even stop them from firing you."

"Mom threw you to the wolves just to make that trailer-trash girl happy. She didn't care about me at all! Everyone takes her side. Everyone treats me like garbage! Why am I even here? What's the point?" She wailed, her chest heaving with ugly, desperate sobs.

Agnes rubbed her back, a sharp, nasty spark flickering in her eyes. She leaned in close, dropping her voice to a conspiratorial whisper. "Hey, cut that out. Don't talk like that. You’re the real deal here, Vivienne. You're the Lancaster heir. That girl is just a parasite. She’s riding high on a DNA test and your parents' stupid guilt trip, that's it."

"If she’s pulling this crap today, she’ll kick you out of the house tomorrow. She’s bold, Vivienne. She wants you gone so she can grab the whole inheritance. If you roll over now, she wins. You're giving her exactly what she wants."

"Listen to me. The way your parents are acting... honestly, they might actually..." Agnes cut herself off, checking Vivienne's face to make sure she wasn't about to totally lose it. She let out a heavy, loaded sigh. "You need a backup plan, sweetie. Fast. You’re too sweet, too naive. You can't fight dirty against a manipulative street rat like her."

Vivienne cried harder, her chest burning with pure, hot jealousy. No way. She wasn't getting kicked out. She couldn't lose her title, her money, her entire life. She refused to end up on the street.

"But... how?" Vivienne gasped, wiping her nose. "I’ve got nothing left to fight with." Her parents worshipped Lyra now. Even Julian—the guy she’d practically clawed her way to get—was totally obsessed with the new girl. She was powerless.

"Who says you have nothing?" Agnes’s voice dropped to a cold, creepy whisper. "Remember the golden rule, Vivienne: the enemy of my enemy is my best friend. You aren't the only one who wants that girl dead and buried."

Vivienne stopped crying, her head snapping up. "Wait. You mean... that girl the Wynns brought home?"

Agnes gave a slow, dark nod, squeezing her shoulder. "Exactly. That long-lost Wynn daughter and her mom. They hate her guts. They’d rip her throat out if they got the chance."

Vivienne’s tears dried instantly. She stared at Agnes, her mind racing.

Agnes smirked. "And hey, isn't the Blackwood girl's birthday bash tomorrow? You handed them VIP invites, right? Those social climbers would kill for a ticket into the big leagues."

Vivienne nodded slowly, a dark realization dawning on her. "So you're saying..."
