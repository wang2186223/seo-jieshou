Chapter 535

We burst into the omega wing. Leanne didn't waste a second—she shoved me straight into her bathroom. "Get naked, now," she barked, slamming the door. "If they catch a whiff of that champagne on you, we're dead meat."

I ripped off my mask. Her jaw hit the floor. "Holy crap, I knew it," she breathed, tossing a towel at my face. "Move it!" Her eyes locked onto my neck. "Your mate is in one hell of a mess, Alpha Lyra."

I scrubbed down in record time. Out, clothes on, and Leanne was already yank-braiding my wild curls into some messy knot. "Gotta do," she muttered, tugging it tight.

She stared at my face, shaking her head. "Honestly, how did nobody spot you? Oh, wait. I know why. Those high-and-mighty wolves? They don't look at us. Not even when they're using our bodies." She glared at the wall behind me, then softened. "Except you."

"Leanne." I put a hand on her shoulder. She flinched, then held still. "When this shitshow ends, you're getting out of here. I swear." She gave a sharp nod, grabbed my mask, and yanked the straps tight behind my head.

"Move. Before Blair starts screeching." We bolted out the side door, bypassed the crowd of girls, and slipped right back into the main pavilion. The shattered glass? Already swept. Orion was still burning a hole through Magnus, who was still groveling on the floor.

"Sire," Damon stepped up, playing the good boy. "The omegas cleaned up. We're good to go." Orion let out a low growl. He spun on Damon, but then his eyes hit mine. He froze, stumbling mid-step.

Damon and Magnus traded a quick look. Magnus couldn't even hide his smug grin. "Please, Your Majesty. My bad for speaking out of turn," Magnus whined, putting on a pathetic little shake. "Just hyped you showed up for my kid's big day. I know this small-fry stuff is beneath you." Magnus snapped his fingers for another drink.

Orion shook his head like he was trying to wake up. Sure enough, the same omega brought over a fresh glass of champagne, loaded with silver. I slipped close, caught it just in time, and mumbled the counter-spell right as Orion lifted the glass. He downed it, waved a hand, and stumbled back to his chair.

"Yeah, whatever," Orion slurred, turning a cold shoulder to Astrid. "I found my real mate. You're history. Get lost. I only came here because Damon hooked me up with the best damn gift of my life, and I wanted to say thanks." Damon looked at Magnus, totally lost. They both shrugged. Astrid bolted upright.

"You can't do this to me!" She stomped like a brat.

Orion just glared. "Watch me. It's done. I'd do it again in a heartbeat. The only reason you were ever my girl was because the council shoved you down my throat. We both know it. But they can't touch the goddess. Now beat it." He flashed Damon a sloppy grin and waved. "Get this show on the road. I ain't got all day."

Damon looked at his old man. Magnus nodded, stood up, and dusted off his suit. "Let's do this." Leanne took off to tip off Blair. A minute later, she was back, giving the signal. The music kicked in. The girls started marching down the aisle. It was that cheesy, slow-ass march you see on TV. Total human wedding vibe. Weird as hell for wolves.

The tune shifted. Luna, Brittany, and Blair stepped out. Their faces were covered, but you could tell Brittany wanted to be anywhere else. Blair, though? She looked like she just won the lottery.

*God, she's stupid,* Umbra cackled in my head. *Look at her. Thinks she hit the jackpot.*

Yeah, right. Her big prize? A cheating bastard who didn't even want her, just wanted to use her as a side piece while he chased someone stronger.

Blair strutted past me. Our eyes locked. She tripped, her mouth dropping open. She was about to scream my name, I knew it. But Brittany yanked her arm. "Eyes front. Unless you want to blow this whole thing right now?" Brittany hissed. It worked. Blair snapped her head back around, totally forgetting about me, eyes glued to her groom.

Damon looked like he was walking to the electric chair. The guy used to light up whenever he saw her. Now? His face was pure stone. Not a single drop of love. He leaned over to Gareth, whispering loud enough for the whole room to hear: "This is a huge mistake."
