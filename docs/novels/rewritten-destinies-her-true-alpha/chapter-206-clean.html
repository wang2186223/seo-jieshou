NovelVibe

Chapter 206

Fast forward a few months. We piled into my place, totally wiped. Daisy face-planted straight onto my couch. "Holy crap," she groaned. "Year one is officially in the books."

"Tell me about it. Pure madness," Luna said, flopping onto the floor. She rolled around on my plush shag rug with a loud groan. "Ugh, do your nesting at our place next time, okay?" Daisy chucked a throw pillow at her head. Luna caught it, shoved it under her neck, and smirked. "What? Don't act like you guys aren't drooling over her setup. Everything in here is literally covered in fur." Fawn curled up on the couch, and Juniper claimed the love seat. Luna pointed a finger at my armchair. "I mean, look at that beast!"

I sank into the cushions. Inside my head, my wolves started purring like happy engines. "Can't lie, it's heaven," I laughed.

Luna crossed her arms, huffing. "See? Told ya!" The room erupted in chuckles.

I looked at them. Damn, we'd changed. They weren't the same girls anymore—they had real muscle now, and they walked with their heads high. "Hey," I said, getting their attention. "I don't say this enough, but I'm seriously glad I met you guys." They all stared. I tried to keep going, but Fawn cut me off with a wave. "Shut it. You sound like you're about to dump us, and that's a hard no. One, you're stuck with us for at least another year. Two..." She softened up, looking at each of us. "I'm glad too. You guys are my ride-or-dies. No way in hell I'd find a crew this badass anywhere else."

Someone sniffled. Then, *bam*—a pillow hit Fawn right in the face. "You jerk," Luna muttered, wiping a tear. "We just survived finals. This is a celebration, and you guys are making it feel like a funeral. Seriously, I hate you both." I snorted. "You obsessed with us," Daisy teased, firing another pillow back. A second of dead silence passed, and then chaos broke loose. Pillows flew everywhere. Just laughs, grunts, and soft thuds—until a heavy knock rattled the front door. We all froze mid-air.

Juniper locked eyes with me. "Who's that?"

"No clue," I whispered. I walked over and pulled the door open an inch. "Yeah?"

"Hey, Lyra." Flint stood there, giving me a stiff nod and a weak wave.

I swung the door wide. "Flint! Man, it's been forever. How's everything? How's your girl? The baby?" Suddenly, it hit me. "Wait, hold that thought!" I bolted, leaving him standing there looking totally lost. I sprinted to my bedroom, grabbed the big gift bag I'd been saving, and rushed back. I shoved it right into his chest. "For you!" I beamed.

Flint's jaw hit the floor as he held the massive bag. "Lyra... come on, you shouldn't have."

"Duh, but I wanted to," I said. "We didn't know the gender, so we went with neutral colors. Hope you guys dig it." The girls crowded behind me. "We all pitched in and went shopping together. Oh, and the receipt is inside, just in case you get duplicates and need to swap stuff."

Flint's eyes welled up, a huge grin breaking across his face. "Thanks, girls. Seriously. Lena is gonna lose her mind." The girls took turns giving him quick hugs before slipping back inside, leaving just the two of us. Flint cleared his throat, shifting the bag. "Look, I actually didn't come here just for a social call."

I leaned against the doorframe. "What's going on?"

"My shift's over, and I was heading out, but..." He scratched his neck, hesitating. "Finnian dropped some news on me." Before he could finish, his radio burst to life with static.

*“Flint. You upstairs?”*

Flint raised a finger to pause our chat and grabbed his radio. "Yeah, Alden. Go ahead."

*“We got guests. Well, two of 'em, looking for Alpha Lyra. You're up there, right? Go knock on her door and give her a heads-up.”*

*“Save him the trip. Just tell me her room number, I'll go up myself.”* Damon's voice cut through the static, cold and sharp. I froze.

*“We'll go together,”* another voice chimed in. Raven. The girls instantly swarmed behind me again, tense. Flint barked a quick "Copy that" into his radio and clipped it back to his belt.

"Flint..." Juniper grabbed my hand, squeezing hard. "Why the hell are Damon and Raven looking for Lyra?"

Flint swallowed hard, his eyes darting away. "It's about tomorrow's announcement," he muttered. "The King... he's officially launching a search. The council is picking candidates for his chosen mate."
