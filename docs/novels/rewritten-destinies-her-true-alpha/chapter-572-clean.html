Chapter 572

I hit the bottom of the stairs, and Orion was right there. "You good?" he asked.

I shook my head. "Hell no. But we gotta move. Now." I scanned the room. "Where's Juniper?"

Orion pointed up. "Daisy needed some tripod thing. Juniper went up to help her grab it."

Right on cue, Juniper clattered down the steps. "My bad! Ready!" She threw a duffel bag at my chest. "For you." I opened my mouth, but she slapped a hand up. "Don't ask. Ronnie said you'd need it today. Period."

Ronnie. God, that guy. "Still pulling strings from the grave, huh?" I muttered.

Juniper let out a dry laugh. "That was my last note from him. Daisy's got one left, I think. But you? You've got a whole damn pile." Her smile faded. "He spent his final year making sure we wouldn't drown without him." She took a shaky breath. "Man, I just hope I'm half as brave as he was."

I blew out a breath. "Yeah. Same." Giggles drifted from the dining room. I headed over to kiss my kid goodbye—hopefully for the last damn time. "Hey, sweetie." I leaned against the doorframe. Lyra Bloom and Luna looked up. "Daddy's gotta run."

Luna's face fell. "I know." She scrambled off her chair, bolted over, and locked her arms around my waist. "Don't get hurt, okay?"

It ripped my chest open. We'd forced this kid to grow up way too fast. "Always." I dropped to my knees and squeezed her tight. "Do me a favor. Stay strong for Grandma Elara, alright? Promise me." She took a deep sniff against my neck, memorizing my scent. It killed me that she felt she had to do that.

She rubbed her face against my shoulder. "I promise," she whispered. Then she pulled back and planted a quick kiss on my cheek.

My stomach did a weird flip. I frowned. "Hey, what's the rule if someone breaks in?" She backed up, staring right at me. I shook off the sudden chill and kept my eyes on my girl.

She flashed a tiny grin. "Bolt for the closet. Lock it behind us."

I tapped her nose. "Spot on." I glanced at Lyra Bloom, who was still stuffing her face. "Hey, where's your dad, kiddo?"

Lyra's eyes went wide, her mouth packed with food. She mumbled some gibberish. Luna giggled, nudged my arm, and took over. "Her mom and dad are in the kitchen. Doing 'grown-up talk.' They gave us snacks to keep us quiet."

I patted her back and stood up. "Got it." I turned toward the kitchen, but a muffled shout from Lyra made me whip around. Luna's eyes were rolling back. Her tiny body was wobbling. "Oh, crap, not again!" I lunged forward just as her knees gave out.

"Visions don't keep a schedule, Three-spirit. They show up when they want to." Luna's voice dropped an octave, sounding like a hundred-year-old crone. Her little body shook under the strain of the download.

"She's too weak for this!" I snapped, scooping my girl up.

"Then she better get tough, fast." Her eyes rolled completely white. "The seal is cracked, but the door is shut. When it swings wide, the Shaman's clock starts ticking." She gasped for air. "But she won't have a future if you don't fight this war. You're done here. Move! Get to the pack bridge. Clock's ticking, buddy."

"What the hell do you mean, running out?" My stomach dropped. Cold dread flooded my gut. Panic started clawing at my brain, ripping away my control. My mind went into a tailspin. Sweat broke out on my neck. I could feel my fangs itching to pop out.

The sheer terror rolling off me must've triggered the alarms. Gideon Stone and Mara Croft burst out of the kitchen, ready for a fight. They scanned the room, but when they saw nothing, their guard dropped into pure confusion. Gideon took a step forward. "What's the hold-up?"

Orion pressed against my back, holding me steady. "We gotta move. Now."

Luna's hands slapped onto my face. "Trust nobody." She glanced past my shoulder at Orion, her eyes flashing with something like pity. "And follow the Celestia's path." Her eyes snapped shut, and her spine arched.

Lyra shrieked. "Is she dead?!"

I nodded quickly, scooped her up, and bolted into the living room. "She's just out cold from the vision." I laid her on the sofa and threw a throw blanket over her. "Just needs to sleep it off." I stepped back as Lyra scrambled onto the cushions to check her friend. "Keep an eye on her, okay?" Lyra nodded, wide-eyed.

"Lyra?" a voice called out from behind me, but I was already busting through the front door.
