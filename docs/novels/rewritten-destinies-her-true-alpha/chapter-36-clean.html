Chapter 36

Dad lunged to grab her. "Grandma!"

She slapped his hand away. "Save it, kiddo. I’m not actually here. That thing dragged me back." She pointed a finger at the book.

"Your Book of Shadows," Dad muttered, staring at the pages. His hands hovered over the paper. "I thought it was gone forever."

"Not gone. Just locked away." She looked at me, her eyes crinkling. "And the right girl found it." She stepped closer, framing my dad’s face with her hands, barely touching him. "Missed you, kid. But my time's up. Watch your back. The dark is coming."

"I love you," he choked out.

"Love you too, baby." She tapped a kiss onto his forehead. Then, poof. Gone.

Dad turned to me, his face totally wrecked. "You actually talked to her?"

I nodded. "Yeah. She helped me dig deep, find my core, and unlock my juice. Even then, the Moon Goddess had to give me a hard shove."

"Wait, you met the Moon Goddess?" Finnian bolted out of his seat and rushed us.

"I mean, maybe?" I shrugged, looking at Dad. He gave me a solid nod.

"We all do," Dad said, running a hand through my hair. "She’s the final spark. That’s why we keep it a secret. She only shows up when you’re ready. If you knew she was coming..."

"...I wouldn't have busted my ass to connect," I finished.

"Exactly," Dad said. "She has to open the tap. It’s a final blessing. But if you're slacking or not ready, she skips you." He leaned down, kissing my head.

"So..." I started.

"So she knew you were ready. You balanced your wolf and your magic. You're doing it right this time."

"Right. Which means in my past life, I never shifted because I couldn't balance the wolves and the power. But this time, I get both."

"Wolves?" Finnian muttered under his breath.

My heart stopped. Dad’s eyebrow shot up, but he covered for me fast. "This run is different. You're going to pull off everything."

He shot me a look. *You're explaining that slip-up later.*

*Deal,* I thought back.

I turned to the Book of Shadows. "Alright, so what was the plan with this?"

Dad glanced at Finnian. Finnian shook his head. "She lost her grip the second your ghost grandma popped in."

"Well, shit." Dad slid the book over, flipping pages until he hit the spot. "Get back to your scent. It went default again. You need to lock it down so it runs on autopilot. Standard rookie training." He tapped the page. I closed my eyes and sparked the mental candle.

"Hey, can our wolves use magic?" I asked.

Dad’s hand froze mid-air. "Say what now?"

"Like, when I'm in this form, Umbra can keep the magic running. She can ping me if it drops. If she handles the upkeep, I don't have to stress about it. Even when I'm asleep. And when she shifts out, I can do the same for her."

Finnian smacked the back of Dad's head. "Dude, why didn't you ever think of that?"

"Hell if I know," Dad laughed, rubbing his neck. "But yeah, she can. The Moon Goddess blessed the whole package—us and the wolves. You're dead on. Umbra handles the magic while you run the show, and vice versa. Obsidian has backed me up like that a million times." He gave me a sheepish grin.

I smiled and kept flipping pages, stopping on a sketch of a half-shifted werewolf. "Quick question. Can a werewolf have multiple forms?"

"Sure," Dad said. "The stronger you are, the better you can hold a partial shift." He let his claws slide out, fur creeping halfway up his arm. "But if you're eyeing that picture..." He pointed at the page. "Forget it. You can't do that one."

"Why not?" I asked. I just wanted to let Umbra and Terra run free.

*I don't need to shift, Lyra,* Terra’s voice echoed, soft and quiet.

Umbra snorted in my head. *Cut the crap, Terra. Everyone needs to stretch their legs. Even you.* She nudged Terra toward the mental clearing.

"Because..." Dad's voice snapped me back to reality. "That's not a werewolf. That's a Lycan."
