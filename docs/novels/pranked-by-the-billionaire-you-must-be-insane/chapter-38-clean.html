Chapter 38

Rhys wouldn't shut up about Caspian. The guy was on a roll, spitting venom like he’d been saving it up for years. Lyra didn't say a word. She just stared out the window, watching the city lights blur into a smear of neon.

Caspian. Four years of being engaged to that jerk.

She remembered her first day back with the Wynns. Cold shoulders all around. She’d been a nervous wreck, practically begging them to love her. Fat chance. They hated her. The night she showed up, it was pouring, and they literally forgot she was alive. Just left her shivering on the porch.

Then Caspian showed up with his dad. He handed her an umbrella, pulled her inside, and forced the Wynns to actually look at her. He told them, *Yeah, she’s my childhood fiancée.* That did the trick. Suddenly the Wynns called a doctor and let her stay. Back then, the guy was her freaking savior.

But guess what? That savior vibe died. Four years of playing chase, getting nothing but cold shoulders and half-assed texts. Then the ultimate blow—he jumped into bed with Genevieve before the Wynns even put Lyra's name on the family tree. Now? The spark was dead. Burnt to a crisp.

The neon-blue sports car tore down the road, vanishing into the dark. Vivienne crept out from behind the iron gates, jaw on the floor as she watched the taillights fade.

Holy crap. Was that... a limited-edition Lambo? No way. How the hell did Lyra know someone with that kind of cash?

Vivienne had lagged behind, too scared to get a close look. All she saw was some guy in a bright green leather jacket hopping into the driver's seat. No face, just a male silhouette. Who owned that ride? And what was he doing with Lyra?

VIP Room 6103, Obsidian Palace.

A guy sat on the leather couch, his black shirt half-unbuttoned to show off a toned chest. He glared at his phone like he wanted to kill it.

"Damn, Caspian, you're losing your mojo," one of the trust-fund babies laughed. "Your little puppy just hung up on you. That's a new one."

"Yeah, man, you really screwed up this time," another chimed in. "Usually, you snap your fingers and she runs to you, even if she's halfway across the state in a hurricane."

"Bro, your dog-whispering skills are trash now. Since your bitch didn't show, you're chugging this whole bottle. Bottoms up!"

The rich heirs in the VIP lounge kept poking the bear, laughing at the guy in the middle of the couch.

Caspian gripped his phone so hard his knuckles turned white. His sharp, handsome face went totally rigid. He threw the phone onto the table and let out a harsh scoff. "She's a dog," he muttered, eyes dark. "Who cares if she shows up?"

Like he actually needed her to drive him home. Like he actually liked those nasty-ass herbal soups she always forced on him. He tried to sound bored. But his white-knuckled fists told a completely different story.
