Chapter 556

Orion hauled me up. I kicked the front door open. Boom—pure chaos.

At least fifty rogues had my family cornered. Mom was in full beast mode, tearing into a pack trying to flank her. Gideon, Mara, Ternin, and Elara stood shoulder-to-shoulder, swinging at anything with a pulse. Elara’s arm was dripping blood, but she wasn't backing down.

We charged. I felt Umbra surge, forcing the shift. Bones cracked, and I was in my wolf-man form. I blindsided the first guy. Dude didn't see it coming at all. I ripped his arm right out of its socket. He shrieked, and the sound made the others whip around.

"Flanked! They're coming from behind!" some rogue screamed.

Orion and I carved a path straight toward Mom. Juniper and the girls trailed right behind us, using the bloody lane we cleared until we hit the center of the mess.

"Mom!" I yelled.

She glanced back, gave me a quick nod, and went right back to shredding.

"Lyra! Watch Mara!" Gideon roared as two bastards tackled him.

Yeah, right. I ignored him, kicked the first guy off, and snapped the second one's neck with a loud *crack*. A blade grazed my hand. I hissed, but Orion was already there, slicing the throat of the guy who cut me.

"Gotcha," I muttered, hauling Gideon up and shoving him toward his girl.

A scream pierced the air. I spun, but some idiot blocked my way.

"Orion, get her!" I pointed at Elara. Two guys were kicking her, and a third had her in a chokehold. Orion hesitated for a split second, nodded, and bolted.

I slashed at the guy in front of me. Caught him off guard and sliced his arm, but damn, he was quick. He dodged every single blow. He wasn't even trying to hit me, just playing defense.

Wait. Something felt off. I spun around just in time to see a sword coming for my spine. I threw a kick, catching the guy square in the gut. He flew backward into the crowd. *Screeech.* His sword sliced another rogue's leg on the way down. Warm blood sprayed right across my face.

"You crazy bitch!" the first guy snarled behind me.

He lunged. I leaped. He hit empty air, and I slammed right onto his back. My wolf-man weight crushed his spine. I clawed down, ripping his neck open. He went limp. I jumped off, looking for the next target.

It was a meat grinder. Men, women—didn't matter. They just kept coming. No breaks, no breathing room. Just blood, guts, and endless screaming. I tried to count the survivors, but two guys rushed me at once.

I ducked a wild swing, but my muscles were burning. The second one slammed into me, knocking me flat. I hit the dirt with a hard groan. She scrambled up, pinning me down. She was young. Red hair, tight braid. But that nasty, smug grin? I knew that face.

"Briar," I growled.

She punched me hard in the ribs. Her eyes went wide. "Who the hell are you?" she spat, hitting me again. "And how do you know my name?"

Suddenly, Umbra slipped back. She was totally gassed, unable to hold the shift.

*Hey, we're in the middle of a fight!* I snapped.

She just chuckled in my head. *Relax. Briar's a weakling. She can't hurt you.*

Terra trotted over. *Stupid girl. It only takes one lucky hit.*

The shift ended. Briar’s eyes popped. "It's you!"

She threw a punch at my face, but I caught her fist mid-air. I grinned. "Well, well. What brings you to this party?"

She lost it, screaming in my face. "This is your fucking fault! You kicked me out! I wanted to be your friend. I literally turned my back on Raven and the whole damn council for you!"

I rolled my eyes. "So you joined a bunch of rabid rogues and attacked my family... because I rejected your friend request? Come on, Briar. Stop crying wolf and own your shit."

She tilted her head, letting out a creepy giggle. Her eyes glowed with her wolf's light. "Ning told her uncle I flipped. So he targeted my pack. My family. Our own Alpha sold us out to Roric. And Roric? He needed females to keep his boys happy. My sisters, my mom... they're all dead because of them. I only survived because I bit my rapist's tongue off. You did this to my family, Lyra. And now, I'm going to rip your fucking throat out."
