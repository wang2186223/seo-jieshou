Chapter 171

Holy shit. I’d never seen the photo before. I looked straight-up dead. Eyes half-slit, mouth smeared with blood. My neck was a total crime scene—torn to shreds, chunked out, with jagged bite marks ringing my throat. One arm was snapped backward like a twig. A jagged bone poked right out of my leg.

The whole room gasped at once. A collective suck of air. Then, every single eye whipped toward me. But I couldn't look away from the screen. Damn. We actually survived that.

*We’re tougher than they think, kid,* Umbra’s voice cut in, solid as a rock. She rubbed against our mental wall. Terra pressed in too, trying to slow my jackhammering pulse. God, I had been a hair away from the grave.

*But the Goddess had our backs,* Terra chimed in, her sweet voice instantly taking the edge off my panic.

*Damn right. She’s got plans for us,* Umbra grunted. The two wolves snuggled up inside my head. I couldn't help but smirk.

"How?" A voice yanked me back to the room. I blinked, looking at Luna. "Huh?" I frowned. She grabbed my hand, squeezing hard. "How the hell are you even breathing after that?"

"Pure luck," I muttered. It was all I had.

"Oh, bull-freaking-shit! That is totally photoshopped!" Raven found her voice, stomping her foot like a brat.

"Say that again." Orion's voice dropped to a deadly, icy register.

"I said she faked it!" Raven spun around, pointing a finger right at my face.

I let out a dry laugh. Before Orion could blow his top, I threw up a hand to stop him. He actually flinched back. I almost giggled, but I kept my eyes locked on Raven. "Okay, let me get this straight. You’re saying I did what? Slapped on some Hollywood SFX makeup and fake blood? Faked a whole doctor's note, sent it to the King, and for what? To play the victim? To score some cheap points?"

Raven jabbed her finger at me again. "Aha! See? You're admitting it!"

"I'm admitting your theory is a load of garbage," I snapped. I crossed my arms, staring her down. "Think about it. Why would I do that? To get the King's pity? Seriously? The guy is losing wolves every single day to these rogue raids. He’s got a whole kingdom to mourn, he doesn't have time for my sob story. Or what, to get on his good side? Why? You think surviving a beatdown makes him want to date me? That's stupid. There are hundreds of female survivors who've been around way longer than me. If he had a thing for trauma survivors, he would've picked one years ago."

Raven shook her head, her face twitching as she realized how dumb she sounded.

"And don't even start with the 'pushing for war' crap," I pressed on, my voice rising as the anger bubbled up. My hands dropped to my sides. "The rogues brought this fight to us! They targeted us! We were chilling in peace for years until they raided our borders. They slaughtered the Lycans." I stepped closer. "They murdered our King. Our Alphas. Our women. Our freaking babies! And you’re blaming me—an eighteen-year-old kid who literally just shifted—for starting a war? Honey, you either think I’m some kind of global mastermind, or you are just so damn jealous and brainless that you can't even hear yourself talk."

Her jaw hit the floor. "So, which is it? Am I your worst nightmare, or are you just green with envy?"

"I... I... that's not..." she sputtered, completely tongue-tied.

Suddenly, this old council guy stepped up, face red as a beet. I cut my eyes to him. "Raven is the future Queen!" he shrieked. "You don't talk to her like that!" Raven's eyes went wide with shock. Orion slowly turned around.

"Raven is my queen?" Orion's voice was a low, vibrating growl. His whole frame shook with rage. He lunged, grabbing the councilman by the throat, lifting him up. "Who the hell gave you permission to pick my mate?"

"M-My King..." The guy choked, clawing at the iron grip on his neck, gasping for air. "You said... if you didn't find one... we could choose for you..."

"I said you could bring me *options*, you idiot! Not make the choice for me!" Orion roared, slamming the guy onto the floor. "I never said yes to Raven. Not once. And you have the balls to scream at a wolf who just laid out the facts? Lyra is 100% right." Orion stomped his boot right onto the man's chest.

"Uncle Hawthorn!" Raven shrieked, rushing onto the stage. Orion paused, his boot still planted.

"Oh, let me get this straight," Orion growled, a sound so deep the floorboards literally vibrated. "The one person you pushed on me, the *only* one you claimed was worthy... is your own damn niece? How freaking dare you!"
