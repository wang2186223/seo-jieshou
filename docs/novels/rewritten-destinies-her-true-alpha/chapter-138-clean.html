Chapter 138

He tripped over his own damn feet. "Wait, what?" He shot me a sideways look.

"Oh, please. You act like I’m blind," Umbra snorted. "We’re wolves, dude. We sniff out everything."

"Fair point," he muttered. "But honestly? Nobody’s ever called me out on it."

"Why the hell not?" We stopped dead, staring him down. "How does a whole-ass wolf have zero scent, and nobody asks questions?"

He plopped down on the ground, looking up at me. "Probably my size. Or my rank. People get intimidated."

"Which is?"

He tilted his head, acting casual. "I'm an alpha."

I shrugged. "Alphas don't scare me."

"Yeah, I can see that." He let out a dry chuckle. "But they terrify everyone else. Well, the sane ones, anyway."

"You calling me crazy?"

His eyes popped. "Whoa, didn't mean it like that," he stammered. We both just busted out laughing.

"Whatever you say." Umbra glanced up at the sky. "Alright, I gotta bounce."

"Wait, why?" He jumped in front of us, but we just slipped right past him.

"Class, duh. I literally bolted out of my last one." We took off running, and Shadow was right on our heels.

It turned into a total game. We tore through the woods, snapping and dodging each other. He tackled me hard, and we rolled through the dirt in a big, messy heap. "When do I get to see you again?" he asked. His voice was deep, but I could hear the hope in it.

"You live around here?" Umbra asked, feeling a little hopeful herself.

Shadow shook his head. "Nah. But I'm in town every Monday and Friday."

"Well, then. Maybe you'll catch me around."

"Maybe?"

"Hey, no promises. We don't even know each other's real names." Umbra rubbed her body against his, gave him a tease, and bolted.

His heavy footsteps thudded behind her, but we made a beeline for the school, slipped through an open door, and burst into the bathroom. We slammed inside, heard a sharp gasp, and immediately shifted back. "My bad!" I threw my hands up. "Didn't mean to give you a heart attack."

A tiny Asian girl with a cute bob and a sweet smile laughed it off. "All good. Just caught me off guard." She grabbed a paper towel to dry her hands. "You're literally the size of a male wolf. I thought some dude just rolled in here." She shook her head, amused.

"Oh, god. Sorry about that." I walked over to the mirror and started fixing my messy hair.

"Don't worry about it," she said, tossing the towel. "By the way, you are gorgeous."

"Thanks."

"I'm Luna." She held out her hand. I grabbed it.

"Lyra."

"Nice to meet you."

"Same." I smiled. "Hey, you got the time?"

"It's six."

"Shit! I gotta move!" I sprinted out of the bathroom and booked it back to the math room. Thank god, the door was still unlocked. I grabbed my backpack and ran to the cafeteria. I shoved a tray of food in my face, swallowed it whole, grabbed a water bottle, and rushed to my next class.

I slid into a back row desk and popped a piece of gum. I whipped out my phone, checked a bunch of missed texts from my mom and Elara, and scrolled through social media for a few minutes while the room filled up.

"Hey, you know who's teaching this?" Two guys walked in and took the front seats.

"No clue. Haven't heard a single thing." They both spun around and locked eyes with me. Oh, great. Two guys from my math class. They both swallowed hard and gave me a weak little wave. I just smiled and waved back.

The rest of the class trickled in. Half of them were from my earlier class, and they kept their distance. The new crowd scattered around, but a couple of guys chose to sit right next to me.

I ignored their staring and went back to my phone. People were whispering, so I pulled out my laptop and plugged it into the wall. Thank god there were outlets everywhere.

Suddenly, the room went dead quiet. I looked up from my screen. A massive guy was standing at the front, writing on the whiteboard. I glanced around—everyone was staring at him like statues.

I shut my laptop and leaned forward, trying to get a good look. The guy stood up straight, and my jaw dropped at his hair. Shaved clean on the sides, but the top was a messy, tarnished silver bun. Inside my head, Umbra and Terra stood up, pushing to the front. Even they were hooked.

Then, a deep, weirdly familiar voice boomed through the room. "Welcome to Coding 101. We're doing coding languages, system building, and my personal favorite—the basics of security systems." He spun around to face us, and my stomach literally did a flip. Holy shit. He was gorgeous. Like, the hottest guy I had ever seen in my life. "I'm Alpha Orion."

Alpha Rowen... My mouth hung open as his eyes locked onto mine.

King Alpha Rowen.
