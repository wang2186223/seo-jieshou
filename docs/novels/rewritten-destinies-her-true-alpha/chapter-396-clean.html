Chapter 396

The world was a blur. Trees zipped past like toothpicks. We leaped over massive bushes. The birds and beasts shut the hell up and cleared out. They knew shit was going down.

Terra blew right past the pack borders. She dodged the patrols—some on duty, some just out for a casual hunt.

We finally skidded to a halt, gasping for air, trying to map our next move. Terra scanned the area. Beside me, Umbra slipped back inside and collapsed.

"You good?" I rushed over to her.

"Yeah," she panted, slumping against me. "Just pushed it too hard. Damn."

I pulled her close, propping her up while she caught her breath. Then it hit me—the sun was gone. Wait, how long had we been running?

Umbra let out a heavy sigh. "Not long. Like, two hours max. Why?"

"Then why is it pitch black?" I looked around. The woods were getting seriously creepy.

Terra looked up. The sun was fine, but the trees were choking out the light. The air felt thick, heavy. "This is bad," she muttered.

"Move!" I snapped. Terra bolted again, and this time, I took the lead.

"Lyra, I have no clue where we're going!"

"I got you, Terra. Just trust me." I felt a sharp yank to the front-right. "Go that way! Follow the pull!"

"Got it." She kicked into high gear, tearing through the brush.

Umbra's voice echoed in my head: "Hurry up! Something's going down!"

Suddenly, a gut feeling told me to halt. I yanked Terra back. "Whoa, slow down. Get low."

Terra dropped flat on her belly. We crawled through the dirt, holding our breath, creeping toward the noise.

Voices. Terra froze. I quickly threw up a silence spell to mask our footsteps, and we crept closer.

"What do you mean the kids aren't ready to harvest?!" Magnus was losing his mind. He was pacing the clearing, stomping his boots. "You swore they'd be ready today! You personally checked the brat last week!" *Smack.* A sharp yelp followed.

"I'm sorry, Magnus! She was ready, I swear! But they got stronger somehow. Their wolves should be dead, their souls ripe for the taking, but the wolves are still kicking! I don't get it!" Terra's ears twitched. She recognized that voice.

*Are you fucking kidding me?* "Get closer, Terra. I need a visual." My heart was hammering against my ribs like a trapped bird.

"Astrid, I swear to God, I am this close to ripping your head off," Magnus growled. "You promised we'd harvest today. I'm losing my goddamn mind here."

"Dad, chill," Damon cut in. "What's the point of throwing a tantrum? So we wait another week. Big deal. You've already waited years." Terra crawled an inch closer.

*Please, goddess, no.* I whispered the prayer, but Umbra heard it.

"Why are you praying?" Umbra whispered back, keeping her voice down so Terra could focus on crawling.

I exhaled slowly. "I don't know. I've got this sick feeling in my stomach. I'm terrified of who I'm about to see out there."

Umbra looked at me, then back at the clearing. "You think Orion's in there, don't you?"

I let out a heavy sigh. "Yeah. Yeah, I do."

Terra poked her nose out of the bush just enough to get a clear shot. Astrid was looming over a kid. Magnus and Damon were pacing around her. But no Orion. I let out a massive breath I didn't know I was holding.

"See? Told you. He's not here," Umbra nudged my side, trying to cheer me up. "You worry too much." She chuckled.

But her laugh cut short. Gideon Stone stepped out from behind a tree. He had this nasty, smug grin, and his eyes were completely dead.

Ronnie and Gideon were laughing as they walked in. Magnus whirled on them, furious. "What took you assholes so long? We've been waiting here forever!"

"Cut the crap, Vin. You've never waited for anything in your life," Gideon sneered, looking down at the kid. "You already had your witch try to drain the pup, didn't you?" His face was totally blank, zero emotion.

"Guilty as charged," Ronnie laughed. Then he groaned. "But man, that bitch leaving me really screwed things up. I was so damn close. You got lucky, Gideon. Your girl got knocked up on the first try. Mine dragged her feet, and then Blair had to go and ruin everything."

Damon snorted. "You ruined it yourself, idiot. And I'm the one walking on eggshells here. You have no idea how pissed Lyra is." He threw his head back. "I almost had her mated and pregnant within two months. But no, your stupid daughter just had to open her big fat mouth."
