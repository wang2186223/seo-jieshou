Chapter 637

Lyra tossed her beat-up canvas bag onto the seat of the private jet. She leaned back, ready to pass out.

Then, a thought popped up. She whipped out her phone and fired off a quick text to Julian Vance: [Wheels up.]

Her screen lit up right away. [Turn your phone on the second you touch down. My people in the States are on standby. Hit them up if anything goes sideways.]

Lyra stared at the screen. Seriously, this guy. She shook her head, totally exasperated, but a tiny smile slipped out anyway.

The jet hit the tarmac at the States' international airport with a soft thud.

Lyra snatched her bag, yanked a baseball cap low over her eyes, and sauntered out of the VIP lounge looking like she couldn't care less.

The main terminal was a madhouse. People everywhere, shouting in like five different languages.

Vivienne burned a hole in Lyra’s back with her eyes. Her fists clenched, pure poison dripping from her stare.

Back on the flight, with all the Lancaster crew watching, she’d put on this big "loving sister" act, hovering over Lyra like a mother hen.

And Lyra? Total silent treatment. Then she literally told her to zip it. Right in front of the flight attendants. Talk about humiliating.

God, what a trailer-trash brat. Like anyone actually wanted to talk to her anyway. Please.

But hey, they were in a whole new country now. This clueless hick didn't know the language or the streets. She’d be crawling back, begging for help soon enough. Plus, any second now...

Grandpa’s butler was showing up. Lyra was about to look like a total clown.

Vivienne smirked. She patted down her brand-new designer dress and strutted forward, keeping her posture perfect.

Right outside the exit, an old guy with silver hair and a fancy British tailcoat stepped up. He bowed low to Vivienne. "Miss Vivienne. Your grandparents have been expecting you."

Seeing Lyra left in the dust gave Vivienne a massive rush of victory.

She flashed a sweet-as-sugar grin and glided over, her voice dripping honey. "Oh, Edmund! It’s been forever. I missed you tons!"

"Very kind of you, Miss Vivienne," Edmund replied with a stiff, polite smile. "The Master and Madam haven't stopped talking about your arrival. Ready to head out?"

Vivienne shot a quick look at Lyra. A smirk threatened to break, but she forced on a worried face. "Wait, Edmund. You’re here for both of us, right? Where are your manners? You forgot my sister."

Edmund squinted, tracking Vivienne's eyes to the girl a few feet away.

She was wearing a basic white tee, plain pants, and beat-up white sneakers. Her canvas bag looked like it had been through the wash a hundred times, and her cap hid most of her face.

She looked... well, straight out of the sticks.

A flash of disgust crossed Edmund's face before he masked it. He gave a stiff bow. "My bad, Miss Selena. You’re dressed so... casual. I didn't recognize you."

Lyra was tapping away, texting Phoenix. She slowly looked up.

Under the cap, her insanely gorgeous face came into view.

She gave Edmund a deadpan look, then went right back to her screen, thumbs flying.

Like the guy didn't even exist.

Edmund froze. Vivienne bit her lip to keep from laughing out loud.

*Go ahead, Lyra, keep digging your own grave.* The ruder she acted, the more their grandparents would hate her. And the more it made Vivienne look like a total angel.

Vivienne was screaming with joy inside, but she kept her voice soft and sweet. "Hey, Lyra, don't be mad at Edmund. He’s been with Grandma and Grandpa forever. They sent him because they care about us, okay?"
