Chapter 541

Damon lunged for Gareth. Roric blocked him, laughing in his face. "Are you brain-dead, kid? Or just deaf? Look around. Everyone is frozen." Roric rolled his eyes.

"Save him!" Damon screamed, losing his mind.

Roric just smirked. "Nah."

"Nah?" Damon’s jaw dropped. "What the hell do you mean, nah?"

Roric shrugged. "Well, 'no' is what people say when they're shutting down a dumb request. Which is exactly what I'm doing."

"But why?" Damon whined like a toddler.

"A few reasons, kid. First, he's basically a vegetable. Second, he did this to himself. He tortured the girl—duh. Now, I love a good torture session, but he made the rookie mistake of letting her live." Roric leaned down, staring into Gareth's blank eyes. "Rule number one: if you break someone's mind like he broke hers, you kill them. Period. Otherwise, you end up like this. And third? This is a teachable moment for you." Roric turned back to Damon. "From here on out, you're on your own. You spent years riding this moron's coattails. And look where that got you. You're mated to some clingy, low-rent bitch who acts like she runs the place. The delusion in this pack is freaking wild." He leaned back. "The kid deserves to die for thinking his little title would save him here."

Damon’s voice went dead cold. "I'll kill you." He glared at Roric. Blair and Magnus whimpered in the background. The sound snapped Damon back. He whipped around to Freya, baring his teeth. "I swear to God, I will end you."

Freya just grinned. "Stupid boy. I died the second he laid a hand on me. You can't touch me." She burst into a psycho laugh. Honestly, she sounded completely unhinged.

*She's playing martyr,* Terra whispered. *She wants them to think she's solo. She thinks we're gonna ditch her.*

*Well, she's as dumb as Damon then,* Umbra snorted. I agreed. No way in hell were we leaving her behind. Not again.

Roric looked at Orion, shaking his head like a disappointed dad. "Kids these days. Useless. But hey, what can you do? Need someone to keep the bloodline going, right?"

"You've got spares, don't you? Astrid... Caspian." Orion's words hit the room. A couple of frozen guards looked totally lost, waiting for the spell to break.

Roric chuckled, nodding. "True. But Leo? He got way too big for his boots." Roric shifted his weight. "That's why I had his brother and sister. I had him right where I wanted him, until my mother started whispering in his ear."

*Brother? My dad has a brother?*

Roric groaned. "My meddling mother... I was thrilled when the old hag finally kicked the bucket." The words hit me like a truck. "Though it pissed me off that when I killed her, she'd already locked her magic away from me."

*He murdered his own mother.* Terra stared at me, pure rage radiating off her. Our magic flared, the spark she'd been holding exploding into a flame. *He killed her... and they told us it was rogues.*

The lie hit me right in the gut. Rogues. Of course they blamed rogues. I wanted to scream, to tear him apart, but a warm hand pressed onto my shoulder.

*Calm down, sweetheart,* her soft voice whispered in my ear. *I knew it was coming. Ronnie helped me prep.* I felt her hand slide down my arm, fingers locking with mine. *I hid my magic in the book. The second you cracked it open, it was yours. You were always the heir. And don't argue—the goddess planned it this way.*

Our magic simmered down. She kissed my cheek. *You know what to do, my love. Stick to the plan. Forget revenge for me; I got my payback when I starved him of my power. Just protect your mate and save our people.*

*Yeah, no pressure, right?* Umbra muttered, stretching. *But she's right. We want to rip their throats out, but we gotta play it smart. Wait for the perfect opening.*

I nodded silently, holding back.

Suddenly, Astrid stepped out from the shadows and started strutting down the aisle.

"How the hell is she moving?" Damon’s eyes darted between Roric and Astrid.

Roric shrugged. "She's my kid."

"So?"

Roric just rolled his eyes. Astrid strolled past Damon and Blair, giving Damon's cheek a patronizing pat. "You really are brainless, huh?" Blair growled, but Astrid just kicked her in the ribs. "I've got my own magic, sweetie. You know the spell, you use the counterspell. Duh."
