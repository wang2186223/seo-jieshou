Chapter 475

My eyes snapped open. Mara Croft was losing her goddamn mind—screaming, sobbing, clawing at the dagger buried deep in Lyra Bloom’s chest. Thank god. I let out a breath, snatched the cup, and lunged. I locked my hands onto her face. "The hell is wrong with you, you crazy bitch?" I roared. "You killed our pup! My freaking baby!" She didn't answer. Instead, she yanked a fistful of hair right out of my scalp. Pain flared, but I didn't care. I just watched her eyes. I needed tears. Exactly five of them.

Her fingernails dug deep, carving bloody tracks down my cheeks. I felt the warm wetness start to drip. Shit. I jerked my head back just in time as a sharp claw swiped a millimeter from my eyeball. But I got them. Five tears splashed right into the cup. I shoved myself away from her. Look, I didn't want to hurt her. I’d already done enough damage.

I spun around and marched over to Gideon Stone. The guy was still bawling, still screaming his lungs out. Mom and Juniper had him pinned by the arms, keeping him from shredding me like his mate just tried to do. I grabbed his jaw, holding him still to catch his five tears while he spat venom in my face. "I hate your guts!" he shrieked. "I should've let Magnus end you when he had the chance!" Yeah, yeah. I let the words slide right off me. But then he dug the knife in deeper. "You're nothing like your dad. You're a cancer on this world, and everyone's gonna realize it soon. That's why the goddess gave you a trash mate. You deserved Damon. You two deserve each other. And that's why she took your kid. You would've just ruined it anyway."

I leaned down and pressed a quick kiss to his forehead. "Hey, maybe you're right," I muttered. I turned right back to Lyra Bloom.

"Get the hell away from her!" Mara shrieked. I ignored her, walking back to the smoking bowl beside Lyra. I tipped a splash of the boiling liquid into my cup, grabbed Lyra by her shoulders, and forced the stuff down her throat. "DON'T YOU TOUCH MY DAUGHTER!" Mara went wild, thrashing against Daisy's iron grip, but Daisy just held on tighter.

I shoved Lyra flat on the table and yanked the dagger out of her chest in one hard pull. Standing right over her, I dumped the rest of the scalding brew straight into the raw, jagged hole in her chest. Gideon went ballistic. "Stop it! Leave her alone!" His scream made my hands shake, but I didn't stop. I grabbed the whole bowl and flipped it, dousing her. Instantly, Lyra burst into flames. I watched the fire catch, feeding it until the sparks jumped inches high.

"NO!" Mara lunged forward, but Daisy tackled her mid-air. "Please, god, no," Mara sobbed, collapsing in a heap right next to her mate.

The sheer, unadulterated hatred burning in their eyes as they clung to each other made my throat go completely dry. "They'll get it later," Grandma whispered from behind me. "Finish the job."

I threw my hands up over Lyra, channeling every ounce of my power straight into her. "Lorna ca shad une ben sou sla clen da shure sav itsha dom." Slowly, the gaping wound in her chest began to knit back together. The nasty brand Magnus had burned into her skin started flaking off like ash. I grabbed the dagger off the table again and waited. The fire raged for a few agonizing minutes while the parents watched, absolutely horrified, as their kid burned to a crisp.

Panic started to creep in. I glanced back, but Grandma was gone. Damn it. Did I screw this up? My knuckles turned white around the dagger's hilt. I closed my eyes and bowed my head. "Celestia, hear me. Get this kid out of the fire. Save her from this crap fate she never asked for. Help me cut the cord tying her here. Let me set her free."

Gideon was struggling to his feet, wheezing hard. "I'm gonna kill you," he gasped, fighting like hell against Mom and Juniper. I kept my eyes glued to the flames dancing over Lyra. "Let me go!" he roared. Mara reached out, grabbed Juniper's leg, and yanked. It was just enough of a distraction. Gideon broke loose. He leaped right over the table, lunging straight for my throat.

Bam. I slammed a magical shield up between him and the table. "Give me back my baby!" he screamed, pounding his fists against the invisible wall. I couldn't look away. Not even for a split second. I had to find the cord. Gideon threw his entire body weight against the barrier. "The second this wall drops, you're dead meat! You hear me?" he snarled, completely blind with rage. "YOU'RE DEAD!"

I didn't even blink. I kept my eyes locked on Lyra. Thank god I did. The moment I saw that thin, black thread, I whipped my hand out and sliced it clean. Magnus's agonizing shriek echoed through the cabin, so loud everyone covered their ears. Except me. I didn't break eye contact with Lyra. I felt warm blood trickle out of my ear, mixing with the cuts on my face.

"I'll kill you!" Mara and Gideon screamed in unison. Gideon charged the shield again, and this time, I let it drop. His hand clamped around my throat like a vice, pulling me close. "I hope the goddess kicks your soul straight to hell," he growled. Then, he squeezed.

"Daddy, let her go."
