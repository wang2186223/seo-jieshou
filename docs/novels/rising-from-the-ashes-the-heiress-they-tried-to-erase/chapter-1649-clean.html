Chapter 1651:

His brows slammed together, carving a deep furrow into his forehead. All the tenderness that had warmed his eyes moments ago evaporated—replaced by a fury so raw it seemed to consume him from within.

“No.”

The word came out cold, trembling with barely contained rage. Chris lifted his head slowly. “Anything but this. I can’t do it.”

Maia blinked, stunned. Chris… was refusing her?

“Why?” she asked, genuinely bewildered. “This is clearly the best option for everyone—”

“I said no!” His voice cracked, rising into something almost hysterical.

His fingers dug into the wheelchair armrests, knuckles bone-white from the pressure. His chest heaved violently, as though some wild beast were thrashing inside him, desperate to break free.

“Do you have any idea—” His voice dropped, low and venomous. “Everything I’ve done. Every humiliation I’ve endured. Every moment I played the fool. Creating The Mask. All of it—every last bit of it—served one single purpose.”

His eyes, bloodshot and burning, locked onto hers. Through clenched teeth, he forced the words out like shards of glass. “To destroy Cooper Group. To wipe that corrupt, rotten clan off the face of the earth. Not to inherit it—to annihilate it!” His breathing came in ragged gasps. “And now you want me to become the head of the family? To carry on that cursed bloodline?”

A bitter, broken laugh escaped him. “Absolutely impossible.”

Maia stood frozen, staring at the man before her—suddenly a stranger. She had known he carried wounds. But she had never imagined his hatred for the Cooper family ran this deep. So deep that even her plea… even the safety of Wront… couldn’t move him.

“Chris…” Her voice came out soft, fragile. She lowered her gaze. “You just promised me…”

When Maia’s words reached him, Chris went still for a heartbeat. Then, as though every ounce of strength had been siphoned from his body, he sagged back into the wheelchair.

“I’m sorry…” The words emerged low and rough, laced with exhaustion and defeat.

He turned his face away, unable to meet her clear, searching gaze—as if the light in her eyes might sear the darkness festering in his chest. “I can’t let it go…” His voice cracked. “My father’s death. My mother’s spiral into despair. Everything I’ve endured all these years. Every time I close my eyes, it all comes rushing back—wrapping around me like shadows, suffocating me. The only time I feel any relief is when I imagine Cooper Group crumbling to dust. When I picture that family burning to ashes.”

Inherit a throne drenched in blood? Protect the very clan that had cast him aside like trash? That would be a fate worse than death.

Maia said nothing. She simply watched him—a man trapped in the jaws of his own hatred. His suffering wasn’t foreign to her. She understood what it meant to be left behind, what it cost to climb out of the abyss alone. But she also knew, with brutal clarity, that if he couldn’t find a way to release this poison, it would devour him entirely.
