Chapter 615

We headed down to the dungeons. Elara was already there by the door, holding a bowl packed with my gear. "Thanks for prepping this, Elara," I said, snatching the bowl. She gave a quick bow. "Anytime, Luna Lyra." My head whipped back like I'd been slapped. I shot a look at Orion. The guy was actually smirking.

Boom. My eyes shut, and suddenly I was back on that freezing metal table. Screaming my lungs out while they sliced my baby out of me. *I’m the Luna.* The memory hit me like a physical blow, knocking the wind right out of me. God, I wanted to puke. Damon’s smug, laughing face flashed in my mind. "Whoa, look at you, shocked," Orion said, dragging me against his side. "But hey, you're not some single Alpha anymore. No more keeping us a secret." He planted a kiss on me. "Get used to it, Luna Lyra. Or Queen. Your choice."

"You... you told her to call me that?" I choked out, barely finding my voice. Acid burned my throat. I was literally gagging. Orion pulled back, his face falling. "Wait. You don't want to be my Luna?"

I cupped his jaw. "Of course I do!" I tried to explain, but the phantom stench of burning silver filled my nose. I dry-heaved. Orion flinched away, totally stung by my reaction. I'd hurt him, bad. "Then what the hell is your problem?" He glared, boring holes right through me. It felt like he was stripping my soul bare, and he hated what he was looking at.

"Nothing!" I squeaked, hating the sheer panic in my own voice. Right then, Mom and Dad rounded the corner. Dad had gone to fetch her while we were heading down. The second Mom saw my face, she bolted over. "Oh my god, what happened?" Dad followed a bit slower, but I could feel every single eye in the room locking onto me.

"Seriously, nothing," I lied. It fell totally flat. Orion backed off another step, his face going completely blank. He was shutting down, putting up walls. *Great. He thinks you're rejecting him,* Umbra growled in my head. *Fix this shit.* Terra tried to soothe Umbra's frantic energy. *Chill out. You know exactly why she's losing it.* Then Terra snapped at me: *Talk to him! Stop hurting our mate and explain!*

"Okay, someone talk to me," Mom demanded, totally lost. Elara bowed again, looking terrified. "I... I called Alpha Lyra 'Luna,' and she kind of... freaked out. I'm sorry, I was just doing what the King ordered." With that, she scrambled out of there. Orion let out a bitter laugh. "Damn. Didn't realize Lyra hated the title that much." He spun on his heel. "I need some air."

Cold panic spiked down my spine. "Wait!" I lunged and grabbed his hand, but he slipped right out of my grip. "Please, just let me explain!" He halted but kept his back to me. "Explain what? Someone calls you my Luna and you look like you're gonna puke." His voice cracked with pure hurt, ripping my heart out. How did everything go to hell this fast? "Bug?" Dad's voice broke through the noise. "Talk to us, kiddo."

My mouth worked, but nothing came out. I was totally paralyzed. Orion scoffed, taking another step away, but Mom cut in. "Lyra... is this the first time anyone's called you Luna since you died?" I spun around and grabbed her hands like a lifeline. "Yes," I choked out. The terror clawing at my chest was completely irrational. "I just..." I trailed off, totally unable to put it into words. Orion turned back, frowning at me. "Why does that even matter?"

Dad stepped up, locking eyes with me. I saw instant recognition in his gaze. "When I first got back after all those years, I'd call out for your mother. Then I'd panic when she didn't reply. I'd spent lifetimes loving her, and then—poof—she was gone. Logically, I knew why. But trauma doesn't care about logic. Anytime someone asked if I'd get a new Luna, I absolutely lost my shit." Mom gasped, and Dad caught her, pulling her tight. "Leo," she whispered, wrapping her arms around his waist. Dad held her close but kept his eyes on Orion. "Her freak-out has zero to do with you, kid."

All eyes landed on me. I took a massive breath. At least Orion had stopped walking. "When she said that word... I was right back on that operating table," I shuddered. "It's been forever since I had a flashback. It caught me totally off guard." I squeezed my eyes shut. "The pain, the smell—it all hit me at once. I felt sick." I reached out and grabbed Orion's hand. "I love being your mate. I love being your Luna. But I haven't heard that title since my first life, and I just lost it." I gently pulled him closer, and this time, he let me. "Dad's right. This wasn't about you. It was about everything that happened back then." Orion searched my eyes. He must have seen the raw truth there, because he finally pulled me into a tight hug. "I'm sorry," he muttered. "I shouldn't have jumped to conclusions."

Mom frowned, looking between us. "Wait, why did both of you react like that?" We all looked at her. She grimaced. "Lyra, you've had years to deal with this trauma. I've never seen you lose your mind like this. Even when you first woke up, you were way more put-together than today." Dad tilted his head, shifting his gaze to Orion. "And you're usually way more chill. You don't just take things as personal attacks." Dad closed his eyes, mumbled a quick spell under his breath, and flicked his wrist. A weird shimmer dissolved into thin air.

"What the actual fuck?" I gasped, taking my first real, deep breath since Elara had opened her mouth. "What just happened?" Orion blinked, his brow furrowing. "Whoa. I feel... better. The anger's gone." He stared at Dad. "What did you just do?" Dad casually dusted off his hands. "Just broke the hex that was targeting your mate bond."
