Chapter 1139

"Dad, I—"

Thwack. Corbin’s boot slammed into her ribs.

"I told you to put Lyra in the dirt on that island!" he roared, towering over her. "Why the hell is she still breathing? Why did you let her walk?"

He kicked her again, hard. "That island was a freebie. A perfect setup. Now she’s back in Vaelion City, and we can’t touch her. She’s safe. You idiot!"

He didn't stop. His fists and boots rained down on her.

Willow curled into a ball, screaming, begging the floorboards to just open up and swallow her.

Finally, Corbin backed off, chest heaving. He stared at her like he wanted to rip her throat out. "Take her to the lab," he spat to the guards.

The lab.

The word hit Willow like ice water. Her face drained of all color. She knew what went down in that basement. Go down there, and you don't come back. You end up in a jar. A human science project. Dead.

She scrambled across the floor, throwing her arms around his ankle, sobbing. "Dad, wait! Please! I didn't screw up, I swear to God! I kept her alive for a reason! I wanted to show you we can crush them in the finals. We’re gonna win, Dad! I guarantee it!"

She was hyperventilating, the words spilling out of her mouth in a desperate rush before he could kick her again. "The Dreamscape Initiative is taking first place! I only let her live because we’ve already won! The neural network report I turned in? It’s gonna smash every record out there. The feds are gonna make it a national standard. We’re talking major federal patents, Dad!"

She collapsed against his leg, gasping for air, tears smearing her face.

Corbin stopped.

He looked down at his daughter, his eyes narrowing. He wasn't buying it.

He knew Willow. Sure, she had some skills, but she wasn't some once-in-a-generation genius. There was no way in hell she could pull off a record-breaking, patent-winning project on her own. She just wasn't that smart.

Willow saw the look on his face. He knew she was full of shit.

Panicking, her face flushed red, she cracked. "Okay, okay! It’s... it’s Lyra’s data."

She swallowed hard. "I snuck in. They weren't looking, and I copied everything. The whole report."

She expected him to smile, to be thrilled. Instead, his scowl got deeper.

"Dad, I’m serious!" she begged, clutching his pant leg tighter. "Lyra’s team wrapped up their testing early. I took it straight from their lab myself. Every single number is real, I swear!"

"Lyra isn't stupid," Corbin muttered, his paranoia kicking in. "You really think a genius like her would just let a brainless brat like you walk away with her life's work? It’s a setup."

Willow’s face burned. The insult stung like a slap, but she swallowed her pride. She couldn't let him see her anger.

"She’s cocky, Dad," Willow argued softly. "She thinks she’s untouchable. She’s so full of herself, she probably never even dreamed someone would have the guts to rob her blind."

Corbin squinted, processing.

A long, tense silence filled the room.

Then, he whipped out his phone. "Get the lab techs ready," he barked into the receiver. "I’m sending over some data. I want a full, top-to-bottom analysis. Now."

He hung up and turned toward the door.

"Bring her," he ordered coldly, gesturing at Willow as he walked out.
