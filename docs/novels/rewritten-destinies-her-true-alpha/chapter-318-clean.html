Chapter 318

I backed up, rubbing my sore hands. Screw the pain, my mind was set. Orion glanced at the door, then turned to me. "Alright, what's the play here?"

I took a second. "I'm playing both sides." He cocked his head. "I'll pretend I'm dying to rebuild, but you're shutting me out. You're locking down the pack lands because of rogues. So, I'm stuck looking for a plan B until you let me back in."

"Why?" he asked.

"Two reasons. First, it makes Magnus think I despise your guts. If we're buddy-buddy, he'll smell a rat. I need him to let his guard down so I can get close."

Orion nodded. "Yeah. That's exactly why your dad and I kept our distance back then." I nodded back. Sure, it made sense, but it still stung like hell. "And the second thing?" he asked.

"It dangles a carrot he's dying to bite," I said, stretching my arms up.

"Which is?"

"A free power upgrade. Zero cost." I twisted my torso until my spine popped. "He thinks he can pull my strings through Damon. But let's be real—Magnus hates the idea of handing the reins to Damon. Sure, he'll do it to pull the strings from the shadows, but it kills him. Damon is a total dumbass. Playing second fiddle to his own idiot kid would drive Magnus crazy. But, what if Damon suddenly becomes the alpha of a completely different pack?"

"Then Magnus keeps Oakenfang for himself," Orion muttered, his eyes widening as the pieces clicked. "That is..."

"Absolutely batshit crazy," Juniper chimed in, looking totally freaked out. "But damn, that bait is too juicy for Magnus to ignore." I nodded. She nailed it. "Think about it," Juniper added. "Controlling two packs and pocketing your cash? He'd be drooling."

"Exactly. I'm going to wave that right in his face," I said.

Orion rubbed his chin. "You've gotta play this super smart." I resisted the urge to roll my eyes. He kept going. "I know you will. But what if..."

"What?" I asked, taking a sip of my drink.

Orion gestured wildly. "Magnus has no clue how loaded your dad's pack actually was." He was clearly cooking up a plan now. Juniper and I just smirked at each other.

"Nope, he's completely in the dark," I agreed.

"He still doesn't trust you, though," Orion said, throwing a sharp look my way. I shook my head.

"And he never will, not 100%."

"But if he thinks he's got you on a leash..." I pointed a finger at him. "Bingo. That's the goal." I nodded.

"Damon got dumped by his mate, but he's keeping it hush-hush," I said.

"Wait, how do you know that?" Orion cut in.

I glanced at Juniper. She gave me a nod. "A couple of reasons. First off, the idiot literally told me yesterday when he was begging me to give him a shot." Juniper let out a loud snort.

She pointed a finger at me. "Don't let her act innocent. She's the one who kicked him to the curb!" She burst out laughing, and I couldn't help but grin.

"Damon... is your mate?" Orion's voice dropped to a whisper.

I flashed a cold, nasty smile. "Not anymore. After all his bullshit? No way in hell."

Orion frowned. "What's that supposed to mean?"

I tilted my head. Something felt weird here. "I'll lay it all out for you once this mess is over."

He looked like I'd just kicked his puppy. "You don't trust me, do you?"

I let out a heavy sigh. "Look, it's not that. It's just..." I trailed off, giving Juniper a desperate 'help me' look. I turned back to him, biting my lip, trying to find the right words.

Juniper stood up with a loud groan. "Cut the crap. She doesn't trust you, plain and simple. And honestly? You don't deserve it." My head whipped toward her, but she was locked onto Orion.

"Wait, what?" Orion muttered, totally lost.

"Juniper, shut up!" I hissed.

"No way. It's about damn time somebody said it."

"Watch your mouth," Orion growled.

Juniper bared her teeth. "Make me! You barged into Lyra's life and made yourself the center of her universe. You two were so sickeningly in love it made me want to puke. But I gave you a pass because you cared about your pack's happiness. But then? Lyra went into heat, and you left her to rot." She sneered. "I loathed you for that. Still, I respected how much you loved your wolf. But then you fucking vanished! You tore her heart out and ghosted her, period. I was the one who had to glue her back together while you were gone. Oh, you sent some random crap in the mail, but did that fix anything? Hell no!"

"That's a cheap shot. We have a plan," Orion shot back.

She scoffed. "Your plan, you mean. Not hers. Not ours. You and her old man cooked it up and shut her out completely. You were supposed to teach her how to hack, but she had to find someone else to do your job. You bailed. You walked away. And now you show up out of nowhere with a goddamn fiancée?"
