Chapter 522

My legs burned from running for hours. The moment the cave mouth loomed ahead, I hit the dirt. Shadow slammed down right next to me. We dragged ourselves under some thick brush, and I threw up a soundproof barrier. Boom. Dead silence. Nobody could hear a peep.

"Mask your scent," I hissed.

He swiped his tongue across my cheek. "Way ahead of you, babe," he muttered, nudging closer. "Wait, did you just put us in some magic bubble?"

I shrugged. "Uh, sort of."

He let out a low chuckle. "I just blocked our sound."

He cocked his head. "Then why the hell are you still whispering?"

I opened my mouth to reply, but Terra burst out laughing in my head. "Hell if I know," I muttered.

"Heads up. Company," Shadow breathed.

He crawled right to the cliff's edge, squinting through the leaves. We froze. We were perched right above the cave's mouth, looking straight down. Below us, a rocky slope cut through the overhang, leading into a dense forest that ran straight out of pack territory. Keep running that way, and you'd hit human land. Bad news for regular wolves. Total suicide for werewolves.

A guy stepped out of the cave, pointing down the slope. "She went that way!" He spun back toward the entrance. "Yeah, yeah, I know the witchling is hunting her, but screw the tracker. Who cares? The Brotherhood wants us at Magnus's place by the weekend." More guys shuffled out of the shadows. "Keep hauling those explosives. The second that punk alpha mates with that whore, the Brotherhood hits the capital. They're going after the Lycan King." He jabbed a finger at some massive crates stacked in the corner. "Get those into the cars. And don't blow us up."

"Yo, what if the witchling snags the tracker?" one goon asked. "Can't we just camp out here?"

The leader paused, thinking. "I'll ask, but there's no way we're the only ones who know about this place."

Another guy waved a smartphone. "We got her phone right here. She only sent one text saying some rogue spotted her."

The leader held up a hand and dialed. "Yeah, the guys want to know if we can stay put in the caves if the witchling grabs the tracker." He held out his hand, and the other guy slid the phone into his palm. "No, she didn't mention the caves. Just said a rogue saw her and she was going dark." A pause. "Hell if I know, man. They're just asking." He groaned. "Fine. Tell the boss it's fine. Everyone's ready. We're moving the bombs tonight. We'll crash here until the wedding. You're 100% sure the King won't show?"

Shadow glanced at me, but my eyes were glued to the thugs.

"Show up for what?" one of the grunts asked his buddy.

"The wedding, dumbass," the second guy sneered. "Damon is marrying that whore. He invited the King because he thinks the guy will actually show up, and then boom—easy kill." He spat on the ground. "Guy is a total moron. He had the baddest she-wolf around, and he dumped her for that trash." He shook his head. "What a loser."

The rest of the crew nodded along.

The leader waved his hands. "Nah, just the guys shooting the shit. So, did the King RSVP? Honestly, I'd rather save the dynamite if that idiot's plan works and we can just drop him with silver."

Suddenly, a voice echoed from deep inside the cave. "Even if he skips the wedding, can't the other witch just take him out? She said she had him brainwashed, right?" A guy stepped into the light. He was missing an arm.

Shadow went stiff as a board. "Holy crap," he breathed.

"What? Who's that guy?" I whispered.

"That bastard... he was the captain of the guard when my dad got murdered. Supposedly died trying to buy my dad time to run." He was literally shaking with rage.

"Well, mystery solved on how they snuck in that night," I muttered. I pressed my body against his, trying to stop the shaking. "Easy, babe. Breathe. We're gonna make every single one of them bleed for this." He took a slow breath and finally nodded.

"Yeah, got it," the leader said into the phone. "We'll keep our eyes peeled for the she-wolf. If she shows, we bag her." He turned and walked back into the dark, the rest of his crew following like lost puppies.

My stomach did a flip, but my brain was already spinning a plan. First things first, though. I gave Shadow a nod and started backing out of there. I'd heard plenty. We already knew they were in bed with the Brotherhood, but hearing those idiots brag about it was the cherry on top.

"Where to?" he whispered as we sprinted away from the cliffs.

"We're going hunting."
