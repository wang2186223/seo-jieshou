"What the hell? Is he trying to murder her? That's a straight-up violation of the track rules!"

"Kick him out! Kick him out! Kick him out!"

"Play fair! Play fair! Play fair!"

The grandstands exploded.

The crowd was totally repulsed by this "Rookie King" clown, Silver Ghost.

Down on the asphalt, Lyra was in deep shit. Three bikes squeezed her tight, and that Dark Serpent GT was barreling in for a nasty T-bone.

She was inches from the turn's sweet spot.

No brakes meant a guaranteed wreck.

She had to back off, or the Ghost would spin out.

But Lyra didn't give a single shit.

Right at the dead center of the curve, she pinned the throttle.

"Holy shit, she's insane! Who speeds up on a hairpin?!"

"She's totally trapped! Silver Ghost is on her tail, where is she even going?!"

"Even Skye's bike can't handle that speed without wiping out!"

"She's gonna fly straight into the barrier!"

A massive wave of screams tore through the crowd.

The rear wheel whipped out. Lyra's bike skimmed the metal guardrail at a ridiculous, razor-thin angle.

Then...

Liftoff.

She went airborne.

Every jaw dropped. Hearts stopped as the Ghost hung in the sky, a collective gasp choking the crowd.

*BRRRRRAP!*

The engine screamed, shaking the air.

The silver-and-black beast was a streak of lightning suspended in mid-air.

Crazy. Just pure, unadulterated madness.

Any idiot knows you don't let your tires leave the dirt when you're redlining.

Especially not mid-drift.

Without traction, physics should have flung her and the bike straight into orbit.

A drop from that height meant a body bag.

The fans were losing their minds.

The three guys trying to block her froze.

This chick had balls of steel.

Even as pros, they wouldn't touch a stunt like that with a ten-foot pole.

*SCREEECH!*

A brutal, metal-on-asphalt screech sliced through the noise.

Up in the air, the nose of the silver-and-black bike pitched down.

The Ghost sailed clean over the bend, dropping onto the lower loop of the track. The front tire slammed down, she feathered the throttle, and the handlebars stopped shaking.

*SKRRRRT!*

Rubber bit the pavement, screaming for grip.

*VROOOOM!*

The heavy bike locked back onto the line. She dumped the clutch, and the speedometer started climbing again.

The stands went absolutely ballistic.

A roar of pure, goosebump-inducing hype erupted from the crowd.

They just watched it happen.

Lyra, riding Skye's mythical machine, had just pulled off the exact same god-tier stunt.

She literally jumped the gap, skipping a whole chunk of the track to land on the next straightaway.

Just like that, she left the pack in the dust.

*SMASH!*

Right in the middle of the jaw-dropping moment.

One of the trailing riders lost his focus, clipped the barrier, and went flying off his seat.

That move...

It was sick. Absolutely unreal.

So this was Skye's legendary Ghost?

Was everyone who rode this thing a total freak of nature?

It was scary how good she was.

What kind of god-level talent did it take to completely embarrass the "Rookie King" like that?
