Chapter 404

The second Magnus yanked the needle out, I snapped. I bolted up, baring my teeth with a nasty growl. I went straight for his throat, but the bastard was too quick. He slipped back and slammed the heavy door right in my face. He let out this smug, irritating laugh. That did it. I threw myself at the bars on purpose. *Clang!* Searing pain ripped through me. Silver. I shrieked and scrambled back, panting.

"Yeah, I wouldn't do that, sweetheart," Magnus sneered, dangling the keys right in front of my nose. "Everything in here is silver. Play nice, and maybe I'll let you out." I wanted to puke. Instead, I threw my weight against the metal again to test their strength. The bars actually groaned and bent a fraction. The guards flinched, jumping back. I let out another fake shriek, hit the floor, and dragged myself back, making sure Magnus got a good look at my smoking, blistered skin. "Stop! Please!" I whimpered.

I backed away, dragging my limbs, letting out a pathetic, weak whine to keep up the act.

*Damn, Lyra. You deserve an Oscar for this,* Umbra chuckled in my head.

*Magnus is totally buying it,* Terra chimed in, shaking her head. *But don't overplay your hand. A cornered wolf fights, but a beaten one stays the hell away from the silver.*

*Got it,* I thought back. *That was the last hit anyway. I just needed to see if I could bust these bars open if things go south.*

*But the silver...* Terra warned.

*It burns like hell, yeah, but I'm tough. I'll shake it off. I'm using my magic to slow down the healing on purpose so Magnus thinks I'm half-dead. But I gotta figure out what this prick is actually planning.*

Magnus finally stood up straight, seeing I was done fighting. He looked at the guard. "Feed her. Normal rations. But watch your step. She's got some kick in her."

"What about the drug?" The guard pointed at me.

"I'll handle the daily shots myself. Nobody enters this cell. Got it?"

The guard stared at me. I was curled up in the back, looking like a broken mess. He swallowed hard and nodded. "Got it, boss."

They started filing out. Damon paused to spit one last insult. "Stupid bitch. You won't last the week. Hope whatever you did was worth it." He snorted and walked. Gideon Stone was the last to leave. He gave me this dead-eyed look, but for a split second, a flash of pure panic crossed his face before he masked it.

Finally, quiet. Just me, my raw burns, and the wolfsbane burning in my veins. I let my magic loose, healing everything except the very top layer of skin. The burns looked nasty as hell, but they were just surface-level. I gasped in relief as my body started flushing out the poison. Years of building up immunity kicked in, and the pain vanished.

I was drifting into a light sleep when the heavy main door creaked open. "Go grab some food, I'll take over the watch," Gideon Stone's voice muttered. Standard protocol for high-value prisoners, apparently, because the guard didn't even question it. He just walked out.

I kept my eyes shut, playing dead. Gideon walked over. I heard a click, and the lights flickered for a second before buzzing back to life. "Okay. Any bugs or cameras in here are dead now," he muttered. He stood there, watching me, then slid down the wall until he hit the floor. "Look, I know you can hear me. And I bet you know who I am, even though I have no fucking clue who you are." He closed his eyes, thumping his head against the wall. "I tried calling Lyra. Texted her too. Nothing."

I let out a low, mocking chuff. Trusting him was a massive gamble. "She trusted you," I rasped. I let the venom drip from every word.

He winced, nodding. "I know. And after today, I look like a piece of shit. But I just hope to God she gives me a second to explain." He squeezed his eyes shut, cursing under his breath. A single tear rolled down his cheek.

"Oh, now you're crying?" I snapped. "She was going to get you and your girl out. She was going to save your kid. And you stabbed her in the back." Every word hit him like a physical punch. He curled inward, looking completely broken.

"I didn't betray her!" he choked out. "Even if she hates my guts, I pray to the Goddess she saves Lyra Bloom. Lyra is the best damn person I know." He stared at his trembling hands, whispering, "Even if it kills me, she has to save Lyra Bloom."

I snorted. "You say you didn't betray her, but look around. Look at what you did today. You're dirty, Gideon."

His head snapped up, eyes locking onto mine. "I know! I know I messed up. I was stupid. I was weak. But I am not the bad guy here!"

I scoffed. "Right. You're just throwing your woman and kid to the wolves."

He bolted up, furious. "I would never do that to Lyra Bloom and Mara Croft! They are my fucking life! I'd die for them!" He slammed his fist against the wall, his hand flying to his neck. "Hell, I *am* dying for them!"
