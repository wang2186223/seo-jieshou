Chapter 345

Total silence. Nobody moved. I scanned the room—jaw, jaw, jaw, all dropped. Even Mom’s. She crept over, muttering, "Well, damn."

We snapped our eyes back to Luna. A quick blink, and boom, those normal brown eyes were back. I dropped to my knees, eye-to-eye with her. "Hey, kiddo. That a regular thing?"

"Who, the goddess?" Her voice bounced back, totally normal. A shrug. "Dad says she only drops in when she’s got mail to deliver." She let out a tiny giggle. "See, my own visions? I remember those. But when she hijacks me? Total blackout."

"How come?"

"She gotta knock me out first. Otherwise, Dad says it’d hurt like hell. I don’t care, though. She’s the boss." Luna whirled around, marching right up to my mom. "Thanks for the curtains, by the way!" Then she skipped off, gone.

Still on the floor, I craned my neck up at Mom. "Yeah, I'm drowning here."

She snorted. "Welcome to parenting. Usually takes a few years to hit this wall, you just got the speedrun." She pecked my cheek. "Get Freya settled. Then, grocery run. We stocking this place?" I gave a quick nod. "Got it. We'll handle it." She pushed past me, stopping right in front of Freya. "Sweetheart, I'm so damn sorry. I did what I could."

Freya’s eyes welled up. "You saved my life, Luna. Seriously. Thank you."

"I hate doing this, but look at me." Mom pressed her forehead right against Freya's. "I see you. Am I your Luna?" Freya nodded, a tear spilling over. "Then here's an order. Live. You survive this shit. Understand?" No alpha-magic spark. Just raw, heavy words. Mom pulled back, hands framing Freya's face. "Live. Build yourself back up. Way stronger than before." Another nod from Freya. Mom's face went stone-cold. "Then you come back. And you murder that motherfucker."

Freya let out a wet laugh. "Count on it, Luna." Mom arched an eyebrow. "I mean... Elara." Freya bowed, then turned her eyes on me. "Swear to me. Swear I'm not gonna be a drag on you."

"I, Seraphina Thorne, swear on my life—you being here is a straight-up blessing." Freya nodded, turned, and dragged her feet into the bedroom. We crowded the hallway, watching her flip the switch. Light flooded the room. She looked around, chest heaving with a deep breath. When she spun back, her lower lip was shaking.

"We'll give you some space." I reached for the doorknob. "I'll yell when dinner's ready." Click. Door shut. I heard her gasp for air through the wood. I walked away, pretending the sound of her breaking down wasn't tearing through my ears.

Daisy and Juniper looked like they’d just watched a puppy get kicked. "Is she gonna make it?" Daisy whispered. I opened my mouth, but Mom beat me to the punch.

"That bastard put her through pure hell." I shot Mom a look, but her face was a brick wall. She shook her head. "Don't ask. I'm not talking. Honestly, I only saw the wreckage anyway."

"Wreckage?" Juniper propped herself against the drywall.

Mom nodded. "Every single day, he dragged her to the pack house. Every single day, she crawled back more shattered. I begged her to tell me. She just kept her mouth shut." Mom stared at her shoes, sighing. "Then I screwed up. I confronted Magnus. Next day? She came back mute." Mom looked away, disgusted. "I'm heading out." Just like that, she walked off.

Daisy stared at the closed door. "We just gotta back her up." Right then, a little giggle drifted from Luna's room.

"Both of 'em," Daisy muttered, turning on her heel to head inside.

Juniper let out a heavy sigh, eyeing the stairs. "Got my phone back. I'm gonna head down and draft up a grocery list." I cracked a smile. "I'll text you what we need."

"Appreciate it." I waved, heading back to the closet.

Mom was already chilling on my mattress when I walked in. "Ready?" She bounced up. I nodded. She rubbed her palms together, grinning. "Sweet. I'm dying to take my new ride for a spin." She actually giggled, then bolted out the door. I double-checked the locks, shut my door tight, and bolted downstairs. Mom was already in the driver's seat of her SUV. *Beep beep!* She rolled the window down. "Move it!" I jumped in, and we tore out of there.
