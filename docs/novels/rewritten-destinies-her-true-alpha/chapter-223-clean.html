Chapter 223

The elevator dinged. Finnian stepped out.

"Tell your wife to enjoy the plants!" I called out, flashing a grin.

"Oh, she's gonna love 'em. Thanks again!" Finnian spun around, threw up a quick wave, and the doors slid shut. Next stop: penthouse.

"Ugh, I'm literally vibrating," Daisy buzzed, practically bouncing on her heels.

"Why?" I snorted, fumbling with my keys while balancing a potted plant.

Daisy snatched the pot right out of my hands. "Gimme that."

I tried to pull back, but she laughed me off. "Dude, look at you. You've got bags, takeout, a whole-ass garden, and your purse. How were you planning to unlock the door? Magic?" She rolled her eyes like I was a total idiot.

Juniper, holding her own plant, just smirked.

"Juniper, help me out here!" I laughed.

She just grinned wider. "Don't look at me. She started bossing me around months ago. You taught her how to punch, but this sassy-assistant routine? She came up with that all on her own."

We cracked up.

"Hey! I'm not bossy!" Daisy grumbled, which only made us laugh harder. "I'm being helpful!"

"Aha!" I pointed a finger at her, finally dragging my keys out. "We're getting you a t-shirt. 'I'm not a bully, I'm just aggressively helpful.'"

Juniper lost it. Daisy turned bright red.

"Come on, we're just teasing." I stepped out of the elevator, unlocked the door, and we piled in, dumping the plants on the floor.

"So, magic time?" Juniper clapped, eyes shining.

I shook my head. "Food first. Otherwise, it's gonna get ice-cold before we even start the spell."

I marched into the kitchen, dropped the bags, and we grabbed our food before crashing in the living room.

Daisy shoved a massive bite of her burger into her mouth. "Mmph. So, what's the game plan?"

I paused. "I need to figure out what's killing my pack." I took a bite of my sandwich.

Juniper nodded, totally unfazed.

Daisy, however, instantly started choking.

"Whoa, easy!" Juniper leaned over and slammed her hand against Daisy's back.

Daisy kept hacking, her face turning a violent shade of purple. Juniper gave her another hard whack. *Thwack.* A chunk of half-chewed meat shot out of Daisy's mouth. She gasped, sucking in air. "There we go."

Daisy looked up, eyes watering, staring at me in sheer panic.

"You good?" I handed her a stack of napkins.

She grabbed them, wiping her face and hands, then leaned back, eyes locked on me. "Wolves? Back up. Explain."

I glanced at Juniper.

Juniper threw her hands up. "Hey, she's my bestie too, but your secrets are your business. I wasn't gonna spill."

I smiled. She nodded back.

"Wolves?" Daisy demanded again. I winced.

"Okay, fast version," I said. She nodded, dead serious. "My dad's side comes from a crazy powerful witch line and the very first werewolf. My mom's side goes back to the first Lycan and Celestia herself."

Daisy's jaw hit the floor.

Juniper did a massive spit-take, coughing up her drink. "The goddess?! You left that part out!" she wheezed, grabbing a napkin to clean up the mess.

"Just found out today, actually." I popped a fry into my mouth. "Turns out she fell for a Lycan she literally created." I shrugged.

"Holy shit." Daisy scrambled to her feet, pacing the room. "So you have a Lycan *and* a wolf inside you."

I nodded. "Yep."

"Which means you get two mates." She whipped around to face me. "Isn't that going to be a massive disaster?"

Juniper snorted. I shot her a warning look.

"No. My wolf's mate is a total douchebag. I'm rejecting him the second I get the chance."

"Wait, you already know who he is?"

I closed my eyes and sighed. "Yeah. Terra's mate is Bradon."

Daisy shrieked, "Are you fucking kidding me?!"

"I wish." I swallowed the last of my burger. "But my witch side gave me a cheat code—a second chance at life."

Daisy collapsed back into her chair, completely brain-fried.

"I won't get into the details. Just know that Damon is a monster. I don't care how much he plays nice or pretends he's 'changed.' He's dead to me. Period."

Daisy nodded slowly. "Good. I've dealt with Damon and Magnus for years. They're absolute trash."

"I know," I said. "But we've got a mountain of shit to do tonight, and we still have day two of interviews tomorrow." I checked the time and felt like crying. "I don't even know how long this ritual is gonna take. Let's clean up and get ready."

"What's our job?" Juniper grabbed her trash and headed to the kitchen.

"Keep me anchored," I said, looking her dead in the eye. "I'm doing a spirit walk. And honestly? I don't know how the hell to get back yet."
