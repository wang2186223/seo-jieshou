Chapter 185

"War?" The word hit the room like ice. Everyone froze. Orion’s grip on my waist went white-knuckle. I fought off a shiver, looked them dead in the eye, and nodded. "Yeah. War."

"How the hell do you know that?" Juniper leaned in, eyes wide. The girls had finally stopped freaking out about the literal Lycan King sitting right next to me, but now this.

I locked eyes with her. "Has the Goddess ever talked to you?"

"Wait, what? The Moon Goddess?" Fawn nearly slipped right off her chair. I just gave her a slow nod.

"Some of us get signs," Orion chimed in. "A gut feeling. A nudge. Shamans get full-on visions." He shot me a look, asking a silent question. I gave him a tiny shake of my head—*not yet*. He got the message and turned back to Juniper. "But some get direct hits. Lycans get a personal visit when they come of age."

"A visit? Like, how?" Daisy was practically drooling.

"When my dad passed, I took the crown, hit the age," Orion said. "Celestia showed up in my dreams."

Luna snorted. "How do you know you weren't just dreaming?"

Right then, my forehead started burning like crazy. I rubbed it, hard. A collective gasp went around the table. Orion let out a low chuckle. "See? She doesn't do subtle." He whipped out his phone, snapped a quick pic, and shoved the screen in my face.

There I was. Messy black curls, a dumbfounded smirk, soft blue eyes. And deep down in my gaze, a stupid amount of longing for the guy holding the phone. But what made my heart stop was the glowing silver crescent branded right on my forehead.

I stared. Orion raised an eyebrow. "You're Moon Blessed?"

"Uh. Yeah." I turned back to the girls. They were staring at me like I had two heads.

"Moon Blessed..." Daisy whispered, totally dazed. "I read about that once. The last one was, like, three hundred years ago."

"Holy shit," Juniper muttered, swallowing hard.

We all whipped our heads toward her. "What?"

"My dad’s pack is pretty new," Juniper said, wiping her face. "But he learned the ropes from this ancient pack up in Canada. Old-school. Almost as old as the Silvermoons."

"The Okanagan pack in BC," I said.

She nodded. "Exactly."

"They had the last shaman before the current one," Orion noted.

"Right," Juniper said. "And that shaman left them a vision. It's been passed down ever since."

Orion’s hand yanked me tight against his side. "What vision? Why wasn't I told?"

"It was classified. Alpha Warner told my dad, my dad told me." Juniper sat up straight, taking a deep breath. "Look, I probably shouldn't even say this, but... when the Moon Blessed shows up, the clock starts ticking. If the Moon Blessed fails, or if the shaman gets corrupted, we're done. Game over. War wipes us off the map. The Moon Blessed is the first line of defense. The shaman is the second. Both have to win, or we all die."

"Both?"

"I don't even know who the damn shaman is," Orion muttered, a rare flash of panic in his eyes.

"I do." I grabbed his free hand, squeezing tight. I felt his inner wolf calm down instantly under my touch. "We can do this."

He nodded, exhaling. Juniper slumped back in her chair. "Damn. War is actually happening."

I looked at the girls, then back to Orion. "Yeah, it is." I squeezed his hand again. "But we're going to fight. For our packs, our families, our future. The rogues? They don't give a shit about our future. There's a damn good reason they're locked out."

"Exactly," Orion agreed. "Those wolves are either completely rabid or kicked out for doing sick, twisted shit. They're desperate. They've got nothing to lose and want to burn the world down." He pulled his hand back, his fingers accidentally brushing my bare spine. Goosebumps exploded across my skin.

"How does a wolf even go rabid?" Luna asked.

That was the million-dollar question. "We don't know for sure," Orion sighed. "But usually, it's triggered by massive, crushing trauma."

I nodded, the memory hitting me. "Three years ago, a pregnant female in my dad's pack got slaughtered. Eight months along. I went with my dad to break the news to her mate. He completely lost his mind. The goddess took him right then."
