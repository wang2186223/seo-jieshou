Chapter 330

Everything went slow-mo. Magnus squeezed his eyes shut, fists tight, but Damon completely lost his damn mind.

"Lyra, I swear to God, I didn't know!" He grabbed my face in his hands. "Please, just look at me."

I growled, my eyes flashing hot. "No, you look at me." I locked eyes with him. "You do this sick shit too?" Pure disgust dripped from every word.

*Perfect,* Terra chimed in. *Rattle them. Make 'em sweat. That's how we get what we want.*

*I just want to rip their throats out,* Umbra grunted. I was too pissed to even laugh.

*I get it,* Terra soothed. *But we need him to sign off on the rejection first.*

*I'll just force him. He's a weakling,* I thought.

Terra shut that down quick. *Nope. Doesn't work like that. If commands worked, any Alpha could force a mate rejection. The Goddess blocked that cheat code. It's gotta be 100% free will.*

*I'll tear them to pieces. Dead guys can't say no,* I muttered, my voice low but dripping with venom.

Umbra curled up. *True that.*

*Chill, girls. We can slaughter them later,* Terra said, pacing. *Let him think he's got you calm. Let Magnus think Damon can handle you. Play the game, get the goods for Freya.*

Damon leaned way too close. He planted a kiss on my forehead. A full-body shiver hit me. I couldn't help it. Magnus saw it and perked up, thinking I was melting. Hell no. It was pure, unadulterated disgust.

"No, baby, please, no," Damon mumbled, kissing me again. I shut my eyes, gagging at his scent. God, in my past life, this would've made my year. He used to be my whole damn world. But then he murdered our baby. Now? This fake-ass love he was pushing? It tasted like straight ash.

I yanked back, putting on a confused, hurt face. "Tell me he doesn't treat women like chew toys."

Magnus dodged my eyes. Gareth let out a dirty chuckle, then winced, clutching his shirt. "I don't have to force shit," Gareth sneered. Magnus relaxed a fraction. Damon let out a breath. "I'm a Beta, sweetheart. Bitches like you just lay down and spread 'em."

*Smack.* Magnus boxed his ear. Gareth growled.

Magnus raised an eyebrow, but I beat him to the punch with a cold laugh. "Bitches like me?" I ditched Damon and marched right up to Gareth's face. "Trust me, asshole, you haven't met a bitch like me." I bared my teeth. "Now tell me. Do you know what a Cath Báis is, or are you too stupid?"

Gareth glanced at Damon, who was frantically waving his hands behind my back. "Huh?" Gareth snickered. "A bitch is a bitch." He turned to Magnus. "What the hell is she whining about? A Cath Báis? What, is that some kinky mating ritual?" He looked back at me and did a vulgar pelvic thrust.

Magnus groaned, burying his face in his hands.

I smirked. "Aren't you just a fucking charm? Sorry to burst your bubble, but a Cath Báis has nothing to do with mating. It's an ancient law. And those old-school rules? Still valid if you call 'em out, right, Alpha Magnus?" My voice went ice-cold. I was thinking of my mom, and the rage was 100% real.

Magnus swallowed hard, his throat bobbing. "Yeah. They can."

"Will somebody tell me what the fuck a Cath Báis is?!" Gareth barked, totally blind to the heavy-ass vibe in the room.

I took a step closer, flashing a grin. The temperature in the office plummeted. Gareth took a step back without even thinking. His gut knew what his brain didn't: I was the apex predator here. "A Cath Báis," I said, my smile turning straight-up feral, "is a fight to the death."

Damon grabbed my arm and yanked me back. Magnus jumped right between Gareth and me. "Whoa, hold on," Magnus stammered. "Let's not do anything crazy here."

But Gareth—the absolute moron—just burst out laughing. He threw his head back, howling like it was the funniest joke he'd ever heard. Magnus closed his eyes, totally defeated, and let out a massive sigh. Damon squeezed my hand tight, probably terrified that Gareth's laughing was gonna make me snap.

"You? Fight me? To the death?" Gareth wheezed, tears literally squeezing out of his eyes. "I'm a Beta, babe. A trained killer. The future Alpha's right-hand man. And you think some flavor-of-the-week chick can just waltz into this office and take my head?" His laugh vanished, replaced by a nasty scowl. "You're even dumber than you look."

My fangs slid down as I grinned. I looked past him to Magnus. "You really need to put a leash on your dog, Magnus."

"Did you just call me a dog, you little whore?" Gareth roared.

But I was officially done playing nice.

"Hey, Gareth, you wanna know why nobody in this room is backing you up?" I ripped my hand out of Damon's grip and took a giant step forward. "Because everyone else here knows who the real boss is." I snapped my jaws, fangs clicking. "And hint: it ain't you."

Then, I let my aura explode.
