Chapter 580

Boom. Blackout. My head spun, but through the dark, the pack was screaming. *Hold the line.* That was it. That was the only thing that mattered. Dad was... No. Don't go there. Do not think about it. Just hold the damn shield. Keep them safe. I chanted it like a mantra. But my inner wolves were slipping away. Obsidian? Poof. Gone. God, I wanted to drop everything and run to him. But I couldn't. I had to lock down this cave.

Tears burned my face. My chest literally ached. And Mom... oh, God, Mom. She just got him back. Now? Gone. A sob ripped out of my throat. My grip slipped. *Crash.* A rogue rock zipped right past my barrier and smashed near someone. A scream echoed. I wanted to look. I wanted to see if they were digging people out. But I was blind as a bat. Just total darkness.

Inside my head, the lights were out. My magic was a pathetic little spark, ready to die. I squeezed out my absolute last drop of juice. The spark flared, just a tiny bit. I prayed to see my wolves huddled around that pot. Nothing. Empty. Shadow, Obsidian—wiped out. Even the pot was gone. My brain was shutting down. I was dying. I knew it. *Just buy them a few more seconds,* I whispered. I hit the dirt, reaching a hand toward that dying spark. Time to go.

"I'm sorry, Dad. I couldn't do it," I choked out. The last of my spark fizzled. Then—bam. Arms locked around my waist. A jolt of pure electricity shot straight through me, reversing the drain. That dying flame in my head? It locked in. Steady.

"Gotcha," Orion breathed right in my ear.

I panicked. "No!" I thrashed, trying to shove him off. "Get away! I'll drain you dry too!" I clawed to get loose, but the guy was a brick wall, and I was running on empty.

Then, out of the blackness: "We got you, ladybug." Finnian. My magic went wild. "Silvermoon!" he roared. Suddenly, my head exploded with a million voices.

"We’re right here, Lyra!"

"Almost there!"

"We got this!"

"You saved me. My turn." A tiny voice squeaked, and a tiny hand grabbed mine, dragging me back to the light. My eyes popped open. Boom—Finn's face. Those bright grey eyes were staring right at me. My brain flashed back to the last time I saw him.

"Whoa," I mumbled. "You grew up."

Poppy shoved through the crowd and slid to a halt next to him. "You got my daddy out," she chirped, throwing her arms around my waist. "Now we're getting yours." Just like that, the fog in my head started to clear.

Then came the stampede. Wolves piled in, hands slamming onto me, onto Orion, dumping their energy straight into us. I lost it and started crying. The power surge was insane. The cracked shield snapped back together, rock-solid, shoving the collapsing ceiling right back up. A few guys broke off, diving under the rubble to grab the wolves still pinned down.

With the barrier holding, rocks started flying out of the cave like pinballs, faster and faster. I couldn't watch. Every time they dragged a body out, my stomach did a sickening flip. So I locked eyes with Finn. The kid didn't blink.

He looked so damn fierce for a little kid. "Hey, it's okay to be scared," he said, sounding way too mature. "My dad says fear is just a heads-up from your body. You can be terrified and still kick ass." His lip trembled. "You're the bravest person alive, Lyra. You saved us all, even when you were bleeding out. And you're tough. My mom says my strength comes from my dad, so yours has to come from yours. If you're this badass, Alpha Caspian must be a total beast." I choked up, nodding as a tear rolled down. Finn cracked a smile. "Don't cry. You're my hero. And if your dad is tougher than you, there's no way the Moon Goddess is taking him down yet. Right?" He stared up, waiting for me to agree. "Right?"

Another tear slipped. "Right," I whispered.

Around us, people were scrambling through the cave. Then, a shout cut through the noise. "Hey!" Someone was stumbling around in the dark. "I got someone over here! We got a live one!"
