Chapter 104

"Umbra, stop!" Mom lunged, throwing herself right between us. "You can't do this, please!"

I just shook my head.

*Why the hell not?* Umbra snarled, glaring at Mom. With a hard shove, Umbra took the wheel, pushing me back into the mind-space right next to Terra.

"She’s been a total wreck since we got back," Terra said, turning to me. "Honestly, I don't blame her. She finally shifted, and that alpha aura mixed with your raging hormones has her on a hair-trigger. Plus, she's had to deal with me crying over my dead mate and trying to claim him."

"Terra?" I stared at her. Like, really looked. Her fur was patchy as hell, and she looked completely wiped out.

"She did what had to be done. She stopped me from claiming Damon, and I don't regret it. It's just... damn, it's hard. But Blair pushed her way past the line. Now, she pays." Terra turned, limping back into the shadows of my soul forest to rest. No way was she fighting Umbra on this. She was 100% on Umbra's side.

I snapped back to reality. Mom was still begging for Blair.

*I said why the hell not? Why can't I wreck her?* Umbra's pure rage caught me off guard. Usually, we're totally in sync, and she's the level-headed one. Unranked wolves are weaklings. They're already at the bottom of the food chain. The second an Alpha or Beta walks in, their wolves instinctively roll over and show their bellies. Ranked wolves like us shouldn't even have to flex on them.

"Umbra, stop! She's unranked. Her wolf is pathetic!" Mom begged.

"Oh, is she?" Umbra spat. "Could've fooled me. Her human is cocky as hell. She struts around this pack, bossing stronger wolves around like she owns them, all by hiding behind her Beta daddy's rank. She doesn't have her own power, so she uses his to get whatever she wants. And everyone just takes it because Ronnie backs her up."

Mom's hand dropped. "Wait... what?"

Umbra's head whipped up. "Don't play stupid with me, Mom." Her voice was pure venom.

That was my cue. I fought to push Umbra back, almost taking over, when—*BOOM*. The door blew off its hinges. Ronnie, Magnus, and Damon burst in, stopping dead in their tracks when they saw the sheer size of my wolf.

"What the actual fuck is going on here?" Magnus roared, throwing his Alpha aura around like a weapon to shut us down.

Umbra's eyes flared. She let out a bone-chilling snarl. "You wanna go toe-to-toe on aura, Alpha? My money's on me." She slowly turned to face the guys at the door. I saw Damon literally shudder.

His eyes flared with sudden heat, and it made me want to puke. Then, a low, miserable howl echoed deep in my soul. Shit. I had to grab the reins right now, or everything was going to hell.

"Umbra! Get back here and help Terra! I can't hold her off alone!" She ignored me, but I felt her panic spike. Another howl ripped through—closer this time. Terra was coming back.

"Get the hell off my daughter!" Ronnie snarled, stepping up. Umbra cut him off with a nasty growl.

"Who the hell are you to order me around?" Umbra snapped, then looked at the Alpha. "Your Beta breaks every rule in the book to protect his brat. And this thing..." Umbra ground her paw right into Blair's face. "...struts around like she's queen, demanding things way above her pay grade. She thinks your son and her daddy will shield her from rank challenges."

Magnus whipped around to glare at the two men. "Is she lying?"

Umbra snorted. "Ronnie put four she-wolves in the hospital, including Sydney, the Beta female. And Damon bullied the entire school into catering to her pathetic little demands."

I shoved hard, fighting for control.

"Umbra, let me handle this! I'll make your point!" I yelled, looking back at the soul forest. Terra had just broken through the tree line, limping but picking up speed. "I'll say whatever you want, but you have to deal with Terra!" I screamed, throwing my weight forward again. This time, she let me slide past and took the back seat.

"You're full of shit!" Magnus roared at me.

I just let out a dry laugh. "Am I? Ask them." I flexed my claws, digging them right into Blair's skin without breaking it. I glared up at Ronnie. "Spill the truth, or I'll blind your little mutt."

"She's... she's telling the truth," Ronnie muttered.

"Louder, Beta! Speak up, nobody can hear you!" I barked.

"She's telling the truth! I put Sydney in the hospital last year because of Blair!" He shot a panicked look at Magnus. "She's just a kid. She's my kid."

"She's weak!" Magnus roared.
