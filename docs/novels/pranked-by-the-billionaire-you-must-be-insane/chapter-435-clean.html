Chapter 438

Lyra looked up, slow and bored. No makeup, but she still looked killer, her lips curved in a nasty little smirk. "Oh, yeah? Try me."

"Seriously, Lyra, drop the act," Genevieve said, putting on her best fake-sad face. "You can't just crash a VIP gig like this. It's the Blackwood heiress's big eighteen. You don't even know her. Trying to force your way in? It's pathetic. You're just making a fool of yourself."

Lyra's eyes thinned, a mean spark dancing in them. "Funny. Because watching you suck up to her five minutes ago was the most embarrassing thing I've ever seen."

Genevieve froze. The memory of Elara almost shutting her down during the gift-giving flashed in her mind, hot and humiliating. But she swallowed it.

"Well, I actually have a ticket," Genevieve snapped, throwing her shoulders back, suddenly all smug again. "And she took my necklace. That means she loves my stuff. Once I start my new gig at Aurevion Jewelers—you know, the Lancaster brand?—I'll probably be making all her custom pieces."

She made sure to loud-mouth the words *my design* and name-drop the billionaire Lancaster family's company.

She’d just flexed her work to the one percent and got a nod from the legendary Ms. Rainsford. That gave her every right to look down on a gold-digging nobody like Lyra, who only got anywhere by sleeping her way up.

Lyra chuckled at the sheer arrogance on Genevieve's face. "Oh, really? You mean that... sketch you dragged out of some dusty trash bin?"

Genevieve’s chest went cold. Her head whipped up, panic instantly plastering her face.

Her hands balled into tight fists. Did this bitch actually know something?

No way. There was zero chance Lyra knew she’d swiped that design from the old Wynn family warehouse.

If Lyra had found those sketches first, she would’ve used them to kiss the Wynns' asses ages ago.

Nobody could prove she stole it. Nobody.

Genevieve forced her breathing to slow down. "I don't even know what you're hinting at. It's *my* design, obviously."

She spat out the words *my design* like a shield, then bit her lip. "Stop deflecting, Lyra. Show us your invite. Right now."

"Ugh, why are you even talking to her?" the second rich girl sneered. "She's dodging because she's a crasher. No invite? Then get the hell out. Don't trash up the Blackwoods' place."

She practically spat the words. "Look at you. Wearing a knockoff dress, playing dress-up. You actually think you belong here? Honey, you can't mask that trailer-trash vibe. You think a pretty face and a loose skirt are going to get you a free pass into high society?"

"Hey, don't be so harsh," Genevieve chimed in, playing the nice girl. "Lyra probably just made a bad call. But... seriously, Lyra, Nicholas Blackwood is way out of your league. You're playing a dangerous game."

The two rich girls gasped, their faces twisting in fury. "Wait, what? She's trying to hook Nicholas?"

"Talk about delusional. She needs a serious reality check."

"Some high school dropout from the sticks thinks she can land Nicholas? You think you're on Vivienne's level? You're not even fit to clean her closet!"

"Why would a guy like Nicholas look at a nobody with zero family name? The next Mrs. Blackwood is going to be a real class act. Someone like Vivienne."

"They're childhood sweethearts. Literally made for each other. And you think you can just wiggle your hips and steal him away? Please. You're a joke."

The mere thought of this trailer-park girl using her body to climb onto the Blackwood throne sent the two rich girls over the edge.

They wouldn't even dare shoot their shot with a god like Julian Vance. Who the hell did Lyra think she was, trying to bag him?

Lyra watched their little temper tantrum, her eyes turning to ice. She stood up, unhurried.

The crimson silk of her dress pooled around her ankles, bold and loud.

She stood tall, shoulders back, looking down at them like they were dirt under her designer heels. The sheer vibe she threw off was pure power.

She swung her arm.

*Slap.*

*Slap.*

Two sharp cracks cut right through the fancy piano music, echoing across the entire room.
