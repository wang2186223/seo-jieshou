Chapter 527

Fast forward a few days. I’m strutting through the pack house, hiding a massive smirk under my mask. God, this place is tacky. White and cream everywhere. Seriously, who the hell picks that combo for a mating ceremony? I snort. Right on cue, Brenda—Blair’s little sidekick—bursts out of a room, sobbing her eyes out. "I fucking hate her!" she shrieks, sprinting past me.

"Well, she seems lovely," a low voice murmurs from behind the curtain next to me.

I lean against the wall by the window, keeping my voice down. "You shouldn't be here. Get lost."

"Can't help myself," he whispers, his voice like velvet. The window clicks open. "Shit, someone's coming. Love you." *Whoosh.* He's gone.

Security here is a joke.

"Ugh, do we seriously have to wear these stupid-ass masks?" Astrid’s whiny, nasal voice echoes from down the hall. I keep moving. She rounds the corner, flanked by some guy in a mask. He looks familiar.

"Shut up for five seconds, will you?" The guy rubs his temples, totally done. "We've been here an hour and my head is already splitting."

"Daddy, stop!" she whines, lip trembling.

I freeze. That build. That voice. That face. No way. My head is shaking before my brain even registers it. He stops right beside me. I hold my breath, chest tight. He turns. "Excuse me?"

"Yes, sir?" I mumble, dropping my pitch.

"Where's the actual ceremony? We're just walking in circles here." He flashes a smile and slides his mask up. Boom. Zero doubt left. It's him.

Astrid yanks her mask up, glaring. "Hello? Trash? He asked you a question." She snarls, lunging to rip my mask off. I duck, slipping out of her reach.

"My apologies. We're forbidden from speaking to high-ranking wolves or taking off our masks," I say, bowing low. "Strict orders." I take a step back, keeping my eyes down. "The ceremony is outside in the clearing. Reception's in the main hall after. Right now, the guys are prepping in the Alpha's office, and the ladies are down the hall to the right."

The guy stares at me, eyes narrowing. "Do I know you from somewhere?"

Panic spikes, but I bow again. "Doubtful, Alpha."

"What's your name?"

Shit. I scramble for a second. "Elara, Alpha."

"Roric," he says, smiling. My stomach does a violent flip. "Just call me Roric."

I force a tight, fake smile. "Anything else I can help you with, Alpha... Roric?"

He chuckles. "Nah. Get back to work." I nod, turning on my heel to walk away.

"Ugh, why are you being so nice to the help, Dad? They're literally trash," Astrid whines as I duck around the corner.

A low growl rumbles from his chest. "How many times do I have to tell you? Omegas run this place. They do the dirty work nobody else touches. Hell, they like it. It's in their low-class DNA. You treat 'em like shit, they run off, and then *you're* the one scrubbing toilets." His voice drops to a dark whisper. "Make the garbage feel special, and they'll die for you." He chuckles. "Every army needs meat shields."

Astrid giggles. "Got it."

Their footsteps fade. I'm frozen around the corner, shaking. Astrid is my... aunt? No fucking way. I had to have misheard that.

"What the hell are you staring at?" a sharp voice snaps.

I spin around. Brenda is glaring at me from an armchair in some fancy reading nook the Luna insisted on building.

"Just walking. Rolled my ankle in these damn things," I say, wiggling my foot. The heels are ridiculous—Blair's sick idea of a uniform for the staff.

Brenda softens for a split second, pressing an ice pack to her own ankle. "Tell me about it. Total nightmare." She sighs, adjusting the ice. "Hey, you seen the Luna?"

I shake my head. "Nope. Saw her heading down to the basement with some other Omega, though. You need her?"

Brenda groans, scanning the empty hallway. She shrugs, deciding I'm harmless. "Whatever. Who's gonna believe an Omega anyway?"

I limp a step closer, acting curious.

"She's being a total psycho. I'm losing my mind. She wants the Luna to walk her down the aisle, but the Luna shut her down." I tilt my head, shuffling closer. "So what does she do? Throws a massive fit and shoves me. Sprained my damn ankle. She knows my wolf is too weak to heal this shit before the ceremony starts!" she snarls.

"What do you want me to do about it?" I ask.

She locks eyes with me, cold and demanding. "Go find the Luna. Make her walk Blair down that fucking aisle." She laces her voice with a weak spark of pack command. "Get the Luna to do it, or go jump off a cliff."

This absolute bitch.
