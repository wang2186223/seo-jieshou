Chapter 199

Caspian’s jaw went tight. His gut twisted. Whatever was coming out of Lyra’s mouth next, it was gonna hurt. Bad.

Boom. Her voice blasted through the speakers, shaking the whole damn speedway.

"Hey, Caspian," Lyra purred into the mic. "Rules are rules, babe. You lost the Overdrive. That means your ride—the one worth just as much as my Ghost—belongs to me now."

Caspian stared, his eyes nearly popping out. The equal-value ride? She meant the Dark Serpent GT. But that beast was gone. It had sailed off the track, plummeted, and shattered into a million pieces along with the poor bastard riding it. Lyra literally watched it explode. Why the hell was she bringing up a ghost bike?

*Click, click, click.*

Zara strutted out of the crowd, her high heels snapping against the asphalt. She had this wicked, sexy smirk plastered on her face—hot, sharp, and freezing cold all at once.

"Oh, Mr. Cole," she cooed, tilting her head. "That Dark Serpent GT was my absolute favorite toy. And you? You just went and lost *my* baby to her?"

She took a slow breath, her eyes gleaming with pure mischief. "Look, that bike is worth a cool hundred mil on the street. But hey, I'm a nice girl. I won't squeeze you too hard." She paused, letting the silence hang. "So, uh, that fifty-million-dollar deposit you handed me? Yeah, I'm keeping that. Consider it a down payment. You've got exactly twenty-four hours to wire me the other fifty million."

She whipped out the paper he’d just signed, waving it like a fan. Then she tapped the rolled-up contract right against his chest, beaming. "Tick-tock, Mr. Cole. Don't be late. I charge a killer interest rate."

Caspian went pale. He took two shaky steps back, his brain short-circuiting. He’d literally begged, borrowed, and scraped every penny from his buddies just to get that first fifty million. Where the hell was he supposed to find another fifty million in one day?

"Miss Barnes, please..." He licked his dry lips, shoving a hand through his wrecked hair, trying to beg. But the words died in his throat.

Lyra’s voice cut him off, sharp as a knife. "Caspian, focus. You lost. You owe me a hundred-million-dollar bike. But since the Serpent is currently scrap metal... how exactly are you gonna pay me back, huh?"

Boom. Caspian felt like he’d just been struck by lightning. His heart stopped. He whipped his head toward Lyra, his eyes wild.

Wait. Was she serious? He had to pay *her* another hundred million too?! Zara already pocketed fifty million and wanted fifty more by tomorrow. And now Lyra wanted a cool hundred million on top of that?!

No way. No freaking way. Even the Coles—Vaelion City royalty—couldn't pull two hundred million in cash out of their asses overnight. That kind of money would completely bankrupt them. It would strip the family bone-dry. The math hit him, and his face turned completely white.

"Lyra, you bitch, stop it!" Genevieve shrieked, her head spinning. Fifty million? A hundred million? The numbers made her dizzy. How did a trailer-trash nobody like Lyra have the balls to demand that kind of cash? She gritted her teeth, choking back a sob as she screamed, "Caspian treated you like gold! You guys were literally engaged! How can you be this cold? Why do you have to ruin him like this?!"
