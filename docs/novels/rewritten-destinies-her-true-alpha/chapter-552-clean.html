Chapter 552

Magnus and Damon stared at Roric’s corpse. Pure panic was written all over their faces. Roric took one last, shaky breath, and his barrier popped like a bubble. Gone. I trotted over to Orion and gave his cheek a big, wet lick. He arched an eyebrow. I shrugged. "Hey, I'm stuck in wolf mode, and I wanted a kiss." He cracked up, throwing his head back. I nudged him. "Gimme your phone."

He pulled it out and handed it over. I just stared at him, raising my own brow. "What?" he asked. I flashed my giant, sharp claws. "Kind of hard to text with these, genius." He looked at the screen, then my paws, then my face. "Oh. Right. Uh... who are we calling?" "My dad. First."

Magnus opened his mouth to speak, but Ronnie beat him to it, shrieking, "What the actual fuck did you just say?" I glared down at the pale, pathetic guy on the dirt. "I said, I'm calling my dad." Magnus shivered, then tried to play it cool. "He's dead, Lyra."

"Yeah, you wish, Magnus." My dad's voice blasted through the speaker. He sounded totally wiped out. "My King... you good?" "I told him to call," I said, leaning closer to the mic. "How's your side looking?" Dad sighed. "Still in the damn caves. They went deep. We're hunting the runners. What about you guys?"

I glanced at the bodies on the ground. "Astrid and Roric are dead. Magnus, Damon, Blair, and Ronnie are pinned down. For now." "You gonna end them?" Dad asked. I looked up at Orion. He shrugged. "Your call, babe. Ru wants them dead, but he's just looking out for you." I turned to the prisoners and smirked. "Haven't decided yet." Blair let out a pathetic whimper. That made me grin even wider.

"You can't do that," Magnus barked. Bold move for a guy with a foot in the grave. I tilted my head, playing dumb. "Oh? And why's that?" "Because we're already neutralized. The Council accords clearly state—"

"Say fucking nothing!" Orion roared. I jumped. Didn't expect him to snap like that. "The Council is dead! Their rules are trash! They lost all their damn power the second they went behind my dad's back, teamed up with Roric, and tried to slaughter the Lycans, my family, and my mate!" He marched over, got right in Magnus's face, and glared into his eyes. "You've got no backup, no rights, nothing. You're done. Treason, murder, conspiracy. You signed your own death warrant the second you shook hands with Roric."

Magnus went white. "You don't have the authority." "I AM THE KING!" Orion bellowed. The ground practically shook. Everyone on the dirt shivered. A heavy silence fell. Then, my dad started chuckling through the phone. "'I am the king,'" Dad mocked, using a deep voice. "Ooh, so scary." Orion snorted, looking at the screen. "You're lucky you're my father-in-law now, Leo." "Oh, trust me, I know," Dad laughed. "How you holding up, pup?"

I paused, acting like I was thinking. "I don't know, Dad. Maybe... maybe they were under some kind of spell?" I heard a sharp gasp. "We were!" Blair shrieked, instantly bursting into tears. "He used magic on us! I'm so, so sorry, Lyra! You're my sister, I love you! The stuff with Damon and me? That was all Roric's sick idea! He wanted to break you!"

Damon stared at her, totally dazed. I casually licked my paw, acting like I was weighing my options, waiting for him to jump on the bandwagon. Right on cue, he piped up. "She's right. You're my mate, Lyra. Roric used me to hurt you. I've loved you forever. Blair was a huge mistake. I would never do that to you on my own." My dad snorted through the speaker. "Are you fucking kidding me right now?"

Magnus saw his opening and jumped in. "Caspian, come on, we used to be boys!" Wait, what? News to me. "We grew up together, man!" The idea of my dad and this snake being buddies made me want to puke. "You know I'd never do this. Your dad forced my hand!"

Ronnie was the only one keeping his mouth shut. I looked over and raised an eyebrow. "What about you, Ronnie?" He paused, thinking, then shook his head. I could tell he was actually going to be real for once. I glanced at the others. Blair had this tiny smirk on her face. She actually thought her little sob story worked. What a joke. Then Ronnie spoke up, looking straight at me. "I did it because I wanted your mother. Not just because she was gorgeous, but because I wanted her to give me a son. A son strong enough to murder Damon and take over this pack." Blair's smirk instantly vanished. Ronnie kept his eyes locked on mine. "I loved her, in my own twisted way. When she dumped me, I lost my mind. But I get it now. I deserved it. Just kill us."
