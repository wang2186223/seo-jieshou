Chapter 1057

Brutus stared at Lyra, his face going totally white. Holy shit. It hit him like a truck. This girl wasn't some random college kid. She was the ghost hacker the Feds and every tech billionaire on the planet were desperate to hire. One click from her, and his secret offshore accounts and encrypted chats would be wide open. The Committee would get the files, and he wouldn't just lose his cushy gig—he'd be wearing a prison jumpsuit. Ruining his life for a paycheck from the Doyles? Hell no. No way in hell was he messing with Lyra again.

"Hey! Chief Arbiter!" Willow shrieked. She dragged herself out of the trash, hair a total mess, looking like a drowned rat. "What are you doing? Call the cops! Get the Committee on the line and kick this bitch out!"

Brutus winced, pulling his head down. He looked at the wrecked room, then at Lyra’s stone-cold eyes. Yeah, his mind was made up. He wiped some blood off his cheek, spun around, and glared at Willow. "Back off! I don't need some kid telling me how to do my job!"

Willow froze. No freaking way. Their own paid guy was talking back to her? Did he hate money or something? Her chest heaved, ready to blow. She opened her mouth to scream.

But Brutus didn't let her. He whipped back to Lyra, plastering on a painful, kiss-ass smile that looked more like a twitch. "Hey, look, it's just a big misunderstanding! Total mix-up!"

Still flat on his ass, he grinned up at her like a beaten dog. "You're totally right, Lyra. 100%."

"This suite is for the top dogs. The Hope Initiative just crushed ten rounds straight. You guys are the best of the best. It only makes sense you stay here. Perfect spot to prep for the finals, right?"

"I just... man, I totally screwed up the room list earlier. My bad. Seriously, my bad."

*Smack!* He slapped his own face hard. He looked pathetic, completely stripped of his ego, just begging for mercy.

The crowd stood there, jaws on the floor. Dead silence. A minute ago, they were pissed off, thinking the Chief Arbiter was power-tripping on Hope. Nobody saw this coming. Brutus couldn't even touch them. Lyra was a straight-up boss. The sheer balls on this girl.

"Are you freaking crazy?!" Willow screamed, staring at Brutus. "She just beat the crap out of you, and you're kissing her boots?" Did Lyra knock his brains out?

"Shut up!" Brutus snapped back. "Lyra was just helping me swat a mosquito! Beat me up? Stop making up crap!"

Willow choked on her words. Vivienne stood there like a statue. The crowd was dead quiet. Nobody could believe how fast this guy folded.

"Whatever." Lyra was done playing. She pulled her foot back. "Since you finally get the picture, get the hell out."

"And clean this dump."

"Fix that door. Now."

Brutus nodded like a bobblehead, shaking all over. "Yes! Yes, absolutely! I'll get a crew on it right now!"
