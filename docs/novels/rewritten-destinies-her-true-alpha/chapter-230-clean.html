NovelVibe

Chapter 230

I bolted. Then, *yank*. Another pull. Every time I freaked out about getting lost, or heard some creepy howl behind me, that invisible leash snapped me back on track. I just kept charging straight toward it.

Everything started blurring out, exactly like it did with Seraphina. My vision went fuzzy at the edges. But that dead chick? She was right on my ass, desperate to beat me to my own damn body.

I was losing my mind. What if she got there first? What if I got stuck ghosting around this hellhole, rotting and crying, while she wore my skin like some cheap outfit?

A shiver hit my spine. I remembered how gross and drained she looked before she started sucking my life force. Like, how does anyone even exist like that?

Then it clicked—it wasn’t living at all. It was the total opposite. You’re just a parasite, waiting to hijack an empty shell like some freaky hermit crab. Except you don't just get a house; you steal a whole new face, a new body, a whole new life. But wait—do you get their wolves too?

My brain was spinning while my legs pumped. Would Umbra and Terra just bond with this random soul while I got dumped in the trash? Or would they get kicked out too, and her own wolf takes the wheel? If she hijacks me, is it just my life gone, or do all three of us die?

Panic choked me. I tripped and hit the dirt. That psycho laughter echoed right in my ear—she was way too close. I scrambled up, but *rip*—her nails gouged my arm, slicing skin. I took off like a bat out of hell.

"So close," she hissed. "Run, little puppy. I catch you, you're dead."

She wasn't bluffing. I felt the chill right in my gut. I dodged through streets and weird buildings. On the way out, I hadn't noticed a thing, but now? Everything felt like slow motion. I could actually see stuff passing by.

Some couple was making out on a bench. An old guy was shivering, walking his dog. Just regular humans doing regular stuff. I was just a ghost passing through, watching them while fighting like hell to stay alive.

I hoped all these living people would distract her. But nope. She was locked onto my back like a laser. I was her golden ticket, her only shot at a reboot, and she wasn't looking away.

"Just give up, kid," she cooed. "Look at these pathetic losers. Life is exhausting, right? I know you're tired. You're miserable. Just let go. Give it to me."

Her voice was pure poison, but damn, she wasn't wrong. I was exhausted. With all the crap I'd been through, I was done. I actually thought about quitting, and my legs started to give out.

Then, voices. Daisy and Juniper.

"Come on, Lyra. Get back here," Daisy pleaded. I felt her hand grab mine.

"You have to fight. Don't you dare quit. Think about your parents. Think about us." Juniper grabbed my other hand, squeezing her fingers tight between mine.

I stared at my empty hands. Somehow, I knew they were holding my real hands back in the apartment. Their energy, their desperation, their souls—they reached right through the void when I was at rock bottom.

I kicked it into overdrive, running till my lungs burned. I was getting my body back. My life. My future.

Behind me, the dead chick shrieked. "No!" she howled, snapping at my heels. "That life is mine!" Her claws ripped down my back. I screamed bloody murder, but I didn't stop.

"Like hell it is!" I yelled back.

My chest pulsed again, and I veered. There it was—my apartment building. I almost cried tears of joy. I ran for the entrance, but screw the elevator—I jumped. I slammed my claws into the brick wall, scrambling up the side of the building. Behind me, the woman was panting, totally losing it.

"Please!" she begged. "It's my turn!"

"You lost your own body!" I screamed. "You're not taking mine!"

"No!" she shrieked as I vaulted over my balcony railing.

I phased right through the wall. There they were—Juniper and Daisy, clutching my hands, staring at my pale, lifeless face.

"Give me that body!" the ghost screamed, bursting through the wall.

I dove. It was now or never. I heard her scream, felt her hair brush my arms as she flew right past me.
