Chapter 645

Lyra pushed ahead. A wall of people already blocked the sidewalk, whispering and pointing.

"Hey, let me through," Lyra said, her accent pure, born-and-bred American.

Heads snapped around. A few guys opened their mouths to snap back, but then they caught her stare. Cold. Sharp. Dead serious. The trash-talk died instantly, and the crowd split like the Red Sea.

She marched right up to the old Asian lady collapsed on the concrete.

"Whoa, back off!" a mall cop yelled, lunging into her path. "Step back, kid! 911 is on the way. Nobody touches her till the EMTs get here. You mess with her, you're liable!"

"I'm a doctor," Lyra shot back, her English sharp as a whip. "So shut your mouth and move." Waiting for a local ambulance was a death sentence; those guys took forever.

She yanked off her baseball cap. Long hair tumbled down, framing a face so stunning it stopped traffic. The bystanders stared, totally thrown by this gorgeous Asian girl's sheer nerve.

"What's her deal?" someone muttered. "No way she's a doctor. She looks like a teenager!" Nobody bought it. She was way too young.

Lyra ignored the chatter. She slid her canvas tote off her shoulder, reached in, and pulled out a small gray wrap. One quick flick of her wrist, and it unrolled. Inside was a row of long, wicked-looking silver needles.

The crowd went wild. "Oh my God! What are those?" a woman shrieked. "Is she doing voodoo?" "She's gonna stab her!" "Yo, get your phone out! Film this!"

The guard panicked. He lunged to grab her arm. "Stop! You're crazy! That's murder!" He figured a skinny girl like her would be easy to toss aside.

Big mistake. The second his hand touched her jacket, Lyra didn't even blink. Her fingers snatched a needle, and with a lightning-fast backhand flick—*thwack*. The silver tip buried itself deep in his wrist.

A white-hot shock of pain blasted up his arm. The guard gasped, his knees buckling as he hit the pavement, totally paralyzed.

"Move," Lyra said. Her voice was flat, freezing, and completely empty of mercy.

The crowd froze. Nobody dared to breathe. Lyra didn't give them a second glance; she was already pulling more needles from the gray cloth.

With her left hand, she ripped open the old lady's collar. Her right hand became a blur of silver light. *Zip, zip, zip.* The needles plunged straight into the skin right over the woman's heart.

To the idiots watching, it looked like a frantic stabbing spree. They thought she was butchering the poor lady. But any real acupuncturist would have dropped to their knees in awe. Every single strike hit a life-saving pressure point with millimeter precision.

"She's nuts! She just stabbed her chest!" "Jesus, she's killing her!" The crowd was convinced the old lady was a goner. How could some cheap metal pins save a dying woman? If traditional Asian medicine actually worked, why did they even bother with modern hospitals?

Then, exactly ten seconds later. *Cough. Hack.*

The old lady's chest heaved. The suffocating blue tint on her face vanished as she sucked in a massive, greedy lungful of air. Her skin flushed back to a healthy, warm pink right before their eyes.

"Holy crap! It's magic! Like, real-deal Eastern magic!" "Dude, she's breathing! She's actually alive!" "That's insane! What the hell are those needles? How did she just pull her back from the dead?"
