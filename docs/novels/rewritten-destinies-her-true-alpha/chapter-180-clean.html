Chapter 180

I glanced at Juniper, then marched straight up to Orion. "Hey. You good, boss?"

Juniper gave a quick nod. "Gonna go nose around your new digs." She threw us a half-assed bow and bolted from the hallway.

"Flint?" I checked my wrist. "No clue when the crew's getting here. Go play lookout for me, will ya?"

"On it, Alpha Lyra." Flint bowed, spun on his heel, and vanished.

Orion watched Juniper's back, then locked eyes with me. "You spilling the beans to her?"

I nodded. "Yeah. Got to."

Orion blew out a heavy breath. "Fine. Who else? I need to make sure we aren't giving false hope to people who already lost their folks."

Shit, I hadn't even thought of that. "Uh, Luna, Daisy, and Fawn."

"Who the hell are they?" Orion's brows knitted together.

I opened my mouth, then realized I didn't have a clue what packs they belonged to. "Yo, Juniper!" I yelled.

Orion snorted. "You don't even know their packs?"

"Look, I ran into Luna in the bathroom. She's that tiny, unranked chick from overseas. Daisy is the secretary's kid. Fawn is some small redhead from the pack next to Raven's. And my dad took in Juniper's pack." I paused. Felt like I was missing someone.

"Wait, Briar is that unranked gamma girl, but she gives me bad vibes." Juniper popped her head back out, and I pointed.

Right, Briar. I forgot her. But before I could call her over, a sudden wave of dread hit my gut. "Actually, scratch that. Keep her out of this."

"Briar?" Orion frowned. "You sure that's her name?"

I nodded. "Dark hair, blue eyes, total knockout."

Orion's face went dead cold. "Do not talk to her."

"Why?" I wasn't planning to anyway, but his sudden dark look made me curious.

"She's a gamma in my pack," he said, his voice tight. "Her folks worked for my old man, and I kept them on. Briar ended up as second gamma. Since her parents lived in the pack house, she grew up around me. Thought we were... close." Orion rubbed his face, looking exhausted. "Damn it. I thought she got over this creepy obsession."

"Wait, what?" I let out a dry laugh. "What's that got to do with me?"

"She probably heard rumors from Raven. Figured you and I are a thing." He grimaced. "And moving you in here definitely didn't help."

"She knows I'm living here?"

Orion shook his head. "Not yet. But secrets don't stay secret for long around here." He sighed. "Just stay the hell away from her. And seriously, don't tell her a damn thing."

"Deal. I'll order everyone to shut their traps," I promised.

"Thanks." He slumped against the wall as Juniper disappeared again. "I'm dead tired, but I had to make sure you were okay."

"We're good here."

He chuckled. "Your crap is literally piled up outside the door, and you're 'good'?"

I shrugged. "Hey, it's just us up here. And I trust us." I grinned, watching him process that.

"Thanks for the trust."

"Duh. You're my king."

Orion shot me a look. "Cut it out. You know that's not what I meant."

"I know, and I didn't mean it either. Just gotta mess with you." I smiled and stepped closer. "Seriously, how are you holding up?"

"I'm fine," he shot back instantly.

I focused, throwing up a silent barrier around us. *Snap.* "Just us now." I grabbed his hand. "Now tell me the truth. How are you?"

Orion stared down at our joined hands, then looked up. His tough-guy act crumbled. His shoulders dropped, his face fell, and his eyes went totally hollow. "Awful."

"Talk to me." I stepped in, wrapping my arm through his and leaning my head on his shoulder. It was pure instinct—the same way I used to lean on my dad or Finnian. Orion took a sharp, shaky breath and let it out slow.

"I screwed up. They're dead because I couldn't figure this shit out in time. And today, I had to look those people in the eye and tell them their families are gone forever." He swallowed hard. "I failed them. And now I have to walk around acting like everything is totally fine."

I squeezed his arm tighter. "In our world? It is fine." I looked up at him. For a second, I saw tears welling up, but he blinked them away instantly. "You didn't fail anyone. You got handed a giant, tangled mess when you were just a kid, and you've been trying to untangle it ever since. Today sucked, yeah. But nobody can say you don't get their pain." He stared at me. "This whole nightmare started with you losing your family and your entire pack. Nobody has lost more than you. You didn't fail. And I'm not gonna let you start now."
