Chapter 614

Boom, boom, boom. Someone was hammering on the door. I groaned, rolling over in the pitch black. Damn it. Three hours of sleep. Max.

"What the hell is that?" Orion rasped, his voice pure gravel.

I let out a dry laugh and hauled my ass out of bed. Thank god I had the brains to snatch my robe before shuffling toward the noise. Orion made a move to get up, but I whipped around and pointed a hard finger at him. "Freeze. Back in bed. Nobody gets a free show of you naked but me."

He chuckled, flopping back onto the mattress and dragging the sheet over his lap. "Babe, we're wolves. Naked is basically our uniform."

I snorted. "Nice try, big guy. You shift and shred your clothes anyway. No one sees you naked. Got it?"

Orion flashed a smirk and gave a lazy nod. I turned back to the door just as another knock rattled the wood. I yanked it wide open.

"Dad? What's wrong?" I stared at him. "Why are you up at this ungodly hour? Thought you'd be busy keeping Mom occupied."

The old man actually blushed, shaking his head. "Well, yeah, I was," he muttered, rubbing his neck. "But we've got a situation. Figured you and Orion would want in on this."

Orion's ears practically perked up. "What's the damage?"

Rustling sheets told me he was moving again. I spun around, letting out a warning growl and pointing right back at the mattress.

Orion rolled his eyes. "Seriously? He's a guy! And he's your dad!" He sounded half-amused, half-annoyed.

"Hey, you should've thought about that before you flashed that Omega. Now you're dealing with two insanely possessive wolves. Congratulations." I smirked, then glanced back at my dad. He was grinning. I pointed a finger at his face. "Wipe that smirk off."

He raised his hands in surrender. "Alright, alright. What's the emergency?"

Dad snorted, but his face went serious fast. "Caspian wants to talk."

My brows knitted together. "Talk about what?"

Dad shrugged. "No clue. But I knew you'd want to be in the room."

I nodded. "Damn right we do. Give us a minute to throw some clothes on, and we'll walk down with you. I also need a second to grab my gear."

"What gear?" Dad grabbed the edge of the door as I tried to swing it shut. "What are you grabbing?"

"For the spells." I looked him dead in the eye, watching the hurt flash across his face. "Look, I know you hate this. But Celestia put this on me. I'm going to use every single weapon in my arsenal to keep us alive."

His hand slipped off the doorframe. "But... magic? That's dark stuff. We shouldn't touch it."

I gave him a soft smile, amused by his black-and-white view of the world. "Darkness is a perspective, Dad." I took his hand. "If someone betrays the pack, you'd torture them and put them down, right?"

He nodded instantly. "Duh. They turn on us, the Alpha protects the family. That's the rule. You know how this works."

"I do. I did it for you once, and I'd do it again in a heartbeat to save our people." I squeezed his hand. "This is no different. Just a bigger playing field. I'm the Queen Luna. I will sacrifice anything, do whatever it takes, to keep our kind safe. Even if I have to burn my own soul—or die trying."

Dad just stared at me. "Man... I still see you as my little girl running around in pigtails, chasing Obsidian." He blinked hard, and holy crap, a tear was actually welling up in his eye. "When did you turn into this badass woman?" He took a shaky breath, clearing his throat to hide the emotion. "You don't need your old man anymore. Honestly, it kind of messes with my head."

I laughed, giving him a playful poke. "I grew up because survival demanded it. But hey, I'm like this because you and Mom raised me right. So you're dead wrong." He looked up, meeting my eyes. "I'm always going to need my dad." I yanked him down, planted a quick kiss on his cheek, and shoved him toward the hallway. "Give us sixty seconds. We'll be right out."

He nodded. "Just mindlink an Omega to fetch your gear. They can bring it downstairs."

I clicked the door shut. Orion finally rolled out of bed and strolled toward the closet. I had to bite my lip to keep from groaning out loud.

*Pfft,* Umbra snorted in my head. *Yeah, our mate is a ten out of ten, but stop drooling over his ass. We have a job to do.*

*Hey, don't look at me,* Terra chimed in with a huff. *I can't blame her. I stare at Shadow all the time.*

I snorted, slipping into the closet after him. He was already buckling his belt. "What's the joke?" he asked.

"Nothing!" I blurted, way too fast. My face instantly burned hot.

"You were checking out my ass, weren't you?" His voice was dripping with smug amusement.

"Was not." I grabbed a pile of clothes and started throwing them on.

"Liar," he teased, pulling his shirt over his head.

"Bold of you to assume I'm lying," I shot back, tugging my own top down.

He laughed, heading for the door. "Bold of you to assume your wolves didn't already sell you out."

The door clicked shut behind him.

*Son of a bitch.*

*Traitors, both of you,* I hissed at my inner wolves. They just cackled in response.
