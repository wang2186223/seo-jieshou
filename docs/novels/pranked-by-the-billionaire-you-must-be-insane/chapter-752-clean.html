Chapter 760

Tears welled up in his eyes instantly. His hand shook, barely managing to pinch her fingers. "Oh, my sweet girl... first Eleanor, now me. You've been running on fumes, haven't you?"

Forget the Vinton empire. Forget his own failing body. The second his eyes popped open, his brain registered only one thing: Lyra, his absolute favorite person in the world.

"You absolute idiot," Eleanor choked out, swiping at her wet cheeks. "About damn time you woke up. Lyra didn't sleep a wink. She stood right there, staring at your monitors all night. She only sat down when the machines stopped beeping like crazy."

Arthur sniffled, his eyes turning bloodshot. He gave her hand a feeble pat. "I know, baby. My Lyra literally dragged me back from the edge of the grave. You wore yourself out for this old man."

"Hey, it's fine," Lyra said, cracking a soft smile. "You're breathing. That's all I care about."

Silas scrambled over with a warm glass. "Dad! Oh thank god. Here, drink some water."

Arthur gave his son a flat look, then immediately turned back to Lyra, his face wrinkling into a massive grin. "Actually, Lyra, you're the one who's been busting your butt. You take a sip first."

He whipped his head toward Silas, glaring daggers. "Are you blind or just stupid? Give the glass to your niece! Seriously, how are you running my company with zero common sense?"

Silas froze, totally speechless. Feeling totally shut down, he sheepishly handed the glass to Lyra.

Lyra chuckled, took the cup, and pressed it right against Arthur's mouth. "I'm good. Drink up, old man. Your throat is dry."

Arthur gulped it down like a good boy. He swallowed a few mouthfuls, then struggled to sit up. Lyra immediately slid behind him, fluffing the pillows to prop him up.

Once he was comfortable, Arthur locked eyes with his son. "Silas. Get the legal team on the phone. Now."

Silas blinked, totally thrown off. Then, the lightbulb went off. "Wait, Dad... are you doing what I think you're doing?"

"Damn right," Arthur barked, his voice suddenly firm as steel. "I'm signing over every single share of Vinton Group to Lyra. She kept me alive. It's all hers now."

We're talking about the Vinton stock—the crown jewels of the family empire. As the majority owner, giving that to Lyra meant she'd instantly become the big boss. She'd hold more cards than Silas, the actual CEO.

But Silas didn't even blink. He just nodded. "Got it. I'll make the call."

"Yeah, no thanks," Lyra chimed in, putting the glass on the nightstand and pulling the duvet up to his chin. "I've got plenty of cash. I don't need stocks. It's too much of a headache."

Seriously, owning that much stock meant dealing with corporate BS. She was already swamped, hated business, and despised boring board meetings.

"No way, you're taking them!" Arthur panicked. "Lyra, it's a gift! You have to! Just pocket the cash and let Silas deal with the boring corporate crap. You won't have to lift a finger!"

Lyra started to argue, but Arthur cut her off with a glare. "Listen to me. If you say no, it means you hate me. And if you hate me, why am I even alive? I swear, if you reject this, I'm stopping all my meds. No pills, no IVs, nothing!"

Lyra just stared at him, totally defeated.

Eleanor nudged her. "Just take the damn shares, Lyra. You know how he is. He'll literally starve himself to get his way."

Lyra looked at the old man, who was currently pouting like a toddler. She let out a heavy sigh. "Fine. You win."

Arthur's pout vanished, replaced by a massive, shit-eating grin. "Hell yeah! You're the big boss of Vinton now! If anyone messes with you, just throw a couple billion at their face!"

Lyra rolled her eyes playfully. "Sure thing. Thanks, Grandpa."
