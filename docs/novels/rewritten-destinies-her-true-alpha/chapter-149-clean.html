Chapter 149

Class ended. I bolted straight for the woods. My brain was fried, and my blood was boiling. For weeks, I’d been in this trash mood, and I couldn't even pinpoint why.

*Oh, cut the crap. You know exactly why,* Umbra snickered in my head.

"Shut up, no I don't!" I snapped, hitting the dirt trail at a dead sprint. I just needed the wind in my face and my claws digging into the dirt.

Terra practically sprained her eyeballs. *Yeah, right. Keep lying to yourself, babe.*

I skidded to a halt, throwing my hands in the air. "I said, I don't!" I yelled.

A bunch of random students stopped and stared. Great.

"Sorry! My bad!" I muttered, waving them off. I bolted deeper into the trees and let Umbra take the wheel. Bones cracked. Fur sprouted.

We flew. We dodged trunks, cleared rotting logs, and slid under thorny brush.

*You're pissed,* Umbra muttered as we ran. *You're just too proud to say it out loud.*

*Spit it out already, Umbra.*

*Orion,* Terra chimed in, sitting back on her mental haunches. *Zero texts. Zero calls. Total radio silence since that last phone call.* She gave me a look. *Some random substitute is teaching his class, he ghosted you, and your stupid pride won't let you call him first.*

"Well, he promised he'd call!" I growled back, my throat vibrating.

Umbra snorted. *Yeah, and now you're a ticking time bomb. Honestly, I don't blame you. We're completely in the dark, and Elara's got a gag order from the higher-ups.*

"God, I hate Alphas," I muttered. My wolves snorted in agreement. "They always think they know what's best for everyone. But I'm supposed to—"

*Supposed to be training to save our pack,* Umbra cut in, her tone sharp. *We need to lock in on that, not obsess over some guy who probably hasn't thought about us once.*

"Pot, meet kettle. You sound pretty damn cranky yourself," I scoffed.

Her mental fur bristled. *Whatever. Men are trash. We're on the same page there.* She put on a burst of speed. *I just... I thought...*

*Thought what?* I pressed.

*That our dad and the king actually had some damn decency,* she muttered.

Suddenly, a sharp, musky scent hit our nose. Deer. We pivoted instantly, tearing down a side trail. The buck spotted us and bolted. Adrenaline surged. We closed the gap, coiled our muscles, and launched into the air.

*Bam!* Something slammed into our ribs mid-air. We hit the dirt, rolling hard. The deer vanished.

Now, we were absolutely livid. A low, murderous growl ripped from our throat as we scrambled up and spun around. A scrawny brown wolf was pacing nearby. Then, more shadows moved. A few more wolves stepped out of the brush.

We circled them, waiting for someone to say something. They clearly had an issue. When they just stood there staring, I bared my fangs. "Speak up! What the fuck do you want?"

"This is school property," a voice barked. "No outsiders allowed in these woods. Students and staff only."

I went rigid. "Are you shitting me? Who the hell do you think you're talking to?" The brown wolf flinched but didn't back down. I shoved Umbra back to take the reins myself.

"Beat it," a cocky voice ordered. A reddish she-wolf strutted out of the shadows. "Like we said. Students and teachers only. You don't belong here."

"Watch your mouth, pup," I snarled. Umbra stepped back, leaving me fully in control of our body. "You just ruined my dinner. I'd back off right now if you want to keep your skin." I snapped my jaws, pinning my ears flat.

"Try us. We'll tear you apart," she threatened.

Then the wind shifted. That cheap, familiar perfume hit my nose. *Raven.* Ugh, kill me now.

I slammed open a mind-link to Alpha Magnus. *Get Raven and her little bitch-squad out of my face right now, or I'm painting these trees with their blood.*

*Whoa, Lyra, what the hell is going on?* his voice shot back.

Time to play dirty. *It's Umbra,* I lied, deepening my mental voice. *I'm running the show right now. Raven and her little clique have me cornered. They're playing dumb, pretending they don't know my scent so they have an excuse to fight. They don't realize I've been trained to kill since I could walk. If they touch me, they die.* I let out a savage, throat-ripping growl through the link to sell the act.

*On my way,* Magnus snapped. A second later, his voice boomed across the pack's open channel: *Everyone, back the hell off! If you want to live, stand down. That black wolf is a student, and she will literally rip your throat out.*

The girls hesitated, exchanging nervous glances. Raven just glared, her eyes burning. I paced back and forth, putting on my best monster act.

"Are you actually brain-dead?" I roared, channeling Umbra's deepest growl. "You think my human and I wouldn't smell your garbage scent from a mile away? Raven, your wolf is just as pathetic and stupid as you are." I lunged forward, looming over her like a nightmare.
