Chapter 742

Talk about a slap in the face. Sterling and his crew stood there, cheeks burning hot. Just seconds ago, they’d been laughing their asses off at Lyra.

Now? The literal gods of medicine—guys Sterling practically prayed to—were swarming the Elyndor girl he’d just called trash. One legend was passing her a scalpel. Another held her gauze. They looked like happy puppies just to get a nod from her.

"How... what the..." Sterling choked on his own spit. He finally found his voice, raspy and weak. "Who the hell is she? Not even the URS royal family could get this lineup in one room. And they're... they're fetching her tools?"

Silas shoved his piece back into his holster and snorted. "Get it now? That’s why she’s running the show."

Sterling’s team kept their mouths shut. Not a single one of them dared to talk back.

"You guys are dead weight, so zip it," Julian Vance chimed in. He flicked his cuffs, wearing a cold, pretty-boy smirk. "Seriously, just stand there and look useless."

The burn on Sterling’s face got ten times hotter.

But Finnian? His brain was totally fried. These guys were medical superstars. He’d only seen them on TV or the front page of journals. Now they were right here, and he was about to wash up and operate alongside them. Holy crap. This was the absolute peak of his existence.

He’d been sweating bullets earlier, terrified Lyra’s backup wouldn't show. What an idiot. This was Lyra they were talking about. He wanted to laugh with sheer relief. He’d risked his whole career on a wild gamble, and damn, it paid off. He just scored a front-row ticket to the biggest medical flex in history.

Once he stepped out of this OR, his career was going straight to the moon. He’d be bragging about this over drinks for the next fifty years.

"Shut it."

Lyra didn't even look up as she slid sterile needles into Reginald’s main nerve points. Then she raised her eyes, freezing the room with a look. Just that one word, and the bickering medical gods instantly went dead silent, standing straight like kids in a principal's office.

"Langford, needles. Smith, first assist. Vance, you’re on..." Lyra barked out orders like a drill sergeant. She locked eyes with them. "Got it?"

"Yes, ma'am!" the world-class surgeons barked back in unison.

"Then let's move," Lyra said.

Penelope didn't know jack about medical journals, but seeing Sterling’s hotshot team shaking in their boots told her everything. These newcomers were big league. Untouchable. Yet here they were, treating Lyra like some kind of queen. Calling her a "divine healer"? Wait... was Lyra actually the real deal?

Panic hit Penelope like a truck. The color rushed out of her face. She’d figured Lyra was just playing doctor, trying to look cool, and that she’d inevitably screw up and make Grandpa worse. Then Sterling’s crew and that famous Dr. Smith—the guy her dad supposedly booked—would swoop in, play the heroes, and fix everything.

That was the plan. Pin the whole mess on Lyra. Make her the scapegoat. Lyra would be the idiot who almost killed Grandpa, and the Vintons would kick that country trash out of the family forever. Hell, maybe even throw her in jail.

But this? With all these geniuses in the room? If they actually saved him, would they... oh god, would they figure out what actually caused Grandpa's sudden crash? They were too damn smart. They’d definitely find out.
