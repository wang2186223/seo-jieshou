Everyone knew that human energy and time were finite. Even a prodigy who devoted four years entirely to study couldn’t have mastered so many fields at once—let alone someone serving time in prison.

Soon, Raegan’s associates joined in, amplifying the storm. Guests began debating among themselves, and online comments flooded in almost instantly.

“So Maia started Annie Crystal while locked up?”

“Is Wront Prison some kind of luxury academy?”

“This story doesn’t add up—maybe she was never actually in jail.”

“I always thought something about it didn’t add up. Finally, someone’s said it out loud.”

Under the harsh glare of the spotlight, Maia stood motionless, her expression unreadable. She finally looked back at Rosanna and spoke evenly. “There’s nothing unbelievable about it. I simply worked harder than everyone else—while they rested, I kept learning.”

Her calm declaration hung in the air—until a man from the crowd suddenly leapt to his feet, fury etched across his face. “You’re lying!” he shouted, pointing straight at her.

The man wearing a suit of deep brown and spectacles with golden rims curled his lip in disdain.

“Two years back, Annie Crystal sprang into existence out of nowhere,” he declared. “And during that period, you were locked away behind bars.”

What he said sparked commotion throughout those gathered.

Right then, Raegan issued commands to every member of The Mask who had infiltrated the event as guests.

Without delay, skeptical remarks erupted once more.

“That’s right! None of it adds up. Running a business from a jail cell? I’ve never come across such a thing.”

“Does Maia possess superhuman abilities or something? Managing studies, operating a corporation, and doing prison time all at once? What inmate could pull that off?”
