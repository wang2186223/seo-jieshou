Chapter 435

I locked eyes with her. Nodded. Out the door.

I went full Terra for the first leg. Just in case. Didn't need some creep in the woods tracking my scent back to this spot. Terra hauled ass past the halfway mark, then we dove under a bush. Dead silence. We waited.

Nothing. No wolf vibes, no magic on my radar. I swapped to Umbra. Girl needed to stretch her legs anyway.

*Man, when we first bonded, I just wanted a seat at the table,* Terra chimed in, her voice hitting me right in the feels. *Just wanted to be heard. But this? Shifting, running, even for a sec? It’s freaking magic.*

*Terra, babe, I'm so sorry we missed out on this back then.*

She nuzzled me. *Hey, no guilt trips. Even Umbra was locked down last time. I’m just saying... thanks. For bringing me and Umbra into the light. We got lucky with you.*

*Love you too.* I hugged her back, then blinked. Holy crap. Umbra was already at the apartment. "Damn, girl. You flyin'?"

She snorted. *While you two were busy crying it out, I was putting in work. I want those damn letters.* She bolted straight to the glass slider.

I spotted Flint at the desk. Boom—shifted back to human. Stepped inside. Flint nearly jumped out of his skin.

"Lyra!" He lunged, throwing his arms around me. "Damn, how've you been?"

I pulled back, grinning. "Living the dream. How’s the fam?"

He whipped out his phone, showing off pics of his girl and their kid. "Can't complain, honestly." He shoved the phone back. "So, what brings you to my neck of the woods?"

I stared him down, smirk ready. "You got something that belongs to me."

Flint froze. Eyes went wide. "Uh, no I don't." He looked away. I let out a dry laugh.

"Relax." I slapped his shoulder. "Tanka Flint."

The guy practically deflated. Like walking into a warm house after a killer shift. Just pure, heavy relief. He looked at me, eyes swimming with emotions. "I swear, I didn't want to keep you in the dark. Honestly, I—"

"Save it. We're good." I rubbed his shoulder. "I heard the whole thing. I know it wasn't you or Finnian. No hard feelings, seriously." I nudged him back toward his desk. "Stop beating yourself up. Let me know when you and your girl are free, and I'll swing by."

I waited for him to catch his breath. "Now that we're cool, can I ask some questions?"

Flint nodded, but his eyes darted to the security camera. Got it. "Off the record, obviously."

"How long?"

He cleared his throat. "I've been waiting for you since freshman year."

Oof. Right in the gut. That long? I nodded. "Damn. Okay." I sighed. "Look, I ran all the way here. Since the coast is clear, can you give me a lift back?"

Flint’s face lit up. He grabbed the phone. "Yeah, it's me. I got Alpha Lyra here. She needs a ride to Oakenfang territory. Yeah, I'll drive her. She's picking up her package." He glanced at me, mouth twitching. "Wasn't my plan, but copy that. Thanks." He slammed the receiver down and stood up. "Alright, I'll grab the wheels and your package. Meet me out front in two minutes flat." He grinned, booking it to the back office.

I laughed, looking around. Weirdly, it felt good to be back. This place used to be home. Life was so simple back in school. Wake up, spar, class, eat, train. Repeat. No drama. No bullshit. No agonizing over who I was supposed to end up with. Cipher and Orion popped into my head.

Then, a snarl. I smelled her a second too late.

The glass door slid open. "What the hell are you doing here?"

Still a bitch. I smirked, turning around to face Raven.
