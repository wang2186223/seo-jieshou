Chapter 652

The deal hit me like a physical punch. It started in my arm, shot through my chest, and settled like lead in my gut. Pluto let go of my hand with a single, sharp nod. "Clock's ticking. Say your goodbyes." He glanced at Freya. "You've got two minutes. Make 'em count."

Freya stood up, a small smile on her face. She walked straight over to my parents, whispered something low, and just like that, they both broke down sobbing. I forced my shaking legs to hold me up and glared at Pluto. "Since when do you play this nice? What's the catch?"

He smirked. "You're not wrong, kid. Normally, I don't do trade-offs. But hey, everyone's got a soft spot. Mine happens to be family." He walked up, slapped my back hard enough to make me stumble. "You'll get it when you're older. But not today." He gave me a quick nudge, turned on his heel, and headed toward Rhea and the rest of the divine crew.

Morrigan flicked her wrist, and boom—that familiar soundproof bubble snapped shut around them. I felt a nasty prickle at the back of my neck. They were all staring right at me.

"Well, no prizes for guessing who they're gossiping about." Juniper slid her arm around my shoulders. I let out a rough laugh and slumped against her. "I won't even ask how you're holding up. I can literally feel it."

I looked up, and then another arm wrapped around me from the other side. "Our wolves are sisters, Lyra," Daisy said, her voice cracking, though she was trying like hell to play it cool. "We're feeling every bit of your agony through the bond."

Juniper nodded. "Seriously, my phone is blowing up. Luna and Fawn are spamming me with a million texts, screaming for updates."

Daisy pulled out her phone, flashing a screen packed with missed alerts. "Yeah, neither of us wanted to text back. Not until we knew how this whole shitshow was gonna end."

Juniper rested her head against mine. "Look, once Orion's back and you've had a second to breathe... we know you're gonna absolutely lose it over Elara. When that crash happens, we'll make sure everyone's here to catch you."

Elara.

God, I'd completely blanked on her the second Orion went down. Guilt washed over me like ice water. I looked over at Kaelen. He was still on his knees, his face a mask of pure, raw horror. It broke my heart. The guy had just lost his kid and his mate. I knew that exact brand of hell. I gave my friends a quick squeeze and walked over to him. "Hey."

He slowly looked up, but his eyes were totally vacant. Nobody was home. He was completely gone, and I had no clue if he'd ever make it back.

"I've got him," Uncle Finnian's voice cut in from behind. I stepped aside to give him room. "I'll take him over to Elara Meadow and the twins." I frowned, but his mouth twitched into a sad smile. "He's been helping her out ever since she showed up with the babies. Elara... well, Morgana was jealous as hell, but he kept showing up anyway." He shrugged. "Besides, she probably gets what he's going through better than anyone." I gave him a tight nod.

"Time's up!" Pluto barked.

Freya walked back over and wrapped me in a tight hug. "Don't you dare second-guess my choice," she whispered right in my ear. "This is on me. I chose this." I nodded against her shoulder, tears blurring my vision. "Thanks for being my friend, Lyra."

The waterworks started all over again. "Thank you for dying for my family." I pulled back, and she flashed me this brilliant, blinding smile. It took me straight back to our first day of training. She'd always been this unstoppable force of nature, so bright and full of life. The last few years had broken her, I knew that. But right now? She looked whole again. She was totally at peace with this. "I hope your afterlife is easy, Freya. No more bullshit."

Pluto dropped a heavy hand on her shoulder. "For what she's doing today, she gets a VIP pass to Elysium. No struggles." She gave my hand one last squeeze, then turned around. "Ready?"

She nodded. "Let's do this."

They walked back to Orion, and I dragged myself after them. Every step made my stomach churn. Hope and sheer terror were tearing me apart inside as I dropped to my knees beside my cold-as-ice mate. His skin looked totally wrong—this creepy, unnatural pale color. I locked my fingers through his.

Phoebe walked up, planted a quick kiss on my forehead, and took her spot at Orion's feet. Pluto gave me a quick wink as he stood by Orion's head. "Get down next to him and hold his hand," Pluto ordered. Freya dropped down right across from me. One by one, the other gods circled up around the three of us. Pluto held his hand out to Aether. Aether just raised an eyebrow. Pluto laughed. "We need a chain, Ura. Grab hold or hit the road." Aether stared at me for a beat, then reached out and grabbed Pluto's hand.

"I'm only doing this for you, kiddo," Aether muttered. I managed a small smile and nodded.

"Thank you." I looked around the circle—Pluto, Aether, Morrigan, Phoebe, Rhea, and finally Neptune. "Seriously. Thank you, all of you."

Pluto snorted. "Rhys, I'm calling you. Get over here."

Phoebe looked up at the sky. "Shadow, I call you. Come to me."

Morrigan closed her eyes and muttered some ancient words: "Ich ta hel for shim. Epar tin clavic shorn." I knew that spell—it was a heavy-duty healing chant. Instantly, the blood pooling around Orion started flowing backward, sinking back into his body. I watched his blood-soaked shirt turn clean again. The massive gash on his chest sealed up tight as the magic did its thing.

I leaned right over his face, holding my breath. Praying.

"Please. Come back to me."

Pluto shifted his weight. "Freya, last chance to back out."

Freya didn't hesitate. She kissed my cheek, then leaned down and kissed Orion's. "Take me. Trade my life for their peace... for their love. Take our souls to pay for my king." I wanted to turn and thank her one last time, but my eyes were locked onto Orion's face. I couldn't look away.

A life for a life.

Pluto roared out again. "Rhys, come to me!"

Freya's body suddenly went limp and hit the dirt next to us. I stopped breathing. In my chest, I felt the bond snap back together, like raw threads fusing. A soul for a soul.

Then, boom. A massive bolt of lightning slammed straight into me. I screamed bloody murder. No, no, no, not like this. The raw power was literally cooking my insides. I slammed my lips onto Orion's. If some goddamn lightning was gonna fry me to death right now, I was going to go out kissing my mate.

I screamed right into his mouth.

And just as the power was about to rip me apart... Orion's eyes snapped open.
