As we drove along, he patiently introduced each place to me. Yesterday, amidst the rain, everything was murky, including my mood, so I didn't pay attention to the scenery.

Today, with the weather being nice, my mood naturally lightened, and I finally felt like I was in a foreign land.

In my mind, Foswood was always my home. Even small towns in the northeast didn't compare.

Especially after bringing my parents to Foswood, I felt that Foswood was my hometown.

But here, everything was unfamiliar. Even the air felt different. Yet, it held a strong allure for me.

At the hospital, we were greeted by several doctors, including the hospital's director. Atlas told me that this hospital was actually under ATL Empire. Atlas didn't just want them to perform a prenatal check-up, but he wanted a full-body examination for me.

"It doesn't need to be so complicated. My health has always been good, and this pregnancy has been going really well. I hardly have any morning sickness. Don't worry so much," I protested to Atlas.

"Be good. We need to do it. What was true before might not be true now. I'll be with you every step of the way," he patiently reassured me, afraid of any mishaps.

During the ultrasound examination, the doctor was very meticulous. Then he said to Atlas, "Mr. Atlas, come take a look. These are your children." "Children?" I looked at the doctor in confusion.

He nodded with a smile. "Yes, children. They're growing well. Mrs. Chloe, come take a look too."

He adjusted the screen to show me and gently moved the device over my abdomen.

Atlas looked over too with great curiosity.

"So these are two embryos, meaning there are two lovely babies growing healthily in your womb."

"Two?" I asked in astonishment.

"Yes, two," the doctor confirmed.

I immediately grabbed Atlas's hand, hardly believing it myself. We were having twins?

Atlas couldn't contain his joy, asking many questions about my health and the precautions to take care of twins.
