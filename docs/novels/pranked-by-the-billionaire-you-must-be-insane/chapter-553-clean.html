NovelVibe

Chapter 560

Frederick pushed his plate away and wiped his mouth. He locked eyes with Eleanor. "So, is Vivienne still burning up?"

Eleanor gave a slow shake of her head. "Thorne’s been checking her. He pumped her with some fever reducers, but nothing's sticking. The fever just keeps spiking back up."

Frederick scowled. "This is getting ridiculous."

They were supposed to pack her off to the country house today.

But now the grandparents were sniffing around.

If Vivienne stayed sick, the old folks would freak out, and the last thing they needed was a medical emergency with the in-laws.

Eleanor let out a heavy breath. "Thorne thinks it’s all in her head."

I mean, makes sense. The Lancasters had treated Vivienne like a princess her whole life.

Now Lyra was back in the picture, and suddenly, everyone was looking at the real daughter.

Sure, Eleanor hadn't changed how she treated Vivienne, but Vivienne?

She couldn't handle the demotion. She’d bottled up all that jealousy and resentment.

With nowhere to vent, her body just shut down. Now she was a total wreck.

Frederick’s scowl deepened. "What does she even have to be sick about? She’s adopted. We took her in to get some good karma so we could find our real kid. She knew the deal."

He threw his hands up. "And it’s not like we’re treating her like garbage. Nothing’s changed. What is she throwing a tantrum for?"

Eleanor reached over, tapping his arm to calm him down. "You know how she is, Fred. She’s always had to be number one. She gets inside her own head and spins out."

She sighed again. "Look, my parents want to see her anyway. Let’s just send her to stay with them for a bit in a couple of days. It’ll keep them happy, and she can get some space to sort herself out."

"Yeah, I guess that’s the play." Frederick turned to Lyra, his face softening with pure guilt. "Lyra, sweetie, I’m so sorry you’re getting the short end of the stick here."

Lyra opened her mouth to tell him she really didn't care.

But Frederick cut her off, shoving a black credit card and a hot-pink key fob right into her palm. "Take these. The second Vivienne packs her bags for your grandparents' place, I’m buying you two office buildings downtown."

Seriously? More?

She already had hundreds of millions sitting in her accounts. She couldn't blow through that cash if she tried.

She looked down at the pink key.

A limited-edition supercar.

Well, that was her family for you. Got a problem? Throw a mountain of cash at it. No point in fighting it. She flashed a quick smile. "Thanks, Dad."

Frederick waved it off. "Don't worry about it. You got dealt a bad hand. It's the least I can do."

"Hey, Lyra, you busy next week?" Reginald chimed in out of nowhere.

Lyra paused, thinking. "Uh, no. I'm free."

Reginald stared at her. "Good. Pack your bags. You and your mom are flying to the States next week to see your grandparents."

The table went dead silent.

Everyone stared at Reginald, totally thrown off.

Reginald narrowed his eyes, a sharp, calculating look flashing across his face. "They haven't met you yet," he said, his voice cool. "You're finally home. It's time they saw who you are."

Lyra locked eyes with him and got the message instantly.

Grandpa knew exactly what he was doing. The only reason the grandparents babied Vivienne was because they hadn't met the real deal yet.

If Vivienne kept running to them to play the victim every time she messed up...
