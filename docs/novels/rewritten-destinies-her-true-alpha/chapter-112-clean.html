Chapter 112

"It was a whammy, babe. Not me. I swear," Dad blurted, trying to smooth things over.

"Hold up. What whammy? What necklace? And who the hell is this other wolf?" Mom fired off, her voice rattling like a machine gun. I almost snorted.

"Elara... babe. Are you jealous?" Dad smirked.

*Smack.* Mom slapped his arm, hard, then whipped her head back to me.

"Look, I knew something was off," I said. "So I locked down my magic. Great-grandma got me through it. And—"

"Whoa, whoa. The dead one?" Mom blinked, totally lost. Magic wasn't her thing. "Look, I get Ronnie's weird mojo, but dead folks? That’s a hard limit, right?"

Dad and I cracked up.

"Think ghost, Mom. Her spirit hooked me up," I explained.

Mom marched over, plopping down right next to me. "You got schooled by a freaking ghost?"

"Mom. We literally turn into giant wolves. And ghosts are where you draw the line?" I patted her hand. She glared daggers at me.

"Hey! Cut me some slack. This is way out of my league." She pouted, looking like a kid. Dad wrapped an arm around her, pulling her tight.

"We get it, babe."

"Anyway," I went on, "Granny helped me tap into the power. Once I dialed in, boom—the Celestia stepped in."

Mom choked on her own spit. "The Celestia? Like, THE Moon Goddess? You met her?!"

"Yep. Perks of the job. Goddess blessing and all that." I grinned.

Mom just stared, shaking her head. "Who even are you, kiddo?" she whispered.

I chuckled. "Still your girl, Mom. But yeah. Once I got a grip on the magic, Granny pointed me to her Book of Shadows. I dug up the exact mojo used on Dad. But I had to be 100% sure. One wrong move and I’d blow up the whole family. Then it clicked. Morgana. That home-wrecking bitch. She had to be the caster. So I rang up the King. Actually, I thought it was the Council. Needed the green light."

"Green light for what?"

*Damn.* I really hoped she wouldn't ask. I wasn't ashamed of what I did, but I didn't want my own mother thinking I was some psycho.

"To... y'know. Torture her," I mumbled, staring hard at my shoes.

Mom gasped. "Lyra!"

"I had to be sure, Mom!"

Mom took a breath, grabbed my hand, and squeezed. "Honestly, baby? I'm just glad you asked first. Me? I would've skinned her first and apologized later."

My jaw dropped. "Mom?!"

"What? You think I'd let some basic bitch steal my man? Hell freaking no." Mom was locked and loaded, eyes flashing.

I laughed, totally hyped now. "Right? So, while I was breaking her, I found the leak. The spell. She screwed up when she used your necklace. The birthstone one with the wolf." I yanked it out from under my collar. "Recognized it instantly."

Mom reached out, her finger tracing the metal. "You had this? The whole time?"

Dad nodded. "Took it to get engraved. Then the world ended. I kept it in your office. Moved all your stuff in there. Well, except your clothes."

"Why?"

"Because seeing your things wrecked me. But I couldn't throw them out. So I locked them in your office. Just to sit there and breathe you in."

"And my clothes?" Mom locked eyes with him. Dad’s cheeks went bright red.

"Left 'em in the closet," he mumbled. "On the really bad days... I'd just sit in there. Smelling you. It kept Obsidian quiet. Stopped the bleeding inside."

My eyes welled up. I didn't even know he did that.

"Caspian..." Mom whispered.

"I love you, Elara. Always. Nothing changes that. Ever."

God. That’s the kind of love I wanted. Total, ride-or-die devotion. Years apart, and Dad was still completely whipped. He got her. Every crazy thing she did, he got it. Zero blame.

Well, he blamed himself.

But honestly? They both just got dealt a crappy hand. They needed to drop the guilt and just be together.

"God, I love you, Leo. I'm so sorry," Mom sobbed.

"Me too, baby. Me too."

They leaned in for a kiss. Then, a howl ripped through the woods. A howl I knew too well.

My stomach dropped. Mom froze. Dad looked at me, his face dead serious.

"Ronnie?"

I could only nod.
