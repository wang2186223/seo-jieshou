Chapter 553

"Daddy!" Blair whined. I slapped a hand up to shut her up.

"Well, huh. Didn't see that coming. Kind of nice, actually," my dad's voice came through, cold as ice. "But Elara is mine. You're never laying eyes on her again. Period."

That broke Ronnie. He went totally blank, then just gave a weak nod. "Can you do me a solid, Alpha Maclean?" His voice was quiet, totally respectful. It threw me off.

"Maybe," Dad grunted, still pissed.

"Just... tell her I'm sorry. For all of it."

My jaw practically dropped.

"Fine," Dad snapped.

*BOOM.*

The phone rattled with a massive explosion.

"Dad?!" I snatched the screen closer. A brutal, bone-shaking crack roared through the line, followed by the heavy rumble of collapsing stone. "Dad!" I screamed. Out of the corner of my eye, I saw Magnus start to smirk. "Dad, talk to me!"

Dad coughed, sounding completely panicked. "Bomb! Get out of the tunnels! Everyone out, now! Baby, I gotta run. The whole place is coming down—"

*Click.* Dead air.

I stared at the blank screen, praying for a ring, a beep, anything. Just waiting for his voice to pop back on. Nothing.

Blair let out a nasty little giggle. "Whoops. Too bad."

I whipped around, plastering a massive smile on my face. "Hilarious, right?"

Her smirk vanished. "Huh?"

"I mean, it's a riot. My pack's in deep shit again, your little scheme is trash, and I literally hold your life in my hands. But hey, watching people die is a blast, isn't it?"

She rolled her eyes. "I didn't say that."

I lunged, grabbing Magnus by the throat and hoisting him off the ground. "You didn't have to say it, Blair. You giggled while I was screaming for my dad. Pretty loud and clear, don't you think?"

Magnus gasped for air, choking out, "I'm... I'm not her dad. Not my fault. Look, if you're gonna whack someone to prove a point... kill Ronnie instead!"

I grinned. "Who said anything about killing you?" I looked over at Orion. "Hey, can you let them go?"

Orion cocked his head, curious. "What's the play here?"

My grin widened. "Just paying back some old debts. Every single one."

Orion stared at me for a beat, then nodded. His crushing aura vanished.

They gasped, getting a split second of relief. They thought they were free. They started scrambling to their feet, shaking. Then I stopped playing nice. I unleashed my own power. A massive wave of pure pressure slammed outward. Before they could even take a breath, they were flattened. Face-first into the dirt. No screaming this time. Just dead silence.

"Holy shit." Orion stumbled, dropping hard onto one knee under the weight of my power. His eyes went wide with shock, then melted into pure, raw worship. "This is you? All of this is your aura?"

I gave him a nod.

"God, that is so hot."

I threw my head back, laughing. "Wait, what?"

He shoved off the grass, forcing himself up, and slowly walked over to me. "Seriously. Your power is incredibly sexy."

I dumped Magnus onto the ground like a sack of garbage and faced my mate. "You're not freaked out?"

Orion chuckled. "Only weak, insecure dudes get scared of strong females. And I'm not weak." He stepped right into my space, eyes burning. "Your strength? Your power? It's gonna protect our pups. Our pack. That's the hottest damn thing I've ever seen." He slid his hand up my neck, cupping my cheek. "I love you, Lyra."

Then he slammed his lips onto mine.

My brain short-circuited for a second. Then his words clicked, and I fell for him all over again. I threw my arms around his back, kissing him hard, totally lost in it—until someone cleared their throat with a loud snicker.

I broke the kiss and spun around. Juniper was standing there, hands up. "Look, I'm thrilled you guys finally got your shit together. Seriously, watching you two dance around each other was painful."

Luna and Fawn nodded along.

"And yeah, making out while everyone else is pinned to the dirt is hilarious. But hey, can you dial down the gravity so we can actually stand up?"

I leaped back, blushing as the girls giggled. "My bad." I sucked back a chunk of my aura. "Alright, get up. Line up. Now."

I directed the command straight at Roric's crew, letting Juniper and the girls stretch and stand. Magnus and Damon tried to fight the order, straining against it, but since I wasn't hurting them, their bodies just obeyed. They couldn't resist.

"What the hell are you doing?" Damon spat, stumbling blindly into the lineup.

"Told you already. Just returning the favor."

A few of them growled, but I ignored them. I grabbed Orion's hand, pulled him to the front, and barked, "Move. Follow me."

I took off, stepping right over the bodies littered across the ground.

"Where are we going?!" Blair shrieked from behind.

"You'll see," I threw back over my shoulder.
