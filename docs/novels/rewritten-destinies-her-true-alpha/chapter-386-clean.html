Chapter 386

Boom. Her phone hit the table, screen up. We all crowded around, eyes glued to the display. Holy shit. There was Ronnie, in high-def, going absolutely to town on his secretary.

Ronnie’s face went white as a sheet. "Where... how the hell did you get that?" He lunged for the screen. Mom snatched it back, just out of reach.

A laugh cut through the room—cold, sharp, totally psycho. "You seriously thought I was blind?" She cocked her head, eyes icy. "You thought I iced you out over some stupid run? A goddamn run?" Her palms slammed the wood. The plates rattled.

Ronnie gaped like a fish. "I—"

"Shut your damn mouth!" Mom shrieked. Then, the rage drained, leaving her hollow. "You've done plenty of talking, Ronnie. Years of it. 'Oh, Elara, it was just a run. Elara, baby, you're my only one. Elara, get in my bed or Lyra gets hurt.'" She looked at him like he was garbage. "I know everything."

Ronnie collapsed back into his seat. "You... you knew?"

"Since last year, asshole." She tapped the screen, pulling up the texts. "Your little side chick had a mate, right? The guy who suddenly vanished? Yeah, he cut his bond and packed his bags. Shocking, right? Turns out even low-tier wolves hate it when some hotshot Beta screws their mates. Go figure."

He slumped forward, head in his hands. "A whole fucking year?" Mom nodded, deadpan. "So all this time, you were pissed about—"

"You banging your secretary? Bingo. That's why you didn't get to touch me. I'm not catching whatever nasty-ass rabies you two are passing back and forth." She bared her teeth.

"Elara, come on, it was nothing. I love you, okay?" He pleaded, desperate. But she wasn't buying a single word. "You lied. You cheated. Now, we're at the end of the road." Her voice dropped to a whisper, eyes dull with raw agony.

Blair bolted upright. "No way."

Mom shook her head. "Oh, yes." She closed her eyes, face to the ceiling. "Because I'm nobody's backup plan, mate. I deserve to be someone's absolute everything." The crack in her voice was 100% real this time. "I deserve respect. I deserve real love. And goddamn it, I deserve a family that actually gives a shit about me." Her gaze locked onto Blair. "Did you ever give a shit, Blair? Did you ever actually love me? I spent sleepless nights raising you, feeding you, putting clothes on your back, keeping you safe and happy. Did you ever once treat me like a mom? Like I actually belonged here?"

Blair winced. "I did! I loved you! Now you're spinning this total bullshit just to drag my dad through the mud!" She raised her hand. I scraped my chair back and stood.

I arched an eyebrow and stepped in. "I had your back, Blair. Don't make me regret it. This isn't your fight." She looked like she'd been slapped. "Whatever drama they have is their business. But you call my mom a liar again, or lay a finger on her, and I will personally kick your ass." Blair shrank back.

Damon stood up. "Lyra, seriously? You don't mean that."

I glared at him. "Oh, I mean every word. This is my mom. I get that Ronnie is Blair's dad, and she'd back him up if the tables were turned. But he's the cheater here, not my mom. He wrecked this family, not her." I locked eyes with Blair. "Try me."

Blair's lip started shaking. "But... I won't have a place to live."

"You've got the pack house. The Beta wing is right there, remember?" I snapped.

Ronnie hit the floor, crawling over on his knees. "Elara, please, baby." He reached out. Smack. She whipped his hand away. "Get your fucking hands off me," she snarled, standing up. "I want the divorce papers signed and done by tomorrow."

"For god's sake, Elara, please." He crawled closer. Mom stopped, then slowly squatted down to his level. "I love you," he whimpered, reaching for her cheek. This time, she didn't flinch. She let him touch her.

"Back in the day, I might've bought that crap." She smiled, leaning close. "But I'm not an idiot anymore. You never loved me. You just loved having me under your thumb." Mom grabbed his chin, squeezing hard. "You loved twisting the knife my actual mate left in my chest. You loved making me feel like absolute trash. You loved seeing me broken." She forced his head around to face me, pressing her cheek to his. "But wanna know what you loved the absolute most?" Her voice went dead, terrifyingly cold. "You loved holding my baby, my little girl, like a gun to my head. You loved using her safety to force your way into my bed, and then my heart." She yanked his face back to hers. "What was your favorite line again, Ronnie?"

"I didn't—" he stammered, but her grip on his jaw tightened like a vice.

"Oh, right! 'If you can't be a real wife to me, Elara, I don't know how I can protect Lyra. If Magnus thinks we're faking it, I can't stop him from turning you both into slaves.'" She shoved his face away, sending him sprawling onto the floor. "I was too broken back then to see it. I actually thought you were looking out for us. But this past year? I saw right through your sick little mind games. And it hit me." She stood up, towering over him. "You threatened a two-year-old baby with slavery just to get laid. You make me sick to my stomach. Now get the hell out of my house."
