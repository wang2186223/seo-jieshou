Chapter 421

I dragged myself out of the office, a tired smirk plastered on my face.

Elara was hovering by her desk, beaming. "Well? Did you pull it off?"

"Down to the wire," I said, popping the 'p'. "Beat the clock by a second."

*Bam!* The door flew open. Elara jumped out of her skin. Magnus barged in, roaring my name. "Lyra!"

Elara smoothly slid behind me, trying to play it cool. "Yes, Alpha Vince? Need something?" She backed straight into Gideon Stone’s office, putting some distance between them. Magnus tracked her with his eyes, but I snatched my purse off the wall hook, forcing his eyes right back onto me.

"You're not calling off the mating ceremony," he said. He was smiling, but his eyes were pure ice. He tried to sound like he was pleading, but it was a straight-up threat. "Damon loves you."

I gave him a slow nod. "And I love him. Enough to actually protect his reputation."

Magnus frowned, his brow knitting together. "The hell is that supposed to mean?"

I let out a heavy sigh, stepping past him and slamming my office door shut. "Look, Alpha Magnus. You're the big boss, so nobody gossips to your face. But the pack is buzzing. Word's out that Blair might be carrying Damon's kid. Everyone's saying they're back together." I shook my head, my stomach churning. "Damon hooked up with an unranked wolf. Sure, some packs don't care about rank, but ours does." God, saying this crap made me want to puke. "Our pack loves to talk. I'm not adding fuel to the fire. If I mate with him now, he looks like a joke. A guy who can't make up his mind. Worse, they'll call him weak for settling for a low-rank wolf. A weak, wishy-washy Alpha ruins a pack. I'm not dragging him down." I walked toward the exit and threw a look over my shoulder. "I'm not Blair. I actually give a shit about him."

"Wh—" He froze. I could practically see the gears grinding in his head as he replayed my words. "What do you mean, you're not Blair?"

I let out a dry laugh. "How do you think those rumors leaked so fast, huh?"

I turned on my heel and walked back toward Gideon's office, leaving Magnus to put the pieces together.

"Motherfucker," he muttered under his breath, then stormed out.

I gave it a minute, then tapped on Gideon's door. "Ready to roll?"

Elara swung the door open, lugging a massive cardboard box. "Yep. Got it." She shoved it into my arms.

"What's this?" I asked, adjusting my grip. The damn thing was heavy, packed to the brim with files.

"Tomorrow's headache," she said, pointing at the box. "Just remembered it. Was gonna leave it on your desk, but since you're here, take it home. Get a head start."

I gave the box a little shake. Nothing rattled. "Okay, but what actually is it?"

"Rick's files," she laughed. "He wants them sorted by firewall. There are different sections in there. Since you've seen them before, he wants you to organize his 'process'." She grabbed her coat and keys. "Oh, and he said to throw out the useless trash." She locked eyes with me on that last part, making sure I got the hint.

"Oh, for God's sake," I groaned, playing my part. "Can't the guy clean up his own mess? Or make you do it?"

She sighed, flipping off the light switch. "Tell me about it. I asked him the same thing, but he said I 'wouldn't get it'." We walked out together.

"Yo." Rhys Thorne rounded the corner. Instantly, a wave of pure stench hit me. I literally gagged. "Ready, babe?" he grinned, looking down at Elara. She nodded. "See ya, Lyra," they waved, turning to leave. His cheap cologne was so strong it practically singed my nose hairs.

The smell knocked me so off-balance I forgot I was holding the heavy box. I went to pinch my nose and almost dropped the whole damn thing.

"Later," Rhys called back, catching my disgusted face and laughing.

I rolled my eyes and headed out. Back home, I grabbed a quick bite, hauled the box up to my room, and locked the door. I'd popped over to the cabin for dinner first to catch up with everyone before heading back to the main house.

"Why are you rushing back?" my mom asked, crowding my space the second I stood up from the table.

"Got a video call with Dad and Cipher. Need the house Wi-Fi," I lied smoothly. "Plus, I've got a mountain of work files to sort through."

"Alright..." She hovered, looking anxious, but I shut the door on her. I needed quiet before the call. Time to find out what the hell was actually hidden in this box.
