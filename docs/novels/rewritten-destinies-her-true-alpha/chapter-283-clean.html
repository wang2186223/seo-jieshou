Chapter 283

Eyes shut. I slumped against the shovel. God, I was so done with tonight. But Ronnie’s grave wasn't deep enough yet. Just a few more feet of dirt to throw.

"You're playing Damon," Umbra growled, squatting on her haunches, glaring daggers. "Using him to get to Magnus. You've been playing the long game for months. Letting him slide in close. Chatting him up."

"And? Your point is?" I snapped back.

Umbra bared her teeth. "We gotta get close to the guy. It’s the only way to do what needs doing."

"Exactly. We do what we must," I muttered. "We use my ex-mate. Period. That’s how we dig up who’s behind these pack hits. It’s the only way to drag our dad—Juniper’s dad too—out of the shadows. The only way Mom gets her mate back."

"And?" I threw my head back, glaring at the moon. The damn thing just hung there, staring down, basically mocking me.

*And maybe Orion is pulling the exact same stunt,* Umbra chimed in.

I let out a heavy sigh. "But... what if he isn't?" The words just floated in the cold air.

Terra’s eyes softened as she looked from me to Umbra. "Then he isn't," she said, her voice quiet but hard as steel. "He moved on. Found his fated mate. So we do the same. Look, I dumped my mate back in another life. You guys were right—he was trash. Same goes for Orion. If he’s trash, we walk. We're young, damn it. We've got our whole lives ahead of us."

"I still love the guy," I mumbled. God, I hated admitting that out loud.

"I know. It's fine," Terra said. "Like you told Luna—it's gonna hurt like hell, and it won't stop hurting tomorrow. But it gets better." With that, she turned and vanished into the trees, leaving me and Umbra to our own messy thoughts.

I kept staring at the sky. "She's right, you know."

Umbra slumped against the mental barrier with a heavy sigh. *I know... I just hate it.*

A dry laugh escaped my throat. What else was I supposed to do? "Yeah. Join the club."

*We gotta talk to him sooner or later,* Umbra whispered.

"Yeah, but not today. Not anytime soon, either." I shoved the shovel back into the dirt. "We've got enough of our own shit to sort out first."

She sighed, dropping her head onto her paws. *They're literally the same guy.*

"Yep."

Thirty minutes of sweating, and the grave was done. I scrambled out, tossed the shovel next to the dirt pile, and headed over to grab Ronnie.

*I don't have a clue how to be a mom,* Umbra's voice cracked.

I cracked a tiny smile. "Ditto."

I navigated the sea of corpses. The dead rogues were piled up like trash, surrounded by firewood. "Well, Orion's been a busy boy."

"He wanted to handle the whole thing," Juniper said, stepping out from the cabin's shadow. "But I told him you'd want to bury Ronnie yourself. So he prepped this pile. I told him to scram the second he heard you coming, though."

"And he actually beat it?" I stared at the pile of dead meat, feeling my heart splinter a little more.

Juniper sighed. "Yeah. But not because of anything I said."

"Meaning what?" I looked up.

She just shook her head. "Figure it out yourself, babe."

She sloshed a jerry can. "Ronnie really covered all the bases." She kicked a hose aside, walked over, and started drenching the corpses in gasoline.

"Yeah." I watched her strike a match and toss it. *Whoosh.* The wood and flesh caught instantly, roaring to life. "This is gonna take all damn night."

"Mm-hmm," she grunted. "The girls and I are taking shifts to keep this fire roaring. Already clued them in."

I looked at her. "Thanks."

She threw an arm around my shoulders. "We're besties, dummy. Cut the thank-yous." She planted a quick kiss on my temple. "Go bury our boy. I got this covered."

I nodded, pulled back, and scooped Ronnie up. I carried him over to the massive oak and hopped down into the pit. I laid him down gently, wiping the dried blood off his face, smoothing his hair, and straightening his shirt.

I went to stand up, but my fingers caught on a scrap of paper poking out of his front pocket. I yanked it out. It was a messy, rushed note: *Thought I had more time. Dumped all the shit in a bowl on the counter.*
