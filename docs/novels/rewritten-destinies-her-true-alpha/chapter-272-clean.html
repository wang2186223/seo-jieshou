Chapter 272

Umbra bolted. She blew past the campus crowd, straight for the tree line. I backed off, grabbing our shared magic. I pumped that raw power right into my veins, then pushed it into the other girls.

"How the hell are we doing this?" Juniper yelled, panting.

I just laughed.

"Magic, baby!" Umbra barked back. A bunch of the girls let out wild howls.

We hit the woods, dodging thick trunks at full speed.

"Umbra?" Thistle yelled, trying to get Luna's wolf's attention.

"Yeah?"

"Where the hell are we heading?" She pushed her legs harder, catching up.

My mind flashed to Luna. Seven max. Honestly, on the phone, she sounded way younger—like five or six. I knew the spot—Grandma’s cabin. But from right here? No clue. Still, I trusted the Goddess. I trusted my pack.

"The Goddess has our back! She'll guide us!" Umbra yelled. I nodded.

Terra shoved her way closer to the barrier. "Terra? What are you pulling?" I snapped.

She glanced at me, then locked eyes with Umbra. "You're wasting juice feeding Umbra. Focus on the pack. I'll pump my own strength into her. That way, we keep your power fresh, use our magic, and save the kid."

I pictured Luna’s tiny, shaking voice. Yeah. Good call. "Umbra?"

"Works for me. Just get us there." I cut the flow, dumping the whole load onto Terra and Umbra.

A howl echoed far off. Sounded familiar. I blocked it out. Who cares. Not important. Luna was.

We tore deeper into the forest, leaving the school behind. Panic started clawing at my chest. How were we supposed to just pop out by the ocean? Sure, the school was near the coast. But was it this coast?

I squeezed my eyes shut. I screamed out to the Goddess in my head. *Mother, I need you. Moon Goddess, please, do something.* I waited. I pushed. I begged. Nothing. No spark. No power. Just dead silence.

Terra shot me a look. "Now what?"

Umbra kept her eyes glued to the trees, but I felt the vibe shift. We were dragging our feet. That poor little girl and her dad were fighting for their lives against god-knows-what, and we were totally lost.

"Umbra!" a voice barked. It snapped us right out of it. We’d been so wrapped up in our own heads that someone had actually caught up to us.

I whipped my head around to look at Daisy’s wolf. But she wasn't looking at me. She was staring back. A massive grey wolf was gaining on us. I caught a flash of fur, then those pale, icy-blue eyes. My stomach did a violent flip.

Shadow.

"Are you fucking kidding me?" Terra roared.

I couldn't help it—a bitter, nasty laugh popped out of me. She glared at me, and I slapped a hand over my mouth.

"Why is it," she hissed, "that today of all days, both your damn men decide to show up and act like total idiots?"

She snorted, hitting the ground hard and locking back onto Umbra to keep her moving. Umbra didn't even look back. She just ran. I opened my mind, trying to feel out the right way to go.

I closed my eyes, replaying that phone call. Her tiny voice was terrified, but there was this deep, heavy sadness in it too. She knew. She knew this was the end for her and her dad. A tear slipped down my cheek. She was way too young to face this kind of hell alone.

*Moon Celestia, please. Give me a sign. A nudge, a gut feeling, anything to show me where to go to save this kid.* I begged. Still, nothing. Just silence.

"Which way?" Umbra yelled, her voice cracking with pure panic.

I had nothing. "I don't know!" I screamed back.

"Well, you better figure it the fuck out! We're running blind here!" she yelled.

What the hell was I supposed to do?

Terra broke away from Umbra for a second and slid up next to me. I looked up, and she nudged my forehead with her wet nose. "Close your eyes. Open up to us, to your power, to the Goddess. Try again. Drop the panic. Focus on the target. Luna."

I shut my eyes. I let go. Bit by bit, I let everything slip away. The panic, the anger, the stress—I let it all float off. I cleared my head, centering myself, until a warm hand touched my shoulder.

I opened my eyes. "I don't know what to do."

"I know, baby. That's why I'm here." Relief washed over me like a wave. I tried to scramble up, but she kept her hand firm on my shoulder.
