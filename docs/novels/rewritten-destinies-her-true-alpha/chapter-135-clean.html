Chapter 135

I scanned the floor. Every single dude was face-down, eating dirt. "What the actual fuck?" someone muttered. I let out a low, nasty growl. These assholes had pushed me way past my limit. Now? Yeah, it was payback time.

"Alpha Lyra..." Jasper whimpered. I whipped around and marched straight to the front of the room. I was holding back, sure. I wasn't trying to break their bones—not yet, anyway—but damn, I was getting real close to not giving a shit.

"Jasper, do me a favor and shut your mouth for a second. Thanks." I dialed down the pressure just enough so they could push themselves up onto their knees. "Alright." I faced the room like a pissed-off substitute teacher, leaning back against the desk, hands balled into tight fists. "I tried playing nice, guys. But you just had to push it."

"Look, we're sorry," some guy stuttered out.

I just shook my head. "Too late for apologies, Greg." My eyes locked onto Rhys. He was giving me the death stare. "Got a problem, Rhys? Spit it out."

"No," he spat, his pride clearly bruised. Man, I really wanted to skip this drama. But could they just leave well enough alone? Nope. They wanted a reckoning, and they got it.

Umbra clawed her way up, my eyes flashing. "No, what, Rhys?" He tried to resist my aura, straining against the weight, but I just casually checked my fingernails. "Honestly? I wanted to spare you guys. But I guess this was bound to happen."

"Do what?" Jasper squeaked.

I snapped my gaze right to him. "Hey, Charles. What's the rule when a new alpha rolls into a pack?"

He frowned, totally clueless. "Uh... nothing?"

I scanned the rest of the room. "Anyone? What does an alpha do when they want to join a new pack?"

Victor, this scrawny, low-tier wolf in the back, piped up. "They fight."

"Bingo." I spun around to the whiteboard. "A wild wolf stakes his claim by taking down the boss. In our world, they fight, they throw orders around, or they just flex their aura to see who bends the knee." I whipped back around. "Kind of like the little stunt you guys just pulled on me, right?"

"My bad," Jasper mumbled. "We just... we didn't think you were actually an alpha."

*They disrespected us,* Umbra growled in my head.

"Look, normally I'd shrug it off," I said, hands out, eyebrows raised. "But I'm new to shifting. My wolf is practically rabid right now. Keeping her on a leash is a 24/7 job. If you'd just believed us, we would've let the insult slide. But no, you had to keep pushing, acting like we're weak. Like you're better than us. Back in my dad's pack, high-ranking wolves actually respect people. But you guys? You treat the unranked in this room like absolute garbage. Hell," I pointed a finger straight at Victor. "When he stood up, half of you laughed. Someone threw trash at him, and you just sat there, Charles. Why?"

"So you think you're above us?" Rhys sneered, his voice dropping to a cold, sharp whisper.

"Did I give you permission to open your mouth, Rhys?" I snapped. The color instantly started draining from his face. "Wait, what?" "I. Said. Did. I. Tell. You. To. Speak?" I marched right up to him, stomping down with every single word.

"I was just—"

I dropped into a squat right in front of him and pressed my finger against his lips. "Shh. You didn't. So don't." If his glare could kill, I'd be six feet under. I stood back up. "I'm setting the ground rules once. Then we're going back to our regular lesson."

"Do what?" someone muttered.

I ignored Rhys, shook my head, and raised my voice so the whole room could hear. "If you ever push me this far again, I'm not using my aura to make a point. Next time, I'm using my fists. Got it?"

Someone snorted in the back, whispering to his buddy, "Is this chick for real?"

Before the buddy could even open his mouth, I let it rip. I unleashed every single ounce of my aura. Boom. The sheer weight slammed everyone flat on their faces, noses pressed hard against the floorboards. "I said, *do you understand me?!*" I roared, forcing them to answer.

A wave of choked-out "yeses" filled the room. I snapped my aura back, locking it away until the air cleared. The guys slowly dragged themselves up on wobbly legs. I watched a ghost-white Rhys scramble to grab his gear and back away from me.

Well, damn. Guess we won't be grabbing beers anytime soon.
