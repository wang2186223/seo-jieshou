Chapter 644

Whispers buzzed around me. I shut my eyes. Aether’s turn. I took a massive drag of air, letting the oxygen hit my lungs like a shot of adrenaline. It surged through my veins, waking up every damn muscle. Wind whipped my hair. Leaves rustled nearby.

"What the hell is she doing?" Elara blurted out.

Mom shut her down instantly. "Lyra’s trying to keep us alive," Mom hissed, pure irritation in her voice. "Zip it. Don't mess up her focus."

I almost laughed watching them. Then I saw Freya sprinting from the house, holding onto something tight. But right then, a sharp yank hit my chest. A warning. No time to waste. I had to wrap this up now.

I threw my arms up and locked my mind back on Aether. I visualized him—this massive, towering beast riding the thermal currents, climbing higher and higher into the blue.

*Summon my youngest boy. You’re running out of time, girl,* Rhea’s voice echoed in my head.

"Sen ta cun di for ple shorn!" I roared.

Boom. Magic surged straight to my fingertips. The wind went nuts, slamming into me, but I planted my feet. I threw my energy into the sky, letting it fuse with the gale. Mini-tornadoes spun around my boots, sucking the raw power right out of my hands and launching it upward.

"Piven din clo verta dom si vil!" I chanted.

Those little dust devils slammed together, merging into one massive, roaring cyclone. Suddenly, my boots left the dirt. The vacuum yanked me straight up into the swirling chaos.

I gasped, but there was no air—the storm literally stole the breath from my throat. The G-force inside this spinning tube was brutal, crushing my ribs. Panic flared, but I forced it down. I knew I wasn't going to die here.

*Spread your arms,* Aether’s deep voice rumbled. He laughed, and it sounded like a crack of thunder. Lightning exploded all around me. Normal people would be screaming, but I felt totally chill in the eye of this hurricane. *Fly with me, kid. Feel the lift. Feel what it’s like to own the damn sky.*

I threw my arms out. Immediately felt like an idiot. Uh, hello? No wings here.

Aether laughed again, throwing more sparks across the dark. *Says who?*

His voice came from way up. I squinted through the flying dirt and trash the tornado was sucking up. Right above the mess, Aether was circling in his massive thunderbird form, whipping the storm into a frenzy. *Come on, kid, let’s fly. Get up here. Hit the center and see what real freedom feels like.*

I spread my arms again and did a hard downward stroke, mimicking a bird. Boom—I launched right through the wall of the cyclone into the dead center. The sudden silence was a total mind-trip after that bone-crushing wind. I rocketed straight up, breaking out of the storm to meet the god. Finally, I sucked in a clean, deep breath.

I peeked down. Holy crap.

He chuckled, sending fresh lightning dancing across the clouds. Below us, the pack lands looked tiny. People were crowding together. Then I spotted Freya, frozen mid-stride halfway to us.

"Wait, why is she stuck like a statue?" I pointed down at her.

*Time doesn't move when you're hanging with us,* he said, sounding highly amused. *Keep up, kid.* He did a sharp dive, and I tailed right behind him.

"Is this actually happening?" I called out.

Aether glanced back. *Hey, reality is a slippery thing, right?* He accelerated, his draft pulling me along as we glided. *Your trip and their trip are two different things.* He glanced down at the crowd. *Does that make this fake? Sure, they see you standing on the dirt. But aren't you up here, flying with me? Feels pretty damn real to you, doesn't it?*

"Yeah, fair point." I put on a burst of speed to catch up.

He laughed. *This way, kid.* His energy grabbed me like a tractor beam, pulling us both toward a massive cloud.

"Is this your home base?" My jaw dropped. We were so high up the blue sky had vanished. Instead, a pitch-black night sky packed with glittering diamond stars stretched over his turf. "Whoa. This is insane."

*Had to build something worthy of my wife's rest,* he said, sounding pretty proud of himself. *Alright. Focus up. Look at the land. Think about the people you've gotta save.*

I nodded and took a dive. I hit the cloud like a giant mattress, sinking hip-deep into the fluffy white stuff. I let out a breath, closed my eyes, and conjured up the map in my head, focusing on every single werewolf I had to protect.

"Aether, sky father, hear me!" I yelled, scraping together every last drop of my magic. I knew this spell would leave me completely running on empty. "Carry my power to the packs! Make them invisible! Let the enemy's eyes slide right past them!"

Boom. My magic exploded out of me like a category-five hurricane, tearing across the landscape to lock the cloaking spell in place.

Aether yanked me out of the cloud and blasted me into a high-speed jet stream heading down. *As soon as you hit the dirt, lock that spell down tight! Keep your people safe! Stay tough, kid!* his voice echoed as I spun back to earth.
