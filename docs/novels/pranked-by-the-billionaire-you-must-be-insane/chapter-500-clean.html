Chapter 506

Vivienne figured it out. It was a scam. Lyra was using the "Ghost Doctor" brand. Why? Because the real doctor was a ghost—nobody knew what they looked like. Lyra knew some basic herbal crap, so she stole the title for clout. Pure fake-it-till-you-make-it. And she was standing there, acting all cool and untouchable. God, it was sickening. The more Vivienne spun the theory in her head, the more she believed her own lie.

Eleanor and Frederick watched their girl from the edge of the crowd. Pride swelled in their chests, mixed with a sharp ache. Their little Lyra. What kind of hell had she survived to get this good at everything? Piano prodigy, top-tier government scientist, and now this medical legend. Was there anything she couldn't do? Their own daughter kept hitting them with shock after shock.

Ethan stepped in. He threw an arm out, blocking the crowd, his tall frame completely shielding Lyra. "Look, you all know the Ghost Doctor's name," he barked. "So you know how she rolls. She's here for Elara's birthday, not to hand out medical advice. Back off. You're crowding her."

The chatter died down.

Lyra swept her sleepy eyes over the room. Her voice was flat, cool. "Hey. Today is Elara's night. Let's keep the focus where it belongs, okay?"

That did it. The legend had spoken.

The VIPs grumbled, but they backed off. You don't piss off a medical god. Who'd be stupid enough to try? Besides, the Blackwoods and the Lancasters were flanking her like Secret Service. No way in.

The crowd dispersed, awkward but still staring.

"Holy crap... that girl is the Ghost Doctor? No wonder Arthur was obsessed."

"No kidding. And look at how the Lancasters are guarding her."

"Man, the Blackwoods just locked down an alliance with her. That's a game-changer. Not just for Vachon City, but the whole country. Globally, even."

"Honestly? They look perfect together."

With the "Ghost Doctor" title on the table, Lyra's family background didn't matter anymore. Nobody would dare object to her marrying into the Blackwood family now.

Lyra looked at her brother's back. He was still standing there like a wall. Her cold eyes softened, a sudden warmth hitting her chest.

Then, the lights killed.

A slow, romantic beat started playing.

A single spotlight sliced through the dark, locking right onto Elara. Time for the first dance. The birthday girl had to open the floor.

Elara's eyes sparkled under the beam. She flashed a huge grin at Lyra, mouthing, *I'm up, wish me luck!*

Then she spun away in her pink gown, grabbed Leo, and dragged him onto the floor to start the dance.

The house lights came up a fraction, casting a soft, warm glow over the room. Lyra stood at the edge of the floor, shadows and light rippling over her. She watched Elara spin and glide, her skirt flaring out. The girl looked so happy. Pure, sweet joy.

Suddenly, a hand reached out right in front of her.
