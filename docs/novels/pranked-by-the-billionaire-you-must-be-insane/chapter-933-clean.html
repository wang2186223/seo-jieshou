Chapter 945

"Whatever," Lyra shrugged, her face a total blank. "Spill. I'll deal with the fallout."

Lydia just stared. Lyra sat there, cool as ice, looking like some badass queen on a throne. Like she could wreck the whole damn world with a snap of her fingers. The Doyles? Yeah, they didn't even register on her radar.

Honestly, it clicked. If Lyra actually gave a crap about the Doyles, she wouldn't have dragged Willow through the mud like that. To Lyra, Willow was just a bug to step on.

Looking at that gorgeous, stone-cold face, Lydia felt a sudden spark of hope. It hit her hard. So, she started talking, letting it all out.

She and Faith were from the Backwoods. Yeah, it’s the freaking twenty-first century, but that place? Stuck in the dark ages. Backward as hell.

Girls there are trash. Lydia’s real name was basically a slur for "worthless." And Faith? She was the oldest, but since their parents wanted a boy, they named her "Bring-a-Brother." No joke.

Back home, girls have one job: work like dogs for the men, then get sold off to the highest bidder to pay for their brothers' lives.

They fought like hell to claw their way out of that swamp. No way were they staying in those mountains, married to some useless local loser, rotting away.

So they hit the books. Hard. They aced every test, bagged scholarships, and got teachers to notice them. That’s how they forced their parents to keep them in school.

They actually made it to Marington College—the top school in Elyndor. But man, that place was crawling with rich geniuses. Their basic scholarships barely covered cheap ramen.

Without the Doyle family’s cash, they’d still be stuck in the mud.

Faith was the smart one, a total prodigy in biopharma. She wanted to graduate, work for Doyle Pharma, and pay them back. But then, two months ago, everything went sideways.

Faith came home buzzing. She’d been picked for some elite overseas training. Once she got back, she’d be a lead researcher at Doyle. Plus, the Doyles wired her family a cool two hundred grand.

"But right before she left, she was... off," Lydia choked, tears welling up. "Like, she kept getting these crazy nosebleeds, and she’d just snap at me. Totally not her. The doctor said it was just exam stress, you know?"

"Then the Doyles sent a guy to get her a full workup. Next thing we know, they’re saying she’s got some 'special biology'—like she’s some rare breed of genius—and they had to fly her out for intensive training."

"She was so hyped, Lyra. Kept saying she’d pay them back for everything," Lydia sobbed. "But... I watched her board that plane, and then? Nothing. Total radio silence."

"I called her a million times. Emailed. Nothing."

"Lyra, I swear to God, something bad happened to her."

"Even if she hated me, she’d never ghost her own family like this."
