Ronnie shrugged. "Should've kept it in your pants, man. You had one job for a few years, and you couldn't even manage that." He glared. "Stop blaming Blair. We both know she isn't the only one you're screwing."

Damon sighed. "True, but damn, she's strong." He grabbed his crotch. "And so stuck-up. Taking her down was gonna feel so good."

Gideon Stone laughed. Actually laughed. But when he turned, I caught it—a flicker in his eyes. He hated this. He wanted out.

*What the hell is going on?* Terra asked. Neither of us had a clue.

"Shut up," Magnus barked, and everyone went quiet. "Astrid. How much longer?"

She sighed, looking at a kid. A familiar kid. Umbra growled. *Wait, is that...?*

Yep. One of the boys from Lyra's class. The ones we checked out last night.

*Oh my god. You saved him.* Terra crept closer, straining to catch Astrid's whispers.

Astrid stood up. "One week to prep."

"You positive?" Magnus growled.

"Hell no, I'm not sure. The spell doesn't come with a damn clock. I told you this from day one. We wait for the wolf to kick the bucket, then we rip out the power."

"And that plugs us straight into the mother?" Gideon asked.

"Yes, damn it, how many times do I have to say it?" Astrid snapped. "See you in a week."

"Hold up." Gideon glared, hand out.

"Oh," Astrid smirked. "Right." She chucked a tiny vial. He snatches it. He forced a smile, but his jaw was tight as hell. "For a guy who got dragged into this, you're pretty eager," she winked, stepping close. "When we're done, maybe we play. I dig older guys. Just ask Gareth."

Gideon forced a grin. "Can't wait." He leaned down. "Just keep my kid breathing until the harvest."

She planted a kiss on his cheek. "You got it." She shifted and bolted into the trees.

My chest was pounding, but I stayed glued to the spot. Got to watch this play out. Magnus grunted, turning to Ronnie. "Where'd she run off to?"

Ronnie scowled. "To that slut's pack. The one Lyra dragged back. What's her name... Astrid? Tonya?" He looked at Damon, who chuckles.

"Juniper," Damon said, squatting to pick up a twig. "She went to Juniper's place. Makes sense. They're tight."

"Then pick another target," Gideon suggested.

Ronnie glared at him, but Damon stood up. "Exactly what I damn well said."

Ronnie growled, but Magnus waved him off. "Whatever. She's gotta show up for the mating, right?" He looked at Damon, who nodded.

"No way Lyra gets bonded without her mother there. And her mom won't skip it. Besides, she hates my guts."

Gideon cracked up. "Don't blame her." He doubled over laughing while Damon glared. "What? Just being real. I wouldn't trust you with my kids either."

Damon stared, then smirked. "Smart move." They both started cracking up.

Magnus rolled his eyes, shaking his head. "Doesn't matter. We'll just snatch her. The spell doesn't care if she's willing. Just needs the bond and the kid to be yours. We grab her, force the marriage, and lock her in chains until she drops a baby."

Terra snapped. She leaped up, growling. Every head whipped our way.

*Oh, shit.* I jumped up, screaming in my head. *Terra, run!* She spun and tore into the brush.

"Who the hell is that?" someone yelled.

I was hauling ass, but we were already running on empty. Umbra shoved her way to the front, taking the wheel from Terra.

*My bad, but we gotta move, and we can't shift,* she said, dead serious.

It went dead quiet behind me, except for heavy boots stomping toward my hiding spot. "No clue. Scent's totally unfamiliar. But how is she moving so quiet?" Damon sounded completely baffled.

A beat of silence. Then Magnus lost his mind. "Are you brain-dead? Do I have to hold your hand every single time shit goes sideways?" Silence again.

Damon's voice was faint. "What?"

A loud smack. Magnus roared, "Go get her, dumbass!"

Bones cracked as they shifted. One by one, growls echoed, then the thud of paws chasing me. Even Gideon. He knew I was out here, and he was still hunting me down. Gideon. That bastard.

*Why did he want us to witness that?* Terra panted, totally exhausted. But Umbra was pushing our body past its limits.

*He wanted us to know,* I thought. *Maybe it started as locker room talk with his buddies. A joke. Or a "what if." Then he fell for Mara.*

Umbra panted, grunting in agreement. *Probably somewhere in between. He thought they were just dickheads talking trash about girls, then boom—he finds his mate. But he couldn't cut ties with his crew.*

*Until his own kid got branded,* Terra added. Umbra agreed.

*I actually feel for the guy,* Terra said, making us hesitate. But Umbra kept us moving. *Think about it. Stuck between your boys and your woman. You think magic is just bullshit. Then suddenly your kid is branded, sick, dying. You know exactly why, but you can't tell your mate because it's a massive betrayal. It was all a joke until it got real, fast, and now your kid's life is on the line.*

A heavy knot twisted in our guts. *Doesn't matter right now. Before we figure out what to do with Gideon and Mara, we gotta escape and save those kids.* Terra retreated, leaving Umbra in control.

*I'm done, totally tapped out,* Terra muttered, collapsing and passing out instantly.

*Umbra? You good?* I asked.

*No, this body is dragging. They're catching up. I'm out of ideas,* she said. Then I heard it. Heavy paws tearing up the dirt right behind us. Too damn close.

I stepped up, merging with her. *How do I help?* We fused our strength, running as one. I grabbed a thread of magic and pumped it straight into our legs.

*How the hell are you doing that?* Umbra gasped.

*Magic,* I smirked, focusing on our paws. Every time they hit the dirt, a tiny spark of magic exploded, launching us forward.

We accelerated, blurring through the trees. *Can you hold this pace?* she asked, putting some real distance between us and the pack.

*I don't—*

Something slammed into my side. We rolled hard across the dirt, a chunk of fur ripping right off our shoulder. Terra jolted awake, whimpering. Umbra and I scrambled up, baring our fangs at... Gideon Stone.

*You son of a bitch,* Umbra growled, ready to fight.

Gideon shifted back to human instantly. "Look, whoever you are—Lyra's friend, ally, whatever—Magnus has this whole place rigged. Magical traps everywhere. How you even got in here is a miracle." He paced, and I quickly looked away from his naked body. No thanks. "I figured Lyra could use her magic to sneak a peek. Dammit." He stepped back, whispering fast. "I had to tackle you. Sorry. I'll text Lyra the second I'm back to let her know you're caught, but Magnus would suspect something if I let you slip."

Panic covered his face until a twig snapped nearby. Instantly, his expression went dead cold. The guy I knew vanished. In his place was a total stranger.

"Over here!" he yelled. "I got her, but she won't turn back. Can you force her, Magnus?"

My stomach dropped. They couldn't find out my identity. Heavy footsteps surrounded me. Suddenly, I was circled by naked guys.

"Guess I'll need a little help from Astrid or the twins," Magnus said, crouching near my face. I snapped my jaws at him. He smirked. "Feisty one." He grabbed the scruff of my neck, hard. I let him do it—for now. "I'm gonna love breaking you, bitch," he whispered, leaning close. "And oh, I will. Once you're human, I'm screwing you every single night until you beg for mercy. But I won't stop."

His sick grin made me want to puke. "I'll break you so bad you'll be begging for my touch." He got too close, and I snapped at his face again, catching him completely off guard.
