Chapter 221

"Damn, this is pure gold." I shoved her phone back into her hand. "Alright, let's go hunt this thing down."

"Well, the app makes it dummy-proof." She swiped her screen for a second. "Boom. Aisle twenty. Follow me." She took off, weaving through the shelves. "And check this out." She pointed right at a blinking red tag hanging halfway down the row. "We just press this."

"Talk about convenient!" Juniper giggled, jogging over. She snatched a box off the shelf, squinting at the label as she walked back. "Whoa. It's a whole kit. Seeds, dirt, the whole nine yards."

"No kidding? That's sick." Finnian strolled over, eyes shining. "Man, my girl would go crazy for this." Then he glanced at the price tag. He let out a sharp whistle. "Uh, scratch that."

"What's the catch?" I rolled the cart closer.

"Just... damn, that price tag is brutal." I leaned in, saw the numbers, and winced. Look, a couple hundred bucks? Pocket change to me. But to a pack guard? That's a massive chunk of their paycheck.

"Don't sweat it." I grabbed a box and tossed it into the cart. I looked at the girls. "Hey, you guys want in on this?" Daisy was staring at the box like it was made of gold. I snorted, grabbing a third one and throwing it in. "Come on, guys. Just speak up." Daisy's face went bright red.

"Yeah, yeah, you say that all the time," Daisy mumbled, nodding.

"But she's terrified you'll think we're just using you for your wallet," Juniper said, wrapping an arm around Daisy's shoulders with a grin. "I keep telling her, but it just won't sink in. You aren't playing sugar mommy because you have to. You do it because you want to. To you, cash is just paper."

Daisy shook her head, stubborn. "Money always matters, okay?"

"Look, I made some killer investments a few months back. Plus, my dad's trust fund? I'm filthy rich, Daisy." I grabbed her hand, squeezing it. "You're my sister. My ride-or-die. If I can help, I'm gonna help. Period." I stared at her until she finally looked up. "Get this through your head: money is a joke to me. If you're drowning, you tell me. I can't fix what I don't know about."

She froze for a second. Then, a tear spilled over. "My Alpha... he's threatening to jack up our rent. And they just slashed my mom's pay."

Juniper lunged forward. "Wait, what? Why the hell did they cut her pay?"

Finnian stepped in, brow furrowed. "Hold up. Are you Eleanor's kid?" Daisy gave a weak nod. I whipped my head toward Finnian. He immediately threw his hands up. "Hey, don't shoot the messenger. I heard some of Magnus's fan-girls gossiping. They said his secretary stepped out of line, and Magnus is absolutely livid."

"Give me details. What else?"

"Just that he suspects her. He thinks she leaked some classified school info to the Ring. She wasn't supposed to know, so now he's squeezing her dry to teach her a lesson."

I whipped out my phone and swiped. I had every single Alpha's personal number saved. I tapped Daisy's Alpha's contact and hit dial. Daisy lunged to grab my phone, but I slapped her hand away and held the phone to my ear. It rang. Once. Twice. Then, a sleepy, annoyed groan came through the speaker. "Yeah? Who's this?"

"Hey Rhys. It's Seraphina Thorne."

"Alpha Lyra? What the hell? Why are you calling at this hour?"

"Oh, just some urgent business I had to handle right this second. Now, Kirk... remember ten years ago?" I let the question hang, waiting for his brain to click. Waiting for him to remember me diving headfirst into that freezing lake at the Alpha Summit. His kid was three, giggling one second, then wandering off and falling right into the deep end. The adults were too busy drinking and talking shop to notice. So, I jumped. I was a scrawny eight-year-old who barely knew how to doggy paddle, but I dragged her out. She wasn't breathing. Panicking, I scooped her up and bolted straight into the main meeting room, dumping her right in front of my dad. He did CPR and brought the pup back. The nanny who lost her? Yeah, she was dead before sunrise.

"Yeah. I remember," his voice cleared up instantly, dead serious now.

"Babe, who is that?" a soft voice asked in the background.

"It's Seraphina Thorne." I heard sheets rustling, then a sharp click. Speakerphone. The Luna's voice came through loud and clear. "Seraphina, if you need a favor, name it. You saved our Willow."

I smirked. "Funny you say that, because I actually do need a favor. One of my close friends is in your pack. Her mom is having some serious drama with her house. I want to settle it. I'll buy the place outright, cash in hand, obviously. I just don't want them stressing over it anymore."

"Who's the family?"

"Eleanor over in Sandlewood. Her daughter, Daisy, is my girl."

Dead silence hit the line. Heavy.

"Make it happen," his Luna whispered fiercely. "No, Kirk, I don't give a damn what Magnus wants. She saved our baby girl, for Goddess' sake!"

Kirk cleared his throat, his tone firm. "Keep your money, Lyra. You did us the ultimate favor back then. It's our turn to pay it back. The house is hers. I'll sign the deed over first thing in the morning."
