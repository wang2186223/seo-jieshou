Chapter 201

"Hit her, Daisy! Harder!" I barked.

Daisy barely tapped the glove. God, I wanted to scream. No, scratch that—I was fucking livid. Orion’s voice was stuck on loop in my head: *I’ll court these girls, see what they know.*

Hell no. He was mine. Period.

*You’re acting pretty damn possessive for a guy who isn’t even our mate,* Terra chimed in.

Umbra, though? Total radio silence. She hadn't said a peep since Orion walked out last night.

*Why the silent treatment?* I nudged Umbra mentally. At the same time, I watched Daisy try a combo. Okay, she was actually getting the hang of it. She nailed the final hook, and I couldn't help but grin.

"Hell yeah, Daisy. Nice."

She bent over, sucking in air, but managed a proud smile. "Thanks. I've been putting in work at home."

"Yeah, I can tell. Go get some water." I pointed to the benches.

I turned back to Umbra, but all I caught was the tip of her tail vanishing into the dark woods.

*What the hell is her problem?* I asked Terra.

Terra stood up. *She’s just trying to process things, I think.* Then she trotted right after Umbra, leaving me completely on my own.

Process what, exactly? I rolled my eyes and sent them a quick mental note: *We’re running tomorrow, got it?*

Nothing. Just dead, heavy silence. Ever since I got my wolf, Umbra had never shut me out like this. It felt wrong. Seriously wrong.

"Yo, Lyra. Earth to Lyra!"

A hand waved right in front of my face. I flinched, jumping back. "Jesus, Luna! You scared the crap out of me." I laughed it off, hands on my knees to catch my breath. "Hey. What's up?"

Luna giggled and shook her head. "Where did you go just now? We’re hitting the cafeteria for breakfast before class. We literally yelled your name, but you were totally zoned out. You coming or what?"

"Yeah, hold on." I held up a finger, jogged over to grab my gear, threw my sweater over my head, threw my bag over my shoulder, and ran to catch up with the pack.

"Whatcha thinking about?" Juniper asked, a weird look in her eye.

I glanced down the hall. Magnus and Damon were lurking in a doorway.

Time to play the part. I let my voice crack. "My dad," I mumbled, just loud enough for the guys to catch it. "Just... thinking about my dad."

Right on cue, the two jerks smirked and looked away. God, I wanted to rip their throats out. The only thing keeping me from doing it was knowing my dad was actually alive. But they didn't know that. They were literally getting off on my misery.

Wait. Both of them. Magnus *and* Damon.

I thought Magnus was the sole puppet master here, but Damon—my absolute trash-bag of a mate—was in on it too. Deep inside, Terra let out a miserable howl. I flooded her with comfort. She was still hurting. Since we hadn't officially rejected him yet, the bond was still active. Damon was still her mate.

Fuck.

How the hell were we supposed to play along and let him get close if he still had that mate pull on us?

Whatever. Future me problem. I snapped back to Juniper. "Yeah," I sighed.

Juniper gave me a sympathetic look and looped her arm through mine. "I get it. I think about my dad a lot, too."

I squeezed out a fake sniffle for maximum drama.

But the second we turned the corner and got out of earshot, I wanted to growl.

"Man, I am starving," Fawn complained, snapping me back to reality. My mind was all over the place today. I shook it off.

"Same here," everyone chimed in.

"That's the workout for you," I said, patting Fawn's head as I walked past. "You're building muscle and making your wolves stronger. That takes fuel. By the way, did your housing requests get approved yet?" I grabbed a tray and slid into the food line.

"Wait, what?" Daisy blinked, tossing her hair back.

"Today's moving day, remember?" I whipped out my phone and waved it. "You guys applied for the apartments. Most of them are fully furnished now. Flint was up half the night hauling furniture."

They all froze, then instantly scrambled to pull out their phones.

"Keep moving, guys," I nudged them along the line as they frantically checked their emails.

"Where is it?" Luna muttered, tapping her screen.

Right then, the cafeteria PA system buzzed to life.

"Morning, students. The housing reassignment emails just went out. You should see them in your inbox any second." Daisy’s mom’s voice sounded sickeningly sweet over the speaker. "Good luck, and have a great day!"
