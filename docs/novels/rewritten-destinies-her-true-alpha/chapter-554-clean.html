Chapter 554

I marched across the pack territory, heading straight for a half-hidden shack. It was right next to the clearing where Blair’s cheap, rushed wedding was set up. Behind me, Vince took a nasty spill. I spun around, grinning. "You good, man?"

"How?" he muttered, his voice cracking.

"How what?" I laughed, turning back to drag the whole crew forward like they were on a leash. My magic yanked them along. Even Magnus was fighting it, but he couldn't break the hold.

Vince tripped again, struggling with everything he had.

I stopped dead at the entrance and whipped around to face him. "Hey, Magnus. What happens when you mix a werewolf with a Lycan? Any guess?"

He looked totally lost. He just stared at my half-wolf face, trying to put the pieces together.

"Yeah, they get insanely strong," I said, nodding.

I glanced at Orion. *Yo, how do I turn this off?*

He flashed a quick smirk. It was gone in a second. His voice echoed in my head, warm and smooth. *Same as always, babe. Just let Umbra step back. Now that you guys mated and unlocked this form, it's gonna be a breeze. She can swap whenever she wants.*

Umbra backed off. *Nice. This'll be perfect for practice.*

"We got the power," I told them. "But here’s the kicker nobody knows." Terra pushed her way out. My skin crawled and shifted. My massive six-foot-seven frame shrank down until I was standing there in Terra’s wolf form.

"No way," he gasped, shaking like a leaf.

"Oh, way." Terra shook out her fur.

"Are you fucking kidding me right now?" Damon growled.

Terra cocked an eyebrow. "Nope. Sorry to burst your bubble, but I wasn't as dumb as you thought. Surprise."

Blair looked at Damon, totally confused. "What's going on?"

Damon fumed for a second, then burst out laughing.

"What the hell is so funny?" Blair snapped.

"We were playing checkers, and Lyra was playing chess," Damon spat, waving his arm around. "She was ten steps ahead the whole time. We caught this wolf spying on us while we were draining that kid. Locked her up right here. But she escaped."

"And that's not all," I said, shifting back to my human self. "You loved taunting me, didn't you? Came to my cell every single day just to mess with my head. And why?" I looked at Magnus. "Because your old man was obsessed with my wolf." I tilted my head. "You looked at me like I was trash." I turned back to Damon. "Which is hilarious, because Terra—my wolf—was actually your fated mate."

The room went dead silent.

"What? Hell no," Damon stuttered. "Umbra was my mate."

I cracked up. "In your dreams, buddy. You're way too weak for Umbra. Orion was always her guy. He ended up as Terra’s second chance. Thank God the Moon Goddess had a backup plan. But you? You were stuck with her first."

He looked like he wanted to puke. Magnus frowned. "Wait. She was a gamma. I felt her aura."

I nodded. "Yep, she is. Umbra is my alpha wolf. But that proves my point, doesn't it?"

"What point?" Magnus asked, squinting.

"Alphas mate with their own level so they can rule together. Damon mated a gamma, then picked some random omega with no rank." I glared at Damon. "You're pathetic. You couldn't even handle a beta. Ronnie would've wiped the floor with you. If he’d gotten his way and had a son with my mom, the Oakenfang pack would be gone by now." I turned to the door. "Whatever. Doesn't matter now." I grabbed the handle and threw the door open.

The smell hit me like a truck. Stale blood—my blood—burnt skin, and piss.

The crew walked in. My friends shivered from the stench, but Orion let out a low, angry growl the second he stepped inside. "They kept you *here*?" He stared down the row of cells. "How did I not notice this place until we were standing right in front of it?"

I grabbed the keys off the wall and nodded. "It's got a cloaking spell on it. You don't see it unless you're dragged inside. Your eyes just skip right over it." I shrugged. "I hated this place back then. But right now? It's perfect." I walked down the hall, unlocking every single cell. Then I turned to them. "Welcome to your new digs."

I shoved the guards into the cells, two by two. Easy enough, since barely any of them survived the fight. Then I threw Ronnie and Magnus into one, and shoved Damon and Blair into the last one. I slammed the heavy door shut. Blair lunged at the bars, but the silver burned her skin. She screamed. "You've got to be fucking kidding me!"

I smirked. "Dead serious. I survived it. You can too."

Blair’s face twisted in rage, but then, like a light switch, she grinned. She started laughing hysterically.

"What's so funny, psycho?" I asked.

She stepped up to the bars. "Lock us up all you want, sweetie. It doesn't change a thing. Your dad is probably dead in those caves right now. Your mom is trapped in some cabin getting ripped apart by the brotherhood. And you? You just wasted all your time playing warden here."
