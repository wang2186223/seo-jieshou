Chapter 128

Blair bounced up like she sat on a tack. "Seriously? You treat me like I'm the freaking plague!"

"Newsflash: to me and my wolf, you *are* a plague," I spat. "Clearly, your brain can't process this, so let me spell it out. You know that psycho, obsessive grip you have on Damon? Like he's your personal property?" I stopped. I locked eyes with her, watching that crazy, greedy look take over her face.

"Yeah," she snapped, her jaw tight.

"Like, if anyone even breathes near him, touches him, or throws him a look, you'd rip their throat out? Am I right?" I kept my eyes glued to her, waiting.

"Yes!"

"Well, guess what? That's how I feel about my space. My bedroom. My sanctuary. And you trashed it just to get some dick." I stood up, my blood boiling. "You're weak, you're pathetic, and you're a straight-up garbage human being. Yet you thought you could touch *my* shit? You crawled into my room, used my personal stuff—things from my mom, my dad, things that are *mine*—just to trap some guy who doesn't even want you." Umbra clawed her way to the front. My vision shifted. Thick fur sprouted down my arms. Razor claws popped out. "You have any idea what that does to my head?"

Blair's face went paper-white. She shrank back, shaking her head like a bobblehead. "I—I don't... I don't know!"

"You damn well know!" I roared. "It's the exact same way you feel about Damon!" I took a step forward. Ronnie jumped up, hands high in the air like he was getting busted. "Lyra, chill, please!"

"You're a beta, you get the whole nesting thing," I growled, turning my glare on him. "How would you feel if some nobody snuck into your room, stole your gear, and then walked around like they owned the place? Especially when they don't have a single shred of rank to their name?"

Ronnie gulped, his throat bobbing. He nodded. "Yeah. I'd lose my mind."

"Bingo!" I threw my hands up. "So go ahead. Ask me one more time why I need a dorm." Ronnie looked at me, then quickly dodged my gaze. "I said, *ask me*."

"Why... why do you need a dorm?" he mumbled.

"So I can get the hell away from your loser daughter. A place with a lock she can't pick. A place where my wolf doesn't feel cornered. So we don't end up doing something highly illegal, like tearing her throat out." My eyes flared wolf-gold. Blair whimpered and curled in on herself.

Ronnie nodded fast. "Okay. Yeah. That actually sounds like a smart move."

My mom's eyes darted between us. "Look, to keep the peace, maybe you should crash at the dorms for a bit. Just until Umbra chills out." She reached over, grabbing my hand and shaking her head. "Baby, look at you. You used to let this crap slide. Now you're right on the edge. Don't ruin your life over a nobody." Blair let out a loud, offended gasp.

"Is that cool with you, Lyra?" Ronnie asked, keeping his voice real low.

"You want me living on campus?" I glared at my mom, feeling the sting of betrayal. But she just gave me this tiny, knowing smirk. "Oh, right, 'safety.' Sure. This has absolutely nothing to do with kicking me out so you two can have the house to yourselves, right?"

My mom threw her head back and cracked up. "Oh, Lyra, please."

"It's the damn truth!" I snapped.

"Even if it were true," my mom said, yanking me back down into my seat, "it wouldn't matter. That ship has sailed, honey." She turned her cold gaze to Ronnie. I watched the color and hope drain straight out of his face. "I don't know how many times I have to beat this into your thick skulls. I am not your wife. I am not your stepmom. I am not your personal chef, and I am sure as hell not your maid. We share a roof, and that is the end of the story."

"Elara, come on..." Ronnie took a step toward her, but she let out a low, warning growl that stopped him cold.

"Fine," I grunted, slumping back into my chair. "I'll take the dorm."

"Fine," Ronnie muttered, sinking back down next to Blair.

"Am I free to go?"

Ronnie started to nod, but Blair grabbed his arm like a leech. "Daddy! What about the food card?"

He let out a heavy sigh, rolling his eyes. "Blair, seriously, cut it out." He looked at her, but she just lost it, leaping to her feet and screaming.

"Look at her!" She pointed a shaking finger right at my face. "She gets every single thing! The rank, the cash, the hot alphas. Everything! And what do I get? Zip! Nada!" she shrieked. "She has a hundred grand loaded onto a freaking meal card! Are you out of your mind?!" She spun on me, eyes wild. "Why the hell do you even need that much cash anyway?"

I stared her dead in the eye and gave a lazy shrug. "I don't know. Might want to buy a snack."

"My card is literally empty!" she wailed, bursting into ugly tears.

"Actually, that's a lie." I leaned in close, a nasty smirk on my face. "I just dropped four million bucks to make sure every single student who signed up has at least ten grand on theirs. Guess you missed the memo."
