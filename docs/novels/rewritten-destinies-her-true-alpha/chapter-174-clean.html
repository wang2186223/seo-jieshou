Chapter 174

"I still don't get it. Why?"

"Because they need to think you're totally on your own." Orion kept massaging my feet. God, it felt incredible. But I had to focus. I yanked my feet back and leaned right into his space.

I grabbed his face with both hands and squeezed his cheeks. "Cut the mysterious bullshit and talk to me. Why the hell do I have to play dead-dad?"

Orion froze. I swear he stopped breathing when I got that close. "Because," he muttered, "we want them thinking you've got nobody but your mom. She’s spent years playing the weak, helpless victim to Magnus and Ronnie. It fits our play perfectly."

"So, you gonna lay out the actual plan or what?" I was losing my patience.

"You gonna keep manhandling my face while I do?" His voice dropped to a low growl. His eyes darted from my eyes down to my nose, then back. I ripped my hands away like I’d touched a hot stove and scrambled back.

"My bad." My face burned hot. He just let out a low chuckle.

"I don't mind it," he said, throwing me a wink. "But hey, grab a mint next time you get that close."

A sudden, sharp heat flared straight between my thighs. The realization hit me like a freight train.

Damn. I wanted him. Bad. I was totally hooked on Orion, and there wasn't a damn thing I could do to stop it.

Then the cold truth punched me right in the gut. Sooner or later, he’d mate. He’d marry some other she-wolf. And it wouldn't be me.

Umbra and Terra instantly surged up, trying to soothe the ache in my chest.

*It’s gonna be fine,* Terra whispered, smoothing over the panic in my mind.

*Hey, we still got Shadow,* Umbra piped up, sounding way too cheerful. I couldn't help but snort.

"What's so funny?" Orion tilted his head, making my face burn all over again.

"Nothing. Just my wolf being weird," I muttered, brushing it off. "Back to the plan?"

"Right." Orion sat up straight and yanked his shirt flat. "Step one. You're moving to the apartment complex."

"Why?"

"We need you isolated. Plus, you need a secure spot where nobody can just walk in." Orion pulled out a key and jingled it right in front of my face. "I'm not the only one with a master key. Magnus has one too."

"Wait, what?"

"It was a safety thing. But now? Knowing Magnus and Damon can just stroll in and touch your stuff? It’s driving your dad and me crazy."

"I have a lockbox for my important gear."

"Those cheap boxes don't mean shit." Orion’s nostrils flared. "Besides, I caught another wolf's scent when I slipped in here. Someone's already been snooping. It’s faint, so it happened a while ago, but still."

Umbra growled in my head. "Someone was in my room?"

Orion nodded. "Doesn't matter now. You're packing. You move to the apartments tonight."

"I don't even have enough furniture for a one-bedroom."

Orion scratched his head. "Actually, scratch that. Knowing someone was in here... yeah, my wolf is losing his mind. You're moving into the other penthouse. That way, when I'm around, we're close."

My eyes popped. I scrambled up onto my knees. "Are you serious? You said that place was staying empty!"

"It was. But my wolf won't let me put you anywhere else now." He shrugged. "I don't argue with him."

I nodded. "Yeah. Fair enough. Most of us don't."

"So, you're safe in the penthouse. But at school? In class? I need you acting totally shut out. Lonely. We need the rumor mill saying Silvermoon is gone and you're easy prey."

"Why?"

"Because bastards like Magnus think women are useless without a man. They see a girl on her own, they see weak." He locked eyes with me. "The second he thinks you're weak, he drops his guard. That's your opening to strike."

It clicked. My dad was playing dead to give me room. Room to get close and dig up the truth.

"Got it."

"Play it smart," Orion said, turning fully toward me. "Don't let them get too close too fast. These next few years are gonna be rough. You're gonna be a major target."

"Targeted by who?"

Inside my head, Umbra and Terra stood tall. Let them try. We could handle whatever came.

"The council. And whatever women they try to 'match' us with. No matter what I tell them, they think we're a thing. That puts a bullseye on your back." Orion rubbed his face, looking exhausted. "And then there's the rogues. They're creeping closer to the school. I've got guards patrolling the woods, though, so you should still be safe to go for runs."

"So... I'm a target just because we're friends."

"Pretty much." Orion pulled out another key and dropped it into my palm. "This is for the penthouse on the right. Pack your bags. You're moving tonight. Got it?"

He stood up, opened his mouth to say something else, then just shook his head and walked out.
