Chapter 107

I flopped down beside her. Hard. "Say what now?"

My mom smirked. "My mom? She was the last pure Lycan around, minus the royal family. Me? I'm a mix. Mostly Lycan. It’s a family secret, just like your dad's magic." She squinted at me. "You're kind of a mutt, sweetie. Not sure you've got enough juice to even have one."

"Have a what? A Lycan?"

"Yep. Purebreds get the giant wolf and the half-man thing. But us mixed ones? We get a double deal. Two wolves."

"Two? Like, seriously?"

"Uh-huh. One Lycan wolf that can morph into different sizes, and one regular wolf. You get both."

To prove it, mom just went for it. *Bam.* She turned into this massive brown beast. Then, *pop*, she was a smaller grey wolf. A second later, she was back to her human self, kneeling on the floor and catching her breath. "Plus, we keep our clothes on. Lycan perk. When people started hunting us, we spread a rumor that any strong wolf could do it. Total lie."

"So... I'm a Lycan?"

"Partly. But it doesn't count unless you can pull off both forms. Well? You got two in there?"

I didn't say a word. I just let Umbra take the wheel. The shift was getting way faster now. Once Umbra was fully out, I tapped Terra. *You ready for this?*

*For real?*

I laughed inside. *Hell yeah. Just for a sec, but a promise is a promise.*

The next swap dragged. Black fur faded to grey. My bones cracked and shrunk. A minute later, we were sitting there, shaking like a leaf.

"Well, who do we have here?" Mom's eyes lit up, reflecting the fire.

"Terra..." She finally stood up. We were tiny. No bigger than a standard wolf, maybe even smaller. "I'm Terra."

"Gorgeous name for a gorgeous girl." Mom stroked her fur. "You're her regular wolf, huh?" Terra gave a little nod.

"I'm just a Gamma."

"Hey, that's plenty high for a Luna." Mom smiled and tapped Terra's nose. "Don't you dare feel second-rate just because her Lycan is stronger. My wolf doesn't even have a rank, and I love her to death. And I know Lyra loves you just as much."

"She does," Terra preened, soaking up the love. "She's listened to me ever since she found me."

"Found you? What's that mean?"

My turn to talk. I pushed back to the front.

I shifted back to human, plopped down, and gasped for air. "Jesus. Shifting back-to-back like that is pure torture." I scanned the room. "We safe to talk here?"

She nodded. "My dad built this cabin so my mom could raise me in secret. Trust me, we had plenty of secrets to keep."

I took a massive breath. "Mom. Time for some real talk."

The kettle shrieked. I jumped, but mom just grabbed it, pouring hot water into two mugs. She threw in some tea bags. I stared at her, raising an eyebrow.

"I restock this place every couple of months," she shrugged. "I hide out here whenever I can. So, what's the big secret?"

"You know dad's grandma was a witch, right? A human with actual magic?"

"Yeah. Your dad told me. She sounded like a total badass." Mom handed me a mug and gestured for me to spill.

"Well, dad left out a major detail. If the Moon Goddess blesses us, we get to keep those magic powers in our wolf forms."

Mom froze. "Your dad had magic?"

"Yep."

"Like, what kind of magic?" She took a sip of her tea, watching me.

"Anything, really. Crazy stuff." I stared into the flames. "Like masking your scent. Or making someone fall head over heels for someone else."

"Can it fake the pain of a broken mate bond?" her voice went barely above a whisper.

I couldn't look her in the eye. "Yeah."

She gasped, the sound sharp in the quiet cabin.

"Why the hell would he do that?" Her hands were shaking now.

I snatched her mug and set it flat on the floor. "Because of what our bloodline does to us."

"Which is?"

"Remember my birthday? How I woke up acting completely psycho?"

"Yeah..."

"I lived a whole other life, Mom. I made it to twenty-four. I was mated to Damon. I was pregnant with his kid. Then Blair framed me for cheating, and Damon killed me and my baby. Next thing I know, I wake up six years in the past, hell-bent on rewriting history. And then I saw you, alive and breathing, and I just lost my damn mind."
