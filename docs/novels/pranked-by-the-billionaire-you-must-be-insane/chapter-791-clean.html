Chapter 799

Vivienne’s heart nearly stopped.

Then she snorted. Please. Raven Thorne was just some trailer-trash hick from the same dirt-poor town as Lyra. A bunch of literal nobodies. Like, had they ever even seen a real gun? Why the hell would they have one? Yeah, she was definitely seeing things.

Vivienne backed away from the pile of bags Lyra's friends left behind. She slid onto the vanity stool, acting totally chill.

Right then, a few rich girls pushed the door open. The second they saw Vivienne, their chatter died. They didn't even try to hide the disgust on their faces.

Just an hour ago, before Lyra showed up, Vivienne was the IT girl. Everyone was kissing her ass. Now? She felt like she was suffocating.

She bit her lip, took a breath, and forced a bright smile. "Hey, guys."

Nothing. They just stared at her like she was some kind of freak, smirking.

They brushed right past her, totally ignoring her, and crowded around the vanity on the other side. They giggled, touching up their lipstick, freezing her out.

Vivienne’s fake smile cracked. Her cheeks burned. God, she wanted to kill them. She balled her hands into fists, spun around, and stormed out of the dressing room.

*Just hold it together,* she thought. *A little longer.* Soon, Lyra would be the laughingstock of this whole damn party. Not her.

Vivienne gripped her hands tight and walked back into the ballroom. She grabbed a glass of champagne. Her fingers shook, but her eyes locked onto Lyra. The Vintors were crowding around Lyra like she was the center of the universe. Elara Royal stood right there, grinning and chatting her up.

Vivienne threw her head back and downed the champagne in one gulp. A nasty smirk crept onto her face. *Keep smiling, Lyra. Enjoy it while it lasts. In five minutes, you’re going to be crying.*

It didn't take long.

Suddenly, things got chaotic near Elara. One of the princess's assistants rushed over, white as a sheet, and whispered in her ear. Elara’s smile vanished. Her brow furrowed in pure anger.

She turned to Julian Blackwood and hissed something. The Vintor family’s faces went totally dark. They waved at the band to cut the music.

Standing a few feet away, Vivienne watched it all go down. She couldn't stop the nasty grin spreading across her face. Looks like Elara finally noticed the Heart of the Sea was gone. Showtime.

Julian Blackwood marched onto the stage and grabbed the mic. His voice was ice. "Listen up, everyone. Sorry to kill the vibe, but we have a situation. The Heart of the Sea—the gift Her Highness Elara Royal gave to my niece, Lyra—is gone."

"It’s a royal treasure. Priceless." He scanned the crowd like a hawk. "So, we need everyone to cooperate. The Vintor family and the royal guards are doing a full sweep."

The room erupted.

"Wait, what? Someone stole the princess's gift?"

"The Heart of the Sea? Like, the actual national treasure?"

"Who has the balls to pull that off here? Stealing from the princess?!"

The rich crowd went into total shock. This wasn't just some lost necklace. It dragged the Vintor name through the mud, and it threatened the whole political alliance between the Vintons and the Royal House of Vaelion.

Reginald’s face turned black with rage. "Lock the doors!" he roared. "Nobody leaves!"
