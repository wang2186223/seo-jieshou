Chapter 16

I let my words hang in the air. Let them sting. "What, shocked? Hey, your words, not mine. I can't make you hurt yourself. Fine. Let's try a different game. How about I order you to hand over every single dime you own?" I glared at her. She couldn't even look me in the eye. "Oh, so you've actually done that, huh?" I growled, eyes burning. "Name. Now."

"Chloe," she whispered.

Boom. I let my aura explode. Umbra took the wheel, pure fury driving us. "Jumping jacks. Now. Don't stop till I say so." Chloe's eyes went wide as saucers. I spun around, trying to lock my wolf back in its cage. Chloe started hopping. A few girls in the back snickered. I lost it. "You think this is a joke? Huh? Want a piece of this? Try being trapped in your own body, forced to move. It sucks, doesn't it?" I snapped my head back toward them, Umbra roaring inside me. I slammed my power down. Hard. Everyone hit the dirt, knees cracking against the floor. "Every single one of you. Start jumping. Now."

They all scrambled up and started bouncing. Even Astrid and Freya. "Not you two. Stop," I barked.

Freya panted, looking at me. "What's the play here?"

"We're weeding them out," I said. "We go down the line. Ask them if they ever used their power to force another wolf to do shit. They say no? They get to sit. They say yes? They keep jumping."

Astrid gave a quick nod. "And if they're guilty? How long do they go?"

"Until I feel like stopping them." I sneered, walking straight toward Skylar. "You ever force anyone to do something they didn't want to?"

Skylar was huffing, face bright red. "No," she gasped out.

I grinned. "You're done. Stop." I flicked a bit of my energy to break the spell and patted her back. "Hang in there. It gets easier." I kept moving.

The three of us worked the room, letting girl after girl off the hook. Pretty soon, only a tiny group was left, clustered right around Briar and Morgana. Figures. No surprises there.

I marched back to the front and spun around. Forty women were scattered across the room. Some were bent double, hands on knees, sucking in air. But eight of them were still sweating bullets, jumping up and down. "The rest of you, beat it," I yelled. "We're doing this again tomorrow. Eat some damn steak tonight. See ya." They scrambled out the door, leaving only the jumpers behind.

"Want us to stick around?" Freya asked.

I nodded. "Yeah. Stay. They're gonna collapse soon, and I want witnesses." I stood there, arms crossed, watching them wobble. Their limbs were shaking like jelly.

"Cut it out!" Morgana yelled.

I let out a dry laugh. "Or what, sweetheart?"

"Please..." Briar whimpered, completely spent.

I marched right up to them, getting dead in Briar's face. "Oh, you tired? Feeling weak? Want to quit?"

"Yes," Briar gasped, sweating.

"And what about the girls you forced?" I snapped. "You think they wanted to do your dirty work? You think they wanted to hand over their cash? Do your chores? Run your errands? You think they didn't have their own damn lives to live?"

Tears started rolling down Briar's face. Her lip was shaking. "No..."

"Then why the hell should I stop?" I growled. "You guys didn't stop. Why is the council good enough to run to when you need protection, but you can't follow their damn rules?"

"I don't know!" she wailed.

"Oh, so you're just above the law, then?"

"No!" she shrieked.

"Then who made you queen? Who gave you the right to boss people around?"

"Nobody!" She was trembling violently now.

"Then why'd you do it?" I waited. Nothing but silence. "Why did any of you idiots think it was okay to play god with other wolves?"

"We just—"

"Shut your mouth!" Morgana snapped.

I shot her a deadly look. "You don't call the shots anymore, sweetie." She tried to snarl back, but she had zero bite left.

Huh. An idea hit me. Why not try it? I slammed a wave of pure power right into them. They shrieked but kept bouncing like puppets. "Stop." Boom. They hit the floor in a heap, gasping for air. One girl scrambled to the corner and started puking her guts out. "Listen up, and listen good," I barked. "I command you: never, ever use your voice to control another wolf again."

"No! That's not fair!" Morgana screamed. "I'm gonna be Luna!"

"You're never gonna be Luna," I spat. "And even if by some miracle you do, you won't be commanding a damn soul. You proved you can't handle that kind of power." I turned on my heel and walked over to Astrid and Freya. "Get them out of here. Call the pack doc if any of them are dying. But make sure every single one of them is back here tomorrow." They nodded. I walked out without looking back.
