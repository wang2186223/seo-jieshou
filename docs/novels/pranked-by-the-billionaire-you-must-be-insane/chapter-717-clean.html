Chapter 724

That voice. Holy shit, that voice.

Four years had passed, but the second it hit his ears, cold sweat broke out all over his skin.

*No way,* he told himself, chest tightening. *No fucking way.* She was dead. Buried. Gone. It had to be a sick joke.

Then came a soft, low giggle from the speaker. It sent a shiver straight down his spine.

"Who am I, you ask?" The girl laughed again, a dry, mocking sound. "Guess my knife didn't go deep enough last time. You healed up and forgot real quick, huh?"

*Smack.*

The phone slipped right out of Fletcher’s hand, clattering onto the floor. Every drop of blood rushed out of his face. He hit the deck, knees slamming into the ground.

"D-Demon..." he choked out. "You... you're alive? No, damn it, no! You died! How the hell are you breathing?!" His voice cracked, rising into a pathetic, high-pitched shriek.

Silas froze, his eyes nearly popping out of his skull. He locked eyes with Lyra, totally paralyzed.

Holy hell. Demon? *The* Demon? The ghost story of the underworld? The psycho who single-handedly painted the entire Gray Zone red and made every mob boss wet their pants?

He remembered when the Phantom Hornets were big-time. They made the dumb mistake of hijacking a Ghost Alliance shipment. Demon rolled up to their base solo, torched the whole damn building, and almost ripped their leader's guts out.

And his sweet little niece was *that* monster?

Silas stared at the quiet, innocent-looking girl sitting right there. He gulped, his throat dry as sand. "Lyra... how old are you, exactly?"

Lyra just cocked an eyebrow, giving him a look that said, *Are you stupid?*

Silas felt his brain short-circuit. She was twenty. Do the math. That meant before she could even legally drive, she was already Skye, the queen of the racetrack... and the terrifying boss of the Ghost Alliance.

This was insane. He wanted to punch himself in the face just to wake up from this trip.

But then, the puzzle pieces clicked. Everything made sense now. No wonder she could kick his ass in a fight. No wonder she snatched stuff from him like he was a toddler. No wonder the Ghost Alliance was tearing the city apart for that damn chip. No wonder she treated the Hornets like annoying bugs.

Lyra flicked her eyes toward him, her lips curling into a tiny smirk, then looked back down. "So, you do remember," she said into the line.

Heavy, panicked wheezing rattled through the speaker.

Lyra let out a soft chuckle. "A few years of peace and you got balls, Fletcher. Stealing my chip? Putting your hands on my guy?"

"I-I didn't know! I swear!" Fletcher was sobbing now, his tough-guy act completely shattered. "It's a mistake! I had no clue Caspian was yours! If I knew, I wouldn't have touched him, I swear to God! Please, Lord Demon, spare me! I'll let him go! Right now, he's free!"

He was rambling like a lunatic. Hell, if he knew Caspian had Demon's backing, he would've rather picked a fight with the US military.

"Too late," Lyra cut in, her voice dropping to sub-zero. "You reached for the Vintons. Now I have to chop your hands off."
