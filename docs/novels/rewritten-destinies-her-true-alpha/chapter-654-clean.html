Chapter 619

Caspian locked eyes with her, dead silent. Then, his lips pulled back, baring bloody fangs as he lunged straight for our faces. "Bullshit!" he snarled, voice scraping like gravel. "You're not the Celestia! You're just pulling some cheap magic trick to make me see Phoebe!"

The Celestia just smirked, casually raising a hand. "Oh, really? Why's that? Because I'm not some sweet, pushover god who forgives every nasty thing you do? I can't be her just because I'm pissed you turned your back on me?" She let out a sharp, mocking laugh. "Hey, I can prove it right now if you want."

"Moon Celestia..." my mom gasped, her voice barely a whisper. The goddess snapped her gaze over. "They... they always told us you guys couldn't interfere with us."

Another chuckle rattled out of my own throat. "Oh, I can't. But check out this loophole. I'm not doing a damn thing. Lyra's the one pulling the strings here. I'm just sitting back, grabbing popcorn, and watching."

Suddenly, a shadow peeled itself right off the wall. Everyone sucked in a breath. The woman's face was a blur, just a shifting, creepy mass of darkness. "Who the hell is that?!" Caspian shrieked, totally losing his cool at the sight of her.

"Sister." I felt the Celestia's muscles lock up inside me. "What are you doing here?" Her voice went dead cold and cautious.

The Three-faced Celestia took a step forward. "Felt your rage. Had to see it for myself." The cloaked woman tilted her head, her face swirling. "So, what's the play here, sis?"

Phoebe let out a heavy sigh. "Just watching."

The Three-faced Celestia snorted. "Oh, please," she scoffed. "You call this watching?"

"Morrigan, you know damn well what these bastards did." Phoebe's voice dripped with pure venom.

The Three-faced Celestia—Morrigan—nodded. "Yeah. I know."

"You gonna stop me?" Phoebe challenged.

Morrigan paused, chewing on it for a second, then shook her head. "Nah, sis. But you can't get your hands dirty. You want to rip his wolf out and shred that bond? Fine. Have your champion use her own magic, or that spear, to do your dirty work."

A flash of hot anger flared inside me, then cooled down just as fast. "Will you back her up?" The question caught Morrigan totally off guard.

Another beat of silence. Then, she yanked back her hood. Even with her face constantly shifting, she was gorgeous. Maiden, Mother, Crone—flipping like a deck of cards. "If she lets me in, I'll show her the way."

"Let who in?" My dad stepped away from the wall, eyes narrow.

Both goddesses whipped around to face him. "You're a smart man," Phoebe said, her tone sharp but surprisingly soft. She smiled. "I get it, you're the protective dad. But she's a grown-up now." Dad opened his mouth to argue, but the goddess shut him down with a raised hand. Then, she turned her focus deep inside my head. "You down for this?"

I looked at my two wolves to see where their heads were at. They just stared right back. Umbra nudged my hand. *Look, the Moon Celestia is already wired into us. She's got our green light. But Morrigan? She's your magic. You gotta sign off on her. Not us.*

Terra gave a firm nod. *Your call, girl. But honestly? We already let Phoebe crash here. What's one more goddess in the roommate pile? As long as she doesn't try to screw us over, I say go for it.*

I nodded, turning my focus back to the goddess waiting for my answer. *I'm in. She gave me her blessing anyway. If this is what it takes to keep our home safe and make things right, let's do it.*

"Good girl," Phoebe smiled. Then, she yanked herself out of me. One second she was riding shotgun in my head, the next she was standing right next to Morrigan. She tossed her hair back, took a deep breath, and stepped right up to Caspian's face. "Corvus, I'm cursing you. Every time you're reborn, you're going to be half a soul. I'm ripping your wolf out. He's going to face trial, and if he helped you play these dirty games, I'm eating his soul."

Caspian stared at her, panicked. "What the hell is that supposed to mean?!"

"It means," she paused, letting the words sink in, "that in every single life you live after this, you'll feel empty. You'll spend eternity with a gaping hole in your chest. No peace, no real happiness, and zero chance of ever finding true love." Caspian choked, gagging on his own breath. "And as for your wolf? I'm judging him the second we get back to my turf. If his soul is poisoned by your crap, I'm absorbing him. He's done. No more lives, no more running wild. I'll dump his power into a brand-new soul so it doesn't go to waste, but the guy he's been for the last seven lifetimes? Gone. Erased."

My jaw hit the floor. "Wait, our wolves have lived before?"

The Moon Celestia flashed me a grin. "Duh. Where do you think mate bonds come from? I don't just hand-pick them every lifetime. Your wolves find their way back to their matches. Shadow and Umbra? They've been riding together for over seventy lifetimes. Same goes for your parents' wolves."

"What about Terra and Kurt?" I blurted out.

She sighed. "They're kind of newbies, but yeah, same deal." She looked down at Caspian, her eyes cold. "Karma's a bitch, huh?"

Morrigan walked over to me. "So, what's the verdict?"

"I consent."

"You sure you know what you're signing up for, kiddo?" Her face shifted into the Mother—warm, caring, totally comforting.

"Yeah. I'm sure." I nodded, closing my eyes, waiting for her to slide into my mind just like Phoebe did. I took a deep breath—but instead of air, my lungs hit a brick wall. My eyes snapped open. I was choking, gagging on thick, pulsing green smoke.

Umbra scrambled to her feet, baring her teeth. *What the actual fuck is that?!*
