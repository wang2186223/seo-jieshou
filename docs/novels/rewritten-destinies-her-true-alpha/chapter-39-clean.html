Chapter 39

"Who says you gotta choose?" I shook my head. "Sure, my dad's pack is short on female fighters right now. But that's changing, fast. You won't be the only girl lining up. And trust me, badass chicks can still have a life. Husbands, babies, the whole deal."

"Yeah, I get it. But I don't wanna be forced into a corner." Elara stared hard at her plate, picking at her food.

"A real mate? A good one? He won't make you choose jack." I patted her hand, then shoved the last bite of steak into my mouth. "The Moon Goddess doesn't screw up. Your fated mate is literally your other half. He’ll back you up. He'll want you out there fighting."

"Seriously? You really think so?" Her eyes lit up, desperate for hope.

"Hell yeah." I scraped my chair back, heading straight for the buffet. My stomach was a black hole.

*Your body is gearing up for the first shift,* Umbra’s voice echoed in my head. *Get used to the pig-out. Alphas burn through fuel like crazy. We shift more, we need more.* I gave a silent nod.

"Damn, girl. You're packing it away," Elara said, watching me sit back down with a mountain of food.

"Umbra says it's pre-shift prep. Alphas have massive wolves, so we burn calories like crazy. Gotta keep the engine running." I demolished my second plate in record time, then looked at Elara's clean plate. "Ready to roll?"

"Yep." We stood up, spun around to leave, and—*bam*. Elara slammed right into Kaelen. The second gamma.

"Oh! Kaelen, my bad!" Elara's face turned instant tomato-red.

Kaelen just let out a low chuckle. "No worries, little wolf. Always a treat running into you." He let his fingers slide slowly down her bare arms before stepping back. Elara visibly shuddered under his touch, gave a frantic nod, and practically bolted for the exit.

"Okay, what the hell was that?" I jogged to catch up with her. "'Little wolf'?"

"Nothing!" she practically shrieked. "Seriously, it's nothing! He just says it to tease me."

"Uh-huh, sure. Then why did you just pull a Usain Bolt?" I pressed. She shook her head, refusing to look at me. The pieces clicked. "Oh my god. Elara. You are totally crushing on him."

"Shut up! Shut your mouth right now!"

"Why? It's fine! You like a guy, big deal. What's the issue?" I grabbed her elbow, dragging her along.

"He's the second gamma, Lyra!" she blurted out.

"So? And?" I looked at her, seeing the instant misery in her eyes.

"You don't get it. We can never happen. Period."

"Why the hell not?" I yanked her to a halt, forcing her to look me in the eye.

"Because I'm unranked!"

I waved my hand in front of her face. "Yeah, no. You're gonna have to do better than that, Elara."

"Ranked wolves do not mate with nobody unrankeds!" She stomped her foot like a frustrated kid.

I let out a heavy sigh. "That's literally a myth, Elara. It happens all the time. Sure, there are rejections, but usually, it's high-ranking females dumping low-ranking guys."

"For real?"

"Yes, for real! Plus, you don't even know if he's your mate, let alone if he'd dump you. You're totally overthinking this. Why not just be friends first instead of ghosting him? Imagine if the Goddess actually paired you up, and instead of showing him how badass you are, you just keep running away like a scared rabbit." I flicked her forehead.

"Lyra, seriously. Guys like him aren't in my league."

"You don't know that. Just try. Even if it's just to hang out. Kaelen is a great guy to have in your corner. He runs my dad's warrior unit. You want in? He's the guy who can make it happen."

Elara ran her hands through her hair, tugging at the roots in frustration. "Fine. I'll try."

"Attagirl." I started walking again, spinning around to face her so I could walk backward. "And hey... he is seriously hot."

"Shut your face!" Elara shoved my shoulder, and I cracked up.

"What? Just stating facts. The guy is total eye candy."

She blushed. "Yeah... okay, he is."

I caught a glimpse of someone walking toward us and smirked. "Any girl would be lucky to have him as a mate."

"Totally. God, Lyra... am I just being an idiot? Give it to me straight. Would the Moon Goddess actually pair a nobody like me with him? And even if she did, would he even want to keep me? I'm literally a nobody."

"Elara, listen—"

"No, don't just reassure me. Think about it. He's elite, I'm dirt. Yeah, you said it's usually the girls doing the rejecting, but he could still throw me away, right? The odds are totally against me."

My eyes darted right over her shoulder. "Sure, there's a chance. But maybe start by actually talking to him? Who says he'd dump you if you guys were fated?"

"I'm just scared to death. He deserves way better than me."

"Who says?" a deep, raspy voice rumbled right behind us.

Elara went completely stiff.
