Chapter 273

My grandma shoved me flat onto the dirt. I stared up at her, frantic. "How do I find her? Where's your cabin? Are we even close?" The words just spilled out. She just smiled, sitting down right next to me.

She grabbed my hands. "Chill, girl. Look at me." She took a deep breath through her nose and let it out. "Do what I do. Match me. Get your head straight, then we find your girl." *My girl.* The words felt weird, but then they hit me deep. She was right. She was mine. Just as much as she was Carl's. I locked eyes with Grandma, matching her breathing. She nodded. "Good. Now close your eyes. Picture the kid."

I shut my eyes. Boom—there she was. The giggly little kid from Ronnie's shop. Tiny, bright, blonde curls bouncing in the light. I remembered how protective Ronnie was. How innocent she was. Then the panic hit. Finding her on the ground. Pale. Eyes wide. Cold as ice.

I remembered holding her. Lifting that tiny body up. She started shaking, and I freaked. I didn't know jack about kids—I'd just lost my own pup, just got reborn. I was clueless, but I knew one thing: I was going to keep her safe from Magnus and Damon.

"Good," Grandma whispered, her voice smooth. "Now, her face." I saw it. Blonde pigtails, chocolate-brown eyes, peanut butter smeared all over her face as she bounced out of the back room. "There she is," Grandma said. Somehow, she was seeing inside my head. "Look at her. Gorgeous, right? Our girl." I smiled. Damn right she was. I'd die for her. "Keep focusing on her face," Grandma said. "And that urge to protect her. She's in deep, and she won't make it if we don't get there fast."

Panic clawed at my chest. I slammed it down. Luna was in danger. Focus. I took a few more deep breaths and brought her face back. "What's next?"

"You tell me," she said. "What do you feel?"

I focused on Luna. Her voice, her pain, her sheer terror. I let go of Grandma's hand and shoved my own straight into the dirt of my magic. Usually, I don't need to touch the source to use it, but right now? I needed this. I dug my fingers deep into my power well, reached way down, and shot it out like a bullet straight toward Luna.

In my head, I watched it fly. It zipped past the trees Umbra was dodging, and then—bam. A jerk. The magic hooked a hard turn. My eyes snapped open. Grandma was gone. Terra was panting next to me.

"Terra?"

"I'm wiped," she gasped. I scrambled up, took the wheel, and let Umbra and Terra rest.

"Guide the magic. I'll run." I dug my claws in, pushed myself to the absolute limit, and chased the magic.

"Umbra?" Juniper called out, taking over for her wolf.

"Lyra," I yelled back. "I know you're beat. But push! We're almost there!" The crew dug in and followed. Shadow was right on my ass, quiet as a ghost.

The magic hooked right. I banked hard, everyone right behind me. I could hear them wheezing. God, I felt like crap making them do this. But then, the air changed. Just a hint at first, then—boom. A few minutes later, it hit hard.

"Salt? Is that the ocean?" Daisy gasped, sounding like she was gonna cry from relief.

"Yeah," Shadow grunted. He looked at me. I ignored his hurt face. No damn time for this. He gunned it, pulled up right next to me, crowding me. I gave him a low growl. He backed off. "We save the kid, then we talk, okay?"

I glared at him. "Are you freaking kidding me? We're running to save a little girl and you want to chat?" I shook my head. I was about to trash-talk him more, but a massive roar tore through the air.

Another roar hit. My stomach dropped. "Lyra!" someone yelled. I didn't care. I pushed harder. I leaped right through the last bunch of trees and charged toward a gorgeous cabin on a hill I knew. The air reeked of blood.

"No!" I screamed. Ten rogues were circling a giant. He was barely hanging on. Ronnie was down on his knees, blood gushing from his shoulder.

"Lyra," he croaked. Weak, but his eyes found mine, and he looked so damn relieved.
