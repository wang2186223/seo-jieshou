Chapter 225

"So, what's the play?"

"Hell if I know." I shrugged. "But look, if I could track you guys down in this life, I can damn sure find you in the spirit world." I forced a weak smile. "Seriously. Elara’s been my only real friend, like a sister. We clicked the second we met. But you two? I felt the exact same way. The second you slid into my booth at lunch, we locked in. Soul-deep, right from the start."

They both nodded.

"Our wolves are in sync." Juniper shut her eyes. When they snapped open, her wolf was staring right at me. "You're my Luna."

I flinched. "Whoa, hold on. No way. You're a badass Luna on your own."

"Doesn't matter. If you told me to run into the wild right now and never look back, I'd do it." The wolf-glow faded, and Juniper blinked, totally in agreement with her beast. "Yeah. What she said."

"Same here." I whipped my head toward Daisy, but it was Wyn's eyes locking onto mine. "You're my Luna, my Alpha, my ride-or-die." Wyn slipped back under, leaving Daisy staring at me. I stood there, totally speechless. "We're all in, Lyra. No doubts."

"And we know the drill." Juniper sat on my left, Daisy took my right. "Do your thing. We've got your back. We'll pull you back."

I took a breath and dragged the metal bowl closer. "Alright. Let's do this."

"Wait!" Daisy bolted. She ran to the kitchen while we just sat there, totally lost. She sprinted back holding a plate. "For the candles. To catch the wax."

"Ah, got it." I slid the plate under the bowl. Daisy grabbed a spare candle, lit it, and dripped hot wax onto the plate. She jammed the white, black, and purple candles right into the puddles. "Boom. Locked down. No mess on the floor." She dusted off her hands.

God, I wanted to smack myself. "Daisy, you're a genius." Juniper beamed at her, and Daisy actually blushed. Huh. Noted.

But I had to focus. "Okay, game time." I sparked the candles and held the herbs over the flame until they charred. I shredded some paper, tossed the scraps into the smoking bowl, and watched them catch fire. "Alright, everything's in."

"You ready?"

I took a deep breath, inhaling the herbal smoke. Then, a thought hit me. "Hey, can you grab me a quick glass of water?"

Juniper nodded and dashed off. She came back with a glass. I dumped in some sea salt and poured in half a bottle of distilled water.

"What's the plan with that?"

I chugged the salty mix, gagging as my stomach tried to throw it right back up. "Sea salt is for purifying, right? Figured I'd detox my body from the inside out." I shrugged. "Worth a shot." I tossed a few more herbs into the bowl and shut my eyes. "*Ifta nota wal spiritian flo sta bint tor fa shen.*" I opened my eyes, dropped the last basil leaves into the flames, and chanted, "*Agin torn thista dor.*" I said it three times. Then, I waited. And waited.

Nothing. Not a damn thing. I shook my head and looked at Juniper. "Well, that was a bust." I stood up, frustrated, stepped right over the altar, and threw my hands on my hips. "I don't get it. What did I mess up?"

But Juniper and Daisy weren't looking at me. They were staring at the floor. I spun around. Holy crap. My body was still sitting there, hands on knees, eyes shut tight. But here I was, standing right in front of the bowl.

It worked. I was out of my shell, walking around as a ghost.

"Keep your head in the game, kiddo." I whipped around. My grandmother was sitting right in my favorite chair.

"Grandma!" I ran over. She smiled, stood up, and cupped my face. And damn, I could actually feel her hands. "Oh my god, you're real." I threw my arms around her, our souls slamming together.

"Focus, Lyra. Lose your grip now, and you're dead for real." She squeezed me tight, kissed my forehead, and spun me back toward my empty shell. "Don't get distracted by the spirit world. Stay on the path. Follow the magic."

I stared at my own body. A thin, pitch-black chain was anchored right in my chest, stretching out into the dark.

"Save your wolves, or die."
