Still, scattered among the mindless mob, a few rational voices refused to be swept up by the tide.

“That photo’s so blurry it could be anyone. If I’m not wrong, they were at L’Auréole, a Michelin three-star restaurant. Places like that have crystal-clear surveillance. If they release the footage, this whole mess will be sorted out. @L’Auréole, please show us the truth!”

Before long, waves of people began tagging L’Auréole, demanding that the restaurant release its security footage.

Meanwhile, Maia moved through her apartment with steady calm, walking to the fridge and pouring herself a glass of water. As she took a sip, her phone chimed softly with a WhatsApp notification. It was from Pattie. “Why are you trending again?! Who’s behind it this time? The Morgan family? Maia, answer me already!”

Before Maia could respond, her phone buzzed with an incoming call. Pattie’s voice burst through the speaker, sharp with barely contained anger. “What the hell is this mess?!” Pattie fumed. “The photo’s a blurry joke, taken at some creepy angle, and people are treating it like it’s hard proof? Have they all lost their minds?”

“Don’t waste your energy,” Maia said, her tone steady, almost indifferent. “They’re still pressing the restaurant to release the surveillance footage, aren’t they?”

As she spoke, a push notification slid across the top of her screen.

Pattie’s voice pitched higher, rattling Maia’s ear. “Maia, check it now! L’Auréole just released a statement. They said the surveillance footage was intentionally sabotaged and can’t be recovered!”

Maia’s hand froze midair. A cold light flashed through her eyes.

From the other end of the line, Pattie’s voice broke the silence. “What are you going to do now?”
