NovelVibe

Chapter 267

I dashed upstairs, grabbed the gifts, and bolted back down. The plan was simple: pile into the beat-up van Daisy borrowed from her mom. But the second we stepped outside, our jaws dropped. A sleek, black limo was idling right by the curb.

We all exchanged confused looks. Then Damon stepped up, wearing this smug-ass grin. "Surprise! Happy birthday, babe. Kept it a secret on purpose," he bragged, swinging the door open. Yeah, right. I smelled bullshit instantly.

Juniper shot me a "is this for real?" look. I gave her a quick nod. "Uh, thanks," she muttered, tucking her dress and climbing inside. The other girls followed, managing their fancy skirts. Flint lingered by the door, leaning close. "No way in hell the kid shelled out for this ride," he whispered. I let out a dry laugh and nodded. "Spot on."

"Is it... him?" I whispered, glancing back. Flint gulped, giving me a grim nod. "Thought so. He's dropping a bombshell today, huh?" Another nod from Flint, his eyes swimming with pure hurt. I spun around to face him. "Hey, cut that out. Don't do this."

"But I just—" he started. I slapped my hand right over his mouth. "Shut it. I need to look shocked when it happens. But listen to me: whatever goes down today, you're my family. Period. Nothing changes that."

The tension left his shoulders. "This is messed up, Lyra. I'm so sorry." He pulled me into a bone-crushing hug. The guy was terrified for me. "Keep your chin up," he breathed in my ear. "They want to see you crumble. Don't give 'em the satisfaction." Footsteps clicked behind us.

"Gotta play the victim so the trap works," I whispered back, pulling away with a fake, bright smile and a wink. Then I raised my voice for the audience. "Thanks for always having my back, Flint! Seriously, you've kept us all safe. I'm gonna miss you like crazy!"

"Miss you too, kiddo," he said, offering a sad smile before retreating back inside the lobby.

Damon hovered right behind me. "Ready to roll?"

"Yep," I said, turning around. "Just saying my goodbyes." I walked toward the limo. I knew damn well Orion paid for this. A cheap peace offering to sweeten the blow before he completely wrecked my life today.

Inside, Juniper’s face was tense. She knew the storm was coming. Her eyes darted from Damon to me, asking the silent question. I gave her a subtle shake of my head: *Nope, not Damon's doing.* Her expression hardened. The rest of the crew saw my head shake, too. The realization hit them all at once.

Boom. Just like that, everyone knew Damon was full of crap and Orion was pulling the strings. The guy had ghosted me, but he always followed the same sick playbook: send a fancy gift right before stabbing me in the back.

Like when he restarted his mate hunt. The council had dragged it out for a year, but six months ago, boom—a massive bouquet showed up at my door. The card just said: *I'm sorry.*

Four months ago? A brand-new gaming console arrived. Same deal—another apology note. Right after that, the news went wild broadcasting his public dates with all these high-society women. I wasn't on the list, obviously. Not "lucky" enough to make the cut. Honestly, though? I played a ton of video games because his stupid dates hijacked every single TV channel. I even shot him a sarcastic "thanks for the console" email.

Aside from that, total radio silence. Haven't laid eyes on him since the night of my heat. Best thing that ever happened, honestly. We woke up the next morning and agreed to focus on our own packs. Looking at my friends now, I couldn't help but smile. If I'd stayed hung up on Orion, we'd be screwed. The guy actually did us a massive favor by leaving.

I leaned back as the limo pulled away. The vibe in the car was weird. My friends were dead silent, but Damon was practically vibrating with excitement. We all stared at him, the heavy tension settling in our chests like lead. Suddenly, Luna nudged me and slipped a small card into my palm. I looked up at her.

She flicked her eyes toward Damon, then back to me. *Not now. Read it later.* I got the message. I nodded, shoving the card deep into my purse just as the limo screeched to a halt. We had arrived at headquarters.

Damon bolted out the door first, and we piled out behind him. Boom—red carpet, flashing cameras, and a horde of reporters. The second my foot hit the carpet, a reporter shoved a mic right into my face. "Lyra! Any comment on your dad being branded a traitor?"
