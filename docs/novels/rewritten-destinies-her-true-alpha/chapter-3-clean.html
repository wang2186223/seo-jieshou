NovelVibe

Chapter 3

"Start packing, Lyra." Mom barged in, cutting right through my brainstorming.

I blinked up from my notebook. "Wait, what? Why?"

"You're crashing at your dad's for the summer. The Alpha already cleared it." Mom beamed, tapping my shoulder. "Move it, kiddo. Get your stuff together."

I scrambled up and bolted over to her. "Seriously? I'm free?"

"Only till fall, sweetie. Then you're back. Had to sign my life away to promise you'd return."

My face fell. "Ugh, Mom, this place sucks. I hate it here." Honestly, I wanted payback, but keeping my distance from *him* came first.

"Trust me, baby. It'll make sense when you see your dad." She smoothed my hair, planted a quick kiss on my cheek, and nudged me. "Chop chop."

"Fine, fine." I kissed her cheek, and she slipped out.

I swiped my phone, locked the screen, and spun toward the closet. I threw clothes into my duffel, then made a move for the bathroom. Right then, the front door downstairs slammed so hard the walls shook.

"I freaking knew it!" Blair's screech echoed through the floorboards.

Whatever. I ignored the drama, swept my toiletries off the counter, grabbed my laptop and chargers, and shoved them into my pack. Time to go.

"Blair, what is your problem?" Ronnie sat at the kitchen table, nursing his coffee, while Mom bustled around.

I rolled my suitcase into the kitchen just as Blair burst in, throwing herself into her dad's arms. "Daddy, I'm not Damon's mate!"

"Whoa, how do you know that?" Ronnie hugged her tight.

I couldn't help it—I snorted.

Blair's head whipped toward me, eyes blazing. She lunged. "It's you! You trashy little bitch!" Her claws aimed straight for my face.

I didn't even blink. I just swung my hand and backhanded her ass across the room.

"Lyra!" Ronnie roared, jumping up.

Mom clicked her tongue. "Oh, shut it, Ronnie. Don't yell at her for self-defense. Blair swung first." Mom set a plate of breakfast on the table. "Sit down and eat, Lyra. Big day ahead."

Ronnie blinked. "Wait, babe? Why is she packed?"

"She's heading to her dad's for the summer." Mom turned to me, arms wide. "Come on, sweetie. Eat up. Your ride's almost here, you gotta be ready."

I slid into a chair and started shoveling food in.

"Why the hell is she going there?" Ronnie muttered, hauling Blair off the floor.

"She wanted some quality time with her dad before college starts. Why would I stop her?" Mom smiled at me, and I beamed back. Honestly, I had no clue how this trip would hide the mate bond from Damon, but I didn't care. I just needed out. Fast.

"Yeah, Dad rang me up this morning," I said, throwing Ronnie a sugary-sweet look. "Haven't seen him since Christmas, and since he offered to buy the ticket, I begged Mom."

Blair actually smiled at that. "Wait, you're actually leaving?"

"Oh, yeah. Gone all summer." I smirked right at her. "But don't get any ideas. My door is locked, and it's staying locked." My face went dead serious. I locked eyes with her and pushed my Alpha command straight into her skull.

"Lyra!" Ronnie barked. "No Alpha commands on your stepsister! You know the rules!"

"I wouldn't have to use it, Ronnie, if she hadn't been sneaking around my closet this morning trying to steal my clothes." I turned my gaze to her, raising an eyebrow. *Go ahead. Try me. Lie.*

"Is that true, Blair?" Ronnie looked down at her.

I kept my eyes locked on her face. She opened her mouth to spin some BS.

"Don't even try to lie," I warned. "I'll just force the truth out of you anyway."

Blair threw a massive tantrum, stomping her foot and screeching. "Fine! Yes!" she yelled. "Happy now? I went in there to grab her clothes because I know she's Damon's mate!"

I threw my hands up, acting totally clueless. "Uh, what? How could you possibly know that? We haven't even done the scent thing yet!"

"Because he smelled you on me, duh!" Her voice went so high it practically shattered glass. My ears literally throbbed.

"Okay, pipe down. You're giving us all a headache. Just explain," Mom said, sitting next to me and rubbing her temples.

Blair pouted, crossing her arms. "Last month, when Damon turned eighteen, I wore Lyra's dress. He sniffed me and said I smelled amazing. But the second I'm not wearing her stuff? He doesn't even look at me."

"So he likes my scent. Big deal. Doesn't make us mates," I scoffed, completely shutting down the idea. No way in hell. If I rejected him, the bond was dead anyway, and there was zero chance I'd ever accept him. That was a promise. Damon was the absolute last guy on earth I'd ever let touch me.

"Stop playing dumb, Lyra! We all know what a wolf liking your scent means!" Blair burst into tears, hiding her face in her hands. "He's the one I want, Daddy! I won't take anyone else! But he wants *her*!" She pointed a shaking finger at me.

I rolled my eyes. "Please. I don't want your sloppy seconds, Blair. Even if he *were* my mate—which I'm not saying he is—hell would freeze over before I ever accepted him. Honestly, I'd rather take the Lycan King over Damon any day."

Blair gasped, and I just threw my head back and laughed.

The Lycan King was basically our world's boogeyman. The last living Lycan, hiding out in the wild. Supposedly, only the high council ever saw his face. But hey, I wasn't joking. I'd take a monster over Damon in a heartbeat.

"You're full of it," Blair snapped. "He's the Alpha!"

"So?"

"Everyone wants to be Luna!"

"Yeah, only idiots want that job." I turned back to my plate and finished my breakfast while Ronnie tried to quiet her down.

A car horn honked outside, and Mom stood up. "That's your ride."

I nodded, stood up, gave Mom a quick goodbye kiss, and walked out the door.
