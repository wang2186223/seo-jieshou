Chapter 832

Vivienne needed an out, fast. She had to dump Agnes and suck up to the Lancasters, or she was dead meat. She lunged, shoving her finger right in Agnes’s face.

"You did this!" she shrieked, spitting the words. "You crazy old bitch, you set me up! You ruined my life! You made me hate my own family! You’re nothing but a trashy maid!"

She didn't just yell. She swung her leg and stomped Agnes. Hard. Then did it again.

"My life is ruined because of you! You did this!"

Vivienne’s face twisted into a hideous, psycho mask. The sweet, rich-girl act? Totally gone.

Agnes hit the hardwood, taking the blows without a peep. No dodging, no crying out. Just tears leaking down her face, staring up at Vivienne with this heartbreaking, pathetic look of pure pity.

The Lancasters watched, totally grossed out.

"Jesus, stop it!" Eleanor snapped, turning away. "Get her off her!"

The guards grabbed Vivienne. Eleanor’s eyes went ice-cold. She was done. Agnes had raised Vivienne from diapers. Hell, you keep a dog for twenty years, you love it. But a human being who spent two decades wiping your nose? Eleanor had kept Vivienne around because she thought the girl had a heart. She actually hoped Vivienne could coexist with her real daughter.

What a joke. Vivienne was pure poison. A total sociopath who only cared about her own skin, willing to beat her lifelong nanny to save herself. Zero empathy.

Eleanor let out a harsh, humorless laugh. "Wow, Vivienne. You really are a piece of work."

The guards pinned Vivienne like a squawking chicken. Seeing the sheer disgust on Eleanor's face, she panicked. "M-Mom... please, I just lost it... she brainwashed me, I swear—"

"Save it," Eleanor spat, cutting her off cold. "Sign the damn papers and get the hell out of our house. You can starve in a ditch for all we care."

She shoved the pen back into Vivienne’s face.

Vivienne stared at the contract, her hands shaking so bad she couldn't even grip the plastic. If she signed, she was finished. No money, no Lancaster name, nothing. How the hell was she supposed to survive in Vaelion City?

She shot a desperate look at Agnes.

Agnes scrambled across the floor, dragging her bruised body toward Eleanor. "Ma'am, please! It’s on me, all of it! Miss Vivienne didn't do anything! She’s been your baby girl for twenty years. She calls you Mom! If you throw her out, she won't make it..."

She reached for Eleanor’s ankle, but Frederick booted her back. "Back off! It’s because of those twenty years that we kept letting you snakes slide, and you used every chance to screw over Lyra!"

He glared down at her. "Oh, and Martha Finch? You really thought you could steal a cool billion from our US safe and just walk away?"

Agnes’s jaw dropped. She trembled, completely speechless.

Eleanor looked down at the heap on the floor, her face like stone. Her voice was pure ice. "Agnes, you worked for us for decades. We treated you like family. And this is how you pay us back?"
