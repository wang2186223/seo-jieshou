Chapter 597

The air in the room was thick enough to choke on. Everyone was freaking out, but Caspian? He just rolled his eyes and snorted. "Please. Magnus was a total idiot," he sneered. "The guy locked everything on his PC behind those stupid-ass codes he loved so much."

Rhys nodded, his face dead serious. "Yeah. The guy was a freak about those passwords."

Evan sucked in a breath, trying to stop his chest from shaking. "And those monthly security answers he blasted out?" He wiped a bead of sweat off his forehead. "We thought it was overkill, but Roric backed him up." He slapped Elder on the shoulder. "Relax, man. We're golden."

Elder slowly nodded, and just like that, the panic evaporated. These clowns got their mojo right back. Elder turned to me, plastering on a fake-ass smile. "Nice try, kid. You almost had us sweating. But we got backups for our backups."

I threw my hands up, playing the clueless victim. "Hey, don't shoot the messenger. I'm just telling you what I heard. Magnus and his top dogs are either in cages or in coffins. Astrid skipped town. And Roric? Whoever the hell that is—he's dead."

Rhys leaned forward in his chair, squinting hard. "You seriously expect us to believe you don't know Roric?" One eyebrow shot up. I could hear the others sniffing the air, trying to catch the scent of a lie.

Umbra glanced at Terra. Terra shuffled over and gave her a soft nudge. "You good?" she whispered.

Terra let out a dry laugh. "Yeah. I'm down, but my scent isn't gonna betray me."

Umbra chuckled. "Damn right. So," she looked at me, "what's the play, boss?"

I had to stall. Playing stupid was my best bet. I could see them starting to boil under their cool-guy acts. I let a tiny smirk slip. Their faces instantly hardened. Oh, they hated that I wasn't shaking in my boots.

Umbra and Terra snickered. "Yeah, fragile dudes hate a smirk," Umbra whispered, leaning in close. She was coiled like a spring, ready to snap. "Alright, let's rattle their cages."

I held my hands up again. "Look, guys, I'm barely twenty-two. I've been stuck in classrooms my whole life, literally just graduated. Unless this Roric guy went to my college or ran with Oakenfang or Silvermoon, I don't know him from Adam."

Caspian stood up and slouched against the doorframe, looking around. "He was Silvermoon. Way back when," he muttered.

Umbra's ears twitched as Caspian blocked the exit. "Anyone else in this house?" Caspian whispered, keeping it low. "I want to talk real business, but no eavesdroppers."

Terra's ears twitched too. Bingo. Perfect timing.

Right on cue, Shadow exploded out of the tree line. He slid to a stop, gave Terra a quick lick on the face, and bolted over to Umbra. "We're set," he panted. "Their guards are rounding up the rest, and the Omegas are locked and loaded outside."

Umbra let out a nasty growl. "They want to butcher everyone while we're boxed in. Cowardly bastards."

I snorted. "Tell Orion we're good to go. I'm jamming the mind-links in this room. If he wants us, he'll have to cross the bridge." Shadow nodded and vanished into the dark. I turned to the council. "Hey, my old man put in a soundproof shield. I can flip the switch, but fair warning: you won't hear a damn thing from the outside world."

Evan frowned. "Why the hell wouldn't we hear anything?"

I shrugged. "Hey, science, not magic," I grinned, and Terra snorted. "It kills all noise. Period. All or nothing." I slid my top drawer open, showing off a fake control panel with a button and a dial—just some junk I'd thrown together to look high-tech. "Your call, boys."

They traded looks, having a silent conversation. I knew what they were thinking. They had telepathy; if shit hit the fan, their guards could ping them. Finally, Elder jerked his chin. "Hit it."

Finnian took a step forward. Umbra's hair stood on end, a growl ripping from her throat. Finnian froze. Then, he tilted his head, showing his neck—total submission. "Sorry to jump you," he mumbled. "I know I'm the bad guy here. I walked in with these jerks." He pointed at the guys by the desk. "But I'm out. I'm not backing this crap anymore. No way."

I smirked and slammed the button. Terra muttered a quick spell under her breath, locking the room down and killing all outside noise.

Evan bared his teeth. "Watch your step, buddy. Don't do something you can't undo."

Finnian scoffed. "If I actually thought things through, I'd have stayed in bed. I wouldn't be standing here like a dumbass, helping you guys commit treason against our actual king."

Caspian's face went purple. "He's not my freaking king!" he roared. "He's a Lycan, not a real wolf! God made a mistake when she cooked up those freaks. We're just cleaning up her mess!"
