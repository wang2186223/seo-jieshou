Chapter 40

Elara went white as a sheet. Man, I just wanted to hug her. So I grabbed her. "Give the guy a shot," I muttered right in her ear. "Seriously, he might blow your mind." I threw him a quick wave. Elara squeezed my hand like a vice.

"Alright, little wolf, let's go. We need to talk." Kaelen slid right up to her, throwing a heavy arm around her waist.

I yanked my hand free. Elara shot me this desperate, begging look, but I just waved and bolted. I spun around on my heel just in time to see her finally face him. She opened her mouth to snap, but boom—he leaned in and slammed his lips on hers. I jumped, totally startled, then sprinted the rest of the way to the training field.

"Hey, you good?" Astrid yelled the second I hit the dirt ring.

"Yeah. Totally. Why?"

Astrid cocked an eyebrow. "Uh, because your dad said you were sick as a dog the last few days. Said that's why you skipped out."

"Oh. Right. Yeah, I'm golden now." I flashed a quick grin, hitting the grass to stretch out next to her. "So, how's the grind been?"

"Good," Astrid said, bouncing on her toes. "Mostly. I mean, Morgana and Briar are still giving us attitude, but Elara? Damn, she's really stepping up. A bunch of others too. Honestly, it's awesome to watch."

"Exactly what I wanted to hear." I popped back up, shaking out my legs. Freya marched right over.

"Look who's back," Freya said.

"Yep. Now, listen up. From here on out, we're splitting the girls into new groups."

Freya frowned, looking me up and down. "Another split? We already got the shifted and non-shifted separated."

"Sure, but this is about what they actually want." I leaned my back against the wooden fence.

Astrid leaned in right next to me. "Okay, spill. What's the plan?"

"Today, we figure out who actually wants to be a real-deal warrior, and who's just here for basic self-defense. That way, we can set up a brutal morning session for the ones who want to go hard."

Astrid's head snapped back. "Female warriors? In this pack? Are you kidding? Your dad shut me down every single time I brought it up. It's a joke. I'm not getting their hopes up again."

"Wait, again?"

"Yeah, last year," Astrid growled, her jaw tight. "A bunch of girls begged Freya and me to talk to the Alpha. We promised we'd fight for them. But your dad? Shot us down. Over and over. And then..."

"We had to go back and tell them their own Alpha didn't give a crap about their loyalty," Freya spat.

"Hold on." I blocked them out and slammed into my dad's mind-link. *Did you seriously ban women from training to be warriors?*

*Lyra? Where is this coming from?*

*Answer me, dad. Did you?*

*Well, yeah. But why do you care?*

*Because I'm setting up a female warrior program before I leave. Dad, it's 2024. Get with the times. Women can fight. Hell, they can kick half the guys' asses.* I practically snarled through the link.

He let out a low chuckle in my head. *Alright, kiddo.*

*Do you swear you'll let them join the ranks?*

*Yes, kiddo, I swear. Look, Astrid and Freya brought it up before, but I didn't think there were actually enough girls who wanted in. Why does the number even matter?*

*I don't know,* he muttered, sounding totally lost. *But look, I promise. They'll take the exact same test as the guys. If they pass, they're in.*

*Good. Love you, dad.*

*Love you too, kiddo.*

I cut the link and turned back to the girls. "He just greenlit it. Honestly, he just seemed confused that more than a couple of girls actually wanted to do it."

Astrid threw her hands in the air. "Why the hell does the number matter?"

"Because men are brainless," Freya muttered.

I nodded, waiting for the rest of the pack to trickle in. The second I saw Elara jog into the ring—face red as a tomato—I stepped up to the crowd.

"Listen up, ladies! We're doing our usual routine today, but with a twist. When one of us comes over to check your form, tell us if you want a shot at the warrior ranks. I'm putting together a brutal morning class for anyone who wants to prep for the trials. Once you pass that, if you want to push even further, Finnian and Cyrus will take over to get you into the elites."

"You really think we can make the elites?" Tara yelled from the back.

"Why the hell not?" I locked eyes with Elara. "One of the baddest elites on the Council is a female wolf named Valerie. You can do whatever the hell you want. Anything."
