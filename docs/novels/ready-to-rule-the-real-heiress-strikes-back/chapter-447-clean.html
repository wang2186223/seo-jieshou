The whole group made their way upstairs. Through the huge, sparkling floor-to- ceiling windows, Paris unfolded outside. The city glowed, lights scattered everywhere like a sky full of stars. It was impossible not to get lost in it for a moment.

As everyone started to sit down, Cameron and Kylie took the main seats at the table. Blake slid in next to Cameron. Lucinda wasted no time grabbing the spot beside Kylie, tossing a smug look in Lydia's direction.

Lydia rolled her eyes and ignored her. Connor walked over to Blake's side, pulled out the chair next to him, and motioned for Lydia to sit. That meant Chris ended up stuck between Lucinda and Lydia.

Lucinda looked absolutely fed up. She couldn't stand Chris, not even a little. If Mikey had shown up tonight, maybe she'd have been interested. She shot a bitter look at Connor. If it weren't for Lydia, Connor would be hers. She wouldn't have to settle for second best.
