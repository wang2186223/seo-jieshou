Chapter 177

Raven squeezed her sister tight, eyes darting between Magnus and Orion. "Yo, you just gonna let her talk to us like that?"

I let go of Juniper, snorted, and dropped into a squat right in front of them. "Man, you guys got it all twisted."

Sable glanced at Raven, then locked eyes with me. "The hell are you talking about?"

"Nobody 'lets' me do squat. Look at me. I’m a top-tier alpha, stronger than any chick out there and most of the guys. Magnus and the King don't run my life. I do what I want, and I deal with the fallout later. And let’s be real—you two talk a big game, but you're weak as hell for alphas. Hell, Juniper could take you both."

"Like hell she can!" Raven spat, glaring daggers. "She's a joke compared to me, and Sable is way tougher than I am."

"See? Dead wrong." I glanced back. Juniper squared her shoulders. I knew I was right. Her wolf was like Elara’s—an alpha, sure, but she kept her power packed inside her shifted form instead of just flexing her aura. "Her wolf is a beast. And I'm gonna train my girls to unlock every bit of that power."

That hit them like a punch to the gut. Even the guys whipped their heads around. "Wait, what do you mean?" Juniper asked, her voice shaking.

"I'm not letting this loss break us, Juniper. We aren't going soft." I stood up, looking her dead in the eye. "Starting this week, we train. In between classes, whatever it takes. I'm done losing my people. And my wolf just claimed you. You're mine now."

Orion’s eyes lit up. He pointed a finger at me. "Damn straight."

"Huh?" I blinked, turning to him.

"You need to train. Hell, everyone does." Orion whipped out his phone and dialed. "Hey. Who's our best trainer we can spare right now?" He paused, listening. "Uh-huh. Yeah. Send Dunn." He tapped the screen, switching apps. "I'm putting him on the floor right below the penthouses. I'll have the paperwork sorted before he even lands. Tell him he's staying. If we need to swap him out later, fine, but I want him permanent here at the school. Yenna's gonna love that." He nodded, quiet for a second. "Yeah, she told me last week. I'll make sure they get a massive apartment so the kid has a room. Let me know what he says." Orion hung up and looked at Magnus. "We're starting training here. I'm bringing in my top guy. Everyone gets a daily slot. Get your secretary to map out the schedule. I'll head to the office to help you plan as soon as I spread the word."

"That's not what I meant, King." I stepped up to him. "Even with your new class, I'm still running my own crew's training."

"Hey, that's your call. But you're right. We've been slacking on your training here, and honestly, I'm pissed it took us this long to realize it." He turned to Magnus. "We're wolves. We train our packs every single day. Why the hell did we stop just because they're at school?"

I didn't want to talk school curriculum right now. My anger was burning out. I grabbed Juniper's hand. "Come on. Let's go to my room."

"Yeah, let's go." Juniper smiled, though her lip was trembling.

I turned and gave Orion a quick nod. "We're heading back to our spot."

Orion nodded back. But Raven lost her mind, screeching, "Are you seriously just letting them walk? No punishment?!"

I ignored her, scooped up my food from the floor where I'd dropped it, and started walking. Juniper threw one last glare at the two girls shivering on the floor, then fell in step behind me.

"Punish them for what, exactly?" Orion's voice made me slow my pace.

"They threatened us!" Sable shrieked.

"From what I saw—and King, correct me if I'm wrong—you two started this mess. You cornered these two right after they lost their families just to talk trash. That about sum it up?"

"Alpha Magnus..." Raven stammered, her face pale.

"Answer him," Orion growled.

"Yes," Sable whispered.

"Then I guess their punishment is not beating your asses right here." Magnus turned his back. "Works for me." Orion nodded. We left them rotting on the floor.
