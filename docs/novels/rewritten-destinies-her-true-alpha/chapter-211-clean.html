NovelVibe

Chapter 211

"You up for this?"

Juniper’s voice dragged me out of sleep. She was beaming, holding out a mug of coffee. God, I wanted to marry her right then and there.

I took a gulp, nodding like a zombie. "Wrapped it up at 3 AM. Already synced to the tablet." I threw my arms up, bones popping. "Jesus. I'm dead on my feet." But a grin broke through my exhaustion. "Worth it, though. Every damn second."

"So, what's his verdict?" Juniper asked, watching me shove my feet into my sneakers.

"A single word. 'Okay.'" I spun around to face her. "Seriously, what kind of half-ass reply is that?"

"Maybe he's got eyes on him?" she suggested.

She wasn't wrong. Probably hit the nail on the head. Still, it pissed me off.

"Any clue when they drop the news?"

Before Juniper could finish, a knock rattled the door. She swung it open. The whole crew was standing there, clutching tablets like shields.

"What is this, a coup?" I downed another swig of coffee, popped my neck, and slumped onto the couch.

Fawn marched in, shoving her screen into my face. "We're pitching in."

"Pitching in?" My eyebrows knitted together. I waved her tablet around. "For what?"

"Juniper's brainchild. From last night, while you were locked away in your cave." Daisy dumped two more tablets—hers and Juniper's—into my lap, then hijacked my favorite armchair.

"Okay, lay it on me. What's the plan?"

All eyes zipped to Juniper. She flashed a smug grin. "Why use one tablet when we can run a five-screen command center? Same app, same setup, blasting every single email straight to Orion." She gave her dark eyebrows a playful wiggle. "We cut the processing time by eighty percent. Boom. If they give us some tight, bullshit deadline, we flood the gates."

Damn. That was brilliant.

"You're a freaking genius!" I bolted off the couch, lunging for my laptop. I fired up the installer, flashed my custom software onto all five devices, and made the girls log into their mailboxes.

Once the screens synced up, we stood up. "Breakfast time," I grinned.

Then my phone buzzed. I swiped accept. "Yeah?"

"Hey, Lyra."

"Flint! What's up, man?"

Silence stretched over the line. Flint cleared his throat, sounding uneasy. "Uh... Alpha Damon is down in the lobby. Says he's here to... help out today?"

His voice trailed off into awkwardness. I stared at the screen in disbelief, pulling the phone back.

"Right. Okay. We're on our way down anyway," I muttered, killing the call.

Daisy nudged my shoulder. "What's the face for?"

"Damon's downstairs. Apparently, he's our new 'helper' today." I glanced at the girls, then pocketed my phone. "Well, girls. Let's go see what he's got up his sleeve."

We hit the lobby. There was Damon, propped against the wall, a massive roll of brown butcher paper resting against his leg. He flashed a wide, cocky grin. I just stared, totally clueless.

"Morning," he said.

"Morning, Damon." I nodded toward the giant paper tube. "What's the cargo?"

His grin stretched even wider. "Look, I know you've got the tech side covered. You don't need me. But I wanted to do something." He shot me a quick, nervous glance, then looked at Flint. "Hey, man. Give me a hand with this?"

Flint eyed me for permission. I gave him a quick nod. He stepped out from behind the reception desk and grabbed one end of the roll.

They pulled it open. It was a massive, hand-painted banner. The moment we saw it, we all burst out laughing with pure joy.

"No freaking way," I gasped.

Damon’s smile turned sharp, almost wicked. "Oh, yes way. I figured you guys were so buried in coding and logistics that you forgot about marketing. You need a crowd-puller. This is it."

Spanned across ten feet of paper were bold, thick black letters trimmed with metallic gold paint: *ROYAL MATE APPLICATIONS*.

"I already dragged a table from the cafeteria into the main lobby," Damon added, rolling the paper back up. "Got a ladder ready to mount this bad boy right behind it. This should get the crowd buzzing, right?"

Flint’s jaw hit the floor as he stared at the sign.

"It's absolutely perfect!" I hopped over and bumped his shoulder. "Thanks, Damon."

He took a sharp breath, his eyes flickering with a sudden, quiet ache. "Anytime."

I braced myself, waiting for Terra to spark up inside me. Nothing. Not a single peep from my wolf, even this close to him. My smile died instantly.

"Hey, what's up?" Damon leaned in closer. I forced myself not to flinch, letting him step into my space.

"Nothing," I lied.

Right on cue, my stomach let out a loud, angry rumble. Thank God. Saved by my own biology. I backed up, turning to the girls. "Alright, I'm freaking starving." I shook my empty mug. "And I need a serious caffeine refill before the madness starts."

Because let's be real—today was going to be an absolute, grade-A shitshow.
