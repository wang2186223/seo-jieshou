Chapter 420

I slammed my palms over my face. Rejection. Pure and simple.

Inside my head, my wolves slumped, totally depressed. *Look, we get it,* they whimpered. *You’re crazy about Orion. And Cipher? Don't even get us started, even if you won't admit it. We swore we’d stay out of your love life, but damn, Shadow just crept right up on us.* Terra let out a heavy groan and collapsed.

Umbra chimed in with a nod. *Yeah. We seriously thought we were over the rage and pain those two dumped on us. We just wanted to play nice and work as a team. Which we did, eventually.* She sighed, dropping her chin onto her paws.

I leaned back, rubbing my temples. "Okay, fair enough. But what went wrong?"

*He was just... him,* Umbra said, shaking her head. *No filters. No human drama messing with his head. Orion overthinks everything. He carries way too much crap on his shoulders. Dude literally thinks his choice of mate will save or destroy the entire realm.* She sighed. *Honestly, I feel bad for him. Carrying that kind of weight? He must be bone-tired 24/7.*

Terra nodded along. *Totally. He never forced anything. Never made me feel like an outsider or creeped me out.*

*He's into you,* Umbra said, staring at her. *Like, really into you.*

If wolves could blush, Terra would be bright red. *Shut up, he’s just being polite. It’s you he loves. Seriously, you should see the way he stares at you.*

*Oh, please,* Umbra snorted. *Just like we can all see how he feels about you.* They both locked eyes with me. *Look, we know this is the last thing you wanted to deal with. We were gonna keep our mouths shut, but here we are. We've got maybe two weeks before our next heat hits. Yeah, we messed up. We shouldn't have caught feelings for Shadow when his human is clearly bad news. But...*

Terra cut her off. *We couldn't help it, okay?*

"Are you guys in love with him?" I demanded, scanning their faces. If they said yes, I was completely screwed. I needed to know what my next move was.

The two of them traded a look, then faced me. Umbra tilted her head. *Look, I’ve never done the love thing. I only know what it is because of you two. So, no, I wouldn't call it love. I like the guy. He's fun to hang out with and talk to. But honestly? I think we all kept our guards up.*

Terra nodded. *Same. I like him, and yeah, it could turn into love if I let it.* She gave me a deadpan look. *But Umbra and I made a pact way before Shadow even showed his face. We’ve already screwed up your love life way too many times. We're done ruining things for you. You only loved Damon because of my feelings. You avoided Orion because Umbra was hung up on Shadow. You just finally glued your broken pieces back together after the messes we caused. This is your life now. Your turn.*

I whipped my head toward Umbra, but she was already nodding in agreement. *We can have our little crush, and still leave the floor open for you to find real love.*

"I would never ask you guys to sacrifice that for me," I protested.

*We know,* Terra said, stepping up to the mental barrier. Umbra flanked her. *That’s exactly why we’re telling you. Our little thing for Shadow doesn't mean squat. Right now, you just need to get ready for your heat.*

*And uh... maybe actually talk to Cipher for once?* Terra threw in. Then they vanished, leaving my brain completely scrambled.

A sharp knock rattled the door. "Alpha Lyra?" I jerked my head up. An omega stood there holding a tray. "Can I bring in your lunch?"

I sat up straight, forcing a smile. "Yeah, go ahead. Come on in."

She laid out my meal—three massive, juicy steaks and a loaded baked potato—and slipped out. I started shoveling food into my mouth. Suddenly, my phone buzzed. A text from Gideon Stone.

**Gideon Stone:** *Elara Meadow tells me you’re stuck on firewall number nine. Told ya, that thing is a beast unless you’ve got a digital battering ram. Ever heard of a back door? Look for one. It’s not gonna be a walk in the park, but if you want to crush Magnus, you gotta hack the mainframe.*

That was like the second or third time he’d dropped hints about a back door. Before I could even re-read the text, it self-destructed. Poof. Gone. I sighed, tossed the phone onto the desk, and stabbed another piece of steak. I just shoved it into my mouth when the phone buzzed again.

**Gideon Stone:** *Oh, and Lyra? You better get your shit together and move fast. I've been talking to Roric. They’re planning to take down the king the second your mating ceremony wraps up.*

Right after the ceremony. Holy crap.

I stared at the calendar, pinching the bridge of my nose. I wolfed down the rest of my food and spun back to my computer screen. My brain was short-circuiting, that last text looping on repeat in my head. Looking at the countdown timer on the monitor made my stomach do flips.

The message was loud and clear: I was officially out of time.
