Chapter 400

I froze. Bolting was a death sentence—they'd grab me in a heartbeat. So, fine. I’d sit tight, keep my eyes peeled, and play the waiting game. No magic. Not yet. I had to save my juice for later. Gideon said this damn forest was crawling with magical traps. Once I cleared the tree line, I was golden. But first? I had to get the hell out of here.

Gideon squinted at me. "Gonna make this hard, huh?"

I didn't answer. Just plopped my ass right on the dirt. Look, I'm not an idiot, but I sure as hell wasn't going to make this easy for him.

He let out a dry chuckle, then barked at Ronnie. "Keep an eye on my kid. Don't let him near the wolf."

Ronnie gave a quick nod. Magnus was already walking off. "Gideon, on me. You're carrying me, our gear, and the brat."

Gideon's face twitched, but he nodded anyway. A nasty smirk crept onto his face. "Right. Poor kid's gotta drag this out for another week." He shifted, vanishing into the trees. That left Ronnie and Damon staring daggers at me.

"Dude, are you seriously still chasing Lyra's mom?" Damon groaned, slumping against a thick trunk. "I mean, sure, she's a total babe. And yeah, the chicks in that bloodline pack some serious voodoo. But she will literally rip your throat out if she catches wind of this."

Ronnie shrugged. "Duh. That's half the fun. Besides, we need their cash. Bad."

"Why?" Damon shoved himself off the bark. "Who gives a shit if a few lycans are still kicking?"

Wait, *what?* Did he just say some lycans survived? Holy shit.

"Who cares about the first family anyway?" Damon kept going. "Why is he so damn obsessed with them?"

Ronnie glanced at me and sighed. Damon just snorted. "Oh, please. Who's she gonna tell? She's a dead girl walking anyway."

I stretched out on the ground, acting completely bored. Their little threats? Zero effect. I wasn't sweating them.

"Come on, spill," Damon whined. "You promised you'd tell me once I finished college. It's been months, man. I'm still totally clueless."

Ronnie stared at me. God, I wanted him to play the classic movie villain so bad. *Come on, dude. Give me the evil monologue. Do it.*

*Seriously? You think they're that dumb?* Umbra snorted in my head, sounding exhausted.

*A girl can dream, okay?* I rolled over, turning my back to them. Maybe if I ignored them, they'd keep blabbing.

"Look, your old man's got a master plan," Ronnie said. "But there's a damn good reason we're targeting the lycans and the first family."

I tucked my chin, keeping one eye cracked open.

Damon threw his hands up. "He keeps saying that! But *why*?"

Ronnie groaned. "Lycans are simple, dude. They're faster, stronger, and they're totally loyal to the king. Your dad spent like, thirty years trying to wipe them off the map."

*Holy freaking crap.* Umbra let out a low growl in my mind.

"Well, your dad and the brotherhood did," Ronnie corrected, pacing around.

*The hell is the brotherhood?* Terra finally caught her breath and crept back toward the barrier.

Damon rolled his eyes. "The Brotherhood of Bloodshed. It's always the fucking brotherhood. Who even are these guys? Why do we care?" He literally banged his forehead against a tree. For real? "Ronnie, man to man. Just tell me. Please."

Ronnie let out another heavy sigh, acting like this was a massive chore. "Do you ever listen? Roric started the brotherhood. He put it together way back when the king kicked out the old-school alphas to put their kids in charge."

"Yeah, yeah, I know that part, but who *are* they? And who the hell is Roric?"

That name rang a bell. It was bouncing around in my skull, but I couldn't quite grab it.

"Doesn't matter. You'll meet the guy soon enough." Ronnie glanced my way. I kept my eyelids heavy, pretending to be fast asleep, totally unbothered. But I was watching their every move through a tiny slit. "Anyway, when the old king booted the alphas, they teamed up. But Roric was the guy pulling the strings, getting everyone in one room."

Damon frowned. "Wait. Isn't Roric the alpha who..." He trailed off, scratching his head.

"Yep. That's him. He's the mastermind behind this whole shitshow."

*Ugh, this is all so damn vague!* I screamed inside my head, venting to my wolves. But they stayed dead silent. It was like they knew exactly what I was forgetting, but weren't talking.

"Long story short," Ronnie said, leaning back against the tree. "The brotherhood's sole purpose is to end the king's bloodline. Roric was sick of the lycans treating us like dirt, acting like they're superior. I mean, we have a first family too, right? The goddess made wolves just like she made them. Roric's point was, we deserve a shot at the throne. Why should the lycans run the whole show just because they got here first? That's why every last lycan has to die. If even one survives, they take the crown. Same goes for the first family. Screw the goddess telling us who's boss. We've got free will, damn it."

Damon's jaw hit the floor. "Wait... all of this is just for that?"

"Duh." Magnus's voice cut through the air. He slid off the back of Gideon's wolf, tossing the kid onto the dirt like a sack of potatoes. "It's always about power."
