Chapter 5

"I already lived once. And I died."

The words stumbled out of my mouth. My dad went stiff as a board. I shot a look at Finnian, and he instantly bolted the door, waving his hands to cast a silence spell. The air in the room went dead quiet, but my eyes were locked on my dad.

"This morning, I woke up six years in the past. Before this, I lived to twenty-four. I had a baby. And my mate? He ripped my pup right out of my stomach."

A double growl shook the damn walls. Dad and Finnian looked ready to kill.

I threw my hands up. "Stop! Listen. He dumped me for my stepsister. She fed him lies about me cheating, said the kid wasn't his. So he butchered us both. Used silver scalpels. That killed any love I had left for him." I stared up at my dad, tears finally spilling over.

"Baby girl..." Dad choked out.

I cut him off with a raised hand. "I’m not mating with him, Dad. No way. I won't survive it. I have to rewrite this whole damn thing. First step? Hiding my scent. I told Mom what I needed, and she dialed you. So, what's the plan? How do we fix this?" Panic started clawing at my throat. "I can't die again. I can't let him tear my baby out of me just to realize it was his own blood. I won't bring a life into this world just to watch it get slaughtered, Dad. I just can't." My chest felt like it was splitting open. I wanted payback, sure, but right now? I was just finally mourning my dead baby.

"Oh, sweetheart," Finnian whispered.

Then, my dad snapped.

"Who?"

The word came straight from the deepest pit of hell. *Crack!* His fist slammed the desk, splintering the thick wood. I looked up. It wasn't my dad anymore. It was Obsidian, his wolf, staring back at me.

"Who touched you, my pup? Who killed my grandpup?" Obsidian’s voice was a terrifying, low rumble. His eyes promised pure murder.

"Damon. The Alpha's son," I whispered.

The final puzzle piece clicked. The heavy silence broke, and my dad’s wolf took over, shifting into a massive black beast. I shrieked. Finnian lunged, throwing his body right in front of the door.

Obsidian bared his fangs, but Finnian didn't budge. "You can't go kill him for something he hasn't even done yet, Obsidian! Get a grip! You know the rules. You went through this exact same shit!"

The black wolf just snarled.

"Dad, please!" I begged. If he walked out that door, Damon and Blair were dead meat, and the Council would hunt him down.

Obsidian whipped around. Seeing my wrecked face, his anger melted. He trotted over, licked the tears off my cheeks, then retreated behind the desk to shift back into human form.

"My bad, Lyra. Lost my head for a second." He grabbed some shorts from a drawer and pulled them on. He wiped his face, then sat back down. "Well. Guess it's time to spill our own secrets."

"Dad?" I sniffled, looking up.

"Look, I'm not just a regular werewolf. Obviously, Obsidian is a huge part of me," he said with a weak chuckle. I managed a small smile. "But my mom? She was a shaman. A witch. My dad had to have her, even if it was just a one-night stand. And boom—here I am."

"A witch?" My hands dropped.

He gave me a sad, tight smile. "Yeah. You would've loved her. But witches don't get our lifespan. They age like humans. Gone in a blink. She passed way before I met your mother." He shook his head. "But she had a gift. Exactly what you just described—a do-over. The Moon Goddess passed that magic down to us. I got my second chance when I was your age, too."

I jumped back, eyes wide. "Wait, what?"

He nodded. "I never actually cheated on your mom," he said softly. "I used an Alpha command on a female wolf to make her lie about us sleeping together. See, in my first life, I stayed with your mom. We had three beautiful pups. But one night, enemies raided us. They slaughtered all four of you." He swallowed hard, his throat tight.

"Dad..."

"I couldn't watch you die again. Not my babies. So when I woke up four years in the past, I made my move. You were just a tiny baby. I never got your brothers or sister back, and that absolutely destroyed me for a while. But I had you." He choked back tears. I rushed to his side. "So I told your mom I cheated."

"She said she felt the bond break," I murmured.

He nodded slowly. "When a wolf betrays their fated mate, the pain is pure agony."

"Yeah, it damn well is," Finnian growled from the door.

I whipped my head toward him. Suddenly, it clicked. "Wait. Mom and Ronnie..."

"It doesn't happen enough to be a habit," my dad growled.

"But it happens enough that you still feel the burn," Finnian shot back.

"Mom has no idea," I said, wrapping my arms around my dad. "She'd lose her mind if she knew the truth."

"I know, princess. That's why you can't say a word. I cheated death, so I beat my fate... but finding out the truth now? It would absolutely destroy her." My dad squeezed me tight.

"So she actually felt you with another woman?" I hugged him back.

He shook his head. "No. I used my magic to mimic the pain of a broken bond. She was sobbing in our bedroom, and I was in the hallway, breaking my own heart."

"Oh, Dad..."

"It was either lose her and you, or lose everything. And I knew this way, I'd still get to watch my little girl grow up." My dad pulled back, wiping his eyes. "Anyway, I'm not telling you this to make you feel sorry for me. I'm telling you because you have that same magic. You can hide your scent, easily. And this summer, I'm going to teach you how to weaponize it."

"Thank you, Dad."

"Don't thank me yet. This summer is going to be absolute hell. But it’s going to keep you alive."

"When this is all over, you have to promise me one thing, Dad."

"What's that, pumpkin?" He always used pet names when he was stressed.

"Once I handle my business, we tell Mom the truth."

"Baby, we can't do that."

"She's tougher than you think, Dad. She never moved on from you. She only took Ronnie in because she thought I needed a protector in the new pack. Plus, she wanted the Alpha to back off."

Dad growled, punching the desk again, then finally nodded. "Fine. We tell her."

"Thanks, Dad." I kissed his cheek.

"Now go get unpacked. We start training tomorrow at dawn. Besides, I know a certain someone who's currently banging down the packhouse doors to see you."

I smiled, shaking my head. Elara. My ride-or-die. "Elara's here?"

"She's been going crazy since she heard you were coming back. Go."

"Love you guys."

"Love you too, kiddo," Finnian said, kissing my forehead. "And for the record? I'd still gladly rip that Alpha mate's throat out if I could."
