Chapter 771

Vivienne was shaking like a leaf, her eyes bloodshot and leaking. "Lyra, come on, don't throw me out! Just say you forgive me, okay? I'll do whatever. Seriously, I'll clean your floors, anything..."

"Shut it," Lyra snapped, her voice dripping with pure annoyance. "Go crawl back to your hospital bed, or go lock yourself in the villa. Pick one."

Vivienne wrung her hands, her knuckles turning white. "Wait... Lyra? Does that mean... I get to stay?"

Was she finally off the hook? No more house arrest?

Lyra didn't bother answering. She just stared right through her with icy eyes.

Julian let out a harsh laugh, a nasty smirk plastered across his face. "Look, babe, what my niece is actually saying is this: scram right now, or you're gonna have a really, really bad day."

That grin made Vivienne flinch, taking a step back.

She scrambled to her feet. "Right. Yeah. Going back to my room. Right now."

She tripped over her own feet rushing out. Blood smeared her face, her hands, and ruined her hospital gown. A total trainwreck. Nobody offered a hand.

Right at the exit, she slammed hard into a guy. Tall, blonde, blue eyes, sharp suit, moving like his hair was on fire.

Vivienne hit the floor with a thud, groaning as fresh tears spilled over.

"Whoa, Miss Vivienne!" The guy—Lucas—yanked her back up. "My bad, seriously. I've got an emergency with Mr. Vinton. Didn't mean to knock you down..."

Her head was spinning.

But Lucas couldn't care less right now. He shoved her onto a nearby chair and bolted inside.

"Mr. Vinton, we're in deep shit!" Lucas's face was white as a sheet. "Someone leaked Arthur's chart. The press is saying he's basically on his deathbed..."

He threw a quick, nervous look at Reginald before spitting out the rest. "And because of that, Vinton Group stock is tanking. Wall Street sharks smelled blood. They launched a massive short attack. In just three hours, we lost tens of billions. Our cash flow... it's completely dry!"

"Say what?!" Arthur shrieked, his face turning a sickly gray. He clutched his chest, hacking violently.

"Dad, hey, breathe!" Silas jumped up, his own face losing all color. He forced a calm look to keep his old man from having a stroke. "It's just a hiccup. I've got this. You just lie back and rest."

He snatched his phone and marched toward the door. "No time to waste. Grab the clinic's boardroom. Set up an emergency global call right now. I want a strategy on my desk in ten minutes flat."

"On it," Lucas barked, already dialing.

Arthur wheezed, clutching his ribs as the door slammed shut. Adeline rubbed his back, mumbling useless sweet nothings, but no words could fix this kind of disaster.

The air in the room turned to lead.

"Arthur," Lyra said, stepping up to the bed. Her face was a total blank slate, cool as ice.
