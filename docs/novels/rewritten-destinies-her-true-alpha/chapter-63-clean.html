Chapter 63

My dad looked like he took a punch to the gut. His whole world was going down in flames right in front of me. God, I hated doing this to him. But the truth is a bitch, and it had to come out. Cyrus was the cancer in this pack. I’d bet my life on it. "You got proof?" Dad whispered. He was practically begging me to tell him I was wrong.

"Nope. No proof," I said, throwing him a bone. "But if Cyrus thinks Finnian kicked the bucket, he’ll get sloppy. That's our shot to nail him. Look, I want to be wrong. Seriously, pray that I am. But my gut is screaming at me. This whole setup smells like trash. We got a rat in the house, and that rat is the one who gutted Finnian."

"You're jumping to conclusions, Lyra."

"Like hell I am." I pointed a finger at Finnian's limp body. "Think about it. He dragged his bleeding ass all the way from the attack site straight to my bedroom. Why? Because he knew his attacker. He realized he couldn't trust a single soul in this pack anymore. If some random rogue jumped him, he would've just mind-linked the guards. But he shut up and ran. Straight to us. Straight to you."

"That still doesn't pin it on Cyrus," Dad muttered.

"True. But Cyrus vanished into thin air right after. He either bolted, or he's six feet under. And guess what? If he thinks Finnian is dead, a dead man isn't going to stroll back into camp."

"So... if Cyrus walks back through that door... he's our guy?"

"It makes him look guilty as hell, yeah. But I'm not throwing him in the dungeons yet. We wait for Finnian to wake up and talk. Hell, I'll even play nice with Cyrus to see what he knows. I don't execute people just because they give me the creeps." Dad gave a slow nod. I grabbed a glass and started chugging.

"We're in for a long night," the doc grunted, shoving more gauze into the bloody mess of Finnian's chest. "Lyra, listen to me. Do not stop stuffing your face. Your wolf is burning through your blood supply to keep him alive. She needs fuel, or you're both going down."

"Any progress?" Dad got up, hovering over the bed. I spun around to the food cart and started shoving sandwiches down my throat.

The doc nodded, wiping sweat from his forehead. "A little. The skin is finally starting to knit."

"Hey, Dad?" I slammed down another glass of water. Something was wrong. The food on this cart wasn't hitting the spot. My insides did a violent flip. Deep down, Umbra was clawing at the cage, begging to break out, even though she knew it would ruin everything.

"Yeah, sweetie?"

"I need steak. Tons of it. Raw, rare, I don't care. And get me more juice." I ripped another sandwich in half, suddenly feeling like I hadn't eaten in years. "Just get me meat. Cold, bloody, whatever."

"Lyra?" Dad rushed over, staring at my face. I could feel my eyes burning, changing color.

"I'm barely holding Umbra back," I gasped, choking down more bread. "I don't know what the hell is happening, but I need meat. Right now!"

The doc froze, his eyes wide. "Do not shift, Lyra! I know you're new to this and your wolf is wild, but if you shift, you break the connection. Finnian dies." I gave a tight nod, forcing my eyes back to the food. Just keep chewing. Keep swallowing. I'd be fine. Right? What the actual hell was happening to me?

*It's Rune,* Umbra's voice echoed in my head, shaking with pure hunger. *Finnian's wolf is awake. He's demanding protein to patch his human up, and he's blasting that craving straight into us. But the guy doesn't know his own strength. Terra is going absolutely feral for it. I'm supposed to be the sane one here, but I'm slipping. You're feeling Terra's hunger, Lyra.* I felt Umbra dig her mental claws into my psyche, fighting like hell not to force the shift.

"Rune's awake," I panted, my voice shaking. "He's blasting his protein cravings into my head. He just wants to save Finnian." I lunged for another sandwich. Nothing. The tray was totally bare. I stared at the empty cart, my heart hammering. "Fuck!" I started to shake violently.

"Lyra, breathe. They're coming with more," Dad said, dropping to his knees in front of me. "Just hold on, baby girl."

I shook my head, tears blurring my vision. "I can't. I really can't." My jaw started to stretch. My bones were popping, reshaping. "The hunger... it's too much!" Suddenly, a sharp, blinding pain shot through my thigh. Dad had slammed his claw right into my leg. I shrieked, my vision snapping back to reality.

I saw the agony in his eyes, but his voice was steady as a rock. "Look at me, baby girl. Keep your eyes on mine. The omegas are loading up the first meat cart right now. They're running as fast as they can. Just stay with me. You hear me?" He started to pull his claw out, but my bones immediately began to warp again. I grabbed his wrist and shoved his claw right back into my flesh, deeper this time. "Lyra!" he gasped.

I threw my head back, gritting my teeth through the pain. "I am not letting him die."
