Chapter 461

Dead silence on the line. The nurse yanked me forward. I let out a sharp grunt.

"Whoa, what's going on over there?" Orion's voice spiked with panic.

The nurse chuckled. "Just shifting Lyra so I can wipe her back down and wrap her chest, relax."

"Wrap her chest?" Orion stammered. "Wait, why?"

I let my head drop, totally embarrassed. The nurse just smirked.

"Well, we had to shred her shirt to get to those busted ribs," she said, her voice dripping with amusement. "Cut her bra right off too. So, yeah, we're wrapping her up for privacy's sake."

A sharp gasp hissed through the speaker.

But she wasn't done. "Plus, some guy called earlier and screamed his head off at the doctor. Doc's terrified of whoever that was. Didn't want to risk any... misunderstandings."

The nurse gave me a heavy eyebrow wiggle, pointing her chin at my phone. I shook my head, but a grin slipped out anyway. She winked. "I'm just doing the chest wrap so the doctor can actually come in and do his job on her ribs without getting sued."

Another awkward beat of silence.

Orion cleared his throat. "Right. Uh. Just make sure she's taken care of." More silence.

I hacked out a dry cough. The nurse giggled. "Alright, raise those arms as high as you can. Let's tuck these in."

I snorted. "My king," I said, ignoring Jenn's sudden blush. "Look, since the mating bond is trashed and I just got jumped, can you sign off on my pack transfer? I need to be out of here by midnight."

"God, I missed that voice," he murmured, soft.

My brows knitted together. "I asked if—"

"I heard you," he cut in. "Yes. You have my permission. Where are you heading?" He knew. He definitely knew.

"I want to try my luck back on my dad's old pack lands."

The nurse paused, then pulled the final tuck tight.

"All set here," Jenn said, shoving the call button into my palm. "Buzz us when you're done." She helped me lean back. A sharp hiss escaped my teeth as my back hit the mattress. The door clicked shut, leaving me alone with the phone.

"Are you out of your mind?" Orion asked, sounding actually worried.

"It's the only move my wolves agree on. Juniper's pack is an option, but she'd be the Alpha..."

"And you're way stronger than her," Orion finished for me.

"Exactly."

"Umbra would force you to take her crown."

"And I'm not doing that to Juniper," I sighed. "I'll be fine. I'll scout the place first, check for rogue activity." I let out a dry laugh. "Seriously, if it's crawling with rogues, I'll just bounce to another pack."

A long pause.

"Fine," he finally muttered.

"Can you fax the approval letter straight to my office?"

"Why there?"

"Because if you send it to Magnus, he'll 'lose' it. Then he'll spend the next week badgering me to marry his kid."

Orion growled, then relented. "Fine. Sending it to your office now."

"Wait. Hold on."

He groaned. "Now what?"

"I have to actually get there first, genius."

*He's a dude, they aren't exactly rocket scientists,* Umbra snorted in my head.

*Or,* Terra chimed in, *maybe he's just so starstruck talking to you that his brain short-circuited.*

I snorted out loud. Orion laughed too. "Yeah, fair point. My bad." I heard him rap his knuckles on a table. "Just text me when you arrive. I'll send it to you first, then hit Magnus with it."

"Thanks, my king."

He hung up before I could even reply. I sighed and mashed the call button.

Rhys and Jenn walked back in. "Ready?"

I nodded.

"Awesome. Let's get you wrapped up and out the door." Rhys grabbed the compression bandage and started winding it around me. "We just need this to hold everything together until whatever poison is in your system flushes out and you start healing."

"Thanks," I mumbled.

Rhys stepped around to look me in the eye. "Jenn says you're planning to head to the office today?" I nodded. "Look, you need to be incredibly careful. You've got internal bleeding, and your ribs are basically glass right now. One wrong twist and they'll rip right through your skin again."

"I know. But I have to go."

He stared at me for a second, then sighed. "Fine." He held out a hand, and Jenn grabbed my other side. "Let's get you up."

I puffed my cheeks, sliding my legs over the edge of the bed.

"Wait," Jenn said, grabbing a shirt. She slid it over my head, helping me navigate the sleeves while I growled through the pain. "There. Got you."

They hauled me to my feet.

And then I threw up.
