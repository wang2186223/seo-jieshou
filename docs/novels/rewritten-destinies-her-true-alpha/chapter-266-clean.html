Chapter 266

My stomach did a flip. Ugh, gross. "Smile," Terra hissed, shoving her way to the front of my mind. "Grin and take the idiot's offer. We need to get close to him."

I dropped my eyes from the letter. My inner wolf was losing her mind, pacing and snarling. *What do you mean you dumped him?* I screamed inside my own head. *I thought we were holding off! When did you pull this crap?* I looked around for Umbra.

Terra glared right back, zero regrets. "Remember when Raven came over to stir up trouble? I saw my shot and took it. Kept you out of the loop so you could focus on real shit."

Real shit? Like what, Terra? What the hell is bigger than my own wolf rejecting her damn mate?

Umbra burst through the mental tree line, skidding to a halt right between us. "You lied," she whimpered. Her voice cracked, sounding just as wrecked as I felt. "We had your back. We could've helped."

"Yeah, we should've. But you shut us out. Why the hell keep it a secret until now?" My chest felt hollow, like I'd taken a fist to the ribs. A whole year of bonding, of busting our asses to heal, and she still locked us out. Umbra and I had finally stopped blaming each other, finally moved past the trauma, and all this time, Terra was playing us. It ruined everything we built.

"My bad." Terra didn't sound sorry at all. She just kept pacing in tight circles. "But you two were boy-crazy, and I needed some damn peace. I needed to heal. And hell no, I wasn't dealing with that annoying-ass prick every single day. Following us around like a sad puppy." She spun around, her fur bristling. "He killed me! He slaughtered me and my baby, and then I had to wake up and repeat this whole nightmare. You guys don't get it."

That hit me like a physical blow. My chest throbbed. My baby. My little boy. I remembered holding him, then losing him, and now my wolf was acting like he only belonged to her? That cut deep. A heavy silence fell over us before I finally whispered, "He killed *us*. And *our* baby. We share one body, one soul. Every win, every loss, every crush, and yeah, every single heartbreak. We share it all. He was my kid, too. I whispered to him every single night. I sang to him. I ate right so he'd grow. I built his nursery, and I went to every single ultrasound. He was mine, Terra."

I felt her pain roll off her in waves. But then she growled. "Yeah? Well, you didn't have to feel your heart getting shredded every single second." She turned to walk away, then snapped back. "No! Screw that. You don't get to guilt-trip me for surviving." She marched right back up to me. "We'll fight about this later. Right now, put on a fake smile. Act like you're actually thinking about mating him. First, we need that job to get close. Second, you promised we'd get payback."

"She's not wrong," Umbra muttered. She still sounded miserable, but she was on board. "This gets us close to Magnus and Damon."

I pasted a sweet, fake smile on my face and looked up. "Wow, I'm flattered. Honestly, I don't even know what to say..."

"Whoa, don't answer yet," he cut in quickly. "Let's just wait until graduation before you make any big moves or decisions." He was sweating it. He thought I'd shut him down. Red flag.

What's the play here? I kept my face totally blank. Why is he so damn sure I'm going to choose him and go work for his old man?

Umbra and Terra stepped right up to my mental barrier. "We're in this together. All of us," Umbra said, her voice steadying.

"Yeah, we are," Terra sighed. "Look, I'm sorry. I'll explain the whole thing later, okay?"

My nerves settled down. I gave my wolves a mental nod. But then Damon flashed me a grin, and my stomach did another nasty flip.

"Alright, people!" Juniper interrupted, stepping up. "Time for cake." She sparked a lighter and lit the candles. "Happy birthday to you..." The whole room chimed in, belting out the song. As the last note faded, everyone went wild. "Happy twenty-first, Lyra!" Juniper planted a kiss on my cheek and shoved the cake under my nose. "Blow 'em out! Make a wish!"

I squeezed my eyes shut. Inside my head, my wolves and I made the exact same wish at the exact same second: *Let us find a way to put Damon and Magnus in the dirt.* My wolves burst out laughing, howling in my head as I grinned and blew out the flames.
