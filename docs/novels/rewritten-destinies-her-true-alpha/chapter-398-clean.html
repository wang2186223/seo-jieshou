What the hell is going on? Terra linked me, but I was clueless.

"Shut your traps," Magnus barked. Everyone went dead silent. "Astrid. How much longer, damn it?"

She sighed, looking down at a little kid. Umbra growled deep in my chest. Wait. Is that...?

Yep. One of the boys from Lyra Bloom’s class. The ones we checked on last night.

Oh my god. You saved him. Terra crept closer, straining to catch Astrid's whispers.

Astrid stood up. "Give him a week to mature."

"You sure about that?" Magnus growled.

"Hell no, I'm not sure! The spell doesn't come with a damn timer. I told you this from day one. We wait for the wolf to kick the bucket, then we grab the power."

"And that links straight to the mother's magic?" Gideon Stone asked.

"Yes! Jesus, how many times do I have to say it?" Astrid snapped, turning on them. "I'm out. See you in a week."

"Hold up." Gideon glared, reaching out a hand.

"Oh," Astrid smirked. "Right." She tossed him a tiny vial. He caught it clean. He smiled, but his jaw was tight as hell. "You know, for a guy who got dragged into this, you're looking pretty eager." She winked, stepping closer. "When this wraps up, maybe we hook up. I've got a thing for older guys." She leaned in. "Just ask Gareth."

Gideon forced a smile. "Sounds like a plan." He leaned down. "Just keep my kid breathing until the harvest."

She kissed his cheek. "You got it." Then she shifted and bolted into the trees.

My heart was pounding like crazy, but I froze. I had to see how this ended. Magnus growled, turning to Ronnie. "Did you find out where she ran off to?"

Ronnie's face hardened. "She went to that slut's pack. The one Lyra brought home. Astrid... Tonya?" He looked at Damon, who burst out laughing.

"Juniper," Damon said, squatting down to grab a twig. "She went to Juniper's place. Makes sense. That's Lyra's ride-or-die."

Ronnie glared at him, but Damon stood up. "Exactly what I fucking said."

Ronnie growled. Magnus waved him off. "Drop it. She has to show up for the mating ceremony anyway, right?" He looked at Damon, who nodded.

"No way Lyra gets mated without her mom. And her mom won't miss it. Plus, she doesn't trust me for shit."

Gideon laughed. "Can you blame her?" He doubled over, laughing harder while Damon glared. "What? Just being real. I wouldn't trust you with my kids either."

Damon stared, then smirked. "Smart move." They both cracked up.

Magnus rolled his eyes, shaking his head. "Whatever. We'll just snatch her then. The spell doesn't care if she's willing. Just needs the bond and the kid. We grab her, force the marriage again, and chain her up until she pops out a baby."

Terra lost it. She jumped up, growling.

Every head snapped our way.

Oh, shit. I stood up, screaming in my head. *Terra, run!* She turned and bolted into the dark woods.

"Who the hell was that?"

I ran like hell, but we were already running on empty. Umbra shoved forward, taking the wheel from Terra.

*Sorry, but we gotta move, and shifting is out.* Her voice was quiet but dead serious.

Total silence behind me, except for heavy footsteps heading right for my spot.

"No clue. Don't know the scent. But how is she moving so quiet?" Damon sounded completely baffled.

A beat of silence. Then Magnus lost his mind. "Are you brain-dead, or do I have to hold your hand for every little thing?"

Silence again.

I barely heard Damon's muffled, "What?"

Magnus slammed something, roaring, "Go after her, you idiot!"

I heard them shift. One by one, heavy paws started tearing up the dirt behind me. Growls, then a full-on stampede. Even Gideon Stone—the guy who knew I'd be out here—was hunting me down.

Gideon. That bastard.

*Why did he want us to see that?* Terra panted, her energy fading fast, though Umbra was pushing our body to the absolute limit.

*I think he needed us to know,* I thought back. *Maybe it started as a joke with his buddies. A 'what if' scenario. Then he fell for Mara Croft.*

Umbra panted, agreeing. *Yeah. Probably somewhere in the middle. He thought they were just assholes talking trash about the girls they used. Then he found his actual mate. But he couldn't just dump his friends.*

*Until his own kid got branded,* Terra added. Umbra agreed.

*I actually feel bad for him,* Terra sighed, making us hesitate. But Umbra kept pushing. *Think about it. You try to stay loyal to your boys, love your mate, and you think magic is just bullshit. Then boom—your kid is branded, sick, dying. You know exactly why, but you can't tell your mate because it's a massive betrayal. It was all a joke until it got way too real, and now your kid's life is on the line.*

A heavy pit sank in our stomachs. *Doesn't matter. Before we figure out what to do with Gideon and Mara, we have to get out of here and save those kids.*

Terra stumbled back behind our mental wall, leaving Umbra in charge.

*I'm done. Out of gas.* She collapsed and instantly passed out.

*Umbra?* I called out. *You good?*

*No. This body is too slow. They're catching up. I don't know what to do,* she cried back. Then I heard it. Heavy thuds right on our heels. Way too close.

I stepped up to run with her. *How do I help?* We merged, combining our strength. I reached into our magic and pumped it straight into our legs.

*How are you doing that?* Umbra gasped, shocked.

*Magic, baby.* I grinned, focusing on her paws. Every time she pushed off the dirt, a tiny burst of magic shot her forward.

We started flying.

*Can you keep this up?* she pushed, putting some distance between us and the guys.

*I don't—*

Boom. Something hit me from the side. We rolled hard across the dirt, a chunk of fur ripping right off our shoulder. Terra woke up whimpering. Umbra and I scrambled up, baring our fangs at... Gideon Stone.

*You son of a bitch,* Umbra snarled.

Gideon quickly shifted back to human. "Look, I don't care if you're Lyra's friend or working with her, but Magnus has this place locked down. Magic traps everywhere. How you even got in is a miracle." He paced around. I looked away from his naked body. No thanks. "I figured Lyra could use her magic to sneak a peek. Damn it." He came back, whispering fast. "I had to catch you. I'm sorry. I'll text Lyra the second I'm back to let her know we got you. Magnus won't buy that you got away from me."

A twig snapped nearby. The panic in his eyes vanished. A cold, dead look took over. The Gideon I knew was gone. This was a stranger.

"Over here!" he yelled. "I got her, but she won't shift. You think you can force her, Magnus?"

My stomach dropped. They couldn't find out who I was. Footsteps closed in. Soon, a bunch of naked guys surrounded me.

"Maybe I need to borrow something from Astrid or the twins," Magnus muttered, crouching near my head. I snapped at his face. He smirked. "Feisty."

He grabbed the back of my neck, hard. I let him. For now.

"I'm gonna love breaking you, bitch," he whispered, leaning in. "And oh, I will break you. Once you're human, I'm screwing you every single night until you beg me to stop. But I won't." His sick grin made me want to puke. "I'm gonna shatter you so bad you'll be begging for me by the end."

He got too close. I snapped my jaws right at his face, catching him completely off guard.
