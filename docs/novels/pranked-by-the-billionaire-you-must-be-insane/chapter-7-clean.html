Chapter 7

One look. That’s all it took to read the whole damn page.

Four years. Lyra lived under the Wynn roof for four whole years, and her entire life’s cost fit on a single, flimsy piece of paper?

If this leaked, high society would think the Wynns went flat broke. They’d look like cheapskates who starved their own kid.

Wallace stared hard at the plastic card. "Run the numbers on this. Now."

The butler snatched the card and the paper, scurrying off to double-check.

Beverly opened her mouth to complain, but Wallace’s face looked like thunder. She shut her trap, rolling her eyes. "Seriously? You’re buying this?" she hissed. "The brat is playing hard to get. It’s a total mind game."

"She wants us to feel guilty. She’s begging for pity, trying to make us soft so we crawl back begging."

No way in hell did Lyra only spend half a million in four years. A basic maid cost way more than that. Beverly knew this trick. She’d seen trashy girls play it a thousand times.

Meanwhile, inside the SUV.

Rhys sat stiff, his buzzed head barely moving as his hands gripped the wheel. He shot a quick look in the rearview. In the back, the girl had her eyes shut, totally checked out.

"Yo, Boss," Rhys said, his voice buzzing with hype. "Now that you’re done with the Wynns, we heading straight back to the Gray Zone? The crew’s dying to see you."

Lyra cracked one eyelid. "For now..." she murmured.

Her phone buzzed, cutting her off. An unsaved Vaelion City number flashed on the screen.

She swiped up. A panicked, older guy’s voice blasted through the speaker. "Miss! It's Silas. Your dad sent me to grab you, but—damn, I’m so sorry—I got wrecked on the way to the Wynns. I’m stuck in the ER at Starlight Royal right now."

Lyra squinted. *Miss? Butler?*

Hold on. The Wynns said her real family lived in some trashy, run-down slum on the edge of Vaelion. Those fancy words didn't scream "ghetto" at all.

"Miss... I really screwed up. I'm so sorry I'm late," Silas groaned, sounding miserable.

Lyra didn't even blink. "Send the address. I'll drive."

Silas started stammering another apology, trying to spit out the location, when a loud shout cut him off.

"Hey! Bed one's family! Anyone here for bed one? Her BP is tanking, heart rate is through the roof! We’ve got internal bleeding—get her to the OR, now!"

Silas gasped. He yelled out, "Doctor! Over here! What’s happening to her?"

The doctor sounded grim. "She’s crashing. The scan shows a bleed right next to a main artery. We need to cut her open immediately."

Silas panicked. "Then do it! Save her! I don't care what it costs, just fix her!"

"It’s not a cash issue," the doctor sighed, sounding stressed. "The bleed is in a nasty spot, and her heart is weak. The risk is insane. Honestly? She’s got less than a ten percent shot."

Translation: no doctor wanted to touch this with a ten-foot pole.

Silas’s voice shook, but he tried to hold it together. "This is Starlight Royal! You guys have the best docs in Vaelion! Is there seriously nobody brave enough to do this?"

"Elias, you’re the hotshot surgeon here. You can't do anything?"

Right then, a nurse sprinted over. "We’re losing her! Bed one's BP is still tanking, and her pupils are blown! If we don't move, she’s dead in ten minutes!"
