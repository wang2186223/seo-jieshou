The way the international students performed this morning, the competition was fierce. Breaking into the top three wouldn't be easy for Lydia.

But Lydia just smiled, totally unfazed. "I believe in myself. What about you two? Think you'll make it to the afternoon round?"

That shut Marilyn and Yara up instantly. The truth was, neither of them felt sure about advancing. They'd fumbled through so many questions, it was probably the worst exam either of them had ever taken. Of course, there was no way they'd ever admit that out loud.

Dr. Tomlinson watched them, disappointment written all over his face. He'd had high hopes for both girls, but they'd been too full of themselves, not listening to anyone's advice. Now, regret wouldn't do them any good.

"Lydia, what should we do about the afternoon competition? Most of our students look completely lost," Mr. Russo asked, his worry obvious.

Both professors were anxious. The competition's theme was all about Al diagnostics and robotic surgery, and that was exactly where their team was weakest. Even if some students managed to squeak by with the theory test this morning, what about the afternoon Q&A or tomorrow's hands-on round? They'd all come here so confident, and now it looked like they might have to leave with their tails between their legs. Just thinking about it was embarrassing.

Lydia thought for a second. "Why not ask in the group chat? If anyone feels like they passed the theory test, we could use the two hours after lunch for an emergency training session. We'll focus on Al diagnostics and robotic surgery. If someone's not interested, that's fine too, no pressure."

Mr. Russo's eyes brightened. "That's a great idea, but... who's going to run the training?"

"If anyone wants to join, I'll do it," Lydia offered.

Both teachers were thrilled. Relief washed over their faces like a wave.

"Perfect, Lydia. We'll go with your plan."

Marilyn and Yara exchanged looks, almost laughing.

"You? Lead the training? Lydia, do you think you're some kind of professor?" Marilyn couldn't help rolling her eyes. She honestly thought Lydia was out of her mind.

Lydia just shot them a look, not interested in arguing. "Lost cause," she muttered.

And with that, she walked away.

Connor was already waiting for her outside the main entrance.

Marilyn and Yara were fuming. Lydia was so full of herself. They couldn't wait to see her embarrass herself on a live stream, washing her ha upside down for everyone to see.
