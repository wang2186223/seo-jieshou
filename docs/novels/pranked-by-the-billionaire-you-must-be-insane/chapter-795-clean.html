Chapter 803

Lyra agreed. Just like that.

Vivienne’s heart did a happy flip. Hook, line, and sinker. What a total dumbass, trusting her crew like that. Just wait. Lyra was about to get dragged through the dirt by the exact people she was sticking her neck out for.

Vivienne opened her mouth to tell Elara’s security to get to work. But she got cut off.

Lyra looked up, her eyes locking onto Vivienne. Cold as ice. "If we’re doing this, nobody gets a pass," Lyra said. "And since you brought it up, you go first."

Vivienne shrugged. "Fine by me. I’ve got nothing to hide, right? Let's get it over with."

She threw her chin up, spread her arms wide, and dared them to touch her. Zero fear.

Inside, she was practically vibrating. She couldn't wait to see the guards pull that massive diamond out of Phoenix’s bag. Lyra would look like a total fool, completely ruined, with zero defense. Vivienne had to fight to keep from laughing out loud.

Elara frowned, cutting a glance at Lyra.

Lyra gave a tiny, barely-there nod.

Elara waved her hand at her two female bodyguards.

The guards stepped up. They did a quick, professional pat-down on Vivienne, then popped her clutch open for everyone to see. Just lipstick, powder, and her phone.

"Clean," the guard said, her voice flat. "Nothing on Miss Isabella or in her purse."

Vivienne smirked at Lyra. "Happy now? I'm clean. Your turn, or rather, your friends' turn."

Phoenix rolled her eyes and spat, "Yeah, whatever. Bring it. Do it already."

The guard walked over, patted Phoenix down, and came up empty.

Vivienne cleared her throat. "Wait. Didn't Miss Iris have a shoulder bag when she got here? If we're doing this right, we check everything."

Phoenix’s eyes went razor-thin.

She was hot-tempered, sure, but she wasn't an idiot. She’d left that bag in the dressing room right after slipping into her gown. Since then, she’d been stuffing her face in the main hall, nowhere near the back rooms. But Vivienne? Vivienne had just gone back there to swap out of that knockoff dress. And now she was pointing fingers at a bag sitting in that exact room.

Oh, hell no.

The psycho had messed with her stuff. That’s why she wanted this search so bad. Phoenix’s gut had been dead on. The real thief was playing the damn victim.

If those guards pulled the diamond out of her bag... look, Phoenix didn't give a shit about her own reputation. She didn't belong to this fancy-ass crowd anyway, and their gossip meant nothing to her. Worst case, she'd just wait until this stupid party was over and beat the absolute crap out of this crazy bitch.

But Lyra? Lyra would be dragged. They’d trash her name, question her character, everything.

Phoenix glared at Vivienne, her eyes turning straight-up lethal. Her brain was working overtime, trying to figure out how to take the hit and keep Lyra out of the blast zone.

Vivienne actually took a step back, visibly flinching. Phoenix was staring at her like she was already six feet under. It was a cold, terrifying promise of violence.

Vivienne bit her lip, trying to shake it off, and blurted, "What's the matter, Miss Iris? Got something to hide from the Prince?"

"Go ahead. Check it," Lyra said, her voice totally flat as she looked up. "Let's check everything so nobody has anything to whine about. Lead the way to the dressing room."

Vivienne smirked. Hook, line, and sinker.
