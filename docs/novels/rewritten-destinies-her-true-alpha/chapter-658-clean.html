Chapter 623

I blinked. Boom—back in the damn cell. No more smoke. Every single eye was locked on me. "Uh, what?" I muttered.

Orion and my old man just snorted. But Mom? She rushed over and slammed her hands onto my cheeks. "Are you okay, baby?"

I tilted my head, flashing her a grin. "Yeah, totally. Why wouldn't I be?"

She stared hard, searching my face. "You've been out for like twenty minutes, sweetie. You were coughing, choking in that smoke, and then... nothing. We literally thought you were dead."

Suddenly, my throat felt weird. My eyes shifted. A soft green light—my own magic—started glowing and reflecting off her skin. She gasped, sucking in air. "Oh my god... your eyes..."

"Relax, I won't hurt her. Just like your Moon Celestia, I chose this girl." The voice coming out of my mouth was a creepy, layered echo. Definitely not mine. Mom tried not to flinch, but she bowed anyway, backing up until her spine hit the wall next to Dad.

"Please," she whispered, gesturing toward the guy tied to the chair.

Caspian was shaking like a leaf as we stepped up. "No, please... you can't do this to me."

My face stretched into a grin. It felt way weirder than when the Moon Celestia took over. Morrigan let out a low chuckle. "My bad, kiddo. Haven't hijacked a human body since ancient Greece." She locked eyes with Caspian. "Now, what exactly can't I do to you, mutt?" Her voice dropped, cold and deadly.

Caspian thrashed in his seat. "You can't just kill me! Doesn't that make you the bad guy here?"

We burst out laughing. "Me? A bad guy? I'm a goddess, pal. A primordial force. I literally built this world. People have worshiped me for thousands of years. I hand out magic, mother the witches, heal the sick, protect travelers, and guide the young. Earth, sea, sky—that's all me. And you, some trash-tier wolf, think you can call me evil?" We laughed again, and bam—our hand shot out, wrapping tight around his windpipe. "You slaughtered my kids. You corrupted my youth. And you think some cheap guilt trip is gonna stop me from getting payback?" She leaned right into his face.

"I... didn't..." Caspian choked out, his throat getting squeezed to hell.

"Hey, you know the difference between the magic I give away and the magic I actually keep?" He just stared, giving a tiny, pathetic shake of his head. "Their magic is watered down. It only works if they're strong. But mine?" She grinned, squeezing even harder. His face turned a nasty shade of purple. "My magic is straight-up divine." She let go, and he slumped forward, hacking up a lung.

Morrigan raised our finger, pointing it right at Caspian. *Carna spo kera speta tru.* "If you ever get strong enough to pull it off, this spell will give you everything you need," Morrigan's voice whispered inside my head. "Keep your mouth shut about it. Anyone else tries it, they'll burn from the inside out and die screaming."

*How do I know if I'm strong enough?* I whispered back. She just chuckled.

*You'll know.* Ugh. Super vague, super annoying. But she was already focusing back on Caspian. "When did you turn on your own people?" Simple question.

Caspian leaned back, still gasping for air. "I don't know the exact year... but it was way before the old king got iced." His eyes practically popped out of his skull. "Wait... how did you make me say that?"

Our lips curled up. "Magic, duh." She walked over to lean against the wall. "Did you sell out your pack to the human government?"

Caspian clamped his teeth together, trying to fight it, but it was useless. "Yes! Roric got cozy with some politician twenty or thirty years ago. We set up a lab near their capital. We've been shipping wolves there ever since."

"Where's the lab, exactly?" Orion barked. Caspian shut his mouth tight.

"Answer him," Morrigan growled. A spark of magic snapped out, making Caspian shriek.

"I don't know the exact spot! Evan ran that show. I was in charge of something else!"

"What?" Orion stepped in closer, like he already knew what was coming.

"I was in charge of slaughtering the Royal Family." Caspian smirked right at Orion. "And yeah, I volunteered for the job."

Orion lunged to punch him, but Morrigan stepped in and blocked him. "Any last words before I harvest your soul?"

Caspian glared up at us, sneering. "I hope you guys said your goodbyes."

Our head tilted. "Oh? Why's that?"

Caspian grinned just as a frantic howl blasted through the mind-link. It was Finnian, sounding totally panicked: *We're under attack!* "Because," Caspian sneered, "I was just stalling you."

Everyone panicked. "Go," we said, turning around. "I'll clean up here and send Lyra to back you up." The goddess waved her hand, and boom—they vanished into thin air. She turned us back to Caspian. "They can handle it." She looked at the other goddess. "Anything else you want to ask him?"

Phoebe stepped up beside us. "Are the humans planning to launch an attack?"

I was praying he'd say no. But a massive grin spread across his face. "You spent all these years crying over your creations, but you didn't do jack to save the Lycans. Now you wanna play hero and save us?" He laughed.

"Who says I didn't save them?" She scoffed, turning her back. "Finish him."

Morrigan shoved our hands straight into his chest. Instead of ripping out his heart, she yanked out two glowing orbs. One was bright like moonlight; the other was a dirty, muddy brown. Caspian choked, staring wide-eyed at the two balls of light in our hands. "What... are... those?"

Our grin widened. "Your souls." She tossed the glowing moon one to Phoebe. Then, as the light faded from Caspian's eyes, she crushed the muddy brown one to dust. "No reincarnation for you, trash."
