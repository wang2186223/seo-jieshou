Chapter 379

We all scrambled to our feet. I locked eyes with Mara Croft. "You ready to swear this thing?"

She sucked in a breath, gave a sharp nod, and said, "Yeah. Let's do it."

"Awesome. Just say what I say." I fed her the lines. "Ready?" Another nod. "I, Mara Croft Bryce, swear to keep my mouth shut to everyone but the Goddess. Anything you say that could screw you over stays locked in my head. Nobody gets our secrets but Her, and I pray she keeps them safe."

Her eyes went wide as saucers. We all stared. She whipped around to Gideon Stone. "Oh, damn. That's what you were talking about." I just cocked an eyebrow. She let out a sudden laugh.

"Seriously, he came back from lunch with you raving about this crazy feeling of finally belonging somewhere," she said, her face turning bright red as she rubbed her chest. "Honestly? I was super jealous at first. I thought he was talking about *you*. But nope, it was just the vow. The Goddess accepting him."

Gideon grinned, nodding along. "Hey, Lyra's awesome, but nothing beats that rush." He looked at Mara. "Well, except when we mated." Then he looked over at Lyra Bloom and threw her a wink. "And, you know, the day you popped out."

I chuckled and grabbed the empty cups. "Alright, guys." I headed into my bedroom, dumped them on the nightstand, and turned back. "Gonna go check on Mom, then I'll get you guys hooked up to the shield." They all nodded. I grabbed the doorknob, pulled it open, and—holy hell. Total chaos.

"What do you mean, get the hell out?!" Ronnie roared.

"I mean exactly what I fucking said! Lyra bought this place for *me*. Not you, and definitely not fucking Blair! You lied straight to my face, so don't even try it. I talked to Magnus!"

*Smash!* Glass shattered downstairs. I bolted out of my room and flew down the stairs like a bat out of hell.

"You and your brat think you're so much fucking better than us!" Blair shrieked.

I walked right into the room, letting a tiny bit of my aura leak out. "Well, yeah. Because we are," I said, keeping my voice dead calm. No need to blow things up, just dropping facts. "Don't take it personally. We're just Alphas." I talked to them like they were toddlers, and the whole room went dead silent. "Look, I have no clue what's going on here," I lied, playing dumb. "But Ronnie, honestly? It's probably best if you and Blair crash at the pack house for a bit. Just a couple of days. Give my mom some space to cool off."

A nasty snarl ripped out of Ronnie's throat. "Are you seriously throwing me out of my own damn house?"

I shook my head. "Come on, I'd never do that. Especially not now, when Blair and I finally got our relationship back on track these last few months. This is just the smartest move for everyone. It gives you and Mom some breathing room to sort things out. Plus, Blair can be right there with Damon to finish up the mating ceremony prep. And, bonus, I can actually let my friends out of my room without making a massive scene and embarrassing the family."

Ronnie's eyes narrowed. The guy was obsessed with status, and the thought of losing face made him squirm. "Wait. Who the hell is up there?"

I dragged a hand down my face. "Elara Meadow and Rhys Thorne. You know, the omegas from work." He scoffed—classic. He didn't give a crap about omegas. "Oh, and Gideon Stone, Mara Croft, and their kid, Lyra Bloom. I was literally just showing her my plushies when you guys started screaming."

Ronnie's face went totally white. "Gideon Stone is upstairs?"

I nodded. "Yep. Look, I'm gonna head back up and let you guys deal with this. Mom, yell for me when it's over." I gave a little wave and headed back up the stairs. Perfect. This would buy me plenty of time while they wrapped up their drama, packed their bags, and got the hell out.

I clicked the lock on my door and turned around. Lyra Bloom was messing around with my stuffed animals, with Gideon and Mara hanging out right next to her. Around the corner, Rhys and Elara were whispering. "Alright, give me a second," I said, grabbing the cups and heading straight for my closet.

"Where do you think you're going?" Gideon asked, watching me swing the closet door open.

"Chill, Daddy, she's gonna show us in a sec," Lyra Bloom piped up, waving at me. I threw her a wink and shut the door behind me.

I sprinted through the cabin, locking their names into the shield one by one, and waved at the three in the living room.

"Lyra!" Ronnie squealed.

I pulled her into a quick hug and whispered, "Be right back with a surprise, okay?" I kissed the top of her head and bolted back to the closet. I yanked the door open and gestured for them to follow. "Alright, let's go." They all filed in behind me.

"Wait, why are we piling into your closet?" Gideon asked.

"Because," I said, "I'm showing you our way out."
