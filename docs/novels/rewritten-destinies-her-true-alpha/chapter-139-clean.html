Chapter 139

The guy smirked right at me. "Yeah, I get it. Some of you figured it out," he said. "Yup, I'm Orion. And yeah, I'm the King. But look, drop the 'Your Majesty' crap. Seriously, don't call me that. At least not in this room." He threw me a wink and spun toward his laptop. He hit the power button on the projector, synced his screen, and faced us again while the machine booted up. "But hey, first things first."

Boom. He unleashed his aura. It hit us like a freight train. Damn. I'd never felt power like this before. My knees buckled, and I had to fight just to stay on my feet. Everyone else hit the floor instantly. Except me. I stood there a split second too long, and he caught it. His eyes went wide. He cranked up the pressure, aiming it right at me. I just winked back, then slowly dropped to my knees to play along. He gave me a quick, approving nod and strolled to the front of the class.

"Look, I'm only teaching this one class," he said, spreading his hands. "So keep your mouths shut about me being here. One, I don't need girls throwing themselves at my feet. Two, this is my hobby. I like doing this, and it keeps me connected to my people. So, yeah, you get why this is a big deal. Nobody outside this room hears a word. The rest of the program knows who I am but they're sworn to silence, and now you are too. What happens in this room, stays in this room. We talk straight here. Got it?"

"Yes," we all chimed in. Well, they did it because his alpha command forced them to. I just faked it. Honestly, I was totally lost. He's the freaking King. Why didn't his power touch me? Whatever. I shook the thought off and powered up my laptop.

"Alright, let's do introductions. Names and ranks," Orion said. "I'll go first. I'm Alpha King Rowen, Royal Lycan pack." He scanned the room and jabbed a finger at the next guy. "You. Go." We went around the room, just like last time, until it was my turn. Orion arched an eyebrow and flicked his finger, gesturing for me to get up.

"I'm Alpha Lyra," I said, standing up. "My dad's from Silvermoon, and my mom's Oakenfang. So, depending on where I am, that's my pack."

Orion grinned. "Well, look at that. Lyra, in the flesh. Who would've thought we'd cross paths like this?" He stepped closer. "So, did you get what you wanted?"

"Yeah. Got exactly what I needed out of that traitor," I said, sitting back down with a smirk. "Thanks for greenlighting the torture, by the way." The room erupted into terrified whispers. My grin only got wider.

Orion's smile vanished. He didn't like what he was hearing. He whipped around to face the guys whispering and staring at me. I caught a few of their words and just shook my head.

"Let me guess," Orion growled, his deep voice tight with anger. "You three don't have ranks, do you?"

"Yeah," two of them mumbled.

"I'm a gamma," the third one piped up.

Orion stepped right up to them, glaring them down. "Then you should damn well know better than to mouth off about an Alpha protecting her pack." He rapped his knuckles on the gamma's desk. "You in line for first or second gamma?"

The guy leaned back, sweating. "First gamma. Why?"

I rolled my eyes and leaned forward, totally annoyed. "Because that job actually means something, genius. If you're the second gamma, you're the head warrior. Your job is training your guys to protect the pack by any means necessary. Yeah, that includes torture. And if you're the first gamma, you protect the Luna. Period. What happens if she gets snatched and you grab a suspect? What's your play? Sit down for a nice little chat and hope he plays nice?"

"Well, no, obviously not," the guy scoffed, letting out a nervous laugh.

"Then maybe—just maybe—use your brain and think about *why* I did it, not just *what* I did. Torture is a tool. Ranked wolves use it to keep the pack safe. We don't just do it for kicks." The three guys wouldn't even look at me. "You think we do this stuff for fun?"

"No, I guess not," the gamma mumbled, shaking his head.

"Then why the hell did you call her sadistic?" Orion snapped. "Look at the facts here, people. She's a young Alpha. She doesn't even have a formal pack structure backing her up. She was terrified and just wanted to save her dad. Anyone else would've gone rogue, and honestly, nobody would've blamed her. But what did she do? She called the Council. She asked for permission. She followed the damn rules so she wouldn't abuse her power. She didn't touch a hair on that traitor's head until she got the green light to save her father." He shook his head in disgust. "That is exactly what a real Alpha looks like. You guys dragged her name through the mud, and she didn't even touch you."
