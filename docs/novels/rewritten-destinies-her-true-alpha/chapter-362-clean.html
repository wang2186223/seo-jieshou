Blair took a step back, reading his face. "Hey, so Lyra mentioned her birthday lunch," she stammered, wincing a bit. "Since we're all, like, super close now, I thought we could grab a bite while she's tied up."

I didn't miss a beat. I slid right next to her, locking my arm in hers. "Oh, Blair, seriously? Best. Idea. Ever."

She stared down at our linked arms, totally thrown. "Wait, really?"

Damon’s jaw dropped. He looked completely lost. "For real?"

I nodded like a bobblehead. "Yeah! I mean, Shan's got a point. We've spent six whole months building this friendship. It makes everything so much easier going forward, you know? No drama, no fighting over you." I flashed him a sweet smile. He started chewing on that.

Boom. Right there. I saw the exact second his brain clicked. He realized he could sneak off for a quick afternoon hookup with his side chick, especially since I wasn't giving him any action. His eyes flared with heat. Hook, line, and sinker.

"Alright, alright," Damon said, throwing his hands up. I had to fight the urge to clap. "You win. Go hang with your crew. I'll grab lunch with Blair. Besides, we've got stuff to talk about."

"Totally," Blair purred, cozying up right next to him. "I mean, your mating ceremony is literally in a few weeks." My stomach did a nasty flip, but I kept the fake smile plastered on and nodded. "Yeah, we still have a ton of wedding prep to hammer out."

"Oh, I know! I'm so stoked," I lied, nodding. I leaned in close and planted a soft kiss on Damon’s cheek. "Can't wait till I get to kiss you on the lips," I breathed right into his ear. He let out a low groan. I pulled back, wearing a smug grin. Honestly, touching him made my skin crawl, but messing with his head? Pure gold. "But seriously, you're right. Lots of details to lock in." I looked over at Blair. "Thanks for pitching in, by the way."

Blair flashed a sweet smile, but her eyes were pure venom. "Duh. You're my sister, and Damon's my bestie. You've got your hands full, so I'm just doing my part." She squeezed Damon’s arm tight. "Ready to roll?"

He glanced at me, then back at her. Right there—that was the moment his brain went completely offline. I knew he’d rushed over here to spy on what we were plotting. But the promise of a quick midday lay with Blair completely wiped his hard drive. Perfect. Just what I wanted.

"Yeah, let's go," he said. He leaned over and pecked my forehead. Gross. "See you after work? We're supposed to hit the bakery and pick out the cake."

I nodded. "Yep, sounds like a plan."

The second they walked away, I turned back to my crew. I grabbed Elara Meadow’s arm. "Alright, hustle up. We've got a reservation to catch." I threw Gideon Stone a quick wink. We marched out of the pack house, piled into my truck, and hit the gas. But the moment we cleared the main driveway and hit the road, I slammed on the brakes and pulled over.

Elara frowned, totally confused. I ignored her, spun around, and pointed a finger straight at Rhys Thorne. "You." Rhys blinked, looked left and right, then pointed at his own chest. "Yeah, you," I snapped. "What the hell happened to you?"

Rhys avoided my eyes, staring out the window. "Drop it. I don't wanna talk about it."

I scanned their faces. I could read them like an open book—they liked me, sure, but they didn't trust me. Not 100%. I shifted the truck back into drive and pulled onto the road. "Look, I get it. You don't trust me. Fair enough. That's why I'm gonna need a vow of silence before I spill my guts."

All three of them whipped their heads around to stare at me. "Wait, you've got secrets?" Gideon Stone asked, raising an eyebrow.

I nodded. "Big ones," I sighed. "But too many people are involved, so I can't just blab. I need that vow. It's the only way to make sure your lips are sealed, even if some alpha tries to force it out of you."

Gideon twisted around in the passenger seat, locking eyes with me. "So, let me get this straight. If we swear this, nobody—no matter who they are—can drag the truth out of us?"

"Bingo," I said, nodding. "And hey, if it makes you guys feel safer, I'll take the vow too." I glanced over at Gideon. "Seriously, I don't mind. That way, whatever we talk about over lunch stays strictly between us."

"Wait, so you couldn't even tell Magnus or Damon?" Elara Meadow asked, leaning forward from the back seat.

"Not a chance," I smirked. "Honestly, even without the vow, those two couldn't force a damn thing out of me. They're way too weak." The car went dead silent as they gasped. I let out a soft chuckle. "Yeah, I know I don't look the part because I keep my aura locked down, but trust me—I'm insanely strong."

Gideon let out a dry laugh. "Honestly? I'm not even shocked. Your folks were absolute powerhouses."

I swung the truck into a spot in the mall parking lot and killed the engine. I turned to them. "Alright. You guys ready for this?"
