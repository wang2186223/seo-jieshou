Chapter 237

I shoved my two buddies out of the elevator the second it hit their floor. Kept going down. Lobby. Finnian was clocking out. I gave him a quick nod, bolted past, and hit the exit.

Pretty sure he yelled my name. Didn't care. The cold morning air hit my face and I just took off. Sprinted around the block, dove straight into the trees, and boom—shifted. Umbra was out.

She stopped dead on a ridge. Let the wind whip through her fur. Closed her eyes. "God, I missed this," she breathed.

I stepped back into my headspace, sitting right next to Terra. "Hey. I promise we’ll get both of you out more." I looked her dead in the eye. "And I wanna train in your form, too."

She went rigid. Turned her head, staring right back. "Why? You got zero reason to fight as me."

I threw an arm around her shoulders. "Better safe than sorry, babe. What if we get jumped while running? We won't have time to stop and swap to Umbra." I stroked her fur. "It makes us a target. Plus, it blows our cover."

She chewed on that for a second. "Yeah, but... barely anyone even knows I exist."

I shrugged. "Juniper and Daisy do. Look, I'll get Juniper to run the wolf drills instead of me one day. I'll show up as you." I nudged her. "Come on, you don't want that?"

She shook her head, her voice barely a whisper. "I do." She looked away, eyes on the dirt. "I just... I figured you thought I wasn't worth the trouble."

Ouch. That stung. But damn, I did this to her. *I'm so sorry, Terra. Seriously. It didn't even click until you guys were gone. I totally blew you off, and that's on me.*

Her eyes went wide. Panic flashed across her face. "No! No, you didn't. That's not what I meant at all."

"Cut the crap, kiddo." Umbra's voice was soft, but she took off, weaving through the trees. "We're a team. You need something? Spit it out. We got your back."

So she'd been hiding this from Umbra, too. Great. "You're both a part of me, Terra." I dropped to my knees, wrapping her in a tight hug. "I'm so sorry we tuned you out."

"Ditto," Umbra called back. Then, her claws dug into the dirt. She skidded to a hard stop. "You."

Terra and I snapped back to reality. Frozen. Umbra let out a nasty, guttural snarl, glaring at the wolf blocking our path. "You got some fucking nerve showing your face here."

A wolf stepped out from the pitch-black shadows. Up close, his fur was a rippling, dark grey. He dropped flat onto his belly, crawling forward, inch by inch. Those icy, white-blue eyes of his locked onto Umbra. "I've been looking for you."

Umbra scoffed. "Yeah, right. I stopped buying your bullshit months ago. Right around the time I stopped looking for you." She spun to bolt, but the guy cut her off, landing right in her path. "Move."

"Please. Just hear me out." He dropped again, rolling straight onto his back, exposing his throat and belly. Total submission. Terra and I went stiff as boards.

"No male should ever show his belly to a female." Umbra's voice was pure ice. She wasn't buying it. Not one bit.

"Any guy would. Especially if he knows he royally fucked up." His deep voice sent a shiver straight down our spines. Still, Umbra turned to walk away. "Please." That desperate plea made her freeze mid-step.

"Spit it out, Shadow. Say what you came to say." Her words cut like glass. Even I flinched.

"Maybe... maybe we should listen?" Terra whispered. Umbra just snorted, turning her back on him.

"Fine. I'll listen. But don't get it twisted—you don't deserve jack shit. I'm doing this because my soul sister asked. Talk." Every word was a slap in the face. I watched him flinch, taking the hit.

"I swear to God, I didn't blow you off." Umbra scoffed, but she took a step closer. "I've been out here every single week. For months. Just looking for you. But you were gone."

His words hit hard. Umbra's stiff posture softened, just a tiny bit. "I looked for you for months. You never showed." Her voice cracked, losing its edge. "So I gave up."

He dropped low again, dragging himself forward. "I couldn't get away. Seriously. I didn't know who to trust. But I sorted it out. I've been out here waiting ever since."

"Why should I believe a word you say?" Umbra snapped.

He paused, searching for the right words, then shook his head. "I know. My word is dirt to you right now. But let me prove it. Just watch me."

"Oh yeah? And how the hell are you gonna do that?" she scoffed.

"Every other day. Four AM sharp until sunrise. I'm right here. I've always been here. That's when I run. Run with me?"
