Chapter 462

Dragging my busted leg, I hauled myself into the pack house. God, it hurt like hell. But I’d flat-out rejected Umbra and Terra’s healing. No way. I needed to reek of hospital disinfectant and fresh blood.

*You’re a total idiot, just like Orion,* Umbra snarled in my head. I dragged my feet up the stairs, moving like a snail on purpose. Let the rumors fly.

An omega rushed over, grabbing my arm. "Thanks, Lysander," I wheezed, leaning on him as we climbed.

He looked shocked, a soft smile hitting his face. "Wait, you actually know my name?"

I forced a grin. "Duh. You brought me food, like, a bunch of times. Why wouldn't I remember?"

He leaned in, whispering like we were sharing some classified info. "Most Alphas don't give a crap about us."

I let out a dry chuckle. "Well, I'm not those jerks." The laugh triggered a hacking cough. Boom—blood splattered right out of my mouth. I swiped it off with my sleeve.

"Oh my god, are you okay?" he gasped, eyes wide.

I gave a weak nod. "Yeah, fine. Just gotta hit my office and wrap up some loose ends."

He froze. "Wrap up? Wait, are you leaving?"

I nodded, making sure my chin did a little trembling act. "Yeah. Got the green light to transfer out of the pack."

He gasped, his grip on my arm tightening. "What? Why?" he cried. "We all wanted you to be our Luna! You can't just pack up and go!"

We hit the top landing. I let out a heavy, dramatic sigh. "I have to, Lysander. Blair's taking the crown. I can't sit around and watch that trainwreck." Yeah, I was milking the drama, but this kid was going to be my megaphone.

"When?" he squeaked, looking totally devastated.

I squeezed my eyes shut, forcing a single tear to roll down my cheek. "Tonight." I grabbed his shoulder. "Hey, do me a solid?"

"Name it," he said, his face falling.

"Spread the word. Tell everyone if they want to say goodbye, swing by my place. I'm grabbing dinner with Gideon Stone and Elara Meadow, but the door's open. Especially the parents of the pups—I really want to see those kids one last time."

He nodded fast. "On it. Consider it done."

We hobbled the rest of the way to the office. By the time I collapsed into my desk chair, I was running on empty.

"Hey, Lyra?" Lysander mumbled, lingering by the door.

"Yeah?" I groaned, closing my eyes to catch my breath.

"It was an honor working for you." My eyes popped open. The kid actually bowed. Like, a full-on, 90-degree bend—he almost cracked his forehead on my desk corner coming back up. "I hope you find whatever you're looking for out there."

Okay, that actually brought a real tear to my eye. "Thanks, buddy," I smiled.

He turned and walked out, leaving me alone. Time to move. I blinked back the waterworks, whipped out my laptop, and slammed the ethernet cord into the office desktop. I booted up my custom program, bypassed the security with my backdoor password, and got to work. Immediately, the office computer started taking hits. I fired back, blocking the digital attacks.

Then—crap. I forgot to text Orion. I snatched my phone, typed *I'm in*, sent it, and dove straight back into the keyboard war.

I shut down the hackers in seconds. Right on cue, the fax machine whined. I limped over, snatched the paper, and folded it up. Just then—*CRASH*. A massive smash echoed from upstairs. I grinned, limping back to my chair.

I slumped down and checked the monitor. Nice. My laptop had breached the mainframe and was ripping all the data. But before I could even celebrate, another wave of cyberattacks slammed my firewall.

I was hammering the keys when the front door exploded open. Magnus. Panic spiked in my chest. I glanced at the progress bar on my laptop. Fifty percent. Are you kidding me?

"Where the hell is she?!" Magnus roared outside, clearly hunting for Gideon.

My eyes darted between the doorway and the screen. Fifty-seven percent. Fuck, fuck, fuck.

Gideon's office door flew open. Dead silence. Just a split second before my own door burst open, I shoved my laptop behind the massive desktop monitor, hiding the screen completely.

"Hey there," I called out, keeping my eyes glued to the screen, pretending to sweat over a brutal hack.

Magnus froze, staring at me. "What the... you're here?"

I looked down at my bloody shirt, then looked him dead in the eye. He actually flinched when he saw my wrecked face. "Yeah. Obviously."
