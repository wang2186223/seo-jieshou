Chapter 716

Julian grinned and passed around the bags. The guy was a mind reader when it came to presents. He nailed everyone's taste so perfectly that saying "no thanks" wasn't even an option. Even Silas got a box. Julian played it cool—no brown-nosed sucking up, no arrogant flexing. Sure, he was a ruthless billionaire shark in the boardroom, but right here? He was just a polite kid paying his respects.

Eleanor looked at her gift. Man, he really went all out. And she knew exactly why. All this effort? It was for Lyra. The guy was head over heels, doing whatever it took to win over her people. Eleanor's smile widened. She glanced back and forth between the two of them, beaming. "Well, aren't you a sweetheart? So, you're the legendary Julian. Lyra brought you up a while back, but wow, I didn't think you'd drop by so fast."

Julian's eyes softened, staring at Lyra like she was his whole world. "Well, the second I heard Lyra was visiting, I cleared my schedule and caught the first flight out. Sorry for crashing the party without calling first."

"Oh, stop it! You didn't crash anything, you're right on time!" Eleanor let out a bright laugh.

"Hell yeah," Arthur chimed in, totally sold on the guy. "Now that you're here, you're staying. Don't even think about leaving anytime soon."

Julian smiled smoothly. "I'll hold you to that. Just hope I don't get on your nerves."

Arthur chuckled. He knew the history. The Lancasters and the Blackwoods went way back. The Blackwoods had stupid money, and Julian was the undisputed king of that empire. Young, sure, but he already ran the whole damn show. Plus, they'd watched him grow up. The kid's character was solid gold.

Just like that, the nasty vibe from Penelope and Vivienne's toxic drama evaporated into thin air.

Eleanor gulped down the last drop of her herbal soup. She reached out, squeezing Lyra's hand, her eyes tight with worry. Then she looked up at Julian. "Julian, look. Lyra's still getting used to us. She's quiet, maybe a little cold on the outside, but she's got the sweetest soul. Please... just be patient with her. Keep her safe." The old lady's voice cracked with real, raw love.

Julian's face went dead serious. "I got you, Grandma. Trust me. Lyra is my only one. I'll shield her with my life. Nobody is ever going to hurt her or make her feel small again." He turned his gaze back to Lyra, his dark eyes burning with pure, unfiltered devotion. "Honestly, finding her was the best thing that ever happened to me."

Sitting on the edge of the mattress, Lyra listened to him pour his heart out. Her thick lashes blinked. Her face stayed totally blank, but her lips twitched, just a tiny bit, into a smirk. Eleanor caught it. She chuckled. "Okay, okay. Hearing you say that? I can finally sleep easy."

"Hey, did you guys even eat yet?" Arthur asked, tossing the empty soup container aside. "Lyra got up at the crack of dawn to brew this stuff. And you look like you haven't slept a wink either." He knew the drill. Penelope had dragged her into that crazy, near-fatal street race last night. Lyra must be running on empty, and then she still woke up early to cook. Arthur felt a sharp stab of guilt. "Silas, take Lyra out for some food right now. Seriously, go eat. Nothing else matters."

Lyra didn't want to stress them out, so she just nodded. She got up and walked out of the room with Julian and Silas, heading down to grab some grub.
