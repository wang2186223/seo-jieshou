His deep voice rang out authoritatively, cracking through the air and slamming into the men seated by the table.

At that moment, none of them dared to speak up.

The reason for the full attendance was because news of Shane’s kidnapping had spread. Everybody who heard about it came to find out the truth.

If the rumor was true, then Thompson Group was in for a new change of leadership.

Shane’s eyes narrowed as Natalie’s face appeared in his mind. Recalling how she had said she would compensate him, his grim expression lightened a little.
