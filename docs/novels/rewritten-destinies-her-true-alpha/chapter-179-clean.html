Chapter 179

"Yo, Lyra?" I killed the engine, slotting the truck right into one of the VIP penthouse spots.

"Yeah?" I hopped out, slamming the door, and made a beeline for the elevator.

"What the hell is this?" Juniper scanned the concrete garage. Her brain was clearly short-circuiting, trying to piece it together. Honestly? Same here.

"Look, I promise I'll spill everything," I said, flashing a quick grin as I punched the penthouse button.

*Beep.*

"Access card required," a robotic voice droned. I whipped out the card Flint handed me, swiped it, and the doors slid shut.

"This is crazy," Juniper muttered, slumping against the metal wall.

I glanced back. "How so?"

"Dude, this place has been on total lockdown since day one. Nobody gets in but the King. But look at it—it's spotless, totally high-tech." She ran a finger along the wall. "Feels like some fancy human hotel. The King wouldn't even let the council set foot on his floor, and now he just hands you the keys?" She shook her head, sighing. "I'm totally in the dark here, aren't I?"

The doors dinged open. I grabbed her hand. "Look, I know today was a total shitshow, and this whole secret-agent routine is annoying as hell. But my gut says I gotta wait until the whole crew is here before I lay it all out." We stepped out. Boom—Flint was standing right there.

"Thought I'd hang back with your bags," Flint said, gesturing to a door. "You're in Penthouse B." He cut his eyes toward the opposite door across the hall. "And I'm guessing you already know who's bunking in Penthouse A."

We both gave a silent nod.

"Look, it blows my mind that the King handed you this floor. Not my job to question him, though. Just do me a favor—keep your guests under control. The last thing I need is to drag hysterical she-wolves away from his doorstep."

I let out a snort. "Wait, is that actually a thing?"

Flint smirked. "Well, you guys are literally the first people besides me and the cleaning crew allowed up here. But yeah, whenever the King rolls in, a whole pack of fangirl she-wolves camps out by the lobby doors."

I threw my head back, laughing. "No way. Seriously?"

Flint nodded. "Dead serious. It got to where he had to sneak out the back alley just to drive to class. That's why he turned the basement into a private garage about three years ago."

Juniper shook her head, sighing. "Man, poor guy."

Flint snorted. "Oh yeah, real tragedy. The guy literally has his pick of any girl in the pack."

I nodded. "But does he actually?"

Flint frowned, totally lost.

"Think about it," I said. "He's the King. He's gotta play the part. He can't just go around hooking up with random she-wolves. One messy affair and the tabloids would have a field day."

"Exactly," Juniper chimed in. "Plus, the whole damn council is breathing down his neck to lock down a mate."

I pointed at her. "Bingo." I swiped my card and shoved the door open. My jaw hit the floor. The place was fully loaded. "Whoa. What is all this?"

"What's wrong?" Flint looked around the room. "Not your style? I can get the guys to swap out the furniture if you want."

"No, sorry, it's not that," I said, spinning around to face him. "I just thought we had to buy all our own gear. I definitely didn't expect... this."

I turned back and stepped into the massive open-concept living area and kitchen. Right opposite the entrance, a massive floor-to-ceiling glass wall showed off a killer view of the woods. The living room was huge—a giant sectional sat on one side, facing a flat-screen mounted over a sleek, freestanding fireplace. The fireplace acted as a room divider, hiding an eight-seater dining table that flowed right into the kitchen.

"Holy crap, this is gorgeous," Juniper breathed, walking over to press her face against the glass.

I just nodded, speechless.

"Penthouses come fully loaded," Flint said, strolling into the kitchen and popping open some empty cabinets. "The lower units just get the basics—you know, the heavy stuff. The rest of the gear gets delivered tomorrow. Honestly, you guys only need to bring what you'd pack for a dorm. Sheets, toothbrushes, personal stuff. The King didn't want to bleed the smaller packs dry making them pay to furnish rooms for their kids."

I nodded, wandering down the hallway. The first door opened to a slick home office. Next door was a clean, basic bathroom. *Yeah, this'll do,* I thought, nodding. Then I pushed open the last door, and my breath hitched. It was a stunning bedroom, complete with a massive four-poster bed. It looked exactly like my old room back at my dad's pack house.

I poked my head into the closet, only to find a huge walk-through wardrobe that opened up into a second, mind-blowing bathroom. We're talking a built-in vanity, a deep soaking tub, a massive walk-in shower, and a separate linen closet.

"This is insane," I muttered, heading back to the living room.

But as I stepped out, I froze. Orion was standing right by the front door, looking completely crushed.
