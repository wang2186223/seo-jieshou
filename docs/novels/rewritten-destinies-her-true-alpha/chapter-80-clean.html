Chapter 80

I locked eyes with Freya. The second it clicked, my face split into a massive grin. "Hell yeah," I yelled, lunging out of my seat to grab her by the shoulders. "You're a freaking genius!"

She smirked, giving me a solid nod. "Damn right. You get to beat him to a pulp. Every single bruise, every cut your mom took—hand it back to him ten times worse. The Alpha's hands are tied by his own damn playbook. He tries to stop you? Boom, you call the King."

I whipped my head toward Finnian. He gave me a slow nod. "Actually, I'll buzz the King right now. Give him a little heads-up." He threw me a quick wink. "Gotta stay ahead of the game, right?"

I plopped back down, nodding along. "Damon’s been breathing down my mom's neck," I muttered, sinking into the cushions. "The guy's trying every sneaky trick to get close to me, too. But Mom locked him out. Literally barred the door."

Finnian let out a dry laugh. "Yep, that's her. She always knew exactly how to handle Alphas."

I shot him a sharp look. Underneath his laugh, I caught a nasty flash of bitterness. "You actually know why she hitched herself to Ronnie?"

Finnian scoffed, shaking his head like I was asking if water was wet. "Uh, because she loved the guy? Your dad screwed her over, so she packed up and moved the hell on."

"Except she never broke the bond," I said.

Finnian's face completely dropped. The easygoing, happy-go-lucky guy vanished. In his place was this raw, ugly mix of shock and fury. It looked totally wrong on him. He had a line, and I just crossed it.

"No way," he breathed. He stared at me like I'd lost my mind.

"When we dragged ourselves to Oakenfang, she was labeled a 'betrayed wolf.' You get what that means?" I asked.

"But she wasn't," he snapped.

I shrugged. "Doesn't matter. Nobody knew the truth. To them, she was just damaged goods with a kid. I was too young to get it then. But when I grew up? Man, I used to scream at her constantly. Until one day, I pushed her too far. I still remember it like it was yesterday."

"What are you talking about?" Finnian frowned.

"My mom drinks. Heavy."

"Bullshit," Finnian shot back instantly. "Her old man was a drunk. She swore on her life she'd never touch a drop."

I just stared at him. Hard. He shut his mouth.

"Normally, yeah, you're right," I said. "But some days? She gets so wasted she can't even stand up. A few years back, she was in the middle of one of those benders, and I went off on her. I screamed in her face. Called her every nasty name in the book for sleeping with Ronnie. For dragging us into this absolute shitshow of a pack. I kept screaming and screaming... until she just snapped."

"She snapped?" Freya leaned way forward, hanging on every word.

I nodded. "Oh, she lost it. Screamed right back at me. She went, 'You think I want this? You think I wanted my fated mate to dump me, so I had to settle for this garbage pack?'"

"Wait, what? That makes no sense," Finnian started, but I slapped my hand up to cut him off.

"She was hysterical," I said. "She told me she wanted nothing more than to just live alone with me forever. But Alpha Magnus? He cornered her. Left her zero choices."

Freya gasped, her jaw dropping. "Oh my god. No way."

I looked her dead in the eye and nodded.

Finnian just sat there, totally lost. "Serious about what? What am I missing?"

Freya sank back into her chair, looking exhausted. "Beta, come on. You know the old laws."

"Of course I know the laws," Finnian muttered. "But what the hell does that have to do with this?"

"The fact that you're clueless just proves you're a decent guy," I sighed. "And incredibly privileged."

Finnian threw his hands up in frustration. "What the hell is that supposed to mean?"

"It means you're a dude," Freya spat, her voice dripping with ice. "You don't have to worry about ancient, messed-up laws stripping away your basic rights just because of what's between your legs."

Finnian looked completely baffled. I let out a long, heavy breath. "The second we set foot in Oakenfang, Alpha Magnus triggered the Betrayed Wolf law."

"Wait, what law?" Finnian squeezed his eyes shut, racking his brain.

"If a female wolf gets rejected by her fated mate," I explained, "she becomes free-for-all property if she's still single by the next new moon. The logic is, if her fated mate threw her out, she's trash. So, any wolf strong enough can just... take her."

Finnian's eyes flew open.

"And from that second on, she's nothing but a slave," Freya added coldly.

"Hold the damn phone," Finnian roared, leaping to his feet.

"Exactly," I said, tasting the bitter poison in my mouth. "Mom had only a few weeks to find some guy to marry her before the deadline. She couldn't get a new fated mate, obviously. Ronnie had just lost his wife and had a kid to feed. So they cut a deal. Mom just wanted to keep her head down and stick to the contract. But then Ronnie decided he wanted to change the terms."

"Why the hell would she agree to change the deal?" Finnian asked.

"Because Magnus made it crystal clear," I said, my voice dropping. "If she ever divorced Ronnie, Magnus would slap that law right back on her. And until I turned eighteen, I was part of the package."
