NovelVibe

Chapter 1

"Stop! Please, just stop!" I kept begging, but they didn't give a shit. I kicked and squirmed. Nothing. "I'm your freaking Luna!" I screamed until my throat burned. Then, a laugh echoed from the next room. His laugh. It instantly crushed my spirit. "Rhys, please..." I croaked out, completely spent.

"Get the kid out, then kill it. Dump her in the woods. Let her rot or survive on her own. I don't care."

No way. He couldn't say that. It was our kid. "Our baby," I whispered.

"That thing is a mistake from the Moon Goddess. And I'm fixing it." His cold voice drifted in. Coward wouldn't even look me in the eye. "Cut her. Now. That's an order."

"Yes, Alpha."

God, coming here was a massive mistake. Giving him my whole damn life? Even bigger mistake.

"Oh, hey, doc?" A sweet, sickening voice chimed in from the hallway. Blair. My stepsister. I clenched my jaw so hard it hurt. "Skip the pain meds. Make sure she feels every single cut."

"Understood, Luna." The doctor looked at me, his eyes full of pity. But the second he grabbed the blade, I knew I was dead meat.

*I'm so sorry, sweetie. Mommy can't protect you. I wanted you so bad.* I tried to reach down to touch my belly, but the nurses had me pinned tight with leather straps. As the doctor stepped up, the metal caught the light. Silver.

"Is that... silver?" I barely breathed the word. He gave a grim nod. That was it. Blair didn't just want my title; she wanted me in a grave. She'd cooked up those fake photos, lied about me sleeping around, and he bought it. I hadn't touched another man. And after this? I never damn well would.

Why, Goddess? Why pair me with this monster just to rip my baby away? My poor little pup.

Tears streamed down my face as the cold metal sliced my stomach open. Inside, my baby kicked wildly. He knew. He knew it was too damn early. This was a straight-up execution for both of us.

"Bring me the dead pup."

"Right away, Alpha." The pain was blinding. Every slice tore through my nerves until I couldn't hold it in anymore. I shrieked. Warm blood rushed down my flanks, dripping onto the cold floor. *Drip. Drip.* I fought the straps, but the silver was already draining my strength.

*Forgive me, baby.*

They were letting me bleed out. I knew it was coming, but it still hurt like hell. I used to worship my mate. But watching them tear my baby out of my own flesh? That love died right then and there.

"Please... just let me hold him. Once." I strained against the cuffs, reaching out. The doctor, tears spilling down his cheeks, actually showed some mercy. He gently placed my baby on my chest.

He was absolutely gorgeous. I nuzzled his tiny face, mixing our scents together. He was my soul. My missing half. Forever.

"Doc, wrap it up. Now."

"Yes, Alpha." The doctor snatched my baby away and bolted, leaving me sliced open and freezing on the table.

My vision started to fade. Then the door clicked. In walked Blair, wearing that disgusting, victorious smirk.

"Told you I'd steal your life, Lyra. Told you I'd take your man. And oh, I have. We've been busy ever since he found out you're a 'cheater'." Blair leaned down and kissed my cheek. I growled, baring my teeth. "He's amazing in bed. Don't worry, though. I'll pop out another son for him." She patted her flat stomach. I burst out laughing. "What the hell are you laughing at?" she snapped.

"I can smell the beta all over you, sis. That's Gareth's kid. And guess what? It's a girl. Nice try, loser."

She hissed, her claws sliding out to slit my throat. But the door literally flew off its hinges. In walked my mate—the man I now loathed with every fiber of my being. His eyes were blood-red. I laughed even harder.

"You lying bitch!" he roared. He backhanded Blair so hard she flew across the room.

"Damon!" Blair shrieked, slamming into the wall. "What are you doing?!" She scrambled to her feet. I felt more blood gush out of my stomach. I let my eyelids flutter shut.

"You lied to me!" he bellowed. His Alpha aura flared, rattling the walls, but I was too numb to care. The silver poison was already creeping up my veins, heading straight for my dying heart. "That was my baby! I smell my own scent on him! He was mine!" Damon's eyes burned red, tears welling up. "You told me she slept around! You said it wasn't mine!"

"She did cheat! Okay? I guess... I guess I got the baby part wrong."

"You told me you smelled another wolf!" He lunged at her again. The last thing I saw before my eyes gave out was him holding our dead baby. God, I hated that sight. He did this to us. Not Blair. Him.

Sure, she played her cards right, but he's the one who didn't trust me. He couldn't even wait a few damn days to check the pup's scent. This was on him. He ruined all of us.

*Goddess, please just let me die. I'm done. Take me to my baby.*

"Save her!"

"No way!" Blair shrieked. "I'm the Luna now! You marked me last night!" Ah. So that explained the sudden, agonizing pain I felt yesterday. The sheer disgust made bile rise in my throat. "I'm carrying your kid!" she lied.

I chuckled again, a bloody sound. I forced my eyes open. Damon was hovering over me, looking panicked. "Lyra, stay with me. Don't you dare close your eyes."

"It's... the beta's... kid," I wheezed, laughing as pure horror washed over his face. "She's screwing... the beta." I grinned, spitting blood right onto his face.

"Fix her!"

"Hell no!" I snapped, unleashing every drop of my remaining power. "Freeze." I pushed my own Alpha command into the room, locking everyone—including my mate—dead in their tracks.

"How...?" Damon stared down at me, begging with his eyes. "Please, Lyra. Let me save you."

"I carry the blood of the Moon Goddess herself, you idiot. You don't get to save me. You didn't deserve our baby, and you sure as hell don't deserve me. You were weak. Now? You've got nothing." I smiled right at his stupid, crying face as my soul slipped away.

And just like that, I was out.
