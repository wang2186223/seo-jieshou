Chapter 425

Cipher shut his trap. Dad looked like he was about to barf. "Hold up. You're telling me you've got three heavy-hitter bloodlines?"

"Yep. Lycan, witch, wolf. The whole package. My size, my vibe, my juice—all courtesy of the Moon Goddess." I gave a dumb shrug, then nodded.

"Well, that explains it," Cipher finally muttered. "I always wondered why your wolf form was such a damn monster."

I cocked an eyebrow. "You've never even seen me."

Dad let out a dry chuckle. "Oh, he has." I shot him a look, and he just shrugged. "I sent him pics. I was proud, okay? Had to brag about how massive Umbra was. Cipher thought I was full of shit."

I shook my head. "Whatever. I'm only bringing this up because of Damon."

Cipher blinked. "Say what now?"

"Damon was Terra's mate. She dumped his ass freshman year. But even with a dead bond, he can still catch my scent."

"Wait a sec," Cipher cut in. "How has he not figured it out yet? And why the hell did you reject him anyway?"

I glanced at Dad. He gave me the green light with a nod. "Because. My dad's family has a cheat code."

"A cheat code?"

I nodded. "We get a do-over. Another shot at life."

"Are you shitting me?"

"I already lived this life once. Made it to twenty-four. I mated Damon back then. I was pregnant with his kid, but Blair got in his head. Convinced him I was sleeping around and the baby wasn't his. So what did he do? Chained me to a table. Had a doctor carve our baby out of me with silver. The kid died right on my chest, and I bled to death. Next thing I know, I wake up on my eighteenth birthday—the exact day I was supposed to catch his scent. I bolted. Dad helped me unlock my magic, I found my second wolf, and this time, I actually managed to shift."

Cipher gasped, completely winded. "You... you mated Damon? Had his kid?"

"And I'm not making that same damn mistake twice."

"Do you still have feelings for the guy?"

"Yeah."

Cipher snorted. "Are you serious right now?"

My eyes went ice-cold. "He murdered my baby. I loathe his guts. I'm going to strip him of everything he owns, and then I'm going to put him down. Real slow."

Cipher let out a low growl. "Look, this is wild and all, but why are you spilling this to us now?"

"Because it's part of the play. Dad showed me how to mask my scent, but I've been letting it slip back on purpose. It's already driving him crazy. I'm going to turn up the dial as we get closer to the mating ceremony."

"Why the hell would you do that?" Dad interrupted.

"To mess with his head. He's already drowning in Blair's drama, with all those rumors flying around. The pack is starting to talk. So while Magnus and Damon are busy dealing with that shitshow, I'm going to use the distraction to hack their network and swipe their data. I'm going to rescue the kids they've been draining. I'm pulling Gideon Stone and his family out, plus Elara Meadow and Rhys Thorne, before anyone sniffs out their pup."

"Why are you moving them?" Dad asked, totally lost.

"Elara and Rhys are omegas. She just found out she's pregnant."

Dad cracked a smile. "Well, that's great news."

"Not in this hellhole. Magnus made a rule. Omegas can't breed without a hall pass. To get it, they have to sleep with a ranked wolf. If they get knocked up by their actual mate first, the ranked wolves get to beat the female until she miscarries."

Cipher growled, slamming his fist down. "Tell me you're making this shit up."

"I wish." I rubbed my face. "They need to get out. Now. Gideon Stone is being poisoned, they're draining his kid, and his mate's got a target on her back."

"You taking them to the cabin?" Cipher asked.

I nodded. "Just until we can get them settled back at Dad's pack."

Dad sighed, shaking his head. "We can't go back there until we track down those rogues."

I suddenly felt like an idiot. "Uh... Daddy? You love me, right?"

"What's up, kid?"

"I found the rogues... back when I was a freshman."

Dad's jaw hit the floor. "Say what?"

Cipher groaned. "Why the hell didn't you tell us?"

"To be fair, I was dealing with a ton of shit! The whole mess with Raven and her sister, Orion's drama, you literally dying, and then my heat hit." I sighed. "It slipped my mind, okay?"

Dad waved his hand, brushing it off. "Where are they?"

"I'm not sure if they're still there, but Morgana had a setup in the dead caves with them. Morgana, Seraphina, and their grandma were all in bed with the rogues. But there's one more thing."

"What?" Dad looked completely dazed.

"Astrid is their sister." Dad's head snapped up. I nodded.

"The chick engaged to the king?" Cipher asked.

"Yep." I nodded again. "She's Morgana and Seraphina's big sister. She's using magic to keep her claws in the king. I just don't know how the hell to save him."
