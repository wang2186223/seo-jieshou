Chapter 432

I shook my head, hands waving. "No way in hell." A fake laugh popped out of me. "I'm no queen." I dodged her eyes. "We had to have sniffed each other right at least once. Just like you said, I probably just blanked on it."

"Okay, so what did Orion actually say when he got a whiff of you?" She stared down at her hand. "Like, when he thought your scent was the real deal?"

"He kept saying I smelled off. Same damn thing with Shadow whenever he sniffed Umbra."

Mom hissed air through her teeth. "Look, baby, I want to lie to make you feel good. But if he thought your smell was off, or if you didn't smell like his true mate, he would've never let his own scent out for you. Period."

I stared at her. "So we're totally in the dark? We don't even know if we're mates?" She gave a slow shake of her head. "You're telling me there's a tiny, freaking chance I'm paired with Orion? The guy literally engaged to a witch?"

"Lyra!" Mom snapped.

I waved her off. "I'm not name-calling, Mom. She is a literal, spell-casting witch." I squeezed my eyes shut. "God, please tell me you're wrong."

Mom let out a heavy sigh. "I wish I could, sweetie. But I don't BS you. The chance is there. Or hey, maybe you both nailed it the first time. But why are you losing your mind over this?"

My mouth popped open, but nothing came out. My eyes darted around the room. I just shrugged.

*Cut the crap, Lyra. You know the truth.* Umbra nudged my mind.

*Just stop, breathe, and actually feel it for a second.* Terra chimed in.

I squeezed my eyes shut, letting the reality sink in. It hit me like a tidal wave from all sides. I was going under. "I'm freaking drowning here. The weight is crushing me. I'm trying to play it cool, put on a happy face, and act like I've got this. I don't. The whole Orion mess damn near destroyed me." My body gave out. I buried my face in my hands, shoulders trembling.

"Oh, baby..." Mom reached out, but I slapped a hand up to stop her.

"I need to spit it all out." I leaned back. "It took forever to figure out his game and his reasons. It almost put me in a grave. Now, I finally drag myself back up, only to realize I'm falling for my friend. I'm out here trying to save families, kids, everyone. I'm trying to crush Magnus, Damon, and that whole damn brotherhood. And now..." I shook my head violently. "Now I have to deal with all that crap *and* this?" I locked eyes with Mom. "I..."

"What is it, sweetie?"

I had to be real, even if it was just to myself. "I'm scared spitless."

Mom gave my hand a hard squeeze. "Why's that?"

"Because... what if he actually is my mate?"

She flashed a smile. "Then he's your mate." Boom. Simple as that to her.

I shook my head, hard. "He's the mate who tore my freaking heart out, even if he was 'protecting' me. I can't even stand to look at him because he's with *her*."

"None of that crap is gonna matter, baby." She grabbed my hand tight.

I let out a ragged breath. "But what if he's my mate and I just can't forgive him? What if this 'peace' I think I have is just a lie I told myself? What if the second I see his face, all that agony and betrayal just slams right back into me?" I swiped a tear off my cheek.

"Baby, trust me, it won't matter."

I wanted to scream in her face. "You're not getting it!"

She shook her head right back. "No, *you're* not getting it." I let out a low growl. She just chuckled.

"What if I can't love him, Mom?" My stomach twisted into a hard knot of pure panic. It did a flip, and I wanted to throw up right there.

"Wait, what?" She actually laughed.

"That's what terrifies me! What if he's my goddess-given mate, but after all the damage we did? After the agony, the heartbreak, and all the absolute bullshit, all I have left for him is rage? Or hate? Or... just nothing?"

Mom cupped my face in her hands. "Look, the mate bond isn't a walk in the park. But you're inside your own head too much. Remember when you found out Damon was your mate in your past life?"

"Yeah. Why?"

"Think about it, really think. You absolutely loathed Damon. All through childhood, all through school. But you still mated him." She locked eyes with me. "Why do you think that happened?"

"Back then? Because I just knew we were meant to be. Nothing before the bond mattered. Only what came after."

She nodded. "Bingo." She poked my shoulder. "All this drama won't mean squat. The Goddess says it herself—everything before the bond snaps into place just evaporates. We don't owe anyone anything until our mate shows up." She kissed my forehead. "Stop overthinking, kiddo."
