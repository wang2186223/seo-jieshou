Chapter 56

My legs shook like crazy. I stared down at the girl at my feet. "What the hell are you?"

"Told you already," Umbra growled, slamming our face right down to Briar’s. "I’m the Alpha here. What I say goes." She threw her head back and let out a massive howl. The remaining wolves shifted and howled right back. They bowed. "Anyone got a problem with that?"

"No, Alpha." The answer echoed. Even Jay and Eliza bowed their heads.

"Mom! Dad!" Briar shrieked.

They turned their backs. Cold. The whole pack did the same. Briar scrambled up, stumbling, looking around like a lost kid.

She stared at Umbra.

"From this second on, Briar Williams is dead to this pack," Umbra barked. "And Morgana is next the moment we drag her back. We don't do traitors here."

My dad stepped up, throwing an arm over our shoulder. "You've got till sunset to pack your crap and get out. Step foot on our dirt after dark, and my guards have orders to rip you apart."

Briar threw a fit, screaming and stomping. Umbra wasn't having it. She bared her fangs, getting right in her face with a nasty growl. "Keep crying and see what happens, brat. Get moving before I lose my mind." Briar flinched, then bolted out of the clearing.

Dad waved his hands. "Everyone else, scram. Stay alert. Mind-link me the second you see anything weird." The pack bowed and cleared out, leaving us shaking with pure rage. "You're good, Lyra," Dad said, but his eyes were locked on Umbra.

I got shoved to the back of my own head, sitting next to Terra, watching Umbra drive. Trippy as hell, but hey, she earned this. Even if she was barely holding her temper together.

"You can still steer even when we're shifted, once you get used to it," Terra purred. I ran my fingers through her fur, watching the show.

"You guys earned some screen time. I'll let you run things for a bit," I said. Terra let out a low chuckle.

Umbra faced Dad. He ran his hands through our fur. "Damn, kid. You're huge." Umbra bared her teeth in a smirk. Dad laughed. "In a good way. You're gorgeous." Umbra beamed inside. Dad cupped her face. "Celestia really blessed you. Man, how am I supposed to keep the guys away from you now?"

Umbra snorted, headbutting his chest. "You'll figure it out."

Suddenly, a mind-link buzzed in. *Morgana crossed the border, but there's rogue scent everywhere. They're on our turf. What's the play? Chase her or hunt the rogues?* Grayson's voice exploded in our head. Umbra bolted instantly.

"Umbra! Hold up!" Dad yelled.

Too late. She was locked in.

*Umbra, wait for Dad!* I yelled in our head. She ignored me. *Umbra!*

"Rogues are here. Pack comes first," Umbra roared through the mind-link.

A massive growl ripped from our left. Lok, Dad's wolf, bounded up beside us. "Don't you ever ditch me like that again, kid. Not with rogues running around."

"Then keep up, old man!" Umbra barked, pushing harder.

We tore through the trees, racing through town toward Kaelen. Before we hit the next tree line, a scent hit us. A split second later, Obsidian caught it too. We both pivoted, tracking the smell. "Obsidian?"

"Stay close. Eyes peeled," he growled.

We ran for a minute before a blood-curdling scream cut through the air.

*We're under attack!* his voice boomed in the link. *Get to your posts! Protect the pack!*

Howls erupted everywhere. Chaos. He looked at me, making a split-second call. "Stay safe, baby girl. I can't lose you."

I nodded. Umbra let me take the wheel. Teamwork makes the dream work. I pushed to the front. "You stay safe too, Dad."

Umbra and I veered off toward the nearest howl. We tore through the streets, rounded a corner, and saw two rogues cornering a family. My vision went red. One of them lunged at a little pup.

We hit the gas. Not on our watch.
