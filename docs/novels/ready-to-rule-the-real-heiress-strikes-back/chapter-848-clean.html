Yara shook her head, just a little. “Tina, stop making stuff up. Grandpa Kevin isn't like that."

Tina made a face. "Then why is he sneaking around the lab so late at night? The lights are still on upstairs. Maybe he's hiding someone in there. No wonder he won't even let you inside."

Yara frowned, just a bit.

Her grandpa was the vice principal, and she'd always been one of the top students. She could even handle basic surgeries by herself now. People said she was a medical genius, that her future was basically set. But even with all that, when she tried to get into the special lab, Grandpa Kevin shot her down without a second thought. He didn't care about her pride at all.

But last time, she'd clearly seen Kevin bringing another girl into that same lab.

And now, here it was again.

She couldn't help but wonder who was in the upstairs lab. Was it the same girl as before?

“Come on, let's just go. And Tina, really, don't spread rumors."

Tina rolled her eyes. "Yara, you're way too nice. If I were you, I'd be walking into any lab I wanted. If Mr. Moore said no, I'd just do it anyway."

Yara sighed. "There have to be rules. Let's go."

Monica and Fiona blinked at each other. "Really?"
