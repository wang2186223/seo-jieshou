Chapter 465

The guy finally cleared out. I slumped back, whipped open my laptop, and fired up the group chat with Cipher and my old man.

Nixthegoddess: Got the mainframe. Download's done.

Deadmanwalking: Hell yeah! Send it over when you get back. I'll start digging.

Cipher: Get the hell out of there. Now.

Nixthegoddess: On it. Leaving tonight.

Cipher: No. Leave right now.

Deadmanwalking: Seriously. Pack up and go.

Nixthegoddess: Still got one thing left to do.

Deadmanwalking: Like what?

Cipher: Doesn't matter. Whatever it is, drop it. Just run.

Nixthegoddess: You guys know I can't. Magnus is literally draining those pups dry. I'm not leaving them to die. Gotta break that curse.

Cipher: Fine. Do it fast, then bounce.

Deadmanwalking: Hold on!

Nixthegoddess: What?

Deadmanwalking: The second you break that first curse, Magnus is gonna know. He'll hunt everyone down. He's way faster than you, and you'll be dead meat. Plus, breaking curses takes a massive toll, and you're already pretty banged up.

Nixthegoddess: I'm not letting those kids die.

Deadmanwalking: I'm not saying you should. Didn't you buy them some time before?

Nixthegoddess: Yeah, pushed it back a week.

Deadmanwalking: When's the mating ceremony?

Nixthegoddess: Cancelled. Obviously.

Deadmanwalking: Don't bet on it, kiddo. My guy close to Damon says he agreed to mate Blair, but Magnus is blocking it. The way I see it, Magnus either caves, or...

Nixthegoddess: Or what?

Deadmanwalking: He's got a backup mate lined up for Damon.

I leaned back, rubbing my temples. Damn. It made sense. Who else would be dumb enough to sign up for Damon? Magnus wouldn't let him mate some random omega. Blair's little fairy tale was dead in the water.

Nixthegoddess: Who's that stupid?

Cipher: Magnus wants status, but after the way Damon dragged you, his options are trash.

I nodded. Spot on. Desperate times. An alpha, beta, or gamma female who was totally out of options. A wild thought hit me. No way. But man, if I was right, it'd be hilarious.

Nixthegoddess: What about Raven? Or Sable?

Cipher: Could be. Their family is barely holding onto their status. But they're both alphas.

Deadmanwalking: Doesn't matter right now. Point is, we have a week to move our pieces. Instead of curing the kids, just extend their clock for another week and run. Magnus will just think Astrid screwed up again. It buys us the time we need.

Smart. He was right.

Nixthegoddess: Fine. But I'm curing Lyra Bloom tonight. No exceptions.

Deadmanwalking: Fine. Just do it after you get clear.

Cipher: Can you heal yourself yet?

Wait, what? I frowned at the sudden pivot.

Nixthegoddess: How'd you know about that?

Cipher: ...

Cipher: Your dad told me. Doesn't matter. Just heal up.

I stared at the screen. Then, a door slammed down the hall. Great. I groaned, throwing my hands up. No time for this crap. I started grabbing my gear. Phone in the purse, laptop zipped up—then my office door practically flew off its hinges.

Damon stood there, chest heaving, eyes locked onto me like a predator. "You."

I didn't even blink. I turned back to my screen, casually tossing pending tasks back into the system queue. None of us would be here tomorrow to do them anyway, but hey, gotta play the part. I looked up and smirked. "Me."

He let out a low growl. I just rolled my eyes. God, men are so predictable. "What do you want, Damon?"

"I'm wrapping up my work before I transfer out."

He froze. "Say what?"

I sighed, dumping the last of the IT tickets back into the queue, and clicked 'deactivate account'. I popped my lips. "You heard me."

His glare could've cut glass. "What the hell do you mean, you're transferring?"

I shut the laptop lid, stood up, and started clearing off my desk. "Exactly what I said, Damon. I'm transferring to my dad's pack."

He threw his head back, laughing. "He's dead."

"And?" That shut him up real quick.

"How the hell do you transfer to a pack that doesn't even exist anymore?"

I let out a heavy sigh, giving him a slow, mocking look. "Look, Damon, I know you're not completely brain-dead. Why would I transfer back to a dead pack that I legally own?"

He blinked. Then, the lightbulb finally went off. "You're going back to rebuild it."

"Ding, ding, ding. Give the man a prize." I zipped my bag shut and pushed myself up off the desk. My ribs flared with a sharp, stabbing pain. Damn, that hurt.

Damon's voice instantly went from tough guy to whiny brat. "But... our mating ceremony is in a week!"
