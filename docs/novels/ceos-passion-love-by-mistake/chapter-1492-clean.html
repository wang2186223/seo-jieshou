Meanwhile, back in the country, Stanley entered an apartment.

The apartment was a cozy living space that used to be Joyce's residence.

All the electrical alliances and floor were covered in dust as the unit had been vacant for quite some time.

As Stanley walked into the apartment, each step he made left an obvious footprint on the floor.

He had never set foot in the apartment. In fact, he never intended to do so.

When he finally decided to come to this place, Joyce had left the apartment for good.

Stanley walked to the living room and stood still. He turned around and took a sidelong glance at the apartment. It was a small unit but had everything a person needed to live comfortably. Dust was all over the furniture pieces and electrical appliances, and many things were left behind as they were.
