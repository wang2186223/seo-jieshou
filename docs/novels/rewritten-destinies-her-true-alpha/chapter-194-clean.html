Chapter 194

Magnus pinched his eyes shut and gave a slow shake of his head. "I'll put out a warning later. Pissing off any wolf is brain-dead, but pulling that crap on an Alpha? That's a straight-up death wish." He let out a heavy sigh. "You cool now, Lyra?"

I put on a show, acting like I was wrestling Umbra for the wheel. My shoulders shook. She backed off slow, cackling the whole way. *Oh, this is gonna be a blast,* Umbra snickered, slipping back into the dark corners of my head.

The fur melted back. My eyes and fangs snapped to normal. Magnus and Damon both let out a massive breath. "Look, I should probably say sorry for snapping, but I'm not. Raven had that beatdown coming." I spat a laugh. "She's lucky she's still breathing." I glanced at Juniper and Daisy. "Thanks, guys."

"Anytime." The two of them gave my ribs a quick squeeze and dug back into their plates.

I locked eyes with Magnus. "Am I getting busted for this?"

He stared. Really drilled his eyes into me, trying to read my play. Then he shook his head. "Hell, anyone would've done the same." He popped his neck. "She's just dumb enough to think she could take you. Probably figured you were too wrecked to fight back, or too weak to hold your own." Magnus scanned the cafeteria, locking eyes with the crowd. "Doubt anyone else is stupid enough to try their luck today."

A few chuckles rippled through the room. Then some guy yelled from the back, "Man, you scared the absolute shit out of everyone! It was terrifying... but, like, kind of hot." The whole cafeteria burst out laughing.

I took a deep breath, shaking off the leftover rage. God, I was so sick of Raven and her trashy friends, but I was stuck in this place for a few more years. I had to lock in on what actually mattered.

"So, am I in trouble?" I asked Magnus again. I knew my eyes were still glaring, but I hoped he wouldn't take it as a threat.

He rubbed his neck, hesitating. Before he could open his mouth, Damon stepped up. "Lyra, yeah, it wasn't great. But for a wolf who just started shifting? That was some insane self-control." He looked at his dad. Magnus's mouth twitched up for a split second. Ah. Staged. "Dad... let's just drop it. Raven started this shit by bringing up yesterday. If some guy did that to me, I'd have ripped his throat out."

Magnus chewed on that for a second. "Honestly? I'd have done the same. But don't let it happen again." I took a breath. "Thanks, Alpha Magnus." I looked at Damon, giving him a tiny nod. "Thanks for having my back."

Damon looked totally whipped for a second, then blinked and got his act together. "Uh, yeah. Sure."

I nodded to both of them and turned back to my food. No rushing. Damon and Magnus knew I hated their guts. If I suddenly started playing nice, they'd smell a rat instantly. I had to play the long game. Give them tiny crumbs. Let them think they were winning me over. That way, when I finally drop the hammer, they won't see it coming.

Juniper grabbed my hands. "Damn, these look raw." I stared at my bruised, red knuckles and grinned. "Doesn't hurt a bit." I cracked my neck and started eating.

"Lyra..."

I looked up. Luna was standing there, pale as a ghost. "Yeah?"

"Are you... are you gonna teach us how to do *that*?" She pointed a shaky finger at the floor. Raven's blood was still smeared on the tiles.

"Yep." I wiped my face. "Damn right I am. Because I want you guys to be able to throw down like that, instead of getting slaughtered like my dad." I said it loud. The whole room went dead quiet, murmurs spreading fast.

Luna, Fawn, and Daisy traded looks. "Count us in," Luna smiled. "Seriously, that was the most badass thing I've ever seen."

Fawn nodded like crazy. "So freaking cool! Did you see that jump?" She mimicked a leap with her hands across the table. "Like, boom! One second you're sitting, the next you're slamming Raven into the dirt. I didn't even see you move!"

"I wanted to cheer so bad," Daisy whispered. "When you popped her nose, I almost clapped. But, man, she would've cornered me and ended me."

I nodded. "She would've. But once we're done training? She won't even be able to touch you. You gotta build your aura, but trust me, I'm gonna turn you guys into some seriously lethal wolves."
