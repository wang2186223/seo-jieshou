Chapter 616

Orion and I locked eyes on my old man.

"A spell hitting our bond?" Orion muttered, his voice dropping. "Who the hell is left with that kind of juice?"

I shook my head, a bitter taste in my mouth. "Nobody. Every single one of 'em is six feet under." I exhaled hard. "Guess we're not out of the woods yet."

My dad let out a dry laugh. "We're never out of the woods, kiddo." He pointed a chin at the bowl in my hands. "You ready for this?"

I nodded. "Give us a sec?" I looked at my parents, waving them off.

"Sure thing, sweetie." Mom planted a quick kiss on my cheek and slipped past us down the stairs. Dad gave Orion's shoulder a heavy slap as he followed her down.

I threw up a silence barrier with a snap of my fingers. Orion raised an eyebrow. I just shrugged. "No eavesdroppers. I don't know who's dirty anymore."

He nodded, getting it.

"Are we good?" I hated how weak I sounded right then.

He stared at me, deadpan, then sighed. "Was that really magic, or do you just secretly regret saying yes to being my Luna?"

Panic spiked. I fumbled, the bowl slipping from my hands. I lunged for him. Thank god Orion was fast—he caught the bowl mid-air while my arms slammed around his neck. I yanked him down, throwing him off balance.

"I love you," I blurted, squeezing tight. "I know I suck at saying it, but I do. Hell, I've loved you since before my first heat. I spent years pissed off at how much I loved you. Now I just want to spend the rest of my life doing it." I pulled back to look at him. A tiny flicker of doubt still lingered in his eyes. "I'm your Luna. Now, tomorrow, and in whatever life comes next. Nobody else. I'm sorry I made you doubt that."

He searched my eyes, looking for a lie. Then he closed them, and my heart crawled up my throat.

But he just took a slow, deep breath. "My bad," he muttered, shaking his head. "That damn spell is sticky." He opened his eyes, and the sheer warmth in them made my chest stop aching. "Love you too, rabbit." He kissed me hard. "Now, let's get down there and handle this. We'll hunt down the magic bastard later."

"Let's move." I nodded, snatched the bowl back, and headed for the stairs. But I paused at the hidden armory. I slipped inside and started grabbing gear.

"Silver?" Orion asked. I thought he'd judge, but he just reached past me for a needle and a small glass bottle.

I tilted my head. "Yeah. Figure I'll start with old-school torture before I waste any magic."

He snorted, popping the cap off the bottle. He took a sniff, jerked back instantly, and let out a massive sneeze.

I cracked up. "That's wolfsbane, you idiot."

Orion rubbed his nose, capping the bottle fast. "I saw the label, just wanted to check the potency." He gave me a weird look. "Why the hell does your dad keep pure wolfsbane in here anyway?"

I grabbed a few more tools and pointed toward the exit. "Because he feeds it to me."

"What the actual fuck? He poisoned your food?" Orion growled, storming out ahead of me.

"Hey, he did it slow. Built up my immunity so nobody could use it to drop me." I bumped his shoulder as I overtook him. "Chill out, bonehead. It's saved my ass more times than you know."

I bypassed the main cells where the guards were locked up and headed straight for the deep, hidden stairs.

Orion stopped at the landing, looking down the noisy hallway. "Aren't we going in there?" He pointed toward the muffled screams.

I shook my head. "Nah, that's just the grunts. Kaelen's got them covered. We're heading to the VIP lounge of Dad's dungeon." I kept walking down.

"VIP lounge?" Orion chuckled.

I threw a grin over my shoulder. "Yeah. Only the real troublemakers get the basement treatment."

I rounded the corner and stepped into the first interrogation room. Caspian was strapped to a chair, gagged, and the room went dead silent. Mom and Dad were leaning against the wall.

"Don't let me interrupt," I said.

Dad snorted. "We can't take the gag off. Bastard will bite his tongue and bleed out. We're trying to figure out how to stop him."

I dumped my pile of tools onto the metal table, clattering them into order.

"How do we even know he wants to talk?" I picked up one specific tool. While everyone was distracted by Caspian's muffled grunts, I wrapped a piece of cloth around it and slipped it into my pocket, turning back to the room.

"He screamed," Dad said. "The guard did a quick yes-or-no drill with him and came to get me."

I walked right up to Caspian. "You ready to spill?"

He nodded like crazy.

"Alright. Don't cry to me about it."

Orion stepped up, grabbed Caspian's head, and ripped the gag off. Before the guy could even think about chomping down, Orion jammed his fingers in, locking his jaws wide open.

"What are you doing?" Mom asked, looking lost.

Then I pulled the pliers out of my pocket.

"Ica silver nox dou," I whispered, waiting for the magic to kick in.

"You looking for silver?" Orion asked.

One by one, the silver knives and clamps on the table glowed a bright, neon blue.

"Yep." I watched the tools, waiting until one specific tooth in Caspian's mouth started glowing the same blue. "Struss car beni."

I clamped the pliers onto the tooth and pulled.

"HOLD ON!" Dad yelled. "You'll shatter it!"

I ignored him and yanked hard, banking on the cloth-wrapped metal and my whispered spell to keep it intact. The tooth popped out of the socket. I spun around, dangling the glowing tooth right in Caspian's face. He stared at it, wide-eyed.

"See? Didn't break a thing."
