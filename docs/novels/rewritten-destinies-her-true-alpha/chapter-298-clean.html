Chapter 298

She glared daggers. I didn't budge. "What, cat got your tongue?" I crossed my arms, but my brain was working overtime. No doubt about it, Astrid was tied to Morgana. Cousin, maybe? She definitely had that same freaky magic.

That whole bloodline was obsessed with punching above their weight class. Morgana went after my dad. Seraphina bagged Cyrus—though honestly, I highly doubt the Goddess actually blessed that mess. And now Astrid was digging her claws into Orion. "Props to you, though," I said. "Out of you and the twins, you definitely shot for the stars."

"I'm worth way more than those two!" she spat. Then she froze, biting her tongue like she'd said too much.

My eyes narrowed. "Sisters? Huh. Wild. Different last names, though. Guess you had different dads." I shook my head, smirking. "Let me guess. Since your grandma bailed them out, you were the firstborn. The oopsie baby."

She lost it. She lunged right at us, screaming, "I am not a mistake! They were weak! Pathetic!" She spun around, pacing like a caged animal. "But nobody ever sees it. Nobody!"

I shot a look at Juniper. She was already grinning. "Hard to see something that doesn't exist, babe," Juniper said, checking her nails. "You're the bastard, aren't you? Born outside the mate bond."

"Shut the hell up!" Astrid shrieked. "You're just like the rest of them! My dad was a powerhouse!" Her eyes went totally wild, full-on psycho. "My mother should've been kissing his feet for choosing her!"

"Chosen?" Juniper frowned, totally lost.

A nasty realization hit me. "Oh, I get it. Your mom was just pretty enough to be the Alpha's personal breeding cow."

Astrid rolled her eyes. "Exactly. That and her magic were her only selling points." She whipped around to face us. "But did she appreciate it? Hell no."

I saw the penny drop for Juniper. Her jaw hit the floor in pure disgust. "Hold up... you're saying she should be happy she got abused? Used as a baby-maker? That he literally ruined her shot at finding her actual soulmate?"

Astrid scoffed. "Please. She found her mate when I was ten. The guy took her back because he was just as pathetic as she was. She could've been the Alpha's main side-piece, but no, she chose some low-life nobody instead."

"The Goddess makes the match. It's not a choice, you idiot," I snapped. "And the fact that you wanted your own mother to be a forced sex slave instead of a real mate is downright sickening."

"Don't you dare look down on me!" she hissed. "I'm an Alpha's daughter!"

Juniper and I burst out laughing. "Yeah, so are we, sweetie," Juniper mocked. "Except we're the legit heirs from real, bonded mates. You're just the consolation prize of a rapist. And yet, you worship the guy instead of seeing him for what he is—a total monster."

She bared her teeth. "My dad is ten times the man yours will ever be! And yeah, bonds are a choice. Clearly. Because Orion chose me."

I cocked my head, locking eyes with her. "Did he, though?" I let my eyes go cold. "Or did you just drug him with another one of your little witchy spells?"

She flinched, her face going white as a sheet. But she recovered fast, throwing her head back with a loud, fake laugh. "Oh, you almost had me there! Look, I'll be real with you. Yeah, at first I used a little magic to block you out. The guy wouldn't look at anyone else, and it was driving me nuts. I was right there for years, and I was invisible to him. But then he had to date the council's approved list. I was on it; you were on his personal list. He promised to finish their list before checking his own. But guess what?" She smirked, looking smug as hell. "He never made it to his list. He stopped at me. The second he actually looked at me, the second he gave me a shot, he was hooked." She flashed a massive grin, but there was a flicker of panic in her eyes. I saw it, but I let it slide for now.

I lost it. I literally burst out laughing. I turned to Juniper, pointing my thumb at Astrid. "Look at her. She actually thinks she won a prize with Orion."

Juniper snorted, shaking her head with a pitying sigh. "Man, she really has no idea who she's dealing with, does she?"

Astrid was practically shaking, her face turning bright red. "What the fuck is so funny?"

I tilted my head. "You're acting like Orion is some kind of trophy."

She scoffed. "Uh, he's the freaking king, hello?"

Juniper nodded. "True. But Lyra here? Yeah, she doesn't do second chances."

Astrid's smug look vanished. "What do you mean by that?"

I leaned in close, dropping my voice like we were besties sharing a secret. "Orion swore up and down he'd never touch a girl who wasn't his fated mate." Her face went completely pale. "He literally walked out on me when I was in heat, begging him for help." I flashed her a sweet, cold smile. "You think forcing him into a bond is gonna change his mind? Babe, he's gonna leave you high and dry. You'll be burning, begging, and hurting, and he won't care. King or not, the guy is a sadist. He's not a prize—he's a nightmare."
