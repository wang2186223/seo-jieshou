Chapter 103

The dude under my palms was shaking like a leaf. I grinned. "You're an alpha, huh?"

"Try again, dipshit. I'm the biggest, baddest alpha in this damn room. And you just insulted me and my alpha mother." I slammed his face hard into the floorboards, looking up at her. "Mom, I know you're always telling me to keep Umbra on a leash. But right now? Screw that. These jerks don't get a pass."

My mom crouched down, getting right in their faces. "Two choices, boys. Get off your asses and do the job right."

"And you do it for free," I snarled, grinding his nose into the rug. I leaned in, baring my fangs right next to his ear with a nasty snap.

"Or..." Mom locked eyes with the other guy. "I let my daughter—who's itching for a fight—take you out back to settle this."

I let out a low growl. "Seriously, let me rip them apart. I'll make it quick, I swear."

"But it's gonna hurt like hell." Mom stood back up, dusting off her hands. "Pick one. Now." She shot me a look. "Make it fast."

My face was already tingling, shifting. Today had been a total shitshow, and Umbra and Terra were dying to tear someone open. My vision went wild, locking right onto the pulse pounding in the guy's neck. I took a deep breath, sucking in the sweet smell of their terror, my grin widening.

"Okay! Okay! We'll do the job!" the guy underneath me squealed, trembling. I just growled louder. "For free! We'll do it for free, I swear!" He started shaking so hard he literally pissed himself. I stood up, disgusted, and stormed downstairs. I had to get away from these two losers before I killed them. Who the hell did they think they were? How dare they test us?

I paced the back room, my skin crawling with rage. "Absolute trash." I tensed up as Blair strutted into the room, looking smug as hell.

"If I were you, I'd walk away right now." My voice was a deep, gravelly warning. But Blair, being the idiot she is, didn't listen.

"Oh, yeah? Or what?" Blair scoffed, rolling her eyes. I could feel Umbra stalking closer in my mind. "Gonna pretend you can shift again? Please. You think some arm fur makes you special? Any basic wolf can do that, even the weak ones."

"Blair, I'm warning you. Back the hell off."

"Or what, bitch?" she screamed, lunging forward and spinning me around by my shoulder. "Or what? You're just a pathetic, useless alpha who can't even shift!"

Boom. Before I could even think, Umbra forced the shift. I slammed Blair onto the floor, pinning her down before she could even blink. My fangs sank right into her throat, a massive growl ripping out of my chest. Her scream cut off instantly as I clamped down.

Mom burst into the room. "Lyra! Umbra! Stop, right now!" I let out a savage, blood-curdling roar that made even my mom freeze in her tracks. "Lyra..." Her voice dropped, getting soft as she knelt down and crept closer.

"Get... get her off me," Blair whimpered, her tears mixing with the drool in my mouth. Even with my teeth on her windpipe, she was still trying to call the shots.

My fangs sliced deeper, and the hot taste of her blood hit my tongue. Blair went totally stiff. With my mouth full, I couldn't talk, so I blasted a mind-link to both of them. *You dare order me around? You're nothing. You act like you're hot shit, but you're a nobody. A beta's brat who can't even hold her own rank, yet you strut around here like you own the place.*

"Blair, what the hell did you do?" Mom's voice was sharp, demanding answers.

"Nothing!" Blair gasped. I felt Umbra's patience snapping.

"Blair, shut up," Mom warned.

But it was too late. Umbra let go of her neck just a second before we ripped her head clean off. "Shift," we ordered, our voice deep and deadly as Umbra sat back on her haunches.

"What?" Blair scrambled backward, clutching her bleeding neck, terrified.

Mom stood up and took a step back. "I said shift." Our voice boomed, cold and lethal. "You've got such a big mouth, thinking you're queen of the world. Let's settle this right now. Shift."

Blair turned white, shaking her head. "No... please."

"I said fucking shift!" we roared together. Blair collapsed to her knees. She couldn't fight my alpha command. Not this time. I threw every ounce of my power into that order.

She writhed on the floor, eyes bulging as fur erupted across her skin. The shift took over her body until she was just a panting, pathetic wolf lying at my feet.
