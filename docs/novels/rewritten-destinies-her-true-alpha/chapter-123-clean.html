Chapter 123

"No biggie," Mom said, trying to cool things down. "I forget stuff constantly." We both laughed, waiting.

Eleanor relaxed a bit and chuckled. "Yeah, some days are just like that." She clicked around on her computer. "First things first. Almost forgot to give you this." She slid a piece of paper over. "Dorm checklist. Blankets, sheets, everyday stuff you don't think about." I nodded. "On the back is what Alpha Magnus wants the Alphas to bring. Says it makes you guys easy to spot."

My brows furrowed. "Huh?" I flipped it over. It was a straight-up checklist for snobs. *Louis Vuitton jewelry. Designer clothes only. Purses and accessories must cost over ten grand. Daily.* I looked up. Eleanor was actually sweating. "Why?"

"What the hell is he thinking?" Mom snatched the paper, shaking her head. "Even for an Alpha, some packs can't afford this crap. What is his deal?" I caught Mom's eye and gave her a tiny shake of my head.

*Can't talk here.* "Well, I'm just not doing it," I said with a shrug. "If Magnus has a problem, he can take it up with me." I went to stand up again.

"Wait," Eleanor said. I dropped back into my seat. "Your food card. Totally forgot."

Mom smacked her forehead. "Oh my god, me too!" I looked at her, raising an eyebrow.

"Wait, you mentioned that earlier. What's the deal? Can't I just use my debit card?" I looked between the two laughing women. "What?"

"Sweetie, the school runs on its own system. You can't buy anything here without that food card."

"We've used it for years to stop kids from flashing cash," Eleanor said, typing away. "We load it up, and it has a strict daily limit you can't go over."

"Why?" I was totally lost. What was going on? In my last life, Damon blocked me from going to college, so I had no clue.

"Years ago, a couple of Alphas loved throwing their weight around. One was a bully, and another stood up to make a point."

"What actually happened?" I turned to Mom. She snickered.

"Well, there was this Alpha throwing his money around," Mom started, chuckling. "Specifically, the year I started here."

"Okay..." I shook my head, confused.

"So, the first Alpha was from a filthy rich pack, and he flaunted it," Eleanor took over as Mom kept giggling. "He tried to buy off his friends and even bought out all the textbooks for a class."

"Screwing over everyone else," Mom chimed in.

"And the other Alpha? From an even richer pack?"

"And a total jerk," Mom cackled.

"Exactly. He went and bought out the entire bookstore. Then he gave everything away to the other students for free. Everyone got their textbooks, except for that first Alpha."

"It was epic." Mom wiped her eyes, finally done laughing.

"It was wild," Eleanor agreed. "I worked in the office back then and saw the chaos they caused. We rolled out the card system a month later."

I nodded. "Who were they?" I stared at Eleanor, but she wasn't talking.

Mom squeezed my hand, coughing a bit. "Magnus was the first Alpha. The second was your dad."

The room went dead silent. Eleanor cleared her throat. "Now, don't go spreading that story around. Keep it to yourself."

"Of course," I said automatically.

"So, how much do you want to load onto the card?"

"How often can we top it up?" Mom asked. "Back then, it was a one-time load, and the kids had to budget."

Eleanor nodded. "Still is. That's why Magnus wrote those Alpha rules. He wanted you guys to know the deal before you had to budget."

Mom gave me a slow nod. "And the leftover money at the end of the semester? Does it roll over, or do you wipe it?"

"We changed that. We used to refund it, but now it rolls over. You can add money at the start of each semester, but once it's gone, you're cut off until next term."
