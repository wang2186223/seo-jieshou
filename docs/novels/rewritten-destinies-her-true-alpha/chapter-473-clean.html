Chapter 473

I marched back, flipped the book open to the exact page, and started reading. Right then, Luna blinked her eyes open. "Hold up!" She squirmed, kicking her legs to get out of Elara Meadow’s grip. "Lyra, wait up!" Elara couldn't hold her and finally let go. Luna bolted straight to the table, scrambled up a chair, and snatched Lyra Bloom’s hand. "Hey, it's okay. I've got you." She shot a look at me. "We're good to go, right?"

I gave a quick nod. "Yeah, we should be. But look, same deal I told the others. This is gonna look brutal, like I'm ripping her apart. But I'm not."

Luna nodded fast. "I saw it. I saw the whole damn thing. That's why I ran over. Lyra Bloom is my ride-or-die, and she needs to know I'm right here." I gave her a firm nod, then locked eyes with Juniper.

Juniper smirked. "Yep, she's one of us. Instant bond, total loyalty." She walked over, planting a quick kiss on Luna’s forehead. "Our girl hasn't shut up about her bestie since she got back. She even gave her dad the whole rundown."

Lyra Bloom squeezed Luna’s hand back, hard. "You're my bestie too." Luna beamed, leaning in close. "Don't sweat it, okay?" Lyra Bloom nodded. "I trust you guys. Totally."

I hacked up a cough. No clue why, but my throat felt tight as hell. "Alright." I stepped back to the bowl, flicked a flame onto the cotton, and started tossing in the herbs. Acorn for defense and raw power. Adder’s tongue to patch her up. Ague weed to smash the curse. Bamboo for extra shield and curse-breaking. My gut twisted. Damn, that's a lot of protective stuff. Usually, you just need one of each, but this mess had layers on layers.

"Get your head in the game, girl." Grandma’s voice snapped me right out of my head. "Throw in the Barberry." I grabbed a fistful, but her hand shot out and slapped mine back. "We're scrubbing Magnus's filth out of her soul. Just one." She stared straight into my eyes. "You've got a whole web of souls to untangle from his strings." I nodded, dropping a single berry into the bowl.

"Basil next, to rip Magnus's grip off her soul. Just one. Hold the benzoin until the fire dies down. That smoke is what purifies her. Toss in the hydrangea to kill the curse. Finally, lady's slipper so nobody else can latch onto her." I finished up, and she nodded. The fire flickered down. "Benzoin. Now. Go. Now grab that feather and fan it over her."

I snatched the scorching bowl. The heat bit into my skin, and I hissed through my teeth.

"Lyra?" Mom lunged forward, but I waved her back.

Grandma blocked her. "Listen. In casting, if the victim bleeds, the caster burns. We don't pass the pain without taking a hit ourselves. It's the law of balance." Mom nodded, but her face was pure panic.

I stepped up to Lyra Bloom’s head and got to work. I swept the smoke over her with a stark white owl feather. Three laps around her, step by step. The fire flared with every stride, feeding on my own magic, turning from gold to a deep, icy blue. I set the bowl by her head, pumping a steady stream of juice to keep the flame roaring.

I grabbed the water bottle from the kit, splashed it into a cup, and threw in a pinch of the same herbs. I propped Lyra Bloom up and forced it down her throat. She coughed, choked, but got it down. I dumped the rest of the water into the burning bowl. The fire kept raging, but the liquid turned a weird, glowing blue.

I leaned over and yanked Lyra Bloom’s shirt up, exposing her belly. Gideon Stone’s face twisted. "What the hell are you doing?"

I dunked a rag into the boiling blue soup. Then I wiped down her stomach. "Scrubbing her clean. Stomach, chest, then the brand. But we start here." I wiped her down, dipping back into the heat over and over. Then I turned, sliding a sleek dagger right into the boiling liquid to sterilize it.

Gideon growled at the blade. Mara Croft took a step closer, eyes wide. "Lyra?" She sounded lost. I didn't answer. I quickly wiped Lyra Bloom’s chest, then locked onto the ugly brand Magnus had burned into her skin.

I rubbed the rag over the brand. Lyra Bloom let out a sharp whimper. "You holding up?" She looked up, nodding. "Ready for the real deal?"

She sucked in a few ragged breaths. "Is it gonna hurt?"

"I'm not gonna sugarcoat it—it's gonna hurt like absolute hell. But we have to purge your soul. The brand is the door, your heart is the hallway, and your soul is sitting right in your core."

"My tummy." I nodded. She squeezed her eyes shut. "Do it."

I took a hard, steady breath. God, I hated this part.

Then I drove the dagger straight into her heart.
