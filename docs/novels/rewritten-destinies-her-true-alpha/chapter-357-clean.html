Chapter 357

I’m stuffing my face with sandwich number two. *Tap, tap.* A soft knock. I look up. Elara Meadow is standing there, eyes glued to her shoes. "Alpha Lyra?" she mumbles. I drop the food and grin.

"Drop the title, Elara. Seriously. Just Lyra."

She shakes her head fast. "Alpha Magnus wants the hierarchy clear."

I sigh. "Fine. What's up, Allanah?"

Another bow from her. "Alpha Damon's here. Wants to grab lunch..." Her eyes dart to my desk, spotting the food. She looks terrified.

"Send him on in," I say.

Damon struts in a second later but freezes. "Oh. Uh..." He glances back. Outside, I hear Allanah scurrying back to her desk. "Didn't know you already had food. Wanted to buy you lunch for your first day."

I wink, flashing a grin. "Rain check. Tomorrow, maybe? Today, I'm booked. Got a date." I rub my palms together, checking the clock.

Damon scowls, walks over, and plops down. "A date? With who?" Wow. The jealousy in his voice is thick. I look up. "Gideon Stone."

Damon's jaw drops. "A date... with Gideon? Old man Gideon? Are you freaking kidding me?"

I lose it. I laugh so hard my eyes start watering. "Hold on," I wheeze.

"What's so funny?" He pouts.

I laugh even harder. "Wait, wait, wait." I wipe my tears, sucking in air, trying to breathe. Damon lets out a low growl. "My bad, my bad," I say, wiping my face again. "It's not a *real* date." His pout finally fades.

"Then what is it?"

I grin. "Gideon's testing me. Right now, he's hiding some rogue code in the mainframe. My job? Hunt it down and wipe it out."

"Oh." Damon glances toward the door. "So, a test?"

I nod. "Yep. He wants to see what I've got. That's why I ate early. He's planting the bug while I finish up. Once the timer hits zero, the hunt is on."

"You're actually hyped for this?" Damon looks totally lost.

I nod, grinning ear to ear. "Hell yeah. I used to do this with a buddy all the time. They taught me so much about hunting bugs. I can't wait to show Gideon what I can do."

"A buddy?"

I nod, spinning a quick lie. "Yeah, a human girl." His eyes go wide. "Met her online. She did the same college course years ago, so she helped me study. Kind of like a mentor."

Damon's mouth hangs open. "You're friends with a human?"

I shrug. "Pretty much."

"That's wild. What do you even talk about?"

I chuckle. "Server stuff. Web design. She actually helped me launch a business freshman year."

Damon leans closer. "A business?"

Inside my head, Umbra and Terra roll their eyes.

*This guy is such a joke, so easy to read,* Umbra scoffs.

*Tell him 'friend,' he thinks 'guy,'* Terra snorts. *Tell him 'human,' he freaks. Tell him 'business,' and boom, he's hooked. Predictable.* I think back to them.

Umbra thrashes her tail. *But we still don't know jack about Cipher.*

*True,* I reply. I snap back to Damon. He has to believe Cipher is a chick, or he'll make my life a living hell. Both wolves nod and back off.

I nod at Damon. "Yeah. Just building websites for human businesses. Nice little side hustle."

"How much cash we talking?"

I pretend to think. "Last year? Dunno, a couple hundred."

He chuckles. "That's it?"

"Well, yeah, I was in school. But five hundred grand isn't exactly pocket change." I laugh. "Gave my investments a nice little kick."

"You cleared half a million on a side gig?" His voice drops to a whisper.

"Yep." I bite my lip to keep from smirking. "It was mostly for my school portfolio anyway. Wasn't even about the cash, just needed the credits for finals." I shrug, clicking open a program. "Dumped the money into investments. Honestly, haven't even checked the balance lately."

A knock rattles the door. We both look. Gideon Stone pops his head in, his smile dropping a bit when he spots Damon. "Ready when you are, Lyra."

"Sweet. Let me just scarf down this sandwich and I'm on it." Gideon is practically vibrating. I chuckle, glancing at Damon. "Give me fifteen minutes max." Gideon nods, taps the doorframe, and heads out.

Damon turns to me. "He's seriously hyped for this?"

I nod. "Same here." I grab my sandwich and take a huge bite. Damon leans back, watching me for a bit, but I just focus on chewing. "Tomorrow, then. Keep your schedule clear for lunch, alright?"

"You got it." I smile, then look back at my screen. He stands up, gives me one last look, and walks out. The second the door shuts, I spring up and sprint to the doorway. "Elara!" She looks up. "Tell Gideon it is *on*." I wink. She cracks a tiny smile. I dash back to my keyboard.
