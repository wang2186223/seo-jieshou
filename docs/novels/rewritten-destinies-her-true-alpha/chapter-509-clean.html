Chapter 509

My dad took a hit. He stumbled back, face pale. "Lyra." He lunged forward, but I slapped my palm in the air.

"Stop right there." I stared him down. For once in my miserable life, he looked pathetic. "You rigged my life. You rigged Rowan. You yanked our strings like we were cheap puppets, and now you're pissed because we're screaming? Screw that."

Orion was vibrating. Pure, raw fury. "Get the hell out." My dad locked eyes with him. Shoulders squared. He wanted a brawl.

"Just go, Dad," I muttered, tipping the bottle back for a massive gulp. "We did your dirty work. You won. I squeezed the info out of Magnus." I glanced at Orion. "Whatever he wanted from you, who cares now? It's dead. We've got a job to do." Orion snapped his head toward me. "Look, I get you're raging, but it's over."

"Can we just talk? Please?" Dad pleaded.

I shook my head. "Maybe next year. Not now. You made this mess, now choke on it." I glared. "Seriously... get out."

Dad froze. He stared at me for a long, heavy beat. "Fine." He nodded. "Fine." He dragged his feet to the door, then stopped. Hand on the knob. "Look..." He turned around. "I screwed up." His eyes flicked to Orion. "Be brave. Be real." Then back to me. "Be you." And he slammed the door behind him.

I turned to Orion. Still shaking like a leaf. "Hey." He was burning a hole in the table with his eyes. "Hey, look at me." He finally raised his head. "Take a breath. It's fine."

"No, it's not," he growled. "I'm losing my mind here."

"Why?" I sighed. "Yeah, it's a shitshow. But it's done."

His eyes went pitch black. "You hated my guts," he whispered. "I was so damn mad at you. I thought you were in on the whole thing and still giving me the cold shoulder. That's why you ghosted me. I tried to play nice, to see it from your side, but some days..."

"Some days you wanted to throttle me," I finished. He nodded. "And now your brain is fried."

He let out a sharp breath. "Yeah. Pretty much. You too?"

"A bit." I let out a dry laugh. "I spent forever hating you. But right before graduation? I actually let it go." He reached for the wine bottle. Empty.

"But," he said, locking his eyes onto mine. "There's always a 'but'."

I snorted. "But then graduation happened, and you shoved Astrid in my face while she called my dad a traitor. That set me off all over again."

He grabbed the last bottle. Chugged it. Hard. He whipped out his phone, dialed, and barked, "Bring more wine. Now." He hung up and drained the rest of the bottle. "I thought you knew everything. That's why I was so damn confused when you looked at me like I was dirt. Then you shifted. Umbra popped out, and Shadow went absolutely wild. He was screaming in my head to claim you. He knew you were his mate. But we couldn't figure out why you wanted us dead."

My eyebrows shot up. "Wait, what?"

"I thought you were just playing the part. I thought you were a genius actress—until that final look. I ran after you to see what that call was about, and honestly? To kiss you. God, I wanted to kiss you so bad, just to feel if you still cared." A knock cut him off. Elara walked in, carrying four fresh bottles. She dumped them on the table, stacked the dirty plates and dead soldiers onto her cart, and rolled out.

"But then you followed me right into a goddamn warzone," I said.

He nodded. "Yeah. And I was terrified to show you my wolf. I was sweating bullets, and then..."

"Ronnie died." I popped open a new bottle. "And his kid showed up."

"You were breaking down, and I just wanted to hold you. But when you looked up and saw *me*, not Ru... it hit me. I'd completely blown it. You weren't acting. You actually, truly hated me." I nodded, blowing a loud raspberry.

"I was totally in the dark. And yeah, I despised you." I looked him dead in the eye. "I wanted to hate you so bad it practically threw me into Damon's arms. Dad was right about that. Once you were gone, I let him get close. And when the Astrid stuff dropped, I let him get even closer." Orion let out a low growl. "But hey, it got us the intel."

"Let's hope," he muttered.
