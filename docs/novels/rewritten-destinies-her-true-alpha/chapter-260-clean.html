Chapter 260

"Seventy-two." My stomach dropped straight to hell. "Seventy-two times? Alone? How the hell did you survive that?"

She let out a heavy sigh. "Had no choice, babe."

"No way. That's crazy." I scrambled for words. "That's like..."

"Every single month. From eighteen—when you met Damon—right up to the pregnancy. Would've been more, but we conceived on your birthday, so that year was just once." Her voice went quiet. "We were almost twenty-five when we kicked the bucket."

That hit me right in the gut. All those times, alone in the damn woods. "God, I'm so sorry."

She took a deep breath and stood up. "They get easier, trust me. Plus, I had a mate's touch to take the edge off. You two just survived your first run with zero help except me. I took the brunt of the heat so you guys could catch your breath."

I threw my arms around her. "You're the toughest wolf I've ever known. Period."

She chuckled. "Yeah, well, you're biased. I'm your wolf."

"Nope." Umbra trotted over and plopped down beside her. "She's dead right. You're stronger than both of us combined. Six years of that hell? We couldn't even handle round one."

I snorted. "Cut the crap, Umbra. We didn't fail. That's your anger talking. You're treating this like some game you have to win. To you, wanting a guy means losing. Come on, we were poisoned! No mate to help us. We just focused on the guys we liked to get through it. We did damn good, all things considered."

Umbra stared at me, then let out a long sigh. "Fine. You're right." She rested her head on her paws. "I was just... so pissed watching you beg Orion, and so damn embarrassed that I was howling for Shadow, that I..." She stopped.

"You were ashamed of both of us," I finished. She nodded.

"But I took it out on you. Which was crappy of me." Another sigh. "Terra's right. I projected my own issues onto you. You didn't deserve that." She stood up, shaking out her fur. "That heat drained me. I felt weak, and honestly? It scared the hell out of me."

Terra gave Umbra's paw a quick lick. "You're the Alpha. Being vulnerable sucks. But we're a team, we balance each other out. If you slip, I got you. If I slip, you got me. And if we both mess up, Lyra's got our backs." I nodded along.

"I know." Umbra paced a bit, then faced me. "I'm sorry. I shouldn't have called you pathetic."

I flinched. "It's fine. I get it."

"No, it's not. You dig Orion, and I know you want him to be your mate. Hell, I like Shadow just as much." Her head drooped. "But after this heat mess, we need to stay the hell away from both of them. No more chasing. I'll focus on running with the girls—we can learn a lot as a tight little pack in the woods. And you? You need to put all your energy into hacking."

Terra nodded. "I'm with Umbra on this. Dragging this out is just asking for trouble. Look at last week. We let our guard down, and boom—Damon forced a heat. If we were on our own, we'd be mated to that piece of trash right now." She growled. "So yeah, keeping our distance from these hot, distracting guys until we find out who's trying to murder everyone? Sounds like a plan."

I threw my head back and snorted. She was so dead serious. "Fine," I said, nodding. "But I've got conditions."

Both wolves tilted their heads. "Which are...?"

I smirked. "We have to actually work as a team. I know we said we would, but look at today—we immediately started pointing fingers and playing the blame game." They both sighed and nodded.

"Honestly, we need to start meditating again." Terra nudged her chin toward my planter.

"Why's that?"

"Because the second we quit, your plant's growth stalled. When we were hunting for your power, we were actually close. We weren't just passing each other by. Chatting is fine, but we need real quality time. We need our souls to lock in and connect with our power. It worked wonders. I felt way stronger back then."

I glanced at Umbra. She was clearly chewing on the idea. "What's your take?"

She stared at the planter, then at Terra. "She's right." She padded over to the plant. "It's totally stuck." She looked up at me. "Let's listen to our Gamma. She's got more brains than the two of us combined."

Terra puffed her chest out, and I grinned. "Deal. One hour, every single day, right here."

*BAM.* The bathroom door slammed open. "I smell blood!" The shower curtain got ripped back. "Why the hell aren't you healing?!"
