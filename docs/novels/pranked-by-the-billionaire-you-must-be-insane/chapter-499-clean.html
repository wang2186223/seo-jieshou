Chapter 505

The crowd finally got the memo.

This girl was the real deal. Gorgeous, insanely talented, and literally the legendary Ghost Doctor who yanks people back from the grave. She had both the Lancasters and the Blackwoods wrapped around her finger.

Take the Lancasters, for starters.

They had a house full of boys, so they treated Vivienne like their little princess, spoiling her rotten.

But the second their precious little girl lost that piano bet to the Ghost Doctor?

Zip. Not a single word to save her skin. Instead, they forced Vivienne to eat her own words, throwing her pride—and their own family name—right into the dirt.

It was basically: "Whatever keeps the Doc happy, goes."

And the Blackwoods? Same vibe.

They weren't as loud about it, but they made their point crystal clear. They had the Ghost Doctor's back, no questions asked.

Hell, they even kicked Beatrice, the golden girl of their second branch, straight out of Elyndor!

No doubt about it—this Ghost Doctor was on a whole different level.

Honestly, just the name "Ghost Doctor" was enough to make every billionaire and power player on the planet drool.

Case in point...

Once the ambulance hauled Magnus off and the drama with the other families settled down, the party got right back on track.

And then, the swarm hit. Global CEOs, mafia-tier family heads, high-ranking politicians...

Every big shot in the room rushed Lyra like crazy.

Their eyes were practically burning with greed.

Some shoved business cards into her hands. Others tried to slickly pry her personal number out of her.

One guy literally yelled out a seven-figure check just to get ten seconds of her time.

Hey, the woman literally robs the Grim Reaper. Her medical skills are straight-up magic.

And those custom pills she rolls by hand? People literally fight to the death for them on the black market.

Even if they couldn't get a private checkup, just scoring one or two of those pills would be a massive win.

I mean, who doesn't get sick? Who doesn't need a cheat code for death?

The richer you get, the more terrified you are of kicking the bucket.

If you can get in good with the Ghost Doctor...

Boom. You just bought yourself another ten or twenty years on this earth.

That thought sent the crowd into a straight-up frenzy. They packed around Lyra, shouting, "Hey, Doc! Over here!" just begging for a single glance.

Right under the flashing lights.

Lyra stood there, looking like a million bucks, totally unfazed by the sea of global elites.

"Saving lives is a matter of vibe," she said, her voice ice-cold. "I only heal the people the universe throws my way."

She didn't even blink. She didn't care about their fat wallets or fancy titles.

To her, they were just background noise.

She was way out of their league—untouchable, elegant, and totally out of reach.

Vivienne’s eyes went bloodshot. She clenched her fists so hard her nails cut right into her palms.

What a total fake!

She was just putting on a show, trying to steal the spotlight so she could kick Vivienne out and take her place as the Lancaster heiress.

Like, who the hell brings an acupuncture kit to a party?

Who packs a whole-ass pill-rolling setup in their purse?

Lyra showed up to Elara’s birthday bash packed like a mobile clinic.

She was obviously just waiting for the perfect moment to strike.

The more Vivienne chewed on it, the more she smelled a rat. She bet her own parents had rigged Magnus’s collapse, teaming up with Lyra just to throw her this massive debut.
