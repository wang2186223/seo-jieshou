Chapter 163

I spun on the second chick. "Your turn. Talk."

She went pale as paper, dropping hard onto her knees. "I-I just did what she told me! She's my Luna!" She literally face-planted onto the floor. God, I wanted to stomp right on her head.

I crouched down. "What pack?"

"Dark Forest," she whimpered.

The girls behind me gasped. But me? I just threw my head back and laughed.

"Are you fucking kidding me?" I dialed back my aura and waited for the two of them to scramble up.

"What?" The Luna sat back on her heels, looking dumbfounded.

"You guys are the smallest, brokest joke of a pack in existence, and you think you have the right to judge anyone?" I snapped my fingers, hand out.

"Excuse me?" The Luna tried to flare her pathetic little aura, but I slammed mine down like a brick wall, crushing hers before it could touch a single soul.

"I said your pack is trash, broke, and pathetic. You raise garbage women who think a local title makes them hot shit everywhere else. Now hand over the damn tablets so I can get these kids where they need to go. And trust me, I'm not just dragging your ass to Magnus—the Council is hearing about this too."

"They won't do jack," she sneered.

"Maybe not," I purred. "But King Rowan? Yeah, he hates this kind of shit."

Boom. The color drained right out of her face. Hook, line, and sinker.

The two of them scrambled to shove the tablets into my hands. I flicked my chin at the other four girls. "Unlock 'em." They did it, fast. I passed them around, spun on my heel, and let my aura rip.

"Shut up!" I roared.

Instantly, dead silence.

"Listen up! We're doing this fast. Alphabetical order, starting with A. Clear a path. When you hear your name, sprint up, check in, and get out of the way. Got it?"

A wave of "Yes, ma'am!" hit me. I lined up my crew, assigned them their letters, and we went to work.

Names flew. Tap, tap, tap. Thank god the app auto-deleted checked-in names. The only bottleneck was the kids running up. I glanced at my watch. Damn. No way we'd make the cutoff.

Time to call the boss. I opened the mind link to Magnus.

*Magnus.*

*Yeah, Lyra?*

*Did you handpick the bitches at the front desk?*

*Sure did. Why?*

*Did you tell them to lock out the unranked wolves so they'd get kicked out of school?*

Silence. Then, way too fake-happy: *No way! Why would I do a thing like that?*

*Great. Then push back the deadline.*

*Whoa, hold on. I can't do that,* he stammered.

*Why the hell not?*

*Because the rule is set. Miss the deadline, you're expelled. Period.*

*Are you freaking kidding me? You're gonna expel a Gamma, a Beta, and two Alphas?* I couldn't believe my ears.

*Wait, what? They had strict orders to let the ranked wolves in first!*

*Aha. So you DID tell them to shut out the unranked.* My blood started to boil.

I whipped out my phone and dialed Orion. Again.

"Lyra, I'm kind of in the middle of something."

"Yeah, sorry to bug you again, but if you don't fix this right now, you're gonna need a new dean. Because I'm about to catch a murder charge."

He choked, instantly locking in. "What did he do?"

"There's that big assembly today, right?"

"Yeah, I'm sitting inside waiting for the crowd to settle."

"Well, I'm stuck outside, busting my ass to check people in. Magnus declared that anyone not registered by—" I checked the screen. "—literally right now, gets booted. And his little Luna pet and her lackey were freezing out the unranked wolves on purpose to get them expelled."

"Say that again." His voice went ice-cold, sending shivers straight down my spine.

"There are over a hundred and fifty kids still stuck out here. I've got an Alpha, a Beta, a Gamma, and two unranked wolves helping me speed-run this. But when I pinged Magnus? He claimed his hands were tied. Said he told the Dark Forest Luna to prioritize ranked wolves. Basically, he set them up to fail. If I have to deal with this myself, Orion, I'm going to paint the walls with his blood."
