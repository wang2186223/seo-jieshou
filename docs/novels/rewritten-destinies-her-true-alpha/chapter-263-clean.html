Chapter 263

Fast forward eighteen months.

"You guys set?" Daisy was practically vibrating, jumping up and down. We’d spent hours frying her hair into those perfect curls this morning, and damn, she killed it in that pastel pink dress. Her black graduation gown just hung off her shoulders while she put the final touches on her makeup. I scanned my living room, grinning like an idiot.

"Seriously, you all look hot as hell," I said. No lie, either. Fawn had her fiery red hair tossed over one shoulder in a thick braid, making that boring dark gown actually look good. Then there was Luna. Talk about a total 180 from freshman year. She’d spent two years growing out her hair, only to chop it into this edgy, textured black bob right before showing up today. "Luna, oh my god. That cut is everything."

"Long hair was fine, but this? This is me," Luna laughed, running her fingers through the messy locks. She yanked her graduation gown over a cute white sundress. "Can you believe we survived three years of this? I thought they were bluffing when they said they’d fast-track us, but damn, they actually did it."

Juniper strutted out of my bedroom, a tight gold dress hugging every single curve, her gown balled up in her hands. "Well, duh," she said, her smile dropping a bit. "With all those crazy attacks lately, getting us back to our packs ASAP was the only smart move." I nodded, walking over to snatch the gown from her. Juniper pouted, tossing her braids back. "Seriously, why do I have to wear this ugly black trash bag? You literally tortured me in the gym for two years. Let me show off the body!" I cracked up, shaking my head as I helped her slide into the gown.

"Drama queen," I teased as she spun around.

"Hey, she’s not wrong," Fawn chimed in, stepping up to the full-length mirror leaning against my wall. "Before I met you guys, my dad literally called me 'rolly-polly.' Didn't matter what I did, to him, I was just the fat kid."

My jaw dropped. "Are you kidding me? You were never fat!"

Fawn offered a weak smile. "Yeah, well, you guys never treated me like that. But my pack? Brutal. My dad actually shipped me off to this school because my sister was getting married, and she didn't want her new in-laws seeing her 'chubby' sister."

"Oh, Fawn..." Daisy rushed over, throwing her arms around her. Fawn tried to laugh it off, but a tear slipped down her cheek. "Why the hell didn't you tell us this?"

"Because you guys made me feel normal. Honestly, at first, I was scared to death that if I brought it up, you'd start judging me too. But then I realized you guys aren't like that. So I let it go. I'm not that girl anymore anyway." She whipped open her gown, zipping it down to show off a skin-tight black dress that hugged her killer figure.

"You've always been a knockout, Fawn. The weight had nothing to do with it." I stepped in, stopping her mid-spin, and grabbed her shoulders to look her dead in the eye. "You've been gorgeous inside and out since day one. If you love this body, awesome, we love it too. But listen to me, and listen good." I waited for her to nod. "Your worth has absolutely zero to do with a scale or a dress size. It's about who you are and what you achieve. Period. Let that sink into your brain before you go back to those jerks and let them get in your head again."

Fawn nodded, the tears really flowing now as she squeezed me tight. "It's so stupid, but my dad called. They're driving down for graduation, and his exact words were, 'Don't embarrass me.'"

Daisy let out a loud snort. "Uh, does the idiot know you're literally valedictorian material?"

Fawn shook her head. "Nope. Today was the first time he's called me since freshman year."

I pulled back, planting a hard kiss on her forehead. "If anyone gives you crap today—and I mean *anyone*—you call me. I will personally wreck your dad and your entire pack."

"You can't just go fighting my pack, Lyra," Fawn laughed, wiping her eyes.

"Watch me," I said, bumping my hip against hers. I spun around to face the whole room. "Look, you guys come from different packs and different ranks. But my wolves? We've claimed you. I don't care where you end up or how far you run, you are our pack. Period. Family." I let that sink in. "It doesn't matter if we don't talk for months, who you end up mating with, or what rank you get." I glanced at my girls who used to be unranked, knowing damn well they had real titles now. Luna and Daisy exchanged a smirk. "You are ours, forever. We love you, we protect you. I don't care if it's trash parents, pissed-off pack members, or—Goddess forbid—some abusive mate. You call, I show up. No questions asked."

The second the words left my mouth, they piled in for a massive group hug.

Blood, bond, or chosen—family is family. And that’s all that matters.
