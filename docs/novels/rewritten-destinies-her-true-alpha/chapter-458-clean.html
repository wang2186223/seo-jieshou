Chapter 458

My eyes snapped open. Rhys was right there, looming. I shrieked, scrambling back like a kicked cat. We both froze, shocked. Clatter! His scalpel hit the floor. Clang! The nurse dropped her whole damn tray.

"Whoa, easy there," Rhys said, hands up. "You blacked out. Pain did it."

I sank back into the mattress. Right. Wrong life. My hand slid down to my stomach. Flat. Empty. No pup. Damn. I shut my eyes, taking a slow drag of air. "My bad," I muttered, shaking my head to clear the fog. Bad move. A bomb went off behind my eyes. I dry-heaved, gagging.

"Don't sweat it. You've been through hell." Rhys turned, snatched a fresh scalpel, and marched back to the bed. The nurse scrambled back in, carrying a clean tray, leaving the ruined one trashed on the floor.

"You gonna clean that up?" I wheezed.

She nodded fast. "After this." She slammed the new tray onto the nightstand.

"Look, we heal fast, but royal doctor rules say keep it sterile, wolf or not." Rhys tossed the scalpel onto the tray. "Alright. I have to slice the skin around your ribs."

"Why?" I narrowed my eyes, instantly on guard.

Rhys just gave me a soft, pitying smile. "You're healing, but it's messy. Your skin literally fused over the broken bone. I have to cut it free, pop the bone back in, stitch you up with dissolvables, and wrap you tight."

A shiver ran down my spine. Inside my head, Umbra burst out of the mental woods. *Just let us heal you!* she yelled, making me feel like crap.

I threw back some calm vibes. *Not yet. I need to look like a total wreck tomorrow. We heal the second we get out of here.* She growled, pissed.

*This is stupid,* she snarled, daring me to push her. *The second the doc pops those ribs, I'm fixing them. And the bleeding.*

I almost laughed. *Just wait till I limp back to the office and handle this shit.*

She snorted, turning her back on me. *Stupid humans and their stupid-ass plans.*

I looked up at Rhys and nodded. "Do it."

He hesitated, frowning. "I'd really rather knock you out cold for this."

I shook my head. "I'll live."

He swallowed hard, grimaced, and nodded. "Your funeral, kid." He looked at the nurse. "Hold her on her side. Whatever happens... do not let her budge."

The nurse looked at me, then at him. "I'll try." She turned to me, eyes wide. "This is gonna hurt like hell. Please don't fight me. I'm not that strong."

I let out a weak laugh and nodded. "I'll try too."

Then she grabbed my shoulder and hip, flipping me over. Holy shit. Blinding, white-hot agony. I screamed bloody murder as she pinned me down. Air fled my lungs. My vision went black and white, flickering.

"Sorry, oh my god, I'm so sorry," she whispered, terrified.

Rhys took a breath, placing a hand on my flank. "Deep breath in. Blow it out." I did it. "Good. Keep doing that. I'd tell you this is gonna help, but I'm not a liar."

I nodded once, eyes shut, focusing on the air going in and out.

That lasted maybe sixty seconds. Then the blade bit in.

The burning pain exploded, swallowing the whole damn room. I couldn't see. I was just gasping, my body forcing air in and out in ragged jerks. It burned like a million wasp stings. Like silver. Like a red-hot iron rod shoved right into my side.

Rhys leaned close. "Scream if you need to. Just stay still."

I shook my head, teeth gritted. "If I start... I won't stop."

I felt him watching me, though I couldn't see shit. He let out a soft sigh. "It's fine. Scream. I don't care, and neither does Jenn."

"Seriously, Miss Lyra, I don't mind at all," Jenn chimed in. Sweet girl, even if she was older than me.

"See?" Rhys said. "Just let it out."

"Why?" I gasped, jaw locked tight.

"Because you're holding your breath. You're turning purple, damn it. If you scream, I know you're breathing. Plus, you're clenching your jaw so hard I'm scared you'll shatter your teeth. Let it go. No one's judging."

I shook my head again.

Rhys groaned. "I haven't even freed one bone yet. You're tensing up, and it's about to get way worse."

I forced one eye open. It burned like hell, but I could see his dead-serious face. "If I start..." I wheezed.

"Then you scream, and we listen. Simple."

I shook my head, shivering. "I'll cry. If I scream, I'll start crying."

Rhys shook his head back, then nodded to the nurse. She draped a sterile cloth right over my face.

"There," Rhys said. "Now nobody will see a thing."
