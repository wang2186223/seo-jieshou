Chapter 576

I snapped back into my human skin. People around me gasped, but I didn't care. I bolted straight for Finnian. "Talk to me. What happened?" Yeah, I knew the cave collapsed. Duh. But I needed the dirty details if I was going to pull anyone out alive.

Finnian grabbed me. Hard. He squeezed so tight he knocked the wind right out of my lungs. His chest heaved, and he planted a rough kiss on my temple. "I'm so fucking sorry, Aim," he choked out. I pulled back. His face was a total mess of tears. "It's on me. All of it."

"What is?" I stared up at my uncle. He let go of me, backing away.

"He's dead. Because I fucked up." He shook his head, a sob ripping out of his throat. He locked eyes with me, tears streaming down his face. "His mind-link went dead. I can't reach him. Nothing." Then he let out this scream. Jesus. It was a sound that was going to screw up my sleep for years.

"Beta Finnian," Orion said, stepping up. But Finnian was already shaking again.

"Don't call me that! Look at me!" Finnian snarled. He hit the dirt, dropping to his knees just as Astrid ran up. "I'm fucking pathetic." He stared down at his hands like he was seeing ghosts.

Astrid approached him like he was a wild animal. She'd tried this before, and it hadn't gone well. She caught my eye and gave a tiny shake of her head. "You bled out half your veins dragging guys to the med tent before the whole damn ceiling came down," she said, dropping beside him. "We found you under a pile of rocks. You almost lost your fucking arm." Her voice cracked. "You're starving, you're exhausted, you're barely healed, and your best friend is buried alive. You don't have to be a tough guy right now. It's okay." She pulled his head against her big, pregnant belly. Finnian just went limp against her.

"Uncle Finnian?" I squatted down. I pressed two fingers to his neck. Weak pulse, but it was there. "Orion? Get him to the tent. Now. He needs a doc." I looked at my mate. He nodded. Astrid stood up as Orion scooped my uncle up and hauled ass up the hill. She started to follow, but I grabbed her wrist. "Wait. Tell me what happened before you go."

Astrid glanced up the hill, then nodded. She pointed toward the cave mouth where a few guys were still digging like crazy. "We hit the cave yesterday." She looked dead on her feet, waddling over to the massive boulders blocking the entrance. "It was going perfect. And then..." She swallowed hard. "Boom. An explosion. Finnian and your dad stayed behind to make sure everyone else got out." She looked at me. "We felt it all the way at the pack house. I was terrified."

I tilted my head. "Felt what?" I touched the massive rock in front of me. An idea started to form.

"The cave-in," Astrid shuddered. "And the panic."

I closed my eyes. I pushed my senses, letting my magic slip through the cracks of the stone. "The panic?"

My eyes stayed shut, but I felt the air shift. She was nodding. "Yeah. It hit the whole pack link. The guys who made it out were screaming for help, but..." She choked back a sob. "The ones trapped inside... they all screamed at once. The pain, the terror—it was too much." She leaned her weight against the rock, her breathing getting ragged. "I'm gonna hear those screams in my nightmares forever." She rested her forehead against the cold stone. "Every wolf in the territory ran here. Kaelen took charge. He got every guy who could lift a rock digging, and told the women to set up the med tent."

"Where was Finnian?" I kept talking, keeping her distracted while my mind crawled deeper into the dark cracks of the rock. I blinked my eyes open for a second. Astrid was clutching her stomach. "Astrid?" Her breathing hitched. Her stomach went tight. Shit. I instantly thought of Elara Meadow going into labor. I reached out to grab her.

She waved me off, taking a few seconds to just breathe. "False alarm. Just stress," she gasped. "The doctor checked me out the last time Finnian..." She trailed off, stretching her neck.

"Finnian did what?" I snapped my focus back to her.

Astrid sighed. "I tried to drag him away from here. He fought me. I fell." I let out a low growl, but she waved her hand again. "It was my own fault. I tripped. But he wouldn't budge. Not from this spot. He wanted to stay where he could still hear your dad." She looked up. "The guilt is eating him alive."

"Why?" I turned back to the rocks. My magic was dancing deep inside the stone cracks now. Suddenly, it hit something wet. Not solid. A metallic, copper taste flooded my mouth. Blood. I knew it before Astrid even spoke.

"Because your dad took the hit for him."
