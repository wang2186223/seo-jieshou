Chapter 1114

The guy with the bloody scratches on his face didn't even blink. He stepped up and smacked the penalty slip right onto Willow’s forehead. He didn't give a crap if she could see it or not.

"Mess with the tournament staff one more time," he growled, "and we’re filing to kick The Dreamscape Initiative out of this game. You’ll be off this island by tonight."

"Like hell you will!" Willow spat. No way these rent-a-cops had that kind of power. She scrambled to her feet, ready to tear into them again.

"Whoa, Willow, stop! Just stop!"

Vivienne, who’d been watching from a safe distance, lost her mind. She lunged forward and locked her arms around Willow, holding her back.

She leaned in, breathing hard, and hissed right into Willow’s ear. "Willow, seriously, chill out! Think about your dad."

Victor Vance.

The name hit Willow like cold water. She went totally stiff.

"Willow, if they boot us off this rock... your dad... oh god, he’ll destroy us. You have to stop picking fights with these guys," Vivienne pleaded, rubbing her back to calm her down.

The reality of it finally clicked. The color drained right out of Willow's face. All that hot rage just evaporated.

Her dad...

Yeah, he wasn't the warm, fuzzy family type.

If she got kicked out, lost her spot, and blew this whole tournament...

He would make her life a living hell.

Willow froze, not daring to twitch.

Right then, the dorm doors swung open. A couple of security guards marched out carrying their suitcases and dropped them right at their feet.

*Thud.*

The heavy bags hit the concrete hard.

Willow ground her teeth, her chest tight with anger and panic. "So... where the hell are we supposed to sleep?"

The guards didn't answer with words. Instead, they tossed three rolled-up bundles onto the ground. *Thud. Thud. Thud.*

"Dorms are full," one of them said.

After Willow's little psycho act, they had zero pity left. "Go sleep out there."

They pointed.

It was a strip of empty, dark beach.

"Nice ocean view. Tons of space. Nobody to bug you," the guard sneered. "Sleep tight, ladies."

They turned on their heels and walked off. Willow shook with rage, screaming every curse word she knew at their backs.

The guards didn't even turn around.

Now what? The two of them just stood there, staring at their bags and the crappy sleeping rolls. It looked bad. Really bad.

Once the sun dipped below the horizon on this island, the temperature plummeted. It got freezing cold, fast.

Add in the brutal ocean wind, and they’d be frozen solid by morning.

This was basically a death sentence.

How could they sleep outside in this? How did these bastards dare treat them like trash?

The sky was turning pitch black.

Standing there in the open, they were already shivering.

Willow hugged herself, rubbing her arms, glaring at the cheap sleeping bags. "Those absolute bastards," she hissed through chattering teeth. "Making me sleep on the damn sand!"

"Just wait till we get back to Vaelion City. I’ll make every single one of them crawl and beg."

Vivienne wrapped her arms tight around her chest.

Night was just starting, and it was already freezing. In a few hours, it was going to be unbearable.

Her eyes darted around, scanning the dark, until they locked onto the glowing lights of the Apex Research Hub in the distance.

She clenched her fists. A memory flashed in her mind—the docks, right before dinner.

She remembered what Lyra had said.

Squeezing her hands even tighter, she turned to Willow. "Hey, Willow. Doesn't this feel super weird to you?"

"Like, Caspian gets taken down, and boom, we get hit with a penalty instantly."

"And... I saw that head officer guy. He was being, like, insanely nice to Lyra."
