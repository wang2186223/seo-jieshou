Chapter 585

We slammed the door shut. Orion let out a sharp laugh. "Damn, Lyra. That was cold, even for you."

I cracked up, tossing my head back. I didn't look back as I started hauling ass up the stairs. "Hey, we were out of options. Rhys was the only play we had right then, and I've gotta get back to my dad's."

"Yeah, you're not wrong," Orion snorted, shaking his head. "But holy shit, his face was priceless." He chuckled again. Then we slipped back through the closet, right into a wall of screaming.

"Push, Elara! Push!"

"No!" Another scream ripped out of her. It was this raw, animal sound that made my skin crawl. Orion froze dead in his tracks. I shoved him forward to clear the doorway and squeezed into the cottage, pushing past him toward the closet.

"Lyra..." Orion's whisper cut through the noise. I stopped.

"What?"

In the dark, he tapped his nose. It took me a beat to get it. *Smell.* I nodded, turned around, and took a massive hit of air.

"You got this, babe. Come on," Rhys Thorne was saying, trying to sound all calm and steady.

"But the second they're out, you're gone!" Elara wailed. "I can't watch you leave twice in twenty-four hours!"

I tuned out their drama and inhaled deep. One sniff was all it took. I caught what Orion already smelled. The baby's scent was dropping. Elara was holding the kid back, literally refusing to push, and the baby was too far down. She was suffocating her own kid.

I whipped around. Orion was already grabbing the gear. "You're stuck here," he said. "You can't walk away from this mess. Let me take the bags to the doc."

"You got this?"

He nodded fast. "I'll go full Lycan. It'll be faster to run the bags on four legs." He planted a quick kiss on my lips. "Catch up when you're done here."

I grabbed his jaw and kissed him back, hard. "Thanks."

"Anytime." He bolted, weaving right past the crowd around the bed.

"Orion?" my mom yelled, but he didn't even blink, just kept running.

"Mom," I said, stepping out of the closet. Her shoulders dropped a fraction. "What's the situation?"

She looked back at Elara, then peeked under the sheet covering her legs. "The kids are ready to pop. But Mom is locking up."

I stared at Elara. She wouldn't look at me. "Elara..."

"No!" she shrieked, thrashing her head on the pillow. "He's leaving the second they're born! If I don't push—"

My jaw clenched. "If you don't push right now," I cut her off, cold as ice, "your kids die. And guess what? Rhys still leaves."

Her head whipped around, her wolf's eyes flashing gold at me. "What?"

I stepped right up to the mattress. "You got a problem with the truth?"

"Lyra, seriously, take it easy on her," Daisy muttered, dabbing sweat off Elara's forehead.

"Why the hell should I?" I growled.

Elara's wolf growled back, a low warning rumble.

I leaned down, getting right in her face. "Growl all you want, babe. I'm your friend, which means I'm not going to sugarcoat this shit."

Rhys's voice broke. "Please, baby, just push."

"No!" she snapped. "I can't go through losing you again!" She stared at her mate, her face softening for a split second, then glared back at me.

I shrugged. "Fine. Then you and the twins die, and you can go hang out with him in the afterlife."

"Lyra!" my mom barked.

I just rolled my eyes. "You guys are coddling her because she's in labor. I don't have time for this crap." I glared at Elara. "Make a choice."

"What?" she stammered, totally lost, but then another contraction ripped through her and she screamed bloody murder.

I stood there, waiting for the contraction to pass before laying it out. "I said make a fucking choice. Option A: push these twins out, get some final moments with your mate, and spend the rest of your life raising your kids. Option B: keep doing this stupid shit. Your baby is already dying—I can literally smell it. You'll lose the kids, your mate, and your own damn life. But you need to pick a lane right fucking now."

"Lyra!" Rhys, Daisy, and my mom all yelled at me at once.

"Listen to me!" I snapped. "I've got one pack hanging by a thread and another barely breathing. Orion is hauling ass with meds to save my dad and half our people from wolfsbane. The Council is marching here to slaughter everyone at the pack house. And here you are, killing your own kids and yourself because the goddess handed you a miracle and it's still not good enough for you!"

Elara's eyes went wide, tears pooling in them.

"I am completely out of patience, and I'm sorry, but that's the cold hard truth. You got to spend the whole night with your mate. That's a massive fucking gift, and you're still whining for more. More from the Celestia, more from Rhys, more from everyone in this room. I love you, Elara, but you are about to make the dumbest mistake of your life. Everyone else spent hours babying you, and look where it got us. I'm not doing it." She blinked, tears spilling down her cheeks. "So choose. Your kid's life, or death. Those are your options. Because either way, Rhys is going back to the Celestia."

"But I need him!" she whimpered as another contraction hit.

"No, you don't. You want him. You love him. But you don't need him to raise these kids." I climbed onto the bed and wiped the sweat from her forehead, letting my voice soften just a bit. "So what's it gonna be? We're out of time." I could smell the fading scent of the baby. "It's literally now or never."

She looked at me, then at Rhys. She took a shaky breath and nodded. "Okay. Let's have these babies."

"Smart move."
