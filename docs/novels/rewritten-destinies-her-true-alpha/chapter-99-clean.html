Chapter 99

The room went dead silent. Ronnie was sobbing over his little girl. I stood there, waiting for someone—anyone—to say something. But Mom and Ronnie just sat there, tight-lipped.

"Lyra," Mom finally muttered.

"Can't we just stall her training?" I blurted out. I was desperate. There had to be a way to keep this kid from losing her dad.

"No way," Ronnie said, his voice barely a whisper. "Try to mess with the timing, and the universe strikes back. We'll end up with a half-baked shaman who doesn't know what the hell she's doing." He finally looked up from his daughter. "Elara... man, I hate to ask you this."

"Don't even say it." Mom leaned down and kissed his cheek.

"I need a timeline. How long do we have?" I pressed.

Ronnie glared at me. "Why do you care?"

"Just because!" I paced away, then spun back. My skin was crawling. Every instinct in my gut was screaming at me to tell them to pack their crap and run right now.

"What are you feeling?" Ronnie's voice went deep, heavy, and totally creepy.

"Wait, what?" Mom looked at Ronnie, but his eyes were glued to me.

"You need to run. Get out of here. Nothing good comes from you staying. You're just putting a target on her back." My voice dropped an octave. The whole room seemed to tilt, and my vision went haywire. But it wasn't my wolf taking over. Everything just went pitch black.

"Oh my god, what's happening to her?" Mom gasped. "How is this even possible?"

"I don't know," Ronnie whispered.

"Darkness is choking this pack," my mouth said, though it didn't feel like me speaking. "If the three-spirited one doesn't figure it out fast, or if those dirty Alphas grab the shaman, we're all screwed. Everything bright in this world will rot." My head snapped toward the little wolf pup. "Hide her. Now."

Mom gasped, her cold hands grabbing my face. "The three-spirited one?" She leaned in close. "Who is that?"

My head tilted with a sickening crack. "You already know who she is, Luna. Dig up your past before you ruin your future." She shivered, but pulled me into a tight hug anyway.

"Ronnie?" Mom's voice shook. I wanted to tell her it was okay, but I wasn't in the driver's seat anymore.

"I know it's terrifying," Ronnie said. "But she'll snap out of it. Just like I do."

"What the hell is this? She's never done anything like this before!"

"What's changed with her lately?"

"Nothing," Mom started, then froze. "Wait. Start of summer. She woke up one day and was just... different." She shifted her weight. "She said she had to mask her scent, so I called my ex. I knew his family had some serious mojo. Everyone in the pack knew it. He helped her, and she came back smelling like someone else. That's it."

"Wolves can't just swap scents. Not alone, and not for long," Ronnie said, dead serious. "The only way Lyra did that is if the Moon Goddess herself handed her her dad's powers."

"What the hell does that even mean?"

"Some wolves come from bloodlines with human ancestors. Humans with magic."

"Right. Caspian's grandma had some crazy gift. Or so he claimed."

"Well, her descendants have to go through an awakening. Just like a shaman. If they survive it and balance the magic with their wolf, they can shift and use that goddess-given power."

Mom gasped, the realization hitting her like a truck. "Three-spirited... Oh god. Celestia, no."

Ronnie went quiet for a beat. "Did you tell her?"

"No."

"You have to. She needs to know." Ronnie shifted.

"The three-spirited one already knows," my voice rasped, thick and totally foreign. "She's been watching you."

"Ronnie?"

"She's right," Ronnie said. "She's got three souls. Her wolf, her witch side, and—"

"Shut up!" Mom snapped. "Not here."

"Fine. But you have to spill the beans soon. She's got a job to do. Keep her in the dark too long, and you'll ruin everything."

They both shut up for a second.

"So, what's your plan?" Mom asked.

"I'm taking your kid's advice. Packing our bags and getting Luna out of here."

"Where will you go?"

"Hell if I know. We don't have a safe house."

Then, that creepy voice spoke through me again. "Go to Silvermoon. You'll be safe there. There's a secret tunnel under the gazebo. Take it straight to the ocean. You'll find a burnt-down cabin on the shore. Rebuild it. She'll be safe there from anyone trying to find her."
