Chapter 275

His jaw dropped. "Wait, did you just order me around?"

I shoved my face right against his, our muzzles practically mashed together. "Damn right. You don't get to tap out of a fight. Not today, not ever. If I have to force you to stay, I will." I let out a nasty growl. Then, a weak wheeze behind me made me whip around.

"Lyra..." Ronnie gasped. I bolted. I shifted back instantly, my clean graduation gown looking totally ridiculous on this blood-soaked dirt.

"Ronnie!" I grabbed his waist, trying to haul him up, but he shrieked in pain. I froze. "Hey, hey, you're fine. Come on," I lied, mostly to myself. I dropped to my knees and cupped his face. "Get up. Right now."

"Lyra..." His eyes welled up.

"Shut up, don't do that," I snapped, leaning closer. "It's too damn early. She can't do this without you."

"I trained her," he muttered.

I shook my head hard. "No. No way, Ronnie. I'm telling you, she's not ready."

He gave me this heartbreaking, pathetic little smile. "It's always too soon, kid." My lips started shaking.

"Please," I choked out. This was going to absolutely destroy his little girl.

He shook his head, his gaze going totally blank. "Left you a letter. In that room... the one I built for you."

"Ronnie, stop it, please!" I squeezed his cheeks, begging him. The others were closing in now. "She's just a kid!"

"She's *our* kid," he whispered. A door creaked open nearby. He sighed. "Promise me. Promise you'll take care of her."

Tears finally spilled over. "I can't. I'm not ready for this."

"Nobody's ever ready to be a parent, Lyra. Literally nobody." He hacked up a wet splash of blood. "You think you've got it figured out. You get months to prep. But the second they dump that tiny pup in your arms? Boom. The love, the pain, the absolute shit-your-pants terror of raising a human being... it hits you. You realize you don't know jack." He closed his eyes as we heard tiny footsteps sprinting toward us. "Keep her safe, okay? Please." His voice cracked, pure panic bleeding through. "Don't let her forget where she came from."

I pressed my forehead right against his. "I swear to the Goddess, Ronnie. She's my kid now. I'll love her like my own. She'll know exactly who her father was. I won't let her forget you. I swear." Tears tracked through the dirt on my face. I kissed his forehead just as a tiny blur slammed into us.

The second Luna threw her arms around our necks, everything went dead silent. Like someone hit pause on the universe. Ronnie and I pulled back. Luna's eyes were pure, glowing white. "The Goddess hears your oath, triple-soul, and it is sealed. Your time is coming. You'll have to protect her—and the whole damn world. Are you ready? Is your pack? Your mate?"

I knew that voice. It wasn't Luna talking. It was Her. "I don't have a mate," I said.

Luna glanced back, scanning the crowd gathered around us. "Things aren't always what they look like, Moon-Blessed." She looked back at me, smiling. "Look at you. You've come a long way since we last walked together." She patted my cheek with her tiny hand. "It's only getting messier from here. Brace yourself. Trust your gut, trust your magic. Lean on your pack to raise the kid. But don't forget—she's yours now. And she's gonna need you a hell of a lot more than you think." Her eyes fluttered shut. When they snapped open, they were chocolate brown again, overflowing with tears.

"Luna?" I whispered.

"You're here," she sobbed. I just nodded. She hugged me tight, but then she scrambled right out of my arms and over to Ronnie. "Daddy! Get up! We gotta go inside so I can patch you up!" She tried to tug him, but Ronnie just wrapped his arms around her, squeezing her tight. "I got the bandages, just like you said!"

I sat back on my heels, watching Ronnie completely fall apart. Tears streamed down his face, soaking into his beard. Luna couldn't see it. I tried to force myself to hope. Maybe he could pull through. I gathered my magic, reaching a hand out to him, but Ronnie grabbed my wrist. I looked at him, and he slowly shook his head.

He was telling me to save my energy. His eyes flicked to the crowd. I didn't care. "I trust them," I said, looking back at my crew. Then my eyes landed on Shadow. "Well, most of them." He flinched like I'd slapped him. Good. I didn't give a shit. I didn't even know who the hell he was. "Honestly, I'd say sorry, but I don't even know you."
