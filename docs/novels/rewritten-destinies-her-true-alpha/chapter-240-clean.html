Chapter 240

Quick pit stop at my place to prep, then I threw myself into the truck and gunned it to the main hall. Needed fuel before the absolute shitshow kicked off. I barely cleared the front doors when a voice cut through the noise.

"Lyra!"

I froze, spun around, and—great—there stood Damon and Magnus.

"Hey, guys." I slapped on a fake-ass smile. "What's the damage?"

Damon closed the gap, stepping right into my space. "Morning. Figured you'd skip this whole circus."

I cranked my smile up a notch, looking him dead in the eye. "Now why on earth would I do that?"

Damon went totally blank, just staring at me like a dazed puppy. He actually started leaning in, so I pivoted fast, locking eyes with Magnus instead. "Alpha Magnus. To what do I owe the honor, huh?"

Magnus cleared his throat, snapping back to reality. "What's with the circus in the lobby?"

"Oh, just made a little application portal for girls wanting to be the King's mate."

"We already have a process for—" Magnus started, but I threw up a hand to cut him off.

"My bad, let me rephrase. I *streamlined* it. Made it dummy-proof for the local girls."

"And why the hell would you do that?" Magnus took a step in, eyes drilling into me. "More competition just tanks your own chances of getting picked." He was fishing, but I'd already rehearsed this exact conversation in my head.

"I'm not throwing my hat in the ring."

*Wait, seriously? You're sitting this one out?* Terra's voice buzzed in my head, pure worry.

*Oh, she's applying alright,* Umbra chimed in, pacing up next to Terra. She was finally getting her mojo back.

*Guys, drop it...*

*No way, Lyra,* Umbra snapped. *Look, I admit Shadow is hot. And yeah, he's totally winning Terra over. But you're still hung up on Orion. If you don't sign up—even just to save face—you're gonna kick yourself later.*

*What if he's waiting for you, you don't sign up, and he's forced to pick some random chick?* Terra flopped down, chin on her paws. *You really wanna lose him over some stupid paperwork?*

I wanted to scream. Dammit, the wolves were right. But I had to deal with the real world first.

Damon was practically beaming. "For real?"

I shook my head. "Well, let's just say it's not on my radar." I caught Magnus shooting a look at Damon and smirked. I scanned the room, desperate for an escape route. Bingo. "Uh, Damon..."

"Yeah?" He leaned in, totally hooked. I flashed him a sweet smile, watching his brain short-circuit for a second.

"You've got a visitor..." I whispered.

Both guys jerked back. "Say what?" Damon spun around, while Magnus locked eyes on the trainwreck stomping toward us.

"You absolute fucking whore." Still got that screechy, nasal whine. Honestly, you'd think breaking her nose would've fixed her voice, but nope. Still grating as hell.

I let out a heavy sigh. "Blair. What a joy. I was really hoping to wrap this whole project without running into you, but hey, guess my luck ran out." I gave Magnus a quick nod. "Catch you guys later. Gotta grab a bite before I open the booth."

Damon stepped up to block Blair. "What the hell are you doing here?"

"What am I doing here? What the fuck are *you* doing here? You said you had to hit the books, but instead, you're here trying to lay my sister?"

I rolled my eyes so hard it hurt and turned to walk, but Magnus blocked my path. "You need something, Alpha?"

He nodded, cutting a glance back toward where Blair was still screeching like a banshee. "Walk with me." He offered his arm. I looped mine through his, letting him guide me toward the building. Once we were out of earshot, he leaned down, voice barely a breath. "The council's here. Well, one of the big shots."

"Why?"

"Blame Raven. She ran crying to her uncle about your little matchmaking setup. They're here to pull the plug."

I snorted.

"Hey, wipe that smirk off. Take this seriously, Lyra. You're tough, but they're the damn council."

I nodded, sobered up. "Fair point. I'll watch my back. Thanks for the heads-up."

Magnus let go of my arm with a nod. "For the record? I actually think your program is a smart move."

"Oh, really?"

"I hate the council playing matchmaker for our Luna. Every Alpha should have the right to wait for his true mate, or at least pick his own damn woman. Forcing the King's hand just gives them a green light to micromanage everyone's love life."

I nodded, playing along with his little speech. "Totally. I mean, if they can boss the King around, what's stopping them from doing the exact same thing to Damon?"

Magnus froze. His face completely fell, like the reality of his own game had just smacked him in the face. I didn't wait for him to recover. I spun on my heel and walked away.
