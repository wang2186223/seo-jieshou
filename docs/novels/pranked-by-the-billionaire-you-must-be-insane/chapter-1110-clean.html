Chapter 1127

The plan was simple: everyone had to pack onto the official ferry to get off this rock. Vivienne’s whole vibe was basically, *Yeah, you guys are pathetic.*

Lydia balled her fists, face hot. "Screw that! Who gives a crap about your stupid boats anyway? For all we know, you’d probably try to sink us on purpose!"

"Unlike you guys, we actually like each other. We’re a team. We aren't like your trashy Dreamscape Initiative, scurrying around like a bunch of hated roaches!"

Willow’s face fell. Man, Lydia was getting way too mouthy lately. Her attitude was getting seriously nasty.

She really should have pumped a heavier dose into the girl back then. Just ship her straight to the lab with that other chick and call it a day.

Whatever. Why waste breath on these broke losers? Just wait until the final scores and the lab data went live. Let’s see how much trash Lydia talks then.

Willow scoffed. "Oh, keep talking big. You hillbillies won't ever set foot on a yacht like this in your entire lives. Seriously. You’re just gonna stand there, drooling, watching me sail away."

The words barely left her mouth when—*HOOOOONK*.

A massive, deep horn blasted from the horizon. It was deafening. It completely swallowed the hum of the three Doyle yachts.

Heads snapped toward the noise. A literal monster of a ship was slicing through the waves, heading dead at them.

It got closer. The crowd gasped. Holy crap. It was... The Golden Sovereign.

The thing was a beast—seven or eight stories tall, easy. Pitch-black hull, dripping in dark gold trim. A literal floating fortress.

As the giant pulled up, those multi-million-dollar Doyle yachts they’d been bragging about? Yeah, they looked like cheap plastic toys. They started bouncing and rocking like crazy in the ship's massive wake.

The whole crowd stood there, jaws dropped.

Someone muttered, "Uh... whose boat is that?"

"Is that even real? What does that even cost?"

"Hundreds of millions, easily."

"Like, there are only a few of those on the planet."

"What kind of billionaire is coming to this dump?"

"The Doyle yachts just got absolutely wrecked."

Willow’s smirk froze. She stared at the ship, her brain totally short-circuiting.

No way. No freaking way. How does a ship like this just show up here? And why right now? Could it... could it be...?

Willow whipped her head around, glaring daggers at Lyra. But Lyra just looked up, totally bored. She didn't even blink.

It was like... she hadn't even called the ship just to flex on her.

The mega-ship slid perfectly into the deep-water dock. Its massive shadow swallowed the whole pier. It felt heavy, like pure power.

Up on the top deck, a red-carpeted ramp rolled down.

A guy stood there. Custom black suit, tall, lean, looking like a total boss.

He was insanely hot, almost dangerously so, with the sun and sea framing him. He was pure class and power, looking down on everyone. Just standing there, he made everything else look completely dull.
