Chapter 79

I stomped down the aisle and slammed into my seat. "What's the joke?" I snapped.

Finnian lost it, laughing even harder. "Man, you learned a ton this summer, kiddo. But seriously? You suck at one major thing." I cocked my head. He reached across the aisle and tapped my nose. "Your poker face. It's garbage." He choked on a laugh. "Seriously, your face was gold. You looked like you were eating glass while texting that guy."

"Oh, shut up," I muttered, rolling my eyes.

That just sent him over the edge. He doubled over, clutching his knees. Then, he sobered up. Fast. "Look, jokes aside. The guy's an Alpha. Those pricks have egos the size of Texas. You give him that 'get away from me' look when he tries to hit on you—or even just talk to you—and he'll ruin you. He'll make your life in that pack a total nightmare." Finnian's eyes went dead serious. "Do not piss him off if you can avoid it."

I sighed and nodded. "Yeah. Fair point." I rubbed my face, sinking into the cushions. "But hey, that's a problem for future me. Once we touch down."

"True that," he said, leaning back.

Right then, Freya popped her head over the headrest. "Alphas are a nightmare," she warned. "I literally had to ditch my old pack because of some guy who couldn't take a hint. Some dudes just can't handle a 'no'." She took a slow, heavy breath. "Just watch your step with this guy. Hope you play it smarter than I did." She disappeared back into her seat.

*Thud.* The cabin door locked shut.

I whipped out my phone, scrolled to the very top, and started reading the massive wall of text my mom had sent. Within seconds, I was sucked in. At first, it was just basic stuff. How's your day, how's mine, the usual. But a week in? Everything went off the rails. My jaw clenched hard as my eyes flew down the screen.

By the time the plane leveled out at cruising altitude, the flight attendant was standing next to me. "Can I get you a drink or a snack?"

"Red wine," I growled. "Whatever cheap crap you have open."

Finnian's head whipped around, staring at me. I ignored him. My blood was boiling, and my nerves were shot. I needed alcohol, fast. Everything was going to hell.

"Whoa, Alm, what's up?" Finnian asked.

I just threw up a hand to shut him down. I had to finish reading this. He got the hint and backed off.

The attendant handed me the glass. I slammed it back in one gulp, handed it right back to her, and stared ahead. She shot a worried look at Finnian, but he just waved her off.

I kept reading, my blood pressure spiking with every word. "Those absolute bastards," I hissed.

"What? What happened?" Finnian leaned in, trying to squint at my screen.

I locked the phone and shoved it down. I was done reading. "Lyra, seriously, talk to me."

"I don't even know where to begin," I muttered, turning to my uncle. He nodded, urging me to spill. I just shook my head, sick to my stomach. "Okay, so mom's texts started normal. Just checking in, asking why I wasn't texting back. But then, a week later, Blair completely lost her mind."

"Why?" Finnian asked.

"Because the Alpha banned her from my room. Turns out, the second I left, she started sneaking in and stealing my clothes to wear around Damon. See, they only started hooking up in the first place because of my..." I paused, glancing at Freya. Whatever, she had to find out sooner or later. "My scent. Damon thought Blair was his fated mate, but actually..."

"It's you," Finnian cut in.

I nodded. "Yeah. So Blair went ballistic. She threw massive tantrums, screaming at my mom to go grab stuff from my room so she could keep up the lie. But mom actually stood her ground." I rubbed my temples, exhausted. "So Blair lost it. She beat her." A low growl ripped from my throat. "And my mom didn't even fight back. Because of Blair's dad, and because of the Alpha. She's just been sitting there, taking the hits."

"Are you shitting me?" Finnian slumped back, stunned.

I shook my head. "She claims it's no big deal. Says she's weak anyway and it doesn't even hurt. But she sent proof." Snarling, I unlocked my phone and shoved the screen right in Finnian's face.

Freya leaned over the seat, staring at the photos. Her face went totally blank. Cold.

"I'm going to murder her," Freya whispered, her voice pure ice.

"You can't," I said, taking a shaky breath. "She's the Beta's kid, and my mom's stepdaughter. In the Oakenfang pack, family business is off-limits. They don't touch domestic abuse." I bared my teeth, spitting out the pack's dirtiest little secret.

Finnian flinched. "What the actual fuck does that mean?"

"It means if you're family, you can beat the hell out of each other and the Alpha won't do shit. He thinks families should 'solve their own problems.' He literally says dealing with domestic drama is beneath him."

Finnian and Freya both snorted in disgust. But then, Freya gripped my shoulder tight. "Wait. This is perfect for you."

"How?" I asked.

"It means you can beat the living shit out of Blair and her dad, and nobody can stop you."
