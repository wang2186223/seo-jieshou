Chapter 335

"Quit calling me 'boy,' damn it. I'm twenty-seven," Gareth spat.

I just smirked. "Then start acting like a man. Earn that Beta title, or shut up."

I whipped around to Magnus. "You ready?"

"Look, I'm slammed today—" Magnus started.

I let out a low, nasty growl. His mouth snapped shut. Hands went up. "Okay, okay. I'll make time. Let's go lift the command."

"Good," I muttered, staring at the floor. "My bad. I'm wound tight. She's my last pack member, and I screwed up." I stared at the wall, waiting.

Magnus nodded. "Hey, no Alpha wants to fail their own. I get the rage. You trusted me with her, and you come back to this crap." He rubbed his neck, playing the guilt card. Fake as hell, but whatever. "My bad for my part in this."

I nodded. "Thanks. Just fix it. Today."

Damon stepped up and grabbed my hand. "I'm coming too." He gave a serious nod. "I hear you, but I need to see Freya. I need to see what this kind of mess does to a wolf."

I locked eyes with him and nodded.

On cue, we all stared down Gareth. I cocked my head. "Well? Pick a side. I'm done playing."

Gareth looked at Damon, then Magnus, and finally slumped. "Fine. I'll take the rejection."

"Great. Move it." I spun, threw the door open, and waved them through. "You guys know where I live, right?"

Damon frowned. "Wait, did you walk?"

I snorted. "Nope. Ran." I slammed the door, leading them down the stairs.

"Tell me we aren't walking," Gareth groaned, looking disgusted.

"I'm driving," Magnus said, already heading for his ride. He wanted this over fast. Perfect. Same here.

I hopped in the back. Damon bypassed the front seat and slid right next to me. He grabbed my hand, resting it on his thigh. "This cool?" He glanced at our fingers, then looked up with a soft smirk.

I stared out the window, totally blanking him. "Yeah, whatever." He squeezed my hand as the car rolled.

"I wasn't lying, you know," he murmured.

"About what?" I looked at him, lost.

"If you say yes, you're my Luna. Period." I let my jaw drop a little. "That letter? I meant every word."

"Damon, we're friends."

He nodded. "Sure. But we could be way more." He leaned in, smiling. He was going for a kiss. My brain went into overdrive—do I let him or push him away?

Then, the car jerked to a halt.

He backed off, but his face went totally pale. The smile vanished, replaced by pure disgust. "You did that to her?" he gasped, horrified. He stared out the front window, completely forgetting I was there. "I saw Freya before school. She was tough, full of energy, funny... She had so much..." He choked on his words.

I knew what he meant. "Life," I said. "She was full of life."

He looked back at me, eyes full of hurt. Real or fake? Hard to tell. "I get it," I said, nodding as I threw the door open.

"Freya!" I ran over. Juniper was holding her up. Freya's sunken eyes burned with pure hate as she stared at the car. Gareth and Magnus piled out and walked over. Freya began to shake violently—rage or terror, I couldn't tell. I threw my arm around her. "I'm here. I've got you," I whispered, holding her tight as I glared at the guys.

Gareth sneered, ready to talk trash.

I slapped my hand up. "Shut it. Think very carefully before you open your mouth. I am this close to snapping. You're only breathing because I respect Bradon and Alpha Magnus. Otherwise, I would've ripped your head off the second I saw your face."

He snorted. "Please. Damon held you back."

Damon sighed. "Dude, she let me hold her back."

Gareth stared at him. "Say what?"

"Lyra's training blows our warriors out of the water. She told you already—her dad trained her," Damon said, while Magnus just shook his head. "She could've ended you before we even blinked."

"Bullshit."

I smirked. "Wanna test that theory? Or are you gonna do what you came here for?"

Magnus smacked the back of Gareth's head and stepped forward. "Freya, I lift every single command I've put on you since you set foot on my pack lands."

Freya went limp as the magic broke.

"Gareth. Your turn," I growled, letting the threat hang in the air.

He spat a curse but stepped up. "I accept your rejection." He spun around and slammed himself back into the car.

"Lyra—" Damon reached out, but Freya turned to me.

"Alpha..." She let out a broken sob, tears bursting out as she collapsed into my chest.

"Shh. It's over. I've got you."
