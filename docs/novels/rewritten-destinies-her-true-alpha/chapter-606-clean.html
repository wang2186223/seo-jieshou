I backed up and shot a look at Finnian. He just shrugged. "Maybe it's your old man," he muttered, totally chill. I glared back like, *Yeah, maybe,* then jerked my chin toward the door. We headed in.

My chest tightened. Damn, this was heavy.

Omegas were running around like crazy, busting their asses. I stopped and looked around. Perfect. Just like I planned. Half the place looked cozy and lived-in. The other half? Dusty, dead, and locked tight.

"What's the play here?" Finnian asked, squinting into the dark, locked-up ballroom. "Why the half-and-half look?"

"To sell the lie," I snapped. We took the stairs. Kaelen was waiting right outside the office. "Where's my dad?" I asked. He jerked his head left, turned, and led the way. He threw a door open. Elara and my dad were sitting there, facing each other.

"Hey," I mumbled.

Dad looked up, giving me a tired-ass grin. The IV bag behind him was dripping, tubes hooked straight into his vein. "Hey, kiddo," he rasped.

"Hey, Dad." I walked over and dropped to my knees next to him. The guy should've looked twenty-eight, but this shit had aged him fast. His face was a nasty, sickly grey-green. He looked completely wiped out. "You holding up?"

He gasped for air, forcing a smirk. "Still breathing. Honestly, could be worse." He tried to laugh, then winced. "Ah, shit. That hurts."

Elara squeezed my shoulder, trying to look positive. "I'll go check the perimeter." Kaelen trailed right behind her. The door clicked shut. Dad waved his hand, throwing up a magic seal on the room.

I leaned in. "Stop wasting magic. You need to rest, damn it."

He reached out and patted my head. "Had to make sure nobody's listening to us."

Finnian slumped into a chair opposite him, wiping his sweaty forehead. "Make it quick, man. The council is rolling up." He checked his screen and cursed. "We're running out of time."

Dad nodded, dead serious. "I know. That's why we're talking now." I tried to get up, but his grip clamped down on my hand.

"Whoa, chill. I'm right here," I said, rubbing his knuckles. I looked at Finnian. "Grab me a chair, will ya?" Finnian stood up and dragged one over. I sat down, finally catching my breath after a crazy hour. I squeezed Dad's hand. "Alright, spill. What's wrong?"

Dad opened his mouth, but my phone buzzed, ruining the moment. I squeezed his hand, and he nodded like, *Go ahead, take it.* "Yeah?" I answered.

Orion was panting on the other end. "We're good to go. I'm heading in. Where you at?"

"In my dad's room. The one we set up."

"On my way." *Click.*

"Orion's coming up," I told Dad. He just nodded.

He let out a heavy sigh, shutting his eyes. "I'll wait then. Bet he's got some intel that fits right into this."

I shot Finnian a look. He threw his hands up. "Don't look at me, I'm clueless," he mumbled. I let out a dry laugh.

A knock rattled the door. Dad pointed at it. "Get that. I locked this place down tight with magic."

Finnian scowled, got up, and yanked the door open. "Get in here." He backed up. Orion walked right past him, grabbed my face, and kissed me hard.

I held his cheek. The guy was vibrating with stress. "Hey. You good?"

He shook his head, scooped me up like I weighed nothing, and pulled me into his lap. It was awkward as hell because I was still holding Dad's hand, but Orion just buried his face in my neck, sniffing me to calm his nerves. "I am now," he muttered.

I looked from my mate to my dad. "Okay, seriously, what's the deal?"

Dad nodded at Orion. "You go first, kid. Then I'll drop my bomb."

Orion squeezed my waist. "My guys are here."

"That's a win, right?" I frowned. I could feel him tense up even more as he took a deep breath.

"Only if they had good news," he said. Goosebumps broke out on my arms. "The council brought their entire army." Finnian and Dad froze. Orion nodded, dead serious. "And they've got the Black Spear."

Dad gasped, his face twisting in pure rage and shock.

"No fucking way," Finnian spat, shaking his head. "They don't have the right to touch that thing."

"They don't give a shit about the rules anymore," Dad sighed. His hand twitched. That IV was burning his veins, but it was working. His skin was losing that nasty grey look. He was bouncing back. "This just proves my point."

"Proves what?" I squirmed. Orion sucked in a sharp breath. "And what the hell is this Black Spear anyway?"

Dad's eyes flared. He looked at Orion, then back at me. "The Celestia gave the Black Spear to the very first Lycan. It lets the King rip a wolf's soul right out of their body when he kills them. No reincarnation. Nothing. You're just gone. It's the worst way to die for our kind." His voice went cold, and his angry, spicy scent flooded the room. "But we have a bigger problem right now."

"What's bigger than a soul-killing spear?" Finnian snorted, but my stomach did a flip.

Dad growled, low and mean. "We got a rat in our circle."

The room went dead silent.
