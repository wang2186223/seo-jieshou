Chapter 271

Boom. I let it loose. For a whole year, I’d kept my power locked down tight because the damn thing was still growing. Back when this shitshow started, I was just a kid, but I was already too much for any of these punks to handle. Now? I’m a whole different beast. A freak of nature. But I still held most of it back. Even so, the pressure hit the room in heavy, suffocating waves. The crowd gasped. Magnus and that bitch stumbled backward, faces pale.

I bared my teeth in a nasty, vicious grin. My fists clenched tight as I dragged the beast back inside. The power fought me, screaming to be let loose, begging to tear this place apart. Too bad. I’m the boss here. I shoved it back into its cage and faced the crowd.

"You know, I actually woke up today feeling hopeful," I said, my voice cutting through the silence. "Hope for us. Hope for the packs. This was supposed to be day one of our real lives. I got dressed with my friends." I whipped my head around, glaring daggers at the bastard who’d rushed the stage. "I just turned twenty-one." He flinched. Good. "I came here to graduate. Had my speech ready, felt light as a feather. I did exactly what my dad told me to do. I worked my ass off, got top of my class, followed every single rule he taught me. And what do I get? I get stopped at the finish line and asked how it feels to have a traitor for a father."

The guy shook his head, trying to backpedal. No way in hell. I leaned in. "You want the truth? The only real traitors here are the assholes running this goddamn kingdom." His eyes went wide as saucers. I didn't give a shit. Maybe my dad agreed to this stupid game. Maybe he was fine with this garbage. Well, I wasn't. And I made damn sure Orion saw it in my eyes.

Orion stepped out, moving slow. He stared at me, his eyes full of some emotion I didn't recognize anymore—and sure as hell didn't feel back. I could see he still cared, could see he wanted me, but my wolves and I had already made a pact. No turning back. He chose to drag my family's name through the mud for whatever sick game he and my dad were playing. Well, screw that.

I glared right back at him, pure fury burning in my chest. And guess what? He was the one who looked away, ashamed. He shuffled over to the podium. I gave it a beat, then turned my back on him.

"Alright, everyone, let's calm down. This is a graduation, not a damn trial," Orion began. I let out a loud snort. I felt his eyes drilling into my spine, but I didn't budge. Giving someone your back is the ultimate "screw you" for a wolf—it means they aren't even worth your time. And yeah, I just did that to my king. He sounded pissed as he kept talking. "Rhys Blackwood was a loyal soldier. Period. He was never a traitor. I don't know who started this rumor, but I'm going to get to the bottom of it." I snorted again. Get to the bottom of it? Please. It came straight from his own crooked-ass council.

Heels clicked behind me. The woman. She cleared her throat, trying to play it cool. "Yes, Orion and I will launch a full investigation," she piped up. But her voice gave her away. Everyone in the room could tell she was lying through her teeth. It was her. She did this.

"Who the hell are you anyway?" a voice shouted from the crowd. I spun around. I wanted to know too. Orion looked at me, his eyes begging me to understand. Then she grabbed his hand. A massive, blinding rock sparkled on her finger. God. The pieces clicked. I looked away, sick to my stomach.

"I'm Astrid La'Shen," she announced. Orion looked like he wanted to crawl into a hole as she finished: "The future Queen. Orion's fiancée."

Fiancée. My head whipped back to Orion. The guy looked like a walking corpse. All the life just drained right out of him.

"Fiancée?" The crowd started whispering, but the noise was just static to me. Astrid smirked at me like she’d won the lottery. I didn't give a damn about her. I was staring at Orion, looking at the absolute hollow ruin in his eyes.

Suddenly, my phone buzzed, loud and annoying, breaking the spell. I yanked it out of my graduation gown and swiped accept without even looking at the caller ID. "Yeah?"

"Lyra?" A tiny, shaking voice sobbed on the other end. "Lyra, is that really you?"

"Yeah... who's this?" I frowned. I knew that voice, but my brain was too fried to connect the dots.

"It's Luna." My heart stopped. Orion's eyes locked onto me like a laser. "There are bad guys here. And my dad..." She choked back a sob. "My dad can't hold them off much longer!"

Boom. Adrenaline kicked in. "Hide, sweetie. I'm coming right now."

"Hurry!" The line went dead. I dropped the phone. I locked eyes with Juniper. Instantly, my whole crew stood up, shoving their way into the aisle.

"Lyra, wait," Orion stepped toward me.

Too late. I shifted right there on the spot. Bones cracked, and Umbra took over. My wolf glared at Orion, curled her lip in a nasty snarl, then spun around and bolted out the door.
