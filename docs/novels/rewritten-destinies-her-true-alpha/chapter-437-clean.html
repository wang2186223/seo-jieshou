NovelVibe

Chapter 437

Raven let out a screeching hiss and stomped off. I just smirked at her retreating back. "Well, that was a blast." I spun around to Flint. He was staring, jaw practically on the floor. "Ready to roll?" I headed out, and he fell into step right behind me.

"That's it? Just like that?" He sounded totally blown away.

I stopped and blinked. "Huh? What do you mean?"

"Your reaction to my whole bomb drop! The Orion connection, the Lycan bloodline... you're seriously not going to freak?" He locked his arms across his chest.

My brows knitted together. "What, did you want a full-blown meltdown?" He shook his head. "Should I start screaming and beg you to put him on speed dial?" He tilted his head, playing along with the idea. I let out a sharp laugh. "Look, Flint. You're my buddy. Period. Nothing you say changes that. Not some fancy title, and definitely not your family tree." I reached out and gave his bicep a quick tap. "Besides," I whipped out my phone, flashing the screen at him, "I already have his digits. If I wanted to talk to the guy, I'd just call him myself." I flashed a grin and walked toward the ride.

"For real? You honestly don't give a crap?" He looked completely lost.

I snicker-laughed. "Not even a little bit. Why should I?" He stared at me for a beat, then walked around to the driver's side and climbed in. The locks clicked. I yanked my door open, only to find a small cardboard box sitting on the passenger seat. I scooped it up, plopped down, and wedged it between my knees. Slamming the door, I caught Flint staring at me again. "What now?"

"I don't know, man. I'm just not used to people playing it this cool." He tapped his fingers on the steering wheel, then shot me another look. "Honestly? It's throwing me off." He chuckled.

I looked over. "Why's that?"

He shook his head, sighing. "It's stupid, but let me just be real with you." I gave him a nod to spit it out. "Every single time someone finds out I'm connected to Orion..." He rubbed the back of his neck. "They immediately try to use me to get to him."

I nodded. "Makes sense." He snapped his head toward me, eyes wide, but I just laughed. "Hey, I said it makes sense, not that it's right." I buckled my seatbelt with a click. "People are naturally selfish. The sleazier they are, the more they try to take." I shrugged. "But I knew Orion before I even met you. If I wanted to get in touch with him, I could do it on my own. I don't need you as a stepping stone." He nodded, finally shifting into drive and pulling away, heading back to my pack's territory.

"True. Still, it's a weird vibe." He shot me a quick glance. "So... why are you so good to me then?" He sighed.

I laughed. "Duh. Because you're my friend."

"So it's not because I'm some big-shot Gamma from the royal pack?"

I snorted. "Please. I only threw that in Raven's face because she's obsessed with Orion. It doesn't actually mean jack to me." He raised an eyebrow, making me chuckle. "Look, let me break it down for you. I was raised to see the person, not the rank. I don't care if you're an Alpha, a Gamma, or a nobody. I'd treat you the exact same way."

He chewed on that for a second, then nodded. "Yeah. Fair point." The car went quiet for a minute. "Anyway, that box is packed with letters and packages. Stuff sent to you that I couldn't deliver before." He pointed down at my lap. "The one right on top? That was the last one."

I looked down at the envelope. "When did this come in?"

He let out a heavy sigh. "Graduation morning. Right before you came downstairs." His grip tightened on the wheel until his knuckles turned white. "I'm so sorry, Lyra."

"Did you open any of these?" I picked up the letter, flipping it between my fingers.

He shook his head. "No. But I have a pretty damn good idea of what's inside." He glanced over, his eyes looking like a kicked puppy, totally eaten up by guilt. "Seriously, I'm sorry."

That look—and those words—sent a cold spike of dread straight through my chest. I slid my finger under the seal, tearing open the envelope. My name was the only thing written on the front. I pulled the paper out and unfolded it.

*Lyra,*

*God only knows why you're ghosting me. I'm guessing you're still pissed, and honestly, I get it. I left you right in the middle of your heat. I swear to God, I didn't want to. I wanted to stay so bad, but I knew if I did, you'd end up hating my guts.*

*And I couldn't live with you hating me.*

*I want our first time to be perfect. I want you sober, making the choice with me. No regrets, no doubts.*

*Today is going to be a total shitshow, and you need to be ready for what's coming. Your dad and I have been planning this for over a year, and now the trap is springing. It's going to hurt like hell for you, but I swear, your dad was the mastermind behind all of it.*

*Just remember, I love you. Only you.*
