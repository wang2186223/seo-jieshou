Chapter 419

I breezed right through the first firewall. Piece of cake. Then—*bam*. The second one tripped the alarm, screaming bloody murder.

"Lyra!"

I let out a heavy sigh. "Yeah, yeah, on it." I patched the leak and threw myself back at it. Again. And again. And freaking again. By noon, I seriously wanted to smash my skull against the monitor.

"Lyra!" Elara's voice cut through the room again, making me groan.

"Are you kidding me?!" I slammed my fist onto the keyboard. I'd made it halfway to the mainframe without a peep, but this damn ninth firewall had me dead in my tracks. I stood up and started pacing like a caged animal.

Elara popped her head in. "Hey, go grab some lunch. Take a thirty-minute breather, clear your head, and then get back in the ring. Once you're settled, you'll still have three hours to wipe that virus." She flashed a smile and tossed a menu onto my desk. "Maybe you're just hangry."

I blew out a breath and gave a stiff nod. "Yeah, okay. You're probably right." Slumping back into my seat, I scanned the menu. My stomach growled. I needed meat. Like, a mountain of meat.

*Everything good?* I reached out to my wolves.

Umbra stared back at me, her eyes wide with panic. *Uh, I think we're about to hit a major snag.*

*What do you mean?* I locked onto them, my mind zoning out from the real world.

Terra crept out from the trees, looking totally rattled. *Shadow was just here.*

*Okay...* I looked around the mental space. *He's gone now, right? So what's the big deal? Look, I hate him dropping by, but I'll get over it.*

They both shook their heads. *You don't get it,* Umbra muttered, pressing close to Terra.

*Just spit it out!* I threw my hands up, totally losing my patience.

Terra locked eyes with Umbra and let out a heavy sigh. *We're going into heat.*

I froze solid. *Wait, what do you mean "we"?* I looked back and forth between them. *When? How the hell did this happen?*

*He said we can maybe hold it off for a week or two,* Umbra cut in. *But...*

*But what?* I stared at my wolves, a cold dread creeping up my spine. No way. I couldn't survive that nightmare alone again.

Terra leaned hard into Umbra, trying to steady herself. *We're both going into heat... at the exact same time.*

The words hit me like a punch to the gut. Panic flooded my system, and I had zero control over it.

I kicked my chair back, sending it crashing into the wall, and started pacing again. *How? Just how? We don't even have a mate! The only reason we went into heat before was because of that damn kudzu. How is this even possible?*

Both wolves avoided my gaze. Then Umbra looked up, looking incredibly guilty. *Once a wolf goes through her first heat, the pump is primed. It can happen again. There are just... triggers that make it way more likely.*

*Like what?* I snatched the menu off the desk. I needed meat. Right now. "Elara?"

"Yeah, Lyra?" She appeared at the door in a flash, looking totally confused.

I shoved the menu back at her. "My brain is completely fried. Can you just order me some steaks? Rare. I need pure protein." I turned my back. "And throw in some carbs."

She stared at me for a second, clearly picking up on my freak-out, and nodded. "You got it."

I snapped my focus back to my wolves. *Alright, spill. How did this happen?* They both slumped down, staring at their paws. They were hiding something, big time. *Talk to me!*

Terra sighed. *We're sorry. We didn't even realize it until today, and now it's too late. The train has already left the station.*

*What happened?*

Umbra finally looked me in the eye. *Shadow happened. He triggered it.*

I stumbled backward and collapsed into my chair. *What do you mean? What did he do to us?*

*Nothing!* Terra scrambled to her feet. *He didn't do anything wrong. He was just being a friend.*

I stared at my wolves, completely speechless. *I'm lost here. Make it make sense.*

Umbra nodded. *Look, after a wolf's first heat, the cycle can restart—even if that first time was forced on us. But normally, it only happens if we meet our true mate... or if we get drugged again.*

*Like with more kudzu.*

*Exactly. But...* Umbra looked away. *It can also trigger if a wolf is around someone they really like. Like... a chosen mate.*

If I weren't already sitting, my knees would have buckled. *But we don't have a chosen mate! We don't have a freaking mate, period!*

Terra nodded. *We know... but our hormones don't care about that.*

*Because...* I shook my head, staring at them in disbelief. *Are you guys kidding me? He literally hurt us.*

They both nodded. *We know. And he didn't mean to.* Umbra looked back toward the woods and sighed. *We didn't want this, but with Orion out of the picture, Shadow is just...*

*Amazing,* Terra finished, her eyes full of sorrow. *After Damon, I swore I'd never have another mate. Not one I actually loved. I was going to play along for you two, but I was keeping my heart locked down. No feelings. No drama.*

*Then what changed?*

*Shadow happened.*
