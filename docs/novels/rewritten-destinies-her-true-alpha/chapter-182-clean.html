Chapter 182

Juniper squeezed my hand hard. "Look, Lyra, thanks for trying, but don't BS me."

"I'm not," I snapped. I marched over to my lockbox, yanked out my burner phone, and dialed my dad. I held my breath, waiting.

"Kiddo, you know you shouldn't be calling this line," his voice came through.

"I know, Dad, I know. But I needed you. And honestly? Someone else here needs their dad way more."

A heavy sigh on the other end. Then, a sharp click. "Alright, kiddo. You're on with all of us."

I tapped the speaker button. "I locked down the room. Total soundproof spell. We're clear."

"Good girl. The Alphas are all right here. What's the emergency?"

I shoved the phone right under Juniper's nose. "Go on. Talk."

Juniper leaned forward, trembling. "Hello? Dad? Baba?"

"Juniper? Baby girl?" That deep, familiar voice boomed out.

Juniper snatched the phone right out of my hand, crushing it against her chest as she burst into tears. "Baba! They told me you were dead! They said everyone was gone—Mom, Tiven, all of them!"

"Hey, hey, we're right here. We're all alive, baby. Lyra's gonna fill you in. You weren't ever supposed to find out about this."

Juniper kept crying, and the rest of the girls just stared, stunned.

I spoke up. "Sorry, Dad. I couldn't keep her in the dark. Not any of them."

"Who exactly is in that room?"

I rattled off the names. "I went with my gut. They had to know."

Silence stretched. I heard some hushed whispering on his end. "You planning on telling them the whole story?"

I paused, weighing it. "As much as the Goddess lets me." Dad got the hint. I'd spill just enough to ease the gut feeling, no more.

"Good," he breathed, sounding way less tense. "Sorry we freaked you out, but we had no choice."

Then Juniper's dad cut back in. "Juniper, sweetheart, listen to Lyra. She knows the play. I'm sorry I kept you in the dark, but nothing is what it looks like."

"Okay, Baba," she sniffled, wiping her wet face.

"Your mom's gonna murder me for this, but damn, it's good to hear you, kiddo. Don't try our regular phones—they're totally dead. Just trust me, everyone's safe."

"How do I reach you then?" Hope practically dripped from her voice.

"You don't," her dad said flatly.

"Well, hold on," my dad chimed in, throwing them a bone. "Maybe not all the time, but we can probably manage a monthly check-in on this specific line."

"You'd really do that?" Juniper's dad's voice went extra deep.

"Absolutely," my dad promised.

"No brainer," I added, locking eyes with Juniper. "This burner is completely untraceable. And I'll make sure my place is a fortress. Bank on it."

Juniper nodded fast. "Thank you."

"I'll coordinate with your folks and text Lyra the details," my dad said.

"Thank you, Alpha," Juniper said, a real, honest-to-god smile finally breaking through her tears.

"Call me Caspian, kid."

Juniper handed me the phone, her face still wet.

"Hey, pup," my dad's voice came through. "Take me off speaker real quick?"

I tapped the screen and pressed the phone to my ear. "Yeah, Dad?"

"You sure about these girls?" I heard Terrence, Juniper's dad, grumble in the background, making my dad chuckle. "Not your kid, Terrence. The other ones."

"Yes, Dad. I feel it in my bones. I trust them. The Goddess hasn't steered me wrong yet."

A beat of silence. "Alright, kiddo. I trust your call."

"Thanks, Dad." I glanced at the totally baffled faces around the room. "Gotta run. Orion's on his way, and we've got a ton of shit to sort out."

"Orion?! As in King Rowan's Orion?!" His voice literally cracked, and I had to bite back a laugh.

"Bye, Dad." I clicked end, tossed the phone back in the lockbox, and snapped it shut.

"Okay, what the hell is happening?" Luna demanded, marching over and hopping onto the bed next to Juniper. "What's the deal?"

Juniper suddenly threw herself into my arms. "Thank you," she sobbed.

Luna was left awkwardly hugging thin air, and I just grinned, wrapping my arms tight around Juniper and pressing my cheek to hers.

"We're in this together," I whispered.

Juniper nodded, pulling back slightly. "Yeah. We are." She looked around the room. "All of us."

"Exactly." I stood up, facing the group. Luna threw an arm around Juniper's shoulders while Fawn and Daisy crawled onto the mattress. Suddenly, this weird, calm energy settled over the room. "This right here? All of us? It's not a coincidence. We were meant to find each other. I can feel it."

"Me too," Daisy agreed with a smile, while Fawn and Luna nodded along.

"Look, I know your heads are spinning," I said. "But I promise we're going to explain everything we can."

"We?" Fawn eyed Juniper, but Juniper just shook her head, clueless.

A quiet knock rattled the door. Everyone froze.

I flicked my wrist, dropping the barrier spell. "Let's head to the living room," I said, waving them forward. "Time to get some answers."

"Wait, is she talking about—"

They all trailed behind me as I walked over and swung the door wide open.

"Hey," I said, stepping aside.

"Holy shit," Fawn breathed.

I couldn't help but smirk as Orion strolled right in, carrying a cold case of beer.
