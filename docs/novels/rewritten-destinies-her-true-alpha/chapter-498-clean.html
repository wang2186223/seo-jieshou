Cipher swallowed hard. "But... what if I screw up again?"

She shrugged. "Then you screw up. Big deal. Playing it safe gets you absolutely nowhere."

She turned her back. "Walk the line, or don't. Your call, guys."

Bam. I was back in my own body. My knees buckled, but Cipher grabbed my arm to keep me up.

"You good?" he muttered softly.

"Yeah, fine." I tapped his hand, and—zap—a jolt of pure electricity shot between us. I frowned, pulling my hand back fast. I turned to face the crowd. "Alright, so I guess you guys have some questions." I forced a fake-ass smile.

Everyone was just staring at me, wide-eyed. "Holy crap, that was the goddess," Rhys gasped, his face totally white.

I nodded. "Yep."

"Wait, could that have been some kind of trick? Magic?" Kael looked around, but the others just shrugged.

I let out a heavy sigh. "Sure, magic can do crazy stuff." I smirked. "I can swap my face. I can fake my voice. But there's one line nobody crosses."

My dad nodded along. "Right. Faking a god is a hard line in the sand."

I nodded. "Exactly. You fake a goddess, even a little bit, and boom—your blessings are gone. Magic, shifting, whatever gifts you got. Your rank, your power, your aura. Stripped. Instantly."

Juniper's dad shook his head hard. "No way in hell that was a fake." He glared at the rest of them. "I've felt the Moon Goddess's vibe before. I know damn well you guys have too." The others nodded. "Look me in the eye right now and tell me that wasn't her."

Ash avoided his eyes. "Look, it felt like her. I was just trying to look at all the options."

I nodded. "Fair enough. But let me ask you something." I tilted my head, locking eyes with him.

He held my gaze and nodded. "Shoot."

The room went dead silent. Kael looked around, sweating, trying to find an ally. "Look, it's not like that..." he finally muttered.

Cipher stepped up, planting himself right behind me like a brick wall. "It's exactly like that." He scanned the room. "I've sat down with you guys plenty of times. Every single time, there's pushback. The second Caspian walks out the door, the whispering starts. I kept my mouth shut because Caspian told me to, but Lyra's 100% right. He saved your asses, did everything for your packs, and you still pull this crap? Why?"

Juniper's dad stood up, spinning around to face the others. "I kept my mouth shut to keep the peace. But the Macleans literally saved my pack's life. My wolves look up to them." He turned to my dad and gave a deep bow. "I should've spoken up sooner."

My dad sighed, looking exhausted. "You did, Conall. Don't worry about it."

"To you, yeah. But I should've laid it out for these guys a long time ago." He whipped back to the other alphas. "You guys whine about Caspian stealing our power and our packs, but he hasn't touched a damn thing. You whine about being weak alphas? Well, you're right. We are weak. But don't go blaming Caspian for that."

I stepped right up to Juniper's dad. "You're not weak." He looked at me, his eyes softening. I shook my head. "None of you are weak alphas. You're just new to this. Your packs are small right now, but we're gonna help you build them up."
