Chapter 436

That gift was easily the priciest thing brought in tonight. Total flex. But Elara didn't give a crap. Vivienne was mid-speech, hyping up the price tag, and Elara literally yawned right in her face. Zero chills. She looked like she'd rather watch paint dry than listen to this chick drone on.

Vivienne’s blood boiled. God, Elara was such a brat. Unbelievably ungrateful. Like, Vivienne just dropped a fortune on her, and she still got treated like garbage? Even worse than Alexandra, who wasn't even family. For years, Vivienne was basically the designated future sister-in-law. She’d spent so much cash and sucked up to Elara forever. And for what? Elara never once treated her like family.

But now? Elara didn't even know Lyra. They’d literally just met. Yet Elara was out here calling her "my brother's future wife" in front of everyone. Just like that. It made Vivienne’s years of hustling look like a joke. Was it seriously just because Lyra had the Lancaster last name? Was that the cheat code? Fine, the Lancasters loved Lyra because of blood. But why the hell did the Blackwoods care so much about Lancaster DNA?

Vivienne shot a look at Julian Vance. He was just staring at his phone, ignoring her. She swallowed her rage and forced a fake-ass smile. "Hey everyone, to celebrate Elara turning eighteen, I actually wrote a piano piece. It’s a custom track just for her big day."

"Miss Hale’s set was, like, super wild and energetic, but maybe she forgot who the actual birthday girl is. I’m gonna play something a bit more focused on Elara. Hope you like it, babe." Vivienne flashed a sweet smile and glided over to the piano bench. The stage lights dimmed and focused right on her, making her look like some pure, glowing angel. It was honestly a killer look.

Over in the corner, Lyra was chilling, sipping some lemonade and scrolling through her phone. She wasn't texting Julian back. Instead, she was reading a thread from someone named [Ms. Rainsford].

[OMG Mentor! Did you seriously get a new student behind my back?!]

[Am I not your favorite anymore? 😭💔]

A bunch of crying-face memes popped up next. Pretty wild coming from a woman pushing fifty.

Lyra just texted back: [?]

The reply came back instantly. [Mentor, some chick just showed off a design... and it’s a total rip-off of your style! Seriously, if it wasn't so messy, I would've sworn you drew it!]

[But nobody can copy you like that! Unless... you actually trained her?]

[Ugh, since when are your standards this low? That work is literally embarrassing!]

[Did she hold you at knifepoint? Tell me! If you can't blackball her, I'll do it myself and kick her out of the industry!]

The screen was practically screaming with exclamation points. The lady was clearly losing her mind over this knockoff.

Lyra sucked down the last of her lemonade, then tapped out a quick reply: [You said it yourself, it’s full of mistakes. Why would I ever teach someone to draw like that?]

A wave of happy emojis popped up, followed by total confusion.

[Okay, but if you didn't teach her, how did she pull that off?]

[Wait! Nobody can copy your vibe. Even I can't do it! If she actually pulled off a knockoff, she must be some kind of genius, right?]

[Actually, scratch that. If she was actually good, why is the necklace such a hot mess? The details are literally trash. Like, the diamond cuts and the chain link? If you had done it...]

Ms. Rainsford was typing at lightspeed. For a middle-aged lady, her thumbs were moving insanely fast—a skill she’d perfected solely to spam her mentor.

Lyra propped her chin on her hand, about to type back, when a loud, fake voice cut through the air. "Oh, wow. Who is this? I don't think I've ever seen her around before. What family is she even from?"

Three rich girls holding wine glasses strutted over. Lyra squinted, her eyes locking onto the one on the left—Genevieve.

The second girl smirked, trying to sound nice. "Right? A pretty face like that, she'd definitely stand out."

"Totally. If she belonged here, we would've heard about her already," the first one added, leaning in with a fake grin. She thrust a wine glass toward Lyra. "So, wanna hang out?"
