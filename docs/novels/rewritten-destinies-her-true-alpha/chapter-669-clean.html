Chapter 634

Same old pitch-black void. I was floating in that familiar, empty hell again. But this time? I didn't fight it. I just let go. If this was the end, I just hoped to God my parents made it out alive.

"They did." Phoebe’s voice drifted in from the dark.

I let out a massive breath. "Thank god." Nobody was around, but a huge weight just lifted off my chest. "Thank god."

"Wait, you don't care about anyone else?" she asked, sounding genuinely curious.

"Of course I do. But Orion? He’ll fight like hell to save everyone now that I’m gone." I let out a weak chuckle. "He’ll be absolutely wrecked, yeah, but he’s a damn good king. An even better man. He’ll make sure everyone is safe before he lets himself fall apart over me."

"Always so full of yourself," Morrigan chimed in, sounding amused. "Hey, sister, did we accidentally build a monster?"

Phoebe laughed, her voice getting closer. "Nope." Something brushed behind me, but I was totally paralyzed. "We built a queen." The words whispered right against my ear.

"You seriously aren't pissed about being stuck here, kiddo?" Morrigan’s voice called out from further away.

I tried to shake my head, or whatever I had left of one. "Nah." I leaned back into the blackness, letting my soul just drift. "I did what I had to do. Saved who I could. I gave it my all, and that’s all anyone can ask for. If I had to die to keep my folks alive, I’m totally fine with that."

"But you didn't finish what you started," Phoebe called out, her voice moving around. "You wanted payback... didn't you?"

A sudden wave of anger flared up in my chest. "Yeah... I did. I still do," I snapped. "I never got to avenge my pup." I tried to touch my stomach, but ugh, no hands.

"Which pup?" Morrigan’s voice had this playful edge that totally threw me off.

"What do you mean by that?" Suddenly, the stiffness broke and I could move again. "Luna is still alive. I don't need to avenge her... right?" Panic hit me like a truck. "Orion would've protected my baby!"

"And he did." Phoebe’s hand patted whatever my soul-body was, sending a sudden jolt of electricity through me. "The shaman pup is safe and sound."

"Wasn't talking about her," Morrigan chuckled.

"Then who? I only have my baby from my first life... and Luna." I tried to frown, but nothing worked.

A soft pink light started pulsing out of nowhere. A tiny, fluttering heartbeat echoed through the dark. "Are you sure about that?" A hand cupped my face, sending another shockwave of power through me. "Look. See for yourself."

Hands grabbed me, spinning me around. Off to the left, a tiny baby was floating inside a glowing pink bubble. "Who... who is that?"

"Your baby," Phoebe whispered. Her silver form stepped up right next to my left side. With a flick of her wrist, the tiny bubble drifted right into her hand.

"My baby from my first life? Damon's?" I reached out with my ghost arms, and the pulsing bubble flew straight into my chest. I hugged him tight. God, it was the first time I’d touched him in years. "I missed you so much, baby."

Phoebe hummed, pausing. "Well, not exactly."

"What do you mean?" I was totally lost, but for the first time since I died, I felt whole. It was like a missing piece of my soul had finally clicked back into place.

Morrigan stepped up on my right, green smoke swirling around her. She reached out, tracing her fingers over the glowing pink bubble protecting my baby. "Your pup's soul with Damon... inside your pup's body with Orion. A second shot at life. A deal's a deal, but only if you finish your job." She looked at the bubble with genuine awe. "And, well, if you survive."

I squeezed him tighter. "Are you serious?" I choked out, tears—or whatever they were—falling onto the glowing bubble. "Wait, what is that?" I watched shiny silver drops drip onto him.

"Soul tears," Phoebe whispered softly. She slid her hand onto my back, pumping waves of power into me. "You don't have a real body here. It's just your soul. When a soul cries, it sheds drops of its own life force. My little gift to you."

"Hence the glowing silver drops," Morrigan mimicked her sister, wrapping an arm around me and sending another jolt of power through my system. "Yeah, it hurts like hell. But you unlocked some crazy hidden power neither of us knew you had. It totally drained you."

Phoebe hummed again. "We definitely need to look into that. But if you want to bust out of here like last time, we need to..." She glanced at Morrigan.

Morrigan instantly shifted into her Maiden form. "Juice your soul up with enough power to kick your way out of limbo, as my followers would say." She grinned, but her body started glitching, getting totally unstable. "I'm burning extra juice to get you out of here."

Phoebe cupped my cheek. "That blast literally ripped your soul apart. Celestial power does that to a person."

"Celestial power? Hold up, what?" I was completely baffled. "What the hell did I even do?"

Morrigan grabbed me and started spinning me around. "Why don't you wake up and find out yourself?" Then, she let go.
