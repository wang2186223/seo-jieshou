Chapter 208

*Ding.* Another email popped up right as Juniper finished reading the first one.

"Hey, look," she said, tapping the screen. "This one's marked personal. Might actually explain this mess."

Me? I couldn't move. I was glued to the mattress, staring blankly. The words burned into my brain: *He has to date other women.* Seriously? What about me? Where did that leave me?

Deep inside my soul, in the pitch-black woods, Umbra let out a pathetic, low howl. Great. She’d been MIA for ages, and Terra only popped up once in a blue moon. They basically dumped all this crap on me and bailed. Honestly? I was getting sick of their attitude. My dad already threw me to the wolves. I had to freeze out my own mother just to weaponize our grief. Orion was off doing his own thing, and here we were, totally fractured. My wolves just straight-up went on strike.

We had a war to plan, and I couldn't even get a word out of them. I shook my head, furious. I’d given them space because, fine, they had their own trauma to deal with. But I was done. I marched right up to the edge of my inner forest and yelled into the dark.

"Alright, cut the crap! I don't care what your excuse is, but you don't get to just leave me hanging like this!"

Nothing. Just dead silence. Fine. I grabbed onto my magic—the only damn thing I had left.

"You're letting some guy ruin us. Again!" I screamed, turning my back on them.

"Ow! Shoot!" Juniper leaped back, snapping me out of it.

My eyes flew open. "What? What happened?"

She flinched away from my hand, pointing. I looked down. Holy crap. Sparks were literally dancing across my knuckles.

"Did I... did I zap you?" My voice sounded dead.

"No, no, it's fine," Juniper said, stepping back in. "Just a freak accident. Caught me off guard, is all."

I squeezed my eyes shut, shoved the magic back into its corner, and muttered a quick *thanks-and-bye*. I couldn't risk pissing off my power and losing that too.

"Sorry," I choked out, tears of pure frustration stinging my eyes. "I just..."

Juniper grabbed my shoulder. "Lyra, talk to me. You’ve been acting completely out of it lately."

"Umbra and Terra ghosted me the second Orion left," I said, wiping my face. "They see everything I'm going through, but apparently their little feelings are more important. They ditched me over a guy." The bitterness tasted like ash in my mouth.

"Maybe they just need time to process?" Juniper offered.

I let out a harsh laugh. "Oh, really? Has Taz ever ignored you because she was having a tantrum? Has your wolf ever locked herself so deep in your head that you couldn't even feel her? Terra throws me a bone once a month, but Umbra? She’s been a ghost for six damn months!" I threw myself backward onto the bed. "I’ve been totally on my own for half a year. I get it, everyone needs space. But come on! When does it end?"

"Oh, sweetie," Juniper sighed, wrapping her arms around me. "Why didn't you tell us?"

"Because I didn't want you guys looking at me like I'm broken. I didn't want to be the pathetic girl without a wolf again." I pulled back to look her in the eye. "I've been there. Before, I had Umbra's voice but couldn't shift. Now? I can shift, but the wolf is gone." I collapsed back against her, my voice dropping to a whisper. "And shifting... it's killing me."

I hated admitting it. Ever since they went silent, every single shift felt like my bones were breaking. The damage was brutal, and the recovery took forever. "After today's training, the pain was so bad I had to beg Flint for willow bark pills just to walk into my exam."

Juniper gasped. "Lyra... you know that stuff is highly addictive, right?"

I just nodded. "I had no choice. I couldn't even stand up," I said, burying my face in my hands. "Yesterday, I barely managed to shift back to run home. And today? Today my damn leg got stuck mid-shift."

Juniper gasped, horrified. "Oh my god. What are we going to do?"

She squeezed me tight, but my phone started buzzing in my pocket. I pulled it out and swiped.

"Yeah?"

"Hey, Lyra... it's Finnian."

I closed my eyes, bracing myself. "Hey, Finnian. What's up?"

"Alpha Raven and Damon are still downstairs. They're waiting for you."
