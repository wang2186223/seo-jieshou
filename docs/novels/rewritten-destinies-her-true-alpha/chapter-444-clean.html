Chapter 444

I shoved her shoulder. "No way. You're joking."

She just shook her head, dead serious. "You don't get it, Lyra. We took care of you, but damn, I thought you were a goner. Going through a heat solo is brutal enough. But with a shredded heart? It almost put you in the dirt." Her face twisted, pure fury in her eyes. "For days, you wouldn't touch food. Not even a drop of water. You literally shrank into a skeleton." She stared down at her empty palms. "By the time you woke up, your wolves were working overtime just to keep you breathing. That's why you didn't realize how close you came. But at the peak of that heat? Your heart flatlined."

Juniper locked eyes with me. "But even after all that hell, I knew. Orion did it because he loved you. He didn't want to break you." She let out a heavy sigh, nodding. "Took me forever to finally see that."

I nodded. "Yeah. Same here." I slumped back onto the couch. "Took me a whole damn year to stop hating his guts. For the heat, for ghosting me. Then graduation hit, and boom—right back to square one. All that pain and rage came screaming back." I dragged my hands down my face. "How the hell do I even look at him?" I choked up, then snapped myself out of it. "I don't even know if he gives a shit anymore. Or what Cipher is thinking." I groaned, throwing my head back. "Fuck. I don't even know what *I* want."

"Well..." Juniper stood up, grabbed my wrists, and hauled me to my feet. She spun me toward the staircase and gave me a hard shove. "Maybe you should go read those damn letters and find out."

I tripped over my own feet, stopping at the bottom step. I looked back, wide-eyed. "I'm... I'm terrified."

She gave me a gentle, knowing smile. "Why's that?"

"What if I actually want both of them? Orion *and* Cipher?"

She burst out laughing. "Oh, the horror! Two hot guys fighting over you?" Juniper threw her hands up. "Shit, I'd settle for just one!"

I snorted. "Not funny. That's not what I meant."

Her grin faded. "I know. But you're stressing over a done deal." She walked over, framing my face in her hands. "You already like both of them, Lyra. Yeah, it's scary. But it is what it is. Stop fighting it. Focus on how you feel about them—and how they feel about you." My body went rigid. She chuckled. "Relax. Stop overthinking. Step one: read the letters. Hopefully, they'll clear up this mess." She whipped me around and shoved me up the first step.

"But what about Luna—"

"Luna is totally fine." Juniper laughed, cutting me off. "We get it. You're swamped until this shitstorm blows over. We knew what we were getting into. Daisy and I talked to Luna. You did too, and so did your mom. She gets it. We all get it. Now get your lazy ass upstairs and see if your guy is on the same page."

My stomach did a flip. "And if he's not?"

Her voice softened, losing the edge. "Then you close the book. No more of this wishy-washy, back-and-forth bullshit." I snorted, and she threw her hands up. "Hey, don't roll your eyes. It's the truth and you know it." She leaned in, planting a quick kiss on my forehead. "Every time you try to walk away, boom—you get dragged right back in." She ran her fingers through my hair. "Honestly, that's why Daisy and I always thought Orion was your mate. No matter what, he's like a magnet." She chuckled. "But not like those toxic, red-flag guys in movies. He just does it by existing." She spun me back toward the stairs. "Go. Now."

My boots felt like lead. "But what if—"

Juniper let out a low growl. "Then we deal with it. Now move your ass up those stairs and stop being a chicken."

I growled right back at her, but finally took a step. Every single stair felt like wading through wet cement.

*Why are you freaking out?* Umbra paced back and forth in my head.

*Probably for the exact same reason you are,* Terra chimed in, chuckling. Umbra growled, but Terra just laughed it off. She was way tougher than she let on. *She's terrified that whatever is in those letters is gonna flip her whole world upside down.*

*So?* Umbra snapped, looking back at me.

Terra sighed, stretching her body against our mental barrier. *Or... it changes absolutely nothing.*

*Wait, what?* Umbra looked back and forth between us. *What the hell does that mean?*

*She's scared the letters will change how she feels, but won't change the reality of the situation.* Umbra still looked completely lost. Terra sighed heavily. *Think about it. What if reading this makes her fall head over heels for Orion again?*

*Then we get to be with Orion and Shadow! Duh!* Umbra barked.

Terra nodded slowly. *Right. But what if we can't?*

*What?* Umbra plopped her butt down, totally baffled. *That makes zero sense.*

Terra nodded again. *What if you find your true mate? Or what if he finds his, and it's some other girl? Or worse—what if Lyra reads everything, feels absolutely nothing, and still wants zero part of them? What then?*
