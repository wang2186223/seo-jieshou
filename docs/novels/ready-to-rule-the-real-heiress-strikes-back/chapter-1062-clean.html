Lucinda wouldn't even look at Frank. She turned her head away and said, "You are not my father."

Her voice shook with anger as she went on, "Grandpa has loved me and spoiled me since I was little. But you? You've never once acted like a real father, not even for a single day. And now you do something so heartless... How could you?"

Her eyes began to shine with tears.

Kylie saw her and rushed over, wrapping an arm around her shoulders. "Lucinda, we never imagined he'd turn out to be this kind of person. Since you've decided to call the police, let them handle it. He needs to face the consequences."

Lucinda nodded right away. "Okay."

When Frank realized Lucinda was actually serious about calling the police, he exploded.

"You ungrateful brat! I came here for you, and this is how you repay me? You really want to send your own father to jail? You're just as heartless as your mother!"

His words got nastier and nastier, but even with all his anger, Frank didn't mention that he'd been working with Lucinda and Grace. If that came out, Lucinda would be in trouble too.

What Frank couldn't wrap his head around was why, even after the Norwoods had handed the decision over to Lucinda, she still chose to turn him in.

Why? He was her father. Everything he'd done, he'd done following Grace's plan, supposedly for Lucinda's own good.

How could she be so cold?

It was only now that Frank realized he'd been set up by both Grace and his own daughter. And there was nothing he could do about it. He couldn't even speak up. The whole thing left him feeling completely defeated.

Kylie couldn't stand to hear another word. She had someone stuff a cloth into Frank's mouth to shut him up until the police arrived.

She shook her head, sighing. “I never thought he'd turn out to be like this. Thank goodness Lucinda never went back to him when she had the chance. Imagine what her life would be like if she had."

It was just an offhand remark, but Lucinda suddenly broke down. She dropped to her knees in front of Kylie and Cameron sobbing. "I'm so Sorry, Mom, Dad. This all my fault. If it weren't for me, Grandpa never would have been hurt. Please... punish me."

In her heart, Lucinda could hardly believe she had such a disgraceful father. She was grateful every day that she'd refused to go back to Frank.

She never expected he'd show up at the house himself. Looking back, she realized she never should've let him inside. All of this trouble could have been avoided.

But after everything that happened, one thing was clear: she could never leave the Norwood family. If she left them and had to rely on her biological father, what kind of life would she have?

Kylie and Cameron hurried over to lift her up.

“Lucinda, this isn't your fault," Kylie said softly. "If anyone is to blame, it's him."

“That's right, sweetheart. Come on, get up. We don't blame you at all," Cameron added.

But Lucinda stayed where she was, crying even harder. "Dad, Mom, 10 know you love me, but he did such a horrible thing and Grandpa got hurt because of it. I can't forgive myself. Please, just punish me."

He was gone. That nightmare was finally over.
