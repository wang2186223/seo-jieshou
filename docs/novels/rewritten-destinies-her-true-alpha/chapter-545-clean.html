Chapter 545

Dead silence. Then Garithi lost it, howling with laughter. "Man, you gotta be kidding me! That is gold!"

Orion let out a low, nasty growl. "Back off Lyra. She’s got nothing to do with this shit."

Damon’s jaw hit the floor. "Wait, you tapped that? You mated Lyra?"

Orion kept his eyes locked right on Roric. "Take my head if you want, but keep your hands off her."

Boom. Umbra and Terra bolted straight back to Orion, completely blowing past me. Whatever. The idiot was playing hero. Orion’s eyes scanned the crowd, locking onto mine for a split second. I read him instantly. We were totally outgunned. He wanted me to sneak out while they were distracted. He’d take a bullet for me in a heartbeat if it meant I got away clean.

Blair lost her damn mind, screaming. "Why is everyone so fucking obsessed with Lyra?!" She spat my name like it was poison. "Big deal, she’s a female Alpha. She’s ugly, useless, and can’t even keep a guy in her bed!"

Orion chuckled, dragging his limp body up, his head wobbling. I lunged to grab him, but Umbra crashed out of the brush. *Hold up. He’s playing them.*

I froze. *What do you mean?*

Umbra panted. *Dude was trained just like you. His old man made him immune to silver. He’s totally fine. He’s just buying us time to run.*

I sneered. *Screw that.*

Umbra snorted. *Hell yeah. Let's do this.* I turned my eyes back to Roric.

Orion finally hauled himself up to look at Blair. "Hey, since I’m about to kick the bucket, let me drop some truth bombs on you." He cocked an eyebrow, waiting for her to nod. "She never gave a crap about Damon."

"Bullshit!" Blair snapped. "He’s an Alpha!"

"A pathetic one," Orion shot back, raising a hand to cut her off. "Wanna know how I’m so sure?"

Blair glared, scoffing. "Oh, do tell." She rolled her eyes like he was full of crap.

"Damon got dumped freshman year, right in the middle of the cafeteria, in front of hundreds of girls. Am I right?" Orion whipped his head toward Damon. "Well? Am I?"

Damon glared, giving a stiff nod. "I guess she talked."

Orion smirked. "Oh, she did. But not for the reason you think." He turned to Roric. "You guys share the same bloodline, so you’ll know if I’m bullshitting. Your mother passed down a little cheat code, didn't she? You live, you die, and then—boom—you get a do-over. A second chance to fix whatever screwed you over the first time. Right?"

The crowd gasped. But Blair just cackled. "Are you out of your mind? That’s insane!" The whole crowd joined in, laughing and whispering that the silver had fried his brain.

Roric snapped his head toward Astrid, eyes blazing. "Is this true?"

The laughter died instantly. "Wait, what?"

Astrid’s face went white. "I... I thought..."

"Thought what?! That dying and waking up years in the past was just a normal Tuesday?!" Roric stood up, towering over her. "When?"

"When was I reborn?"

"Yeah, when were you reborn?!" Roric mocked her in a high-pitched whine, slamming his fist onto the wooden pew. *Crack.* "Spit it out! When did you come back?!"

Astrid shrank back, looking around. "Twenty. I was twenty. My mate killed me after finding out I was a bastard. Right before I died, my mom told me about you. So when I woke up in the past, I tracked you down first and dumped him."

Roric stared, disgusted. "You played me."

"No! I thought it was a gift from the Goddess! I kept it a secret. You never told me you came back too!" She started sobbing, but Roric shut her up with a cold glare and a raised hand. "We'll deal with this later." He turned back to Orion. "So Lyra came back too?"

Orion nodded. "Her first mate murdered her when she was twenty-four."

Roric narrowed his eyes. "And what does that have to do with today?"

Orion grinned. "Everything. See, Lyra met her mate on her eighteenth birthday. Mated him a month later, became Luna. She tried her best, but she never shifted in her past life."

"Never balanced her wolf and her magic," Roric snorted.

"Because nobody taught her," Orion coughed. "Her dad died before he could show her. But she was still a badass Alpha."

"Who gives a flying fuck?!" Blair shrieked.

"Trust me, there’s a punchline," Orion chuckled. "Her mate cheated on her with Blair. Then, in a fit of rage, he killed their unborn pup." Blair’s brow furrowed, but Damon went completely rigid. "So when she woke up on her eighteenth birthday again, she ran straight to her dad’s pack. He helped her unlock her magic. I'm sure you knew about that, right?" He looked at Caspian.

"No way..." Damon whispered, his voice shaking.

Orion clapped his hands. "Bingo! You got it." Orion laughed. "You made the exact same mistake twice. You threw away a prime Alpha mate for her trashy, whore sister. So she dumped your ass in college, and used your pathetic, desperate chasing to get close to you."

Damon whispered, "Why?"
