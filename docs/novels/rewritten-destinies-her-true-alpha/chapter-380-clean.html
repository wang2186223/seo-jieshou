Chapter 380

"Wait, a closet? That's your big getaway plan?" Rhys Thorne looked totally lost. Gideon Stone's face matched his.

I just smirked. "Move it. Trust me."

I stepped inside. They crowded in right behind me.

"Shut it, Rhys. Tight."

"Got it," he muttered.

*Click.* Darkness. Then I grabbed the handle of the second door, swung it open, and popped the one right behind it.

"Uh, a door inside a door?" Lyra Bloom let out a tiny giggle.

I blocked their view with my shoulder, keeping the mystery alive. "Yep. Wanna guess why?"

"Why?" Lyra nudged closer.

I threw both doors wide open. "Boom. Magic closet. Let's go."

I gestured them through. We marched straight through my bedroom, hit the hallway, and headed down the stairs.

"No way. A whole freaking house inside a closet?" Mara Croft's jaw hit the floor. She jabbed Gideon in the ribs. "Dude, we're drowning in bills for one place, and she's got a whole mansion hidden in her wardrobe."

I chuckled, throwing a wink at Lyra. "Come here, kiddo. Got someone cool for you to meet." I reached down, and she held up her hands.

"Sorry," she mumbled into my shoulder as we started down.

We hit the bottom landing, and I looked at her. "Hey, what's the sorry for?"

She stared at her tiny fingers. "Stairs... they suck. They make me super tired. I don't want to be a pain. You don't have to carry me."

I tapped her cheek with my finger. "Listen to me. Carrying you? Never a chore. Not ever. Got it?"

"Lyra!"

A high-pitched squeal cut through the air. Before I could even turn, Luna launched herself at me. I caught her mid-air.

"Hey, sweet pea! How was your day?" I pushed her messy curls back and planted a kiss on her forehead.

"Awesome! Aunt Daisy and Aunt Juniper took me running. Oh, and we had lunch with Daddy!"

I grinned. "Sounds like a killer day. But guess what? I've got a surprise."

Behind me, Daisy and Juniper froze in their tracks, spotting the crowd.

"A surprise? What is it?" Luna peeked over my shoulder. Her eyes went huge.

"Meet..." I stepped to the side, gently pulling Lyra next to me. "...Lyra Bloom."

Luna's face lit up. She squirmed to get down. "Hi!"

Lyra gave a shy little wave. "Hi."

"I'm Luna!" Luna beamed. "Wanna be besties?"

Lyra's smile grew. "Yeah. I'd love that."

"Wanna see my room? I've got tons of toys!" Luna reached out, but I gently tapped her shoulder.

"Hold on, sweetie. Lyra's legs are a bit tired today. Stairs are tough for her right now."

Luna blinked, then spun around to Daisy and Juniper. "Can Aunt Daisy and Aunt Juniper carry her?" She looked back at the adults. "That way, you guys can talk and do the whole house tour thing."

I locked eyes with Mara and Gideon. "Daisy and Juniper are my ride-or-dies. They'd protect Lyra with their lives." I ruffled Luna's hair. "I trust them with my own kid every single second."

The parents exchanged a tense look, then glanced down at the two eager little faces. Mara let out a long, slow sigh. She looked at me. "You swear she'll be safe?"

"On my life," I said, nodding. I turned to the girls. "You guys up for this?"

"Duh, of course!" Daisy grinned, stepping up to Lyra. "Hey there, I'm Daisy. Mind if I give you a lift upstairs?"

Lyra looked back at her mom and dad. They both gave her a reassuring nod. She turned back to Daisy, holding her arms up. "Yes, please." Daisy scooped her up like a feather.

"Come on, Luna!" Juniper opened her arms, and Luna leaped right in. Just like that, the four of them headed off, leaving us to talk.

I gestured for the rest of the crew to follow me out the front door. "Welcome to our safe house." We stepped onto the lush grass, and I turned to show off the cabin. "My great-grandma owned this land. She lived here, loved hard, and died... well, pretty much."

That got a chuckle out of them.

"And this whole place is... inside your closet?" Rhys's eyes were practically popping out of his skull.

"Basically. The closet is a permanent portal. Always open." I led them around the side of the building. "Look, it's not a five-star resort, but it's secure. Magnus won't find us here. It's too far. Plus, jackpot—we've got plenty of bathrooms. Bedrooms, though? Yeah, we're gonna be tight."

Gideon caught Mara's eye. "We can handle it. It's temporary."

"Damn right it is," I agreed. "My dad's pack is going to rebuild. We're all joining them. You guys will get real homes, real jobs, and—thank the goddess—actual decent pay." I pointedly looked at Elara and Rhys. "No BS rules about omega pregnancies. No getting punished just for wanting a promotion." I looked back out over the fields. "And once the war is over, your kids can grow up without looking over their shoulders."

Mara gasped, her face going pale. "Wait... war?"
