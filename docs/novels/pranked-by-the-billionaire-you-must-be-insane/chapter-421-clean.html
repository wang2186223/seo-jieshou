Chapter 424

Sure, VIPs like them could hire any tutor they wanted. But the Blackwoods? They brought in the actual heavy hitters. Global legends. The absolute bosses of their industries.

Elara couldn't hoard them all.

Which meant the leftovers were up for grabs.

Show off tonight, grab a quick compliment, or—fingers crossed—get scouted by one of these giants? Boom. Instant clout.

But there was a bigger prize.

Tonight, Elara officially hit eighteen.

She was legal. Fair game for dating.

Lock her down, and you lock down the entire Blackwood empire. It was the ultimate merger.

Yeah, everyone whispered about her and the Keller kid. Sure, the Kellers only had money because the Blackwoods bankrolled them.

But look around.

This was the heiress's big debut.

And where the hell were the Kellers? Nowhere.

Translation? The spot was wide open.

Meanwhile, every girl in the room kept sliding their eyes toward the guy chilling next to Arthur. He hadn't said a word, but he owned the room.

One look at that ridiculously hot face, and girls were literally blushing.

Vivienne watched them sneak looks at Julian Vance, all of them acting like they were about to audition for his bed. She dug her nails into her palms, ready to snap them.

Thirsty, shameless sluts.

Did they seriously forget the Lancasters and Blackwoods had a deal?

Didn't they know Julian was basically her fiancé?

And here they were, throwing themselves at him.

Total homewreckers.

And they called themselves high society. Gross.

"Why the long face, Miss Lancaster?"

A gorgeous girl in a tight champagne dress walked over, holding a glass and wearing a smug, red-lipped grin.

She smirked, totally shameless. "Jealous because everyone's drooling over Nicholas?"

Vivienne stiffened. She knew this chick.

Alexandra Hale. Her dad was the richest guy in Marivelle Port.

Lately, the Hales and Blackwoods were co-signing some massive deal, so she’d been hanging around Vaelion City a lot.

Making a lot of noise, too.

Vivienne glared. Alexandra swirled her champagne, clicking her tongue. "You know, everyone talks about this big merger. But I’ve been in town for weeks, hit a ton of parties, and I haven't seen you two say a single word to each other."

She leaned in. "Actually, tonight? Nicholas hasn't even looked at you, has he?"

Vivienne’s face went pale. Alexandra’s grin widened. "You’ve been hyping up this engagement for years, but the Blackwoods haven't said a peep. Is it even real? Or are you just playing pretend, Miss Lancaster?"

The words stung like acid. Vivienne shook, her hands trembling with pure humiliation. She could barely keep her face straight.

"Watch your mouth, Miss Hale! You're an outsider. Keep your nose out of Lancaster business!" Beatrice snapped, stepping in front of Vivienne. She’d always hated this arrogant Marivelle Port brat. "You're only attacking Vivienne because you want Julian Vance for yourself!"

Caught red-handed, Alexandra didn't even blink. She just laughed.

She threw her chin up, totally owning it. "So what if I want Nicholas?"

"My family’s got just as much cash as yours."

"I'm hotter. Obviously."

"And smarts? I started at my dad's company at eighteen. By twenty, I was running three international branches by myself."

"How am I worse than her? Why shouldn't I go after him? It's a free market, sweetie. May the best woman win. Got a problem with that?"

"Or does someone think a fake title gives them a free pass?"

Each word hit like a slap. Bold, aggressive, and 100% confident.

Vivienne literally shook.

Even Beatrice stood there, mouth open, completely shut down.

Vivienne swallowed her rage, grabbing Beatrice's arm to hold her back.

She forced her signature sweet, polite smile onto her face. "Beatrice, it's fine. Miss Hale is right. Julian is amazing. It’s only natural girls want him."

She glanced over at Julian. "He’s always cold to everyone anyway. Honestly, I’m just happy he’s so popular."

She said it like they were already married, playing the classy, secure wife.
